## Section A — Identification (the master term)

### A.0 The titular redefinition: from persuasion to identification

Burke's single most consequential move in the *Rhetoric* is the relocation of rhetoric's master term from **persuasion** to **identification** (RM-00 pp.xiii–xiv; RM-01 pp.19–20, 45–46). `burke-direct` He reaches it not by rejecting persuasion but by a dialectical raising of the level of generalization: an imagery of slaying is a special case of *transformation*, and transformation involves the ideas and imagery of identification — because to change a thing's nature, and to state that nature before and after, is to *identify* it (p.20). Rhetoric is therefore "reduced" to the wider-scope term identification, with strife, killing, and persuasion treated as species or facets. The strategic payoff Burke announces frankly: by this choice one can treat war as a "special case of peace," a derivative perversion of communion rather than a primary motive (p.20).

Persuasion re-enters at the close as one inseparable facet, not the genus: "there is no chance of our keeping apart the meanings of persuasion, identification ('consubstantiality') and communication (the nature of rhetoric as 'addressed')" (p.46). `burke-direct` The master claim is that the three are facets of one motive.

**Why identification and not persuasion (the intermediate-area argument).** RM-00 (pp.xiii–xiv) gives the deep reason: persuasion implies deliberate intent, but the most consequential rhetoric inhabits an **intermediate area** between the wholly deliberate and the wholly unconscious — the politician sincerely self-deceived, the literary work doing political work at one remove (Milton's *Samson*). `burke-direct` Identification captures this intermediate area; classical "persuasion" cannot.

### A.1 Identification defined (operational entry)

**Operational definition (burke-direct, ≤60 words, pp.19–20).** A is identified with B when their interests are joined — or when A *assumes or is persuaded* that they are joined — even though A and B remain non-identical. To identify A with B is the rhetorical act; it spans the deliberate, the cunning, the unconscious, and the self-addressed.

**Question answered.** With whom (or what) is this agent made one, and how?

**Boundary criteria (burke-direct):**
1. **Distinct from identity.** Identification is not sameness; the parties remain "both joined and separate" (p.21). It is also not mere similarity — its ground is shared *act/substance*, not resemblance.
2. **Ambivalently real-or-persuaded.** Interests may be genuinely joined or A may merely be "persuaded to believe" they are (p.20). Identification covers both. This is constitutive, not a defect (tension T1, RM-01).
3. **Can operate without conscious design.** The rhetorical motive works "by default," through self-imposed inadequate terms ("profitable malingering"), and through self-address (pp.35–39).
4. **Compensatory to division.** Identification is affirmed *because* there is division; it presupposes prior separateness (p.22).
5. **Accomplished as making-consubstantial.** To identify A with B *is* to make them consubstantial (p.21) — Section B is the substance-theoretic ground.

### A.2 The orders / variants of identification

Burke develops identification through a series of concrete fields and registers. The following catalog (all `burke-direct`) is the operational inventory the integration draws on:

| Variant | Gloss | Anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **Identification by property** | A thing is identified by its properties; rhetorically, often by *economic* property. Surrounding himself with properties that "name his number," man is ethical, yet property-against-property breeds discord. | RM-01 pp.23–27 |
| **Identification by interests / "proving opposites"** | Persuasion = identifying your cause with another's interests. Where cooperation ends and exploitation begins cannot be scientifically fixed; this ambiguity is the invitation to rhetoric (per Aristotle, rhetoric "proves opposites"). | RM-01 p.25 |
| **Identification by default (the "autonomous")** | An activity's intrinsic autonomy does not free it from identification with extrinsic orders; "'Belonging' … is rhetorical." To praise a specialty without dissociating from its uses is to identify with them by default. | RM-01 pp.27–28 |
| **Ingenuous vs cunning identification** | The motive operating without design ("profitable malingering") vs. terms "not incisive enough to criticize" an interest — bringing rhetoric "to the edge of cunning" (Iago, Tartuffe as "demons of Rhetoric"). | RM-01 pp.35–37 |
| **Identification as addressed / self-address** | Persuasion implies an audience; a man can be his own audience — "an 'I' addressing its 'me'" (Mead). Socialization is completed "from within." | RM-01 pp.37–39 |
| **Identification through form (formal appeal)** | Purely formal structures (antithesis, gradatio, amplification) arouse "collaborative expectancy," priming assent independent of propositional content — a species of identification with the speaker's *movement*. | RM-02 pp.57–59, 65–69 |
| **Identification through the image** | The image is "built of identifications": translating an argument into an image yields an "unearned increment" of consubstantiality not explicitly contracted. | RM-02 pp.84–88 |
| **Identification across ranks (courtship)** | Suasive address across hierarchic estrangement; the aim and product of courtship. | RM-04, RM-06 (Section C) |
| **Identification through mystery** | Differential identification across a categorical (class/rank) boundary; mystery is its medium. | RM-03, RM-04 (Section C) |
| **Ultimate identification** | Identification pushed to its dialectical limit: mystical union with the universal order / hierarchic principle ("the end of all desire"). | RM-07 pp.328–333 (Section C) |

**Identification's dual valence (tension, burke-direct).** The same mechanism that binds a community can be "cloaked malice." Identification names *both* a genuine sharing of substance (acting-together) *and* a strategic/cunning device that masks real division (RM-00 tension-03; RM-01 tension T1). Burke holds both without resolving which is primary — a tension the integration must carry into the bridge (Section D, divergence 2).

## Section B — Consubstantiality / Consubstantiation

### B.1 The substance-theoretic core

**Operational definition (burke-direct, p.21).** To identify A with B is to make A "consubstantial" with B: A is "substantially one" with a person other than himself yet remains "an individual locus of motives" — "both joined and separate, at once a distinct substance and consubstantial with another." Consubstantiality is **not** identity and **not** mere similarity; it is a precise *double structure* (joined-AND-separate held simultaneously).

**The act-basis ("acting-together").** Its ground is *action*. On the old philosophies "substance … was an act"; a way of life is "an acting-together"; and in acting together, men come to share the sensations, concepts, images, ideas, and *attitudes* "that make them consubstantial" (p.21). `burke-direct` Three consequences the integration depends on:
1. Consubstantiality is **act-based** — a co-*action*, not a static likeness.
2. It is **substance-theoretic** — carried directly from the *Grammar*'s paradox of substance.
3. **Attitude is internal to it** — among what acting-together makes men share are *attitudes* (the seed of the sixth term; see the companion manual `rm-attitude-as-sixth-term`).

### B.2 Substance abolished as term, retained as function (tension T2)

Burke concedes that "substance" is an abstruse, paradox-ridden term that many modern thinkers "really did abolish … from their terminologies of motives," yet insists they cannot abolish its **function**: "a doctrine of consubstantiality … may be necessary to any way of life" (p.21). `burke-direct` This is the load-bearing move for the bridge: a vocabulary can *perform* the consubstantial function (substantially-one-yet-distinct) while never naming "substance." The homology to Calleja is therefore **functional, not lexical** (Section D).

### B.3 Compensatory to division; the paradox of substance in sacrifice

**Compensatory to division (p.22).** "Identification is affirmed with earnestness precisely because there is division. Identification is compensatory to division." `burke-direct` If men were truly of one substance, "absolute communication would be of man's very essence." Consubstantiation is thus inherently dynamic, partisan, and never static — it is a *response* to prior separateness. Division is its "ironic counterpart" (pp.22–23); rhetoric is "concerned with the state of Babel after the Fall."

**The paradox of substance in sacrifice (RM-07 p.261, 266).** `burke-direct` Insofar as father and son are consubstantial, a fantasy of killing the son is simultaneously self-destruction and self-preservation — "simultaneous losing-and-having." Christian sacrifice (ultimate father, ultimate son, consubstantial) is the perfect paradigm. This extends consubstantiality from *shared standing* (RM-01) into *enacted communal ritual* (the Rattlesnake Club, RM-07: boys made consubstantial by common participation in a solemn experience, the kill being the occasion not the essence).

### B.4 Link to the Grammar's paradox of substance

`burke-direct` (architecture) / `anticipatory-projection` (integration claims). RM-01 explicitly positions the trilogy: the *Grammar* built about **substance** (universal paradoxes, placement); the *Rhetoric* about **identification** (substance applied to persuasion/dissuasion, partisan classification); the projected *Symbolic* about **identity** (unique individuals at peace) (pp.21–23). The consubstantiation apparatus the Grammar pipeline could only *seed* — GM-00 p.xix ("A may become non-A … consubstantial with non-A"), GM-02 antinomy-of-definition type-16 "consubstantial-substance," GM-13 irony peripety (A returns as non-A) — is here given its `burke-direct` definitional ground. **This discharges the Grammar manual's Section-E `pending-future-rhetoric-of-motives-pipeline` flag** (consolidated in Section D and patched in `rm-pentad-extension-patch`).

## Section C — The Social-Motive Apparatus for Multiplayer Behavior

Burke's social-motive apparatus — mystery, hierarchy, courtship, the kill/victimage — is the secondary capture that operationalizes Calleja's **Shared** involvement dimension (IG-06: cohabitation / cooperation / competition micro-phase; guild/society macro-phase). The apparatus is a single interlocking system: hierarchy generates mystery; mystery is the suasive resource of courtship; courtship's tension (and hierarchy's guilt) discharges in the kill. Each entry below is `burke-direct` in its Burke exposition; the Calleja mapping is `anticipatory-projection`.

### C.1 Mystery (RM-03; extended RM-04)

**Burke (burke-direct).** Mystery is the rhetorical condition arising wherever different *kinds* of beings communicate across a structural social divide — it requires both strangeness *and* the possibility of communion (RM-03 p.115). It is not a subjective feeling but an objective structural effect of hierarchic difference; any pronounced social distinction (rank, class, age, race) sets its conditions (RM-03 pos-04). **Mystification** is Marx's dyslogistic name for the same phenomenon read as concealment of class interest; **mystery** is Carlyle's eulogistic name for it read as revelation. Burke refuses to adjudicate, equating mystery with class distinctions beneath both framings (RM-03 p.122, tension-02). RM-04 deepens it: mystery is *intrinsic to the persuasive act as such* — the courtship structure of communication-between-kinds generates mystery as an a priori condition of identification, not merely an ideological mystification to be debunked (RM-04 pos-05).

**Game-behavior (anticipatory-projection).** Maps to Calleja **Shared** involvement: the fascination/deference between players of different "social kinds" — elite vs casual, raider vs leveler, veteran vs newcomer. The "glamour" of high rank (RM-06 — etymologically "gramarye," necromancy) anticipates the hierarchic radiance of prestige gear, exclusive titles, and raided-out players that exceeds their intrinsic skill. Secondary: **Ludic** (opaque drop-rates / matchmaking as Marxist mystification of system-logic) and **Narrative** (Bentham's eulogistic/dyslogistic naming of player choices).

### C.2 Hierarchy (RM-04; RM-05 terministic; RM-07 Order)

**Burke (burke-direct).** Hierarchy is the universal motivational-symbolic principle organizing existence on a scale of higher/lower — inevitable in systematic thought, *reversible at the level of pure principle*, and the generator of courtship, mystery, and mystification (RM-04 pos-01). Key sub-concepts: the **entelechial principle** (treating the culminating/top stage as the representative image of the whole, thereby mystifying particular privilege as universal principle, RM-04 pos-06); **hierarchic psychosis** (the pervasive rank-consciousness produced when the hierarchic principle structures consciousness, RM-04); **competition as out-imitation** (Veblen's "invidious" is a special case of *imitation*, which is itself consubstantiality-through-community-of-ways, RM-04 pos-02); and **homo dialecticus** (man as the classifying animal whose formal aptitude for classification is *prior* to social hierarchy, RM-07 p.283). RM-05 supplies the terministic apparatus: positive → dialectical → **ultimate** vocabulary orders, the ultimate "principle of principles" (god-term) ranking all motives in a developmental hierarchy rather than "parliamentary jangle" (RM-05 pos-02).

**Game-behavior (anticipatory-projection).** Maps to Calleja **Shared**: leaderboards, skill-tiers, gear-score, guild rank as hierarchic-psychosis operationalized; the entelechial pull toward the top-ranked player/guild as the image of the whole. The ultimate win-condition / god-term (RM-05) orders all positive-token optimization (HP, gold, farming) — **Ludic** + **Narrative** (the campaign/season telos as developmental series). Out-imitation competition (RM-04) is the consubstantiality-through-shared-practice beneath copying meta builds.

### C.3 Courtship (RM-06; seeded RM-03/RM-04)

**Burke (burke-direct).** Courtship is the use of suasive devices for *transcending social estrangement* — the rhetoric generated wherever beings of different social kinds communicate across the mystery of hierarchic difference (RM-06 p.208). It is **not primarily sexual but structural**: embarrassment, deference, display, glamour, and calculated withdrawal are its symptoms. Burke's paradigm/caricature pair: **Castiglione's *Courtier*** (symmetrical dialectical paradigm — endowments-as-appeal, "glory" as an addressed motive, martyrdom-as-witness, laughter as fraternal solidarity, ascending to Bembo's Platonic rapture) vs. **Kafka's *Castle*** (grotesque caricature — the access-seeker who never gains access, mystery intensified by its own opacity) (RM-06 P4–P5). The **hazing/initiation** dynamic (informal rites testing outsiders before admission; "mystery trampled" by newcomers who bypass them, RM-06 pp.235–238) and the **reversibility of social and religious reverence** (RM-06 p.233) are the load-bearing social mechanisms. The central tension (T1): courtship *exploits the gap it simultaneously seeks to bridge* — mystery requires maintained distance for its suasive power, yet identification aims at closing it.

**Game-behavior (anticipatory-projection).** The highest-density mapping to Calleja **Shared** involvement:
- **Guild recruitment / initiation** = the hazing dynamic (ambiguous standing before earning full membership; veteran resistance to mass-influx "loss of mystery").
- **Mentor–mentee / ranked play** = the courtier's deference posture + display before an audience of peers; skill performed in witnessed contexts (the "addressed quality").
- **Elite-player allure** = "glamour" / hierarchic radiance.
- **Cross-faction / cross-server drama** = mystery between "different kinds" — neither pure identification nor pure antagonism.
- **Opaque progression (Kafka)** = RNG-gated content, opaque matchmaking, gatekeeping felt as arbitrary; engagement with the opacity itself becomes part of the appeal once players are socialized in.
Secondary: **Tactical** (calculated display/timing) and **Affective** (embarrassment as bodily pre-rhetorical attitude). Perpetual courtship (Kierkegaard, RM-06 p.251) anticipates **completionist** / eternal-return players whose engagement is sustained by the impossibility of final acquisition.

### C.4 The Kill / victimage; Pure persuasion; Ultimate identification (RM-07)

**Burke (burke-direct).** RM-07 completes the apparatus with the structural triad **Order–Secret–Kill**: hierarchy necessarily generates guilt (invidious differentiation breeds envy, resentment, unworthiness in both directions), and guilt seeks discharge in **victimage** — the scapegoating of a sacrificial victim, the **katharma** who is simultaneously sacred and loathsome (RM-07 pp.252–266, 328–329). Crucially, the kill is *not* the essence; **participation in a solemn shared experience** is, the killing being merely the occasion (Rattlesnake Club, p.266). **Pure persuasion** is the limit-concept: appeal pursued for a satisfaction intrinsic to the saying, with **self-interference / "standoffishness"** as its formal hallmark — because "a persuasion that succeeds, dies" (RM-07 pp.267–274). **Ultimate identification** is the apex of the whole arc: identification pushed to its dialectical limit becomes mystical union — dissolution of the individual locus into "boundless being," consubstantiality with the universal order, "the end of all desire" (RM-07 pp.328–333).

**Game-behavior (anticipatory-projection).**
- **Kill/victimage** → **Shared** + **Affective**: ranked hierarchy → invidious distinction → accumulated resentment → scapegoating (toxic-community blaming, "carrying" discourse, hostility to low-performers/out-groups). The Rattlesnake Club (consubstantiality through shared contest, then fracture into cliques) is the precise miniature of competitive-community formation and fragmentation.
- **Pure persuasion** → **Ludic** + **Performative**: the formal archetype of autotelic play / flow; self-interference = difficulty calibration (a game too easy "kills" the appeal; maintained challenge perpetuates the form). Keats's-Urn "single need, forever courted" = the player in flow, perpetually approaching mastery without exhausting it.
- **Ultimate identification** → **Incorporation** at its fullest (the keystone culminating mapping; see Section D and `rm-07-deep`).
- **Elation/accidie** (Hopkins arc) → **Affective**: the emotional grammar of flow-sustained vs flow-break disengagement.

The bridge is therefore consolidated here. See `rm-pentad-extension-patch` §III for the exact delta that flips the Grammar flag to `"consolidated-via-RM-01"`.

### D.1 What consubstantiation means for Burke (burke-direct)

To identify A with B is to make A "consubstantial" with B (p.20): A is "substantially one" with a person other than himself yet "remains unique, an individual locus of motives" — "both joined and separate, at once a distinct substance and consubstantial with another" (p.21). Its ground is action ("substance … was an act; a way of life is an acting-together," p.21). Consubstantiality is therefore (i) substance-theoretic, (ii) act-based, (iii) compensatory to division (p.22), and (iv) *the very thing identification accomplishes*. Its **function survives even where the term is abolished** (p.21) — so it can be present implicitly in a vocabulary (such as Calleja's) that never names "substance."

### D.2 The homology — three structural alignments (anticipatory-projection)

Calleja's **incorporation** (IG-10, canonical definition book p.169) is, paraphrased, the absorption of a virtual environment into consciousness (yielding a sense of habitation) as supported by the systemically upheld embodiment of the player in a single avatar-represented location. It is a **dual-axis** construct with a **simultaneity condition** (per the incorporation rubric: R1 assimilation axis, R2 embodiment axis, R3 simultaneity, R4 spatial+kinesthetic cornerstone, R5 blending). The mapping to consubstantiality is structurally exact:

| Burke (consubstantiation, burke-direct text) | Calleja (incorporation, IG-10) | Alignment |
|---|---|---|
| **"A is substantially one with" the other** (p.21) | **Axis 1 / R1 — assimilation**: environment absorbed INTO consciousness | The gameworld becomes part of the player's own structure of motives, as the other becomes substantially one with A. |
| **"yet he remains unique, an individual locus of motives … both joined and separate"** (p.21) | **Axis 2 / R2 — embodiment**: player absorbed INTO the environment via the avatar, a single systemically-upheld location | The **avatar is precisely the distinct locus** through which the player is joined to the world while remaining a distinct substance. |
| **both-joined-AND-separate held simultaneously** (p.21) | **R3 — simultaneity condition**: both axes must obtain concurrently or the state reduces to mere involvement | Consubstantial union does **not** dissolve distinctness: one in act yet two in being — the same paradox-of-substance simultaneity Calleja's double-axis independently reconstructs. |
| **acting-together makes men consubstantial** (p.21) | **R4/R5 — cornerstone + blending**: spatial+kinesthetic internalized, multiple dimensions blended | Consubstantiality is co-*action*; incorporation requires the internalized spatial+kinesthetic *acting-in* the world as its cornerstone. |
| **ultimate identification — dissolution into boundless being** (RM-07 p.333) | **Incorporation at its fullest** (peak state) | The limit-case: the individual locus dissolved into the gameworld's universal order; the composite mystic portrait (RM-07 pp.329–331) is phenomenologically isomorphic with Calleja's deepest incorporation. |

**The homology is functional, not lexical.** Calleja never uses "substance"; Burke never uses "embodiment." The two arrive at the *identical dynamic* — a being made substantially one with something other than itself while remaining a distinct locus — from incommensurable starting points (Burke: substance-metaphysics + symbol-action; Calleja: experientialist embodied cognition, Lakoff & Johnson 2003 / Damasio 2000 / Dennett 1991). This functional-not-lexical character is exactly what Burke's tension T2 ("substance abolished as term, retained as function," §B.2) licenses.

### D.3 The five divergences (anticipatory-projection)

The bridge is a homology, **not an equation**. The integration must hold five differences in view:

1. **Medium of union.** Burke = rhetorical/symbolic identification through shared *substance* — property, interests, ideology, terministic "Belonging" (p.28). Calleja = phenomenological *absorption* through six involvement dimensions (kinesthetic, spatial, shared, narrative, affective, ludic), with the **spatial+kinesthetic cornerstone** (R4).
2. **Ontological status.** Burke's consubstantiality is **ambivalently real-or-persuaded** (one can be "persuaded to believe" interests are joined, p.20) and can be strategic/cunning ("by default," "profitable malingering"). Calleja's incorporation is a **sustained-but-fleeting experiential state** that either obtains phenomenologically or slips back to involvement — not a persuaded belief. (This is RM-01 tension T1 deployed: is player-avatar/guild consubstantiality a real phenomenological absorption or a persuaded/symbolic belonging? The divergence keeps both in view.)
3. **Grounding theory.** Burke grounds union in act/substance-metaphysics and a "realism of the act" (p.43); Calleja grounds it in experientialist gestalts and consciousness-as-internally-generated-construct — and, notably, **not** in Merleau-Ponty or Heidegger (those are *absent* from IG-10; only one peripheral Heidegger cite in the whole volume, at book p.21).
4. **Directionality.** Calleja insists on **bidirectionality** as a corrective to the "unidirectional plunge" metaphor; Burke's consubstantiality is **symmetrical-in-structure but asymmetrically-deployed** — the rhetorician engineers A's identification with B, often across *unequal* status (p.48; the whole courtship apparatus, Section C), closer to a persuasion-directed vector than to a mutual double-axis.
5. **Scope.** Burke's identification is **society-wide**, includes **self-address** ("an I addressing its me," p.38), and operates **unconsciously / by default**; Calleja's incorporation is **bounded to ergodic media** that acknowledge the player's presence and agency (precludes non-ergodic media; Tetris-style absorption fails the R4 cornerstone — no habitable space, no avatar-located embodiment).

### D.4 How identification grounds player identification AND Attitude (anticipatory-projection)

The same mechanism — "substantially one yet a distinct locus," accomplished through acting-together — is the symbolic ground beneath the player's nested identifications:
- **Avatar / character.** The avatar is the systemically-upheld locus through which the player is substantially one with the gameworld while remaining a distinct locus of motives (Calleja embodiment-axis R2; PIM **Shared** + **Kinesthetic**).
- **Guild / faction / playerbase.** Burke's "acting-together" and "'Belonging' is rhetorical" (pp.21, 28) supply the substance-theoretic *why* beneath Calleja's descriptive *what* of co-presence and group belonging (IG-06 **Shared**: cohabitation/cooperation/competition micro; guild/society macro). RM-07's Rattlesnake Club grounds the *enacted-ritual* register of this belonging.
- **Attitude (the hinge).** Because consubstantiality is itself partly constituted by shared *attitudes* (p.21), and because "attitude is an incipient act" (p.42), identification is the ground of **Attitude**: a player's disposition/stance toward avatar, guild, and gameworld is an *incipient act of identification*. This simultaneously grounds the **Affective** involvement dimension and the pentad's sixth (Attitude) term. (Fully developed in the companion manual `rm-attitude-as-sixth-term`.)

The bridge therefore does **double duty**: it links consubstantiation to incorporation at the *substance/embodiment* level (D.2) and to Shared involvement at the *social-belonging* level (D.4), while seeding Attitude as the dispositional hinge between them.
