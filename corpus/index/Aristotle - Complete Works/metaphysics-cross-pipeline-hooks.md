## 1. Heidegger BCAP (GA 18) — Basic Concepts of Aristotelian Philosophy

### 1.1 οὐσία as Anwesenheit (Presence)
[INTERP-high]

BCAP's central interpretive move is reading οὐσία not as "substance" (the scholastic-Latinate rendering) but as *Anwesenheit* — presence, the being-present of what is. Metaphysics Gamma.2's focal-meaning (*pros hen*) doctrine — all senses of "being" refer to substance — is the primary textual basis for Heidegger's claim that Greek ontology is organized around a single understanding of being as *Vorhandenheit* (presence-at-hand).

- **Met. Gamma.2** (1003a33-b19) — the *pros hen* structure → BCAP's analysis of the unity of being through *ousia*
- **Met. Delta.8** (1017b10-26) — substance as "the separable this" → BCAP's reading of *ousia* as what shows itself in its own standing presence
- **Met. Z.1** (1028a10-b7) — substance primary in formula, knowledge, time → BCAP's thesis that *ousia* governs the Greek understanding of temporality

### 1.2 κίνησις as Bewegtheit (Movedness)
[INTERP-high]

BCAP SS25-28 devotes extensive analysis to *kinesis* as the fundamental character of being for Aristotle. Lambda's argument that eternal motion requires an actual (not merely potential) mover is the culmination Heidegger reads as revealing the Greek ontological horizon.

- **Met. Lambda.6** (1071b3-1072a18) — eternal motion requires pure actuality → BCAP's argument that the Greek understanding of *kinesis* is oriented by *energeia*
- **Met. Kappa.9** (1065b5-1066a34) — motion as "the actuality of the potential *qua* potential" → BCAP's phenomenological unpacking of what it means for potential being to be "at work" while remaining potential
- **Met. Theta.6** (1048b18-35) — the kinesis/energeia distinction → BCAP's distinction between *Bewegung* (process) and *Bewegtheit* (the being-moved character)

### 1.3 ἐνέργεια / δύναμις as the Ontological Pair
[INTERP-high]

Heidegger in BCAP deconstructs the translation of ἐνέργεια as *Wirklichkeit* (mere "effectiveness") and argues for recovering its original sense as "being-at-work." Lambda 1071b12-22 is the decisive passage: the unmoved mover's substance *is* pure energeia.

- **Met. Lambda.6-7** (1071b12-1072b4) — the unmoved mover as pure energeia → BCAP's thesis that Greek ontology culminates in the identification of the highest being with *Energeia*
- **Met. Theta.8** (1049b3-1051a2) — priority of actuality over potentiality → BCAP's reading of the Greek privileging of what is "finished" and "present" over what is still becoming
- **Met. Theta.6** (1048a25-b9) — actuality defined by analogy → BCAP's claim that *energeia* resists formal definition because it names the most originary phenomenon
- **Met. Theta.2** (1046a36-b28) — rational potentialities and *logos* → BCAP's analysis of how *techne* (productive art) reveals the being-structure of *dynamis*

### 1.4 εἶδος / ὕλη and the Priority of Form
[INTERP-high]

Heidegger's BCAP reading of *ousia* as oriented by production (*Herstellung*) draws directly on Z.vii-ix's analysis of generation as the paradigm for understanding substance. The form is "in the soul of the maker" (1032b1); this is the textual basis for Heidegger's claim that Greek ontology privileges the *Hergestelltsein* (produced-ness) of beings.

- **Met. Z.vii** (1032a-1033b) — the three kinds of generation; form in the soul of the maker → BCAP's *Herstellung* thesis
- **Met. Z.viii** (1033b-1034a) — form is not generated; Platonic Forms unnecessary → BCAP's point that the *eidos* is always already there, governing production
- **Met. Z.xvii** (1041a-b) — substance as formal cause → BCAP's culminating claim that *ousia* = *eidos* = the "look" (*Aussehen*) of a thing

## 2. Heidegger Being and Time (B&T)

### 2.1 The Seinsfrage and Being qua Being
[INTERP-high]

In B&T Introduction (SS1, H.1-2), Heidegger explicitly names the forgetting of the question of being (*Seinsfrage*) as the impetus for his project. The Aristotelian formulation "being qua being" (*to on hei on*) is the antecedent.

- **Met. Gamma.1** (1003a21-32) — being qua being as the subject of first philosophy → B&T SS1-2: Heidegger takes up this question but argues the tradition forgot it by reducing being to beings (the "ontic-ontological difference")
- **Met. Gamma.2** (1003a33-b19) — the focal-meaning structure → B&T's critique of the "analogical" unity of being as insufficient; Heidegger seeks a more originary unity

### 2.2 Substance (Vorhandenheit) as the Dominant Mode
[INTERP-high]

B&T's critique of *Vorhandenheit* (presence-at-hand) as the dominant mode of being in the tradition implicitly targets the Aristotelian identification of substance with form-as-actuality (*eidos*/*energeia*).

- **Met. Z.xiii** (1038b-1039a) — substance is always a "this" (*tode ti*), not a "such" → B&T's insistence on *Jemeinigkeit* (mineness): Dasein is always *mine*, never a generic universal
- **Met. Z.xvii** (1041a-b) — substance as form/cause → B&T SS15-18: the "worldhood" of the world cannot be grasped through the substance/attribute framework; equipment (*Zuhandenheit*) is ontologically prior to *Vorhandenheit*
- **Met. Delta.8** (1017b10-26) — substance as the ultimate substratum or separable "this" → B&T SS19-21: the *res extensa* tradition reduces beings to present-at-hand things

### 2.3 νόησις νοήσεως νόησις and Erschlossenheit
[INTERP-high]

The self-referential structure of divine thought (Lambda.9) — thought thinking itself — is a key point of contact for Heidegger's analysis of Dasein's self-disclosedness (*Erschlossenheit*) in B&T SS29-34. Heidegger breaks with the theistic-contemplative framing while preserving the structural insight that the highest activity is self-referential.

- **Met. Lambda.9** (1074b15-1075a10) — noesis noeseos noesis → B&T SS29-34: Dasein's disclosedness (*Befindlichkeit*, *Verstehen*, *Rede*) as the existential analogue; but finitude, not divine self-sufficiency, is primary
- **Met. Theta.10** (1051b17-1052a4) — truth as contact (*thigein*) for incomposites → B&T SS44: *aletheia* as uncoveredness (*Unverborgenheit*); Heidegger reads the Theta.10 doctrine as evidence that the Greeks had a pre-propositional concept of truth

### 2.4 ἀλήθεια — Truth as Unconcealedness
[INTERP-high]

Heidegger's contested reading of Greek *aletheia* as "unconcealedness" (*Unverborgenheit*) rather than propositional correspondence draws on the Metaphysics.

- **Met. Gamma.7** (1011b25) — "to say of what is that it is, is true" → B&T SS44: Heidegger acknowledges this "correspondence" formulation but argues a more originary phenomenon (uncoveredness) underlies it
- **Met. Theta.10** (1051a34-1052a4) — being-as-truth for composites and incomposites → B&T SS44: the Theta.10 passage on incomposites is Heidegger's key evidence for a non-propositional Greek concept of truth

## 3. Uexkull, *Foray into the Worlds of Animals and Humans* (Streifzuge)

### 3.1 εἶδος / ὕλη and Bauplan
[INTERP-high]

Uexkull's *Bauplan* (body plan / structural plan) is the biological analogue of the Aristotelian *eidos*-*hyle* distinction. The Bauplan determines the functional circle (*Funktionskreis*) of each organism, just as Aristotelian form determines what a composite substance *is*.

- **Met. Z.vii-ix** (1032a-1034b) — form pre-exists in actuality; generation is form actualized in matter → Uexkull's Bauplan as the pre-given structural plan that determines the organism's Umwelt
- **Met. Z.xii** (1037b-1038a) — genus as matter, differentia as form → Uexkull's taxonomic principle: the Bauplan differentiates organisms within a genus by their specific functional circles
- **Met. Eta.6** (1045b7-23) — matter and form as "one and the same, the one potentially, the other actually" → Uexkull's thesis that the organism and its environment are structurally unified; the body (matter) and the Bauplan (form) are not separate but co-constitutive

### 3.2 ἐνέργεια / δύναμις and the Functional Circle
[INTERP-high]

The Theta potentiality/actuality framework maps onto Uexkull's account of how organisms actualize their capacities within species-specific environments.

- **Met. Theta.8** (1049b3-1051a2) — actuality prior to potentiality; potentiality for the sake of actuality → Uexkull: the organism's perceptual and effector capacities (*Merkwelt* / *Wirkwelt*) exist for the sake of their actualization in the functional circle
- **Met. Theta.7** (1048b37-1049b2) — when is something potentially X? Only when it has its own motive principle → Uexkull: an organism is "potentially" responsive to certain environmental features only given its specific Bauplan

### 3.3 τέλος and Planmassigkeit
[INTERP-high]

Uexkull's concept of *Planmassigkeit* (plan-conformity) is the biosemiotic analogue of Aristotelian teleology.

- **Met. Lambda.10** (1075a11-1076a4) — the universe's good is both immanent (as order) and transcendent (as a separate principle) → Uexkull: nature exhibits plan-conformity (*Planmassigkeit*) without requiring a conscious designer; the order is immanent in the Bauplan structure of each organism

## 4. Rickert, *Ambient Rhetoric*

### 4.1 ὕλη and the Rehabilitation of Materiality
[INTERP-high]

Rickert's ambient rhetoric challenges the priority of form over matter that the Metaphysics establishes. Zeta's subordination of matter — "matter is unknowable in itself" (Z.x, 1036a9) — is precisely what ambient rhetoric resists.

- **Met. Z.x** (1036a8-9) — "matter is unknowable in itself" → Rickert's counter-thesis: materiality (chora, ambiance, environment) is not merely passive substrate but an active condition of rhetorical experience
- **Met. Z.iii** (1029a10-24) — if all predicates are stripped away, nothing remains but matter → Rickert's claim that the stripping-away itself reveals something: the ambient, the pre-formal, the environmental ground
- **Met. Z.xvii** (1041a-b) — substance as formal cause → Rickert: ambient rhetoric works precisely in the register that formal causation cannot capture — atmosphere, mood, material arrangement

### 4.2 PNC and the Conditions of Intelligibility
[INTERP-high]

The elenctic defence of PNC (Gamma.4) has a rhetorical dimension: anyone who speaks meaningfully is already within the space PNC governs. This resonates with Rickert's notion that rhetoric is not merely persuasion but a condition of intelligibility.

- **Met. Gamma.4** (1006a11-1009a5) — elenctic argument: meaningful speech presupposes PNC → Rickert: rhetoric operates at the level of ambient conditions that make discourse possible, not just at the level of explicit argumentation
- **Met. Gamma.2** (1004b17-26) — philosophy vs. dialectic vs. sophistic → Rickert: ambient rhetoric blurs these boundaries; the "ambient" is the ground that all three share

### 4.3 ἐνέργεια and Rhetorical Efficacy
[INTERP-high]

The actuality concept connects to Rhetoric III.11's "setting before the eyes" (*pro ommaton poiein*), where metaphor's power lies in presenting things "as actual" (*energounta*). The Metaphysics provides the ontological ground for this rhetorical doctrine.

- **Met. Theta.6** (1048a25-b9) — actuality defined by analogy → Rickert/Rhetoric III.11: rhetorical *energeia* makes the audience "see" things as at work, as present, as actual; the connection between ontological actuality and rhetorical vividness
- **Met. Theta.8** (1050a4-b6) — actuality as *telos*; matter for the sake of form → Rickert's inversion: ambient rhetoric shows that the material/environmental dimension is not merely "for the sake of" formal meaning but co-constitutive of it
