# Cross-Pipeline Hooks — Sense and Sensibilia

**Source**: Phase 3 ontology of *Sense and Sensibilia* (SS-01 + SS-02).
**Date**: 2026-03-09

---

## 1. αἴσθησις → Uexküll Merkwelt [INTERP-high]

**Aristotelian concept**: αἴσθησις (C3) — sensation as a joint function of soul and body, mediated by external elements; the senses are not deceived about their proper objects (τὰ ἴδια αἰσθητά).

**Uexküll connection**: The Aristotelian doctrine that each sense has its proper object (colour to sight, sound to hearing) and that the common sensibles (figure, magnitude, motion, number) are shared across modalities maps structurally onto Uexkull's *Merkwelt* — the perceptual world constituted by the animal's specific sensory capacities. The treatise's insistence that "all animals possessing locomotion" have the distance senses (437a8-14) anticipates the Uexkullian thesis that the perceptual world is correlated with the organism's motor capacities.

**Key passages**: 436a1-b8 (sensation as soul-body conjunction), 437a3-b10 (hierarchy of senses correlated with animal capacities), 442b2-10 (senses not deceived about proper objects).

**Integration priority**: HIGH — the proper/common sensibles distinction provides the Aristotelian vocabulary for discussing species-specific perceptual worlds.

---

## 2. τὸ διαφανές as μεταξύ → B&T world-disclosure [INTERP-high]

**Aristotelian concept**: τὸ διαφανές (C1) — the transparent as medium (μεταξύ) between perceiver and perceived; a "common nature and power" not peculiar to any element.

**B&T connection**: The transparent is Aristotle's most developed account of a perceptual *medium* — something that is not itself an object of perception but the condition for the appearance of objects (colour). This structural role anticipates Heidegger's analysis of worldhood as that which is not itself an entity but the condition for entities showing up. The transparent is "not peculiar to air or water" (439a25-b5) but a shared nature subsisting in all bodies — a claim about the ontological status of the medium that resonates with the non-ontic character of Dasein's world.

**Key passages**: 439a18-b14 (definition of the transparent), 438b25-30 (without light, vision impossible — the medium must be actualized).

**Integration priority**: HIGH — the μεταξύ doctrine is central to any argument about the ambient, environmental character of perception in Aristotle.

---

## 3. κοινὴ αἴσθησις → BCAP on φαντασία [INTERP-high]

**Aristotelian concept**: κοινὴ αἴσθησις (C4) — the common sense faculty, numerically one but specifically different for each sense-province; perceives time, magnitude, and motion; the faculty through which simultaneous cross-modal perception occurs.

**BCAP connection**: Heidegger in GA 18 treats φαντασία as the faculty of "making-present-to-itself" and connects it to the common sense's synthetic function. The Sense and Sensibilia account of the common sense as "numerically one but different in being" (449a5-20) provides the Aristotelian foundation for the φαντασία-as-synthesis reading: if the common sense unifies diverse sensory inputs, φαντασία operates on these unified presentations.

**Key passages**: 448a10-449a20 (simultaneous perception and unity of the faculty), 449a5-20 (the faculty is *qua* one indivisible in actualization but different in being).

**Integration priority**: HIGH — bridges the De Sensu common-sense doctrine to the DA III.3/BCAP φαντασία account.

---

## 4. δύναμις/ἐνέργεια in perception → BCAP on δύναμις [INTERP-high]

**Aristotelian concept**: δύναμις/ἐνέργεια (C6) — potentiality/actuality applied to perceptibility; sensible qualities exist potentially below the threshold of perception and become actual only at sufficient scale.

**BCAP connection**: Heidegger in GA 18 reinterprets δύναμις not as modal-logical "possibility" but as the being-character of being-there-already as available-for. The De Sensu application of δύναμις/ἐνέργεια to perception — where a grain of millet has colour *potentially* but is not *actually* visible — concretizes this: the perceptual world is constituted by the threshold between potential and actual, and this threshold is not merely epistemological but ontological.

**Key passages**: 446a20-b20 (resolution of infinite-divisibility via potential/actual), 449a20-b5 (perceptual threshold as indivisible boundary).

**Integration priority**: MEDIUM — supports the BCAP reading but requires mediation through the DA perceptual theory.

---

## 5. χρῶμα / μῖξις → On Colours colour theory [INTERP-low]

**Aristotelian concept**: χρῶμα (C2) as the limit of the transparent; μῖξις (C5) as genuine blending vs. juxtaposition.

**On Colours connection**: The De Coloribus colour theory (COL-01) simplifies the De Sensu account by deriving colours from elements rather than from the transparent. The De Sensu theory is more ontologically sophisticated (colour as the limit of the transparent in determinate bodies), while De Coloribus is more empirically detailed (maturation, dyeing, animal colouring). A complete Aristotelian/Peripatetic colour theory would integrate both.

**Key tension**: De Sensu has the transparent; De Coloribus lacks it entirely. Whether this represents different developmental stages, different levels of exposition, or different authors remains debated.

**Integration priority**: LOW — internal to the Aristotelian corpus; does not directly connect to Heidegger, Rickert, or Uexkull.

## 6. χυμός / ὀσμή → Sense-media and environmental perception [INTERP-med]

**Aristotelian concept**: χυμός (I2) as savour generated by the sapid dry in the moist; ὀσμή (I3) as odour operating through air and water.

**Cross-pipeline relevance**: The savour and odour theories complete the sense-media doctrine by showing that taste and smell, like sight, require a material medium (the moist, air/water) for their operation. The second species of odour — pleasant-in-themselves, peculiar to humans due to the coldness of the brain — introduces a distinctively human perceptual capacity not tied to nutrition. This has implications for Uexkull's Merkwelt theory (species-specific perceptual worlds) and for any account of human perception as exceeding mere biological utility.

**Key passages**: 441b15-22 (savour as sapid dry in moist), 443b20-444b1 (two species of odour), 445a5-15 (smell as middle position between tactual and distance senses).

**Integration priority**: MEDIUM — relevant to the environmental-perceptual argument but not directly tied to a specific external pipeline.
