## Section 3C: Tension Layer

Tension nodes are concept-pairs that bear *both* a dependency-type edge (depends_on, presupposes, supports, explains) *and* a conflict-type edge (contrasts_with, undermines) between them, either directly or through a one-hop mediator. These productive tensions define the dynamic structure of GA 18.

### Tension 1: ἐνέργεια -- ἐντελέχεια

- **Dependency**: κίνησις is defined *as* ἐντελέχεια τοῦ δυνάμει ὄντος, and the τέλειον analysis grounds ἐντελέχεια (FP2b, SP2a).
- **Conflict**: ἐνέργεια and ἐντελέχεια are explicitly distinguished as two modes of presence -- ἐνέργεια = becoming-completed vs. ἐντελέχεια = completedness -- yet the definition of κίνησις uses "ἐντελέχεια" where ἐνέργεια seems to be meant (SP2a-T1).
- **Productive character**: This tension is constitutive of the concept of κίνησις itself. Movement *is* the presence (ἐντελέχεια) of what is able to be there precisely insofar as it is not yet completed (ἐνέργεια). The equivocation is not a defect but an ontological necessity: κίνησις names the coincidence of presence and incompleteness.

### Tension 2: δύναμις (already-there) -- δύναμις (not-yet)

- **Dependency**: δύναμις enters the definition of κίνησις as the being-character of the δυνάμει ὄν (SP2a, SP2b).
- **Conflict**: Heidegger insists δύναμις is not "not yet actual" but *already there* as availability/meaningfulness (the tree trunk *is* already available for shipbuilding), yet the definition requires the δυνάμει ὄν to be "not yet" completed (SP2a-T2).
- **Productive character**: The tension reveals that meaningfulness (Bedeutsamkeit) and potentiality are two faces of the same phenomenon. The world is encountered primarily as meaningful-available-for, and it is precisely this availability that constitutes the "from...toward..." structure of movement.

### Tension 3: λόγος as disclosure -- λόγος as concealment

- **Dependency**: λόγος is the fundamental determination of human being-there and the bearer/mastery of interpretedness (FP1a, FP1b, SP1).
- **Conflict**: λόγος equally enables idle chatter (Gerede), dissimulation, and the dominance of the false (ψεῦδος); dominant λόγοι can render beings inaccessible (SP1-P6, SP1-P7).
- **Productive character**: Conceptuality has a "double sense" precisely because the same λόγος that discloses also conceals. The fore-structures (Vorhabe/Vorsicht/Vorgriff) are simultaneously what must be overcome and what must be explicitly appropriated. This tension drives the entire Second Part's program of "radical grasping of the interpretedness of being-there."

### Tension 4: δόξα as everydayness -- δόξα as ground of ἐπιστήμη

- **Dependency**: δόξα is the genuine discoveredness of being-with-one-another and simultaneously the *basis* of theoretical comportment including ἐπιστήμη (FP3b-P6).
- **Conflict**: δόξα is characterized by revisability, stubbornness, and the anonymity of "one has the view" (das Man), contrasting with the secure knowing of ἐπιστήμη which is directed toward the ἀεί (FP3b-P3).
- **Productive character**: Theoretical science arises from and against the background of δόξα. The same structure that makes everyday opinion stubborn and anonymous also makes the transition to science possible -- science is the transformation, not the abolition, of δόξα.

### Tension 5: πάθη as ground of λόγος -- λόγος as fundamental determination

- **Dependency**: The πάθη are "the ground out of which speaking arises, and which what is expressed grows back into" (FP3e-P8).
- **Conflict**: λόγος is the fundamental determination of the being of the human being (FP1a-P4, FP1b), making λόγος appear more fundamental than the πάθη.
- **Productive character**: Neither λόγος nor πάθη is simply "more fundamental." They are equiprimordial (gleichursprünglich) -- a co-originarity that anticipates BT's analysis of Rede, Befindlichkeit, and Verstehen as equiprimordial existentialia. The rhetoric analysis (Ch. 3) demonstrates that speaking always arises from disposition and disposition always articulates itself in speaking.

### Tension 6: ἀρετή as μεσότης -- ἀρετή as ἀκρότης

- **Dependency**: the οὐσία of ἀρετή = μεσότης; the mean πρὸς ἡμᾶς (FP3c).
- **Conflict**: "taken ontologically, the ἀρετή μεσότης is ἀκρότης with regard to the εὖ" -- the mean is the highest (FP3c-P7).
- **Productive character**: The tension dissolves when μεσότης is understood as a way of being-composed (the "how") rather than a quantitative midpoint. The mean is the extreme because it is the mode of being most genuinely related to the concrete situation (καιρός). This reveals that Aristotelian ethics is not a "bourgeois morality" of moderation but an ontology of situated existence.

### Tension 7: φόβος as disposition vs. φοβερά as world-side structure

- **Dependency**: fear requires both the dispositional ground (οἴεσθαι) and the world-side encounter characters (σημεῖα, πλησιασμός) (FP3e-P2, P5).
- **Conflict**: "there is *no parallel* between the frightening and being-afraid" (FP3e-P3) -- the two sides cannot be mapped onto each other.
- **Productive character**: This non-parallelism is the master principle of the phenomenology of πάθη. It shows that dispositions are not reactions to pre-given objects but disclosive modes of being-in-the-world. The gap between φοβερά and φόβος is what makes fear a *finding-oneself* rather than a causal response.

## Section 3D: Contested / Unstable Nodes

Contested nodes are concepts whose meaning shifts significantly across the course trajectory.

### D1: δύναμις

- **U-Intro**: methodological sense -- τρόπος τῆς δυνάμεως distinguishing philosophy from dialectic (Met. Γ2).
- **U-FP3b, FP3c**: one of three γινόμενα in the soul alongside πάθη and ἕξεις; "co-given possibilities of being."
- **U-SP2a**: ontological sense -- NOT modal possibility but the being-character of meaningfulness (Bedeutsamkeit); a being that is δυνάμει is already there as available-for.
- **U-SP2b**: enters the genuine definition of κίνησις through ποιητικόν/παθητικόν.
- **Trajectory**: From methodological criterion to psychic capacity to fundamental ontological determination. The richest transformation of any concept in the course.

### D2: πέρας

- **U-Intro, FP1a**: standard -- limit, boundary; ὁρισμός as circumscription by πέρας.
- **U-FP2b**: existential -- "not a spatial boundary but the limit that constitutes the genuine there of a being"; connected to τέλος as ἔσχατον.
- **U-FP3e**: cosmological/ontological -- "a being-aspect in beings; πέρας is its site, its place, its being-produced, being-in-its-place"; site (τόπος) as positive determination of being.
- **U-SP2a, SP2b**: connected to κίνησις -- movement does not remain in its πέρας; κίνησις as ἀτελής is precisely the not-yet-at-the-limit.
- **Trajectory**: From definitional circumscription to existential there-determination to cosmological site-structure to the not-yet-at-the-limit of κίνησις.

### D3: ψυχή

- **U-FP1b, FP2a**: the being of the human being as ζῷον λόγον ἔχον; ψυχή as being-in-the-world.
- **U-FP2b**: ψυχή as πρακτικὴ ἐνέργεια; "psychology has nothing to do with consciousness."
- **U-FP3c, FP3d**: De Anima A1 -- the entire human being (not just consciousness) as primary object; πάθη are μετὰ σώματος; ψυχή as λόγοι ἔνυλοι.
- **U-SP2b**: νοῦς as highest possibility of ψυχή, now read through the πρός τι structure of κίνησις.
- **Trajectory**: From practical-existential determination through psychosomatic unity to the ontological register of κίνησις. The de-psychologization of ψυχή is progressive throughout the course.

### D4: ἕξις

- **U-FP3b**: manner/mode of being in a πάθος; "how we carry ourselves."
- **U-FP3c**: "the determination of the genuineness of being-there in a moment of being-composed as to something"; four senses of ἔχειν; ἕξις as ἐνέργεια of having; oriented toward καιρός.
- **U-SP1**: conceptuality itself characterized as ἕξις πρὸς ἄλλον -- "against" something from which it extricates itself.
- **Trajectory**: From dispositional mode to existential composure to the structure of conceptuality itself. In the Second Part, ἕξις becomes the formal structure of the relation between interpretedness and genuine knowing.

### D5: ἐνέργεια

- **U-FP2a, FP2b**: being-at-work; ψυχῆς ἐνέργεια κατ' ἀρετήν = the formula for the human good.
- **U-SP2a**: distinguished from ἐντελέχεια: ἐνέργεια = being-grasped-in-becoming-completed; kinesis is ἐνέργεια, not ἐντελέχεια.
- **U-SP2b**: κίνησις = ἀτελὴς ἐνέργεια; the genuine definition through ποιητικόν/παθητικόν.
- **Trajectory**: From practical (ethical-existential) to cosmological-ontological. The Second Part radicalizes ἐνέργεια by showing that it names the being of beings in movement -- not just the being-at-work of human life but the fundamental character of worldly presence.

## Section 3E: Course Trajectory Narrative

The trajectory of GA 18 moves through three phases that constitute a single arc: from the existential-rhetorical ground of conceptuality, through the hermeneutic pivot of the fore-structures, to the ontological payoff in the analysis of κίνησις.

**Phase 1 (§§1--12, Introduction through FP Ch. 2): The Ground of Conceptuality.** Heidegger opens with the programmatic demand to recover the "ground" from which Aristotle's basic concepts arise (§§1-2) and immediately identifies that ground as the being-there (Dasein) of human beings, not as an abstract theoretical subject but as the concrete being that "is in a world" and "has its world" through λόγος (§§3-8). The analysis of definition (ὁρισμός) as λόγος οὐσίας establishes that conceptuality is rooted in speech, not in formal logic. The movement from ζῷον λόγον ἔχον (§9) through the ἀγαθόν investigation (§10) to the τέλειον analysis (§11) and the formula ψυχῆς ἐνέργεια κατ' ἀρετήν (§12) progressively secures the existential coordinates: being-there is being-in-the-world characterized by λόγος, oriented toward the ἀγαθόν as τέλος, and disclosed in its completedness through ἐντελέχεια. This phase establishes the five core concepts -- λόγος, οὐσία, ψυχή, ἐνέργεια, and πρᾶξις -- that will be transformed but not abandoned in what follows.

**Phase 2 (§§13--22, FP Ch. 3): The Rhetorical-Affective Deepening.** The turn to rhetoric in §13 is the decisive architectonic move of the course. By treating the *Rhetoric* as "a hermeneutic of being-there itself" (§14), Heidegger shows that the basic concepts established in Phase 1 are not theoretical abstractions but are rooted in the πάθη -- the dispositions in which being-there finds itself. The five units of Chapter 3 (FP3a-e) carry out an increasingly concrete analysis: from πίστις and the threefold structure of rhetorical speech (§14), through the fourfold demarcation of δόξα and the das-Man structure of public opinion (§15), to ἕξις and ἀρετή as μεσότης oriented toward the καιρός (§17), the four general meanings of πάθος from Met. Δ21 (§18), the φυσικός's methodology and ἡδονή/λύπη as fundamental determinations (§§19-20), and the culminating analysis of φόβος as the πάθος that discloses the futural structure of being-there (§21). The chapter's macro-conclusion -- that the πάθη are "the ground out of which speaking arises" and "the basic possibilities in which being-there itself is primarily oriented toward itself" -- completes the circuit between λόγος and πάθη. The supplements on ἀληθεύειν (§22a) and nature as world (§22b) prepare the transition to the Second Part by showing that truth-disclosing is an ἕξις and that nature is the always-already-being-there of the world.

**Phase 3 (§§23--28, Second Part): The Ontological Payoff.** The Second Part executes the "radical grasping of the interpretedness of being-there" through the concept of κίνησις. The pivot (§§23-24) introduces the double sense of conceptuality (negative: fore-structures as interpretedness to be overcome; positive: νοῦς as διανοεῖσθαι) and identifies the as-structure (κατά/ἀπό, σύνθεσις/διαίρεσις) as the ground of both error and truth. The Physics analysis (§§25-28) then demonstrates that κίνησις is not a regional phenomenon of natural science but the fundamental mode of being of worldly beings, defined first as ἐντελέχεια τοῦ δυνάμει ὄντος (§26) and then, through the critique of earlier philosophers and the analysis of ἀτελής ἐνέργεια (§27), as ἐνέργεια τοῦ δυνάμει ποιητικοῦ καὶ παθητικοῦ (§28). The final thesis -- that Greek ontology derives not from nature but from ποίησις and πρᾶξις as primary modes of being-in-the-world -- retroactively illuminates the entire course: the First Part's analysis of practical-rhetorical being-there was not a detour but the necessary preparation for understanding that being itself, for the Greeks, was apprehended from the horizon of making and acting. The concepts ἐνέργεια, δύναμις, and στέρησις, which emerged tentatively in the First Part, are now revealed as the fundamental ontological determinations that articulate the being of movement, which is the being of beings as such.
