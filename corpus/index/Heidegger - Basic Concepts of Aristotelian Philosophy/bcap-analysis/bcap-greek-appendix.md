# Section 3F: Greek Appendix for GA 18

**Source**: Martin Heidegger, *Basic Concepts of Aristotelian Philosophy* (GA 18), trans. Metcalf & Tanzer (2009).
**Date**: 2026-03-08

This appendix aggregates all Greek terms encountered across the 13 Phase 2 unit analyses into canonical lemma entries. Terms are sorted by frequency of occurrence (number of units).

## Tier 1: Terms Appearing in 7+ Units

### 1. λόγος (logos)
- **Greek Unicode**: λόγος
- **Forms encountered**: λόγος, λόγοι, λέγειν, λόγον ἔχον, λόγον ἔχειν
- **Conventional translations**: speech, account, discourse, reason, definition
- **Heidegger's usage**: "Bringing a matter to sight" (ἀποφαίνεσθαι); the fundamental determination of human being-there; bearer and mastery of interpretedness; every λόγος is σύνθεσις or διαίρεσις; achieves independence as ἀποφαίνεσθαι when separated from πρᾶξις; a basic phenomenon of κοινωνία.
- **Key Aristotelian loci**: *Politics* A 2; *De Anima* Γ 4, Γ 6; *Rhetoric* A 1; *De Interpretatione* 1; *Metaphysics* Gamma 2; *Nicomachean Ethics* A 6
- **Units**: U-Intro, U-FP1a, U-FP1b, U-FP2a, U-FP2b, U-FP3a, U-FP3b, U-FP3c, U-FP3e, U-SP1, U-SP2a, U-SP2b
- **Pages**: 3-222 (throughout)
- **Translation status**: Systematically reinterpreted. Standard "reason" rejected; existential-ontological rendering as "speech" or "discourse" that discloses beings.
- **Canonical node link**: C1 (λόγος)

### 2. οὐσία (ousia)
- **Greek Unicode**: οὐσία
- **Forms encountered**: οὐσία, οὐσίαι
- **Conventional translations**: substance, essence, being
- **Heidegger's usage**: "Being-there" in existential rendering; the being of a being; five being-characters from Met. Δ8 (τὸ τί ἦν εἶναι, τὸ καθόλου, τὸ γένος, τὸ ὑποκείμενον, and a fifth connected to πέρας); λόγος οὐσίας = definition that discloses the being of beings.
- **Key Aristotelian loci**: *Metaphysics* Δ 8; *Categories*; *De Anima* B 1
- **Units**: U-Intro, U-FP1a, U-FP1b, U-FP2a, U-FP2b, U-FP3c, U-SP2a, U-SP2b
- **Pages**: 3, 14, 17-25, 55, 69, 125, 200-212
- **Translation status**: Non-standard. Heidegger renders as "being-there" or "beingness" rather than traditional "substance."
- **Canonical node link**: C2 (οὐσία)

### 3. ψυχή (psuchē)
- **Greek Unicode**: ψυχή
- **Forms encountered**: ψυχή, ψυχῆς, ψυχαί
- **Conventional translations**: soul, mind, life-principle
- **Heidegger's usage**: "The ontologically basic determination of living"; ψυχή as πρακτικὴ ἐνέργεια; "psychology has nothing to do with consciousness but is the doctrine of the being of living things"; ψυχή as the being-taken of beings as living things; πάθη as λόγοι ἔνυλοι of ψυχή.
- **Key Aristotelian loci**: *De Anima* A 1, B 1 (412a 27 sq.), Γ 4; *NE* A 6 (1098a 16)
- **Units**: U-FP1b, U-FP2a, U-FP2b, U-FP3a, U-FP3c, U-FP3d, U-FP3e, U-SP1, U-SP2b
- **Pages**: 14, 32-45, 62, 69-70, 129-139, 190
- **Translation status**: Reinterpreted. De-psychologized: not "consciousness" or "mind" but ontological determination of living.
- **Canonical node link**: C4 (ψυχή)

### 4. πάθος / πάθη (pathos / pathē)
- **Greek Unicode**: πάθος, πάθη
- **Forms encountered**: πάθος, πάθη, πάσχειν, παθητικόν
- **Conventional translations**: passion, emotion, affection, experience
- **Heidegger's usage**: Four general meanings from Met. Δ21: (1) quality admitting alteration, (2) ἐνέργειαι/ἀλλοιώσεις already underway, (3) harmful alterations especially involving λύπη, (4) great magnitudes of misfortune. As πίστις in rhetoric; three γινόμενα in ψυχή (πάθη, δυνάμεις, ἕξεις); "the ground out of which speaking arises."
- **Key Aristotelian loci**: *Metaphysics* Δ 21; *NE* B 4 (1105b 19 sqq.); *Rhetoric* B 1; *De Anima* A 1
- **Units**: U-Intro, U-FP3a, U-FP3b, U-FP3c, U-FP3d, U-FP3e, U-SP2a
- **Pages**: 4, 71-176
- **Translation status**: Reinterpreted. Not "passion" or "emotion" but disposition, being-affected, the manner of finding-oneself in the world.
- **Canonical node link**: C7 (πάθος)

### 5. ἕξις (hexis)
- **Greek Unicode**: ἕξις
- **Forms encountered**: ἕξις, ἕξεις, ἔχειν
- **Conventional translations**: habit, state, disposition, possession
- **Heidegger's usage**: "The determination of the genuineness of being-there in a moment of being-composed as to something"; four senses of ἔχειν from Met. Δ23; ἕξις = ἐνέργεια of the having (Met. Δ20); ἕξις as μεταξύ; ἀρετή = ἕξις προαιρετική ἐν μεσότητι; conceptuality as ἕξις πρὸς ἄλλον.
- **Key Aristotelian loci**: *Metaphysics* Δ 20, Δ 23; *NE* B 1-6; *Rhetoric* B 1
- **Units**: U-Intro, U-FP3b, U-FP3c, U-FP3d, U-FP3e, U-SP1, U-SP2b
- **Pages**: 4, 113-128, 175-178, 186-188
- **Translation status**: Non-standard. Not "habit" but "being-composed," "having-in-itself," the how of being-there's disposition.
- **Canonical node link**: C8 (ἕξις)

### 6. πέρας (peras)
- **Greek Unicode**: πέρας
- **Forms encountered**: πέρας
- **Conventional translations**: limit, boundary, end
- **Heidegger's usage**: Not spatial boundary but "the limit that constitutes the genuine there of a being"; connected to τέλος as ἔσχατον; "a being-aspect in beings; πέρας is its site, its place, its being-produced, being-in-its-place"; site (τόπος) as positive ontological determination.
- **Key Aristotelian loci**: *Metaphysics* Δ 17; *Physics* Δ 4; *NE* A 5
- **Units**: U-Intro, U-FP1a, U-FP1b, U-FP2b, U-FP3e, U-SP2a, U-SP2b
- **Pages**: 4, 11, 23, 61-62, 179, 211
- **Translation status**: Standard in early occurrences; reinterpreted as existential-ontological from FP2b onward.
- **Canonical node link**: C10 (πέρας)

### 7. ἀρετή (aretē)
- **Greek Unicode**: ἀρετή
- **Forms encountered**: ἀρετή, ἀρεταί
- **Conventional translations**: virtue, excellence
- **Heidegger's usage**: "Competence -- having one's being-possibility at one's disposal"; not "virtue" in moralizing sense; ἕξις προαιρετική ἐν μεσότητι; determined by temporality through ἔθος; the being of ἀρετή = μεσότης, simultaneously ἀκρότης with regard to the εὖ.
- **Key Aristotelian loci**: *NE* A 6, B 1-6; *Rhetoric* B 1 (1378a1)
- **Units**: U-FP2a, U-FP2b, U-FP3b, U-FP3c, U-FP3d, U-FP3e
- **Pages**: 55-56, 69, 111, 119-130
- **Translation status**: Reinterpreted. "Excellence" preferred over "virtue"; existential rendering as mode of being-there.
- **Canonical node link**: C9 (ἀρετή)

## Tier 2: Terms Appearing in 4--6 Units

### 8. πρᾶξις (praxis)
- **Units**: U-FP2a, U-FP2b, U-FP3a, U-FP3b, U-FP3c, U-FP3d, U-SP1, U-SP2a, U-SP2b
- **Heidegger's usage**: Concern; the mode of being proper to human beings; πρακτική τοῦ λόγον ἔχοντος; distinguished from ποίησις.
- **Translation status**: Reinterpreted as "concern" rather than "action."
- **Canonical node link**: C11 (πρᾶξις)

### 9. ἐνέργεια (energeia)
- **Units**: U-FP2a, U-FP2b, U-FP3c, U-SP2a, U-SP2b
- **Heidegger's usage**: Being-at-work; being-grasped-in-becoming-completed; "stretches itself out toward ἐντελέχεια"; κίνησις is a mode of ἐνέργεια.
- **Translation status**: Reinterpreted. Not "actuality" but being-at-work/becoming-completed.
- **Canonical node link**: C5 (ἐνέργεια)

### 10. δύναμις (dunamis)
- **Units**: U-Intro, U-FP1b, U-FP3b, U-FP3c, U-FP3e, U-SP2a, U-SP2b
- **Heidegger's usage**: Methodological (τρόπος τῆς δυνάμεως); psychic (co-given possibilities); ontological (meaningfulness, Bedeutsamkeit, NOT modal possibility).
- **Translation status**: Reinterpreted. Not "possibility" but capacity/meaningfulness/availability-for.
- **Canonical node link**: C6 (δύναμις)

### 11. δόξα (doxa)
- **Units**: U-FP3a, U-FP3b, U-FP3e, U-SP1
- **Heidegger's usage**: "Having-a-view"; the basic phenomenon of everydayness; genuine discoveredness of being-with-one-another; reaches πάντα.
- **Translation status**: Partially reinterpreted. Standard "opinion" supplemented with existential "having-a-view."
- **Canonical node link**: I4 (δόξα)

### 12. φαντασία (phantasia)
- **Units**: U-FP3b, U-FP3c, U-FP3e, U-SP1
- **Heidegger's usage**: Making-present-to-itself; ground of νοεῖν; not "imagination" in the modern sense; bodily being-in-the-world; distinguished from αἴσθησις.
- **Translation status**: Reinterpreted. Not "imagination" but making-present-to-itself.
- **Canonical node link**: I9 (φαντασία)

### 13. κοινωνία (koinōnia)
- **Units**: U-FP2a, U-FP3a, U-FP3b, U-FP3e
- **Heidegger's usage**: Being-with-one-another; community; fulfilled in δόξα; the social context of λόγος.
- **Translation status**: Standard with existential inflection.
- **Canonical node link**: I8 (κοινωνία)

### 14. προαίρεσις (proairesis)
- **Units**: U-FP3b, U-FP3c, U-FP3e, U-SP1
- **Heidegger's usage**: Being-resolved; resolve; acting from out of oneself; distinguished from δόξα in seven respects.
- **Translation status**: Non-standard. Not "choice" but "being-resolved" or "resolving-oneself."
- **Canonical node link**: I13 (προαίρεσις)

### 15. φρόνησις (phronēsis)
- **Units**: U-FP3b, U-FP3c, U-FP3e
- **Heidegger's usage**: Looking-around; practical insight; first aspect of ἦθος-cultivation; mode of sight in being-in-care.
- **Translation status**: Non-standard. Not "practical wisdom" but "looking-around" or "circumspection."
- **Canonical node link**: I7 (φρόνησις)

### 16. καιρός (kairos)
- **Units**: U-FP3b, U-FP3c, U-SP2a
- **Heidegger's usage**: The moment; the decisive moment; the entirety of circumstances; the mean = καιρός; ἀγαθόν is always κατὰ τὸν καιρόν.
- **Translation status**: Standard with existential emphasis.
- **Canonical node link**: I14 (καιρός)

### 17. ἐντελέχεια (entelecheia)
- **Units**: U-FP2b, U-SP2a, U-SP2b
- **Heidegger's usage**: Maintaining-itself-in-its-being-completed; enteles + echein = possession of completion; the fundamental concept of Aristotelian ontology; what excludes every δύναμις.
- **Translation status**: Reinterpreted. Not "actuality" but "being-completed," "maintaining-itself-at-the-end."
- **Canonical node link**: I1 (ἐντελέχεια)

### 18. στέρησις (sterēsis)
- **Units**: U-Intro, U-FP1b, U-SP2a, U-SP2b
- **Heidegger's usage**: Privation as constitutive of being; "negation is a position"; "I miss him very much" = a determinate way of his being-there for me.
- **Translation status**: Reinterpreted. Not mere lack but positive ontological determination.
- **Canonical node link**: I3 (στέρησις)

### 19. νοῦς (nous)
- **Units**: U-FP3c, U-SP1, U-SP2b
- **Heidegger's usage**: Basic condition of the possibility of being-in-the-world; goes toward τὰ πάντα; the "so-called" νοῦς = διανοεῖσθαι; ἀπαθές, δεκτικὸν τοῦ εἴδους.
- **Translation status**: Reinterpreted. Not "intellect" but the fundamental openness of being-in-the-world.
- **Canonical node link**: I10 (νοῦς)

### 20. ἡδονή / λύπη (hēdonē / lupē)
- **Units**: U-FP3b, U-FP3c, U-FP3d, U-SP1
- **Heidegger's usage**: Fundamental determinations of being-in-the-world; co-given with all πάθη; ground of δίωξις and φυγή; ζωή identified with ἡδονή.
- **Translation status**: Standard (pleasure/pain) with existential deepening.
- **Canonical node link**: I11 (ἡδονή / λύπη)
