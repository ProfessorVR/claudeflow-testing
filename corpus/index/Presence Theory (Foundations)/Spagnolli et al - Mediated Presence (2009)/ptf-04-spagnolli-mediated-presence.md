## 1. Thesis / Aim

Framing the special issue from PRESENCE 2008, Spagnolli, Lombard & Gamberini survey **technology-mediated presence** across **virtual reality, mixed environments, and social networks**, treating the human capacity to "bring presence from the 'real' world and from 'real' human interactions to mediated worlds and mediated social relations" as an opportunity that "represents an important chance to enrich human experience" (FE, p.137). They highlight that three of the issue's papers share a method: **the analysis of participants' action** "as a method to investigate presence and social presence" (FE, p.138).

## 2. Structure

Three pages: (p.137) the human project of establishing presence through mediating technologies, from early communication/transportation devices to telepresence conferencing, remote medicine, and multiplayer games; (p.138) the special issue's range of approaches and the shared action-analytic method across three papers; (p.139) closing framing. Editorial, no original data.

## 3. Key constructs

- **Mediated presence across environment types** (core, FE) — presence studied uniformly across VR, mixed/augmented environments, and social networks.
- **Action-based method for presence & social presence** (core, FE) — participants' *action* as the investigative unit — connecting the physical-presence behavioral tradition (ptf-03) to *social* presence.
- **Presence as a human/technological endeavor** (important, FE) — presence-extension as a continuous species project, not a novelty of VR.
