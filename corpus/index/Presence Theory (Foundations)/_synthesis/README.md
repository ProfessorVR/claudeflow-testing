# Presence Theory (Foundations)

A compact four-source theory entry built 2026-07-20 (Phase 3 of the new-media presence work). It holds the non-social,
*foundational* presence sources behind Part III's presence framing.

## Sources / units
- **ptf-01** — Mantovani & Riva, "'Real' Presence" (*Presence* 8.5, 1999) — ontology-dependence of presence criteria. **⚠ OCR-only source: all verbatims `****** UNVERIFIED:`.**
- **ptf-02** — Riva, "Is presence a technology issue?" (*Virtual Reality* 13, 2009) — presence as cognition, not technology.
- **ptf-03** — Lepecq et al., "Afforded actions…" (*Virtual Reality* 13, 2009) — presence assessed behaviorally via afforded action; **PRIMARY Part II bridge**.
- **ptf-04** — Spagnolli, Lombard & Gamberini, "Mediated presence" (*Virtual Reality* 13, 2009) — mediated presence across environment types + the action-based method (editorial).

## What the cluster argues
Presence has no ontology-neutral criterion (ptf-01); it is cognition — enacting intentions in a disclosed world — not a
media effect (ptf-02); it can be measured through the body's afforded action (ptf-03); and the field's method turns on
*action* across VR/mixed/social-network environments (ptf-04). Together they support the Part III worry that a scored
feeling under-describes how a world opens, while ptf-03 shows what a disclosure-faithful measure would look like.
