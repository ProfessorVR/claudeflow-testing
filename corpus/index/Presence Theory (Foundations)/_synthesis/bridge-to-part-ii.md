## 1. The problem the bridge solves

Part III's presence framing says the field's instruments "score" presence but "cannot say how a world is opened to its inhabitants." Lepecq is the apparent exception: a *behavioral* presence measure. The bridge's question is whether Lepecq's measure is merely a better score, or whether it actually registers **world-disclosedness** — and if so, in what vocabulary. The claim developed here: Lepecq's afforded action is a **Funktionskreis** (Uexküllian functional cycle), and a completed functional cycle is **world-disclosedness made behaviorally observable** — so his "objective indication of presence" is, read through this bridge, an objective indication of *disclosure*. This is exactly the datum Part II's world-pole needs.

## 2. The two faithful records

**Lepecq et al., ptf-03 (FAITHFUL-LEPECQ; printed page = PDF + 140, verified).** Subjects walk through virtual apertures; body orientation is "adapted to the width of the aperture and to their own shoulder width" (p.147), showing "a behavioral transition from frontal walking to body rotation" as apertures narrow (pp.148–149) — the Warren & Whang (1987) real-world transition, reproduced in VR. "This behavioral adjustment is thus assumed to be an objective indication of presence" (p.141); the study "behaviorally objectivates physical presence in VR by the way of an afforded action" (p.150); and "every afforded action could be a potential tool for sensorimotor assessment of physical presence" (p.141).

**Von Uexküll entry (FAITHFUL-UEXKÜLL; node # / unit id).** Relevant anchors: **Funktionskreis** (node 2; U-F0, U-F2, U-F3) — the closed perception-organ↔effect-organ loop, "perception mark activates the cycle; effect mark extinguishes the perception mark, closing it." **Wirkton / Ton** (node 17; U-F4) — objects "encountered not as bare things but as carriers of action-affordances perceived 'with the same assurance' as shape or color." **Merkwelt + Wirkwelt = Umwelt** (nodes 1,3,4; U-F0). **Wirkraum / subject-constituted space** (node 27; U-F1) — "no universal space but three subject-constituted spaces," bounded by the organism's own apparatus. **Stimmung** (node 22; U-F4) — the mood selecting which effect-image activates, glossed by the entry as a "structural parallel to Heidegger's Befindlichkeit."

## 4. Keystone — the afforded action *is* a functional cycle, and a closed functional cycle is disclosure made observable (ANTICIPATORY-APPLICATION)

Lepecq's aperture-crossing has exactly the structure of a **Funktionskreis**: a perception-mark (the aperture's width, read off relative to the body) activates an effect (shoulder-rotation) that closes the loop by resolving the perceived layout. Uexküll's functional cycle is *the* unit of an organism inhabiting an Umwelt — of a world being disclosed to a subject as a field of perception-and-action. Therefore Lepecq's "objective indication of presence" (p.141) is, on this reading, an **objective indication that a world has been disclosed to the walker's body** — the walker rotates *because the aperture is disclosed as passable-or-not for this body* (a Wirkton, node 17), not because it computed a width. This is the bridge's payload: it converts a presence *score* into a behavioral *signature of world-disclosedness*, which is precisely the register Part II's world-pole works in. It is the one claim the bridge most wants scrutinized.

## 5. Secondary axis — Mantovani & Riva as the meta-level license (FAITHFUL-MANTOVANI `******`, paraphrase)

Why is the §3 mapping legitimate rather than a category error? Because presence criteria are **ontology-relative** (ptf-01). On a **naive-realist** ontology, Lepecq's data are *mere motor behavior* correlated with a display; the disclosure reading looks like over-interpretation. On a **situated / disclosedness** ontology — the one Part II holds — the afforded action is *constitutive* of presence, and the Funktionskreis/world-disclosedness reading is the correct criterion, not an overlay. So Mantovani & Riva operate one level up: they license the choice of ontology under which §3's mappings are faithful rather than projected. ## 6. Where this plugs into Part II (purpose note — no prose drafted)

The user flagged Lepecq as likely useful for **Part II's world-disclosedness element**. Concretely, this bridge plugs in at:
- **The world-disclosedness pole of rhetorical incorporation** (Part II's world-/co-disclosedness poles, BT 145 + 160): Lepecq supplies an *empirical, embodied datum* for the **world-pole** — presence as the body's disclosure of a field of affordances (a Wirkton-structured Umwelt). It is the psychophysical instance of the phenomenological structure the Von Uexküll entry treats.
- **The RODA methodology's world-pole** (world-disclosedness): the afforded-action index is a candidate *operationalization* of world-disclosedness — a way to point, in an analysis, at where a virtual environment does or does not disclose a world to the acting body.
- **Limit to respect:** Lepecq measures **solo, physical** presence — the **world-pole only.** He does **not** reach **co-disclosedness** (the WE / being-with pole); nothing in the aperture task is social. Cite him for the world-pole; do not stretch him to co-disclosedness. (The co-disclosedness material belongs with the social-presence cluster and Part II's being-with sources.)

## 8. Category caution (honesty)

The mapping borrows a **structural grammar** — functional cycle, Umwelt, affordance-tone, subject-constituted space — as a heuristic for reading a psychophysics experiment in disclosedness terms. It does **not** claim Lepecq's subjects underwent a thematic phenomenological disclosure, nor that Uexküll and Heidegger are interchangeable (the entry itself flags the Stimmung↔Befindlichkeit link as a *structural parallel*, and its terminology material records the Umwelt↔Kantian-thing-in-itself tension). The bridge transfers *structure*, not metaphysical scope; the naive-realist reading of Lepecq's data remains available and is exactly what §5 says the disclosedness ontology must be chosen over.
