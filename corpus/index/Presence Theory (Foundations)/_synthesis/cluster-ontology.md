# Cluster-Level Ontology — Presence Theory (Foundations)

A four-source foundations cluster built 2026-07-20 (Phase 3 of the new-media presence work). It gathers the
non-social, *theoretical* presence sources behind Part III's presence framing: **Mantovani & Riva** (ptf-01,
ontology-dependence of presence criteria), **Riva** (ptf-02, presence as cognition not technology), **Lepecq et al.**
(ptf-03, presence assessed behaviorally via afforded action), and **Spagnolli et al.** (ptf-04, mediated presence
across environment types + the action-based method). Two sources — **ptf-01 and ptf-03** — carry an explicit **bridge
to Part II world-disclosedness** (see `bridge-to-part-ii.md`). Loci are each source's own printed pages (offset-verified),
except ptf-01, a scanned OCR-only source whose verbatims are all `****** UNVERIFIED:` (see its unit). Provenance tags:
**FE** = source's own claim; **P** = our interpretive link, never the source's own claim.

Companion files: `concept-matrix.csv`, `citations.md`, `drafting-loci.md`, `bridge-to-part-ii.md`, `README.md`.

## 1. The governing question

The cluster answers one question from four angles: **what is presence, and can it be measured without losing how a
world is opened to its inhabitant?** ptf-01 says the *criterion* of presence is ontology-relative (so any single score
presupposes an ontology); ptf-02 relocates presence from media to cognition (enacting intentions); ptf-03 measures it
through the body's afforded action (world-disclosure in a psychophysical register); ptf-04 frames the field's turn to
*action* as the method and extends presence across VR/mixed/social-network environments.

## 2. How the cluster disposes the Part III presence framing

The Part III introduction frames presence as "a feeling; the field's instruments score it but cannot say how a world is
opened to its inhabitants."
- **Supports** it: ptf-01 (a score presupposes an ontology and cannot itself say *how* the world is disclosed);
  ptf-02 (presence is enacting intentions in a disclosed world, not a media effect); ptf-04 (the field foregrounds
  *action*, not only self-report).
- **Sharpens/complicates** it: ptf-03 offers a genuine *instrument* — an afforded-action index — that does track
  world-openness (for physical presence), showing what a disclosure-faithful measure would have to look like.

## 3. The Part II bridge (recorded, not built)

ptf-01 (ontology of the real) and **ptf-03 (afforded action; PRIMARY)** bridge to Part II world-disclosedness and the
Von Uexküll/Umwelt material: presence as the actor's meaningful, embodied engagement within a disclosed field of
affordances rather than correspondence to a mind-independent world. Recorded in `bridge-to-part-ii.md`; no cross-entry
edges are constructed this pass.

## 3A. Canonical Node List

*Registration-format node list (harvested by `scripts/compile-corpus-index.py`). `units` lists the `ptf-NN` units in
which the node is genuinely treated.*

#### 1. Ontology-dependence of presence criteria
- **definition**: There is no ontology-neutral criterion of presence; what counts as "real" presence is generated by the ontology one holds (naive realism vs. cultural constructivism). ptf-01's central claim; a single presence score already presupposes an ontology.
- **type**: THEORY-MODEL
- **units**: ptf-01
- **centrality**: core

#### 2. Presence as cognition, not technology
- **definition**: Presence is a core neuropsychological phenomenon, not a property of a medium; ptf-02 relocates presence from media effect to the achievement of enacting one's intentions in a world (virtual or real).
- **type**: THEORY-MODEL
- **units**: ptf-02
- **centrality**: core

#### 3. Presence as enacting intentions (agency and control)
- **definition**: One is "present" to the degree one can realize one's intentions in an external world; presence produces a sense of agency and control (ptf-02).
- **type**: CONSTRUCT
- **units**: ptf-02
- **centrality**: core

#### 4. Afforded action as behavioral presence measure
- **definition**: Physical presence indexed by an action the environment affords (aperture-crossing shoulder-rotation), an objective behavioral measure against the questionnaire tradition (ptf-03).
- **type**: TEST-RESULT
- **units**: ptf-03
- **centrality**: core

#### 5. Physical / spatial presence
- **definition**: Presence as body-in-space (distinct from social presence); the presence-type Lepecq measures and the behavioral/action tradition targets.
- **type**: CONSTRUCT
- **units**: ptf-03, ptf-04
- **centrality**: important

#### 6. Naive realism vs. cultural constructivism
- **definition**: The two ontologies ptf-01 contrasts — presence as faithful reproduction of a mind-independent physical state vs. presence as culturally-mediated situated action.
- **type**: DEBATE-AXIS
- **units**: ptf-01
- **centrality**: core

#### 7. Differentiated opportunity structure of the environment
- **definition**: An environment does not provide undifferentiated, equal-for-everyone information; it offers differentiated action-opportunities and produces presence by supporting the user's intentions (ptf-02), a claim Lepecq operationalizes behaviorally (ptf-03).
- **type**: CONSTRUCT
- **units**: ptf-02, ptf-03
- **centrality**: important

#### 8. Action-based method for presence and social presence
- **definition**: The analysis of participants' action as the investigative unit for presence and social presence — the field's methodological turn foregrounded by ptf-04 and instantiated by ptf-03.
- **type**: METHOD
- **units**: ptf-03, ptf-04
- **centrality**: core

#### 9. Mediated presence across environment types
- **definition**: Presence studied uniformly across VR, mixed/augmented environments, and social networks; presence-extension as a continuous human/technological endeavor (ptf-04).
- **type**: CONSTRUCT
- **units**: ptf-04
- **centrality**: important

#### 10. World-disclosedness bridge (affordance / Umwelt)
- **definition**: The cluster's Part II hinge — presence as the actor's embodied, intentional engagement within a disclosed field of affordances (an Umwelt), not correspondence to a mind-independent world; links ptf-01 (ontology of the real) and ptf-03 (afforded action, PRIMARY) to the Von Uexküll / world-disclosedness material. Recorded, not built, as cross-entry edges.
- **type**: THEORY-MODEL
- **units**: ptf-01, ptf-03
- **centrality**: core
