# rdr-12 — Analysis of Immersion Levels vs. UX Design Quality in RDR2

**Citation:** (anonymous), "Analysis of the relationship between user immersion levels and the quality of UX design in *Red Dead Redemption 2*." **Final Degree Project**, Universitat Oberta de Catalunya (UOC), n.d. **Depth:** map. **Format:** 9-slide presentation deck (image-only PDF; image-read — no OCR tool available). Loci = slide numbers. **Provenance note:** a student FDP (lower scholarly weight than the peer-reviewed/thesis cluster members) — included for its empirical UX/immersion data, flagged accordingly.

## Argument
Driving question (slides 2–3): **"Are the good, great things in RDR2 outshined by its problems?"** — motivated by whether RDR2's *UX design* is "worthy of the great storytelling and visuals." The study answers via a **three-method, user-facing design** (slide 4): **heuristic evaluation + usability test + immersion test**, "with users."

## Findings
- **Heuristic evaluation** (slide 5): problems sorted into **Narrative / Structural / Mechanics**; **~70 distinct problems** identified.
- **Usability test** (slide 6): **4 users · 7 tasks · ~35 min** (expected). Users **struggled with two tasks**, which **reinforced the heuristic-evaluation conclusions** (slide 8).
- **Immersion test** (slide 7): a **1–5 scale** across five dimensions — **Sense of Control · Visual Presence · Auditory Presence · Interface Awareness · Real-World Awareness**. (This is a presence/immersion-questionnaire structure.)
- **Results** (slide 8): even the **least immersion-prone participant scored above average**; **Auditory Presence rated highest** of all categories ("exceptional").
- **Conclusion** (slide 9): to the driving question — **"NO."** Despite many usability problems, **RDR2 still engages the player well**, though users agree it "could use some changes."

## Why it matters for the dissertation (diss-hooks — `anticipatory-application`)
- *diss-hook:* the **engagement-despite-usability-friction** result is the same shape as the BME VLE seed read in the Wendt entry (friction present, yet engagement holds) — a small empirical corroboration of the design-conditions thesis.
- *diss-hook:* the immersion dimensions (**visual/auditory presence, interface awareness, real-world awareness, sense of control**) sit at **A0/A1** of the actualization chain (the designed medium structuring perception/presence) and map onto Wendt's **transparency-vs-invisibility** and Ihde's **embodiment/background** relations; "interface awareness" is precisely the (non-)withdrawal of the equipment.
- *diss-hook:* **auditory presence as the standout** flags sound design as a high-leverage A0/A1 lever — relevant to RODA's scene-level analysis (the Valentine bar-fight's diegetic audio, ambient soundscape).

## Game-elements / UX targets treated
Interface/HUD and "interface awareness"; the control scheme and game **mechanics** (a heuristic-evaluation category); **audio/sound design** (auditory presence, rated exceptional); overall onboarding/usability via 7 (unspecified) tasks. The deck treats RDR2 at the **UX-system level**, not specific missions/scenes.
