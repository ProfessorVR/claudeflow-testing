## 1. To the actualization-of-desire chain (A0→A4)
The cluster's findings localize on the chain, turning a scattered literature into node-indexed scholarship:
- **A0 / A1 (object + medium + horizon → perception):** **gameplace** (rdr-02) = the lived-place horizon; **slow game time** (rdr-06) = designed tempo shaping perceptual pacing; **auditory presence / interface awareness** (rdr-12) and **landscape/nature** (rdr-04, rdr-07) = the perceptual surface. ## 5. The keystone contribution
The cluster's **deepest split** is experiential/phenomenological (Moser, Westerside) vs structural/ideological (Tuominen, Wright) — see debate D10. The dissertation's **actualization-of-desire chain mediates exactly this split**: it gives a *structural* account *of* the *experiential* (how a designed world moves desire through perception → phantasma → doxa/emotion → action), so the cluster's two warring registers become two ends of one analysis. That is the cluster's single largest payoff for the dissertation: not another reading of RDR2, but a field whose central methodological tension the dissertation is built to resolve — entered near-first on the tutorial bar-fight and the disruption scene, with the honor system as the shared hub.
