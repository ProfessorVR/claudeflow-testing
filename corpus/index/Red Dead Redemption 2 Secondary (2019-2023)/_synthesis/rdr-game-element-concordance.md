## E. Coverage observations (for the dissertation)
- **Hot spots:** the **honor system** (7), **Saint Denis** (7), **TB/illness** (4), **frontier-myth missions** (KKK/suffragette/eugenics) — heavily theorized; the dissertation should *position against* this density.
- **Open lanes:** the **Valentine tutorial bar-fight** (only McEvoy) and the **Saint Denis disruption scene** (only Moser) are under-treated — the dissertation's RODA scene analyses are close to first-mover there, with Moser as the single prior interlocutor on the disruption scene.
- **Strand-by-element bias:** mechanics (honor, hunting, slow-time) draw ideology/ecology/temporality strands; place draws phenomenology; illness/empathy draw affect — useful for choosing which scholar to cite at which chain-node.
