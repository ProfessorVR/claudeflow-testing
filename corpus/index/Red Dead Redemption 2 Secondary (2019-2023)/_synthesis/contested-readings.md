# dfd… — Contested Readings (RDR2 cluster)

Cross-article disagreements where the 12 units take incompatible positions on the same question. (Faithful; positions are the articles', not the dissertation's.) The structured version is `debate-map.json`.

## 1. Is RDR2 complicit or critical? (the central debate)
The cluster's load-bearing disagreement. **Tuominen (rdr-03)** argues the series, read whole, is *subversive* — "mainstream but radically progressive," unmasking the neoliberal state via the Spaghetti-Western's anti-capitalist RSA-plot. **Wright (rdr-05)** holds the critique is *surface-level and self-cancelling* — the frontier myth Rockstar sells is the very thing the games gesture at condemning, and the critique is undercut by branding and profit. **Bagnoli (rdr-01)** sits between: RDR2 genuinely embeds progressive values (feminism, anti-racism, ecology) via procedural rhetoric, but (with Sicart) the honor system inscribes morality in *Arthur*, not the player. Tuominen explicitly contests the **"dominant reading"** (Humphreys, Morgan — non-cluster) that RDR reproduces neoliberalism/white-supremacy/hegemonic masculinity.

## 2. Authenticity: accuracy vs feel vs marketed discourse
**Donald & Reid (rdr-11)** name the axis: RDR2 is *not accurate* but *feels authentic*; accuracy is the servant of authenticity. **Wright (rdr-05)** reframes again: authenticity is neither accuracy nor feel but a *constructed marketing claim* — and asks why make authenticity claims at all if exempt from historical standards. **Bagnoli (rdr-01)** and **McEvoy (rdr-08)**: period-accurate detail is the *vehicle* for deliberately anachronistic, present-day values / persuasion.

## 3. Player agency: meaningful, illusory, or foreclosed?
**Vanderhoef & Payne (rdr-06)** and **Ruffino (rdr-10)** both land on *scripted constraint* — slow time and auto-run *remove* agency; TB stages "a consequence with no cause and no cure," withdrawing the protagonist's mastery. **Tuominen (rdr-03)**: freedom is *illusory* (every choice already made; interpellation). Against this, **McEvoy (rdr-08)** and **Moser (rdr-09)** treat open-world freedom as a genuine *ethical opportunity* (bounded by a moral "fence"). The honor system is the shared battleground.

## 4. Nature: resource, ecological, or multifaceted?
**Crowley (rdr-04)** (empirical) sits *across* the resource↔ecological axis — the hunting mechanic is exploitative, yet the simulation measurably teaches ecology (the Naturalist role as counter-frame). **Ventomäki (rdr-07)** refuses the either/or: nature is Romantic, exploited, antagonistic, and autonomous *at once*, with player choice deciding which pole dominates.

## 5. The honor system: moral pedagogy, interpellation, or arithmetic morality?
The hub mechanic is read three ways. **Moser (rdr-09)** & **McEvoy (rdr-08)**: tacit *moral pedagogy* that persuades toward virtue. **Tuominen (rdr-03)**: an *interpellation* device — progression as ideological absorption (note the shared "KKK-killing costs no honor" exception, flagged by both Bagnoli and Tuominen). **Bagnoli (rdr-01)** (with Sicart/Bogost): a *linear, arithmetic* morality that models ethics too simply and inscribes it in Arthur rather than coercing the player.

## 6. The "empathy machine": affirmative vs skeptical
**Moser (rdr-09)** is strongly *affirmative* (games generate prosocial empathy; player-as-performer), positioned against the violence-effects tradition (Anderson et al.) and conceding limits. No other cluster member works the empathy question directly — a gap the dissertation's A3/being-moved analysis can occupy.

## 7. Locus of meaning: narrative/fiction vs systems/procedure
**McEvoy (rdr-08)**, **Bagnoli (rdr-01)**, **Tuominen (rdr-03)** shift meaning toward *procedure* (Bogost) — the honor system and reactive code, not plot. **Westerside & Holopainen (rdr-02)** shift it elsewhere again — to *place* (gameplace) as the affective carrier. **Moser (rdr-09)** locates it in *performance/lifeworld*.

## 8. Scope & framing: single title vs series; American artifact vs European critique
**Tuominen (rdr-03)** insists RDR2 + RDR be read as *one series* (only then is the progressive reading legible) and shifts the frame from *American cultural artifact* to the *European (Spaghetti-Western) capitalism-critique tradition* — explicitly against Humphreys/Bagnoli/Morgan's single-title, American-artifact framing.

## 9. Western mode: nostalgic vs revisionist
**Ventomäki (rdr-07)** reads RDR2 as predominantly *nostalgic/Romantic* (Old West idealized; Arthur's death = New West victory) while registering *revisionist* currents (industrial critique, the treatment of horses). **Wright (rdr-05)** reads the nostalgia itself as ideologically loaded (the "vanishing American," the safety-valve myth).

## 10. Reading register: experiential/phenomenological vs structural/ideological
**Moser (rdr-09)** and **Westerside & Holopainen (rdr-02)** read RDR2 *experientially* (lifeworld, felt emotion, place), explicitly contrasting with the *ideology-critique* register of **Tuominen (rdr-03)** and **Wright (rdr-05)**. This methodological split — first-person felt experience vs third-person structural unmasking — is the cluster's deepest meta-disagreement, and the one the dissertation's actualization-of-desire frame is positioned to mediate.
