# dfd… — Scholarly Evolution of RDR2 Criticism (2019–2023)

The window is short (the game shipped Oct 2018), so the "evolution" is **thematic fan-out**, not generational turnover. Every chronological claim is anchored to a unit.

## 2019 — two poles set at once
The field opens with its two enduring registers already opposed (the D10 split, in embryo):
- **Ideology / procedural discourse** — **Bagnoli (rdr-01)** reads RDR2 via Bogost + Althusser: the honor system and free-roam verbs smuggle present-day values into a period setting.
- **Phenomenology of place** — **Westerside & Holopainen (rdr-02)** coin **gameplace**, locating RDR2's power in lived, affect-bearing place rather than ideology.
Both already orbit the **honor system** and the **frontier myth** — the constants of the whole window.

## 2020 — ideology critique deepens (and the only consolidation)
**Tuominen (rdr-03)** pushes the Althusserian line to its strongest form — *series-as-whole*, *subversive-progressive*, RDR2 as European capitalism-critique — and, uniquely, **cites three cluster-mates** (Bagnoli, Westerside & Holopainen, Donald & Reid). This is the field's single moment of self-consolidation; it does not recur.

## 2021 — diversification; the "is it critical?" question sharpens
- **Wright (rdr-05)** brings *American Studies + paratext*: the critique is surface-level and undercut by branding/profit — reframing authenticity as **marketed discourse** and sharpening D1 against Tuominen.
- **Crowley (rdr-04)** opens an *empirical* front (conservation science, n=586): incidental ecological education — the cluster's only quantitative study; cites Westerside & Holopainen.

## 2022 — new analytic angles fan out
- **Vanderhoef & Payne (rdr-06)** — *temporality*: slow game time exposes neoliberal chrononormativity.
- **Ventomäki (rdr-07)** — *ecocriticism*: nature as irreducibly multifaceted.
- **Moser (rdr-09)** — *empathy/performance*: player-as-performer; the cluster's affect/phenomenology peak (and the only treatment of the disruption scene).
- **Ruffino (rdr-10)** — *paratext/agency*: TB as withdrawn agency; player videos as remediation.

## 2023 — rhetorical synthesis
**McEvoy (rdr-08)** fuses *reader-response + procedural rhetoric*, returning to Bogost (now joined to literary reading) and to the honor system as "the seat of the game's rhetoric" — and explicitly indexes the **Valentine tutorial bar-fight**.

## Undated / adjacent
**Donald & Reid (rdr-11, post-2019)** name the **authenticity-as-feel** axis; the **immersion/UX FDP (rdr-12)** supplies a small empirical engagement-despite-problems result.

## Trajectory (the shape of the field)
1. **Two poles in 2019** — ideology (Bagnoli) vs phenomenology-of-place (Westerside) — recur as the cluster's deepest methodological split (D10).
2. **One consolidation (2020, Tuominen)**, then **fan-out (2021–2023)** into ecology, temporality, empathy, paratext, authenticity, UX — with **no second consolidation**: the field stays **pre-paradigmatic** (sparse citation).
3. **Two constants throughout: the honor system and the frontier myth.** Every strand routes through them.
4. **The opening for the dissertation:** a field that has fanned out but not unified, whose two registers (felt-experiential vs structural-ideological) remain unreconciled — exactly what the actualization-of-desire chain can integrate, entering near-first on the tutorial bar-fight and the disruption scene.
