## Reading
- **Bogost is the cluster's intellectual center of gravity** (procedural rhetoric, 3 units) even though those units don't cite each other — the shared *method*, not shared *citations*, unifies the rhetoric/ideology strand.
- **The phenomenology strand (Heidegger/Husserl/Calleja, rdr-02 + rdr-09) is the cluster's most direct on-ramp to the dissertation** — these are the units to lean on when wiring the cluster to the actualization-of-desire chain (see the cross-pipeline bridge).
- The **Humphreys/Morgan "dominant reading"** functions as the field's shared antagonist; the dissertation can enter the field by taking a position relative to it (with Tuominen's subversive reading and Wright's surface-critique as the two cluster poles).
