# dfd… — Citation Network (intra-cluster)

**Headline finding: the cluster is citationally sparse.** These 12 pieces (2019–2023) are largely *parallel, independent* work; they connect through **shared game-elements** (the concordance) and **shared interlocutors** (`rdr-secondary-literature-network.md`), not by citing one another. This is itself a result: the RDR2 secondary literature had not yet consolidated into a self-citing field as of 2023 — the dissertation can be among the first to read it *as* a field.

- **Humphreys** and **Morgan** — the "dominant reading" foils — are cited by rdr-03 (Tuominen), rdr-02 (Westerside), and engaged by rdr-05 (Wright): the closest thing to a shared citational anchor, but they sit *outside* the cluster.
- **Byrd** ("Red Dead Conventions") and **Margini** (*Red Dead Redemption*, Boss Fight Books) — cited by rdr-05; RDR-specific, non-cluster.
- **Denham & Spokes** (Lefebvrian RDR2 spatial study) — parallel to rdr-07 Ventomäki, not cited by cluster-mates.

## Implication
Because mutual citation is near-absent, the **debate-map** and **game-element concordance** — not a citation graph — are the instruments that make this a *cluster*. The connective backbone is the shared-interlocutor network (next file): Bogost, Althusser, Calleja, Genette, Salen & Zimmerman, plus the Humphreys/Morgan "dominant reading" that several units position against.
