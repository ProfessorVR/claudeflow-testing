## Theme map (concept/debate axes)
**Themes:** rhetoric of the American West / frontier myth · narratives of progress & ideology critique · nature/ecology · slow time & temporality · empathy & character performance · gameplace / open-world spatiality · authenticity & accuracy · paratexts & agency · immersion/UX · environmental education.
**Debate axes:** celebratory ↔ critical-ideological · authenticity-as-accuracy ↔ authenticity-as-feel · player-agency ↔ scripted-constraint · nature-as-resource ↔ nature-as-aesthetic/ecological · narrative-determinism ↔ player-authored experience.
**Discipline split:** game studies · rhetoric · history/American studies · ecocriticism · media/cultural studies.

## The primary-object innovation
Unlike the phantasia cluster (concords to Aristotle's *text* by Bekker locus), this cluster concords to **the game itself**. The `rdr-game-element-concordance` maps each article to the RDR2 elements it treats — missions/scenes, mechanics/systems (honor, Dead Eye, hunting, camp, "Press X" prompts, bounty), regions (Ambarino, New Hanover, Lemoyne, the Heartlands, Blackwater/Great Plains, Guarma), characters (Arthur, Dutch, the gang), and story beats (Blackwater massacre, the Downes/tuberculosis arc, the epilogue/John) — and cross-references the dissertation's target scenes.
