# dfd… — RDR2 Cluster Ontology

The cluster is not a single argument but a **debate about one object** (*Red Dead Redemption 2*) conducted across five discipline strands. This file deduplicates the concepts and interpretive readings across the 12 units into canonical nodes. Faithful (article-says); the dissertation mapping lives in the cross-pipeline bridge.

## Organizing structure
- **Object:** RDR2 read as a text/system. **Strands:** ideology critique · history/authenticity · rhetoric · ecocriticism · phenomenology of place · empathy/performance · temporality · paratext/media · UX/immersion.
- **The connective tissue is NOT mutual citation** (the cluster barely cites itself — see `citation-network.md`) but **shared interlocutors** (Bogost, Althusser, Calleja, Genette, Salen & Zimmerman) and **shared game-elements** (above all the **honor system**, treated by 7 of 12).

## Canonical READING nodes (one interpretive thesis per unit)
- **rdr-03 Tuominen** — *subversive-progressive*: read as a whole, the series unmasks the neoliberal state ("mainstream but radically progressive").
- **rdr-05 Wright** — *surface-critique-undercut-by-profit*: the games' frontier critique is selective and contradicted by Rockstar's branding/profit.
- **rdr-01 Bagnoli** — *modern-values-via-procedural-rhetoric*: RDR2 smuggles present-day values into a period setting, chiefly through the honor system.
- **rdr-08 McEvoy** — *game-as-readable-rhetoric*: reader-response + procedural rhetoric; the honor system persuades toward moral choice while teaching/romanticizing history.
- **rdr-09 Moser** — *empathy-via-performance*: the player-as-actor practices empathy on Arthur that bridges to real others.
- **rdr-06 Vanderhoef & Payne** — *slow-time-exposes-chrononormativity*: deliberate slowness makes AAA's neoliberal tempo visible.
- **rdr-10 Ruffino** — *illness-withdraws-agency*: TB removes the protagonist's mastery; paratexts try (and fail) to restore it.
- **rdr-11 Donald & Reid** — *authenticity-not-accuracy*: RDR2 feels authentic without being accurate; read games as historical text.
- **rdr-04 Crowley** — *incidental-ecological-education*: the simulation measurably teaches wildlife/ecology despite no didactic intent.
- **rdr-07 Ventomäki** — *nature-as-multifaceted*: nature is Romantic, exploited, antagonistic, and autonomous at once, co-authored by player choice.
- **rdr-02 Westerside & Holopainen** — *gameplace*: place qua place is the game's central affective resource.
- **rdr-12** — *engagement-despite-UX-problems*: ~70 usability problems, yet immersion holds (auditory presence exceptional).
