## 0. The method in one paragraph

RODA analyzes a player's behavior as **the actualization of desire**: the movement of a disposed, desiring being from perceptual encounter to completed action (the A₀→A₄ chain), driven by an object of desire (*orekton*), within a world *designed* to intensify, pattern, and sediment that movement. Its central claim is that a designed world drives this actualization toward a **substantial union — *rhetorical incorporation*** — in which the player becomes one-with the world she acts *in* and one-with the avatar/others she acts *as* and *with*, while remaining a distinct locus; and that this union, once achieved, **sediments into the player's dispositional ground (*hexis*)** and can therefore **reshape her being-in-the-world beyond the game**. Calleja's *incorporation* and Burke's *identification/consubstantiation* are not two lenses applied in turn; they are re-grounded as the **two poles of this one event**, and their descriptive apparatus is absorbed as vocabulary for the world's *designed channels* and for the *rhetoric* by which motive is rendered.

## 2. The ontological frame — every episode is a *motion*

> **Why this section exists.** The chain does not float. Every behavior RODA reads is, in Aristotle's own terms, a **motion of a substance, explained by the four causes**; the actualization-of-desire chain (§3) is the *fine structure of one of those causes* — the efficient-final causation of a desiring agent — not a free-standing apparatus. Seating it here makes the method continuous with the dissertation's own ontology (Part I §§3–4) and forecloses the objection that the chain's five nodes are stipulated rather than derived. The frame is established **once**; thereafter it is invoked **only where it does work** (Step 0; the causal diagnosis), never run as a per-beat checklist.

**The frame in one paragraph.** Whatever an analysis takes as its episode — a brawl, a heist, a forced cutscene — is ontologically a **change (*metabolē*) undergone by a substance, indexed to a time**: a determinate matter–form complex passing from potency to act. Every such change is explained by the **four causes** (material · formal · efficient · final — *Met.* I.3, 983a24–32; *Phys.* II.3, 194b24–195a3) and, insofar as it is a motion, transmitted through the **three-factor schema** (relative unmoved mover → moved mover → moved). When the substance is *a rational animal acting*, the efficient and final causes are individuated by Aristotle's **seven causes of action** (*Rhet.* I.10), whose desire-sourced interior — the part where a rational animal's own motion "gets started and directed" — is precisely the **actualization-of-desire chain (A₀→A₄, §3)**. The chain is the magnification of a single cell of the four-cause grid; this section lays the grid so the magnification is grounded.

### 2.0 · The standing premise: three kinds and the kind-distinction firewall

> **Why this comes first (imported from Part II, deferred to here).** Before any episode is analyzed as a motion (§2.1), each engaged item must be placed in its **kind of being**. The metaphysics carries the reality-burden and finalizes this result; the method imports it as a **standing premise it never re-proves**, and inherits the **firewall** below into every union-verdict downstream.

**Place each engaged item in exactly one of three KINDS, each with its own dependence-profile.** **NON-VIRTUAL** — already fully actual; once it has come to be it **depends on nothing further** to be what it is (the non-virtual apple persists unperceived). **VIRTUAL** — depends on (i) code (the digital object), (ii) the hardware that stores and renders it, and (iii) a mediating display (e.g. an HMD); it exists **only while actively rendered**. **PHANTASMIC** — depends on (i) the resonant trace (the *resonant kinēsis*), (ii) an ensouled being that stores it, and (iii) *phantasia* as the rendering capacity; it exists **only while actively imagined**. In each analysis, target the **PERCEPTUAL rendered object the player responds to** — the virtual lightsaber she dodges, not the data structure; the *phantasma* she fears, not the trace — and always carry a standing **renderer** term (engine + HMD for the virtual; the soul-through-*phantasia* for the phantasmic). (REWRITE-v1 §1.2, §3; the asymmetric-dependence + Leibniz-Law argument that a virtual object is *not* the digital object on which it depends.)

**The genus.** Phantasmic objects **are** virtual objects: the genus is *virtual object* — the *not-non-virtual*, the *as-if* (etymologically "not actually, but as if"; *virtualis* = of or relating to potency), a **potency rendered into act** by its renderer. The ***phantasma* is the ORIGINARY virtual object** and the VR object **its late technological prosthesis** — the imagination renders virtual objects natively; VR is a prosthesis for a power the soul has always exercised. Both player-facing objects are a **second actuality of a first-actuality substrate**: the *resonant* trace is the first actuality and the *phantasma* its second; the *digital* object the first actuality and the *virtual* object its second (*DA* II.5 417a21–b2, the first/second-actuality distinction — VERIFIED against REWRITE-v1 §2 and the *De Anima*). This is the metaphysical underside of the chain's own ground/render pairing (*resonant aisthēma + resonant epithymia* → *phantasma*, §3).

**THE FIREWALL (hard, never relaxed): EXPERIENTIAL CONVERGENCE ≠ KIND-IDENTITY.** Suppose technology closes the **data-discrepancy** entirely, so that the virtual apple becomes phenomenologically indistinguishable from the non-virtual. The mode-of-being difference is **untouched** — for the difference between them was never experiential but **ontological**: the virtual remains a potency rendered into act and ontologically dependent on its ground, while the non-virtual is already fully actual and depends on nothing further. The analyst may therefore **NEVER infer from seamless or maximal immersion that the virtual has become non-virtual** (REWRITE-v1 §3: "The two apples may become phenomenologically indistinguishable; they cannot thereby become the same kind of thing"). This kind-difference *beneath* maximal verisimilitude is the **precondition for the rupture/disruption tier** (§6 instrument #9): there is a kind-difference there to be concealed only because the firewall holds. The firewall parallels the faculty-level convergence ≠ identity guard the keystone already carries — "the actualization of the sensible object and that of the percipient sense is one and the same actualization, **though their being is not the same**" (*DA* III.7 431a12–13 / III.2 425b26 — VERIFIED) — and it is the standing premise the whole method imports and **never re-proves** (the metaphysics carries the reality burden, §0).

### 2.1 · The episode as a motion of a substance

The overarching term is **actuality** — *energeia* in its **broad sense**, being-actual as such. (*Energeia* bears two senses in Aristotle: this broad one — actual at all — and the **narrow** one below, the self-complete activity. Holding them apart is what lets a *kinēsis* be called an *energeia atelēs* without contradiction.) Within actuality, Aristotle marks a contrast that is load-bearing for games — two ways an actuality stands to its *telos*. The locus classicus is *Met.* IX.6, 1048b18–34, which divides "these processes" into "movements" and "actualities":

- ***kinēsis*** (motion) — the **actuality of a potential *qua* potential** (*Phys.* III.1, 201a10–11). The potentiality remains *operative and present throughout*, because the *telos* lies **outside** the activity: "of the actions which have a limit none is an end but all are relative to the end" (1048b18–19). Building reaches completion only when the house stands *apart from* the building, so one cannot "at the same time … be building and have built." The end **cancels** the motion (the house finished, building stops: "the process would have had sometime to cease, as the process of making thin ceases"). "Every movement is incomplete — making thin, learning, walking, building" — an ***energeia atelēs***.
- ***energeia*** (narrow) — the **actuality whose *telos* is internal and co-present**; "that in which the end is present is an action." "At the same time we are seeing and have seen, are understanding and have understood, are thinking and have thought … we are living well and have lived well." The potentiality-*qua*-potentiality is **absorbed into the act, not preserved**; the activity does "not cease." The end **sustains** the activity: seeing does not terminate in sight.

"Of these processes, then, we must call the one set movements, and the other actualities" (1048b28–29): the formula *actuality of the potential qua potential* is the mark of ***kinēsis* alone** — the contrast against which *energeia* (narrow) is set off — **not** a heading standing above both.

Part I §3.2 already grounds the chain in this distinction (*kinēsis* as *energeia atelēs*): its **nodes are the actualities the chain reaches; its links are motions (*kinēseis*), each named from its terminus**. One consequence: **the terminal act A₄ is itself one or the other** — a *poiēsis*, whose end is a product *beyond* the doing (the built fort, the looted reward — a *kinēsis*), or a *praxis*, whose end is *in* the doing (the play, the comportment — an *energeia*). Much of design's work converts the one into the other; and the *technē*-hexis **capture** (§6) is exactly a *kinēsis* whose wished-*telos* has dropped out, so that it runs on without reaching the completion that would cancel it, yet never becomes a self-sustaining *energeia* — an endless restoration (cf. pleasure-as-restoration, itself a *kinēsis* of the soul, *Rhet.* I.11, 1369b34–1370a5; against pleasure as *energeia*, *NE* X).

A change is further specified by its **category** — Step 0's first question:

| Kind of change | Greek | In a designed world |
|---|---|---|
| substance (coming-to-be / passing-away) | *genesis* / *phthora* | crafting/spawning; death — *strictly* a *metabolē*, **not** a *kinēsis* (*Phys.* V.1) |
| quality (alteration) | *alloiōsis* | a state changed — health, attunement, **the player's own *hexis*** |
| quantity (growth / diminution) | *auxēsis* / *phthisis* | leveling, resource accrual |
| place (locomotion) | *phora* | avatar movement, traversal |

**Whose** substance changes must be named. Two series run at once: the **player-substance**, undergoing perceptual→cognitive→dispositional *alloiōsis* that sediments as *hexis* — *this is the chain*; and the **world/avatar-substance**, altered by the player's *poiēsis*. They meet at **A₄**, where one act is at once a *praxis* (it alters the agent) and a *poiēsis* (it alters the world). And the method's central event, *rhetorical incorporation* (§4), re-describes from this angle as the **designed convergence of the two substance-series toward "one in substrate, different in being"** (DA III.7) — a second derivation of the keystone, from substance rather than from union. *(This is also where "ontological change" gains precision: the capstone's supplanting of dispositional ground is an *alloiōsis* of the ethical *hexis* so deep it strains toward — but, per Aristotle, cannot literally be — a *genesis* of new character.)*

**The horizon is a *dwelling*-horizon (dwelling, sublated from Rickert).** The player never opens a pass into neutral space; she is *always already* enworlded — thrown into a designed *Umwelt* that conditions and claims her before any *orekton* solicits her. Rickert, reading Heidegger, names this **dwelling**: "not a worldview but a principle of being … a way of being conditioned and permeated by things so that they are inseparable from what it means to live in the world" (Rickert 2013, p. 223). Dwelling is the *prior* condition the chain presupposes — one cannot take in a world unless one is already underway in one — and it is **ecological and active, not passive**: "an active if conditioned comportment toward the world" (p. 224) in which the player is *bethinged*, "gathered by things" (p. 225) as much as she gathers them. Two cautions fix its place. **(i)** Dwelling answers the **worldhood** question (*how is the player enworlded such that action is possible?*) — which lives at the **horizon (A₀)** and in the **diachronic ground (§7)**, *not* the action question the chain answers; it *enriches* the horizon, it does not replace the chain. **(ii)** The "bethinged" is the **patient-pole** of the very encounter whose **agent-pole** is the actualizing player: the keystone's *"one in substrate, different in being"* (DA III.7) holds **both** — the player is at once gathered-by-world and gathering-world — so RODA keeps the desiring agent that dwelling, taken alone, tends to dissolve. *(That "dwelling is possible anywhere … even in a digital … realm" [p. 246 — ****** page unverified] is what licenses applying it to designed virtual worlds at all.)*

> **The frame is three structures of widening grain (Part I §A₄) — at once an *ontology* of action and a *method* for diagnosing it.** The **four causes** (§2.2) give the general form of explanation; the **three-factor schema** (§2.3) is the bare anatomy of self-movement *within* them (three of the four causes lie inside the three factors, the formal read off their convergence); the **seven causes** (§2.4) individuate, for human action, *which* source moves and *whether* the motion is the agent's own. Nested lenses, not rival accounts.

### 2.2 · The four causes — two analyses, hinged (not a partition)

The four causes are stated twice in the texts the method leans on — *Met.* I.3 (983a24–32: substance/essence · matter/substratum · source-of-change · "that for the sake of which and the good") and *Phys.* II.3 (194b24–195a3). Two features do specific work.

First, the **final cause is the terminus of a continuous change**: "if a thing undergoes a continuous change toward some end, that last stage is actually that for the sake of which" (*Phys.* II.2, 194a28–31). This licenses reading the whole A₀→A₄ chain as **one teleological motion** individuated by its *orekton* — not five separate events.

Second, "there are **several causes of the same thing** (not merely accidentally)" (*Phys.* II.3, 195a4–14): the statue is caused at once by the sculptor (efficient) and the bronze (material) — *one* thing, *all four* causes together. This is the corrective to a tempting error: because the four causes belong to a **single** change, they **cannot be parcelled out between two agents.** **Designing** and **playing** are two different actions, each owed its own complete four whys.

**Two complete four-cause analyses, joined at one hinge:**

| | *Material* | *Formal* | *Efficient* | *Final* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Designer's making** (*poiēsis*) | the medium (engine, assets, systems) | the design (rules + intended-experience-as-form) | the designer | that the game be *played-and-experienced thus* — the player's activity-to-come, **anticipated** |
| **Player's playing** (*praxis*) | the game-as-given (world, avatar, affordances) | the form *she* knows and completes (avatar-form inhabited; rules-as-the-shape-of-good-play) | her own *orexis* | her own *orekton* |

**The hinge** is Aristotle's producing-art / using-art relation (*Phys.* II.2, 194a34–b5): the art that **uses** "knows the form" and "is in a sense directive"; the art that **produces** "knows the matter." The **designer is the producing-art**, the **player the using-art** — and the using-art is **architectonic**: the form of a game is defined by its being-played, so **the player's playing is the for-the-sake-of-which the design serves.** One artifact, two angles — the designer's **end-product** (his final cause) **is** the player's **given means** (her material and formal). *This*, not a split of one act's causes, is what "**what the world *supplies* vs what the player *does***" names: the **material + formal of the player's act are furnished by the designer's making**, while the **efficient + final remain her own.** The using-art's "knowing the form" dignifies the player as **co-author** — the ontological warrant for *phantasia*-conscription and alterbiography (§6).

Two diagnostics fall out. **Final-cause (mis)alignment:** the player's *actual orekton* set against the designer's *anticipated* end — their **coincidence** is a smooth design, the player's end **exceeding** the anticipation is emergent/counter-play, and its **collapse** into the designed end one face of capture. **The occasioning of the act:** the designer cannot be the efficient cause of the player's act (he cannot supply her desire); he **alters her material world** to solicit a response — so "why now" is the designer's manipulation of the material, "which way" her own efficient-and-final (the ontological form of the *felt-vs-structural* axis). And two further features anchor downstream instruments: **reciprocal causation** — "hard work causes fitness and *vice versa* … the one as end, the other as the principle of motion" (195a4–14) — is the **synchronic↔diachronic *hexis* spiral** verbatim (act sediments *hexis*; *hexis* grounds act); and the **potential/actual** cause — "the cause of a house being built is either a house-builder or a house-builder building" (195b4–6) — is the *hexis*/*energeia* distinction at the level of cause (disposition vs its exercise).

### 2.3 · The three-factor schema: where final and efficient meet in the motion

The three-factor schema — **relative unmoved mover → moved mover → moved** — is not a second overlay competing with the four causes; it is **how the efficient and final causes operate dynamically within a motion**. The four causes answer the four *whys* (a static grid); the schema specifies the *mechanism* of an efficient cause in transit. They meet in animal action: the ***orekton*** (final cause) is the **relative unmoved originator** — "itself remaining unmoved, it originates the movement by being apprehended in thought or imagination"; ***orexis*** (efficient cause) is the **moved mover**; the body, via the joint, is **the moved** (DA III.10, 433b10–18). In every motion of the chain, then, final and efficient cause are *the same actualization under two descriptions* — which is why the chain is **read forward (efficiently, link by link) yet understood backward (finally, from the *orekton*)**. Part I §3.1 establishes the relativization that lets the schema recur at every stage, not only at the cosmological first mover.

### 2.4 · The seven causes: individuating the efficient-final complex

When the moving substance is a human being acting, the efficient-final complex is individuated by Aristotle's **seven causes** (*Rhet.* I.10, 1368b33–1369a6): *chance, nature, compulsion, habit, reasoning, anger, appetite*. Their own structure — stated in the text — yields the A₄ causal layer:

```
  involuntary (not due to the agent) ── chance │ necessity { compulsion · nature }
  voluntary   (due to the agent)     ── habit + desire { rational = wishing · irrational = anger · appetite }
```

This **corrects** the casual mapping earlier drafts carried; the four action-types are *derived, not posited*:
- **chance · nature · compulsion (*bia*)** → the **non-agent branch** (chance set against *necessity*, under which Aristotle groups *both* compulsion and nature).
- **habit (*ethos*)** → the **repetition-tendency** through which a ***hexis*** is **cultivated** (not itself the settled dispositional ground — the *hexis* of §7 — which habit forms but is not) — the one voluntary cause that is *not* a species of *orexis*; hence §7, not a Type.
- the three orectic species the text names — **rational desire = wishing (*boulēsis*)**, and the irrational **anger (*thymos*)** and **appetite (*epithymia*)** — are **Types 3, 2, 1**. ("Reasoning," in the seven-fold list, *is* this rational desire: "Rational desire is wishing, and wishing is a desire for good.")
- The fourth kind, the ***prohairetic***, is **not a fourth species of *orexis*** but the **deliberative completion of the bouletic**; it is **not** in the *Rhetorica*'s list — it is supplied from *NE* III.2–3 as **deliberated desire** (choice of means to a wished end), and is latent already in the *Rhetorica*'s gloss of reasoning as "useful either as ends or as contributing to an end" (1369a32–b29). *Boulēsis* wishes the end; *prohairesis* chooses and enacts the means — the **completion, not the peer**, of Type 3. The voluntary, desire-sourced kinds are therefore **three *orexis*-keyed types, the third carried through to choice**, not four coordinate desires.

Two further yields of the same passage become Step-4 instruments. **Voluntariness** turns on the locus of the *archē*: external = *bia* ("compulsion … contrary to the desire or reason of the agents themselves," 1369a32–b29), internal = voluntary (*NE* III.1) — and *all* voluntary action reduces to "the good or the pleasant," i.e. to the *orekton* (the apparent good). And Aristotle's **definitions of the causes are the diagnostic criteria**: *chance* = that "whose cause cannot be determined, that has no purpose … no fixed way" — a definition in-game RNG **fails** (it is an authored distribution), so designed "chance" is *technē* wearing the mask of *tychē*.

### 2.5 · The seat of the chain, and a discipline

The chain (A₀→A₄, §3) is, then, the **interior of the efficient-final cause of voluntary, desire-sourced action** — the magnification of the single causal cell at which a rational animal's own motion is started and directed. This secures its originality: the chain is not a stipulation but the **systematic elaboration of Aristotle's own account of action**, thickened with Heidegger's temporal-dispositional structure (Part I) and turned toward designed worlds. Calleja and Burke are absorbed *within* this frame — Calleja's channels name *where the designed efficient cause presses on the chain*; Burke's pentad names *how the resulting motion's cause is rendered and concealed* (§5).

> **Discipline (so the frame seats rather than swallows).** This apparatus is *general* metaphysics — true of houses and stones no less than players. The method's living work is the phenomenology of the chain and the union; the frame is established **once**, here, and thereafter **invoked only where it does work** — at Step 0 (classify the change; name the substance; distinguish the two four-cause analyses — designing/playing) and at the causal-diagnosis step (the seven causes; voluntariness; the *bia* test). It is **not** a per-beat checklist; running it mechanically would re-import exactly the exhaustiveness the method shed when it demoted the 6×6 grid (Appendix A).

## 3. The generative spine — the actualization of desire (A₀→A₄)

**The chain is the fine structure of the efficient-final cause individuated in §2** — the interior of the one causal slot at which a rational animal's own motion is started and directed; what follows magnifies that slot. Every analyzed behavior is a **pass** (or set of passes) of the chain. The chain is read forward, understood backward; each node is an actuality (an *energeia*), each link a motion (a *kinēsis* named from its terminus, §2.1), and the **object of desire (*orekton*) is the unmoved originator** — "itself remaining unmoved, it originates the movement by being apprehended in thought or imagination" (DA III.10, 433b10–18). The three-factor kinetic schema (*unmoved originator → moved mover [orexis] → moved [the animal via the joint]*) holds at every motion.

| Node | What it is | Greek |
|---|---|---|
| **A₀** | Ontological horizon: sensible object + sense-faculty within motion & time (the **designed *Umwelt***) | *aisthēton + aisthētikon · kinēsis kai chronos* |
| **A₁** | Completed perception + dual residue (form + hedonic tone) | *aisthēsis · resonant aisthēma + resonant epithymia · paschein* |
| **A₂** | The *phantasma* proper — determinate, available, representational charged image (the pivot) | *phantasia / phantasma · "making-present-as"* |
| **A₃** | Completed cognition: intellection / memory / discursive + the orthogonal committal dimension *doxa* | *nous · mnēmē · phantasia bouleutikē · doxa* |
| **A₄** | Completed action (*praxis*); recursive — sediments into *hexis* | *orexis → orektikon → praxis* |

**The three termini of A₄ (Part I §A₄).** The chain comes to rest in three ways: as ***poiēsis*** (a making whose *telos* is a product/*ergon* outlasting the doing, governed by *technē*); as ***praxis*** (a doing whose *telos* is immanent, governed by an ethical *hexis*); and — the *nous*-route's terminus — as ***theōria***, which issues in no deed and is **set aside** here. One deed may be at once *poiēsis* and *praxis*; this cut is the **terminus-level face of the *hexis* bivalence** (§7). Beneath both lies the **floor of action**: bare pre-doxastic movement (the startle, the flinch) in which perception and *epithymia* alone suffice — no *doxa*, no cultivated *how* — the Type-1 limit every richer action presupposes.

**Individuation at A₄ — why *this* action.** The causal layer is the seven causes of *Rhetorica* I.10, fully derived in **§2.4**; in brief it bifurcates:

```
  NON-agent (involuntary) ─ chance (designed RNG) │ necessity { compulsion (bia — force; §6) · nature (reflex / pre-doxastic kritikon recoil) }
  AGENT (voluntary, desire-sourced) ─ habit (the hexis-ground, §7) through which one orectic species fires:
        epithymia → Type 1 Epithymetic · thymos → Type 2 Thymotic · boulēsis → Type 3 Bouletic
        (↳ Type 3 carried through to deliberated choice = *prohairesis*, the bouletic's completion — NE III.2–3; not a fourth species)
```

Most play is **agent-caused** (the player freely actualizing desire = the four types — Burke's "why, given free choice"); the **non-agent branch** is where *the world acts on the player* (designed chance; reflex; and **bia** — the forced/cutscene limit). **The most consequential analytic moments are where action crosses the bar between branches** (a forced cutscene stripping a voluntary agent of agency → §6). The four types are not compartments; they name the **predominant source-desire**; *doxa* cross-cuts all four; civic passions (anger, fear, pity, shame…) are the *species of being-affected* through which a desire is sourced, not a type.

> **Three-orthogonal-axes lock.** The desire-**TYPE** (which *orexis*-species predominantly sources the act), the *poiēsis* / *praxis* **TERMINUS** (whether the end lies beyond the doing or is immanent), and the *doxa*-**RATIFICATION** (whether — and with what content — a *doxa* takes the appearance as true) are **three orthogonal axes, scored INDEPENDENTLY**. Knowing the type settles neither the terminus nor the ratification: an act sprung from any of the desires may be a making or a doing, ratified by *doxa* or not. (This independence is what keeps an intemperance from being mistaken for a compulsion — Section 6 Live l.225 — VERIFIED.)

## 4. The central event — *rhetorical incorporation* (the substantial union, two-poled)

This is the method's original core. At the **A₃→A₄ transition** the chain achieves a **substantial union** structured by Aristotle's *"one in substrate, different in being"* (DA III.7, 431a13–14), extended to **social substance**: at A₃, *doxa* ratifies the *phantasma*-of-acting-together ("WE are doing this"); at M₃→A₄, *orexis* moves toward a **jointly-realized A₄**. The union has **two poles**, one event:

- **World-disclosedness — *incorporation* (re-grounded from Calleja).** The player becomes one-with the world she acts *in*. *Not* "intensified involvement" but the chain's world-axis union: the gameworld assimilated into consciousness **and** the player embodied (via avatar) into the world, **held simultaneously**. Assessed by the **R1–R7 differentiator** (Calleja IG-10, pp. 169–173, 178 — see Appendix B): R1 assimilation · R2 embodiment · R3 simultaneity · R4 spatial-kinesthetic cornerstone · R5 multi-dimensional blending · **R6 temporal-apex** (culmination of the macro→micro arc; intensifies with duration) · **R7 intrusion-integration** (real-world stimuli *blend in* rather than rupture — the porosity, §4.3).
- **Co-disclosedness — *consubstantiation* (re-grounded from Burke).** The player becomes one-with the avatar she acts *as* and the gang/community she acts *with* — "substantially one … yet a distinct locus of motives, both joined and separate" (RoM 21). *Not* symbolic persuasion but the **A₃ doxa-ratified "WE"**: identification is *phantasma*-borne (seeded at A₂), ratified at A₃, consummated at M₃→A₄.

**The two poles are the same actualization under two *logoi*** — my agency / the collective's, the world's pull / my reaching — realized in one act. *That neither Calleja (world-disclosedness only, experientialist grounding) nor Burke (co-disclosedness only, symbolic grounding) sees this — and that the dissertation unifies them through the actualization of desire — is the contribution.*

> **Guard (the firewall, §2.0).** The "substantial union" of *rhetorical incorporation* is **EXPERIENTIAL convergence** — the player becomes one-WITH the world she acts *in* and the others she acts *as* and *with* — **NOT a kind-identity of the virtual with the non-virtual.** A maximally-incorporated player has **not made the virtual non-virtual**; the kind-difference (§2.0) holds beneath any depth of immersion. Read every union-verdict (Step 2) under this constraint.

**The world-disclosedness's ontological content — incorporation *as dwelling* (the re-grounding, filled in).** v3 promised to re-ground incorporation "psychological→ontological"; **dwelling is that ontological content.** The world-disclosedness is the player **dwelling** in the designed *Umwelt* — not "the absorption of a virtual environment into consciousness" (Calleja's cognitocentrist formula, IG-10) but *being constituted with* it. Here RODA is **better-armed than either source**: where Calleja's metaphor runs world→mind and Rickert's corrective runs loosely toward "co-disclosure," the keystone gives the exact structure — incorporation is the player-substance and world-substance becoming **one in substrate, different in being** (DA III.7), the dyadic co-actualization already secured at A₁ by *"the activity of the sensible object and that of the sense is one and the same activity"* (DA III.2). The cognitocentrism Rickert rightly diagnoses in Calleja is thus corrected **without abandoning the agent** — by the Aristotelian non-dualism the method already carries. *Dwelling supplies the ecological thickening; DA III.7 supplies the precision Rickert's "co-disclosure" lacks.*

**Two refinements on the co-disclosedness (from the same integration).** First, the player arrives **divided** — actual body vs avatar — and identification is "compensatory to division" (Burke); that division-and-overcoming is the motive-structure of **R2 embodiment** (the player/avatar split closed), not a separate pillar. Second, the doxa-ratified "WE" is possible at all only because identification's "spaces of possibility [are] hollowed in advance by … a priori affectability" (Rickert 2013, p. 161): the prior **dwelling-horizon (§2.1) grounds the co-disclosedness no less than the world-disclosedness** — which is why both are poles of *one* event.

### 4.1 The union as a *source* of action (the headline instrument)
The union is not merely a *state* the chain reaches; it can be **what sources the next action**. A **Type-2 thymotic** act whose *orekton* is *collective* — "back the gang" — is *sourced by* consubstantiation: the gang's slight becomes mine *because* I am substantially one with them (validated on the RDR2 saloon brawl). "Acting-together makes consubstantial" (Burke) and "*thymos* needs a *toward-whom*" (Aristotle) converge: the keystone is generative, not decorative.

### 4.2 Rupture (the union's negative)
- **Agency-rupture** — the union is *stripped* by *bia* (control seized): R3 simultaneity collapses from "joined-and-separate" to "acted-upon." (The rape-scene cutscene; G&G's miniaturization is its *wondrous* sibling — a designed dissimilitude that ruptures the for-the-sake-of-which without trauma.)
- **Social/consubstantiation-rupture** — the collective doxastically re-ratifies the player as a distinct/subordinate locus (the WoW loot-passing-over): the next pass's A₁ *resonant epithymia* shifts from "we" to "I-against-them."
- **The union is never a fixed possession (dwelling's never-stilled ontology).** Beyond these *acute* ruptures there is a *chronic* instability: dwelling is "an ongoing and never stilled process" (Rickert 2013, p. 246 [****** page unverified]), so incorporation is an accomplishment **held against constant slippage** — exactly what Calleja observes as incorporation "slipping back into involvement" the moment any dimension demands full conscious attention. Rupture is the acute failure; this is the standing condition that makes pacing and re-attunement perpetual design problems (and R6 temporal-apex a *won*, not a *given*, state).

### 4.3 Porosity → the virtual→actual carry-over (why ontological change is possible)
Because the union is **bidirectional habitation, not a sealed plunge**, real-world stimuli *blend in* rather than shattering it (R7; Calleja's anti-magic-circle, IG-10 pp. 171–172). Scaled up and sedimented, this porosity *is* the channel by which what happens in the game **crosses into the player's real-world being-in-the-world** (Calleja's continuity thesis, p. 179) — the ontological-change mechanism the capstone needs. **This is where dwelling discharges its largest debt to the method:** Rickert's thesis that *"rhetoric … transforms our fundamental disposition concerning how we are in the world, how we dwell"* (Rickert 2013, Introduction) **is** the virtual→actual carry-over stated as rhetorical theory — the rhetorical-ontological warrant the capstone would otherwise have to borrow from Aristotelian habituation alone. So "rhetorical" in ***rhetorical incorporation*** now does **triple duty**: consubstantial (Burke), dwelling-transforming (Rickert), desire-actualizing (the chain).

**Existence-fragility (imported from Part II §2.0).** The player-facing **virtual / phantasmic object is renderer-dependent**: it exists **only DURING active rendering**. Power-loss, program-close, or attention-shift makes it **CEASE** — not merely dim, but pass out of being **as a perceptible object** (an **ontological cessation event**), while its **digital / resonant GROUND persists** in machine-memory or person-memory. The analyst should therefore **model immersion-breaks and disruption as ontological cessation**, not as a degree-of-immersion change, and **distinguish causal-historical dependence from ongoing ontological dependence** (the non-virtual apple is merely *causally* derived from its tree and persists without it; the virtual / phantasmic apple is *ontologically* dependent on its renderer at every instant it exists). ## 6. The method's own instruments (the emergent originality — belonging to no source)

These are the concepts RODA generates that are neither Calleja's, nor Burke's, nor plain Aristotle's. They are the analytic's distinctive yield:

1. **The union as a source of action** (§4.1) — consubstantiation/incorporation *sourcing* a chain-pass, not just crowning one.
2. **Phantasia-conscription** — when a world *withholds* depiction, it supplies a deliberately **under-determined *phantasma*** at A₂ and conscripts the player's own *phantasia* to author the unshown; *what* gets authored is fixed by her settled *doxai*/*hexis* (adult ratifies "rape," child "robbery"). Withholding can be *more* forceful than showing — the player co-authors her own experience. (*enargeia* is its positive-space twin.)
3. **The agent→patient (*praxis*→*paschein*) inversion** — *bia* forces a **de-actualization**: it drags the player from the A₄ agent-pole back to the A₁ patient-pole, as **destructive *paschein* (*phthora*)**, not the preservative *paschein* (*sōtēria*) of ordinary perception (the two modes of being-affected, *DA* II.5 417b2–5 / Part I §1.2: maintenance-of-the-potential vs extinction-by-a-contrary). The structural account of a scene's ontological violence.
4. **The two compulsions** — *bia* (external force; non-agent branch; the cutscene) **vs** *technē*-hexis **capture** (habituated runaway where the wished-end drops out; the agent branch; the grind/dark-pattern). Kept strictly distinct (v1 conflated them); the *energeia*/*kinēsis* frame (§2.1) names the difference precisely — *bia* annuls the *energeia* of agency, capture is a *kinēsis* whose *telos* has dropped out. *Unskippable **push** narrative = push-at-the-bia-limit* (§5.1). **Grounded in Part I §A₄** (intemperance ≠ compulsion): the runaway is *voluntary* — its *archē* the agent's own appetite/*hexis* — so it is **not** *bia*. "Capture" covers two such voluntary runaways: the *technē*-routine whose wished-end has dropped out (the grind) and the *epithymetic* intemperance (the addict's appetite).
5. ***Hexis*-engineering (skill ≠ wisdom, by design)** — a world cultivates *technē*-hexis (procedural transparency, prompted mechanically) and/or ethical *hexis* (the evaluative "how," staged narratively); the bivalence is the method's primary diagnostic of what a design *makes of a player* (§7).
6. **The 2D↔VR media differential** — VR doesn't change the chain; it intensifies *how it is entered*: A₀ sensory **supplanting** (HMD), A₁ **symbiotic** bodily kinesthesis (room-scale 1:1), **literal** R2 embodiment, and **veri(dis)similitude** (verisimilar enough to throw-in, dissimilar enough to have ontological force; the dissimilar shock = a designed incorporation-rupture). **The differential turns on the *data-discrepancy / sensory-bandwidth* axis (the explicit metaphysics→method handoff).** Sort media and environments by **sensory bandwidth** — the non-virtual standing at the limit of *full sensorial actuality*, the virtual and the phantasmic each a *narrower renderer-actualized bandwidth* — and use the axis to **rank felt-reality and predict affective intensity** (each progressive increase in sensorial data amplifies the viscerally felt response: the D-Day photo < the documentary < the *Saving Private Ryan* clip). Model also the **apportionment of rendering labor** between medium-supplied content and *phantasia* gap-filling: the gap between what a medium presents and the full sensorial field of its referent is filled by *phantasia* conscripting the backlog of prior perception — an **inverse *phantasia*-load** (larger data-discrepancy → more *phantasia* work; smaller → less). Because *phantasia* scales, **both 2D and VR can reach full incorporation** — the axis is a *route*, not a *reachability*, distinction. (REWRITE-v1 §3 l.208, l.218 — VERIFIED.)
7. **The virtual→actual carry-over** (§4.3) — the ontological-change mechanism: sedimented union + porosity supplant the prior dispositional ground.
8. **Recalcitrance — *thing* vs *object* (a design diagnostic; sublated from Rickert/Heidegger).** Does the designed environment afford genuine **dwelling** — making *claims*, pushing back, mattering beyond use — or does it serve **objects on demand** (teleportation, omniscient maps, invisible walls), a "navigable database"? In four-cause terms this is the **material cause's resistance**; at the limit, *bia* (the rape-scene) is the environment making a claim the player cannot refuse. (Take the thing/object distinction only this far — *not* into the Fourfold; §5.3.)
9. **The foreclosed rupture (the *Gestell* limit; the disruption/change axis).** This is the method's **disruption / CHANGE axis** — it gives ontological change its *negative / limit* case, the complement to the positive carry-over of #7. **The failure mode:** a **total verisimilitude that CONCEALS the kind-difference** (§2.0) forecloses the **dis-similarity (rupture)** that would individualize the player out of fallen absorption — so where rupture is foreclosed, absorbed routine **deepens** (the *technē*-runaway / intemperance of #4) and the **ethical *hexis* goes unexercised**. **The positive content:** rupture (dis-similarity) is the **condition for unconcealing** and for **individualizing the player out of fallen absorption** — this is what the G&G miniaturization keystone delivers, the *wondrous* designed dissimilitude that re-grounds the player's transparently-held constitution of her being-in-the-world rather than expelling her (re-grounding the existing `gg-RODA-matrix` worksheet result — the miniaturization "unconceals the contingency of the player's ordinary scale/orientation," a *productive* rupture — onto the metaphysics' *Gestell*-concealment-of-the-kind-difference framing; this lifts that worksheet's analysis into the canonical method). **The guard:** the VR is **NOT itself "inauthentic"** — inauthenticity is a mode of *Dasein*, not a property of an object; the risk lies in the **foreclosed rupture**, not in the virtuality. ## 7. The dispositional ground and the two registers

**The player never enters as a blank slate.** Every pass is conditioned by prior *doxai*, attunements, and especially the bivalent ***hexis*** (the habit-cause of §2.4, the *archē* of the agent-branch):
- ***technē*-hexis** — cultivated procedural transparency (controller fluency, parry timing, rotations); *reduces deliberation*; withdraws like ready-to-hand equipment.
- **ethical *hexis*** — the socially/evaluatively inflected "how" of being-in-action (what one takes as fitting, shameful, cruel, honorable); *holds-oneself-open*; exercises a two-level reach (modulates A₁ basic-valence + A₃ doxastic ratification). Names the **skill ≠ wisdom** distinction precisely.

**Three *doxai*, told apart (Part I).** The method must distinguish three layers of *doxa* and **trace which layer supplies a given evaluative aspect**: the **RESONANT** *doxa* (the standing, encultured opinion — a *having-been*: that an unreturned greeting *counts as* a slight at all); the **OCCURRENT** ratification (the involuntary, immediate *taking-as-true-of-this-now* — "this, to me, is a slight"); and the **DELIBERATIVE** *doxa* (later reflection, which may confirm or overturn the first two). Immediate anger **draws on the resonant and consists in the occurrent**, and does *not* wait upon the deliberative — which is why an affect can appear to spring from sheer appearance. And **that an emotion lapses on a change of the deliberative *doxa*** (catch sight of the earbuds, reason "no slight," the anger lapses) **is the surest sign a *doxa* was constitutive throughout**. The resonant layer is precisely what *phantasia*-conscription (§6.2) silently draws on. (Part I — DA III.11 434a5–11; DA III.3 427b21–24 — both VERIFIED.)

**Two registers, three rigorously-separate loops.** The two registers are still **synchronic** (one directional pass A₀→A₄ within a bounded episode) and **diachronic** (across passes). But within and across them run **three loops that must never be conflated:**
- **(1) The WORLD-loop — A₄→A₀′ (cross-event).** A completed act alters the world/avatar-substance, so the next pass opens on a changed horizon; this is **world-change** (alterbiography re-enters here, §5.1).
- **(2) The DISPOSITION-loop — A₄→*hexeis*→inputs-to-the-next-pass — deposited TYPED BY ROUTE.** How completed actions **sediment into *hexis*** and recondition future passes (the **reciprocal causation** of §2.2: act as efficient → *hexis* as end → *hexis* as principle of the next motion). The deposit is typed by the terminus that produced it: ***poiēsis* → *technē*-hexis**; **immanent *praxis* → ethical *hexis***; **pre-doxastic appetite → temperance / intemperance residue**. Its two mechanisms are **Stimmung-saturation** (affective history biases basic valence at A₁ — "the tone") and **corrective-faculty impairment** (states bias *doxa* at M₂→A₃ — "the figure," re-entering at the *forming* phantasma M₁→A₂).
- **(3) The intra-event EMOTION-feedback loop (the pathetic loop).** *Within a single pass*, an aroused emotion **impairs the corrective faculty** — "the faculty in virtue of which the controlling sense judges is not identical with that in virtue of which images come before the mind" (*De Insomniis* 460b16–18 — VERIFIED) — so the **inflected *phantasma* is taken-as-true unopposed** and the emotion **escalates**. This is v6's corrective-faculty-impairment content, re-filed as a loop *distinct from* (2): it is intra-event, not cross-pass.

> **Conflation guard.** The world-loop (1), the disposition-loop (2), and the intra-event emotion-loop (3) are **three different feedback structures** — do not collapse the emotion-loop into the diachronic disposition-loop (v6 folded it there), nor read disposition-sedimentation as world-change.

The chain therefore **spirals**, not merely iterates; this is what makes design pacing consequential and ontological change possible. (Feed-forward across passes — "repeated … action sediments experience": *Met* I.1 980b28–981a12 — VERIFIED; the intra-event emotion loop: *De Insomniis* 460b16–18 — VERIFIED.)

## 8. The protocol (chain-generative — 8 steps, 0–7)

> The order *enacts the architecture*: frame the motion, trace the actualization, diagnose the union, then read motive, cause, and sediment. The §2 frame is invoked at Step 0 and Step 4 only. Calleja's channels and Burke's motive-rhetoric appear **as sub-moments of chain-steps, never as separate passes.** Output: a chain-trace, a union-verdict, a one-paragraph diagnosis, and (diachronic) a carry-over forecast.

- **Step 0 — Frame (with the ontological seat, §2).** Scope/circumference; **scale** (discrete / protracted / ontological-change); **entry point** (A₀-sense / A₂-imagination / A₃-thought); register; **name the *orekton*** — individual or **collective/identificatory** (the latter signals a union-sourced act). Then the §2 framing where it earns its place: **classify the change** (substance/quality/quantity/place) and the **substance(s)** undergoing it (player-series / world-series); note the **motion-form** (*praxis*/*poiēsis*); where the supply/agency question is live, **distinguish the two four-cause analyses** (designing vs playing) at their producing-art/using-art hinge. **Place the engaged objects in their KIND** (non-virtual / virtual / phantasmic, §2.0) **and name the renderer; the convergence ≠ identity firewall governs every union-verdict downstream.**
- **Step 1 — Trace the actualization.** Walk A₀→A₄ for the episode; at each motion fill the three-factor schema; note **what the designed world supplies vs what the player does** (the designer's *making* furnishes the player's material + formal; the efficient + final are the player's own — §2.2). *Name a Calleja channel only where the world is applying designed pressure at that stage* (§5.1).
- **Step 2 — Diagnose the union (*rhetorical incorporation*).** Assess **both poles** at A₃→A₄: world-disclosedness via **R1–R7**; co-disclosedness via **consubstantiation** (with what/whom; genuine / strategic / ruptured). Verdict: union **achieved / partial / merely-involved / ruptured** (agency or social). **Decisive question: does the union *source* the action (§4.1)?**
- **Step 3 — Read the motive-rhetoric (Burke, absorbed).** Dominant ratio; what the player **attributes** the action to; which dispositional source is **concealed**; attitude register; any hierarchy/mystery/courtship or victimage.
- **Step 4 — Causal & type diagnosis (the individuation of the efficient-final complex, §2.4).** 7-causes (agent / non-agent / **bia**): involuntary {chance │ necessity{compulsion·nature}} vs voluntary {habit-as-ground + an orectic species}; if agent → predominant **type**: the three *orexis*-keyed kinds (*epithymia*=1 · *thymos*=2 · *boulēsis*=3), with *prohairesis* = **Type 3 carried through to deliberated choice** (its completion, *NE* III.2–3 — not a fourth coordinate type). **Voluntariness** = locus of the *archē* (the *bia* test: "contrary to the desire or reason of the agent") with the **felt-vs-structural** axis (mechanically chosen / execution-only-foreclosed / framing-only); **hexis-mode** (*technē* / ethical / capture).
- **Step 5 — Phantasia & affective specifics.** **Phantasia-conscription** (withheld content authored by *hexis*/*doxa*? — trace *which doxa-layer* (§7: resonant / occurrent / deliberative) supplies each evaluative aspect); pathetic-loop effects (tone @M₀→A₁ · figure @M₁→A₂ · gate @A₂→A₃); **agent→patient inversion** if *bia*. **Classify the cognitive engagement among the three non-committal orientational modes** — *intellection* (atemporal universal) · *memory* (retrospective, *qua*-past) · *deliberative-discursive* (prospective) — **index it to a temporal orientation, and keep it strictly separate from the orthogonal involuntary *doxa*** (the truth-commitment): the mode is *how* the soul is cognitively oriented; the *doxa* is *whether* it takes the appearance as true. (Structure lifted from `ontological-wiring-A0-A4.md` §2, **with correction** per Delta C2 — its stale "Type 4 / four-type" framing is *not* imported; *prohairesis* remains the bouletic's completion.)
- **Step 6 — Diachronic & carry-over.** Which *hexis* sediments; Stimmung/*doxai* for the next pass; **(ontological scale)** the **virtual→actual carry-over** + the **supplanting-of-dispositional-ground verdict**.
- **Step 7 — Diagnosis.** One paragraph naming: the union-state + *whether it sourced the act*; the action-type & cause; the attitude/hexis register; the *hexis* sedimented; the downstream/carry-over.
