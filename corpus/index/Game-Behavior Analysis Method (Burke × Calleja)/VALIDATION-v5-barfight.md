# v5 Re-Validation: the RDR2 Saloon Brawl through ADA v5 (dwelling-integrated)
### Does the dwelling integration earn its keep — even on its *least* favorable case?

**Date:** 2026-06-09 · **Method:** `CANONICAL-METHODOLOGY-v5.md` · **Baselines:** `VALIDATION-v4-barfight.md` (the v4 run, unchanged) + `VALIDATION-v3-barfight-comparison.md` · **Evidence:** the Valentine saloon brawl, 148s–341s (as documented in the prior runs; `/tmp/rdr2-preflight/` cleared by an overnight reboot).

> **What this tests.** v5 changed *nothing* of v4's substance; it **sublated Rickert's dwelling** into the world-face / A₀-horizon. The honest question is narrow and deliberately unfavorable: on a *discrete, agent-centered, union-sourced* beat — **not** dwelling's home turf — does dwelling **add signal**, or is it overhead? (Dwelling's biggest gifts are aimed at the **G&G VR** case and the **capstone**; the brawl is the stress test on the *least* favorable scale.)

## Part 1 — where dwelling enters the brawl (the v5 increments over v4)

The entire v4 run stands unchanged — a **Type-2 thymotic act sourced by the gang-consubstantiation**, an *alloiōsis* of combat-*hexis*, technē-hexis cultivated live + the ethical-hexis mercy fork. *(Four-cause re-validation, 2026-06-09 — §2.2 corrected: the old "designer/player partition" is superseded by **two analyses, hinged**. The player's playing has all four causes [material = the given world; formal = the melee enacted; efficient = the player's own spirited desire; final = back the gang]; the designer's making **furnished** that material + formal and **anticipated** the end. The sharper finding: the designer's anticipated end and the player's actual one **coincide** here — a tutorial built so the player wants exactly what it teaches — which is precisely why the brawl is frictionless-but-foreclosed. No analytic content lost.)* v5 adds **four** things, all at the horizon / world-face:

1. **The dwelling-horizon grounds the union-source (§2.1 / A₀).** v4 established that the brawl is *sourced by* the consubstantial union with the gang. v5 explains *why that union can fire at all*, and so fast: Arthur is **already dwelling** with the gang — the camp, the shared history, the frontier *Umwelt* — *before* Bill's shove. The "WE" is "hollowed in advance by … a priori affectability" (Rickert 2013, p. 161): the consubstantiation is not assembled from scratch in the saloon; it is **drawn from a prior enworldment**. This is a real deepening — it accounts for the *unreflective speed* with which Arthur wades in (he does not deliberate his way into the "WE"; he is already in it). *Dwelling supplies the ground the union-source presupposes.*

2. **Recalcitrance shows why the brawl feels *embodied*, not cinematic (§6.8 — the new diagnostic).** The muddy street, the fists, the bottles, the crowd's bodily pressure, and above all the **grapple ("BREAK FREE [F]")** that *pushes back* are **things, not objects** — they make claims, resist, demand response. This **recalcitrance** is what makes the brawl *dwelt* rather than merely watched. The diagnostic's discriminating power is in the counterfactual: had the fight been an instant-win or a frictionless cutscene (an "object on demand"), the world-face would collapse — the player would *occupy* the scene, not *dwell* in it. A precise split the diagnostic makes visible: the **outcome is foreclosed** (scripted to win) — *low recalcitrance at the final-cause level* — even as the moment-to-moment combat is *high recalcitrance at the material/kinesthetic level*. (This sharpens v4's felt-vs-structural axis with an ontological grain.)

3. **The *bethinged* names the grapple's micro-inversion exactly (§2.1 patient-pole).** v4 flagged the grapple as a "micro agent→patient inversion." v5 sharpens it: the grapple is a momentary **bethinging** — the world *gathers* Arthur, claims him, holds him — held *with* the agent-pole (he mashes free) in "one substrate, two in being." The brawl thus rehearses, in miniature and without trauma, the agent/patient structure the **capstone will rupture** at full force.

4. **The never-stilled union explains the brawl's *building, fleeting* incorporation (§4.2).** v4 noted R6 temporal-apex is "only partial" (early tutorial). v5 supplies the ontology: incorporation is **never a fixed possession** but "an ongoing and never stilled process" (Rickert 2013, p. 246), held against constant slippage. The brawl's union is *being won*, not *had* — which is precisely why it is intense-but-fragile and lapses the moment the beat ends. (R6 temporal-apex is thereby a *won* state, not a *given* one.)

## Part 2 — does dwelling earn its keep here?

**Honest verdict: yes — but modestly, and that is the correct result.** On the brawl, dwelling adds a genuine increment (the prior-dwelling ground of the consubstantiation; the recalcitrance that makes the beat embodied; the bethinged micro-inversion; the never-stilled union) — but it does **not** transform the reading the way the §2 four-cause frame did in v4. That is exactly what the **discipline rule (§2.5, §5.3)** predicts: dwelling is invoked *only where it earns its place*, and a discrete, agent-sourced brawl is **not** where dwelling is most load-bearing.

**Where dwelling's keep is large (and rightly reserved):**
- **Gnomes & Goblins (the VR comparative case):** open/exploratory incorporation, *dwelling in a virtual world*, the environment's "affordances and recalcitrances," and Rickert's "dwelling is possible … even in a digital … realm" (p. 246) — here dwelling is the **primary lens**, not a supplement, and the recalcitrance diagnostic does heavy work (VR room-scale 1:1 raises recalcitrance to the literal).
- **The capstone (the rape scene):** the carry-over **is** "rhetoric … transforms our fundamental disposition … how we dwell" (the §4.3 warrant); the supplanting of the dispositional ground **is** a change in how the player dwells; *bia* **is** the environment making a claim that cannot be refused — recalcitrance at the limit, the bethinged become total.

**No v4 loss; no bloat.** The four-cause analysis, the union-as-source, the corrected causal derivation all stand untouched; dwelling sits *beneath* them at the horizon, invoked at A₀ and §6.8 only — never as a checklist.

**Verdict: v5 earns its keep.** Dwelling adds genuine signal **even on the least favorable beat**, while its full payoff is held for the cases built to need it — and the discipline rule keeps it from inflating the method. The sublation holds: dwelling deepened the world-face's ontology without displacing the chain or dissolving the agent. **Proceed to propagate (matrix / diagram / brief).**
