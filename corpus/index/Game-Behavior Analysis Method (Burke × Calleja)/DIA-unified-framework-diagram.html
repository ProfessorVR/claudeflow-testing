Four Types of Action at A₄ — individuated by the species of orexis that sources them

  1. Type 1 · Epithymetic (epithymia) — the pleasant/painful. The only type that can fire pre‑doxastically — the perceptual kritikon alone (DA III.7 431a8–12).
  2. Type 2 · Thymotic (thymos) — honor, slight, self‑assertion. Always doxastic; irreducibly social (a toward‑whom, Mitsein).
  3. Type 3 · Bouletic (boulēsis) — rational wish for the end. Always doxastic; may reach impossibles / what depends on others.
  4. Type 4 · Prohairetic (prohairesis) — deliberate choice of means. Always doxastic; bounded by “in our power” (NE III.3 1112a31).

Civic passions (anger, fear, pity, shame, indignation, envy, emulation, kindness, friendship) form no type of their own: they are the species of being‑affected through which one of the four desires gets sourced (§1.4).
