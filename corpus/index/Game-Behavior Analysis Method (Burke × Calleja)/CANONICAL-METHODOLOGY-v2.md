## 0. What the Method is, in one paragraph

The Method analyzes user behavior in game worlds as **the actualization of desire** — the movement from perceptual encounter to completed action (the A₀→A₄ chain of the dissertation's rhetorical ontology) — and reads that movement through two instruments: **Calleja's Player Involvement Model**, which decomposes the designed structures of involvement and measures their lived convergence as **incorporation**; and **Burke's dramatistics**, which diagnoses **motive** (the pentad + ratios) and the **identification/consubstantiality** that binds the player to avatar, world, and others. The chain is the spine (the ontology of *why and how* any action happens); Calleja is the instrument of *structure and presence*; Burke is the instrument of *motive*. The Method runs in two registers — **synchronic** (one pass through the chain, within an episode) and **diachronic** (how completed actions sediment into **hexis** and recondition future passes) — which is what lets it analyze a discrete event, a protracted sequence, and genuine ontological change with one apparatus.

## 1. Architecture: one spine, two instruments

**1.1 Spine — the actualization of desire (A₀→A₄).** Every analyzed behavior is a pass (or set of passes) through the chain:

| Node | What it is | Greek / key terms |
|---|---|---|
| **A₀** | Ontological horizon: the dyadic first actuality of sensible object + sense-faculty, within motion & time | *aisthēton + aisthētikon*; *kinēsis kai chronos* |
| **A₁** | Completed perception + the dual residue it deposits | *aisthēsis*; *resonant aisthēma* (formal) + *resonant epithymia* (hedonic tone); *paschein* |
| **A₂** | The *phantasma* proper — determinate, available, representational charged image (the pivot) | *phantasia* / *phantasma*; "making-present-as" |
| **A₃** | Completed cognitive actuality: the three orientational modes (intellection, memory, discursive) + the orthogonal committal dimension *doxa* | *nous* / *mnēmē* / *phantasia bouleutikē*; *doxa* (taking-as-true) |
| **A₄** | Completed action (*praxis*); recursive — feeds back into *hexis* | *orexis* → *orektikon* → *praxis*; the four types |

Driving force: **the object of desire (*to orekton*) is the unmoved originator** — "that which, itself remaining unmoved, originates the movement by being apprehended in thought or imagination" (DA III.10, 433b10–18). At each motion the **three-factor kinetic schema** holds: *unmoved originator → moved mover (orexis) → moved (the animal via the joint)*.

**1.2 Instrument 1 — Calleja (structure + presence).** Decomposes the game and the player's interaction into **six involvement dimensions** — kinesthetic, spatial, shared, narrative, affective, ludic — each across a **macro** (off-session) and **micro** (in-session) phase; and supplies **incorporation** (the R1–R7 rubric, §4) as the measure of presence — the lived convergence of those dimensions with the chain. *Subordinate but indispensable:* Calleja names the **designed conditions** under which the chain is engineered, not the source of action.

**1.3 Instrument 2 — Burke (motive).** Diagnoses **why** the player acts when given free choice: the **pentad** (Act, Scene, Agent, Agency, Purpose) + the sixth transverse term **Attitude**; the **ratios** between terms (the motive-engine); and **identification/consubstantiality** — "substantially one … yet a distinct locus of motives, both joined and separate" (RoM 21) — the bond mechanism beneath incorporation and beneath collective doxa-formation. *Diagnostic, not foundational:* Burke names how motive is lived and afterward rendered intelligible, not the ontology of action.

**1.4 The discipline.** Keep the registers distinguishable on the page: the chain is ontology (the dissertation's own construction); Calleja's and Burke's *own* claims are source material; every binding *between* the three and every game-behavioral application is the dissertation's **anticipatory projection** (see §9).

## 2. The causation layer — why *this* action (Aristotle's 7 causes, Rhetoric I.10)

The master typology of *why an action occurs*, which **bifurcates** cleanly and is the bridge between Burke's grammar of motive and the chain's four types:

```
                 ┌─ chance (tychē)        ─ designed RNG / emergent events
   NOT agent-    ├─ nature (physis)       ─ given capacities; hardwired reflex / pre-doxastic kritikon recoil
   caused        └─ COMPULSION (bia)      ─ FORCE: agent contributes nothing; acted-upon (see §6a)
   ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   AGENT-caused  ┌─ habit (hexis/ethos)   ─ the dispositional ground (technē- vs ethical-hexis; §7)
   (= voluntary, ├─ reasoning (boulēsis)  ─→ Type 3 Bouletic
    desire-      ├─ anger (thymos)        ─→ Type 2 Thymotic
    sourced)     └─ appetite (epithymia)  ─→ Type 1 Epithymetic
                    + prohairesis         ─→ Type 4 Prohairetic (deliberate choice of means)
```

**This is the methodological payoff of the 7 causes:** most gameplay is **agent-caused** = the player freely actualizing desire = the **four types of action** (this is Burke's "why players do what they do when given free choice"). The **non-agent branch** is where the *game acts on the player*: designed **chance** (loot tables, procedural events), **nature** (reflex/the startle-flinch floor), and **compulsion/*bia*** (forced sequences, unskippable cutscenes). A complete behavioral diagnosis names which cause is operative — and the most consequential analytic moments are where action **crosses the bar** between the branches (e.g., a forced cutscene stripping a voluntary agent of agency: §6a, §6e).

### 2.1 The four types of action (the agent-caused branch, individuated by source-desire)

| Type | Source-desire | Signature | Doxastic? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 Epithymetic** | *epithymia* (appetite) | pursue pleasant / avoid painful; has a pre-doxastic floor (the *kritikon* startle-recoil, DA III.7 431a8–12) | the **only** type that can fire pre-doxastically (also runs doxastically) |
| **2 Thymotic** | *thymos* (spirit/honor) | answers a slight, asserts the self; irreducibly social (*toward-whom*, *Mitsein*) | always doxastic |
| **3 Bouletic** | *boulēsis* (rational wish) | wish for the end-as-good; may reach toward impossibles | always doxastic |
| **4 Prohairetic** | *prohairesis* (deliberate choice) | choice of means to a wished end; bounded by "what is in our power" (NE III.3 1112a31) | always doxastic |

Not mutually exclusive compartments — they name the **predominant source-desire**; one episode may chain several (pity → bouletic wish to help → prohairetic choice of means). **Civic passions** (anger, fear, pity, shame, indignation, envy, emulation, kindness) are **not** a type — they are the *species of being-affected* through which one of the four desires gets sourced. *Doxa* is **not a fifth source**; it cross-cuts all four.

## 3. Instrument 1 detail — Calleja: structural decomposition

Map the six dimensions × macro/micro, each with **intensity** (low/med/high), **internalization trajectory** (conceptual→inhabited; conscious→fluent; scripted→alterbiographic), and **evidence** (the observation/transcript/frame datum). Anchor each to its chain locus (the crosswalk, §5):

- **Kinesthetic** — control-as-motor-mediation; Calleja's symbolic→mimetic→**symbiotic** continuum. Laid down as A₁ fluency-residue; internalized = *technē*-hexis. (In VR: the symbiotic pole is bodily-literal — §6f.)
- **Spatial** — environment internalized as habitable place (the *inhabitation criterion*). Seated at A₀ horizon + A₁ inhabitation.
- **Shared** — co-/counter-agents; cohabitation→cooperation→competition. Instantiates at A₂ (*phantasma* of role/other) + A₃ (doxa-ratified collective agent).
- **Narrative** — two poles (Calleja IG-07): **scripted narrative** (designer-authored — cut scenes, quest text, dialog, in-world objects, audio diaries) ↔ **alterbiography** (the player's own emergent story, generated in the micro phase, p. 131). Key dynamic: **scripted-becomes-alterbiography** (designed content absorbed into the player's story — e.g. *Bioshock*'s Steinman foreshadowing → the scripted surgery absorbed into the playthrough). Calleja further distinguishes (after **Levine 2008**) *how* scripted narrative is delivered — the analytic that **identifies the kinds of narrative event**:
  - **Push narrative** — captive-audience delivery; the **cut scene** is the paradigm, *suspending player action to deliver story*. **Purpose-saturated, Act-constraining** (Burke). At its limit an *unskippable* cut scene shades into **bia** (§6a): the player is acted-upon — *the rape-scene cutscene is push-at-the-bia-limit*.
  - **Pull narrative** — player-elicited; designer-embedded elements (*Bioshock* audio diaries, *Oblivion* in-world books, environmental storytelling) the player must **seek and integrate**; missing them is a feature, not a defect. **Releases Purpose to player-Act** (Type 3/4); the synthetic work of integrating pulled material *is* the alterbiography. (Uexküll: pulled = active *Bedeutungsträger*; pushed cut scenes = non-*Bedeutungsträger* interruptions.)
  - *Chain/Burke:* push/scripted → designer supplies the *phantasma* at A₂/A₃ (Burke **Purpose**, Act-constraining); pull → the player's *orexis* drives the seeking → A₄ (Burke **Act + Purpose**); alterbiography accumulates diachronically (A₄→A₀′). *Provenance: scripted/alterbiography/push/pull = `calleja-direct` (IG-07); the Burke/Uexküll/chain mappings = `anticipatory-projection`.*
- **Affective** — emotional-dispositional tone. Saturates from A₁ as basic valence; concretizes at A₃ as civic passion.
- **Ludic** — **split** (the fusion's refinement): the rule-*system* (mechanics/economy) = Agency/A₃-intellection; the *choosing-under-repercussion* = Act+Purpose / A₃-deliberative→A₄.

## 4. The keystone — incorporation ↔ consubstantiation (read across the whole apparatus)

Calleja's **incorporation** and Burke's **consubstantiation** name the same structural event from two sides, grounded ontologically in Aristotle's **"one substrate, two in being"** (DA III.7 431a13–14) reaching its **social-substantial** articulation at the **A₃→A₄ substantial union**: at A₃, *doxa* ratifies the *phantasma*-of-acting-together ("WE are doing this"); at M₃→A₄, *orexis* moves toward a jointly-realized A₄. The rubric maps node-by-node — **R1–R5** = the consubstantiation↔incorporation homology; **R6–R7** = incorporation-only extensions (no consubstantiation counterpart):

| Rubric | Burke side | Chain locus |
|---|---|---|
| **R1 Assimilation** (world → consciousness) | "substantially one" | A₁ (*resonant aisthēma*) |
| **R2 Embodiment** (player → world via avatar) | "distinct locus / the avatar" | A₂→A₃ (*phantasma*-of-avatar, doxa-ratified) |
| **R3 Simultaneity** (joined *and* separate) | "both joined and separate" | A₃→A₄ (one substrate, two in being) |
| **R4 Spatial-kinesthetic cornerstone** | acting-together | A₀→A₁ (inhabitation + fluency) |
| **R5 Blending** (4–5 dimensions co-active) | ultimate identification | A₃ (woven into one cognitive actuality) |
| **R6 Temporal-apex** (culmination of the macro→micro arc; intensifies with duration, p. 178) | — *(incorporation-only; cf. Burke **entelechy**, the drive-to-completion)* | **diachronic register §7**; incorporation peaks at A₄ *as the arc's culmination*, not cold |
| **R7 Intrusion-integration** (real-world stimuli **blend in** rather than rupture; contra the magic circle, pp. 171–172) | — *(incorporation-only; a porosity consubstantiation doesn't address)* | keystone **robustness** = inverse of incorporation-rupture (§6c); anchors the **virtual→actual carry-over** (Layer 5 — the continuity thesis, p. 179) |

> **Provenance of the R1–R7 rubric.** Not invented here — carried from the Phase-2/3 crosswalk (`validated-crosswalk.json`, `validation-report.md`) and **text-anchored to Calleja, IG-10 "From Immersion to Incorporation," pp. 169–173, 178**. **R1 assimilation + R2 embodiment** = his *double-axis* (VE-into-consciousness / player-into-VE-via-avatar, p. 169). **R3–R7** = his own five-component "incorporation-vs-merely-involved" differentiator (*"failure on any reduces the state to involvement"*): **R3 simultaneity** (p. 169 — "the strictest gatekeeper"); **R4 cornerstone** (spatial+kinesthetic minimum-pair, p. 170); **R5 blending** (multi-dimensional, pp. 171–172); **R6 temporal-apex** (macro→micro culmination, p. 178); **R7 intrusion-integration** (real-world stimuli blend in, pp. 171–172). The underlying criteria are `calleja-direct`; the R1–R7 numbering/checklist is the dissertation's operationalization. **R6–R7 are incorporation-only** (no consubstantiation counterpart); **R7 is the per-moment seed of the continuity thesis (p. 179)** — virtual environments "continuous with the media-saturated reality of everyday life," not "experientially separate otherworlds" — and thus the micro-anchor of the Layer-5 virtual→actual carry-over.

Verdict options: incorporation **met / partial / none / ruptured**; consubstantiation **genuine / strategic / ruptured**. Five preserved **divergences** keep it a homology, not an equation (medium · ontological status · grounding theory · directionality · scope).

## 6. Conceptual extensions (the gap-fills new to v2)

### 6a. Two senses of "compulsion" — disambiguated
- **Compulsion-as-*bia* (force)** — Rhetoric I.10 cause #3 + NE III.1: the moving principle is **external**, the agent **contributes nothing** and is **acted-upon**. → the **non-agent branch**. *Game form:* unskippable cutscene, scripted seizure of control, forced movement. **This is the rape-scene mechanism.**
- **Compulsion-as-capture (habituated)** — the method's earlier "Type-4 → Type-1 regression via *technē*-hexis runaway": still **agent-caused** (habit #4 + appetite #7), but the wished-end has dropped from the doxastic horizon ("I don't even know why I keep doing this"). → the **agent branch**, a *pathology of habit*, NOT *bia*. *Game form:* the grind, daily-login, gacha — the **Agency–Purpose collapse** where designed means become ends. → the dark-pattern diagnostic.

Keeping these distinct is essential: the rape scene and the loot-box are opposite causal structures (force vs habituation) that the v1 method conflated under "compulsion."

### 6b. The voluntary / involuntary axis (NE III.1) — layered onto causation
For any analyzed action, classify: **voluntary** (principle in the agent + knowledge of particulars) / **involuntary-by-force** (*bia*) / **involuntary-by-ignorance** / **mixed** (voluntary-in-the-circumstance, e.g., a forced moral choice under threat — "more like voluntary"). Games specialize in engineering **mixed** actions (a "choice" the design has pre-narrowed) and in the **felt** experience of voluntariness over a **structurally** foreclosed action-space (the *illusory-freedom* finding, §6f). The Method names the gap between *felt* and *structural* voluntariness as a primary diagnostic.

### 6c. Incorporation-rupture (the keystone's negative image)
Incorporation is theorized in v1 only as an achievement; v2 adds **rupture** as a category, in two forms:
- **Agency-rupture** — incorporation is stripped by *bia* (control seized): R3 simultaneity collapses from "joined-and-separate" to "acted-upon." (Rape scene; G&G's miniaturization is its *wondrous* sibling — a designed dissimilitude that ruptures the for-the-sake-of-which without trauma.)
- **Social/consubstantiation-rupture** — the collective re-ratifies the player as a distinct/subordinate locus (the WoW loot-passing-over): R3 fails socially; the next pass's A₁ *resonant epithymia* shifts from "we" to "I-against-them."

### 6d. Phantasia-conscription (the implicitness mechanism)
When a game **withholds** depiction (the rape is never shown; only audio cues, "my pet," saddle-soreness, depleted cores, Bill's later dialogue), it supplies a deliberately **under-determined *phantasma*** at A₂ and **conscripts the player's own *phantasia* to author the unshown content**. *What* gets authored is fixed by the player's **settled doxai + hexis** (experiential backlog): the adult's *doxa* ratifies "rape," the child's ratifies "robbery." This is the dissertation's A₂/*doxa*/*hexis* machinery operating exactly as theorized (cf. the child-at-play whose charged *phantasma* overrides a standing *doxa*), and it explains why a withheld event can be *more* ontologically forceful than a depicted one — the player is made co-author of their own violation. *enargeia* (vivid supplying of *phantasmata*) is the positive-space twin of this withholding.

### 6e. The agent ↔ patient (praxis ↔ paschein) inversion
The chain's poles are **A₄ = agent** (actualizing desire, *praxis*) and **A₁ = patient** (being-affected, *paschein*). *Bia* forces a **de-actualization**: it drags the player from the A₄ pole it spent hours building back to the **A₁ patient-pole** — but as **destructive *paschein* (*phthora*)**, not the **preservative *paschein* (*sōtēria*)** of ordinary perception. The structural account of the rape scene's ontological violence: a medium constituted entirely as *your* desiring action inverts you into something acted-upon and harmed. (This is the single sharpest result the Method generates — and it is produced *mechanically* by reading the scene through §§6a–6d.)

### 6f. The 2D ↔ VR media differential (for the comparative case)
VR does not change the chain; it changes **how the chain is entered and intensified**:
- **A₀** — *sensory deprivation + supplanting* (the HMD replaces the world's sensory horizon wholesale), not merely depicts it.
- **A₁** — *room-scale 1:1 bodily kinesthesis* (6 DoF); the **symbiotic** pole of Calleja's kinesthetic continuum is reached bodily, not via controller.
- **Keystone** — R2 embodiment is **literal** (the body is the avatar's locus).
- **veri(dis)similitude** (MA-thesis term) — VR must be **verisimilar** enough to "throw" the player in, yet derives its rhetorical/ontological force from being **dissimilar** (not bound by earthly law); the dissimilar shock (miniaturization) = a designed **incorporation-rupture**. Uexküll's *Umwelt* / functional-tones is the VR-native vocabulary for the A₀–A₁ designed environment.
- **Illusory freedom** — VR's open/exploratory feel maximizes *felt* voluntariness while the action-space stays *structurally* foreclosed (§6b); "the user constituted as an object of the programmer's motive" is a Burkean motive-diagnosis waiting to be made explicit.

## 7. Registers — synchronic, diachronic, and the dispositional ground

- **Synchronic** — one directional pass A₀→A₄ within a bounded episode (Steps 0–7).
- **Diachronic** — how completed actions **sediment into *hexis*** and recondition future passes (Step 8). Two mechanisms: **Stimmung-saturation** (affective history biases basic valence at A₁ — "the tone") and **corrective-faculty impairment** (emotional/dispositional states bias *doxa* at M₂→A₃ — "the figure," via the **pathetic loop**, which re-enters at the *forming* phantasma M₁→A₂). The chain is therefore **spiraling**, not merely iterative.
- **Hexis bivalence** (the dispositional ground; cross-cuts the four types):
  - ***technē*-hexis** — cultivated procedural transparency (controller fluency, rotations, parry timing); *reduces deliberation*; withdraws like ready-to-hand equipment.
  - **ethical *hexis*** — the socially/evaluatively inflected "how" of being-in-action (what one takes as fitting, shameful, cruel, honorable); *holds-oneself-open*; exercises a **two-level reach** (modulates A₁ basic-valence + A₃ doxastic ratification).
  This names the **skill ≠ wisdom** distinction precisely, and is the engine of the diachronic claim that gameworld actualizations deposit durable tendencies carried back into ordinary being-in-the-world.

## 8. The protocol (canonical 9 steps, 0–8) — handoff-ready

> Inputs: a documented episode (recording/transcript/frames; macro-phase traces where available). Output: a filled **matrix** (see `THE-MATRIX`), a one-paragraph behavioral diagnosis, and (diachronic) a sedimentation forecast.

- **Step 0 — Scope & frame.** Set circumference (episode boundary); declare **scale** (discrete / protracted / ontological-change); identify **entry point** (A₀-sense / A₂-imagination / A₃-thought); declare register (synchronic / diachronic); name the **orekton** driving the pass.
- **Step 1 — Involvement profile (Calleja).** Six dimensions × macro/micro × intensity × internalization × evidence; apply the **Ludic split**; chain-anchor each.
- **Step 2 — Motive grammar (Burke).** Fill the six pentad terms; mark **featured term(s)**; tag each filler as *anticipated* (A₀–A₂) / *ratified* (A₃) / *enacted* (A₄).
- **Step 3 — Ratio dynamics.** Name the dominant ratio; specify its transition-of-instantiation; record what the player *attributes* the behavior to.
- **Step 4 — Crosswalk binding.** Fuse Burke↔Calleja via the validated crosswalk; chain-locus-tag each binding; produce the statements neither framework yields alone.
- **Step 5 — Keystone.** Assess incorporation R1–R7 + consubstantiation; verdict met/partial/none/**ruptured** + genuine/strategic/**ruptured**; locate the A₃→A₄ substantial union (or its rupture).
- **Step 6 — Attitude / hexis.** Dominant attitude register (competitive / completionist / explorer / devotional / ironic-meta); its **hexis-status** (*technē* vs ethical); elation↔accidie arc.
- **Step 7 — Causal diagnosis.** Name the operative **cause(s)** (7-causes layer): agent-caused → which **type (1–4)**; or non-agent → chance / nature / **bia**. Classify **voluntariness** (NE III.1). State the chain-locus carrying behavioral weight + keystone state.
- **Step 8 — Diachronic outlook.** Project which **hexis** sediments (*technē*/ethical), which **Stimmung** saturates the next A₀, which **doxai** join the settled band, the entry-point of the next pass — and, at the ontological-change scale, the **virtual→actual carry-over** + the **supplanting-of-dispositional-ground verdict**.

### Scale variants
- **Discrete event** — Steps 0–7, single circumference, synchronic. (Validation case: one RDR2 beat.)
- **Protracted sequence** — a *sequence* of 0–7 passes + heavy Step 8; unit = the **engineered hexis-bundle** across passes; Calleja macro/micro load-bearing. (RDR2 tutorial.)
- **Ontological change** — a single **rupture** pass (Steps 0–7 emphasizing §§6a–6e: *bia*, incorporation-rupture, phantasia-conscription, agent→patient inversion) + Step 8 virtual→actual carry-over. (Rape scene.)
