## The episode (raw description)

A 25-player guild, three weeks into progression on an end-boss, raids 19:30–23:00 on a weeknight. The night is ~18 attempts ("pulls"), each ending in a wipe at progressively later boss phases, until pull 17 — a clean execution that drops the boss with two players left alive. Euphoria on voice comms; a screenshot of the corpse; loot is distributed by loot council; one long-attending DPS player is passed over for the trinket and goes quiet. Afterward, half the raid lingers in voice for an hour replaying the kill; two players log off immediately to post the parse. Over the following week, attendance softens — the characteristic post-progression trough.

## Step 0 — Scope & circumference

**Unit of analysis:** the single progression night (19:30–23:00), with a macro-phase penumbra of the surrounding week (theorycrafting before, attendance-softening after). **Representative anecdote:** the wipe→wipe→first-kill arc is the representative form of *WoW* end-game raiding; the loot-council passing-over is the representative form of its hierarchic-guilt structure. Circumference set deliberately at "one night + its week-long penumbra" so both micro (the pulls) and macro (scheduling, parse-posting, burnout) are in frame.

## Step 3 — Ratio dynamics

- **Agency–Purpose (dominant):** the rule-system's means (rotations, cooldown-stacking, positioning) are bent wholly toward the single purpose of the kill; for the night, *means are subordinated to end* — the healthy inverse of compulsion's "means become end." But note the macro risk: once the god-term (first kill) is consumed, the means lose their ordering purpose → the **attendance trough** (Step 7).
- **Act–Agent:** the kill *remakes the agents* — "we are now a guild that has killed this boss" (alterbiography; the collective-agent is constituted by the act). This is the night's identity-payoff.
- **Scene–Act:** the boss arena and the 25-role interdependence *constrain and enable* the act — the encounter's design dictates the shape of legitimate play (rails the raid onto a narrow solution path).
- **Agent–Agent (co/counter):** the raid is a **collective agent** against the boss as **counter-agent**; internally, the loot economy introduces a competitive agent-agent ratio (the passed-over player vs. the loot recipient).

**Player attribution:** the raid attributes success to *coordination* (Act-Agent: "we pulled it together") and difficulty to *the encounter* (Scene-Act: "the design is punishing") — a healthy externalization that protects individual agents from blame, until the loot decision relocates motive to *agent-agent* rivalry.

## Step 4 — Crosswalk binding (the fusion)

Reading Axis M through Axis I via `validated-crosswalk.json`:

- **Purpose × Ludic + Narrative** → the progression god-term *is* the very-high Ludic intensity: the plan→execute→wipe→re-plan loop is Purpose enacted through Ludic involvement; the "night we got it" is Purpose closing into Narrative (alterbiography).
- **Agent × Shared** → the agent-position shift (self→collective) *is* the very-high Shared involvement: 25-way cooperation is the dramatistic collective-agent being assembled in real time.
- **Agency-split** → the Ludic split is visible: the **rule-system** (boss mechanics, DKP) operates as **Agency** (the means/instrument), while the **per-pull choosing-under-repercussion** is **Act+Purpose**. The DKP economy is pure Agency (an instrument for distributing a scarce good) — which is exactly why, when it produces the passing-over, it generates an *Agency-level* grievance that the player experiences *Affectively*.
- **Attitude × Affective** → the competitive→devotional arc *is* the affective trajectory (rising tension → kill-euphoria); the passed-over player's competitive attitude, denied its loot-payoff, slides to **accidie** (post-flow-break deflation).

**Fusion statement (what neither framework says alone):** *The night's behavior is a collective Act under a subordinating Purpose (god-term: the first kill), enacted through very-high Ludic + Shared involvement, in which the rule-system-as-Agency (DKP) — normally invisible — becomes affectively salient at the moment it allocates scarce reward against a long-attending agent, converting a cooperative agent-agent ratio into a competitive one and seeding the post-progression social trough.*

## Step 5 — Identification & consubstantiation (the keystone)

- **Avatar/role identification:** each player is *substantially one* with their role (tank/healer/DPS) — the role is the systemically-upheld locus through which they act in the raid — while remaining a distinct locus of motives (their own parse, their own loot interest). Classic consubstantiation: joined-and-separate.
- **Guild/collective identification:** at the kill, the raid achieves **consubstantiation as collective agent** — "*we* got it" is not a figure of speech but the lived state of acting-together (Burke's ground of consubstantiality, RM-01 p.21). The hour of lingering in voice afterward is the *enacted-ritual* register (RM-07 Rattlesnake Club): consubstantiality through a shared solemn experience, the kill being the occasion, not the essence.
- **Incorporation verdict (rubric R1–R5):** for the veteran raiders during the successful pull, **incorporation is MET** — R1 (the arena absorbed into consciousness), R2 (avatar-embodiment in the role), R3 (simultaneity during the clean execution), R4 (spatial+kinesthetic cornerstone met), R5 (4–5 dimensions blended). The kill-moment is **ultimate identification** in miniature: the individual locus briefly dissolved into the collective-agent's success ("consciousness and environment integrated fluidly," Calleja's register).
- **Consubstantiation-rupture:** the passed-over player is the keystone's negative image — their belonging, real enough to sustain weeks of attendance (genuine acting-together), is exposed by the loot decision as *also* strategic/hierarchic (RM-01 tension T1: consubstantial belonging vs. cunning/by-default belonging). The grievance is precisely a felt **rupture of consubstantiation**: the collective-agent they were substantially one with has treated them as a distinct (and subordinate) locus.
- **Social-motive apparatus:** the loot council is **hierarchy** (RM-04) operationalized — an authority ranking claims; its opacity carries **mystery** (why *that* allocation?); the passing-over risks **victimage** if the player is later scapegoated for the softening attendance. Guild leadership's task is now a **courtship** problem: re-identifying the estranged member across the rank-gap the loot decision opened.

---

## Step 6 — Attitude (the disposition)

- **Dominant register:** **competitive** (agonistic striving toward the kill, up the server-progression hierarchy), shading **devotional** at the kill-moment (reverent absorption into the collective achievement).
- **Divergent register:** the two players who log off immediately to post the parse are **ironic-meta/competitive** — oriented to the *external* hierarchy (Warcraftlogs rankings) more than the internal collective high; their attitude is toward the game's *meta-rhetoric* (the parse as god-term) rather than within the raid's devotional moment.
- **Elation↔accidie arc:** the raid rides **elation** (sustained identification) through the kill and the lingering hour; the passed-over player enters **accidie** (the self left alone after the flow-break) — and the week's attendance-softening is **collective accidie**: with the god-term (first kill) consumed, the means lose their ordering purpose and the affective charge drains until a new progression target restores it.

---

## Step 7 — Diagnosis & ends

**One-paragraph behavioral diagnosis.** The progression night is a **collective Act under a subordinating Purpose-god-term (the first kill)**, enacted through very-high **Ludic + Shared** involvement and reaching genuine **incorporation/consubstantiation** at the kill (the individual locus briefly dissolved into the raid-as-collective-agent — ultimate identification in miniature). Its dominant motive-engine is a *healthy* **Agency–Purpose** ratio (means subordinated to end) — but with a built-in instability: the **rule-system-as-Agency** (DKP/loot council), invisible while it serves the shared purpose, becomes **affectively salient** the moment it allocates scarce reward against a long-attending agent, **rupturing that agent's consubstantiation** and converting a cooperative agent-agent ratio into a competitive one. Once the god-term is consumed, the Agency–Purpose ordering lapses and the guild enters **collective accidie** (the attendance trough) until a new progression target re-subordinates the means.

**Ends this analysis serves:**

- **(E.3 Social/identity behavior)** The guild's post-progression friction is *not* "drama" or "burnout" in the folk sense but a **predictable two-part structure**: (a) a consubstantiation-rupture localized at the scarce-reward allocation, and (b) a collective accidie following god-term consumption. Both are addressable: (a) by making the loot-Agency's logic *less opaque* (reducing the mystery that converts a decision into a grievance) and re-courting the estranged member across the rank-gap; (b) by staging the next progression god-term *before* the current one is fully consumed, so the Agency–Purpose ordering never fully lapses.
- **(E.1 Design critique)** The diagnosis localizes *where* a retention-minded design intervenes — and flags the ethical line. Healthy design sustains the **Agency–Purpose** ordering by supplying a *next* meaningful end (new tier) before accidie sets in. **Dark-pattern** design instead engineers an **Agency–Purpose collapse** — making the means (login, grind, FOMO-gated currency) into ends so that engagement persists *without* a genuine subordinating purpose. DIA names the difference precisely: the former re-purposes the means; the latter purposes the means away. The same grid cell (Agency×Ludic under Purpose) is the site of both good pacing and predatory retention — which is exactly the cell a design ethics review should scrutinize.
