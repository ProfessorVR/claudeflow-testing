## 6. One-paragraph diagnosis + the insight it produces

**Diagnosis.** The saloon brawl is a **Type-2 thymotic Act sourced by consubstantiation**: Arthur is pulled into Bill's fight not by his own slight but because he is "substantially one" with the gang (*"we can handle these fools"*), so the **keystone (A₃→A₄ consubstantial union) is what *sources* the action**. It runs through very-high **Shared + Affective + Ludic/Kinesthetic** involvement to **incorporation MET**; its motive-engine is the **Agent–Agent ratio** (Bill triggers → gang backs Arthur → crowd backs Tommy), staged in a **Scene** (saloon → muddy street) whose gathering crowd actively **sources** motive (*"Finish him, Tommy!"*). Causally it is **agent-caused but structurally foreclosed** (scripted to occur and be won — freedom relocated to execution), cultivating **combat technē-hexis** while the closing **mercy appeal** stages the **ethical-hexis** fork.

**The philosophical payoff (what the matrix *generated*):**
1. **The keystone is a *source*, not just a state.** A thymotic act with a *collective* object of desire: Burke's "acting-together makes consubstantial" and Aristotle's "*thymos* needs a *toward-whom*" converge — the gang's slight becomes Arthur's *because* he is consubstantial with them. The brawl is literally **"one act under two logoi"** (Arthur's fists / the gang's assertion) — the social-substance extension of DA III.7 at work in a discrete event.
2. **Skill ≠ wisdom, in a single episode.** The brawl *trains* skill (technē-hexis: block/grapple, mechanically prompted) while the mercy beat *tests* wisdom (ethical-hexis: when to stop, staged narratively). RDR2 cultivates the first by mechanics and the second by cinema — a precise instance of the bivalence, and a preview of the tutorial's larger ethical-hexis project.
3. **Felt vs structural freedom.** You *feel* you are freely brawling; the encounter is scripted to occur and to be won. Agency is relocated to *execution*, not *outcome* — §6b's gap, validated at the micro-scale, and the seed of the tutorial's "illusory freedom in the service of hexis."

4. **Ethical forks are sometimes cinematic, not mechanical.** The mercy beat is staged, not chosen. → **Layer 4 hexis flag: interactive fork vs game-staged framing** (both feed ethical-hexis, differently).
