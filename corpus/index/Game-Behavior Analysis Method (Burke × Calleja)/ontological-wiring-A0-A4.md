## 0. What this document does, and how to read it

The DIA method (Parts A–E of `game-behavior-analysis-method.md`) is a runnable protocol for analyzing user behavior in virtual environments through the dual axis of Burke's 6 motive terms × Calleja's 6 involvement dimensions × macro/micro phase, driven by Burke's ratios, and anchored by the consubstantiation↔incorporation keystone. Part F was left as an explicit stub: a reserved hook for the eventual wiring of DIA into the dissertation's A₀→A₄ "actualization of desire" hierarchy, which was still in development at the time the method was first compiled.

That hierarchy is now first-draft complete (§§1.0–1.5 v3, 2026-05-28; diagram v9 of the same date). This document closes the stub. It does so in five moves:

1. **Specifies the chain** as the dissertation now articulates it — five completed actualities, four motion-transitions, three orientational modes plus an orthogonal committal dimension, a bivalent hexis-band, a synchronic/diachronic pathetic loop, and four (not three) types of action terminal at A₄ — and identifies the load-bearing structural features each node contributes to a theory of game behavior.
2. **Maps each DIA element onto the chain.** Every Burke motive term, every Calleja involvement dimension, every ratio, the Ludic split, the four player-attitude registers, and the keystone receives a determinate ontological location: which node it instantiates at, which motion it rides, which kinetic role (unmoved originator / moved mover / moved) it discharges, and what work it does.
3. **Discharges the keystone.** Consubstantiation↔incorporation is shown to be the structural achievement of Aristotle's "one substrate, two in being" condition (DA III.2, 425b26; III.7, 431a13–14) extended diachronically across the chain — first ratified at A₃, consummated at A₄, sedimented as hexis, and re-disclosed at the next A₀. The likely primary attachment site flagged in the original Part F stub (A₃→A₄) is confirmed and given its precise ontological articulation.
4. **Identifies the Four Types of Action** at A₄ as the terminal individuation of Burke's pentad in concrete game-behavioral form, with the dissertation's source-desire / mode-of-actualization distinctions giving DIA its compulsion- and habituation-diagnostics with new precision.
5. **Returns to the runnable method** with augmented steps that exploit the ontological grounding for behavioral and game-design diagnosis the prior un-anchored grid could not deliver.

The document is keyed throughout to dissertation locations (`§N.M`, `BCAP p.`, `BT §`, `RoM p.`, `IG-N p.`, `Rhet. I.M`, `DA III.K`) and to the diagram's v9 labels. A worked example threading the *WoW* progression-raid episode of `worked-example-wow.md` through the A₀→A₄ chain appears in §7; the cell-by-cell crosswalk-with-anchoring is tabulated in Appendix A.

A note on the document's audience and provenance. The DIA method is a dissertation construction: every cross-framework binding remains `anticipatory-projection` in the sense the validation report formalizes (neither Burke nor Calleja makes the cross-claim, and the dissertation's Aristotle does not make the game-behavior claim either). What the present document adds is not new authorial commitment from any of those four sources but a structural identification: the ontological architecture the dissertation derives independently from Aristotle and Heidegger turns out to be the exact apparatus that the DIA grid's behavioral analysis presupposes. The two were built without coordination and converge.

## 1. The chain as the dissertation now states it

This section consolidates the five-node architecture from §§1.0–1.5 v3 in the form DIA will consume. Readers familiar with the dissertation may skim it; the mapping in §2 assumes its terms.

### 1.1 The five nodes and four transitions

| Node | Title (diagram v9) | Constituent content (§§1.0–1.5) |
|---|---|---|
| **A₀** | Sensible Object & Aisthētikon | Dyadic first actuality of (i) the sensible object holding its active potency (*dynamis*) and (ii) the *aisthētikon* holding its receptive potency, situated within the encompassing horizon of motion (*kinēsis*) and time (*chronos*) as co-eternal conditions of any natural process (§1.1; *Phys.* III.1 201a10–14; *Met.* XII.6 1071b6–11). |
| **A₁** | Completed Perception | The joint actualization of the sensible object's active potency with the sense-organ's receptive potency (*aisthēma*) co-given with basic affective valence (*epithymia* / *resonant epithymia*) as a single "one substrate, two in being" event (§1.2; DA III.2 425b26, III.7 431a13–14), depositing the dual residual trace — *resonant aisthēma* + *resonant epithymia* — at the rational soul. |
| **A₂** | The *Phantasma* Proper | The stabilized residual motion crystallized by *phantasia* at the central organ, exhibiting three constitutive characteristics — determinacy, availability, representationality (§1.3) — and bearing the dual-aspect inheritance (formal-eidetic imprint + affective-orectic charge). |

|---|---|---|

### 1.2 The three-factor schema at every motion

Aristotle's *De Anima* III.10 schema — (1) that which originates motion (unmoved originator), (2) that by means of which (moved mover), (3) that which is moved (433b13–18) — applies, in the dissertation's reading, at every motion in the chain (§1.0, §1.5). Per node:

| Motion | Unmoved originator | Moved mover | Moved |
|---|---|---|---|
| M₀→A₁ | A₀ horizon + sensible object's active potency | sense faculty | sense-organ / perceiver |
| M₁→A₂ | A₁ (completed perception + dual resonance) | *phantasia* | psycho-somatic state |
| M₂→A₃ | A₂ (the *phantasma*) + held *doxai* + cultivated *hexeis* | rational cognitive apparatus | rational animal |
| M₃→A₄ | realizable good disclosed at A₃ | *orexis* | animal via the joint (433b21–25) |

This schema is the chain's universal kinetic grammar. DIA's analysis at every cell can be reread as a specification of who/what occupies each kinetic role under the gameworld's conditions.

### 1.3 The three entry points

The chain admits three entry points, after *De Motu Animalium* 7, 701a32–33 ("I want to drink, says appetite; this is drink, says sense or imagination or thought: straightaway I drink"):

- **Sense entry at A₁**: a present perceptual event opens the chain.
- **Imagination entry at A₂**: an already-actualized *phantasma* is re-engaged (memory, anticipation, prior gameplay residue).
- **Thought entry at A₃**: an already-actualized cognitive content is engaged inferentially (theorycraft, deliberation, learned principle).

All three issue in A₄ via the *orektikon* motion when the engaged content discloses a realizable good. They are not three parallel chains but three modes of activating *the same chain*.

### 1.4 The three orientational modes + doxa at A₃

The dissertation's revised cognitive architecture at A₃ (§1.3 v3) is a **four-dimensional achievement**:

- **Three orientational modes** (axes of non-committal engagement with the *phantasma*):
  1. **Intellection**: *phantasma* taken as bearing a universal; *atemporal*; produces "universal grasped" as A₃ content.
  2. **Memory**: *phantasma* taken as likeness of past *qua* past; *retrospective* (Heideggerian *Gewesenheit*-correlate at the perceptual-cognitive level); produces "past recalled and recognized as past."
  3. **Discursive (synthetic) mode**: multiple *phantasmata* synthesized into a single object of cognitive address; divides into
     - *Deliberative* (prospective practical): projective-synthetic + calculative-practical; produces "anticipated possibility evaluated as to-be-pursued or to-be-avoided"; presupposed by *prohairesis*.
     - *Speculative* (atemporal-contemplative theoretical): synthesis toward what cannot be otherwise; produces "truth contemplated"; **moves nothing**: in the speculative case the chain terminates at A₃.
- **One committal dimension**: ***doxa*** — the involuntary propositional taking-as-true. ***Doxa* is not a fourth parallel mode but an orthogonal layer** that may supervene upon any orientational mode and bind the soul to a truth-claim about what that mode has disclosed.

Two-level taking-as governs the entire transition: *phantasia*'s pre-propositional *taking-as* (presenting the *phantasma* under a determinate aspect with its co-given hedonic-orectic valence) is what *doxa*'s propositional *taking-as-true* operates upon.

### 1.5 The hexis band: a bivalence cross-cutting all four action-types

Heidegger's reading of Aristotle's *hexis* in *Basic Concepts of Aristotelian Philosophy* §17 (recovered in §1.5 of the dissertation) splits the dispositional ground into two structurally distinct modes of cultivated readiness:

- ***Technē*-hexis** (the *hexis* of production, *poiēsis*): "training has the precise sense of *reducing deliberation* insofar as it is through training that the completedness of attaining a result comes about" (*BCAP* 127). Its end lies in a product *beyond* the activity; its operation is means-to-end and effectively transparent; it fires the chain through M₂→A₃ on a procedural recognition without engaging the evaluative axis. Structural analogue: Heidegger's ready-to-hand equipment (the hammer withdrawing into the hammering, BT §15 H.69).
- **Ethical *hexis*** (the *hexis* of *praxis*, *aretē*): "the how is only appropriated in such a way that the human being enables himself *to be composed at each moment*; not routine but holding-oneself-open" (*BCAP* 128). Its end is *immanent in the doing*; it exercises a **two-level reach** uniquely available to it: modulating both basic-valence input at A₁ (Stimmung-saturation) *and* the doxastic ratification at M₂→A₃.

Both function as **additional unmoved originators** of M₂→A₃ alongside A₂. The bivalence is orthogonal to the four-type taxonomy of action (§1.6): any of the four types may run under either kind of *hexis*.

### 1.6 Four Types of Action at A₄

§1.5 v3 establishes that A₄ admits not three but **four structurally distinct types of action**, each individuated by the species of *orexis* that sources it:

1. **Type 1 — Epithymetic Action** (*epithymia*): pursuit of the pleasant, avoidance of the painful; **the only type that can fire pre-doxastically** (the *kritikon* of perception itself discriminates "this-as-to-be-pursued" without doxastic ratification — DA III.7, 431a8–12); may also run doxastically.
2. **Type 2 — Thymotic Action** (*thymos*): answers a slight, defends honor, asserts the self; **always doxastic**; **irreducibly social** — its object is disclosed within a *toward-whom*, a Being-with (*Mitsein*) of co-agents and counter-agents.
3. **Type 3 — Bouletic Action** (*boulēsis*): the rational wish for the *end* (the good held as end); **always doxastic**; may reach toward impossibles (immortality) or what depends on others.
4. **Type 4 — Prohairetic Action** (*prohairesis*): the deliberate choice of *means* to a wished end; **always doxastic**; bounded by what is "in our power" (NE III.3 1112a31).

These are not mutually exclusive compartments but name the desire that *sources* a given action. A single episode may pass through several (pity sources first a Bouletic wish to relieve suffering, then a Prohairetic choice of means). The civic passions of *Rhetoric* II (anger, fear, pity, shame, indignation, envy, emulation, kindness, friendship) form no type of their own; they are the species of being-affected through which one of the four desires gets sourced. The dissertation's §1.4 establishes pathē as the five-fold ontological structure (disposition / toward-which+toward-whom / bodily form / temporal range / disclosive function) that any *pathos* satisfies, with civic passions as its maximal articulational concretion.

### 1.7 The pathetic loop and recursion

A₄ is the chain's terminus, not its conclusion. Each completed *praxis* sediments back into the agent's *hexeis*, reshaping the dispositional ground that conditions subsequent chain-passes (§1.4, §1.5 v3). The loop runs in two registers:

- **Synchronic**: a single chain-pass from A₀ through A₄ within one discrete event; *doxa*'s ratification of evaluatively complex content at A₃ determines whether higher-order *pathē* concretize and which species of *orexis* mobilizes.
- **Diachronic**: the residues of prior actualizations modulate subsequent passes through two distinct mechanisms — *Stimmung*-saturation (biasing basic valence at A₁: De Insomn. 460b3–11) and corrective-faculty impairment (biasing doxastic ratification at M₂→A₃: De Insomn. 459a24–28). Together they constitute the recursive structure that makes ethical transformation difficult and game-design pacing consequential.

Diagram v9 renders the recursion as a back-arrow: A₄ → fresh A₀ cycle, "completed action changes the world."

## 2. Node-by-node ontological mapping of DIA

This section places every DIA element on the chain. Each subsection treats one node or motion: states what the dissertation puts there, then specifies which Burke motive term and which Calleja involvement dimension (in macro / micro phase) instantiates at that locus, then names the kinetic role each occupies, the relevant Burke ratios that operate across that locus, and the game-behavioral content the locus discloses.

### 2.1 A₀ — Ontological Horizon × Sensible Object & Aisthētikon

#### What is here (§1.1)

A₀ is the dyadic first actuality of the sensible object's active potency and the *aisthētikon*'s receptive potency, situated within motion and time as the encompassing horizon. Crucially: motion and time are **co-eternal** (Met. XII.6 1071b6–11); the chain does not begin with an inert potentiality awakened from outside but with actualities that are themselves already active. The dyadic structure of *kinēsis* (Phys. III.3 202a13–20; 202b13–14, the Thebes-Athens analogy: one road, two descriptions) is the primitive ontological feature inherited at every subsequent node: **motion is never a property of a single substance but a single actuality with two formal aspects**.

Heidegger's existential radicalization (§1.1) treats this dyadic relational *kinēsis* as the proto-form of *In-der-Welt-sein* (being-in-the-world) and the *Bewandtnisganzheit* (totality of involvements) that structures *Zuhandenheit* (BT §18 H.84). The dyadic mover-moved relation *is* the motion, just as the unitary phenomenon "being-in-the-world" cannot be broken into subject + object (BT §12 H.53). Sublunary motion is, on Heidegger's reading of Aristotle, the categorial-structural trace of how Dasein, whose mode of being is being-underway-toward, holds itself open to its possibilities.

#### DIA mapping at A₀

| DIA element | Mapping at A₀ | Macro / micro phase |
|---|---|---|
| **Scene** (Burke) | The **gameworld as ontological horizon** + the player's perceptual-corporeal situation. Burke: "the *scene-act* ratio" is the primary container-of-action ratio (GM §A.2); Heidegger: *Befindlichkeit*-saturated worldhood (BT §29). | **Macro**: the persistent gameworld + the player's physical room (the outer Scene — couch, desk, arcade, tournament stage) + the temporality of the play-session/career. **Micro**: the current encounter-arena, lobby, hub, or local cell. |
| **Spatial** (Calleja) | The Spatial dimension's "place-as-inhabited" criterion *is the structural achievement that licenses Scene to be Scene*. Calleja's macro/micro phase distinction is structurally identical to the dissertation's distinction between A₀ as encompassing horizon and the particular A₀-conditioning of a present chain-pass. | **Macro**: the gameworld memorized and inhabited across sessions ("known ground"). **Micro**: foot-positioning, sight-lines, the body-as-positioned-in-this-arena. |
| **Shared** (Calleja, macro) | The persistent co-inhabited world (server, faction, guild's standing). Burke's Scene partially absorbs this when the Shared dimension is *spatially* discharged (the server *is* the place where the agents are); the rest of Shared instantiates at A₂ (the avatar/role) and A₃ (the collective-agent). | **Macro only** at A₀: the social fact of *which gameworld* (which server population, guild, faction) is the player's. |
| **Agent** (Burke) | The player as **the ensouled body holding aisthētikon first-actuality** within the horizon. Pre-Scene "I" identity is not yet here — that emerges with avatar-identification at A₂. At A₀ the agent is the bare *Mitsein*-positioned perceiving body. | (Agent is fully foregrounded at A₂.) |
| **Time** (Aristotelian *chronos* / Heideggerian *Innerzeitigkeit*) | The play-temporality: tick-rate, encounter-clock, session-time, raid-night structure, server-time, real-life-time → all are *Innerzeitigkeit*-articulations that the player's rational soul must count to render fully actual (Phys. IV.14 223a21–26). | **Macro**: career-time, patch-cycle, raid-tier-progression, IRL day/week. **Micro**: cooldown-tick, GCD, boss-phase timer, pull-timer. |

#### Kinetic role at M₀→A₁

The motion proceeding from A₀ takes its name from its terminus, completed perception at A₁ (Phys. V.1 224b7–9). Unmoved originator: **A₀ horizon + sensible object's active potency** — that is, the gameworld's *outputs* (the rendered image, the sound, the controller's haptic), each holding its proper sensible quality in actuality. Moved mover: the **sense faculty** (the player's visual, auditory, kinesthetic apparatus). Moved: the **animal via the sense-organ**.

#### Operative Burke ratios at A₀

The dominant ratio is **Scene–Act**: how the gameworld constrains and enables what the player can do. In game design language this is the entire "rule-system as physics + spatial layout + designed affordances" complex; in Heideggerian language it is the *Bewandtnisganzheit* (the totality of involvements) that structures the player's possibility-space. A subsidiary **Scene–Agent** ratio operates whenever the gameworld qualifies *who* the player is allowed to be in it (faction selection, race/class lock, faction-vs-faction territoriality).

#### Why A₀ is not the keystone

A₀'s achievement is *being-in-a-gameworld* — what Calleja's incorporation criterion R4 (*spatial+kinesthetic cornerstone*) requires as a precondition. It is necessary but not sufficient for the keystone. The keystone requires the substantial-union event that consummates only at A₃→A₄ once the chain has converted A₀'s horizon-disclosure into ratified-as-true cognitive actuality.

### 2.2 M₀→A₁ — Perceptual Motion (Scene–Act ratio activating)

#### What this motion is (§§1.0, 1.2)

M₀→A₁ is the perceptual motion in which the sensible object's active potency and the sense-organ's receptive potency are jointly actualized as a single event (DA II.5 416b33–417a1; III.2 425b26). The motion is itself a *kinēsis* (DA II.5 416b33–417a1, "Sensation depends on a process of movement or affection from without"); the three-factor schema applies directly, not analogically, since *aisthēsis* itself is a motion (§1.2). The motion is enmattered preservative *alloiōsis* (II.5 417b2–7): not destruction of the organ but its bringing-into-actuality.

A second feature is decisive for DIA: the motion's actualization is **one substrate, two in being** (DA III.7 431a13–14). The perceptual and orectic aspects co-actualize as a single event — the *aisthēma* and basic *epithymia* are not sequenced (perception first, valence added) but co-given. This is the formal anchor that the incorporation/consubstantiation keystone will inherit.

#### DIA mapping at M₀→A₁

The motion **activates the Scene–Act ratio at its perceptual minimum**. The gameworld's affordances are encountered as actually-acted-upon and acted-against. Calleja's **Kinesthetic micro-phase** is operative as the controller-input/output loop (mimetic and symbiotic registers); the **Spatial micro-phase** is operative as the body-positioning within the encounter arena.

| Kinetic role | DIA filler |
|---|---|
| Unmoved originator | A₀'s gameworld outputs (rendered audiovisual, haptic) holding proper-sensible quality. |
| Moved mover | The player's sense faculty (perception + the controller-as-motor-mediation prosthesis). |
| Moved | The player. |

#### Operative attitude register

A₀ + M₀→A₁ already carry a basic-affective-valence saturation — the *Stimmung* with which the player enters the session. The diagram v9 labels this the "Basic Affective Valence track" (*pathos*: pleasure/pain, good/bad, co-constitutive with perception). This is the structural locus where macro-phase Attitude (cultivated player-disposition: competitive, completionist, explorer, devotional, ironic-meta — Part B.3 of the DIA manual) saturates the chain *before* the present pass even reaches doxastic ratification at A₃.

This finding is consequential: **the player's Attitude register saturates the chain from the perceptual entrance forward, not only at the affective-doxastic crystallization at M₃→A₄**. A devotional player and an ironic-meta player encountering the same gameworld at A₀ are already perceiving differently — the *Stimmung* biases what *Befindlichkeit* discloses.

### 2.3 A₁ — Completed Perception + Dual Resonance

#### What is here (§1.2, diagram v9 "Dual resonance: resonant aisthēma + resonant epithymia")

A₁ is the joint actualization of perceiver and perceptible together with the **dual residual trace** deposited at the rational soul:

- ***Resonant aisthēma***: the formal-eidetic residue of perception — the *eidos* of the perceived particular carried forward as kinetic content that *phantasia* will subsequently take up.
- ***Resonant epithymia***: the affective-orectic charge — *epithymia* (the orectic species couplable to bare *aisthēsis*) persisting as *dynamis* once the perceptual object withdraws. (The other orectic species — *thymos*, *boulēsis* — require higher cognitive enrichment and are not couplable to bare *aisthēsis*; only *epithymia* is.)

The dual resonance carries forward as a *single substrate-two-in-being* unity (the dissertation's "dual-resonance" replacing the earlier "dual-trace" terminology; per memory note 2026-05-23). Across both registers a basic hedonic tonality (good/bad, pursue/avoid) is co-given — what the diagram labels the *pathos* basic affective valence track that runs through every node.

Heidegger's structural correlate: *aisthēsis*'s receptive-disclosive character (BCAP 264–265, *paschein* as *dechesthai*, becoming-affected by beings as world) is the structural ancestor of *Befindlichkeit* (BT §29 H.134–137), and the disclosive function the dissertation's §1.4 develops as the fifth element of the *pathos* five-fold.

#### DIA mapping at A₁

A₁ is the structural locus where two Burke-Calleja bindings activate in their most rigorous form:

| Binding | Mapping at A₁ |
|---|---|
| **Scene ↔ Spatial** (the strongest validated binding) | Calleja's Spatial *inhabitation criterion* — the gameworld encountered as a habitable place rather than a conceptual map — is the *resonant aisthēma* of A₀'s designed-environment outputs. The veteran raider for whom the boss arena is "known ground by pull 10" has internalized A₁-residue across many chain-passes into a stable spatial disposition. |
| **Agency ↔ Kinesthetic** (validated, control-as-means) | The micro-phase Kinesthetic dimension's mimetic/symbiotic-cognitive continuum (Calleja Layer-B "symbolic→mimetic→symbiotic") is the *resonant aisthēma* of controller-feedback as cultivated into A₁'s residue. Fluent-vs-conscious-rotation is the diagnostic for A₁'s internalization trajectory. |

Two further bindings have their *secondary-tie origin* here (per the validation report):

- **Scene ↔ Kinesthetic** (secondary, "bodily-orientation residue of Scene"): A₁ is where the body's positioning-as-positioned-in-this-space is laid down as residual kinetic content.
- **Act ↔ Kinesthetic** (secondary, "physical-execution pole of Act"): the motor pole of an action's enactment is laid down here as residual *aisthēma*.

#### The pathos-as-genus track and Attitude saturation

The dissertation's *pathos* four-sense gloss (Met. Δ.21 1022b15–21, glossed in §§1.0 and 1.4) treats the basic-affective-valence layer at A₁ as the minimal articulational concretion of the same *pathos* structure that the civic passions instantiate maximally at A₃→A₄. **Attitude (Burke's transverse 6th term) ↔ Affective (Calleja's transverse modifier)** is, accordingly, *layered through every node of the chain* — but its concretizing into a determinate higher-order *pathos* awaits A₃'s doxastic ratification. At A₁ the Affective dimension is operative as basic valence, not as determinate civic passion.

This places the validated Attitude↔Affective binding under a precise *articulational* description: **Attitude/Affective saturates every node as basic valence; it is articulationally concretized only when doxa ratifies evaluatively complex content at A₃**.

#### Why A₁ contains the seed of incorporation R1

The *resonant aisthēma* (gameworld-form persisting as kinetic content in the player's perceptual residue) is the structural ancestor of Calleja's R1 *assimilation axis* — the gameworld absorbed into the player's consciousness. The cornerstone Calleja requires (R4: spatial + kinesthetic) achieves *necessary* conditions for incorporation at A₀+M₀→A₁; the *sufficient* condition requires the full chain to A₃ and the doxastic ratification of the avatar-identification at A₂.

### 2.4 M₁→A₂ — Phantastic Motion

#### What this motion is (§1.3)

The phantastic motion is the secondary actualization through which the *resonant kinēsis* deposited at A₁ is taken up by *phantasia* and constituted at the central organ into the *phantasma* proper, with the dual-resonance inheritance carrying through. *Phantasia* is itself a *kinēsis* (DA III.3 429a1–2 "movement resulting from an actual exercise of a power of sense"); the same three-factor schema applies.

Heidegger: *phantasia* as *poiein paron* ("making-present"), not as inner image-formation but as a mode of being-in-the-world — "the making-present-to-itself of the world, in which what is made present is not actually there" (BCAP 134). The *phantasma* is *eidos*-bearing in the sense of holding the look of the perceived being, and *resonant epithymia*-bearing in the sense of holding the hedonic-orectic charge under which that being shows up *as mattering for me*.

#### DIA mapping at M₁→A₂

This motion is the **internalization trajectory** of Calleja's Spatial and Kinesthetic dimensions — the conceptual-to-inhabited (Spatial) and conscious-to-fluent (Kinesthetic) transitions, in DIA terms. It is also where the **Narrative-dimension micro phase** begins to constitute itself: the residual *aisthēma* + *epithymia* of "what just happened in the encounter" persists as the kinetic content that subsequent chain-passes will compose into a single phenomenal manifold (Aristotle's *phantasia* "ties abstract thought to concrete perceptible objects or situations," Nussbaum, quoted §1.0; Heidegger's *phantasia* as the temporal medium of past + present + future, §1.0).

| Kinetic role | DIA filler |
|---|---|
| Unmoved originator | A₁ (completed perception + dual resonance). |
| Moved mover | *Phantasia* (retentive-projective faculty). |
| Moved | The psycho-somatic state at the central organ. |

#### What this motion delivers downstream

The *phantasma* that crystallizes at A₂ inherits the dual-resonance structure: it is determinate, available, and representational (§1.3's three characteristics) — and it carries both the *eidos* of the perceived gameworld-content and the resonant-orectic charge under which that content presents as mattering. The *phantasma* of "the boss in its current phase" is not affectively neutral; it is "boss-as-killable-target-with-this-much-HP-and-this-pattern-of-mechanics" with hedonic-orectic valence built in.

The decisive consequence for DIA: **the phantasma is the bearer of the avatar/character/role identification**.

### 2.5 A₂ — The *Phantasma* Proper

#### What is here (§1.3)

A₂ is the *phantasma* proper, the determinate available representational image (the diagram's "pivotal actuality: everything prior converges upon it; everything subsequent operates upon it as indispensable material"). Three characteristics constitute it: **determinacy** (eidos-character of the look), **availability** (the soul holds the image-for-the-mind ready for cognitive operations), **representationality** (structural likeness to the sensible particular, allowing the *phantasma* to function as a sign).

The dissertation marks A₂ as the **chain's pivotal actuality** for a precise reason: all three entry points (sense at A₁, *phantasia* at A₂, *nous* at A₃) require a *phantasma* to operate upon, and the chain's "thought never thinks without an image" (DA III.7 431a16–17) doctrine routes all higher operations through this node.

#### The Burke explicit foothold at A₂: the Hazlitt-Burke identification quote

**§1.3 already explicitly cites Burke at this exact node** (`v3.tex` L53 footnote, RoM pp. 83–84): "The only reason for my preferring my future interest to that of others must arise from my anticipating it with greater warmth of present imagination. It is this greater liveliness and force with which I can enter into my future feelings, that in a manner identifies them with my present being." The Hazlitt passage that Burke deploys in *A Rhetoric of Motives* names the structural mechanism by which **identification operates through the phantasma**: the warmth/liveliness of the *phantasma* is what makes "future-self" or "another-with-whom-I-act-together" present-as-mattering-for-me, and this presenting-as-mattering is the formal condition of identification.

This is decisive for DIA. The Burke RM-01 keystone (consubstantiation, "substantially one … yet a distinct locus of motives") has its **structural ground at A₂**: identification is *phantasma*-borne, and the dual-aspect (formal-eidetic + affective-orectic) inheritance of the *phantasma* is precisely the apparatus a "joined and separate" union requires.

#### DIA mapping at A₂

| DIA element | Mapping at A₂ |
|---|---|
| **Agent ↔ Shared** (the second validated binding) | The *phantasma* of "me-as-this-avatar/role/character" is the determinate-available-representational image through which the player's *agent-position* shifts (player-as-self ↔ avatar ↔ character ↔ role-as-collective-functor). Burke's avatar-identification, character-identification, and guild-identification all instantiate as A₂ *phantasmata* of varying scope. |
| **Kinesthetic micro (mimetic / symbiotic)** | The avatar-as-extension-of-my-motor-system is the *resonant aisthēma* now stabilized — the fluent rotation, the spatial-as-bodily-orientation. |

#### The dual origin and the three Burke entry points

The diagram v9 marks A₂ as carrying a "Dual origin" annotation: thinking-initiated cases enter the chain at A₂ with stored *phantasmata* (MA 702a20–21), while A₀-A₁ remain the *genetic* origin. The three Burke entry points to the DIA grid (§1.3 case studies) map onto the *MA* 7 entry-points as follows:

- **Sense-driven case** (drink-as-perceived): the gameworld delivers a fresh phantasma the player did not already hold (a new encounter, a surprise event); chain enters at A₁.
- **Imagination-driven case** (canteen-in-pack-imagined): the gameworld disappears momentarily from sense (loading screen, menu, idle moment) and the chain re-engages via a held *phantasma*: the next pull, the planned strategy, the remembered earlier failure; chain enters at A₂.
- **Thought-driven case** (rainwater-in-basin-inferred): the chain enters at A₃ via theorycraft, learned principle, or rule-system mastery: the player reasons "this composition cannot survive phase 3 without an external CD" before any specific phantasma of phase 3 is engaged. The eidos "drink" is held under universal premise; the eidos "raid-clear" is held under universal premise.

DIA's grid-row "featured term" analysis acquires here a new dimension: the *featured motive term* analysis must specify *which entry point* fires the chain in the analyzed episode. Compulsion-driven gameplay frequently enters at A₂ (the *phantasma*-of-the-next-pull never leaves the player); flow-state gameplay sustains A₁-entry across many passes; theorycraft-driven gameplay enters at A₃ and may even *terminate at A₃* if the engagement is purely speculative.

### 2.6 M₂→A₃ — Cognitive Engagement (Burke ratios + Calleja Ludic split)

#### What this motion is (§1.3)

The cognitive engagement of the *phantasma* through three orientational modes — intellection (atemporal universal), memory (retrospective likeness of past), and discursive (synthesizing multiple *phantasmata*, divides into deliberative-prospective practical and speculative-atemporal-contemplative). Plus the orthogonal committal dimension *doxa*.

The diagram v9 renders the three modes as "branching cognitive actualities" at A₃ — emphasizing that A₃ is not a single homogeneous content but a multi-dimensional achievement, with each mode terminating in a distinct sort of completed actuality (universal grasped, past recalled, possibility constructed and evaluated, truth contemplated).

The hexis band (§1.5) feeds into M₂→A₃ as additional unmoved originator: the cultivated *technē*-hexis fires the chain through M₂→A₃ on procedural recognition (the practiced rotation, the memorized pull-plan), while the cultivated ethical *hexis* (Type 3 paradigm) reaches both upstream to A₁ (Stimmung-saturation) and to M₂→A₃ (doxastic ratification).

#### DIA mapping at M₂→A₃

This is where the **largest cluster of Burke-Calleja bindings activates**. The validated crosswalk's cluster bindings (`validated-crosswalk.json` §cluster_bindings_many_to_many) and the Ludic split both have their ontological seat here:

| Burke motive term | Calleja dimension | Mapping at M₂→A₃ |
|---|---|---|
| **Act** | **Ludic (choosing)** + **Narrative** | The player's act-of-choosing-under-repercussion is the deliberative-phantasia synthesis terminating in *prohairesis* (§§1.3, 1.5) — the projection of multiple possible courses + the calculative-practical comparison + the resolution. The "act" Burke names is the *prohairetic* moment where choice institutes action. |
| **Scene** | **Spatial** | Already activated at A₀-A₁ but ratified-as-such-and-such at A₃ when doxa commits to "the arena IS a boss arena where these mechanics will fire." |
| **Agent** | **Shared** | The collective-agent constitution at A₃ ("we are the raid that is about to pull") is the doxastically-ratified *phantasma* of the guild-as-collective-agent. The Hazlitt-Burke identification mechanism (§1.3) operates here: the warmth of the present *phantasma* of "us" identifies the future-of-us with the present-being-of-me. |
| **Agency** | **Kinesthetic** + **Ludic (rule-system)** | The rule-system as *means* — what Burke marks as agency — is grasped at A₃ in the intellection mode as universal (the cooldown structure, the DKP rules, the damage formula) and in the deliberative mode as the determinate operative-in-this-pull means. This is where **the Ludic split's resolution lives**: the rule-system (Agency) is grasped *intellectively* (atemporal universal: the rules-as-such); the choosing (Act+Purpose) is grasped *deliberatively* (prospective practical: the choice-under-repercussion). |

| **Attitude** | **Affective** | Attitude saturates already at A₁ as basic valence; at A₃ it concretizes into determinate disposition-register when doxa ratifies the *phantasma* under one of the five attitude-registers (competitive, completionist, explorer, devotional, ironic-meta). The articulational threshold (§1.4's *Rhetoric* threefold + five-fold synthesis) is here. |

#### How the three orientational modes serve DIA

Each orientational mode supplies the DIA grid with a distinct cognitive-temporal content:

- **Intellection (atemporal universal)** = the *rule-system as such* — what every experienced player intellects: "stuns break after damage threshold," "tanks hold threat," "healers triage." Burke: this is the universal under which **Agency** is held. Calleja: this is the **Ludic-rule-system** in its most abstracted form.
- **Memory (retrospective)** = the *alterbiography* — the past plays, the prior wipes, the previous patch's strategy, the long history with this guild. Burke: this is **Act-Agent** ratio's diachronic register (what we've done has made us who we are). Calleja: this is the **Narrative-macro** dimension at its richest, and the structural condition for the **Affective**-Attitude saturation that the next pass will inherit.
- **Deliberative (prospective practical)** = the *strategy synthesis* — the projected next-pull, the planned route, the contingency tree, the practical syllogism that will issue in *prohairesis*. Burke: this is the **Purpose** terminus when held as deliberatively-projected end. Calleja: this is the **Ludic-choosing** dimension at its analytic moment.
- **Speculative (atemporal-contemplative)** = the *theorycraft* — the truth about the game's underlying mechanics that cannot be otherwise (the actual damage formula, the actual proc rate, the actual server's behavior). Burke: this is **god-term territory** (RM-05 / 06) — the apex term that ranks all the player's other ends. Calleja: this is the **Ludic-rule-system** at its highest mastery, the meta-game.

Critically: **the speculative mode terminates the chain at A₃** (DA III.10 433a13–15). The player engaged in pure theorycraft for its own sake — calculating the optimal stat budget without intending to apply it — is not en route to A₄. The chain has discharged itself in contemplation. This is DIA-diagnostic: theorycraft *for itself* is the player who has rotated out of the practical chain into the speculative — a form of disengagement that wears the costume of high engagement.

#### Doxa's orthogonal supervenience

Doxa is the **involuntary propositional taking-as-true** that may engage upon any orientational mode (§1.3). It is the rational animal's commitment to the truth-claim that the mode has produced — "the boss IS in phase 3 now" (doxa supervening upon memory + deliberation), "this rotation IS optimal" (doxa supervening upon speculative truth), "we ARE going to clear it tonight" (doxa supervening upon deliberative projection), "the loot system IS fair" / "the guild leader IS competent" (doxa supervening upon memory + intellection of social rules).

Doxa is the **rational axis of the two-axis threshold** that the dissertation's §1.5 v3 names *doxastic ratification* — the joint condition (doxa-engaged + content-evaluatively-complex) for higher-order *pathē*. The validated crosswalk's Attitude↔Affective primary binding ratifies here: only when doxa's content has evaluative complexity (slight, threat, undeserved suffering, disgrace, honor) does the basic-valence Stimmung crystallize into a determinate civic passion that will mobilize *thymos*, *boulēsis*, or *prohairesis* in the M₃→A₄ motion.

In game-behavioral terms: the **content-conditionality of doxastic ratification** is the diagnostic for whether an episode passes into thymotic/bouletic/prohairetic register or remains epithymetic. The same play-action — say, executing a clean rotation — can be a Type 1 epithymetic appetite-discharge (pleasant rotation, the body's drink) or a Type 4 prohairetic deliberate-means (chosen as the means to clearing the pull), depending on what content doxa has ratified.

### 2.7 A₃ — Completed Cognitive Actuality + Doxastic Ratification

#### What is here (§§1.3, 1.5)

A₃ is the four-dimensional completed cognitive actuality: a content produced by an orientational mode and (in evaluatively complex cases) ratified by doxa. The dissertation §1.5's "doxastic ratification and its content-conditionality" subsection specifies the joint condition: (i) doxa must form as involuntary taking-as-true; (ii) the content must be evaluatively complex (slight, threat, undeserved suffering, disgrace, or kin). When both conditions obtain, the chain runs through evaluative articulational concretion; when they do not, it bypasses that register.

The diagram v9 renders this as the "Doxastic ratification as a two-axis threshold (§1.5 L163–178)" and the "Doxa Band" + "Settled Doxai" panels.

#### DIA mapping at A₃

A₃ is the **motive-grammar locus**. Step 2 of the DIA method ("Motive grammar (Axis M)") fills the six pentad terms for the episode — and the locus where each term is *completed-as-such* is A₃. Until A₃, the motive grammar is in process; at A₃, it is determinate.

| Burke term | A₃ content |
|---|---|
| **Act** | The deliberated/intended act, held in view as the projected possibility just before *prohairesis* institutes it. |
| **Scene** | The ratified-as-such gameworld — "we are in this place, against this boss, with these conditions." |
| **Agent** | The doxastically-committed agent-position — "I am the tank," "we are the guild," "I am playing as this character." |
| **Agency** | The means-grasped: rule-system understood, controller-fluency held as ready. |
| **Purpose** | The end held in view, ratified as worth pursuing. |
| **Attitude** | The dominant attitude-register concretized at this moment: competitive, completionist, explorer, devotional, ironic-meta. |

The **DIA grid's "filled cells" achieve their determinacy at A₃**. The DIA report's filled-grid output is, in ontological terms, the report of A₃'s completed cognitive actuality for the analyzed episode.

#### The civic-passions concretization

Per §1.4's five-fold synthesis, the determinate civic passions (anger, fear, pity, shame, indignation, envy, emulation, kindness, friendship) concretize when doxa ratifies evaluatively complex content at A₃. The five elements of the *pathos* structure achieve their determinate filling here:

1. **Disposition** (Befindlichkeit-finding-oneself-disposed) is concretized: the player finds themselves disposed-as-having-been-slighted-by-the-loot-council; disposed-as-confronted-with-undeserved-suffering-of-a-guildmate-being-toxic-toward.
2. **Toward-which + toward-whom**: the social-ontological structure (Mitsein) crystallizes — the slighter, the threatener, the sufferer-with-whom-I-could-imagine-myself, the audience-that-matters.
3. **Bodily form**: the boiling of blood in anger, the cold of fear, the hot flush of shame — all bodily articulations that DA I.1 403a25–b19 specifies as constitutive, not symptomatic, of the *pathos*.
4. **Temporal range**: each *pathos*'s primary ecstasis (fear = futural, anger = having-been, pity = present, shame = all three).
5. **Disclosive function**: the *pathos* brings to *krisis* (the position-taking that the next action will enact).

This is **the structural mapping of the validated Attitude↔Affective binding at its articulationally maximal point**.

#### Where the chain terminates vs. where it continues

- **Speculative case**: A₃ is the terminus. No realizable good disclosed; no orexis mobilized. The player who has engaged in pure theorycraft has produced an A₃-truth that "moves nothing" (DA III.10 433a13–15).
- **Practical case**: A₃ is the unmoved originator of M₃→A₄. The disclosed realizable good (the clearable boss, the avoidable threat, the available reward) moves *orexis*, which moves the animal.

In DIA terms: **the question "did the chain terminate at A₃, or did it issue in A₄?" is a new diagnostic dimension** that the un-anchored grid could not directly state. Theorycraft-forum lurking that never logs in is A₃-terminal. The cleared raid is A₃→A₄.

### 2.8 The Hexis Band — Standing Dispositions cross-cutting all four types

#### What is here (§1.5)

The hexis band feeds into M₂→A₃ as **additional unmoved originator** alongside A₂. Two cultivated dispositional modes operate:

- ***Technē*-hexis** (Type 2 routine in v9 diagram terms, but per §1.5 it cross-cuts all four types when the action's end lies in a product): the cultivated craft-recognition fires on procedural *doxa* without engaging the evaluative axis. End external to the activity; transparent operation (the ready-to-hand hammer).
- **Ethical *hexis*** (Type 3 open resolution in v9 diagram terms, but per §1.5 the praxis-mode cross-cuts all four types when the action's end is immanent): holds the agent open for fresh *kairos*-resolution; uniquely exercises a **two-level reach** modulating both A₁ basic-valence input (Stimmung-saturation) *and* M₂→A₃ doxastic ratification.

#### DIA mapping at the Hexis band

The hexis band is the structural locus of two long-pending DIA features:

| DIA element | Mapping at Hexis band |
|---|---|
| **Attitude registers as cultivated** | The five attitude registers (competitive / completionist / explorer / devotional / ironic-meta) are *hexeis* — durable settled dispositions cultivated through repeated chain-passes. They are not single-episode states but the dispositional architecture from which any given episode draws. **Hexis bivalence applies**: the player whose attitude is cultivated as *technē* (efficiency-maximizing competitive: an ergonomic, deliberation-reducing register) operates structurally differently from the player whose attitude is cultivated as ethical *hexis* (the *philia*-attentive friendship-prioritizing competitive: a *kairos*-responsive, holding-oneself-open register). |
| **Agency ↔ Kinesthetic mastery** | When Calleja's Kinesthetic dimension is internalized to fluency, this is *technē-hexis* in the precise sense — the cooldown rotation withdraws into the rotation-itself, becoming thematic only when it breaks. |
| **Skill ↔ wisdom split** | A long-recognized but un-articulated tension in game-research: "skilled" players are not the same as "wise" players. The *technē*-hexis / ethical-hexis split names this precisely. *Technē*-hexis explains skill at the rule-system / Kinesthetic / mechanical-execution level. Ethical hexis explains *judgment* — when to push, when to call a wipe, when to defuse the loot dispute, when to leave the guild. |

#### Compulsion as Agency-Purpose collapse via technē-hexis runaway

The DIA Part E.1 design-critique diagnostic (compulsion loops = Agency-Purpose collapse) acquires new precision at the hexis band:

> A *technē-hexis* that *should* have served a wished end (the player's chosen game-experience) instead **sediments without an ethical-hexis correlate to hold it open to fresh evaluation**. The means becomes the end not as a single event but as a *cultivated dispositional reduction*: the player who has logged in for the daily for 600 days has developed a *technē-hexis* of logging in that runs the chain through M₂→A₃ on transparent procedural *doxa*, never engaging the evaluative axis at which an ethical hexis would ask "is this still serving an end I would wish?"

This is the structural mechanism of dark-pattern design effectiveness: it cultivates *technē-hexis* (transparent routine) where an ethical *hexis* (holding-oneself-open) would have caught the Agency-Purpose collapse.

### 2.9 M₃→A₄ — *Orektikon* Motion (species-specific desire mobilizes)

#### What this motion is (§§1.0, 1.4, 1.5)

The *orektikon* motion is the final transition: the realizable good disclosed at A₃ moves *orexis*, which moves the animal via the bodily instrument (DA III.10 433b13–25). The "moved mover" structure (*orexis* is moved by the apparent good and moves the body) is preserved across all four types of action; what varies is **which species of *orexis*** the realizable good actualizes.

§1.4's five-fold *pathos* analysis specifies that emotion is the form desire takes under evaluative disclosure — and the somatic-preparation moment (the *pathē* between desire and the organic parts, MA 702a17–19 read against §1.4) is the bodily form constitutive of the determinate civic passion (anger's boiling blood, fear's chill, shame's hot flush).

#### DIA mapping at M₃→A₄

This is **the Four Types of Action stage**. Each Type instantiates a specific Burke-Calleja DIA cell-configuration:

| Type | Burke term-cluster | Calleja dimension-cluster | Diagnostic |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Type 1 — Epithymetic** | Act + Agency (kinesthetic execution pole) + pre-Attitude basic valence | Kinesthetic + Ludic-rule-system (when bypassing) + Affective basic valence | The bare appetite/avoidance loop. **Can fire pre-doxastically** (the *kritikon* of perception discriminates pursue/avoid at the most basic level — the startle, the flinch, the dopamine-loop slot-machine response). Cross-references the diagram's "bypass" path. |
| **Type 2 — Thymotic** | Agent + Agent-Agent ratio + Attitude (always doxastic; always toward-whom) | Shared + Affective (anger/honor register) | The PvP retaliation, the guild-defense against external slight, the rank-claim contest. Always doxastic; Mitsein-structured; civic passion concretized (anger most paradigmatically). |
| **Type 3 — Bouletic** | Purpose (end held as wish) + Act-Agent ratio | Ludic (purpose-as-end) + Narrative + Affective (devotional/aspirational register) | The wish for the clear, the wish for the legendary, the wish for "being the kind of player who..." — the *end* held in view as such, before any means is weighed. The bouletic player can wish for impossibles (immortality of guild, mythical perfection). |
| **Type 4 — Prohairetic** | Agency-Purpose ratio (the means-to-end) + Act + Attitude (always doxastic) | Ludic-choosing + Kinesthetic + Affective (competitive register most paradigmatically) | The deliberate-means execution: chosen rotation, planned strategy, executed call. Bounded by what is "in our power" — the prohairetic player does not choose to win against an opponent twice their skill; that is impossible and so a *boulēsis* (wish) at best. |

#### Civic passions as one species of being-affected, not a type of their own

§1.5 v3 is precise on this point: the civic passions form no type of their own. Anger is one species of being-affected that may *source* a thymotic action (the angered player retaliates). Pity is another that may source first a bouletic wish (relieve suffering) and then a prohairetic choice of means (heal the dying party-member, defend the player being harassed). The typology marks the predominant source-desire, not an exclusive one.

This is consequential for DIA: **the civic passions are the *route* by which the chain passes through Type 2-4, but they are not themselves a typing-axis. The typing-axis is the source-desire (epithymia/thymos/boulēsis/prohairesis), which is orthogonal to which civic passion sources it.**

#### The technē/ethical-hexis crosscut

Per §1.5, the bivalence cross-cuts all four types: a Type 4 prohairetic action can run under *technē-hexis* (the rotation chosen long ago and now executed transparently; expert-pianist register) *or* under ethical *hexis* (the rotation chosen this pull as the *kairos*-appropriate response to this specific situation). The Burke **Attitude** transverse modifier captures this exactly: the *attitude* with which the action is enacted is the dispositional how that the cultivated hexis articulates.

Note on Type 1's special place: appetite that fires pre-doxastically engages *no settled disposition at all* (§1.5's hexis-band passage). The chain runs from A₁ through A₂ to A₄ without dispositional mediation. This is the structural reading of "compulsion below the level of habit" — the dopamine-hit loop that has not yet sedimented as habit but is also no longer choice.

### 2.10 A₄ — Completed Action (*praxis*) + Recursion

#### What is here (§1.5)

A₄ is "the convergence at *praxis*" — the gathering-point at which material perturbation (the somatic preparation), formal determination (the *eidos* of the action: this drinking, this fleeing, this standing-firm), efficient trigger (*orexis*), and final cause (the realizable good) meet in a single concrete orientation toward the world. All four causes — material, formal, efficient, final — converge here, and what unifies them, per §1.5, is the **affective-practical horizon itself, not a further factor ranged alongside the others**.

The diagram v9 names this node "Affective Architecture of Being-in-the-World" — its content is the totality of involvements within which the agent already dwells, made manifest as enacted comportment.

#### DIA mapping at A₄

A₄ is the **terminal pentad convergence**. Each motive term reaches its complete-actuality form here:

- **Act**: the enacted action itself (the kill, the heal, the call, the flame, the leave-the-guild).
- **Scene**: the gameworld as it has been acted upon (and as it has acted-back-upon the player).
- **Agent**: the agent as remade by the act (alterbiography is constituted *here* — "we are now a guild that has killed this boss"; "I am now a player who flamed in chat").
- **Agency**: the means as discharged.
- **Purpose**: the end as realized (or as failed, which the next chain-pass will register).
- **Attitude**: the disposition as enacted.

Calleja's incorporation (R1–R5) achieves its peak intensity here, *when met*: assimilation + embodiment + simultaneity + cornerstone + blending.

#### The diagram's "recursive, not linear" annotation

A₄ feeds back into the agent's *hexis* (§1.4's diachronic register) and reshapes the dispositional ground from which the next chain-pass will proceed (§1.5's pathetic loop). The diagram's recursive loop arrow is the visual marker of this: A₄ → fresh A₀ cycle, "completed action changes the world."

Two diachronic mechanisms operate:

1. **Stimmung-saturation at A₁**: the agent's accumulated affective history biases basic valence at A₁, coloring the perceptual disclosure of the next gameworld-encounter.
2. **Corrective-faculty impairment at M₂→A₃** (De Insomn. 460b3–16): existing emotional states modulate doxastic ratification — the corrective faculty that would normally resist the *taking-as-true* of evaluatively distorting *phantasmata* is weakened.

For DIA: this is **the diachronic structure that explains the guild's post-progression accidie, the toxic guild's downward spiral, the addicted player's escalating commitment, the explorer's deepening attachment to the world**. Each completed action reshapes the affective architecture; the next chain-pass enters a subtly (or drastically) shifted A₀.

### 2.11 The recursive loop and the diachronic register

The full diachronic recursion is the structural ground of game-behavior's *career-level* phenomena:

- **Mastery** = repeated Type 4 prohairetic actions sedimenting *technē-hexis* in the rule-system → mechanical-fluency = Kinesthetic-cornerstone + Ludic-rule-system internalization.
- **Wisdom** = repeated Type 4 prohairetic actions sedimenting ethical *hexis* via the two-level reach → judgment at the *kairos* = competent leadership, gracious play, *phronetic* response.
- **Compulsion** = repeated Type 1 epithymetic actions failing to sediment higher-order hexis → the gachi-loop, the daily-grind, the addicted return without dispositional accumulation toward a wished end.
- **Alterbiography** = the Narrative-macro accumulation of A₄-residues sedimenting as the player's story-of-themselves.
- **Burnout / accidie** = the post-progression collapse Calleja and the *WoW* example describe — the god-term consumed, the Agency-Purpose ordering lapsed, the chain entering A₀ with affectively-drained Stimmung.

DIA's macro/micro distinction maps cleanly: the **macro phase = the diachronic register** (across chain-passes); the **micro phase = the synchronic register** (within a chain-pass).

## 3. The keystone discharged: Consubstantiation ↔ Incorporation as A₃→A₄ substantial union

The original Part F stub flagged the keystone (consubstantiation/incorporation as substantial union completing the motion) as the likely primary attachment site for ontological wiring. This section confirms and articulates that mapping.

### 3.1 The Aristotelian/Heideggerian structural anchor

The dissertation establishes an architectural feature at multiple nodes that turns out to be exactly the apparatus Burke's consubstantiation and Calleja's incorporation both name:

> **One substrate, two in being** — DA III.7 431a13–14: *aisthēsis* and the *orektikon* are one in substrate but two in being. The actualization of the perceptible and the actualization of the percipient sense are "one and the same actualization, though their being is not the same" (DA III.2 425b26).

This formal structure — one actuality with two formal aspects under different *logoi* — runs through every node of the chain (§1.0 v3, "self-applicative + co-given actualization + higher-order awareness" three-move argument). It is, in Aristotle's own register, the structural condition of the cognitive/affective co-actuality the dissertation names the chain's "two-logoi" structure.

Heidegger's existential radicalization (§1.1) generalizes: Dasein and world are "co-constituted" in the *In-der-Welt-sein* unitary phenomenon (BT §12 H.53). The dyadic relational *kinēsis* the chain inherits from A₀ is the categorial-structural trace of this co-constitution.

### 3.2 Burke and Calleja both name this exact structure

The validation report's keystone analysis (§7) and `rm-identification-consubstantiation-manual` §D establish that:

- **Burke RM-01 p.21**: A is consubstantial with B when they are "substantially one … yet a distinct locus of motives — both joined and separate." The grounding is *acting-together* — consubstantiation is constituted by joint action under a shared substance-attribution.
- **Calleja IG-10 p.169**: incorporation is the simultaneous achievement of (R1) assimilation axis (world INTO consciousness) + (R2) embodiment axis (player INTO world via avatar) + (R3) simultaneity condition.

The structural homology — which the validation report classifies as `validated_burke_direct_grounded` — has four moments:

| Burke | Calleja | Aristotelian / Heideggerian ground at the chain |
|---|---|---|
| "substantially one" | R1 assimilation | One substrate (DA III.2 425b26) |
| "distinct locus of motives" / the avatar | R2 embodiment | Two in being (DA III.7 431a13–14) |
| "both joined and separate, held simultaneously" | R3 simultaneity | The single actualization-under-two-logoi structure |
| "acting-together makes consubstantial" | R4/R5 spatial+kinesthetic cornerstone + blending | The chain *as iterated motion* from A₀ to A₄; *acting-together* = joint chain-actualization |

### 3.3 Where on the chain the keystone is achieved

Per the Part F stub's hint — and confirmed by the present analysis — **the keystone is the A₃→A₄ substantial union**. The precise ontological event is this:

> At A₃, the rational animal's *phantasma*-of-acting-together (the *guild-as-collective-agent*, the *avatar-as-me*, the *party-in-formation*, the *server-as-co-inhabited-world*) is doxastically ratified as bearing on reality — "WE are doing this." The orexis that this realizable good moves at M₃→A₄ has, as its terminus, a jointly-realized A₄: the action's completion is achieved *by us*, not *by me-and-then-by-you*. The "one substrate, two in being" structure that characterizes every motion in the chain reaches at this transition its *social-substantial* articulation: the joint chain-pass is one actualization-under-two-logoi where the two *logoi* are (i) my agency and (ii) the collective's agency, both realized in one act.

This is the formal-ontological reading of consubstantiation/incorporation. The R1-R5 rubric of incorporation maps onto the chain as follows:

- **R1 assimilation** (gameworld → consciousness) = the *resonant aisthēma* deposited at A₁, persisting as kinetic content through the chain. Diachronic accumulation across many passes constitutes the player's *inhabited* gameworld.
- **R2 embodiment** (player → world via avatar) = the *phantasma*-of-me-as-avatar at A₂, doxastically ratified at A₃ as the agent-locus.
- **R3 simultaneity** = the "one substrate, two in being" condition exercised at every transition, reaching its keystone articulation when the substrate is the joint agent and the two logoi are individual + collective agency.
- **R4 spatial+kinesthetic cornerstone** = met at A₀+M₀→A₁ (Spatial inhabitation criterion + Kinesthetic mimetic/symbiotic fluency).
- **R5 blending** (4-5+ dimensions co-active) = met at A₃ when the doxastically-ratified content weaves multiple involvement dimensions into a single cognitive actuality (raid-night = Shared + Ludic + Kinesthetic + Affective + Narrative all blended).

### 3.4 Why the homology remains a homology (the five divergences preserved)

The validation report's `five_divergences` are not erased by the ontological wiring; they are structurally preserved at the chain level:

1. **Medium of union** (symbolic vs phenomenological): Burke's medium is symbolic (the *logos* of identification); Calleja's is phenomenological (the lived involvement). At the chain: this is the *doxa*-mediated vs *aisthēsis*-mediated distinction. Doxa is constitutively *logos*-dependent (DA III.3 428a19–24); *aisthēsis* is not. Burke's consubstantiation operates primarily at A₃'s doxa layer; Calleja's incorporation operates primarily at A₁'s perceptual-cornerstone layer. Both must converge for the keystone to be achieved, but they enter the chain at different nodes.
2. **Ontological status** (real-or-persuaded vs experiential): preserved at the *real-vs-apparent-good* distinction (DA III.10 433a27–29); incorporation is felt as experientially real; consubstantiation may be persuasive-but-strategic (Burke RM-01 tension T1).
3. **Grounding theory** (substance-metaphysics vs experientialist cognition): Burke grounds in *ousia*; Calleja in experientialist cognition. At the chain: this is the distinction between the *eidos* the *phantasma* bears (formal-ontological) and the *resonant epithymia* the *phantasma* carries (phenomenologically-experiential).
4. **Directionality** (asymmetric-deployed vs bidirectional): Burke's identification is asymmetric (orator → audience); Calleja's incorporation is bidirectional (player ↔ world). At the chain: the Burke direction is the doxa-formation event (the rhetor cultivates the audience's *phantasma*); the Calleja direction is the dual-resonance deposit (the gameworld and the player co-actualize).
5. **Scope** (society-wide vs ergodic-bounded): preserved as a difference in macro/micro phase distribution. Burke's identification scales to society-wide (RM Part II's hierarchy / courtship analyses); Calleja's incorporation is ergodic-bounded (within-the-game).

### 3.5 The negative case: consubstantiation-rupture as keystone-failure

The worked example (`worked-example-wow.md` Step 5) named consubstantiation-rupture at the loot-passing-over. The ontological reading of that event:

> The DKP-loot-allocation moment is an A₃ doxastic ratification of "the collective-agent treats me as a distinct (and subordinate) locus." The *phantasma* that A₂ supplied — the guild as the collective with whom the player has been substantially one — is now doxastically re-ratified under different content. The *resonant epithymia* that the next chain-pass will inherit at A₁ is shifted from "we" to "I-as-against-them." The R3 simultaneity condition fails: joined-and-separate becomes either-or; the substantial union is structurally dissolved. The next chain-pass enters an A₀ in which the player and the guild are no longer co-constituting one chain-actualization but two.

This is the precise mechanism of **consubstantiation-rupture as keystone failure at A₃**, with its diachronic consequence (the player's softened attendance, eventual gquit). DIA's E.3 social/identity-behavior ends are addressable at this exact ontological locus.

## 4. Four Types of Action × Burke Motive Terms — terminal individuation at A₄

The dissertation's Four-Types taxonomy at A₄ is **the structural mechanism by which Burke's pentad reaches behavioral determinacy**. Each Type instantiates a distinct configuration of motive-grammar + involvement-architecture, and each carries distinct DIA-diagnostic implications.

### 4.1 Type 1 — Epithymetic Action

**Source-desire**: *epithymia* (appetite).
**Doxastic status**: the *only* type that can fire pre-doxastically (also runs doxastically when the appetite engages an evaluative judgment, e.g., "this would be a pleasing companion to drink with").
**Pre-doxastic mechanism**: the *kritikon* of perception itself discriminates pursue/avoid at the most basic level (DA III.7 431a8–12); the orexis recoils from what is discriminated as bad without doxastic ratification.

**Burke motive-cluster**: Act + Agency (kinesthetic execution pole) + basic-valence Attitude.
**Calleja dimension-cluster**: Kinesthetic + (in bypassing case) Ludic-rule-system + Affective-basic-valence.
**Hexis status**: pre-doxastic Type 1 engages no settled disposition; doxastic Type 1 may run under *technē-hexis* (the practiced eating in survival games, the auto-piloted PvE clear) or under no hexis (the raw appetite of a freshly-installed game).

**Game-behavioral signatures**:
- **Dopamine-loop slot-machine mechanics** (gacha-pull, loot-box-open, lockbox-open): bare appetite/avoidance loop firing pre-doxastically.
- **Survival-game basic loops** (eating, drinking, sleeping in the game): Type 1 in its routine appetitive form.
- **Reflexive PvP input** (the dodge, the parry, the headshot-snap): Type 1 at the kinesthetic-as-reflex level (and importantly *not* Type 2 thymotic — the kinesthetic-as-reflex predates any honor-defense doxa).
- **Compulsion below habit**: the unwished-for appetite that the agent has not yet sedimented into a hexis ("I just keep opening the app for 5 seconds at a time").

**DIA diagnostic**: **Type 1 pre-doxastic gameplay is the structural register dark-pattern design targets**. The chain bypasses A₃'s evaluative axis where ethical *hexis* could have engaged. Designing-for-Type-1 is the dark-pattern operationalization; designing-for-Type-4-ethical-hexis is its inverse.

### 4.2 Type 2 — Thymotic Action

**Source-desire**: *thymos* (spiritedness, honor-defense, self-assertion).
**Doxastic status**: always doxastic (anger is "desire accompanied by pain for conspicuous revenge for a conspicuous slight" — *Rhet.* II.2 1378a30; the slight is doxastically ratified before *thymos* mobilizes).
**Social-ontological structure**: irreducibly social — its object is disclosed within a *toward-whom* (Mitsein).

**Burke motive-cluster**: Agent + Agent-Agent ratio (co/counter-agent) + Attitude (competitive register at its most agonistic).
**Calleja dimension-cluster**: Shared + Affective (anger/honor register).
**Hexis status**: Type 2 runs under cultivated *hexeis* — Aristotle's anger-virtue is "good temper" (NE IV.5: *praotēs*); the cultivated *hexis* is the cultivated *how* of standing in anger.

**Game-behavioral signatures**:
- **PvP retaliation cycles**: the "I will not let that gank stand" mobilization.
- **Guild-defense / faction-defense against external slight**: the guild's mobilization when raided / streamed-against / harassed by a rival.
- **Rank-claim contests**: the ladder-climbing motivated by standing-against an opponent (vs. the rank-climbing motivated by mastery-for-its-own-sake — which is Type 3 or 4).
- **Tilt-chains in competitive games**: the doxa-ratified slight (the opponent's flame, the random teammate's mistake) mobilizes successive thymotic actions, each leaving residue in the corrective-faculty-impairment register (De Insomn. 460b3–16) so that the *next* slight is even more readily ratified.
- **The toxicity-cascade**: structurally identical to the tilt-chain at the social level — the corrective-faculty impairment runs at the community level.

**DIA diagnostic**: **the toxicity-and-tilt cluster is Type 2 thymotic acting under impaired corrective-faculty**. Naming it as such locates the design-intervention: cool-down systems that interrupt the doxastic ratification of slights, mute systems that prevent the *phantasma*'s further charging, post-game queues that prevent immediate re-encounter — each is an intervention at the M₂→A₃ corrective-gating level.

### 4.3 Type 3 — Bouletic Action

**Source-desire**: *boulēsis* (rational wish for the *end* / the good held as end).
**Doxastic status**: always doxastic.
**Boundedness**: *unbounded* — *boulēsis* may aim where *prohairesis* cannot (impossibles, what depends on others). The player may *wish* their guild were the world-first guild even when this is impossible; they may *wish* to retire from gaming and play only for the pleasure-of-the-doing even when their *technē-hexis* in fact won't allow it.

**Burke motive-cluster**: Purpose (end held as wish, the god-term) + Act-Agent ratio (the alterbiography as projected wish: "we are/are becoming the kind of guild that...").
**Calleja dimension-cluster**: Ludic (purpose-as-end) + Narrative (the wished-for story) + Affective (devotional or aspirational register).
**Hexis status**: Type 3 is the paradigmatic ethical-hexis territory when the wish is for an end immanent in the doing (eu-praxia, the playing-well-with-friends, the *philia*-attentive friendship); it may also run under *technē-hexis* when the wish is for an end external to the doing (the trophy, the achievement, the rating).

**Game-behavioral signatures**:
- **The progression-night god-term**: "we want to kill this boss tonight" (Purpose held as wish, before any means is yet weighed for the next pull).
- **The alterbiographic wish**: "I want to be the kind of player who...".
- **Aspirational class-fantasy engagement**: "I want to be a knight," "I want to be a master mage" — the wish for an *end* that the gameplay realizes.
- **The "main this character"-decision**: the bouletic commitment to a character / class / role as the end-in-view, prior to any means-weighing.
- **The genuine devotional register** (Part B.3 attitude): self-subordination to the gameworld's order *toward incorporation at its fullest* — Type 3 bouletic with R4-R5 incorporation cornerstones cultivated.

**DIA diagnostic**: **Bouletic action is the structural register of voluntary engagement at its most ethically-substantive**. The player who can *wish* the gameplay-itself as the end (not the trophy, not the rating, not the rank) is exercising the rational wish that the philosophy-of-games and game-design-ethics tradition (from McLuhan to Bogost to Sicart) tries to specify. Bouletic engagement is the *philia*-ground for guild-attachment, the *eu-praxia*-ground for play-as-its-own-end, the *kalon*-ground for play-as-aspiration.

### 4.4 Type 4 — Prohairetic Action

**Source-desire**: *prohairesis* (deliberate choice of *means* to a wished end).
**Doxastic status**: always doxastic.
**Boundedness**: bounded by what is "in our power" — *prohairesis* "never goes after something that is impossible" (*BCAP* 99).

**Burke motive-cluster**: Agency-Purpose ratio + Act (the institutiing-of-the-act through choice) + Attitude (competitive register at its most strategic).
**Calleja dimension-cluster**: Ludic-choosing (the choice-and-repercussion moment) + Kinesthetic (the executed means) + Affective (the determinate competitive/devotional register the attitude has concretized to).
**Hexis status**: Type 4 paradigmatically the *technē-hexis* registry when the chosen means is the cultivated craft-judgment (the expert-pianist rotation, the speedrunner's executed route) — but can equally run under ethical *hexis* when the means is *kairos*-resolved freshly each pull.

**Game-behavioral signatures**:
- **The chosen rotation / strategy / build**: the deliberate-means execution that the practical syllogism issues in.
- **The speedrun**: Type 4 prohairetic with chosen-route as deliberated-means; runs paradigmatically under *technē-hexis*.
- **The competitive-match plan**: macro-strategy choice at the pre-game; in-match adjustments are micro-prohairesis.
- **Conscious build-crafting**: choosing the stat priority, the talent tree, the loadout.
- **The raid-leader's call**: when the call is *freshly weighed in the moment* (this pull, the boss is enrage-phasing one second earlier, *I am calling we burn now*) it is Type 4 under ethical *hexis*; when the call is *the rehearsed call* (the pre-planned phase-transition trigger) it is Type 4 under *technē-hexis*.

**DIA diagnostic**: **The *technē-hexis* / ethical-hexis split inside Type 4 names the *skilled* vs *wise* distinction precisely**. The player or community that has cultivated only *technē-hexis* prohairetic dispositions is *skilled* but cannot meet the *kairos*; the player or community that has cultivated ethical *hexis* prohairetic dispositions is *phronetic* — capable of fresh judgment when the situation does not match the rehearsed pattern. Game design that depends on rehearsal (PvE scripted content) develops *technē-hexis*; game design that depends on improvisation (PvP, open-ended sandbox) develops ethical *hexis*.

### 4.5 The Cross-cut: technē-hexis (poiēsis) vs ethical-hexis (praxis)

§1.5's bivalence cross-cuts the four types: any of the four may terminate either in a *making* (whose end is a product beyond the doing) or in a *doing* whose end is internal to it.

| Type | Under *technē-hexis* (poiēsis-mode) | Under ethical *hexis* (praxis-mode) |
|---|---|---|
| Type 1 | The auto-pilot grind (the daily, the dailies-as-product) | (Rare: epithymia rarely cultivates ethical hexis; the closest is the *temperate* appetite — Aristotle's *sōphrosynē* (NE III.10–12) — which is genuinely cultivated even at the appetitive level) |
| Type 2 | The script-thymos: the rehearsed indignation-display (streamer-rage as performance) | The *praotēs*-cultivated good-temper anger: anger expressed at the right time, in the right way, toward the right person |
| Type 3 | (Difficult: bouletic action's end is the wished-end, not a separable product; but degenerate cases where the wish has been instrumentalized — wishing-as-a-display of commitment) | The genuine devotional / aspirational engagement: the wished-end as immanent in the wishing-and-doing |
| Type 4 | The expert routine: chosen-once-and-now-rehearsed means (the speedrunner's route) | The *phronetic* call: chosen-freshly each *kairos* |

### 4.6 The compulsion diagnostic: Agency-Purpose collapse as Type-4 → Type-1 regression

The original DIA Part E.1 design-critique diagnostic (compulsion loops = Agency-Purpose collapse) acquires precise ontological articulation here:

> **Compulsion is Type-4 → Type-1 regression**: action that *began* as prohairetic (chosen means to a wished end) sediments via *technē-hexis* runaway into pre-doxastic appetitive loop. The means (the daily-login, the gacha-pull, the grind, the FOMO-gated currency) was once chosen as means to an end the agent had wished. Repeated execution under transparent *technē-hexis* sediments the means as habitual; the original wished-end recedes from the doxastic horizon; the chain begins to fire pre-doxastically at Type 1 level, with the action's "purpose" no longer doxastically articulated. The agent who says "I don't even know why I keep doing this" is reporting the structural fact: the *boulēsis* that originally sourced the action has dissolved, the *prohairesis* has reduced to routine, and the action is firing as Type 1 appetite with no remaining doxastic articulation of why.

This is the precise design-ethics diagnostic. **Healthy progression-design re-poses the boulēsis** before the technē-hexis runaway can complete — supplying a *next* wished-end before the current one is fully consumed and the prohairetic means lose their doxastic ordering. **Dark-pattern design instead engineers the technē-hexis to runaway** — making the means (login, grind, FOMO) into ends so that engagement persists without a subordinating purpose.

## 5. The Pathetic Loop in gameworlds

§1.5's pathetic-loop subsection specifies the recursion in two temporal registers. The integration extends DIA's diagnostic reach into both.

### 5.1 The synchronic register — within an episode

Within a single episode, the chain runs from A₀ through A₄ with the synchronic transitions: dual-resonance deposit at A₁ → *phantasma* at A₂ → orientation-mode engagement + doxastic ratification at A₃ → species-specific *orexis* mobilization at M₃→A₄ → completed action at A₄.

DIA Steps 1–6 are the synchronic-register analysis: Step 1 maps the involvement profile, Step 2 the motive grammar, Step 3 the ratios, Step 4 the crosswalk binding, Step 5 the identification/consubstantiation, Step 6 the attitude register. All six are synchronic.

### 5.2 The diachronic register — across episodes

§1.5 specifies two mechanisms of diachronic modulation:

- **Stimmung-saturation at A₁**: prior chain-passes sediment as the dispositional ground that biases the basic-valence input of the next pass. The cowardly agent (NE) sees enemies in shadows because fear-Stimmung has re-saturated perceptual encounter (De Insomn. 460b1–11).
- **Corrective-faculty impairment at M₂→A₃**: existing emotional states modulate doxastic ratification — the corrective capacity that would normally resist evaluatively-distorting *phantasmata* is weakened under emotional excitement.

DIA's macro-phase analysis was previously specified in Calleja's terms as the off-line / between-session phase. The integration reads this as the *diachronic register* — the macro-phase analysis is the analysis of the diachronic loop's accumulated state.

#### Game-behavioral consequences

- **Stimmung-saturation explains:** the guild's pre-raid mood (positive/negative attitudes the player brings *to* the encounter); the "guild's reputation precedes them" effect (other players' *Stimmung* shapes their perception of the guild); the **post-progression accidie** (consummated god-term drains the Stimmung-charge that subordinated means to ends); the **tilt-spiral** (each thymotic act sediments increased ease of doxastic-ratifying the next slight); the **addicted player's escalating commitment** (each session sediments hexis-deepening that makes the next session more easily engaged).
- **Corrective-faculty impairment explains:** the **toxicity-cascade** at community level (corrective-faculty-impairment runs socially: an angry community ratifies each subsequent slight more readily); the **rage-quit cascade** (one *pathos*-actualization impairs the corrective faculty for the next, until external intervention or somatic exhaustion breaks the recursion); the **streamer-meta-influence** (the streamer's doxa-formation about the patch / game / mechanic propagates as a corrective-faculty-modulating *phantasma* the audience inherits).

### 5.3 The pathetic loop and Burke's "Attitude" as cultivated disposition

Burke's Attitude in the *Rhetoric*/RM sense is *cultivated disposition* in the precise hexis-cultivating sense the dissertation §1.5 articulates. The five player-attitude registers (competitive / completionist / explorer / devotional / ironic-meta — Part B.3 of the DIA manual) are *hexeis* — cultivated through the diachronic recursion.

The integration is consequential:

> The five attitude registers are *not single-episode-states*; they are diachronically-cultivated dispositional architectures from which the player draws at each new chain-pass. The competitive player has cultivated a hexis that disposes them to ratify "winnable contest" content doxastically; the explorer player has cultivated a hexis that disposes them to ratify "navigable mystery" content doxastically; the devotional player has cultivated a hexis that disposes them to ratify "world-substance worth incorporating into" content doxastically; the ironic-meta player has cultivated a hexis that disposes them to ratify "rhetoric-of-the-game" content doxastically; the completionist player has cultivated a hexis that disposes them to ratify "completability" content doxastically.

Each register has its characteristic doxa-ratification pattern and characteristic hexis-bivalence. **DIA's attitude-register analysis is, ontologically, the analysis of the cultivated hexis-state**.

## 6. The revised DIA method with ontological anchoring

The DIA method (Parts A–E of `game-behavior-analysis-method.md`) remains as specified. The ontological wiring adds **anchoring-augmentations** at each step. The method's 8-step protocol with augmentations:

### Step 0 — Scope & circumference (unchanged in form; augmented in scope)

**Original**: fix the episode boundary and the representative anecdote.

**Augmentation**: explicitly specify (a) which entry point of MA 7, 701a32–33 fires the episode — A₁ (sense), A₂ (imagination/*phantasma*), or A₃ (thought) — and (b) whether the episode is bounded within one synchronic chain-pass or spans the diachronic register.

### Step 1 — Involvement profile (Axis I)

**Original**: map all six PIM dimensions × macro/micro with intensity + internalization-trajectory + evidence.

**Augmentation**: cross-tag each high-intensity dimension with its A-node anchor (§2.2 mapping):
- High Kinesthetic-micro = A₁ Kinesthetic-mimetic/symbiotic cornerstone + Kinesthetic-Agency rule-system fluency.
- High Spatial-macro/micro = A₀ Scene-inhabitation criterion met.
- High Shared = A₂ avatar/role identification + A₃ collective-agent ratification.
- High Narrative-macro = diachronic A₄→A₀ alterbiography accumulation.
- High Affective = check whether basic-valence (A₁), proto-attitude saturation (across-chain), or determinate civic-passion (A₃ doxa-ratified).
- High Ludic = **apply the split**: rule-system → A₃ intellection (atemporal universal) vs. choosing-under-repercussion → A₃ deliberative + M₃→A₄ prohairetic.

### Step 2 — Motive grammar (Axis M)

**Original**: fill the six pentad terms; identify featured term(s).

**Augmentation**: for each pentad-term, specify whether the term's filler is at the *anticipated* level (A₀-A₂: yet-to-be-acted-upon) or the *ratified* level (A₃: doxastically committed) or the *enacted* level (A₄: completed). Most especially: Attitude. Is the attitude-register present as basic-valence-Stimmung (A₁ saturation), as cultivated-hexis (the agent's diachronic state), or as concretized civic-passion at A₃?

### Step 3 — Ratio dynamics (unchanged in form; augmented in mechanism)

**Original**: identify operative ratios; name the dominant one.

**Augmentation**: specify each ratio's *transition-of-instantiation*: 
- **Scene-Act** operates primarily at A₀→A₁ (the gameworld constrains the perceptual reception).
- **Act-Agent** operates primarily at A₄→A₀ (the completed act remakes the agent across the recursive loop).
- **Agency-Purpose** operates primarily at A₃→A₄ (means-to-end relation at the doxastic-ratification + prohairetic-choice moments).
- **Scene-Agent** operates primarily at A₀ and A₂ (the gameworld qualifies the agent at the horizon-and-phantasma level).
- **Agent-Agent (co/counter)** operates primarily at A₃ (collective-agent constitution via doxastic ratification of the "we"/"them" *phantasma*).

The dominant-ratio identification thus carries *node-locus* information that the un-anchored DIA grid did not deliver.

### Step 4 — Crosswalk binding (the fusion) — augmented with ontological warrant

**Original**: bind Axis M to Axis I using `validated-crosswalk.json`.

**Augmentation**: each binding is now also chain-locus-tagged per §2. Example outputs:
- "The high Ludic-micro intensity (I) is an Agency-Purpose collapse (M) **operating as Type-4 → Type-1 regression at M₃→A₄**: the rule-system's means (originally chosen prohairetically) have become the player's appetitive ends, doxa-ratification no longer engaging."
- "The Agent×Shared collective-agent constitution (I+M) is achieved **at A₃ via doxastic ratification of the 'we'-*phantasma*** and consummated **at A₄ via joint *praxis*** — the keystone consubstantiation/incorporation event."

### Step 5 — Identification & consubstantiation (the keystone) — augmented with the chain mechanism

**Original**: analyze identifications + locate consubstantiation + assess incorporation rubric R1–R5.

**Augmentation**: identifications are *phantasma*-borne (A₂); consubstantiation is achieved when doxa at A₃ ratifies the *phantasma*-of-acting-together and *orexis* at M₃→A₄ moves toward jointly-realized A₄; **incorporation rubric R1–R5 maps onto chain nodes per §3.3**. Each R-criterion can now be assessed *at its precise chain locus*.

Consubstantiation-rupture is read as A₃-doxastic-re-ratification event with diachronic A₀-shift consequences (§3.5).

### Step 6 — Attitude — augmented with hexis-bivalence

**Original**: identify dominant attitude register + elation↔accidie arc.

**Augmentation**: specify the attitude-register's **hexis-status**: is it operative as *technē-hexis* (cultivated as transparent-routine: the auto-pilot competitive, the script-display thymotic anger) or as ethical *hexis* (cultivated as holding-oneself-open: the *phronetic* competitive, the *praotēs*-cultivated good-temper)? The diagnostic carries directly into Step 7's ends.

### Step 7 — Diagnosis & ends — augmented with the action-type taxonomy

**Original**: synthesize Steps 1–6 into a one-paragraph behavioral diagnosis tied to a use-case.

**Augmentation**: the diagnosis now names (a) the **dominant action-type** (Type 1 / 2 / 3 / 4) the episode foregrounds; (b) the **hexis-mode** under which it runs (*technē-hexis* / ethical *hexis* / no settled hexis); (c) the **chain-locus** at which the dominant ratio's force is concentrated; (d) the **keystone status** (R1-R5 met / partially / not at all; consubstantiation achieved / ruptured / strategic-only); (e) the **diachronic register**'s anticipated downstream effect (the next chain-pass's expected Stimmung-saturation + corrective-faculty-state).

### Step 8 — A new step: Recursion and diachronic outlook

The chain's recursive structure (A₄ → fresh A₀) is not previously thematized in the DIA method's 7-step protocol. Augmenting:

**Step 8 — Recursion and diachronic outlook**: project the analyzed episode's downstream consequences for the next chain-pass. Which hexis is being sedimented? Which *Stimmung* will saturate the next A₁? Which doxai are being added to the *settled-doxai* band? What is the expected next-pass entry-point given this episode's state?

This step is what enables DIA to function as a *career-level* analytic instrument, not just an episode-level one.

## 7. Worked example update — the WoW raid through A₀-A₄

This section threads the `worked-example-wow.md` raid-progression episode through the chain, exhibiting the augmented Step protocol on a worked instance.

### 7.1 Chain entry and A₀

The episode opens with the raid roster logging in at 19:30. **Entry point: A₂ for veterans, A₁ for newer recruits.** The veterans engage the chain with already-actualized *phantasmata* — the boss-arena memorized, the fight-pattern internalized, the guild-as-collective-agent already present as held *phantasma* from prior nights. The newer recruit engages at A₁ — the boss arena delivered fresh by perception, the fight-pattern not yet internalized.

**A₀ content:**
- Scene (macro): the persistent WoW gameworld; the guild's server-standing; the raid-tier-progression context.
- Scene (micro): the boss arena.
- Spatial (macro): inhabited gameworld; (micro): foot-positioning + sight-lines.
- Shared (macro): the 25-player roster + guild + Discord theorycraft.
- Agent: the player(s) as ensouled-body holding aisthētikon first-actuality within the gameworld-horizon.
- Time (macro): weeknight schedule, three weeks into progression, server's tier-progression race; (micro): pull-timer, GCD, boss-phase clock.
- *Stimmung*-saturation: pre-raid mood (anticipatory excitement, attendance-pressure, prior wipes still vivid).
- Cultivated hexis-state: the guild's collective competitive-Attitude *hexis* (cultivated across the three weeks of progression); the veterans' *technē-hexis* in rotation; the *philia*-cultivated ethical *hexis* among long-time members.

### 7.2 M₀→A₁ — Perceptual motion

The pull begins. The Scene-Act ratio activates at its perceptual minimum: the boss-room delivers visual + audio + haptic outputs that the player's perceptual apparatus receives. *Aisthēma* + *epithymia* co-actualize.

**Three-factor schema instance:**
- Unmoved originator: A₀-horizon + sensible-object active potency (the rendered boss, the audio-cue, the haptic).
- Moved mover: the sense faculty (visual processing + controller-as-motor-mediation).
- Moved: the player.

The basic-valence Stimmung is operative throughout: the veterans encounter the boss-as-familiar-foe-to-be-defeated; the recruit encounters it as unfamiliar-challenge-to-be-survived.

### 7.3 A₁ — Completed perception + dual resonance

A₁ deposits:
- *Resonant aisthēma*: the boss-form, the room-layout, the position-of-other-raiders.
- *Resonant epithymia*: the orectic charge toward "boss-as-killable-target" and away from "boss-mechanic-as-painful-event."

**Calleja-bindings activated:**
- Scene ↔ Spatial: the boss-arena as inhabited place (veterans: cornerstone met; recruit: cornerstone still being met).
- Agency ↔ Kinesthetic: the rotation as fluent execution-pole (veterans: fluent; recruit: conscious).

### 7.4 M₁→A₂ — Phantastic motion

The *phantasia* takes up the *resonant kinēsis* and crystallizes a *phantasma* of "this-boss-in-this-phase-now." The phantasma is determinate, available, representational. It carries the dual-aspect inheritance — formal-eidetic (boss-pattern-as-recognized) + affective-orectic (boss-as-to-be-defeated).

Crucially: the *phantasma* of "me-as-tank" / "me-as-healer" / "me-as-DPS" is also actualized — the avatar/role identification. The Hazlitt-Burke identification mechanism (§1.3) fires: the warmth of present *phantasma* identifies the future-self ("the role I am about to execute") with present-being-of-me.

### 7.5 A₂ — The *phantasma* proper

The pivotal actuality. Multiple *phantasmata* are operative: "the boss in phase 2," "me-as-this-role," "we-as-the-raid-now-mid-pull." All available for cognitive engagement at A₃.

The Burke-Calleja binding **Agent ↔ Shared** is structurally seated here: the *phantasma*-of-"we" is the determinate-available-representational image through which the collective-agent will be doxastically ratified.

### 7.6 M₂→A₃ — Cognitive engagement

Three orientational modes engage:
- **Intellection (atemporal universal)**: the boss-mechanic-as-rule (the universal under which the specific mechanic falls) is grasped — "this is a Knockback-mechanic; that means players must stack at the wall."
- **Memory (retrospective)**: the prior pull's mistake is recalled — "last time we wiped at this point because Bob fell off the platform."
- **Deliberative (prospective practical)**: the next 10 seconds projected — "we will burn here, then transition at 40% to phase 3."

Doxa orthogonally engages: "the boss IS at 65% HP," "we ARE on track for the kill," "the call to burn IS the right call."

**Hexis band feeds in:**
- *Technē-hexis*: the veterans' rotation fires transparently; the call is rehearsed.
- Ethical *hexis*: the raid leader's call to burn this pull (when the situation does not match the rehearsed pattern) is a fresh *kairos*-resolution.

### 7.7 A₃ — Completed cognitive actuality + doxastic ratification

The motive grammar reaches determinacy:
- Act: the projected progression-act (the kill, composed of the in-progress pulls).
- Scene: the boss-arena + the 25-player social field.
- Agent: shifting — player ↔ avatar ↔ role ↔ raid-as-collective-agent.
- Agency: rotation + DKP + voice comms + boss-mods as means.
- Purpose: the god-term (first-kill).
- Attitude: competitive → devotional arc.

**Doxastic ratification** of evaluatively complex content concretizes the civic passions: anticipatory excitement → triumph-rush at the kill, frustration → near-anger at the wipe, anxiety → relief at the avoid.

### 7.8 M₃→A₄ — Orektikon motion (Four Types in operation)

**Multiple Types fire across the episode:**
- **Type 4 prohairetic** dominates the rotation execution (chosen means, deliberate execution).
- **Type 3 bouletic** dominates the overall progression-wish (the kill as wished end).
- **Type 1 epithymetic** fires for the kinesthetic reflexes (the dodge, the parry-reflex) and possibly for some appetitive sub-loops (the loot-anticipation dopamine-hit).
- **Type 2 thymotic** fires for the passed-over player's loot-grievance and for any retaliatory in-raid social moves.

### 7.9 A₄ — Completed action + recursion

**The kill (pull 17 success):**
A₄ is the convergence: bodily-realized motion + formal *eidos* of the action (the determinate "killing-of-this-boss") + efficient-trigger (*orexis* mobilized through the *orektikon*) + final-cause (the realizable good, the kill).

**Keystone met for veterans during the successful pull:**
- R1 assimilation: the arena absorbed.
- R2 embodiment: avatar-as-role.
- R3 simultaneity: the joint actualization of all players + the boss-encounter at one act-under-multiple-logoi.
- R4 cornerstone: spatial + kinesthetic.
- R5 blending: Shared + Ludic + Kinesthetic + Affective + Narrative co-active.

**Burke's consubstantiation achieved**: "WE killed it" is not figure of speech but the substantial-union event — joined-and-separate (each player remains a distinct locus of motives — their parse, their own loot interest — but the act is achieved by us).

**Consubstantiation-rupture at the loot decision**: doxastic re-ratification of "the collective treats me as subordinate locus." R3 simultaneity fails for that player. The next chain-pass enters A₀ with shifted Stimmung.

**Diachronic outlook (Step 8 augmentation):**
- *Stimmung* deposited at next A₁: collective elation (most of raid) + accidie (passed-over player); long-tail post-progression accidie (the god-term consumed → next session enters with reduced Agency-Purpose ordering).
- Hexis sedimented: deeper *technē-hexis* in this fight; deeper ethical *hexis* in the *philia*-attentive moments (the call to burn, the post-kill lingering); for the passed-over player, a possible incipient hexis of strategic-rather-than-consubstantial belonging.
- Doxai added to the settled-doxai band: "we are a guild that has killed this boss"; "the loot council can be opaque"; "the DKP rules privilege X over Y."

## 8. Theoretical yields of the integration

Closing the Part F stub generates several theoretical yields that the un-anchored DIA grid could not deliver:

### 8.1 The "what is a virtual environment" question

Virtual environments, on the ontological reading, are **designed *Bewandtnisganzheit* (totalities of involvements)** — gameworlds that, like Heidegger's everyday world of the workshop or the kitchen, are constituted by chains of involvement (*Bewandtnis*) within which entities show up as ready-to-hand (*Zuhandenheit*). The game's designed-Umwelt is the A₀-horizon within which player-action is intelligible.

The "virtuality" of the virtual environment is not a deficient mode of the real environment but **a Umwelt designed with specific control over which chain-passes can occur and how the diachronic recursion can sediment**. The designer cultivates the player's *hexis* by structuring the gameworld's affordances and the constraints on the four action-types.

### 8.2 The skill / engagement / addiction differentiation

The integration makes precise three distinctions that game-research has long noted but un-articulated:

- **Skill** = Type 4 prohairetic + *technē-hexis* sedimentation = mechanical mastery at the Agency-Kinesthetic level.
- **Engagement** = Type 3 bouletic + ethical *hexis* cultivation = wished-end-immanent-in-doing + holding-oneself-open for fresh resolution.
- **Addiction** = Type 1 epithymetic + *technē-hexis* runaway + Agency-Purpose collapse = the means-become-end loop firing without doxastic articulation of why.

Each has a distinct ontological signature, a distinct chain-locus, and a distinct design-intervention point.

### 8.3 The hexis-bivalence as ethics-of-design framework

Game-ethics has historically struggled to specify *what* makes a design ethical or unethical without resorting either to consequentialist outcomes (how-much-time-spent-is-too-much) or content-prescription (no-gambling-mechanics). The integration supplies a structural criterion:

> **An ethical design cultivates ethical *hexis* in its players** — designs that demand holding-oneself-open for fresh *kairos*-resolution, that route the chain through doxastic ratification at A₃, that engage the evaluative axis. **An unethical design cultivates pure *technē-hexis* runaway** — designs that engineer the chain to bypass A₃'s evaluative axis, that route through Type-1 pre-doxastic appetitive loops, that drive Agency-Purpose collapse.

This is a structural criterion, not a content-or-outcome criterion. It can apply to any game-genre and any specific mechanic.

### 8.4 The career-level analytic grip

DIA's macro-phase analysis previously specified Calleja's between-session work (forum-posting, theorycrafting, guild-management). The integration extends this into the full diachronic register: the macro-phase is the analysis of the *career-level* recursive accumulation — the diachronic sedimentation of hexis, the accumulation of Stimmung-saturation, the settled-doxai band's growth. **DIA becomes a career-level analytic instrument**, not only an episode-level one.

This is the structural ground for **longitudinal qualitative game-research**: the same protocol that codes a single episode can be applied across a player's career to track the sedimentation of hexis and the shift in chain-pass-entry patterns.

### 8.5 The "what is community" question

Calleja's Shared dimension and Burke's Agent / Mitsein motive complex receive a precise ontological articulation:

> A gaming community is the structural achievement at A₃ of doxastic-ratification of the *phantasma*-of-collective-agent across multiple players. Each player's chain-pass at A₃ ratifies the "we" *phantasma*, and the joint A₄ achievement *consummates the consubstantiation*. A community persists insofar as the diachronic recursion repeatedly produces the joint A₄ achievement, sedimenting the cultivated *hexis* of "we-as-this-community."

A community-rupture (gquit, server-merge-failure, guild-collapse) is the failure of the keystone-event at A₃→A₄ across enough players that the joint chain-pass becomes structurally impossible. The DIA E.3 social-behavior-analyses gain new precision: what looks like "guild drama" is *consubstantiation-rupture at A₃ with diachronic A₀-shift consequences* — and this can be measured (which doxai have been re-ratified? which Stimmung is now saturating the next pass? which hexis is being sedimented?).

### 8.6 The "what is fun" question — articulationally rather than reductively

The fun-question can finally be addressed without collapsing into Csikszentmihalyi's flow-condition or reductive reward-schedule operationalization:

> **Fun is articulationally graded across the four types and the hexis-bivalence**. The pleasant *epithymia* of the dopamine-hit is Type-1 pleasure (the slot-machine, the lockbox); the satisfied *boulēsis* of the achieved-wish is Type-3 pleasure (the cleared boss, the legendary obtained); the executed *prohairesis* of the deliberate-means-paid-off is Type-4 pleasure (the clean rotation, the speedrun PB); the discharged *thymos* of the avenged-slight is Type-2 pleasure (the PvP-revenge, the rival-defeat). All four can be present; all four are articulationally distinct. **Healthy game design cultivates the higher types alongside Type 1**, not just Type 1 at the expense of higher.

This is a structural ethics-of-fun criterion that does not require dismissing the lower-type pleasures (Type-1 pleasure is genuine) but does require not letting them displace the higher-type pleasures (which is what dark-pattern design effectively does — engineering Type-4 → Type-1 regression).

### 8.7 The dissertation's reciprocal yield from DIA

The integration is not asymmetric. DIA also yields back to the dissertation:

> The DIA grid and method **operationalize the dissertation's actualization chain in a specific domain (virtual environments) where the chain's structural features become empirically accessible**. The diachronic register of the chain (the pathetic loop) is methodologically difficult to access in everyday ethical analysis — the agent's hexis-sedimentation occurs over years and is not directly observable. In virtual environments, by contrast, the diachronic recursion is *time-compressed* (career arcs of months rather than decades), *behaviorally-traced* (logs, parses, recordings), and *socially-saturated* (multi-player coordination making the consubstantiation-event visible). DIA is the methodological apparatus that makes the chain's diachronic register empirically tractable.

This reciprocal yield positions the dissertation's chapter on game-environment analysis as not merely an applied chapter but as the **load-bearing empirical case** for the chain's diachronic-register claims.
