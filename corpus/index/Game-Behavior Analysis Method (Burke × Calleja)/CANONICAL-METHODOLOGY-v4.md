## 0. The method in one paragraph

ADA analyzes a player's behavior as **the actualization of desire**: the movement of a disposed, desiring being from perceptual encounter to completed action (the A₀→A₄ chain), driven by an object of desire (*orekton*), within a world *designed* to intensify, pattern, and sediment that movement. Its central claim is that a designed world drives this actualization toward a **substantial union — *rhetorical incorporation*** — in which the player becomes one-with the world she acts *in* and one-with the avatar/others she acts *as* and *with*, while remaining a distinct locus; and that this union, once achieved, **sediments into the player's dispositional ground (*hexis*)** and can therefore **reshape her being-in-the-world beyond the game**. Calleja's *incorporation* and Burke's *identification/consubstantiation* are not two lenses applied in turn; they are re-grounded as the **two faces of this one event**, and their descriptive apparatus is absorbed as vocabulary for the world's *designed channels* and for the *rhetoric* by which motive is rendered.

## 1. The original object and the contribution to knowledge

**The object** is not "game involvement" (Calleja) or "the grammar of motive" (Burke) but **the actualization of desire in a designed world** — an ontological process the dissertation constructs from Aristotle (*De Anima*, *De Motu Animalium*, *Rhetorica*) and Heidegger, which neither game-studies nor dramatism possesses.

**The contribution (one sentence):** *an ontological account of how designed worlds actualize desire into a substantial union with world-and-others — re-grounding Calleja's incorporation and Burke's consubstantiation as the two faces of a single event — and thereby of how game experience can reshape a player's being-in-the-world.*

**Why this is original, not a coordination of two frameworks:**
1. **The generative engine is the dissertation's own** — the A₀→A₄ chain. Calleja and Burke have no such structure; here it does the organizing work their grids did in v2.
2. **The two kept kernels are re-grounded, not imported.** *Incorporation* ceases to be a psychological intensification of involvement (Calleja's experientialist-cognition grounding) and becomes the chain's **world-axis substantial union**; *consubstantiation* ceases to be symbolic persuasion (Burke's substance-rhetoric) and becomes the chain's **social-axis union that *sources* action**. The re-grounding is the originality.
3. **The fusion is the dissertation's.** Neither author has *both* faces, and neither grounds the union in the actualization of desire. Unifying them — via DA III.7 "one substrate, two in being" extended to social substance — is a new theoretical object: *rhetorical incorporation*.
4. **The method generates claims neither source can** (§6): the union *as a source* of action; *phantasia-conscription*; the *agent→patient* inversion; *hexis*-engineering; *push-at-the-bia-limit*; the *virtual→actual carry-over* (ontological change).

This is **sublation** (*preserve–transform–elevate*), and — fittingly — the method enacts its own thesis: it takes Calleja and Burke as *matter* and the chain gives them new *form*. ADA is itself an actualization of desire.

## 2. The ontological frame — every episode is a *motion*

> **Why this section exists.** The chain does not float. Every behavior ADA reads is, in Aristotle's own terms, a **motion of a substance, explained by the four causes**; the actualization-of-desire chain (§3) is the *fine structure of one of those causes* — the efficient-final causation of a desiring agent — not a free-standing apparatus. Seating it here makes the method continuous with the dissertation's own ontology (Part I §§3–4) and forecloses the objection that the chain's five nodes are stipulated rather than derived. The frame is established **once**; thereafter it is invoked **only where it does work** (Step 0; the causal diagnosis), never run as a per-beat checklist.

**The frame in one paragraph.** Whatever an analysis takes as its episode — a brawl, a heist, a forced cutscene — is ontologically a **change (*metabolē*) undergone by a substance, indexed to a time**: a determinate matter–form complex passing from potency to act. Every such change is explained by the **four causes** (material · formal · efficient · final — *Met.* I.3, 983a24–32; *Phys.* II.3, 194b24–195a3) and, insofar as it is a motion, transmitted through the **three-factor schema** (relative unmoved mover → moved mover → moved). When the substance is *a rational animal acting*, the efficient and final causes are individuated by Aristotle's **seven causes of action** (*Rhet.* I.10), whose desire-sourced interior — the part where a rational animal's own motion "gets started and directed" — is precisely the **actualization-of-desire chain (A₀→A₄, §3)**. The chain is the magnification of a single cell of the four-cause grid; this section lays the grid so the magnification is grounded.

### 2.1 · The episode as a motion of a substance

The overarching term is **actuality** — *energeia* in its **broad sense**, being-actual as such. (*Energeia* bears two senses in Aristotle: this broad one — actual at all — and the **narrow** one below, the self-complete activity. Holding them apart is what lets a *kinēsis* be called an *energeia atelēs* without contradiction.) Within actuality, Aristotle marks a contrast that is load-bearing for games — two ways an actuality stands to its *telos*. The locus classicus is *Met.* IX.6, 1048b18–34, which divides "these processes" into "movements" and "actualities":

- ***kinēsis*** (motion) — the **actuality of a potential *qua* potential** (*Phys.* III.1, 201a10–11). The potentiality remains *operative and present throughout*, because the *telos* lies **outside** the activity: "of the actions which have a limit none is an end but all are relative to the end" (1048b18–19). Building reaches completion only when the house stands *apart from* the building, so one cannot "at the same time … be building and have built." The end **cancels** the motion (the house finished, building stops: "the process would have had sometime to cease, as the process of making thin ceases"). "Every movement is incomplete — making thin, learning, walking, building" — an ***energeia atelēs***.
- ***energeia*** (narrow) — the **actuality whose *telos* is internal and co-present**; "that in which the end is present is an action." "At the same time we are seeing and have seen, are understanding and have understood, are thinking and have thought … we are living well and have lived well." The potentiality-*qua*-potentiality is **absorbed into the act, not preserved**; the activity does "not cease." The end **sustains** the activity: seeing does not terminate in sight.

"Of these processes, then, we must call the one set movements, and the other actualities" (1048b28–29): the formula *actuality of the potential qua potential* is the mark of ***kinēsis* alone** — the contrast against which *energeia* (narrow) is set off — **not** a heading standing above both.

Part I §3.2 already grounds the chain in this distinction (*kinēsis* as *energeia atelēs*): its **nodes are the actualities the chain reaches; its links are motions (*kinēseis*), each named from its terminus**. One consequence: **the terminal act A₄ is itself one or the other** — a *poiēsis*, whose end is a product *beyond* the doing (the built fort, the looted reward — a *kinēsis*), or a *praxis*, whose end is *in* the doing (the play, the comportment — an *energeia*). Much of design's work converts the one into the other; and the *technē*-hexis **capture** (§6) is exactly a *kinēsis* whose wished-*telos* has dropped out, so that it runs on without reaching the completion that would cancel it, yet never becomes a self-sustaining *energeia* — an endless restoration (cf. pleasure-as-restoration, itself a *kinēsis* of the soul, *Rhet.* I.11, 1369b34–1370a5; against pleasure as *energeia*, *NE* X).

A change is further specified by its **category** — Step 0's first question:

| Kind of change | Greek | In a designed world |
|---|---|---|
| substance (coming-to-be / passing-away) | *genesis* / *phthora* | crafting/spawning; death — *strictly* a *metabolē*, **not** a *kinēsis* (*Phys.* V.1) |
| quality (alteration) | *alloiōsis* | a state changed — health, attunement, **the player's own *hexis*** |
| quantity (growth / diminution) | *auxēsis* / *phthisis* | leveling, resource accrual |
| place (locomotion) | *phora* | avatar movement, traversal |

**Whose** substance changes must be named. Two series run at once: the **player-substance**, undergoing perceptual→cognitive→dispositional *alloiōsis* that sediments as *hexis* — *this is the chain*; and the **world/avatar-substance**, altered by the player's *poiēsis*. They meet at **A₄**, where one act is at once a *praxis* (it alters the agent) and a *poiēsis* (it alters the world). And the method's central event, *rhetorical incorporation* (§4), re-describes from this angle as the **designed convergence of the two substance-series toward "one substrate, two in being"** (DA III.7) — a second derivation of the keystone, from substance rather than from union. *(This is also where "ontological change" gains precision: the capstone's supplanting of dispositional ground is an *alloiōsis* of the ethical *hexis* so deep it strains toward — but, per Aristotle, cannot literally be — a *genesis* of new character.)*

### 2.2 · The four causes, and the designer/player partition

The four causes are stated twice in the texts the method leans on — *Met.* I.3 (983a24–32: substance/essence · matter/substratum · source-of-change · "that for the sake of which and the good") and *Phys.* II.3 (194b24–195a3). Two features do specific work.

First, the **final cause is the terminus of a continuous change**: "if a thing undergoes a continuous change toward some end, that last stage is actually that for the sake of which" (*Phys.* II.2, 194a28–31). This licenses reading the whole A₀→A₄ chain as **one teleological motion** individuated by its *orekton* — not five separate events.

Second, "there are **several causes of the same thing** (not merely accidentally)" (*Phys.* II.3, 195a4–14): the statue is caused at once by the sculptor (efficient) and the bronze (material). A designed world is just such plural causation — which gives the method's signature move ("what the world *supplies* vs what the player *does*") an ontological backbone: **the four causes partition between designer and player.**

| Cause | The designer supplies | The player supplies |
|---|---|---|
| **Material** | assets, affordances, the "stuff" acted with | — (receives) |
| **Formal** | rules, level-design, the **avatar-form** given | takes on / inhabits the form |
| **Efficient** | the *trigger* — "why **now**" (scripting, spawns, prompts) | her own moving principle (the seven causes → the chain) |
| **Final** | the **designed** *telos* (win-condition, intended experience) | her **own** *orekton* |

The partition is warranted by Aristotle's distinction between the two arts (*Phys.* II.2, 194a34–b5): the art that **uses** a product "knows the form" and "is in a sense directive"; the art that **produces** it "knows the matter." The **designer is the producing-art** (knows the matter); the **player is the using-art** (knows the form / the for-the-sake-of-which, and is *directive*) — dignifying the player as **co-author**, not a material recipient: the ontological warrant for *phantasia*-conscription and alterbiography (§6).

Two diagnostics fall out — **final-cause divergence** (designed *telos* vs player *orekton* coming apart = emergent/counter-play; collapsing = one face of capture) and **efficient-cause splitting** ("why now" = designer's; "which way" = player's — the ontological form of the *felt-vs-structural* axis). And two further features anchor downstream instruments: **reciprocal causation** — "hard work causes fitness and *vice versa* … the one as end, the other as the principle of motion" (195a4–14) — is the **synchronic↔diachronic *hexis* spiral** verbatim (act sediments *hexis*; *hexis* grounds act); and the **potential/actual** cause — "the cause of a house being built is either a house-builder or a house-builder building" (195b4–6) — is the *hexis*/*energeia* distinction at the level of cause (disposition vs its exercise).

### 2.3 · The three-factor schema: where final and efficient meet in the motion

The three-factor schema — **relative unmoved mover → moved mover → moved** — is not a second overlay competing with the four causes; it is **how the efficient and final causes operate dynamically within a motion**. The four causes answer the four *whys* (a static grid); the schema specifies the *mechanism* of an efficient cause in transit. They meet in animal action: the ***orekton*** (final cause) is the **relative unmoved originator** — "itself remaining unmoved, it originates the movement by being apprehended in thought or imagination"; ***orexis*** (efficient cause) is the **moved mover**; the body, via the joint, is **the moved** (DA III.10, 433b10–18). In every motion of the chain, then, final and efficient cause are *the same actualization under two descriptions* — which is why the chain is **read forward (efficiently, link by link) yet understood backward (finally, from the *orekton*)**. Part I §3.1 establishes the relativization that lets the schema recur at every stage, not only at the cosmological first mover.

### 2.4 · The seven causes: individuating the efficient-final complex

When the moving substance is a human being acting, the efficient-final complex is individuated by Aristotle's **seven causes** (*Rhet.* I.10, 1368b33–1369a6): *chance, nature, compulsion, habit, reasoning, anger, appetite*. Their own structure — stated in the text — yields the A₄ causal layer:

```
  involuntary (not due to the agent) ── chance │ necessity { compulsion · nature }
  voluntary   (due to the agent)     ── habit + desire { rational = wishing · irrational = anger · appetite }
```

This **corrects** the casual mapping earlier drafts carried; the four action-types are *derived, not posited*:
- **chance · nature · compulsion (*bia*)** → the **non-agent branch** (chance set against *necessity*, under which Aristotle groups *both* compulsion and nature).
- **habit (*ethos*)** → **not** a fourth desire but the **dispositional ground (*hexis*)** through which the desires are exercised — the one voluntary cause that is *not* a species of *orexis*; hence §7, not a Type.
- the three orectic species the text names — **rational desire = wishing (*boulēsis*)**, and the irrational **anger (*thymos*)** and **appetite (*epithymia*)** — are **Types 3, 2, 1**. ("Reasoning," in the seven-fold list, *is* this rational desire: "Rational desire is wishing, and wishing is a desire for good.")
- **Type 4, *prohairesis***, is **not** in the *Rhetorica*'s list — it is supplied from *NE* III.2–3 as **deliberated desire** (choice of means to a wished end), and is latent already in the *Rhetorica*'s gloss of reasoning as "useful either as ends or as contributing to an end" (1369a32–b29). *Boulēsis* wishes the end; *prohairesis* chooses the means.

Two further yields of the same passage become Step-4 instruments. **Voluntariness** turns on the locus of the *archē*: external = *bia* ("compulsion … contrary to the desire or reason of the agents themselves," 1369a32–b29), internal = voluntary (*NE* III.1) — and *all* voluntary action reduces to "the good or the pleasant," i.e. to the *orekton* (the apparent good). And Aristotle's **definitions of the causes are the diagnostic criteria**: *chance* = that "whose cause cannot be determined, that has no purpose … no fixed way" — a definition in-game RNG **fails** (it is an authored distribution), so designed "chance" is *technē* wearing the mask of *tychē*.

### 2.5 · The seat of the chain, and a discipline

The chain (A₀→A₄, §3) is, then, the **interior of the efficient-final cause of voluntary, desire-sourced action** — the magnification of the single causal cell at which a rational animal's own motion is started and directed. This secures its originality: the chain is not a stipulation but the **systematic elaboration of Aristotle's own account of action**, thickened with Heidegger's temporal-dispositional structure (Part I) and turned toward designed worlds. Calleja and Burke are absorbed *within* this frame — Calleja's channels name *where the designed efficient cause presses on the chain*; Burke's pentad names *how the resulting motion's cause is rendered and concealed* (§5).

> **Discipline (so the frame seats rather than swallows).** This apparatus is *general* metaphysics — true of houses and stones no less than players. The method's living work is the phenomenology of the chain and the union; the frame is established **once**, here, and thereafter **invoked only where it does work** — at Step 0 (classify the change; name the substance; partition the causes designer/player) and at the causal-diagnosis step (the seven causes; voluntariness; the *bia* test). It is **not** a per-beat checklist; running it mechanically would re-import exactly the exhaustiveness the method shed when it demoted the 6×6 grid (Appendix A).

## 3. The generative spine — the actualization of desire (A₀→A₄)

**The chain is the fine structure of the efficient-final cause individuated in §2** — the interior of the one causal slot at which a rational animal's own motion is started and directed; what follows magnifies that slot. Every analyzed behavior is a **pass** (or set of passes) of the chain. The chain is read forward, understood backward; each node is an actuality (an *energeia*), each link a motion (a *kinēsis* named from its terminus, §2.1), and the **object of desire (*orekton*) is the unmoved originator** — "itself remaining unmoved, it originates the movement by being apprehended in thought or imagination" (DA III.10, 433b10–18). The three-factor kinetic schema (*unmoved originator → moved mover [orexis] → moved [the animal via the joint]*) holds at every motion.

| Node | What it is | Greek |
|---|---|---|
| **A₀** | Ontological horizon: sensible object + sense-faculty within motion & time (the **designed *Umwelt***) | *aisthēton + aisthētikon · kinēsis kai chronos* |
| **A₁** | Completed perception + dual residue (form + hedonic tone) | *aisthēsis · resonant aisthēma + resonant epithymia · paschein* |
| **A₂** | The *phantasma* proper — determinate, available, representational charged image (the pivot) | *phantasia / phantasma · "making-present-as"* |
| **A₃** | Completed cognition: intellection / memory / discursive + the orthogonal committal dimension *doxa* | *nous · mnēmē · phantasia bouleutikē · doxa* |
| **A₄** | Completed action (*praxis*); recursive — sediments into *hexis* | *orexis → orektikon → praxis* |

**Individuation at A₄ — why *this* action.** The causal layer is the seven causes of *Rhetorica* I.10, fully derived in **§2.4**; in brief it bifurcates:

```
  NON-agent (involuntary) ─ chance (designed RNG) │ necessity { compulsion (bia — force; §6) · nature (reflex / pre-doxastic kritikon recoil) }
  AGENT (voluntary, desire-sourced) ─ habit (the hexis-ground, §7) through which one orectic species fires:
        epithymia → Type 1 Epithymetic · thymos → Type 2 Thymotic · boulēsis → Type 3 Bouletic
        + prohairesis → Type 4 Prohairetic (deliberated desire, NE III.2–3 — not in Rhet. I.10's seven)
```

Most play is **agent-caused** (the player freely actualizing desire = the four types — Burke's "why, given free choice"); the **non-agent branch** is where *the world acts on the player* (designed chance; reflex; and **bia** — the forced/cutscene limit). **The most consequential analytic moments are where action crosses the bar between branches** (a forced cutscene stripping a voluntary agent of agency → §6). The four types are not compartments; they name the **predominant source-desire**; *doxa* cross-cuts all four; civic passions (anger, fear, pity, shame…) are the *species of being-affected* through which a desire is sourced, not a type.

## 4. The central event — *rhetorical incorporation* (the substantial union, two-faced)

This is the method's original core. At the **A₃→A₄ transition** the chain achieves a **substantial union** structured by Aristotle's *"one substrate, two in being"* (DA III.7, 431a13–14), extended to **social substance**: at A₃, *doxa* ratifies the *phantasma*-of-acting-together ("WE are doing this"); at M₃→A₄, *orexis* moves toward a **jointly-realized A₄**. The union has **two faces**, one event:

- **World-face — *incorporation* (re-grounded from Calleja).** The player becomes one-with the world she acts *in*. *Not* "intensified involvement" but the chain's world-axis union: the gameworld assimilated into consciousness **and** the player embodied (via avatar) into the world, **held simultaneously**. Assessed by the **R1–R7 differentiator** (Calleja IG-10, pp. 169–173, 178 — see Appendix B): R1 assimilation · R2 embodiment · R3 simultaneity · R4 spatial-kinesthetic cornerstone · R5 multi-dimensional blending · **R6 temporal-apex** (culmination of the macro→micro arc; intensifies with duration) · **R7 intrusion-integration** (real-world stimuli *blend in* rather than rupture — the porosity, §4.3).
- **Other-face — *consubstantiation* (re-grounded from Burke).** The player becomes one-with the avatar she acts *as* and the gang/community she acts *with* — "substantially one … yet a distinct locus of motives, both joined and separate" (RoM 21). *Not* symbolic persuasion but the **A₃ doxa-ratified "WE"**: identification is *phantasma*-borne (seeded at A₂), ratified at A₃, consummated at M₃→A₄.

**The two faces are the same actualization under two *logoi*** — my agency / the collective's, the world's pull / my reaching — realized in one act. *That neither Calleja (world-face only, experientialist grounding) nor Burke (other-face only, symbolic grounding) sees this — and that the dissertation unifies them through the actualization of desire — is the contribution.*

### 4.1 The union as a *source* of action (the headline instrument)
The union is not merely a *state* the chain reaches; it can be **what sources the next action**. A **Type-2 thymotic** act whose *orekton* is *collective* — "back the gang" — is *sourced by* consubstantiation: the gang's slight becomes mine *because* I am substantially one with them (validated on the RDR2 saloon brawl). "Acting-together makes consubstantial" (Burke) and "*thymos* needs a *toward-whom*" (Aristotle) converge: the keystone is generative, not decorative.

### 4.2 Rupture (the union's negative)
- **Agency-rupture** — the union is *stripped* by *bia* (control seized): R3 simultaneity collapses from "joined-and-separate" to "acted-upon." (The rape-scene cutscene; G&G's miniaturization is its *wondrous* sibling — a designed dissimilitude that ruptures the for-the-sake-of-which without trauma.)
- **Social/consubstantiation-rupture** — the collective doxastically re-ratifies the player as a distinct/subordinate locus (the WoW loot-passing-over): the next pass's A₁ *resonant epithymia* shifts from "we" to "I-against-them."

### 4.3 Porosity → the virtual→actual carry-over (why ontological change is possible)
Because the union is **bidirectional habitation, not a sealed plunge**, real-world stimuli *blend in* rather than shattering it (R7; Calleja's anti-magic-circle, IG-10 pp. 171–172). Scaled up and sedimented, this porosity *is* the channel by which what happens in the game **crosses into the player's real-world being-in-the-world** (Calleja's continuity thesis, p. 179) — the ontological-change mechanism the capstone needs.

## 5. The absorbed materials (sublated — used as vocabulary, never as the structure)

> Rule of use: **do not "apply a dimension" or "apply a pentad term."** Name a channel or a motive-term *only where it illuminates a chain-stage or the union*. The chain and the union structure the analysis; these supply its vocabulary.

### 5.1 Calleja → the **designed channels of actualization**
The six involvement dimensions are recast as the **routes through which a designed world applies pressure to the chain** and drives it toward incorporation — *where* the engineering acts (the efficient cause pressing on the chain, §2.2), not an inventory to fill:

| Channel | The designed pressure it exerts | Chain locus it works on |
|---|---|---|
| **Kinesthetic** | control→fluency (symbolic→mimetic→**symbiotic**); the substrate of the other five | A₁ fluency-residue → *technē*-hexis; M₃→A₄ execution |
| **Spatial** | space internalized as inhabited **place** (the cornerstone) | A₀ horizon + A₁ inhabitation |
| **Shared** | co-/counter-agents; cohabitation→cooperation→competition | A₂ *phantasma*-of-role + A₃ "WE" (→ other-face of the union) |
| **Narrative** | scripted ↔ **alterbiography**, delivered as **push** (cut scene, *suspends* Act; Purpose-saturated; *unskippable → bia*) or **pull** (diaries/lore the player must *seek*; releases Purpose to player-Act; the synthetic integration *is* alterbiography) — Levine 2008 via IG-07 | push → designer-supplied *phantasma* at A₂/A₃; pull → *orexis*-driven seeking → A₄; alterbiography → A₄→A₀′ |
| **Affective** | basic valence (Stimmung) + designed-vs-precipitated emotion; transverse | A₁ tone + A₃ civic-passion concretization |
| **Ludic** | the rule-system (means) + choosing-under-repercussion (the wager) | rule-system → A₃ intellection/Agency; choosing → A₄ |

### 5.2 Burke → the **rhetoric of motive**
The pentad/ratios are recast as **how the actualized action is rendered intelligible and how its dispositional source is concealed or attributed** — Burke's unique yield, used *after* the chain has produced the action:
- **Motive-attribution / the ratios:** what does the player attribute the behavior to — the situation (Scene–Act), her own development (Act–Agent), the tools (Agency–Purpose), the others (Agent–Agent)? The dominant ratio is the episode's motive-engine; the *concealed* term is often the player's own sedimented *hexis*.
- **Identification** (elevated, not merely listed): the *phantasma*-borne bond that *is* the other-face of the union (§4) — its Hazlitt mechanism (warmth/liveliness of the *phantasma* makes the other present-as-mattering, RoM 83–84) is the dissertation's own A₂ bridge.
- **Attitude** (the sixth term): the disposition-with-which (competitive / completionist / explorer / devotional / ironic-meta) — read as *hexeis* (§7), not as a static label.
- **Social-motive cluster** (where relevant): hierarchy / mystery / courtship (the Shared field's structure) and victimage / the scapegoat (discharge of hierarchic guilt — toxicity, out-grouping).

## 6. The method's own instruments (the emergent originality — belonging to no source)

These are the concepts ADA generates that are neither Calleja's, nor Burke's, nor plain Aristotle's. They are the analytic's distinctive yield:

1. **The union as a source of action** (§4.1) — consubstantiation/incorporation *sourcing* a chain-pass, not just crowning one.
2. **Phantasia-conscription** — when a world *withholds* depiction, it supplies a deliberately **under-determined *phantasma*** at A₂ and conscripts the player's own *phantasia* to author the unshown; *what* gets authored is fixed by her settled *doxai*/*hexis* (adult ratifies "rape," child "robbery"). Withholding can be *more* forceful than showing — the player co-authors her own experience. (*enargeia* is its positive-space twin.)
3. **The agent→patient (*praxis*→*paschein*) inversion** — *bia* forces a **de-actualization**: it drags the player from the A₄ agent-pole back to the A₁ patient-pole, as **destructive *paschein* (*phthora*)**, not the preservative *paschein* (*sōtēria*) of ordinary perception. The structural account of a scene's ontological violence.
4. **The two compulsions** — *bia* (external force; non-agent branch; the cutscene) **vs** *technē*-hexis **capture** (habituated runaway where the wished-end drops out; the agent branch; the grind/dark-pattern). Kept strictly distinct (v1 conflated them); the *energeia*/*kinēsis* frame (§2.1) names the difference precisely — *bia* annuls the *energeia* of agency, capture is a *kinēsis* whose *telos* has dropped out. *Unskippable **push** narrative = push-at-the-bia-limit* (§5.1).
5. ***Hexis*-engineering (skill ≠ wisdom, by design)** — a world cultivates *technē*-hexis (procedural transparency, prompted mechanically) and/or ethical *hexis* (the evaluative "how," staged narratively); the bivalence is the method's primary diagnostic of what a design *makes of a player* (§7).
6. **The 2D↔VR media differential** — VR doesn't change the chain; it intensifies *how it is entered*: A₀ sensory **supplanting** (HMD), A₁ **symbiotic** bodily kinesthesis (room-scale 1:1), **literal** R2 embodiment, and **veri(dis)similitude** (verisimilar enough to throw-in, dissimilar enough to have ontological force; the dissimilar shock = a designed incorporation-rupture).
7. **The virtual→actual carry-over** (§4.3) — the ontological-change mechanism: sedimented union + porosity supplant the prior dispositional ground.

## 7. The dispositional ground and the two registers

**The player never enters as a blank slate.** Every pass is conditioned by prior *doxai*, attunements, and especially the bivalent ***hexis*** (the habit-cause of §2.4, the *archē* of the agent-branch):
- ***technē*-hexis** — cultivated procedural transparency (controller fluency, parry timing, rotations); *reduces deliberation*; withdraws like ready-to-hand equipment.
- **ethical *hexis*** — the socially/evaluatively inflected "how" of being-in-action (what one takes as fitting, shameful, cruel, honorable); *holds-oneself-open*; exercises a two-level reach (modulates A₁ basic-valence + A₃ doxastic ratification). Names the **skill ≠ wisdom** distinction precisely.

**Two registers:**
- **Synchronic** — one directional pass A₀→A₄ within a bounded episode.
- **Diachronic** — how completed actions **sediment into *hexis*** and recondition future passes (the **reciprocal causation** of §2.2: act as efficient → *hexis* as end → *hexis* as principle of the next motion). Two mechanisms: **Stimmung-saturation** (affective history biases basic valence at A₁ — "the tone") and **corrective-faculty impairment** (states bias *doxa* at M₂→A₃ — "the figure," via the **pathetic loop**, re-entering at the *forming* phantasma M₁→A₂). The chain therefore **spirals**, not merely iterates; this is what makes design pacing consequential and ontological change possible.

## 8. The protocol (chain-generative — 8 steps, 0–7)

> The order *enacts the architecture*: frame the motion, trace the actualization, diagnose the union, then read motive, cause, and sediment. The §2 frame is invoked at Step 0 and Step 4 only. Calleja's channels and Burke's motive-rhetoric appear **as sub-moments of chain-steps, never as separate passes.** Output: a chain-trace, a union-verdict, a one-paragraph diagnosis, and (diachronic) a carry-over forecast.

- **Step 0 — Frame (with the ontological seat, §2).** Scope/circumference; **scale** (discrete / protracted / ontological-change); **entry point** (A₀-sense / A₂-imagination / A₃-thought); register; **name the *orekton*** — individual or **collective/identificatory** (the latter signals a union-sourced act). Then the §2 framing where it earns its place: **classify the change** (substance/quality/quantity/place) and the **substance(s)** undergoing it (player-series / world-series); note the **motion-form** (*praxis*/*poiēsis*); where the supply/agency question is live, **partition the four causes** designer↔player.
- **Step 1 — Trace the actualization.** Walk A₀→A₄ for the episode; at each motion fill the three-factor schema; note **what the designed world supplies vs what the player does** (the efficient-cause partition, §2.2). *Name a Calleja channel only where the world is applying designed pressure at that stage* (§5.1).
- **Step 2 — Diagnose the union (*rhetorical incorporation*).** Assess **both faces** at A₃→A₄: world-face via **R1–R7**; other-face via **consubstantiation** (with what/whom; genuine / strategic / ruptured). Verdict: union **achieved / partial / merely-involved / ruptured** (agency or social). **Decisive question: does the union *source* the action (§4.1)?**
- **Step 3 — Read the motive-rhetoric (Burke, absorbed).** Dominant ratio; what the player **attributes** the action to; which dispositional source is **concealed**; attitude register; any hierarchy/mystery/courtship or victimage.
- **Step 4 — Causal & type diagnosis (the individuation of the efficient-final complex, §2.4).** 7-causes (agent / non-agent / **bia**): involuntary {chance │ necessity{compulsion·nature}} vs voluntary {habit-as-ground + an orectic species}; if agent → predominant **type (1–4)** (*epithymia*/*thymos*/*boulēsis*; *prohairesis* per *NE* III.2–3). **Voluntariness** = locus of the *archē* (the *bia* test: "contrary to the desire or reason of the agent") with the **felt-vs-structural** axis (mechanically chosen / execution-only-foreclosed / framing-only); **hexis-mode** (*technē* / ethical / capture).
- **Step 5 — Phantasmatic & affective specifics.** **Phantasia-conscription** (withheld content authored by *hexis*/*doxa*?); pathetic-loop effects (tone @M₀→A₁ · figure @M₁→A₂ · gate @A₂→A₃); **agent→patient inversion** if *bia*.
- **Step 6 — Diachronic & carry-over.** Which *hexis* sediments; Stimmung/*doxai* for the next pass; **(ontological scale)** the **virtual→actual carry-over** + the **supplanting-of-dispositional-ground verdict**.
- **Step 7 — Diagnosis.** One paragraph naming: the union-state + *whether it sourced the act*; the action-type & cause; the attitude/hexis register; the *hexis* sedimented; the downstream/carry-over.

## 9. Scale variants
- **Discrete event** (a boss fight / one beat): Steps 0–5, single synchronic pass; decompose internal sub-beats (trigger / escalation / climax / resolution).
- **Protracted sequence** (RDR2 tutorial): a *sequence* of passes + heavy Step 6; the unit is the **engineered *hexis*-bundle** across passes; Calleja macro/micro and R6 temporal-apex are load-bearing.
- **Ontological change** (the rape scene): a single **rupture** pass — Step 2 foregrounds rupture, Step 4 *bia*, Step 5 phantasia-conscription + the agent→patient inversion; Step 6 = the **carry-over + supplanting verdict** (the capstone claim, via §4.3 porosity).
