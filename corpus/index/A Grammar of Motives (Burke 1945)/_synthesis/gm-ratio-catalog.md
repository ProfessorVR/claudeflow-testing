# Burke Ratio Catalog

**10 Dyadic Ratios + Higher-Order Combinations**
*Subsidiary to Pentad Operationalization Manual Section B*

Burke (*Grammar of Motives* 1945, GM-01 p.19) singles out **scene-act** and **scene-agent** as the only ratios "fitting with complete comfort" in the container-and-thing-contained framework; **act-agent** "tugs at its edges"; the remaining seven require additional dialectical apparatus.

All 10 ratios are Burke-direct (Burke develops at length in GM-01, with selected reasoned-extensions for under-developed dyads). All Calleja PIM-pair mappings are **anticipatory-projection** (dissertation): Burke 1945 predates Calleja by 66 years.

---

## R-SCENE-ACT (scene-act)

- **Operational gloss.** Quality of scene implies (contains, calls forth) correspondingly qualified act; convertibly, act demands or constructs a scene of corresponding quality. Reversible; deterministic or hortatory.
- **Directionality.** Reversible. Forward: scene calls forth act. Reverse: act constructs scene (Lavinia's command, GM-01 pp.5-6). Burke classification: **positional_strong**.
- **Philosophic-school deployment.** Primary: **Materialism** (Marxist 'objective situation'; Darwinian environmental determinism). Secondary: Idealism (Hegelian Reason-as-Grundprinzip); Realism (cosmic-scene → cosmic-act).
- **Paradigm literary examples.** Ibsen *Enemy of the People* — bourgeois sitting-room / editorial office / disordered study (GM-01 pp.3-5); O'Neill *Mourning Becomes Electra* (Lavinia nails shutters; pp.5-6); Horatio's Hamlet warning (p.6); Coleridge Pantisocracy (p.14).
- **Candidate game-user-motive deployment.** How does the gameworld's affordance-structure constrain or enable the session-act? Lavinia-reversal: player-built bases in survival games become the scene of subsequent play.
- **Candidate Calleja PIM-pair.** **SPATIAL ↔ LUDIC** primary; with Kinesthetic modulation in heavy-navigation cases.

## R-SCENE-AGENT (scene-agent)

- **Operational gloss.** Synecdochic correspondence: place-quality implies inhabitant-quality. Positional in strong sense.
- **Directionality.** Reversible. Reverse: body-as-scenic (theologians treat body as dwelling-place of soul, GM-01 p.10). **positional_strong**.
- **Philosophic-school deployment.** Primary: **Idealism** (19th-century *Einklang zwischen Innen- und Aussenwelt*; 'climates of opinion' Becker). Secondary: Materialism (Darwinian environment-shaping-organism); Realism.
- **Paradigm literary examples.** Carlyle on Arabs (GM-01 pp.7-8); Wordsworth "beauteous evening" sonnet (p.8); Swift Laputans (p.9); Seurat pointillism (p.8); Moore basilisk-in-basilica (GM-12).
- **Candidate game-user-motive deployment.** Spatial-internalization phenomenology — how does WoW's Azeroth socialize different agent-types (warrior in Mulgore vs mage in Dalaran)?
- **Candidate Calleja PIM-pair.** **SPATIAL ↔ KINESTHETIC** primary; with Shared modulation in multi-agent environments.

## R-SCENE-AGENCY (scene-agency)

- **Operational gloss.** Scene shapes available agencies; new agencies transform the scene. Reverse direction is Burke's signature **Biddle pattern**.
- **Directionality.** Reversible. Reverse: agencies-in-accumulation become scenic. **instrumental_methodological**.
- **Philosophic-school deployment.** Primary: **Pragmatism** (agencies become scenic — instrument-as-ancestor); with Materialism (terrain-as-affordance).
- **Paradigm literary example.** Francis Biddle: agencies of communication (railroad, telegraph, telephone, automobile, movie, airplane, radio) by accumulation become scenic and motivate political acts (GM-01 p.18).
- **Candidate game-user-motive deployment.** VR-hardware, voice-chat, streaming-overlays, Discord, third-party trackers began as agencies but in accumulation now constitute the operative scene of competitive play.
- **Candidate Calleja PIM-pair.** **SPATIAL ↔ KINESTHETIC** (agency=control surface) OR **SPATIAL ↔ LUDIC** (agency=available game-systems).

## R-SCENE-PURPOSE (scene-purpose)

- **Operational gloss.** Scene shapes conceivable purposes; purpose may project a scene proportioned to it (mystical exegesis).
- **Directionality.** Likely reversible. Burke defers full development to GM-07. **teleological**.
- **Philosophic-school deployment.** Primary: **Mysticism** (Plotinus, Bonaventura — cosmic-scenes proportioned to mystical Purpose). Secondary: Idealism (Hegelian Reason-as-Grundprinzip, GM-01 p.12).
- **Paradigm literary examples.** Hegelian Reason as Grundprinzip (GM-01 p.12); heretical-Christian Judas case (GM-00 p.xxi); mystical sacred geographies.
- **Candidate game-user-motive deployment.** How does Skyrim's open-world scene afford purposes that linear FPS scenes preclude? Narrative-scene-design dictates purpose-structure (rails vs emergent).
- **Candidate Calleja PIM-pair.** **SPATIAL ↔ NARRATIVE** primary; with Ludic modulation.

## R-ACT-AGENT (act-agent)

- **Operational gloss.** Agent authors acts; acts make or remake the agent. NOT strict containment — agent does not contain the act, though results pre-exist virtually within him.
- **Directionality.** **temporal_sequential**. Reverse: acts make or remake agent (offices, vestments shape character).
- **Philosophic-school deployment.** Primary: **Idealism** (agent-character authoring acts; Marxist reverse — class-act remaking class-agent). Secondary: Realism.
- **Paradigm literary examples.** "Sobering Presidency" (GM-01 p.16); priestly vestments transform wearer (p.16); Russian wartime resistance attributed to patriotism not socialism (pp.16-17); "democratic people" perform democratic acts (p.17).
- **Candidate game-user-motive deployment.** Alterbiography emergence (Calleja Ch.7) — what acts have remade this player-agent? How does a 1000-hour main-character history shape current playstyle?
- **Candidate Calleja PIM-pair.** **LUDIC ↔ SHARED** primary; with Kinesthetic modulation.

## R-ACT-AGENCY (act-agency)

- **Operational gloss.** Act overlaps with agency when method (*met-hodos*) becomes means-of-doing rather than act-of-being. Way-words (*hodos*, *tao*, *yoga*, *dike*) drift toward agency under instrumentalist reading.
- **Directionality.** Reversible. **instrumental_methodological**.
- **Philosophic-school deployment.** Primary: **Pragmatism** (act-agency dominance — James, Dewey, Mead).
- **Paradigm literary examples.** Way-words analysis (GM-01 p.15); writing as act overlapping with agency of language.
- **Candidate game-user-motive deployment.** Kinesthetic transparency / Bridgman-operationalist limit-case: in an FGC player's frame-perfect inputs, is the act ("execute the combo") distinguishable from the agency ("press these inputs")?
- **Candidate Calleja PIM-pair.** **LUDIC ↔ KINESTHETIC** primary.

## R-ACT-PURPOSE (act-purpose)

- **Operational gloss.** Act is consummated by realizing its purpose; purpose is articulate only via concrete act. Reciprocal teleological closure.
- **Directionality.** Reversible in principle. **teleological**.
- **Philosophic-school deployment.** Primary: **Mysticism** (Christ "I am the way"); secondary: Realism (Aristotelian act ordered toward telos).
- **Paradigm literary examples.** Hero-file "break bonds (act) in order to escape (purpose)" (GM-00); Aeschylean Erinyes→Eumenides (GM-01 p.20); Keats Beauty-is-truth (GM-10).
- **Candidate game-user-motive deployment.** Emergent-story engine — the act-purpose ratio generates alterbiography narrative arc. How does completing a raid realize the purpose of mastery-display?
- **Candidate Calleja PIM-pair.** **LUDIC ↔ NARRATIVE** primary.

## R-AGENT-AGENCY (agent-agency)

- **Operational gloss.** Agent's character implies fit agencies; habituated agencies constitute character. Symbolic-of-agency mother-locus (GM-07).
- **Directionality.** Reversible. **instrumental_methodological**.
- **Philosophic-school deployment.** Primary: **Pragmatism** (instrument constitutes agent-as-craftsman).
- **Paradigm literary examples.** Seurat pointillism (GM-01 p.8); voter as politician's agency (GM-00); orator's preferred rhetorical figures.
- **Candidate game-user-motive deployment.** How does a "main" character (main-class, main-build) function as Symbolic-of-agency mother-locus for the player? Signature build as agency-constituting-identity.
- **Candidate Calleja PIM-pair.** **SHARED ↔ KINESTHETIC** primary; with Ludic modulation.

## R-AGENT-PURPOSE (agent-purpose)

- **Operational gloss.** Needs are a property of agents (GM-01 p.18); agent-purpose relates an agent's needs to the purposes they generate.
- **Directionality.** Reversible. **teleological**.
- **Philosophic-school deployment.** Primary: **Idealism** (agent's will authoring action); secondary: Mysticism (agent-identifying with cosmic Purpose).
- **Paradigm literary examples.** Biddle: "needs are a property of agents" (GM-01 p.18); Stockmann's twelve disciples (GM-01 p.5).
- **Candidate game-user-motive deployment.** Alterbiography-as-need-generator: a long-term WoW raider's identity generates the purpose to complete Cutting Edge achievements.
- **Candidate Calleja PIM-pair.** **SHARED ↔ LUDIC** primary; with Narrative+Affective modulation.

## R-AGENCY-PURPOSE (agency-purpose) — **GM-07'S CULMINATING RATIO**

- **Operational gloss.** Agency drifts toward its own teleology under sustained use; purpose dictates fit agencies. THE diagnostic ratio for distinguishing pragmatism (Agency-pole) from mysticism (Purpose-pole).
- **Directionality.** Reversible. **teleological**.
- **Philosophic-school deployment.** Primary: **Pragmatism** (Bridgman purpose-elimination paradox); **Mysticism** (Huxley Mysticism of Means, critiqued limit-case).
- **Paradigm literary examples.** "End justifies the means" dispute; Bridgman purpose-elimination (GM-07); Huxley pacifism (GM-07); sacraments shaped by soteriological purpose.
- **Candidate game-user-motive deployment.** Where does means become end (speedrun/grind/metagame as purpose-drift)? Has the speedrunner's frame-perfect exploit-execution become the purpose, eclipsing the game-internal goal?
- **Candidate Calleja PIM-pair.** **KINESTHETIC+LUDIC ↔ NARRATIVE+AFFECTIVE** — the pragmatist pole privileges agency-as-end; the mystical pole privileges purpose-absorption. Maps onto Calleja's most acute internal tension.
