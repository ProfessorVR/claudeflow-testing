## Section A — Five Pentad-Term Entries

### A.1 ACT

**Operational definition (68 w).** Act is the pentad term answering *what was done?*. On Burke's realist construal (GM-06), an act is an actualization — the bringing of a potentiality (*dynamis*) into full realization (*energeia*) in accordance with a form proper to its kind. An act has internal directionality (entelechy: end-within-itself), is reciprocally related to potency, and is distinguishable from mere motion (which lies in instruments).

**Question answered.** What was done?

**Paradigm examples (Burke).**
- Hero-file example: breaking bonds in order to escape (GM-00 p.xx)
- Aeschylus Erinyes→Eumenides transformation (GM-01 p.20)
- Aristotelian *energeia* / actuality as defining-mark of the real (GM-06 p.227)
- Existence as "the act of essence" (Aquinas; GM-06 p.227)
- Soul as "act of an organic physical body capable of life" (Aquinas on *De Anima*; GM-06 p.227)
- Incipient act (Richards) — perception as response in form of incipient action (GM-06 p.235)
- Symbolic action as the realm of incipient/attitudinal (GM-06 p.243)
- Constitutional enactment as Act (GM-08, *Declaration of Independence*)

**Philosophic school featuring.** Realism (scholastic / Aristotelian-Thomist).

**Burke's exegetical move.** Burke reads Aristotle's *energeia*/entelechy and Aquinas's actus/potentia as the operationalization of Act as motive-locus. The Aristotelian *in rebus* realism (things-strive-toward-perfection-of-kind) replaces the Platonic *ante rem* (transcendent Idea generative). Burke distinguishes act from motion via the action-motion watershed: reduction-to-motion crosses out of dramatism into operationalism/scenic-reduction. Three intermediate states extend the term: attitude (Mead), incipient act (Richards), delayed act.

**Decision rules.**
1. Vocabulary derives motive from actualization in accordance with form-of-kind → Act-featuring.
2. Vocabulary reduces act to motion (no internal source of movement) → scenic-reduction; out of dramatism.
3. Vocabulary treats action as substitute for or beginning of overt action → incipient-act (rhetoric, symbolic action).
4. Verb has connotations of consciousness or purpose → act-unit (GM-01 p.14).
5. Aristotelian first-by-tribal/familial-substance classification → realist Act-featuring; first-by-individuals → nominalist (not realist).

**Calleja PIM mapping** (anticipatory-projection). **Ludic + Narrative + Kinesthetic.** Act = actualization of plans into executed moves. Calleja's strategy/tactics/microtactic hierarchy is a phenomenology of act-formation under rule-system constraint; narrative involvement is featured-act involvement (Burke's plot-as-foremost-element-of-tragedy reading of Aristotle's *Poetics* p.230); kinesthetic carries the physical-input pole.

**Uexküll mapping** (anticipatory-projection). **Wirkmal-actualization within Funktionskreis.** Burke's Act-as-what-was-done parallels the Wirkmal-actualization pole of the functional cycle — the effect-side completion of the receptor/effector loop in which the subject acts upon the meaning-carrier in its Umwelt.

**Ontological target** — **EXEGETICAL-DIRECT.** Aristotelian *energeia*/*dynamis* + Aquinas *actus*/*potentia*. The pipeline's UNIQUE exegetical anchor. Burke devotes GM-06 to Aristotle/Aquinas; cites Baldwin's *Dictionary* chain at p.227; treats "existence as act of essence" and "soul as act of body" directly. Cross-anchor: Aristotle Complete Works (*Metaphysics* IX, *Physics* I.6-7, *De Anima*, *Poetics* 6, NE) + Phantasia Cluster + Bowin AMT indices.

**Forward reference to Rhetoric of Motives' Attitude.** Textual seeds: GM-06 p.236 (attitude as beginning of acts, Mead); p.243 (symbolic action as realm of incipient/attitudinal); GM-10 stanza V Urn-as-Attitude reading (p.458); GM-09 1962 Addendum names Attitude as 6th term. The Rhetoric pipeline will require an Attitude-modifier sub-term capturing the suspended/incipient/symbolic-rehearsal mode mediating between potency and *energeia*.

### A.2 SCENE

**Operational definition (71 w).** Scene is the pentad term answering *where and in what situation was the act done?*. It names the background, setting, situation, or containing environment that frames an act. As containing-principle for action, scene fixes the circumference within which motive may be located; it carries the implicit claim that the act/agent it surrounds can be in part derived from it (the scene-act and scene-agent ratios).

**Question answered.** Where (and in what situation, ground, environment, condition) was the act done?

**Paradigm examples (Burke).** Room of confinement (hero-file, GM-00 p.xx) · Ibsen *Enemy* five rooms (GM-01 pp.3-5) · Industrial scene of accumulated machines (GM-00) · Hobbesian external body causing sense (GM-04 p.133) · Spinozistic God-Nature as scenic substance (GM-04 pp.137-138) · Darwinian Conditions of Existence (GM-04 p.153) · Lucretian atomic void (GM-04 pp.159-160) · Constitution-behind-the-Constitution as scenic ultimate (GM-08).

**Philosophic school featuring.** Materialism.

**Burke's exegetical move.** Burke (GM-04) diagnoses Materialism as the philosophic terminology corresponding to Scene-featuring. It narrows the scenic circumference such that action is reduced to motion and personal/spiritual causes are excluded. Canonical materialists develop a body-and-motion Ausgangspunkt-vocabulary while suppressing, reducing, or scenically deriving the other four pentad terms (Hobbes: will-as-last-appetite; Spinoza: conatus-from-Substance; Darwin: organism-as-agent-minus; Lucretius: clinamen-as-scenic-freedom; Stoicism: purpose-as-providential-scene).

**Decision rules.**
1. Motive-statement explains by environmental conditions, situation, surrounding context → scene-featuring.
2. Putative agent derived from / reducible to larger ground → scene-featuring-with-agent-minus.
3. Action-vocabulary systematically replaced by motion-vocabulary, even where surface terms survive → scene-featuring (Hobbes test).
4. "Situation" used to mean motives peculiar to medium or to men-as-men → NOT scene-featuring; borrowed by agency/agent schools (GM-04 pp.129-130).
5. Putative scenic ground endowed with purposive properties (Stoic *pronoia*) or internal-tendency-to-deviate (Epicurean clinamen) → scene-featuring-with-pre-agentification.
6. Resolve scene/agent ambiguity by tracing the Ausgangspunkt.

**Calleja PIM mapping** (anticipatory). **Spatial primarily; Kinesthetic secondarily.** Scene-as-blanket-container maps onto Spatial involvement (the navigable virtual environment as bounded subjectively-constituted place). Macro-Scene of the player's physical room + inner-Scene of the gameworld together compose the Burkean containing-principle.

**Uexküll mapping** (anticipatory). **Umwelt (Merkwelt + Wirkwelt).** STRONGEST LAYER-C CONVERGENCE. Burke's Scene-as-blanket-container parallels Uexküll's Umwelt as the subjectively-constituted environment closing the functional circle. Both arrive at "motive-locus as bounded subjectively-constituted environment" from non-overlapping traditions.

**Ontological target** (anticipatory). Heideggerian *In-der-Welt-sein* + *Befindlichkeit*. Burke 1945 has 0 Heidegger hits (Phase 0 cite-check). Convergence only: Scene as motive-loaded containing-principle resembles Being-in-the-world's circumspective Umsicht.

**Forward reference to Attitude.** GM-10 p.458 Urn-as-Attitude reading seeds an attitudinal-scene modifier (situation-as-already-evaluated, the rhetorical scene of address).

### A.3 AGENT

**Operational definition (68 w).** Agent is the pentad term naming the doer of any act — the constituting subject from which, in idealist construal, motive originates. Idealism features Agent by deriving scene, act, agency, and purpose from properties of the agent (mind, ego, will, spirit, consciousness, super-agent). Admits subdivision into co-agents (friends) and counter-agents (enemies) and gathers personal properties (will, ideas, malice, intuition, creative imagination) as motive-bearing.

**Question answered.** Who did it? (Whose act is this? In whose consciousness, by which subject?)

**Paradigm examples (Burke).** Hero/co-agent/counter-agent (GM-00) · Dr. Stockmann (GM-01 Ibsen) · Ego/self/super-ego/consciousness/will (GM-05 p.171) · Soul/spirit/oversoul (GM-05 p.171) · Super-persons: church, race, nation (GM-05 p.171) · Leibnizian monad as atom-agent (GM-05 p.184) · Kantian universalized agent / "the understanding" (GM-05 p.187) · Marxist class as collective agent (GM-05 p.200) · Hegelian Spirit / World-Historical Hero (GM-05 p.203) · Marianne Moore appropriate-scene-to-agent ratio (GM-12).

**Philosophic school featuring.** Idealism (with Marxism as scene-grounded-but-agent-featuring hybrid).

**Burke's exegetical move.** Burke (GM-05) reads idealism as the school whose vocabulary is generated by treating Agent as Ausgangspunkt. Berkeley is the cleanest exhibit (matter dissolved into ideas; *esse* is *percipi*; scenic residue routed through God as "Almighty Agent"). Hume narrows the circumference (laboratory empiricism); Leibniz rationalizes it (monad as atom-agent with pre-established harmony); Kant universalizes it ("the understanding" as locus of categories, solving Hume's medium-demand); Hegel mediates Spirit through history; Marxism inverts but retains agent-featuring through class-as-collective-agent.

**Decision rules.** (i) Scenic features derived FROM properties of subject/mind/consciousness → Agent. (ii) Material ground turns out to be a "super-agent" (God, Spirit, Nation-Will, Class) → Agent. (iii) Consciousness/idea treated as PRIMARY, matter derivative → Agent (Berkeley test). (iv) Collective agent (class, party, race) bears properties → Agent even where materialist vocabulary dominates. (v) Distinguish from Agency: a controller is Agency; the player who wields it is Agent; the avatar through which the player acts is also Agent (shifting-agent ratio). (vi) Personal properties gathered under single locus → Agent (GM-00 gathering test).

**Calleja PIM mapping** (anticipatory). **Shared (+ avatar question as Agent-position-shift).** Burke's Agent-with-co/counter-agent maps onto Calleja's Shared involvement. Dissertation move: player-avatar-character triple as a question of which agent-position holds at which moment.

**Uexküll mapping** (anticipatory). **Subject of the Funktionskreis / Innenwelt-pole.** Burke's Agent parallels the subjectival pole of the functional cycle — the Innenwelt-bearer that receives Merkmal and issues Wirkmal.

**Ontological target** (anticipatory). Aristotelian *psuchē* + Heideggerian *Dasein*. No Burke exegetical contact on these grounds.

**Forward reference to Attitude.** Burke's super-agent inventory and Marxist class-as-collective-agent seed the Rhetoric's identification/consubstantiation apparatus. This is the **load-bearing forward-bridge** to the Calleja-incorporation↔Burke-consubstantiation theoretical move.

### A.4 AGENCY

**Operational definition (77 w).** Agency is the pentad term naming the means, instruments, methods, or mediating media through which an act is brought about. Its diagnostic question is *How? By what means?*. As motive-locus it features in pragmatism: action explained by the medium that mediates it. Burke distinguishes Agency from Agent (Agency is impersonal mediation; Agent is personal locus) and from Act (Agency is means; Act is the doing). Modern science's elimination of final cause raises Agency to ancestral term.

**Question answered.** How? By what means?

**Paradigm examples (Burke).** File as instrument (hero-file) · Pragmatist nesting: file/hand/brain/educational-system (GM-00 p.xxi) · Machines as obvious instruments yet in accumulation becoming industrial Scene · Music/piping in Keats Ode (GM-10 st. II) · William James method-only/cash-value · Dewey intelligence as instrument embodied in scientific method · Mead *Philosophy of the Act* · Bridgman operationalism · Aristotelian *organon* as "incipiently pragmatist" (GM-07 p.276) · Procedure/judicial-review as Agency in constitutional analysis (GM-08).

**Philosophic school featuring.** Pragmatism.

**Burke's exegetical move.** Burke (GM-07) diagnoses Pragmatism as the school featuring Agency. James foregrounds method, cash-value, instrument; Dewey's Instrumentalism makes intelligence-as-method ancestral; Mead names his work "philosophy of the act" yet his analytical apparatus is agency-foregrounding (file/hand/brain/educational-system chain). Burke critique: Bridgman's operationalism is the limit-case — he "writes about his purpose in eliminating the concept of purpose," demonstrating that instruments carry purpose embedded in structure and design. Burke names pragmatism the "mediator-form" among the five featurings.

**Decision rules.** (i) Motive derived from medium/instrument/method → Agency. (ii) "Experience" continuous with "nature" via instrumental method (Dewey continuity-replaces-separation) → Agency, even where act/scene vocabulary survives. (iii) Operations equated with meaning (Bridgman) → Agency at its limit-case. (iv) Distinguish from Act: way-words (hodos, tao, yoga) drift act→agency when method becomes means-of-doing rather than act-of-being (GM-01 p.15). (v) Money as paradigm Agency (universal medium). (vi) Instruments-in-accumulation become Scene (Biddle pattern, GM-01 p.18) → flag scene-agency transition.

**Calleja PIM mapping** (anticipatory). **Kinesthetic (controls) + Ludic (rules-as-means).** Agency = how-by-what-means maps onto Kinesthetic (controls as motor mediation) and Ludic (rules as instrumental system). Burke's "instruments have purpose embedded in structure and design" is the strongest anticipatory anchor.

**Uexküll mapping** (anticipatory). **Merk-organ + Wirk-organ pairing.** The receptor/effector instrumentation is structurally Agency. Burke's "physiology of mysticism" / "pre-motion" passages (nervous impulses glowing without muscular discharge) independently arrive at a receptor-effector decoupling description.

**Ontological target** (anticipatory). Aristotelian *technē*. Burke reads *organon* as "incipiently pragmatist" but does NOT pursue technē mapping — anticipatory only.

**Forward reference to Attitude.** GM-07's culminating agency-purpose ratio + agencies-become-scenic seed the Rhetoric's identification-via-shared-instruments. Attitude-modifier captures the rhetorical-instrumentality of address.

### A.5 PURPOSE

**Operational definition (75 w).** Purpose is the pentad term naming the end, *telos*, or motivating-toward-which of an act. Its diagnostic question is *Why?*. As motive-locus it features in mysticism: action explained by the cosmic end toward which it is drawn. Burke's mark of mysticism is "unity of the individual with some cosmic or universal purpose," not mere unity. Pure Purpose collapses into the paradox-of-substance (*docta ignorantia*), into mechanism, into the negative; survives implicitly even where formally eliminated.

**Question answered.** Why?

**Paradigm examples (Burke).** Hero's purpose of escape · Aeschylus Erinyes→Eumenides purpose-transformation · Plotinus *Enneads* I.3 journey to the Good · Plato's Good = One · Bonaventura · Thomas à Kempis *Imitation* — "In all things behold the end" · Aristotelian *eudaimonia* as realistic-not-mystical synonym for Purpose · Tolstoy's drought / James's "Sick Soul" · Eliot *Murder in the Cathedral*; *Little Gidding* · Dostoevsky Raskolnikov's representative act · Constitutional "unwritten clause" of reconciliation (GM-08) · Keats Ode oracular "Beauty is truth, truth beauty" (GM-10).

**Philosophic school featuring.** Mysticism.

**Burke's exegetical move.** Burke (GM-07) diagnoses Mysticism as the school featuring Purpose. The mystic Upward Way to the Primal-Principle (Plotinus); the *via negativa* (Spinoza's omnis determinatio est negatio; Decalogue's "thou shalt not"); reflexive doublings (Nietzsche eternal recurrence, Eliot's wheel, Shelley *Alastor*); the mystic "moment" / rebirth-grammar; demonic trinity (cloacal-mystical imagery); the Silence-and-Hunt biological substrate. Burke critique: pure Purpose passes into paradox-of-substance (compulsion, mechanism); mysticism arrives at the same narrowing-of-circumference as materialism by absolutizing Purpose into fatality.

**Decision rules.** (i) Motive from cosmic/universal end → Purpose (mysticism). (ii) Unity-of-individual-with-universal as motive-engine → mysticism (Burke's mark, not mere unity). (iii) Aristotelian *eudaimonia* is NEAR but NOT mystical (realistic synonym). (iv) *Via negativa* replaces positive end-specification → pure-Purpose limit-case. (v) Means become end (Mysticism of Means, Huxley pacifism) → agency-purpose merger critique. (vi) Purpose reducible to quantifiable goal-state → crossed into Agency (metonymy-of-reduction).

**Calleja PIM mapping** (anticipatory). **Ludic goals + Narrative arc + Affective (regulation/mastery).** Purpose = why maps onto Ludic (goal pursuit) + Narrative (story-arc teleological closure) + Affective. Burke's "unity of individual with cosmic purpose" is the template for narrative-immersion.

**Uexküll mapping** (anticipatory). **Closure of the functional loop on a Bedeutungstrager keyed to biological need.** Burke's "Silence and the Hunt" subsection independently converges: imagery of the desired as that for which we hunger.

**Ontological target** (anticipatory; partial-exegetical via *eudaimonia* mention). Aristotelian *telos* + Heideggerian *Worumwillen* / *Umwillen*.

**Forward reference to Attitude.** GM-07's via-negativa + reflexive-doubling seed the Rhetoric's identification-as-purpose-fusion. The **Calleja-incorporation parallel**: incorporation as substantial absorption of the player into the gameworld's purpose-structure.

## Section B — 10 Ratios Catalog

Burke (GM-01 p.19) singles out **scene-act** and **scene-agent** as the only ratios fitting with complete comfort in the container framework; **act-agent** "tugs at its edges"; the remaining seven require additional dialectical apparatus.

| Ratio | Operational gloss | Burke's key examples | Motive-analytic question | Directionality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **scene-act** *(positional_strong)* | Scene-quality implies/contains/calls-forth correspondingly qualified act; convertibly act demands/constructs scene. The chapter's titular ratio. | Ibsen *Enemy* five rooms; O'Neill *Electra* (Lavinia nails shutters); Marxist objective situation; "terrain determines tactics"; Coleridge Pantisocracy | How does situation determine/permit/constrain the act? | Reversible (deterministic or hortatory) |
| **scene-agent** *(positional_strong)* | Synecdochic correspondence between person and place. Place-quality implies inhabitant-quality. | Carlyle on Arabs; Wordsworth "beauteous evening" sonnet; Swift Laputans; Seurat pointillism; idealist *Einklang* | How does situation qualify the inhabiting agent? | Reversible; bodily-as-scenic reverse case |
| **scene-agency** *(instrumental)* | Scene shapes available agencies; new agencies transform the scene. | Biddle: railroad/telegraph/automobile/radio as agencies-become-scenic (GM-01 p.18) | What instruments does this circumference afford? | Reversible (Biddle pattern is the reverse) |
| **scene-purpose** *(teleological)* | Scene shapes conceivable purposes; purpose may demand a scene proportioned to it. | Hegelian Reason-as-Grundprinzip; heretical-Judas cosmic-Scene; sacred geographies | What ends does this circumference make conceivable? | Reversible; Burke defers full development to GM-07 |
| **act-agent** *(temporal_sequential)* | Agent authors acts; acts make or remake the agent. NOT strict containment. | "Sobering Presidency"; vestments transform wearer; Russian-resistance attribution; "democratic people" perform democratic acts | How does what-is-done modify who-does-it? | Temporal-sequential, not synecdochic |

| **act-purpose** *(teleological)* | Act is consummated by realizing its purpose; purpose is articulate only via concrete act. | Hero-file: break-bonds-in-order-to-escape; Aeschylus Erinyes→Eumenides | How does the act realize/transform its purpose? | Reversible in principle |
| **agent-agency** *(instrumental)* | Agent's character implies fit agencies; habituated agencies constitute character. | Seurat pointillism as artist-identity-constituting; orator's preferred figures | How does means-choice constitute agent-identity? | Reversible |

| **agency-purpose** *(teleological)* | Agency drifts toward own teleology under sustained use; purpose dictates fit agencies. GM-07's CULMINATING ratio. | "End justifies the means" dispute; Bridgman purpose-elimination paradox; Huxley Mysticism of Means | How are means proportioned to ends? Where does means become end? | Reversible; diagnostic for pragmatism (Agency-pole) vs mysticism (Purpose-pole) |

### Higher-order combinations

- **scene-act-agent triadic — Russian-resistance** (GM-01 p.16). Scene-act (Soviet structure) competes with act-agent (Russian patriotism) — Burke uses the triadic to expose rhetorical-deflection.
- **scene-act-agent triadic — Edenic paradigm** (GM-01 p.19). Mutual ratification across all three positional ratios in one unending moment.
- **act-purpose-agent triadic — Aeschylus** (GM-01 p.20). Orestes' suffering changes Furies' purpose, transforming acts and identity.
- **Biddle four-term cascade — scene-agency-act-agent** (GM-01 p.18). Agencies-become-scenic → scenic-agencies motivate acts → unsatisfied needs return motivation to agent.

## Section C — Worked Example Walkthroughs

### C.1 Ibsen *Enemy of the People* (from GM-01)

Burke works Ibsen's five-act structure as the paradigm **scene-act** ratio. Each act-stage is keyed to a distinct room: bourgeois sitting-room (I-II) → editorial office (III) → semi-public room (IV) → disordered study (V). The plot is "an internality directed outwards." Dr. Stockmann's plan to enroll twelve disciples and his closing "stand alone" pronouncement are framed by the disordered-study scene.

| Term | Identification |
|---|---|
| **Act** | Stockmann's exposure of contaminated baths; decision to stand alone; plan to enroll twelve disciples |
| **Scene** | Five rooms keyed to five act-stages |
| **Agent** | Dr. Stockmann (protagonist); townspeople (counter-agents); family (co-agents) |
| **Agency** | Medical report; intended press publication |
| **Purpose** | Health of the town → integrity-against-the-majority |

**Ratios deployed.** scene-act (primary); act-agent (Stockmann remade from physician to prophetic outcast); scene-agent (rooms qualify their inhabitant).

**Discovery.** The scene-act ratio is the master compositional engine: Ibsen's act-divisions are not arbitrary but pentadic.

### C.2 Keats "Ode on a Grecian Urn" (from GM-10)

Burke reads the Ode through the scene-act ratio as **prediction-engine**: the transcendent act of stanza III demands a transcendent scene to match it ("the quality of the act shares the quality of the scene in which it is enacted," GM-10 p.454). Stanza V's chiastic "Beauty is truth, truth beauty" is the Urn-as-Attitude personified — the artifact transmuted into an agent. The Urn's incipient act is an arrested, or incipient act.

| Term | Identifications |
|---|---|
| **Act** | Speaker's apostrophe (I-II); arrested acts of depicted figures; poet's symbolic act of making the poem; transcendent mental act split off from bodily passion (III); Urn's incipient act / Attitude (V) |
| **Scene** | Urn as scene-of-frozen-time; bodily fever as ground of mental action; deathy-deathless transcendent scene (green altar); scene "behind" the Urn; 19th-century romanticism + capitalist-individualist property structure |
| **Agent** | Speaker/poet; depicted deities/mortals/Bold Lover/priest/marble men; biographical Keats (body as patient, mind as agent); Urn-as-Attitude personified |
| **Agency** | Music/piping (II); language as mode of action; urn's visual surface (ekphrasis) |
| **Purpose** | Oracular "Beauty is truth, truth beauty"; transcendence of romantic split; redemption of bodily suffering by poetic act |

**Discovery.** The Urn-as-Attitude reading at stanza V is the textual seed for Burke's *Rhetoric of Motives* sixth term. Attitude mediates between potency and *energeia*.

### C.3 Marianne Moore oeuvre (from GM-12)

Burke applies dramatism at **corpus scale**: a poet's body of work as evidence of stable motivational substance. Moore's signature act is attentive descriptive observation; scene functions as ground that contains the same quality as the final assertive act; agent and scene-internal agents (armored animals, the Irish girl, the elephant, the unicorn, the basilisk) are paired through appropriate-scene-to-agent ratio.

- **Featured terms.** Act, Scene, Agent.
- **Recessive terms.** Agency (almost wholly absent), Purpose (present only in appreciation-as-motive subroutine).
- **Featured ratios.** act-agent (attentive act expresses agent-character); scene-act (description as logical-compositional ground); scene-agent (basilisk lives in basilica).
- **Recurrent motifs as substance evidence.** Armored animals; visibles/invisibles; water-in-imprisonment; clocks-for-clocks; contractility; jeopardy; withinness-of-withinness.

**Discovery.** The corpus-scale template (6 transfer steps): (1) choose corpus boundary; (2) catalog recurrent motifs; (3) identify underlying motivational substance; (4) diagnose featured pentad terms; (5) use one internal exhibit as exegetical key; (6) test substance against the corpus.

### C.4 Dissertation-deployable template — game-user-motive analysis

Apply pentad to a paradigmatic player-session in a Calleja Appendix case-game (*World of Warcraft*, *Planetside*, *EVE*).

**Step 1 — identify pentad terms.**
- **Scene.** (a) physical room; (b) game-world environment; (c) social field (raid group/guild/server); (d) macro-cultural-historical scene (genre, platform, monetization).
- **Act.** (a) micro (keypress); (b) tactic (rotation, combo); (c) strategy (build-order, route); (d) session-arc act; (e) campaign-arc act (career).
- **Agent.** (a) player-as-self; (b) avatar; (c) character; (d) co-agents (party/guild/faction); (e) counter-agents (PvP opponents, AI antagonists, design-as-counter-agent). **FLAG**: which agent-position holds at which moment (Burke's shifting-agent ratio).
- **Agency.** (a) controller/mouse/keyboard/VR-hardware (Kinesthetic); (b) rule-system/mechanics/cooldowns (Ludic); (c) UI/HUD (perceptual); (d) comms (voice, chat, ping) (Shared); (e) third-party tools (DPS-meters, Discord, wikis).
- **Purpose.** (a) game-internal goal; (b) flow-seeking; (c) affective regulation; (d) social goal (belonging, status); (e) mastery-pursuit; (f) exploration; (g) narrative-resolution; (h) external (streaming, esports).

**Step 2 — identify ratios in operation.**
- scene-act: How does affordance-structure constrain or enable session-act? (*Planetside*'s continental scale enables logistic-acts unavailable in a battle-arena.)
- act-agent: How does session-act remake player-agent? (alterbiography emergence — Calleja Ch. 7)
- scene-agent: Spatial-internalization phenomenology (Burke's body-as-scenic applied to avatar-as-scenic).
- act-agency: Where does act-of-play merge with controller-agency (kinesthetic transparency, Bridgman limit-case)?
- agency-purpose: Where do available mechanics drift toward becoming their own ends (speedrun, grinding, min-max)?
- agent-purpose: What does this player want, given who this player is? (alterbiography-as-need-generator)
- agency-scene: How does new platform-tech reconfigure the gameworld scene? (Biddle pattern applied to VR / cloud / cross-play.)

**Step 3 — Calleja PIM crosswalk** (5 lines, see Section E table).

## Section D — Application Checklist + Analytic-Question Battery (≥40 questions)

### Scene / Spatial PIM
1. What features of the virtual environment foreground themselves as containing-principle?
2. What does the room/physical-context contribute as outer Scene?
3. Where do scene-cues shift between linguistic and material delivery (Burke's GM-01 distinction)?
4. *Uexküll:* What is this player's Umwelt — which features register as Merkmal-laden?
5. *Heidegger:* Where is the circumspective Umsicht — what shows up as ready-to-hand vs present-at-hand?

### Act / Ludic+Narrative+Kinesthetic PIM
6. What is the player's session-arc act? What sub-acts compose it?
7. Where does act collapse into motion (the Burkean watershed)? What play-segments are 'sheer motion'?
8. Where is incipient-act / attitude detectable — suspended decision, rehearsed play, theorycrafting?
9. *Aristotle:* What is the act's *energeia* / entelechy? What kind of thing is this act fully being?
10. Where is act titular (Mead 'philosophy of the act' as game-genre ideology)?

### Agent / Shared PIM
11. Who is acting — player? Avatar? Character? When does the agent-position shift?
12. Who are the co-agents (guild, party, friend-circle)? Who counter-agents?
13. Where do super-agents emerge (the playerbase, community, studio, genre)?
14. *Uexküll:* What receptor/effector specializations characterize this player-subject?
15. *Heidegger:* How is the player's Dasein structured by Mitsein?

### Agency / Kinesthetic+Ludic PIM
16. What means is the player using — controller? Rule-system? Other players' moves?
17. Where does act-of-play merge with agency of input (kinesthetic transparency)?
18. Which agencies have become scenic by accumulation (Biddle pattern — VR, voice-chat, streaming-overlays)?
19. *Aristotle:* Is this agency *technē* or *organon*? Does it carry its own internal *aitia*?
20. Bridgman test: has agency drifted into purpose-elimination paradox (means-as-end)?

### Purpose / Ludic+Narrative+Affective PIM
21. Game-internal goal? Flow? Affective regulation? Social? Mastery? Exploration?
22. Does this play feature mysticism (unity-with-cosmic-purpose)?
23. Where does pure Purpose collapse into mechanism (compulsion-loop critique)?
24. *Aristotle:* What is this play's *telos* / *eudaimonia*? Goal external (winning) or internal (good-play)?
25. *Heidegger:* What is the *Worumwillen*?

### Ratio-level questions
26. **scene-act**: How does the game-world constrain or enable the player's acts?
27. Deterministic surface ('design made me do it') or hortatory ('design demands I act')?
28. Where does the Lavinia-reversal hold (act produces scene that produces further acts)?
29. **act-agent**: How does what-is-being-done modify who-is-doing-it?
30. Where is alterbiography emerging — what acts have remade this player?
31. Where is picturesque-conflict operative (fools in wise situations / wise players in foolish design)?
32. **agency-purpose**: How does the controller/rule-system limit available purposes?
33. Where does means become end (speedrun / grind / metagame as purpose-drift)?
34. Pragmatist or mystic toward the agency-purpose dyad?
35. **scene-purpose**: What ends does this game-world make conceivable that others would not?
36. How does narrative-scene-design dictate purpose-structure (rails vs emergent)?
37. **agent-agency**: How do habituated control-schemes constitute player-identity?
38. Where does Symbolic-of-agency mother-locus emerge (signature build/main)?

### Irony / dialectic meta-questions
39. Where does design intent (A) return as its dialectical opposite (non-A) in actual play (Burke's peripety)?
40. Does the analyst deploy **classic/humble irony** (consubstantiality with the toxic optimizer) or **romantic/Pharisaic irony** (toxic player as 'not me')?
41. Is the speedrun/glitch-exploit a perversion or the rational extension of the designed incentive?

### The incorporation ↔ consubstantiation bridge

The Burke × Calleja primary theoretical move at the apparatus-intersection: Calleja's **incorporation** (player substantially absorbed into the game-world) and Burke's **consubstantiation** (identification through substantial sharing) converge on the same dynamic from incommensurable traditions.

- **Burke textual seeds.** GM-00 p.xix proto-consubstantial figure (A may become non-A . . . consubstantial with non-A); GM-02 substance type-16 "consubstantial-substance"; GM-13 irony peripety-formula (A returns as non-A).
- **Calleja anchor.** Incorporation as 6-dimension absorption (Calleja PIM Operationalization Manual).
- **Blocker.** *Consubstantiation* is *Rhetoric of Motives* (1950) vocabulary, not *Grammar* vocabulary. Bridge can be flagged from the Grammar side but cannot be consolidated until the Rhetoric pipeline is built.
- **`consubstantiation_pipeline_status` = `"pending-future-rhetoric-of-motives-pipeline"`**

### The Umwelt ↔ Scene convergence

Layer C's signature anchor at the Burke × Uexküll intersection. Burke's containing-Scene and Uexküll's Umwelt converge on the same insight (motive-locus is the bounded, subjectively-constituted environment) from non-overlapping traditions. **Risk:** forcing the parallel — Burke's Scene is symbolic-rhetorical-historical-cultural; Uexküll's Umwelt is biosemiotic-objectivist. Genuine convergence at the abstract level but divergent operationalizations at the concrete level. The dissertation must flag the divergence rather than collapse it. **Value:** two independent traditions arriving at the same insight = strong evidence the insight is structural rather than disciplinary artifact.
