## Pentadic Apparatus (Tier 1 core)

**The five key terms** — *Act, Scene, Agent, Agency, Purpose* — and **the Pentad** itself, plus the **ten ratios**, anchor the entire volume. Each pentadic term has a corresponding **philosophical school**:

| Pentad term | Philosophical school | Etymological / Greek-Latin ground |
|---|---|---|
| Act | Realism (scholastic) | L. *actus*; Gk. *energeia* (actuality) / *dynamis* (potency) |
| Scene | Materialism | L. *scaena*; Gk. *skene* (stage) |
| Agent | Idealism | L. *agens* (driver) |
| Agency | Pragmatism | L. *agentia* (means) |
| Purpose | Mysticism (when raised to ultimate-term) | Gk. *telos*; *teleios* (complete) |

**Ten Ratios** (5C2): scene-act, scene-agent, scene-agency, scene-purpose, act-agent, act-agency, act-purpose, agent-agency, agent-purpose, agency-purpose; plus the chapter-section **lyric ratio** as scene-agent variant.

## Substance Cluster (Tier 1)

*Substance* (sub-stantia — what stands under) is the pre-pentadic ground-term whose **paradox** ("what stands under is defined by what stands beside") motivates dramatistic dialectic. Variants: **contextual** (Spinozistic), **familial** (kinship/race), **geometric** (Spinozistic-Euclidean), **directional** (movement-toward), **dialectic** (negation-defined), **tribal**, **positional**, **universal-motives-as-substance**.

Companion volume-wide terms: **circumference** (size of scenic background), **scope** (the angle of vision), **reduction** (translating one order into a lower one — atomistic, materialistic, monetary, theological).

## The Four Master Tropes (Tier 1, Appendix D)

| Trope | Literal sense | Meta-cognitive sense |
|---|---|---|
| **Metaphor** (Gk. *meta-phora* = carrying-over) | figurative carrying-over | **Perspective** — seeing one thing in terms of another |
| **Metonymy** (Gk. *meta-onymia* = change-of-name) | substitution by association | **Reduction** — translating to a lower-order vocabulary |
| **Synecdoche** (Gk. *syn-ek-doche* = receiving-together) | part-for-whole | **Representation** — a part standing in for the whole |
| **Irony** (Gk. *eironeia* = dissembling) | saying the opposite | **Dialectic** — perspective-of-perspectives |

## Dialectical Apparatus (Tier 1)

**Dialectic** (Gk. *dialektike*) ≈ **Dramatic**; co-extensive with **Irony** as fourth master trope. Its fundamental operation is **merger-and-division** (with sub-variants action-passion, being-nothing, mind-body, life-death). The chief upward move is **Socratic transcendence** through stages of dialectical ascent to an **ultimate term**.
