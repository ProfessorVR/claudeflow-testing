# Burke Four Master Tropes Manual

**Parallel analytical apparatus to the pentad**

From GM-13 (Appendix D — The Four Master Tropes). Each trope is a "discovery and description of truth" function. The four tropes underwrite distinguishable discourse-regimes that construct the game-user-motive in different ways.

**Parallel apparatus status.** The four tropes are a parallel analytical apparatus to the pentad: every trope-deployment is also a pentad-deployment. The dissertation deploys both as a **5 × 4 cell matrix** (pentad term × trope-operation).

---

## 1. METAPHOR — perspective

**Operational gloss (≤80 w).** Seeing one character (anything thinkable as distinct) in terms of another; bringing out the *thisness* of a *that*. To consider A from the point of view of B is to use B as a perspective upon A. Reality is not dissolved by multi-perspectivism — it is *established* by it. Degrees of being correspond to variety-of-perspectives-supportable.

**Burke's examples.** Human motivation in terms of conditioned reflexes / chemicals / class struggles / love of God / neurosis / pilgrimage / power / planetary motion / geography / sun spots (p.504) · 17th-Century poet's 'conceit' as proto-perspective · multi-perspective object-establishment (lifting / smelling / tasting / tapping).

**Pentad interaction.** Most-implicates **AGENCY** (a perspective is a means-of-considering) and **SCENE** (the chosen perspective re-scenes the act). Cross-cuts all five terms. Each philosophic-school's featured pentad term is itself a metaphor / perspective on motivation.

**Dissertation game-user deployment.** Cross-domain mapping in game-discourse: player-as-consumer (economic metaphor); player-as-laborer (labor metaphor); player-as-co-author (literary metaphor); player-as-inhabitant (architectural metaphor); player-as-athlete (sport metaphor). Game-studies sub-schools (Aarseth's ergodic / Bogost's procedural / Calleja's incorporation / Sicart's play) feature different metaphor-perspectives on the user.

**Discourse regime.** Gamification rhetoric and skeuomorphic ports of work/school/fitness into 'play.'

---

## 2. METONYMY — reduction

**Operational gloss (≤80 w).** Conveying an incorporeal or intangible state in terms of the corporeal or tangible. 'Heart' for 'emotions'; 'commotion' for emotion. All spiritual-realm vocabulary was metonymic in origin. **Reduction** is the one-direction (quality-to-quantity) special case: scientific realism's trope. Two senses must be tracked: poet's metonymy as figure/symbolization, and scientific behaviorist's metonymy as substantial reduction.

**Burke's examples.** 'Heart' for 'emotions' (p.506) · I. A. Richards substituting 'commotion' for 'emotion' (*Principles of Literary Criticism*, p.506) · Western materialism's reduction of consciousness to motion · Neurath's isotypes (*Modern Man in the Making*) as reductive vs. realistic portrait painting (p.510) · statistical surveys in sociology as fabulously-amassed reductive misrepresentations (p.510) · Korzybski's 'Structural Differential' (p.511) · Thurman Arnold's psychiatric metaphor of social relations (p.511).

**Pentad interaction.** Most-implicates **AGENCY** (reduction = methodological apparatus) and **SCENE** (lower-order realm into which higher is reduced). Decisively implicates **ACT** when motive is reduced to behavior. Operates the implicit cross-pentad collapse that produces materialist motive-accounts (Agent → Scene; Purpose → Agency).

**Dissertation game-user deployment.** **Player-reduced-to-scoreboard-number**: KDA ratio, win rate, MMR, engagement minutes, retention curve, conversion funnel as metonymic reductions of player-motive to quantitative correlates. The dissertation distinguishes:
- **(a) telemetry-as-figure** (poet's use: numbers as symbolization of a quality whose substance lies elsewhere)
- **(b) telemetry-as-substantial-reduction** (scientific-behaviorist use: motive IS the number)

**Load-bearing methodological move** for diagnosing 'gamification' rhetoric: gamification often deploys metonymy-as-real-reduction while claiming the warrants of metonymy-as-figure.

**Discourse regime.** Telemetric reduction — player as KDA / MMR / retention-curve / conversion-funnel.

---

## 3. SYNECDOCHE — representation

**Operational gloss (≤80 w).** Convertibility between two terms in integral relationship: part-for-whole, whole-for-part, container-for-contained, sign-for-signified, material-for-thing-made, cause-for-effect, genus-for-species — and all reversals. The 'noblest synecdoche' is microcosm-macrocosm identity (Leibniz). Two-way (unlike metonymy's one-way). Extended by Burke to before/after, implicit/explicit, temporal/logical sequence, name/narrative, disease/cure, hero/villain.

**Burke's examples.** Leibniz's monadology as grand-scale synecdoche; 'representation' is Leibniz's own word (p.508) · Rousseau's *volonté générale* as political-representation synecdoche (p.508) · microcosm-macrocosm identity as 'noblest synecdoche' · hero/villain dialectic; disease/cure pairs.

**Pentad interaction.** Most-implicates **AGENT** (part-whole convertibility holds especially between an agent and the class/society/cosmos) and **PURPOSE** (macrocosmic whole that microcosmic part instantiates). Underwrites every representative-anecdote choice: which Act/Scene/Agent/Agency/Purpose stands-for the whole of human motivation? Each philosophic-school featuring is a synecdochic election.

**Dissertation game-user deployment.** **Avatar-stands-for-player** — the canonical game-studies synecdoche. Sub-deployments:
- party-stands-for-player (RPG)
- civilization-stands-for-player (4X)
- username-stands-for-player (multiplayer)
- screenshot/clip-stands-for-session (streaming culture)
- leaderboard-rank-stands-for-skill (esports)

Each is a synecdoche the dissertation analyzes for representativeness: does the avatar truly represent the player, or does it reductively misrepresent — collapsing into metonymy? The 'well-formed game session' is internally synecdochic in Burke's sense: tutorial contains endgame, endgame sums tutorial.

**Discourse regime.** Avatar / leaderboard / clip as representative part-for-whole.

## 4. IRONY — dialectic

**Operational gloss (≤80 w).** Arises when one tries, by the interaction of terms upon one another, to produce a development that uses all the terms. From the standpoint of this total form ('perspective of perspectives'), no participating sub-perspective is precisely right or wrong; all are voices integrally affecting one another. **True/classic/humble irony** is grounded in consubstantiality with the 'enemy'; **romantic/Pharisaic irony** is grounded in superiority. Over-all formula: **A returns as non-A** (peripety).

**Burke's examples.** Socrates / proletariat as primus-inter-pares characters · peripety as A→non-A (classical tragedy reversal) · Flaubert / Newton / Falstaff as consubstantial-with-enemy figures · distinction: classic-humble irony (consubstantiality with the enemy) vs romantic-Pharisaic irony (superiority over the enemy).

**Pentad interaction.** Implicates **ALL FIVE** pentad terms by structural definition: irony requires that all terms (and agents) participate, none precisely right or wrong. Operates on the ratios as a whole rather than on any individual term. Implicates **PURPOSE** most centrally in its peripety-formula (A→non-A) and **ACT** in its peripety-as-reversal mechanic. The primus-inter-pares character is itself a synecdochic election — irony folds back into synecdoche at the level of representative-character selection.

**Dissertation game-user deployment.** **Meta-gaming dialectic** — the player who optimizes most ruthlessly for the designed objective often produces emergent behavior that subverts the design (speedrunning, glitch-exploitation, RMT, botting, grief-play, role-playing-against-roleplay).

Burke's **A → non-A peripety** maps directly:
- difficulty curve designed to produce mastery (A) → toxic gatekeeping (non-A)
- social system designed to foster cooperation (A) → harassment-vector (non-A)
- reward economy designed to retain players (A) → compulsion-loop critique (non-A)

**The dissertation's NORMATIVE CRITERION:** the developer who treats the toxic player as 'not me' deploys **romantic-Pharisaic irony**; the developer who recognizes **consubstantiality** — that the toxic optimizer is the rational extension of the designed incentive — deploys **classic-humble irony**. **Consubstantial irony is the diagnostic of good faith.**

**Discourse regime.** Meta-gaming, speedrunning, glitch-exploitation, emergent-play, the consubstantiality of designer and exploiter.

## Trope × Pentad Interaction Matrix (5 × 4 = 20 cells)

|  | Metaphor (perspective) | Metonymy (reduction) | Synecdoche (representation) | Irony (dialectic) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Act** | Choosing the verb-frame (act as conquest) | Reducing act to behavior (poetic vs scientific behaviorism, p.506) | Election of one act as standing-for-all-acts | Peripety: A → non-A; act returns as reversal |
| **Scene** | Choosing the circumference (marketplace vs battlefield) | Reducing scene to physical-coordinate substrate | Microcosm-macrocosm: scene-fragment represents cosmos (Leibniz) | Scene reframed by perspective of perspectives |
| **Agent** | Choosing agent-type-as-frame (consumer / citizen / believer) | Reducing agent to body / biology / demographics | Political representation (Rousseau *volonté générale*); avatar-stands-for-player | Consubstantiality with 'enemy' agent (Flaubert / Newton / Falstaff) |
| **Agency** | Choosing instrument-as-frame (tool vs obstacle vs ritual) | Reducing means to technique-shorn-of-meaning | Instrument represents the order it instantiates (cross representing salvation) | Dialectical interaction of means produces emergent unintended results |
| **Purpose** | Choosing the end-as-frame (profit vs salvation vs mastery) | Reducing purpose to quantifiable goal-state | End-state represents developmental whole (primus-inter-pares character) | Internal fatality: purpose arose in time perishes in time |

*Burke does not lay out this matrix explicitly; every trope-pair operation in the essay is also a pentad-term operation. This is the dissertation's parallel-apparatus 5×4 grid.*

## Mutual transformability

The four tropes mutually transform at their margins, just as the pentad terms do:

- A **metaphor** sustained into systematic correspondence becomes **synecdoche** (microcosm-macrocosm).
- A **synecdoche** enforced one-directionally (part-substituted-for-whole as the only legitimate move) becomes **metonymy** (reduction).
- A **metonymy** whose direction is reversed by exposing its substitution becomes **irony** (A returned as non-A).

**Diagnostic move.** When a game-studies discourse claims metaphor-warrants (figural, multi-perspectival) but operationalizes metonymy-machinery (one-direction reductive), the dissertation can name the rhetorical slippage. **Gamification is the standing case:** claims metaphor-warrants ('play is like work'), operationalizes metonymy ('engagement is the number of clicks').

## Parallel apparatus to the pentad

Both are organons of motive-analysis; both cross-cut all five pentad terms; both admit non-bijective featuring.

- **Pentad** answers: WHAT IS THE FORMAL STRUCTURE of a motive-statement?
- **Tropes** answer: WHAT KIND OF FIGURAL OPERATION is being performed within that structure?

The dissertation deploys both: each user-motive analysis names its featured pentad term + its featured trope-operation.
