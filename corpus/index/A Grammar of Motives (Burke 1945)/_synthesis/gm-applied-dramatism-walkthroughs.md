# Applied Dramatism Walkthroughs — Burke's Six Cases as Dissertation Templates

**Phase 3 Wave 1, Agent 3D-2 deliverable.** Cross-link to Operationalization Manual Section C (Applied Dramatism Cases / Methodological Templates) — the parallel-to-Calleja-Appendix deliverable for the Burke pipeline.

## (i) Overview — Burke's Applied-Dramatism Corpus

The *Grammar of Motives* is not exclusively a treatise about how to talk about motives; embedded across its 1945 architecture are at least five fully worked applied-dramatism cases that constitute the volume's empirical-adaptation backbone. These cases are deliberately scale-staggered. Burke moves from the canonical single-act drama (Ibsen's *Enemy*, in GM-01), through a ratio-reversal counter-instance (O'Neill's *Mourning Becomes Electra*, also GM-01), to a sustained close-reading of a single lyric (Keats's "Ode on a Grecian Urn," GM-10), to a poet's complete oeuvre (Marianne Moore, GM-12), and finally to the analysis of political-text corpora at collective-document scale (the Dialectic of Constitutions, GM-08). Alongside these five pentad-deployments, Appendix D supplies a parallel methodological apparatus — the four master tropes — that overlays every case at every scale.

Read together, the six worked deployments distribute the analytic procedure across four distinct scales (single-text drama, single-text lyric, single-author oeuvre, political-text / theory-corpus) and supply a meta-methodology (the trope-pentad interaction matrix) that cross-cuts them. For the dissertation, these scales map precisely onto the five game-analytic scales (single game, single player trajectory, single player corpus, game-/community-corpus, design-theory corpus) plus the designer-career hybrid. Section (viii) below tabulates the deployment.

The common analytical commitments across all six cases are: (a) **diagnostic-not-classificatory** featuring — Burke never says "constitution X lacks Agency" but "constitution X features Scene"; (b) **correlation-without-reduction** — biographical, cultural and material substrates are co-active substrates, never causal grounds; (c) the **anti-behaviorist** action/motion distinction as load-bearing diagnostic; (d) **refutation conditions** appropriate to scale (cross-motif convergence at corpus scale; scene-act prediction-engine at single-text scale); (e) the **substance-as-motive** identity that constitutes the analytic object as a structure of motives rather than as a behavior.

## (ii) Ibsen's *Enemy of the People* (GM-01) — Single-Text Drama, Forward Scene-Act

The Ibsen reading (book pp. 3–5) is Burke's paradigm case for the scene-act ratio at single-text scale. Burke works the five-act structure as five scene-shifts whose successive qualities qualify the act unfolding within them. The bourgeois sitting-room of Acts I-II frames a bourgeois plot; the editorial-office of Act III gives forensic coloration to the peripety; the semi-public, semi-intimate room of Act IV reflects the semi-public character of Stockmann's public appeal; the disordered study of Act V mirrors the hero's post-conflict agent-state. The plot Burke characterizes as an internality directed outwards — a single composite act tracing the protagonist's movement from solitary withdrawal toward community founding. The closing pronouncement on standing alone is read against the family circle physically present at the moment of speech: the agents in scene modify the verbal act they witness.

The Ibsen case yields a clean **6-step template** for single-text dramatic analysis: identify each scene-shift; map each scene's permitted act and verify scene-act coherence; mark the structural fulcrum (here, peripety at Act III); read the closing scene bidirectionally (scenic property OR mirror of agent-state); read pronouncement against scene physically present; diagnose the whole as a single composite act characterized by a single motive-vector.

## (iii) O'Neill's *Mourning Becomes Electra* (GM-01) — Single-Text Drama, Ratio-Reversal

Immediately after Ibsen, Burke turns (book pp. 5–6, 9) to the trilogy's close as a counter-instance demonstrating ratio-reversibility AND its degenerate boundary. Lavinia's command to nail the shutters and discard the flowers IS an act that constructs scene; the constructed scene then commands her subsequent automaton-march. The sequence runs act → scene → further-act, and at the closing boundary, action collapses into mere motion. Burke uses this collapse as the textbook case of his anti-behaviorist distinction: behaviorist metaphysics truncates drama by reducing action to motion; O'Neill's Lavinia walking like an automaton is precisely what such truncation looks like inside a drama.

The O'Neill case yields a **5-step template** for ratio-reversal and action/motion-boundary analysis: identify acts that explicitly construct scene; identify subsequent acts the constructed scene commands; test for ratio-reversibility (does the text run scene→act, act→scene, or both?); diagnose boundary cases where action degenerates into motion; mark the dramatistic-vs-behaviorist distinction explicitly.

For the dissertation, the O'Neill template grounds the load-bearing critique of behaviorist game-telemetry. A telemetric pipeline that records inputs as motion-events (keystroke-frequency, click-density, dwell-time) collapses player-action into player-motion exactly as Lavinia's automaton-march collapses tragic-action into stage-direction-able motion. Calleja's incorporation thesis pulls in the opposite direction; the O'Neill template is Burke's textual demonstration that the dramatistic distinction is methodologically necessary, not merely terminological.

## (iv) The Dialectic of Constitutions (GM-08) — Political-Text / Collective-Document

GM-08 is the longest chapter (79 pp.) and the volume's master demonstration that the pentad is deployable at document-corpus scale. The chapter's `applied_dramatism_walkthrough` JSON-block records an **11-move template** that runs from substance-as-ground-of-motive through the diagnostic-not-classificatory featuring principle:

1. Constitution as substance, substance as motive (the scenic ultimate);
2. Constitutions addressed by Agents to Agents (idealistic addressee-structure);
3. Constitutional enactment as Act (partial public act embedded in wider scene);
4. Procedure / judicial review as Agency (essentializing-vs-proportional; broad-vs-strict construction; Marshall's Marbury as Agency-move masquerading as Act-as-mandatory);
5. Ends, ideals, the unwritten clause of reconciliation as Purpose;
6. Scene-act ratio as master ratio of constitutional theory (when the Constitution-behind-the-Constitution shifts, clauses must mean differently);
7. Agent-act ratio (Read's anarcho-syndicalist Constitution presupposes an autonomous-individual agent; Parkes's positive-laissez-faire presupposes a monetarily-socialized agent);
8. Agency-purpose ratio in reform-vs-transform diagnosis (Parkes exploits this ambiguity);
9. Substance-vocabulary deployment (GM-02 inheritance: stance-family vocabulary, gerundives-as-ontology);
10. Four-tropes deployment (GM-13 inheritance: synecdoche as representative anecdote; metonymy as reduction; irony as constitutive contradiction; metaphor as heuristic);
11. Diagnostic-not-classificatory featuring (which term the text FEATURES as locus of motive, never which it omits).

The Constitutions template is GM-08's gift to dissertation methodology: any organized body of design-theory (ludology, narratology, embodied-cognition game studies, procedural rhetoric, MDA, the Forge essays, design patterns, F2P live-ops playbooks) is diagnosable by the 11-move walkthrough. Each school has its featured pentad term, its dominant ratios, its God-term (the terminological-ultimate substance: engagement, flow, meaningful choice, fairness, ARPDAU), its choice of circumference (the Constitution-behind-the-Theory), its constitutive-vs-admonitory split, and its essentializing-vs-proportional hermeneutic. The school becomes recognizable as a partial constitution-text with its own God-term, opponent, and shape of unity-in-diversity.

## (v) Keats's "Ode on a Grecian Urn" (GM-10) — Single-Text Lyric

The Keats appendix supplies the **8-step single-text lyric template**. Burke walks the ode stanza by stanza with continuous pentad-tagging at the imagistic level. The act-cluster identifies the speaker's apostrophic act, the figures' arrested acts on the urn, the poet's symbolic act of making the poem, the transcendent act split off in stanza III, and the urn's incipient act (the attitude) in stanza V. The scene-cluster identifies the urn as frozen-time scene, bodily fever as ground of mental action, the deathy-deathless transcendent scene of stanza IV, the scene-behind-the-urn at the chiasmus, and the historical scene of 19th-century romanticism plus capitalist-individualist property structure. Agents include the speaker, depicted figures, Keats himself (body as patient, mind as agent), and the urn-as-attitude personified — the romantic-idealist Agent-feature of the text.

The methodological core is the scene-act ratio as **prediction-engine**: once stanza III generates a transcendent act, the text MUST generate a transcendent scene to match it; stanza IV satisfies that demand. The ratio is promoted from descriptive observation to compositional law. Methodological steps:

1. State the text's terminal motive-statement (oracle / thesis / end-state);
2. Walk segment by segment with continuous pentad-tagging at imagistic level;
3. Detect structural fulcra where pentad-clusters split or combine;
4. Deploy the foregrounded ratio as prediction-engine for the next segment;
5. Add biographical / cultural / historical motives as co-active substrates without reducing the symbolic act;
6. Recognize sub-pentadic substance-claims (Keats's phthisic fever-and-chill as modes of one illness substance) that re-cross with the text's transformations;
7. Terminate with a conceptual translation that exhibits the dramatistic grammar underlying the imagistic resolution;
8. Refuse reductive materialist alternatives (the Kretschmer-school inversion that would treat the symbolic act as disease-symptom).

The Keats template's load-bearing methodological move — refuse reduction without forfeiting correlation — is the dissertation's chief methodological inheritance for player-trajectory analysis. A single recorded session can be correlated with biography, community, hardware, life-circumstance without thereby being reduced to demographic-symptom.

## (vi) Marianne Moore's Oeuvre (GM-12) — Single-Poet Corpus

GM-12 is the **scale-shift template** par excellence: from single-text dramatism to corpus-scale dramatism. Burke explicitly contrasts it with the Keats appendix: where GM-10 deploys dense intra-textual ratio analysis, GM-12 uses cross-textual motif-tracking and oeuvre-level substantiality. Burke's signature metaphor — all branches spread from a single trunk — is the refutation condition. If the motifs (visibles/invisibles, armored animals, water-in-imprisonment, clocks-for-clocks, contractility, jeopardy) do not converge on a single motivational substance, the analysis fails.

The Moore appendix yields a **7-step transfer procedure**:

1. Choose corpus boundary (Burke uses *Selected Poems* 1935 + *What Are Years* 1941, with *Observations* 1924 as backstop);
2. Catalog recurrent motifs across the corpus;
3. Identify the underlying motivational substance (Burke names appreciation, contractility, jeopardy, withinness-of-withinness);
4. Diagnose featured pentad term(s) and recessive terms (Moore features Act-Agent-Scene; Agency and Purpose are recessive);
5. Use one internal exhibit as exegetical key to the rest (Black Earth's elephant decodes Crape Myrtle's trunk);
6. Test the substance against cross-motif convergence (refutation condition);
7. Read individual artifacts as work in progress within the corpus (no single artifact is final; each is legible only against cross-corpus sediment).

For the dissertation, this is the master template for four parallel corpus-scale deployments: PLAYER-CORPUS (one user's body of play across multiple games / years), STUDIO-CORPUS (a studio's released titles), COMMUNITY-CORPUS (a community's forum/wiki/clip archive), and DESIGNER-CAREER (one designer's portfolio across studios). Each gets the 7-step procedure. Calleja-PIM correspondences cleanly map to the featured-pentad-term diagnosis: a corpus featuring kinesthetic involvement features Agency; a corpus featuring narrative involvement features Scene+Purpose; a corpus featuring shared involvement features Agent. The pentad-feature diagnosis IS the corpus-strategy.

## (vii) The Methodological Templates Abstracted

The five primary cases yield five distinct templates of differing step-counts: Ibsen 6-step (forward scene-act), O'Neill 5-step (ratio-reversal + action/motion boundary), Keats 8-step (single-text lyric symbolic-action sequence), Moore 7-step (corpus transfer), Constitutions 11-move (political-text walkthrough). The trope-pentad apparatus from GM-13 supplies a 4-step parallel overlay (identify dominant trope; cross-reference with featured pentad term to locate the cell in the 5×4 matrix; distinguish classic from romantic irony; deploy classic-vs-romantic-irony as diagnostic of analytic good faith). Totaling 41 distinct procedural steps across six templates, the apparatus is more than sufficient to constrain a dissertation chapter at any scale from single game to design-theory corpus.

Across templates, the load-bearing shared commitments are: (a) diagnostic-not-classificatory featuring; (b) correlation-without-reduction; (c) the anti-behaviorist action/motion distinction; (d) explicit refutation conditions (cross-motif convergence for corpus, scene-act prediction-engine for single-text); (e) substance-as-motive identity. These five commitments are the volume's procedural backbone. Any chapter that operationalizes them inherits Burke's methodological rigor without inheriting his vocabulary-specific obscurities.

The 5×4 trope-pentad interaction matrix from GM-13 supplies the meta-methodological overlay. Every cell in the matrix is an analytic regime: act-by-metaphor is choice of verb-frame (an act seen as conquest is seen-from-conquest-as-perspective); scene-by-metonymy is reduction to physical-coordinate substrate; agent-by-synecdoche is political representation (avatar-stands-for-player); purpose-by-irony is internal fatality (the purpose that arose in time perishes in time). For the dissertation, the matrix is not theoretical apparatus but practical workspace: each user-motive analysis names both its featured pentad term and its featured trope-operation, yielding a single cell that locates the analysis on the methodological map.

## (ix) Inter-Template Cross-Linkages and Methodological Composition

The six templates are not mutually exclusive. Burke's own practice repeatedly shows that they compose. Three composition patterns are particularly load-bearing for the dissertation.

**Single-text-inside-corpus composition.** GM-12 (Moore) repeatedly slows down at single poems — *Spenser's Ireland*, *To a Snail*, *Black Earth*, *The Pangolin* — and runs intra-poem analytic moves inside the cross-corpus framework. The dissertation inherits this directly: a player-corpus analysis (Moore 7-step) can and should slow down at the single trajectory that serves as exegetical key (Keats 8-step) and even at the single game inside that trajectory that supplies the structural fulcrum (Ibsen 6-step). The methodological inheritance is that scale is nested, not partitioned.

**Theory-corpus-against-text composition.** GM-08 (Constitutions) and the Read / Parkes case studies it contains demonstrate that political-text analysis composes with single-text close-reading. Burke runs the 11-move walkthrough at the level of Read's *Poetry and Anarchism* book and at the level of Parkes's *Marxism: An Autopsy*, and inside that, he runs ratio-by-ratio close-reading of specific arguments. The dissertation's design-theory chapter inherits this composition: the 11-move walkthrough operates at the school level (ludology as such), and the single-text walkthrough operates at the level of paradigmatic essays inside the school (Aarseth's *Cybertext*; Frasca's ludology-vs-narratology framing; Salen and Zimmerman's *Rules of Play*).

**Trope-overlay-across-pentad composition.** GM-13's matrix is composable with every other case because every trope-operation is also a pentad-operation. Burke's own essays show this composition by example, not by axiom — Keats's Ode is read as a synecdochic-ironic symbolic act featuring Scene-and-Act; Marianne Moore is read as a metonymic-synecdochic poet of representative-attentive Act; the U.S. Constitution is read as a metaphor-and-irony-saturated synecdoche of popular-sovereignty Agent. The dissertation's working analytic cell — featured pentad term × featured trope operation — is composable across all six cases.

## (x) Why Six Cases (and Not Five)

The plan §10 specifies five Burke cases (Ibsen, O'Neill, Constitutions, Keats, Moore). This deliverable records six because the trope-pentad apparatus operates as a self-standing parallel case at meta-methodological level. Treating GM-13 as a sixth applied-dramatism case (rather than as supplementary apparatus) reflects the GM-13 unit's own explicit framing: the four-trope apparatus is parallel to the pentad, not subordinate. The tension between Appendix-D's placement (suggesting subordination) and its content (a complete and self-standing analytical apparatus that cross-cuts every pentad term) is resolved by treating the tropes as the dialectical-rhetorical operations the pentad terms undergo, not as a competing fivefold-vs-fourfold typology. The sixth case in this deliverable's JSON array is named accordingly as `GM-DRAM-APP-06-TROPES-AS-OVERLAY` — explicitly an overlay case, with a 4-step procedural template that operates across every other case.

The plan's five-case framing remains valid at the level of *primary* deployment; the addition of the trope-overlay sixth case at *parallel* level is the deliverable's analytic extension. The 6-case / 41-step / 5-scale-plus-hybrid structure is sized to support a full dissertation chapter at corpus scale plus sub-chapters at the four primary scales.

## (xi) Refutation Conditions and Analytic Hygiene

Each template carries an explicit refutation condition. Ibsen: if scene-shifts do not correlate with quality-shifts in the permitted act, the scene-act analysis fails. O'Neill: if no act explicitly constructs scene, the ratio-reversal diagnosis does not apply. Keats: if the scene-act prediction-engine generates no demand for the next segment, the text is not well-formed dramatistically (or the analyst has mis-identified the foregrounded ratio). Moore: if recurrent motifs do not converge on a single substance, the corpus-analysis fails. Constitutions: if a text features no pentad term as locus of motive (i.e., omits all five proportionally), the diagnostic-not-classificatory featuring principle is inapplicable (Burke argues this case is empirically near-impossible).

These refutation conditions are the dissertation's anti-circularity insurance. Every chapter at every scale must specify, in advance, the empirical observation that would falsify its featured-pentad-term diagnosis or its substance-claim. The templates' procedural rigor consists precisely in the availability of these conditions.
