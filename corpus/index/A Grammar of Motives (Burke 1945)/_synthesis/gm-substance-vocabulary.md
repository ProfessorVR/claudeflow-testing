# Burke Substance Vocabulary

**18 substance types from GM-02 (Antinomies of Definition) + scattered elaborations**

Burke's substance-grammar is the dialectical machinery underlying the pentad. Where the pentad names motive-loci, substance-vocabulary names the moves by which a thinker stabilizes the motive-ascription at one locus while covertly importing terms from another. The 18 types below exhaust Burke's GM-02 taxonomy; scattered elaborations in GM-01 and GM-06 (especially actus/status as Aristotelian apparatus) extend the catalog.

The master irony, captured by **paradox-of-substance** (entry 1): the word designates what a thing intrinsically *is*, but etymologically refers to something *outside* the thing that stands beneath or supports it. Every substance-claim therefore covertly imports a contextual reference into an intrinsic claim.

---

## 1. paradox-of-substance — Paradox of substance (general)
Master-irony. Locke's 'something I know not what'; tortoise-supporting-the-elephant regress (p.22). **Move:** every substance-claim oscillates between intrinsic and extrinsic poles. **Pentad:** all five.

## 2. contextual-substance — Contextual (positional, geometric, definitional)
Definition by placement within context. Spinoza's universal substance is the maximalist version (*omnis determinatio est negatio*). Southern Agrarians *I'll Take My Stand* as a stand 'in Dixie' (pp.24-25). **Move:** treats the thing as 'in terms of' its scene; intrinsic/extrinsic swap places at the boundary. **Pentad:** Scene.

## 3. geometric-substance — Geometric substance
Subspecies of contextual: logical-spatial relations from which everything is necessarily derivable. Spinoza's geometric method; Dreiser's 'chemism' (p.29). **Move:** reduces motive to inevitable derivations within a closed scenic totality; necessity purchased by erasing the agent. **Pentad:** Scene.

## 4. familial-substance — Familial (tribal, ancestral)
Definition by ancestral cause — biological descent or spiritualized analogue (covenant, founder, eponym). Plato's forms; Aristotle's entelechy; cluster 'general/generic/genetic/genitive' (pp.26-28). **Move:** stresses derivation rather than placement; medieval *universalia* debate (ante rem / in re / post rem) is its dialectical-realist/Aristotelian/nominalist refraction. **Pentad:** Agent (and Act qua entelechy).

## 5. directional-substance — Directional substance
Motion toward; 'the way.' Strongly futuristic and purposive. Cluster motion/movement/cause/emotion/moment. I.A. Richards's pun on emotion as motion (pp.31-32). **Move:** converts 'free' motion into determined motion via statistical regularity; substantial paradox in 'tendency.' **Pentad:** Purpose (and Agency).

## 6. nutritive-substance — Nutritive (transubstantial)
Merger of contextual and familial: eating as transubstantiation. Eucharist; totemic rites; manganese as 'mother love chemical' (pp.30-31). **Move:** dissolves morphological boundary between organism and environment (Angyal's biosphere; Bernard's 'internal environment'). **Pentad:** Scene-Agent ratio.

## 7. dialectic-substance — Dialectic substance
The over-all category. Substance arising from systematic contemplation of antinomies; the *agon* of dramatistic super-category. Coleridge's 'rivales as opposite banks of the same stream'; 'we become the image of the thing we hate' (pp.33-34). **Move:** treats motive in terms of verbal/symbolic action; cultural accretions of language as 'second nature.' **Pentad:** Act (symbolic action).

## 8. paradox-of-purity — Substance under the paradox of purity
Any substance purified to absolute tends toward its negation: God as 'pure person' is impersonal; pure being is not-being; Aristotle's Pure Act = eternal repose (pp.35-37). **Move:** identifies the asymptote where intensification flips into dialectical antithesis — engine of negative theology. **Pentad:** Purpose (god-terms) and Agent (pure personality).

## 9. divisive-substance — Divisive (split) substance
Agent divided into 'two substances' by competing motivational scenes. Soldier killing nationally but not individually; man at work vs at home as 'split personality' (p.37). **Move:** names the formal mechanism by which an agent is multiply substantial — anchors later analyses of identification and ideology. **Pentad:** Agent.

## 10. tragic-dialectic-substance — *poiēma / pathēma / mathēma*
Tragic triad: act calls forth counter-assertion which agent suffers; from the suffered comes knowledge transcending the original act. Greek proverb *ta pathēmata mathēmata*; Galileo's experimental 'ordeal' (p.38). **Move:** couples act-agent ratio to dramatistic-temporal structure of trial-suffering-insight. **Pentad:** Act-Agent (temporal).

## 11. actus-status — Actus / status substance
Dyadic reduction: act vs state; motion vs rest; drama vs lyric; faith-as-act vs knowledge-as-state. Maps onto potentiality/actuality. *Aretē/virtus* from Ares — 'prowess' → potentiality → state of mind → not-killing; kingship from heroic act to inheritable state — 'L'état c'est moi' (pp.41-43). **Move:** articulates with the pentad most tightly via Act-Agent ratio. **Pentad:** Act-Agent ratio.

## 12. class-substance — Class (occupational) substance
Sociological refraction of actus/status: occupational acts harden into class-defining states. Nobility from horsemanship-in-war turning to pageantry and sport; kingship as sheer inheritable state (p.42). **Move:** anchors actus/status sociologically; formal grammar for diagnosing the petrification of action into property. **Pentad:** Agent (via class).

## 13. universal-monetary-substance — Universal motive as substance
The One God / Gold Standard. Universal motive supplying common motivational ground; monotheism and monetary motive structurally parallel; hypostasis preserved across local scenes. Universal religion / Stoic cosmopolitanism / British imperial 'dressing for dinner'; monetary motive shadowing monotheistic (pp.43-45). **Move:** universal motives are incipiently polytheistic — must split into adjectival sub-motives ('saints,' 'nationalisms,' 'wage slavery' vs 'capital'). **Pentad:** Scene / Purpose.

## 14. intrinsic-extrinsic — Intrinsic / extrinsic substance
Dialectical pairing of motivations-within-the-agent vs motivations-derived-from-scene. Hegel pairs Matter (extrinsic, gravity) with Spirit (intrinsic, *Bei-sich-selbst-sein*); Korzybski's 'inside-the-skin / outside-the-skin' (pp.46-49). **Move:** whenever this distinction is present, paradoxes follow. Spinoza's *causa sui* marks the limit. **Pentad:** Scene-Agent ratio (meta-clarification).

## 15. rhetorical-substance — Rhetorical substance
The ambiguity of substance as a deployable persuasion-resource. 'Substantially,' 'essentially,' 'in principle,' 'in the long run.' Slave 'substantially free'; League of Nations accepting proposals 'in principle' (pp.51-53). **Move:** locates rhetoric of substantiation in legal, political, scientific discourse — including disciplines that disavow substance. **Pentad:** Agency (linguistic means).

## 16. departure-substance — Substance as point of departure
Used in two opposite manners: (i) consistently ('reasons by a therefore'); (ii) compensatorily ('reasons by a however' — departing toward near-antithesis). The god-term retains its rhetorical anchor while the structure built under its name actually moves elsewhere. 'Naturalism' begun as man-in-nature, becoming a name for technology-and-money; Descartes's 'new proofs of God' that subtly redefine God (pp.53-55). **Move:** diachronic mechanism by which a substance-term's content evolves without changing its name. **Pentad:** all five (any term can be a god-term).

## 17. consubstantial-substance — Consubstantial (epithetic, identificatory) **[FORWARD-PIPELINE SEED]**
Substance as it operates in epithets and identification-tactics. To call a man brother is to proclaim consubstantiality; to call him bastard is to attack his familial authorship. 'Friend' / 'brother' / 'bastard' as substance-assignations; pragmatics of injurious and gratifying speech (p.57). **Move:** FORWARD-ANCHORS the consubstantiation apparatus of *A Rhetoric of Motives* (1950). **Load-bearing seed for the incorporation↔consubstantiation bridge to Calleja.** **Pentad:** Agent (identification).

## 18. centrality-substance — Centrality-of-substance (the synopsis-claim)
Burke's closing thesis: substance dissolves under scrutiny but re-forms as soon as scrutiny relaxes; the pentad is 'a philosopher's stone for the synopsis of writings that have sought' it. All theology, metaphysics, philosophy, criticism, poetry, drama, fiction, political exhortation involve substance-tactics (pp.55-58). **Move:** recapitulates the chapter as a meta-apparatus — every substance-type is a way of stabilizing motive-ascription; the chapter is a topography of those ways. **Pentad:** all five (synopsis-frame).
