## The keystone (held at the FCM entry; pointer)
**RESTATED at O-9, 2026-07-29.** As published, the keystone was a cross-instrument contradiction: 2023 EEG reading clinical as boring-like while 2024 gaze read it as engaging-like, with self-report engaged and felt time dilated. **That version does not survive the N=8 reprocessing** — P1's reading came from its one clean subject, and across all eight clinical's DMN power is low (engaging-like), with the boring-like reading holding in 4/8, which is chance. The corrected keystone is **all-physiology-engaged vs self-report-bored** on the clinical stimulus: every processed surface places clinical with the engaged pole while self-report rates it 6.0/9, between boring's 7.0 and interesting's 4.5. It remains a two-channel signature detectable only as disagreement, and it is stronger than the published version because it no longer rests on the noisiest instrument. Only pupil dilation is significant at N=8 (Boring < Clinical in all 8, p=0.008); the rest corroborate in direction only. **Canonical statement: FCM `_synthesis/fcm-king-salvo-bridge.md` — that file still carries the superseded wording and its repair is pending an author ruling on how the surface/depth layers reassign.**
