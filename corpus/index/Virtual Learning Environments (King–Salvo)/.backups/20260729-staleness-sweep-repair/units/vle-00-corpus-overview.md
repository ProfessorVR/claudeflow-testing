Part III's empirical corpus mirrors Part II's G&G↔RDR2 structure with the author's own studies at the poles:
- **VR pole (lab + pilot, 2022–2024):** P1 physiological (ASEE 2023 #37129, N=3, EEG/DMN + HMD, 17-min stimuli boring/interesting/clinical) · P2 eye-tracking (ASEE 2024 #44685, N=12, gaze-deviation variance; clinical-vs-boring p=0.0004; searching-gaze vs zombie-stare) · P3 phenomenological evaluation (Biomed Eng Educ 4 (2024): 381–397, DOI 10.1007/s43683-024-00139-5; BME 179 spring 2022, N=22/20; four formats; presence 70% yet 40% judge plain 2D most beneficial) · the boredom raw dataset (8 subjects, EEG/HMD/OBS + per-video 7-item self-reports incl. felt duration).
