A *contested node* in this taxonomy is a canonical CONCEPT (or POSITION-as-CONCEPT) whose Calleja-internal treatment exhibits one or more of:
(a) **internal-to-Calleja contradiction** — the same concept is endorsed and disclaimed in different registers within the volume;
(b) **cross-unit incompatibility** — adjacent chapters treat the concept with incompatible commitments;
(c) **cite-base mismatch** — the concept's authority lineage is incomplete relative to how heavily Calleja leans on it;
(d) **structural defect** — typo / enumeration slip / missing subsection that the reader must adjudicate;
(e) **acknowledged under-development** — Calleja flags the concept's coverage as thin.

Ten contested nodes are documented below, ordered roughly by dissertation-load-bearingness.

## 1. C-003 — Incorporation (volume-titular concept)

- **Canonical ID + label:** C-003 Incorporation
- **Nature of contest:** internal-to-Calleja — *metaphor-disclaimer vs. analytic-superiority*. In IG-00 (pp.3–5) Calleja calls incorporation "ultimately a metaphor like presence or immersion"; in IG-10 (pp.169–174) he claims it provides "a more robust concept" that resolves the four challenges of presence/immersion. The robustness must therefore live in the metaphor's tracking-fidelity to the multi-dimensional phenomenon, not in any literal-ontological gain (flagged in ontology FLAG-2 and tension T-002).
- **Supporting units + anchors:** IG-00 p.3 (announcement); IG-10 pp.169–174 (full elaboration); IG-11 p.183 (recap).
- **Implication for dissertation augmentation layers:** Layer A (Heidegger-BCAP) can supply the literal-ontological grounding Calleja explicitly disclaims — incorporation-as-*In-der-Welt-sein* rather than incorporation-as-trope. The disclaimer is a *direct invitation* to augment, not an obstacle. Layer B (Burke pentad) provides the analytic apparatus (scene/agent/act/agency/purpose ratios) that lets incorporation operate as a *taxonomy of involvement gestalts* rather than as a single undifferentiated frame.

## 2. C-008 — Virtual environment habitation

- **Canonical ID + label:** C-008 Virtual environment habitation
- **Nature of contest:** cite-base mismatch + cross-unit register-shift. Habitation is IG-05's load-bearing metaphor and the master-trope of incorporation in IG-10, yet IG-05 itself names the concept under at least 8 synonymous formulations (habitation / metaphorically-habitable-environment / place [Tuan] / virtual-environment habitation / inhabited-place / dwelling / on-screen-place / inhabited-space) across 5 chapters without resolving them into a stable nomenclature. The phenomenological weight (Heideggerian *Wohnen*, Tuan's *place*) is borrowed without sustained cite-development: Tuan 1977 is the only consistently anchored source; Heidegger is referenced once in the volume (IG-02 p.21).
- **Supporting units + anchors:** IG-00 p.5; IG-02 p.21; IG-05 pp.73–90; IG-06 p.94 (passing); IG-10 pp.169–174; IG-11 p.183. T-012 in tension-edges.json.
- **Implication for dissertation augmentation layers:** Layer A grounds habitation in *In-der-Welt-sein* and Layer C re-anchors it in **Umwelt** (Uexküll) rather than Welt — Umwelt captures the species-specific/designer-bounded character of game-worlds that Welt does not. The dissertation should treat habitation as the *single most augmentation-ready* node in the PIM.

## 3. C-018 / IG-02 — Immersion (Murray-sense): critique-and-rescue pattern

- **Canonical ID + label:** C-018 Four conceptual challenges of presence/immersion (and the underlying Murray-absorption construct)
- **Nature of contest:** cross-unit contradiction. IG-02 (pp.17–33) systematically critiques Murray's transportation/absorption metaphor as one of the four challenges; IG-10 (p.172) then quietly preserves Murray's experiential intuition ("absorbed-not-refused") under the incorporation rubric while jettisoning her vocabulary. Murray is rescued without acknowledgment of the rescue.
- **Supporting units + anchors:** IG-02 pp.17–33; IG-10 p.172; T-004 in tension-edges.json.
- **Implication for dissertation augmentation layers:** The vocabulary-residue (T-004, T-005) confirms that incorporation is *not* a clean break from the phenomenological tradition — it inherits Murray's absorption-intuition while disowning its lineage. The dissertation can argue that the inherited intuition awaits the phenomenological-ontological grounding Layer A provides, since Calleja's preferred Lakoff+Johnson/Damasio naturalism cannot discharge the residue.

## 4. C-009 — Alterbiography (occurs-in-micro-phase without labeled Micro-Phase subsection)

- **Canonical ID + label:** C-009 Alterbiography (emergent player-narrative)
- **Nature of contest:** structural defect. IG-07 defines alterbiography as a moment-to-moment emergent phenomenon and states explicitly (p.131) that it "occurs in micro phase," yet IG-07 is the only dimension chapter (Ch.4–9) that lacks a labeled "Micro Phase" subsection. The structural template of the volume breaks at exactly the concept the chapter is named for.
- **Supporting units + anchors:** IG-07 pp.115–131; T-007 in tension-edges.json.
- **Implication for dissertation augmentation layers:** Reinforces T-003 (macro/micro split is honored in the breach). Treat alterbiography as a *kairic* phenomenon (BCAP-grounded Layer A) — a temporally-emergent trace of agency exercised within scripted-narrative architecture — rather than as a micro-phase narrative slot. Burke pentad ratio (agency/scene+act) gives the analytic operationalization.

## 5. C-027 — Three-not-four shared subtypes (cohabitation/cooperation/competition)

- **Canonical ID + label:** C-027 Cohabitation / cooperation / competition
- **Nature of contest:** editorial defect (enumeration slip). IG-06 figure-level diagram presents three subtypes; prose at pp.93–97 and 100–101 enumerates a fourth (variously "communication" or "collaboration"). Reader must reconcile a three-cell figure with a four-cell prose enumeration.
- **Supporting units + anchors:** IG-06 pp.100–101; FLAG-7 in ontology; T-006 in tension-edges.json.
- **Implication for dissertation augmentation layers:** When operationalizing SHARED involvement (D-003), the dissertation must elect either the three-subtype or four-subtype taxonomy and justify. Recommend four-subtype with explicit acknowledgment of figure/prose mismatch; Burke pentad's *scene+co-agent* ratio supplies the principled basis (communication is co-agent-mediated, distinct from cohabitation-as-scene).

## 6. T-001/T-002 (C-004/C-005) — Macro/micro split: clean-split-as-modeled vs. continuous-attention-gradient-as-experienced

- **Canonical ID + label:** C-004 Macro temporal phase + C-005 Micro temporal phase (T-001/T-002 in the temporal_phases registry)
- **Nature of contest:** internal-to-Calleja + cross-unit asymmetry. Calleja presents macro/micro as binary partitions (IG-03 pp.38–46) but his own examples — mid-session strategy daydreaming, off-hours raid scheduling — show the phases as a continuous attentional-pressure gradient. FLAG-4 in ontology notes macro is comparatively under-theorized across IG-03 through IG-09. The split is heuristically useful but ontologically suspect.
- **Supporting units + anchors:** IG-03 pp.38–46; IG-04 pp.55–70; IG-09 pp.148–152; T-003 in tension-edges.json; FLAG-4 in ontology.
- **Implication for dissertation augmentation layers:** Justifies replacing macro/micro with **kairic phasing** from BCAP (Aristotelian *kairos*) — the bleed is a Bowin-AMT (Aristotelian motion-toward-end) continuous phenomenon; phantasia-cluster anticipatory/retentional structure provides the cross-phase integrator. Treat macro/micro as a *heuristic scaffold* not a partition.

## 7. C-031 / D-005 — Affective involvement: under-development (Calleja-acknowledged)

- **Canonical ID + label:** D-005 AFFECTIVE involvement + C-031 Excitatory homeostasis
- **Nature of contest:** acknowledged under-development + internal conflation. IG-08 is the volume's shortest dimension chapter (12 pp.) and Calleja flags it as substantively under-developed (P-015). The chapter also conflates atmospherics-as-affect-source with rules-as-affect-source (T-008) and mood with emotion (T-009) — both Calleja-side flattenings, since Bryant+Davies 2006 and Grodal 2000 preserve the distinctions.
- **Supporting units + anchors:** IG-08 pp.135–146; FLAG-8 in ontology; T-008 and T-009 in tension-edges.json.
- **Implication for dissertation augmentation layers:** Strongest single opening for **Gross 2017 Uncomfortable Situations** (situated emotion adjudicates atmospherics-vs-rules as a single situation), **Uexküll Merktone/Wirktone** (Layer C: environmental affect-tones and rule-induced effect-tones are both Funktionskreis components), and **Burke pentad** (attitude maps to mood, purpose-inflected emotion maps to episodic affect — restoring T-009's lost distinction).

## 8. C-035 (+ C-036, C-037) — Magic-circle / lusory-attitude / fun excision

- **Canonical ID + label:** C-035 Magic-circle excision (with C-036 lusory-attitude and C-037 fun-essentialism)
- **Nature of contest:** internal-to-Calleja (selective excision). Calleja excises three constructs from IG-03 (pp.46–53) — magic-circle (Huizinga/Salen+Zimmerman/Juul), lusory-attitude (Suits), fun-essentialism — yet participants in his interview data and the games themselves continue to operate within voluntary-attitude-toward-rules brackets (the entire MMOG raid-scheduling apparatus presupposes a lusory commitment), and the prose retains evaluative language ("immersive," "engaging," "compelling") that re-imports fun's normative function. The excision is principled at the analytic level but porous at the descriptive level.
- **Supporting units + anchors:** IG-03 pp.46–53; IG-06 (MMOG-governance critique using magic-circle, p.94 — see C-035 appearance in IG-06); IG-10 (retirement of magic-circle, C-035 appearance).
- **Implication for dissertation augmentation layers:** Layer B (Burke) can restore lusory-attitude as the *purpose* term of the pentad without re-essentializing it; Layer A (BCAP) can restore the magic-circle's bracketing function as a phenomenological-attitudinal *Einstellung* (Husserlian phenomenological attitude) rather than a spatial bracket; the dissertation thereby preserves Calleja's analytic clarity while restoring descriptive adequacy.

## 9. C-010 — Ergodicity as dispositional readiness (Calleja's pickup-and-extend of Aarseth)

- **Canonical ID + label:** C-010 Ergodicity as dispositional readiness to act
- **Nature of contest:** cite-base mismatch. Calleja reformulates Aarseth's *Cybertext* (1997) ergodic input — originally a physical-effort criterion — into a dispositional-readiness-to-act criterion (IG-03 p.41; IG-04 pp.55–70). The reformulation is substantive (it lets cognitive effort and patient alertness count as ergodic) but is not flagged as a departure from Aarseth, and Aarseth is not re-cited in the extension itself. The reformulation passes as Aarseth's view.
- **Supporting units + anchors:** IG-01; IG-02 p.28; IG-03 p.41; IG-04 pp.55–70; IG-09.
- **Implication for dissertation augmentation layers:** Direct bridge to **Bowin-AMT** (Aristotelian motion-toward-end) — dispositional readiness IS *dunamis*-oriented-toward-energeia. Layer A grounds the extension Calleja makes without ground. The dissertation can flag Calleja's silent reformulation and supply the ontological lineage he leaves implicit.

## 10. Volume-wide — Residual phenomenological vocabulary (T-005 cluster)

- **Canonical ID + label:** Cross-cutting (not a single canonical concept) — instantiated as C-008 habitation, C-016 experiential gestalts, P-013 continuous-with-everyday-life, and the *inhabit / lived / comportment / experience* terminology pervading IG-04 / IG-05 / IG-06 / IG-07 / IG-08 / IG-10.
- **Nature of contest:** vocabulary-residue (cross-unit). Calleja invokes Lakoff+Johnson 2003 experiential-gestalts and Damasio 2000 consciousness as the preferred (cognitivist-naturalist) grounding in IG-10, yet the descriptive vocabulary across the volume is unmistakably phenomenological. The naturalist scaffold cannot discharge the phenomenological residue. The single Heidegger reference (IG-02 p.21) is preserved as a sympathetic foothold that does no analytic work.
- **Supporting units + anchors:** IG-02 p.21; IG-04–IG-08 *passim*; IG-10 pp.167–173; T-005 in tension-edges.json.
- **Implication for dissertation augmentation layers:** Volume-wide **direct invitation** to Layer A (Heidegger-BCAP). Calleja's phenomenological vocabulary is awaiting ontological grounding in BCAP; the Lakoff+Johnson cognitivist scaffold is principled but inadequate. The dissertation can argue this as the *primary justification* for the Heidegger-BCAP augmentation: not that Calleja got it wrong, but that he could not get it fully right within his chosen naturalist framework.
