## Aggregate observations

- **WoW is sui generis** as the only game cited in all 9 chapter-units that admit per-game citation; its centrality is the empirical-methodological backbone of the volume.
- **Planetside is the second backbone** (8 units) — together WoW + Planetside form the binary comparative scaffold (per IG-APX, P-018).
- **The Tetris / Counter-Strike : WoW : tabletop-board-game contrast** is the volume's stratification key: Tetris = involvement-without-incorporation (no cornerstone); CS:S = incorporation-when-cornerstone-internalized; WoW = incorporation across all six dimensions sustained over time; tabletop = potential incorporation despite low-fidelity (per IG-10's anti-fidelity-determinism argument).
- **Genre distribution**: MMORPG (heavy), MMOFPS (Planetside-dominant), CS-genre competitive FPS (heavy in micro-phase), narrative FPS (Bioshock, Half-Life series), open-world action (GTA IV, Oblivion, Fallout 3), strategy (Total War series), sports (FIFA, Gran Turismo), puzzle (Tetris), parkour (Mirror's Edge), aesthetic (Flower).
- **Notable absences** (potential dissertation-relevant counter-cases not in Calleja's corpus): no Dark Souls family; no Minecraft; no League of Legends / Dota 2 (MOBA absent); no Skyrim (post-2011 cutoff explains).
