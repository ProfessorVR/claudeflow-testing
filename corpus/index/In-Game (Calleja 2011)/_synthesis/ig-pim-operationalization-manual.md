## Section A — Six Dimension Entries

Each dimension entry below carries: operational definition (≤60 words), boundary criteria (4–6), macro/micro phase signatures, paradigm worked examples (4–6), ambiguous-case decision rules, dissertation Layer-A ontological target, Layer-B Burke-pentad mapping, Layer-C Uexküllian-construct mapping, and residual-phenomenology anchor sites (Layer-A leverage points where Calleja uses phenomenological-register language without phenomenologist cite).

### A.1 KINESTHETIC

**Operational definition (53 words, book p.43):** Engagement with avatar or game-piece control in a virtual environment, spanning from conscious learning of the controls through to fluent internalized movement; foregrounded when the controls make themselves present, either through unmastery or because a situation demands a complex sequence of actions that taxes the player.

**Boundary criteria (5):**
1. *Locus of attention is the control surface (avatar manipulation) rather than the environment itself* — distinguishes from SPATIAL (locus = environment-as-place). (Ch.3 pp.43, 45.)
2. *Foregrounded under unmastery or complex-sequence demand; recedes upon internalization* — only KINESTHETIC exhibits this control-presence/internalization dynamic. (Ch.3 p.43, 45.)
3. *Ergodic-dispositional under-determination: includes readiness-to-act even when no physical input is occurring* — confusable with LUDIC under strategy cases (apply R1). (Ch.3 p.42, Red Orchestra sniper.)
4. *Internalized state shows reduced attentional load measurable in resource-reallocation to other dimensions* — Calleja's "Quan" / thinking-with-fingers signature (IG-04 p.67–68, Bladerunner interview).
5. *Avatar/miniature scaling: control can vest in a single avatar or be distributed across multiple miniatures (e.g., Empire: Total War's 20-unit command); both count as KINESTHETIC.* (IG-04 Figure 4.1.)

**Macro signature:** Recalling impressive feats of avatar control between sessions; mental rehearsal of complex-sequence inputs; motor-habit retention.
**Micro signature:** Control-internalization sequence; readiness-to-act poised on conditions (Red Orchestra sniper waiting on a covered street with no enemies in sight, Figure 3.2); continuity-of-movement under parkour-style demand (Mirror's Edge).

**Paradigm worked examples (8 — all from IG-04):**
- *Counter-Strike: Source* (symbolic) — grenade-throw dissociates agency from intention; Giddens-over-Murray argument.
- *Tetris* (symbolic) — single-miniature focus with three-dimension micro-interleaving (rotate / monitor-next / clear-layer).
- *Empire: Total War* (symbolic, distributed) — paradigm of simultaneous-miniature command up to 20 units (Figure 4.1).
- *FIFA 2009* (symbolic, oscillating) — avatar/miniature oscillation between active-player and formation-as-whole (Figure 4.2).
- *Wii Sports* (mimetic) — Wii-mote tennis swing maps onto avatar swing.
- *Kinect / Project Natal* (symbiotic) — full-body capture; design ambition to eliminate the controller.
- *Max Payne* (symbolic, exception-rule) — bullet-time / Neo-fantasy as designed exception to ordinary kinematics (Figure 4.4).
- *Mirror's Edge* (symbolic, momentum-rewarding) — continuity-of-movement as kinesthetic pleasure peak (Figure 4.5); the chapter's anchor for generic-non-Gibson "affordances".

**Ambiguous-case decision rules (R1, R2):**
- **R1 (KIN vs LUDIC)** — If dispositional readiness is oriented toward execution when conditions are met (firing on an emerging enemy), code KINESTHETIC. If cognitive effort is rehearsal/evaluation of choice-and-repercussion structures, code LUDIC. Red Orchestra sniper blends both — report weighted.
- **R2 (KIN vs SPATIAL)** — Foregrounded controls + recessed place = KINESTHETIC. Internalized controls + foregrounded place = SPATIAL. Half-Life 2 heavy-navigation toggles between the two.

**Dissertation Layer-A ontological target:** *aisthesis-kinesis* (Bowin AMT — Aristotelian Motion and Time) + *dunamis/hexis* (Aristotelian potency/settled disposition). Readiness-to-act is *dunamis-as-hexis*; learned-internalized control is the actualization of *hexis*. The continuity-of-motion analysis (Sunniva's "continuous motion"; Mirror's Edge) is the paradigm Bowin AMT bridge target. Strongest chapter in the volume for this Layer-A anchor.

**Layer-B Burke-pentad mapping:** **AGENCY**. The whole expository thrust of the chapter is exertion-of-agency through control and movement. The symbolic/mimetic/symbiotic continuum (Figure 4.3, the chapter's load-bearing innovation, C-020) is a typology of Agency in Burke's pentadal sense. Secondary: player-as-extension framing (Morris via IG-04 p.68) parallels Burke consubstantiation/identification. *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*

**Layer-C Uexküllian-construct mapping:** ***Funktionskreis*** (functional circle: Merkmal/Merkträger → object → Wirkmal/Wirkträger → back to perception). The player-avatar feedback loop IS a functional-circle structure; internalization closes the loop into automaticity. Symbolic ↔ loose Merkorgan-Wirkorgan coupling; mimetic ↔ partial body-mapping; symbiotic ↔ maximally tight body-mapping. *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*

**Residual-phenomenology anchor sites (Layer-A leverage points):**
- IG-04 p.68 (Morris on internalization): "the player feels like an extension of the game or the game feels like an extension of the player" — Heideggerian *Zuhandenheit* / Bowin AMT *hexis* target without phenomenologist cite.
- IG-04 p.67 (Bladerunner "Quan"): "your mind is willing those actions" — Aristotelian *boulesis/orexis* + Husserlian "I-can" target.
- IG-04 p.67 (Sunniva "continuous focus"): "continuous motion that keeps my attention on the game…a certain steady, continuous focus" — Bowin AMT continuity-of-motion / ekstatic-temporality target.

### A.2 SPATIAL

**Operational definition (41 words, book p.43):** Engagement with the spatial qualities of a virtual environment via spatial control, navigation, and exploration; accounts for the process of internalizing game-spaces so that the player senses inhabiting a place rather than merely perceiving a representation of space.

**Boundary criteria (5):**
1. *Locus is the virtual environment construed as place-to-inhabit, not the controls* — distinguishes from KINESTHETIC. (Ch.3 p.43.)
2. *Operative mode is navigation / exploration / spatial-internalization, not narrative legibility* — distinguishes from NARRATIVE (where embedded objects generate story-significance rather than place-significance; resolve via R3).
3. *Inhabitation-of-place vs mere perception-of-representation* — Pong fails this; Oblivion and Half-Life 2 succeed (Ch.3 p.44).
4. *Non-navigable spaces (FIFA stands, Battlefield 1942 outside-combat-area) are representational backdrop, NOT spatial-involvement loci.* (Ch.3 p.49.)
5. *Macro-phase signature: spatial mental models maintained off-line* — Ratchet-to-Feathermoon route held in memory between sessions; distinguishes from KIN-macro (recalling control-feats).

**Macro signature:** Spatial mental models maintained off-line (WoW Ratchet-to-Feathermoon route-planning); cognitive cartography sustained across sessions; landmark-anchored wayfinding rehearsal.
**Micro signature:** In-session navigation + exploration + place-internalization; the moment Tuanian *space* shifts into Tuanian *place* via familiarity.

**Paradigm worked examples (8 — all from IG-05):**
- *Medal of Honor: Allied Assault* opening (pp.78–79) — unicursal-corridor disguise + invisible-wall cognitive-map update.
- *Fable* hill-and-rock channeling + desert-ring (pp.80–81) — 1st/3rd-person multicursal-maze.
- *Fable II* teleport + *Mass Effect* zone-jumps (pp.81–82) — rhizomatic worked-example pair (intra-region vs inter-region rhizome).
- *Counter-Strike: Source* de_dust2 B-rush (pp.83–86 + Figures 5.2/5.3) — chapter's most detailed worked example.
- *WoW Azeroth vs Planetside Auraxis* (pp.88–90) — imageability-contrast worked example (high-imageability/geographic-implausibility vs lower-imageability/geographic-plausibility); meaningfulness trumps fidelity.
- *Oblivion map↔3D-environment transition* (pp.89–90 + Figure 5.4) — indexical-to-inhabited shift.
- *Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War II* miniature landscape (p.91 + Figure 5.5) — tactical-field miniature space.
- *Empire: Total War* Portugal+Silesia simultaneity (p.91) — distributed-agency miniature; disembodied-omniscient spatial control.

**Ambiguous-case decision rules (R2, R3):**
- **R2 (SPA vs KIN)** — see above under KINESTHETIC.
- **R3 (SPA vs NAR under environmental-storytelling)** — Place-as-inhabited focus (route, layout, scale, atmosphere) = SPATIAL. Environment-as-story-cipher (what does this room tell me) = NARRATIVE. The same physical environment toggles as attentional foregrounding shifts.

**Dissertation Layer-A ontological target:** *Phantasia* spatial-imagery (Phantasia Cluster — Hawhee rhetorical vision; Papachristou deliberative phantasia). Internalization of game-space is the maintenance of spatial *phantasmata* in memory between sessions; macro-phase route-planning IS phantasia-deliberative. Secondary: Heideggerian *Wohnen* / In-der-Welt-sein supplies the dwelling-in-the-world register Calleja invokes via Tuan without phenomenological grounding.

**Layer-B Burke-pentad mapping:** **SCENE**. Place-as-inhabited is the Burkean Scene; the spatial-internalization process is the Agent's coming-to-dwell-in the Scene. Scene/Agent ratio in incorporation: the cornerstone-pair (R4 of incorporation rubric) is the dramatistic ground on which the Agent acts. *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*

**Layer-C Uexküllian-construct mapping:** ***Umwelt*** (+ *Merkwelt* / *Wirkwelt*). The virtual environment as inhabited-place is precisely *Umwelt* — the subjectively-constituted world of meaning-carriers; *Merkwelt* (perceptual world) and *Wirkwelt* (effect world) jointly compose the navigable virtual space. **This is the volume's strongest Layer-C anchor** — the bounded, designer-constituted nature of virtual environments is arguably better fit by *Umwelt* than by Heideggerian *Welt* (see Section E). *Provenance: anticipatory-projection (singly-anchored — Gibson bridge falsified at Phase 0).*

**Residual-phenomenology anchor sites:**
- IG-05 pp.75–77 ("Lost in the Game World" section): Heideggerian *Verlorenheit* / disorientation phenomenology routed through Tuan + Klastrup + Borges; chapter's largest residual-phenomenology site.
- IG-05 pp.75, 76–77 (body-relative orientation; front/back/right/left, futurity-ahead): Merleau-Ponty + Husserl body-schema target, anchored only via Tuan.
- IG-05 pp.85–90 ("From Space to Place"): Heideggerian *Wohnen* / In-der-Welt-sein register routed through Tuan (1977/2007) + Lynch + Tolman. The chapter's "sense of inhabiting a place rather than perceiving a representation of space" formula (book p.43) is the volume's single most Heideggerian sentence with no Heidegger.

### A.3 SHARED

**Operational definition (44 words, book p.43):** Engagement deriving from awareness of and interaction with other agents (human- or computer-controlled) in the game environment under the modalities of cohabitation, cooperation, and competition; encompasses collaborative strategy, guild-political discussion, and ambient awareness that one's actions occur in a social context.

**Boundary criteria (4):**
1. *Presence of other agents (human or AI) is the constitutive condition.* Remove the agents and the dimension drops out — distinguishes from all other dimensions, which can operate in single-player isolation.
2. *Includes ambient awareness of social context even absent direct interaction.* Distinguishes from LUDIC-under-cooperative-play (resolve via R4).
3. *Modalities span cohabitation / cooperation / competition* (three-not-four enumeration slip flagged at 3B FLAG-7 and tension T-006 — IG-06 prose occasionally implies a fourth "communication" or "collaboration" sub-dimension). Dissertation operationalization recommendation: ELECT four-subtype prose-level taxonomy with explicit acknowledgment of figure/prose mismatch.
4. *Human-vs-AI co-agent distinction restructures disposition* — even rudimentary AI grounds cohabitation (GTA IV Dimitri-Rascalov betrayal; Reeves-Nass media-equation) but only persistent human-controlled agents support the reputation-and-betrayal layer (EVE Online Guiding Hand heist).

**Macro signature:** Clan-website work, guild-political deliberation, persisting social bonds (the majority macro-phase report in IG-03 interview data); pre-session social-coordination; cross-session reputation accumulation.
**Micro signature:** In-session cohabitation/cooperation/competition + ambient social-context awareness; designed-interdependence mechanics (Left 4 Dead Hunter/Smoker rescue-required; single-use shared medical pack).

**Paradigm worked examples (7 — from IG-06):**
- *World of Warcraft* (pp.94–97, 101, 107) — all three sub-dimensions: Ironforge city cohabitation; end-game guild cooperation; PvP-server competition; Sara Andrews governance case; Austin Texas geographic guild as bridging-spillover.
- *Planetside* (pp.101, 106, 107, 109) — mass-scale cooperation (Bladerunner virtual armies; Ghosts of the Revolution outfit, Figure 6.3); BunkerBoy86 human-vs-AI preference; large-scale FPS cooperation-competition fusion.
- *Left 4 Dead* (p.105) — designed-interdependence cooperation paradigm: AI director, Hunter/Smoker rescue-required, see-through-walls cohesion, single-use shared medpack.
- *FIFA 2009* (pp.104, 108) — co-located living-room spectatorship; spectatorship-as-cohabitation.
- *Grand Theft Auto IV* (p.102) — Dimitri Rascalov betrayal of Nico operationalizes media-equation cohabitation (AI-only co-presence grounds shared involvement).
- *Counter-Strike / CoD IV / Red Orchestra* (p.111) — casual-to-semipro arc (Rambusch et al. 2007); institutionalized shift from individual to team-play identity.
- *EVE Online (Guiding Hand Social Club heist)* (p.94) — macro-phase MMOG-society betrayal sub-genre; virtual social structure carrying economic consequence sufficient to make corporate-takedown plotlines legible as *social* events.

**Ambiguous-case decision rules (R4):**
- **R4 (SHA vs LUD under cooperative goal-directed play)** — Joint goal-pursuit + choice-coordination = primarily LUDIC + SHARED-secondary modulator. Social-context awareness / guild-political dynamics / cohabitation independent of joint goal = primarily SHARED. Cooperative strategy formation is joint — both weighted.

**Dissertation Layer-A ontological target:** *Mitsein* (Heidegger BCAP / SZ §§25–27) + ethical-political register of *phronesis*. Being-with as ontological mode of co-inhabitation supplies the existential-analytic register Calleja's "social-context awareness" lacks. *Das Man* (SZ §27) for server-consensus self-policing (Sara Andrews case).

**Layer-B Burke-pentad mapping:** **AGENT (with co-agent ratios)**. Other agents populate the dramatistic field; SHARED foregrounds the Agent-Agent ratios (cohabitation / cooperation / competition). *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*

**Layer-C Uexküllian-construct mapping:** ***Umwelt*-overlap** (intersubjective biosemiotic field). Multiple agents inhabit overlapping but non-identical Umwelten; the three modalities are biosemiotic modulations of overlap. *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*

**Residual-phenomenology anchor sites:**
- IG-06 pp.94–95: MMOGs as "full-fledged on-line societies" with friendships, marriages, betrayals — strongest Layer-A *Mitsein* anchor in the chapter; no Heidegger cite.
- IG-06 pp.97–99: Sara Andrews / Blizzard case + server-consensus self-policing — *das Man* register; no Heidegger.
- IG-06 pp.103–104: Spectatorship and performance-for-audience — Goffmanian dramaturgy register; no Goffman.
- IG-06 pp.101–102: Reeves-Nass media-equation passage — intersubjectivity register treated cognitivistically; no phenomenology.

### A.4 NARRATIVE

**Operational definition (33 words, book pp.43–44):** Engagement with both story elements scripted into the game and story elements emergent from the player's ongoing interaction with the game world, its embedded objects, its inhabitants, and the events that occur there.

**Boundary criteria (4):**
1. *Object of engagement is story-content (scripted) OR alterbiography-emergent story* — distinguishes from SPATIAL (place-content) and SHARED (agent-interaction).
2. *Dual scripted / emergent character* — purely scripted-narrative reductions miss the emergent component; purely emergent readings miss the designer-installed pull-elements.
3. *Macro-phase signature: speculating about designed-narrative development; story-construction featuring one's character* — distinguishes from ludic-macro (winning-strategy formation) and shared-macro (community-site work).
4. *Story-significance vs place-significance toggle* — environmental-storytelling boundary contested with SPATIAL; resolve via R3.

**Macro signature:** Speculation about designed-narrative development; story-construction featuring one's character; cross-session emplotment of alterbiography (e.g., Muun's Figure 7.2 alterbiography example).
**Micro signature:** In-session engagement with scripted + emergent story-elements; alterbiography-occurrence-locus; pull-narrative pursuit (Bioshock audio-diary collection). N.B. IG-07 lacks a clearly labeled "Micro Phase" subsection paralleling Ch.4–6/8–9 — structural-template defect flagged at 3B tension T-007.

**Paradigm worked examples (7 — from IG-07):**
- *Bioshock* (pp.123, 132–133 + Figure 7.3) — PULL NARRATIVE paradigm + SCRIPTED-becomes-ALTERBIOGRAPHY paradigm (Dr. Steinman foreshadowing → surgery-encounter absorbed into player alterbiography).
- *Grand Theft Auto IV* (pp.117–119) — cybertextually-grounded alterbiography walkthrough (Nico/Roman/Michelle); cut-scene-dominant push narrative.
- *Max Payne* (pp.115, 125) — anti-Juul demonstration that scripted narrative supports flashback/flashforward; alterbiography-of-entity (third-person disposition).
- *Halo 3* (pp.121–122) — linear scripted-narrative-progression (checkpoint-gated).
- *Fallout 3* (pp.122, 126) — branching-backbone + alterbiography-of-self (first-person disposition).
- *World of Warcraft* (pp.115+) — no-main-story-line-with-side-quests-only; Muun Figure 7.2 alterbiography example; two-interview-quote disposition contrast.
- *Oblivion* (pp.115+) — scripted-to-open-world transition (Uriel Septim escape); pull narrative via in-world books; CRPG attribute/representation synthesis (Redguard/Breton).

**Ambiguous-case decision rules (R3 + R11):**
- **R3 (NAR vs SPA)** — see above under SPATIAL.
- **R11 (NAR vs LUD under quest-gated branching)** — Engagement with the story-significance of the choice = NARRATIVE. Engagement with the choice-and-repercussion structure (opportunity cost) = LUDIC. Alterbiography is precisely the construct that integrates the two (tension T-011 resolved via alterbiography).

**Dissertation Layer-A ontological target:** ***Muthos* / emplotment** (Aristotle *Poetics* ch.6–10). Alterbiography is functionally Aristotelian *muthos* in everything but vocabulary — synthesis of incidents (*kineseis*) into a unified action with beginning/middle/end, now distributed across game sessions. IG-07 tabletop-RPG worked example (pp.129–130) presents muthos in miniature.

**Layer-B Burke-pentad mapping:** **ACT + PURPOSE**. Story-content is the sequence of Acts toward Purposes (scripted and emergent); alterbiography is the emergent-Act layer. Push narrative = Purpose-saturated + Act-constraining; pull narrative releases Purpose to Player-Act. Bioshock-Steinman intersection is the chapter's paradigm Act/Purpose ratio. *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*

**Layer-C Uexküllian-construct mapping:** ***Bedeutungsträger* (meaning-carrier) sequence within *Umwelt***. NPCs, quest items, audio diaries, environmental details function as successive meaning-bearers. Pull narrative maps especially well: player-pulled *Bedeutungsträger* are biosemiotically active; pushed cut scenes function as designer-imposed non-*Bedeutungsträger* interruptions. *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*

**Residual-phenomenology anchor sites:**
- IG-07 p.127 (Iser 1991 quoted directly on reader-synthesis): "the reader must put together what wandering viewpoint has divided up; syntheses of dual nature, projections from reader plus textual signals." **MEDIATED-EXEGETICAL anchor — Iser IS cited.**
- IG-07 p.128 (Sartre 1967 *What Is Literature?* quoted directly): author/reader interdependence — "the combined efforts of author and reader bring into being the concrete and imaginary object which is the work of the mind." **MEDIATED-EXEGETICAL anchor — Sartre IS cited.**
- IG-07 p.130 (Sartre 1995 *The Imaginary* invoked): synthesizing efforts becoming apparent when representational qualities are ambiguous. **MEDIATED-EXEGETICAL anchor.**

### A.5 AFFECTIVE

**Operational definition (49 words, book p.44):** Engagement with the range of emotional states elicited by play — from the calming sensation of an aesthetically pleasing scene through the adrenaline rush of competitive first-person-shooter rounds to the uncanny of an action-horror episode — including both designer-installed rhetorical strategies of affect and player-precipitated affective interpretations.

**Boundary criteria (4):**
1. *Object of engagement is the player's emotional state, irrespective of trigger-dimension* — distinguishes from all other dimensions, which name trigger-content.
2. *Designer-installed (rhetorical strategies) AND player-precipitated (individual interpretation) sources both count.*
3. *Range spans calming / adrenaline / uncanny — multi-valenced, NOT coextensive with positive affect / fun.* (Excises fun-essentialism per IG-03 C-FUN-EXC.)
4. *Always co-occurs with another dimension* (which supplies the trigger); never code AFFECTIVE in isolation (R5).
5. *Mood vs emotion distinction (FLAG: tension T-009)* — Calleja conflates these; dissertation should restore the distinction (mood = diffuse/sustained/non-intentional; emotion = acute/episodic/intentional).

**Macro signature:** Sense of inner peace following travels in aesthetically moving surroundings (Oriel on Menethil Harbor, Aprile on Night Elf Land); anticipatory excitement / anxiety about next session.
**Micro signature:** In-session calming / adrenaline / uncanny responses to designer-installed + player-precipitated triggers; cross-dimensional affective amplification (F.E.A.R. flanking-AI → horror sequence).

**Paradigm worked examples (7 — from IG-08):**
- *Inauro on Duskwood Forest (WoW)* — atmospherics-as-affect-design: "Mirkwood" aesthetic of lighting + sound + monster placement + thematic consistency.
- *Oriel on Menethil Harbor (WoW)* — aesthetic-habitation desire: "I want to live there"; habitable-secondary-world-as-affective-space.
- *Aprile on Night Elf Land (WoW)* — aesthetic-mood-regulation: "whenever I don't feel like playing, I come here"; deliberate-non-play-affective-engagement.
- *Medal of Honor vs Red Orchestra* (lines 4956–4991) — mechanic-as-affect-source vs atmospherics-as-affect-source duality (Hollywood-pastiche vs historical-accuracy).
- *Red Orchestra suppressing-fire mechanic* (lines 4992–5019) — mechanic-as-designed-affect (graphical distortion + audio + screen-blur + destructible-cover dust+fragments + historical inaccuracy → "heightened sense of urgency, if not panic"). Anchor for Aristotelian *pathē*-as-rhetorical-strategy.
- *F.E.A.R.* flanking AI + light-bulb/scampering-child sequence (lines 5053–5085) — adaptive-AI alertness generator + horror amplifier; cross-dimensional amplification anchor.
- *Left 4 Dead 2* director + Hunter cooperative-panic (lines 5086–5123) — dynamic-affect-modulation; Calleja's strongest single statement of the dual-sourcing thesis ("rhetorical strategies of affect tightly combine the aesthetic and systemic dimensions", line 5119).

**Ambiguous-case decision rules (R5):**
- **R5 (AFF vs any other dimension)** — AFFECTIVE always co-occurs with another dimension (trigger-dimension). Code AFFECTIVE as primary when the player's report foregrounds the emotional response itself rather than the triggering content ("the music made me cry" = AFF primary; "the boss fight was hard" = LUD + KIN primary, AFF secondary).
- *Atmospherics-vs-rules adjudication (tension T-008)* — both are *pathē*-strategies; do not adjudicate primacy without specific case-evidence.

**Dissertation Layer-A ontological target:** ***Pathē*** (Aristotle *Rhetoric* II) + Gross 2017 *Uncomfortable Situations* (cross-pipeline bridge) + Uexküll *Merktöne/Wirktöne*. Aristotelian *pathē* supply the systematic taxonomy Calleja's affective dimension lacks; Gross's situated-emotion theory adjudicates the atmospherics-vs-rules disjunct by treating both as a single affective situation.

**Layer-B Burke-pentad mapping:** **ATTITUDE** (Burke's later sixth term). Attitude captures the emotional-dispositional coloring of action; AFFECTIVE is precisely the response-state dimension. *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*

**Layer-C Uexküllian-construct mapping:** ***Merktöne / Wirktöne*** (perception-tones / effect-tones). Qualitative coloring of perceived objects (their tone) is Uexküll's nearest construct to affective response; multi-valenced affective range (calming / adrenaline / uncanny) maps onto differential tone-coloring. *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*

**Residual-phenomenology anchor sites:** IG-08 phase 2 did not extract a dedicated residual-phenomenology array; anchor sites distributed throughout the chapter (esp. interview-vernacular at lines 4867–5027). Strongest anchor: Inauro/Oriel/Aprile habitable-secondary-world vocabulary (no Tolkien-as-phenomenologist; no Heidegger; no Merleau-Ponty).

### A.6 LUDIC

**Operational definition (49 words, book p.44):** Engagement with choices and their repercussions, where choices may be directed toward game-stipulated, player-stipulated, or community-stipulated goals, may be made on the spur of the moment without overarching goal, and where opportunity cost is constitutive of well-balanced game systems — without repercussions, actions lose their meaning.

**Boundary criteria (5):**
1. *Object of engagement is choice + repercussion + opportunity cost* — distinguishes from KINESTHETIC (control-execution) and AFFECTIVE (emotional state).
2. *Goal-source-agnostic* — game-stipulated, player-stipulated, community-stipulated all count; spur-of-the-moment-without-overarching-goal also counts.
3. *Repercussions-meaningfulness criterion* — without repercussions, actions lose meaning; choice-without-consequence is not ludic.
4. *Macro-phase signature: winning-strategy formation, character-development planning, opportunity-cost rehearsal between sessions* — distinguishes from narrative-macro (content-anticipation).
5. *Ergodic-disposition overlap with KINESTHETIC under strategy-game cases* — apply R1.

**Macro signature:** Winning-strategy formation; character-development planning; opportunity-cost rehearsal between sessions; (FIFA 09 Ultimate Team Italian-team build).
**Micro signature:** In-session choice + repercussion + opportunity-cost weighting; strategy/tactics/microtactics three-tier hierarchy (C-033).

**Paradigm worked examples (8 — from IG-09):**
- *Grand Theft Auto IV* — volume's premier ludic case study; system-validated + player-defined + community-negotiated goals across full rewards-typology (spatial-area-unlock; scripted-narrative; aesthetic-beauty).
- *Call of Duty IV (capture-the-flag)* — ergodic-vs-non-ergodic semiosis exhibit; flag-meaning is rule-system-assigned not iconographic. Strongest Uexküll *Wirkmal* candidate in the volume.
- *Fallout 3* — primary-goal-with-cascading-subgoals paradigm (find-fugitive-father → radio-station → equipment → town → currency → job); alternative-primary-goal exhibit (murder-everyone via corpse-persistence rule).
- *World of Warcraft (Ananke's Darkshore swim)* — signature self-set-goal exhibit; no system reward; satisfaction = sense-of-agency + spatial exploration + journey-story contributed to alterbiography. Sole occurrence of "agency" in Ch.9.
- *FIFA 09 Ultimate Team* — most elaborated single design-economy analysis: strategy (build all-Italian team and win online cup) + cascading subgoals (contract-time + money + staff + morale) + tactics + microtactic exemplar (right-winger faced by defender → pass/dribble/cross).

## Section B — Incorporation Rubric (5 components, from IG-10)

The 5-component rubric (per IG-10 Phase 2 normalization of the 8 candidate components surfaced in Phase 1):

### R1 — Assimilation axis

**Component:** Virtual environment incorporated INTO player consciousness.
**Page anchor:** book p.169 lines 5934–5944.
**Maps to dimensions:** SPATIAL (primary), NARRATIVE (secondary), AFFECTIVE (secondary).
**Maps to temporal phase:** primarily micro-phase, with macro-phase residue (cognitive maps + internalized lore persist between sessions).
**Differentiator from merely-involved:** merely-involved play allows attentional engagement with the environment-as-object; assimilation-axis incorporation absorbs the environment into the structure of consciousness itself, organized per existing experiential gestalts (Lakoff+Johnson 2003).

### R2 — Embodiment axis

**Component:** Player incorporated INTO virtual environment via avatar, single systemically-upheld location.
**Page anchor:** p.169 lines 5938–5944.
**Maps to dimensions:** KINESTHETIC (primary), SPATIAL (primary), SHARED (secondary — when other agents validate location).
**Maps to temporal phase:** exclusively micro-phase (avatar is always in one location at one time, in-session only).
**Differentiator from merely-involved:** non-avatar games (Tetris, abstract puzzles) where the player has no systemically-upheld location are excluded. Embodiment-axis incorporation requires precisely that systemic-uphold of single-location avatar presence.

### R3 — Simultaneity condition

**Component:** Both axes (R1 + R2) active concurrently.
**Page anchor:** p.169 lines 5944–5948.
**Maps to dimensions:** constitutive across all six (both axes must operate simultaneously for any dimensional blending to count as incorporation).
**Maps to temporal phase:** micro-phase only (macro cannot meet simultaneity — player not currently embodied).
**Differentiator from merely-involved:** the strictest gatekeeper. Planning a raid off-line = assimilation only; mechanically navigating a tutorial without internalizing environment = embodiment only. Only the concurrent obtaining of both yields incorporation. This is what Calleja's "sustainment-but-fleeting" property tracks.

### R4 — Spatial+kinesthetic cornerstone

**Component:** Necessary minimum-pair: SPATIAL + KINESTHETIC must both be internalized.
**Page anchor:** p.170 lines 5972–5985.
**Maps to dimensions:** KINESTHETIC (necessary), SPATIAL (necessary).
**Maps to temporal phase:** both phases (macro spatial = cognitive maps; micro spatial = present-tense navigation; macro kinesthetic = motor-habit; micro kinesthetic = control-flow).
**Differentiator from merely-involved:** an involved player who cannot move (new-player CS:S double-door fumbling) cannot enter incorporation regardless of other-dimensional engagement. Spatial+kinesthetic must be internalized to the point that they do not demand conscious attention. *This is what makes Tetris-style absorption ineligible for incorporation: no habitable space, no avatar-located embodiment.*

### R5 — Blending of involvement dimensions

**Component:** Multiple dimensions blended; intensification beyond baseline involvement.
**Page anchor:** p.169–170 lines 5961–5972 + p.178 lines 6270–6273.
**Maps to dimensions:** all six — minimum cornerstone-pair plus ≥1 other; typically 3–4 total (Rheric vignette: spatial+kinesthetic+ludic+narrative+affective; Kumacho vignette: spatial+kinesthetic+shared+ludic+affective).
**Maps to temporal phase:** culmination of macro→micro temporal arc (p.178 lines 6270–6273): incorporation is "the blending of involvement dimensions" at the apex of the temporal axis from off-line involvement to conscious attention during gameplay.
**Differentiator from merely-involved:** merely-involved play is typically single-dimension or thin-blend (kinesthetic-heavy in twitch games; spatial-and-narrative in walking simulators). Incorporation requires intensified blending across multiple dimensions, occurring at the apex of the macro→micro temporal arc, such that the experience is described holistically (Calleja: "consciousness and environment were integrated fluidly", p.173 lines 6093–6094) rather than as engagement-with-features.

### Differentiator summary

**Incorporated ≠ merely-involved when ALL of:**
1. Both axes obtain simultaneously (R3).
2. Spatial+kinesthetic cornerstone is internalized (R4 — no longer demanding conscious attention).
3. Multiple dimensions blend (R5).
4. The experience reaches the temporal-axis apex (post-internalization, sustained-attention micro-phase).
5. Intrusions are integrated rather than ruptured.

Failure on any of these five reduces the state to involvement. Phenomenologically, incorporation is recognized retrospectively via holistic narration (Rheric, Kumacho), synesthesiac additions (Rheric's "breeze" testimony), and continuity-of-self testimony ("a real experience that I carry with me").

### Candidate theoretical bridge: Incorporation ↔ Burke consubstantiation/identification

**Anticipatory bridge (per Phase 0 cite-check: Burke wholly absent from volume).** Burkean *consubstantiation* (the rhetorical move by which actor and instrument / actor and scene / actor and co-actor become "of one substance") is structurally parallel to incorporation's double-axis: assimilation-axis = environment-consubstantiated-with-player; embodiment-axis = player-consubstantiated-with-avatar; SHARED-overlap = player-consubstantiated-with-co-agents.

The bridge is *theoretically rich* and *exegetically inadmissible*. The dissertation must argue the bridge against the grain of Calleja's metaphor-disclaimer (FLAG-2 / tension T-005). **Flagged anticipatory throughout: provenance = anticipatory-projection (dissertation).**

## Section C — Worked Example Walkthrough: *World of Warcraft*

This walkthrough applies the full PIM + 5-component Incorporation Rubric to *World of Warcraft* (Blizzard 2004) as the dissertation's deployable template. It draws on the dimension chapters' WoW vignettes (IG-04 through IG-09), the IG-APX profile capture, IG-03's methodology, and IG-10's Rheric/Kumacho incorporation vignettes (each grounded in WoW or Planetside play).

### C.1 Genre + topographic profile (from IG-APX)

WoW is the prototypical MMORPG: pen-and-paper RPG lineage (Dungeons & Dragons via Fine 1983 / Mackay 2001); level-gated absolute power; queued, latency-tolerant action; gear-as-identity; scripted quest economy. Azeroth is a 96-region fantasy world with two factions (Alliance, Horde) and contested zones. Region transitions are "unnaturally abrupt" (high-imageability, geographic-implausibility) yet the contrast does not break players' sense of exploratory novelty (per IG-05 pp.88–90 Azeroth-vs-Auraxis comparison). Server architecture: clone-worlds (parallel shards). The WoW topography is the chapter's premier worked example of *high imageability + low geographic-fidelity yielding strong spatial involvement*.

### C.2 Six-dimension operational mapping

**KINESTHETIC** — Avatar control via WASD + mouse-look (symbolic register on the IG-04 Figure 4.3 continuum); internalization sequence yields class-skill-rotation as motor-habit. Macro-phase: between-session rehearsal of damage-rotations, dungeon-pull patterns. Micro-phase: in-session rotation execution + situational awareness blend. Paradigm Calleja anchor: Bladerunner "Quan" / thinking-with-fingers internalization (though Bladerunner is a Planetside player, the principle generalizes). Boundary check: WoW is NOT mimetic (no body-tracking) and NOT symbiotic — it is paradigmatic *symbolic* control.

**SPATIAL** — Azeroth-as-inhabited-place. Macro-phase: Ratchet-to-Feathermoon route held in memory between sessions (IG-03 p.42 worked example); cognitive cartography sustained across sessions; landmark + wayfinding orientation. Micro-phase: in-session navigation through region-transitions; intra-zone exploration. Paradigm Calleja anchor: Rheric's testimony about Azeroth aesthetic-imageability (IG-05). Boundary check: WoW PASSES the inhabitation-criterion strongly (sense of inhabiting a place rather than perceiving a representation).

**SHARED** — All three modalities operative. Cohabitation: Ironforge city avatars, ambient social-context awareness ("whole living world", Baal). Cooperation: end-game guild content (raid-night PvE coordination), Sara Andrews governance case (server-consensus self-policing). Competition: PvP-server combat. Bridging-spillover example: Austin Texas geographic guild. Macro-phase: clan-website work, guild-political deliberation, persisting social bonds. Micro-phase: in-session raid coordination + ambient sociality. Boundary check: WoW PASSES with all three subtypes; the four-subtype-prose-vs-three-subtype-figure mismatch (T-006) is moot because all three minimum subtypes are clearly present.

**NARRATIVE** — Both scripted (lore-as-narrative-scaffold per IG-APX C-APX-04; quest-text) and alterbiographic (Muun's character-trajectory, Figure 7.2; two-interview-quote disposition contrast). The "no-main-story-line-with-side-quests-only" structure (IG-07) means alterbiography is heavier than scripted-narrative for most WoW players. Pull narrative present (in-world books, in-game lore-discovery). Macro-phase: speculation about expansion-content; cross-session emplotment of character arc. Micro-phase: in-session pull-narrative pursuit + alterbiography accretion. Boundary check: WoW PASSES — both components operative; alterbiography is the dominant carrier.

**AFFECTIVE** — Atmospherics-dominant via designed environment (Inauro on Duskwood "Mirkwood" aesthetic; Oriel on Menethil Harbor — habitable-secondary-world; Aprile on Night Elf Land — deliberate-non-play mood-regulation). Designed: lighting + sound + monster placement + thematic consistency. Player-precipitated: aesthetic-habitation desire ("I want to live there"). Macro-phase: inner peace after aesthetic travels; anticipatory excitement. Micro-phase: in-session calming/awe responses to environmental atmospherics. Boundary check: WoW PASSES — multi-valenced range present; both designer-installed and player-precipitated sources active.

**LUDIC** — All three goal-sources operative. Game-stipulated: quest objectives, raid-progression. Player-stipulated: signature *Ananke Darkshore swim* (sole "agency" occurrence in Ch.9; no system reward; satisfaction from sense-of-agency + spatial exploration + alterbiography contribution). Community-stipulated: guild-set raid goals, PvP-rating targets. Strategy/tactics/microtactics hierarchy: strategy = level-cap progression; tactics = dungeon-clearing approaches; microtactics = boss-mechanic execution. Macro-phase: character-development planning, opportunity-cost rehearsal. Micro-phase: in-session choice + repercussion weighting. Boundary check: WoW PASSES strongly — all three goal-sources represented, full strategy/tactics/microtactics hierarchy operative.

### C.3 Macro-phase + micro-phase signatures present

**Macro:** clan-website work; route-memory between sessions; aesthetic-mood-regulation; character-development planning; raid-attendance scheduling; cross-session alterbiography emplotment. *All six dimensions present in WoW macro-phase reports.*

**Micro:** in-session navigation + control execution + ambient sociality + alterbiography accretion + atmospheric-affective response + tactical decision-making. *All six dimensions present in WoW micro-phase reports.*

### C.4 Incorporation rubric check (all 5 components)

- **R1 (Assimilation axis)** — MET. Cognitive maps of Azeroth + internalized lore persist across sessions; environment is absorbed into the structure of consciousness (Rheric/Kestra testimony, IG-05).
- **R2 (Embodiment axis)** — MET. Single systemically-upheld avatar-location; in-session embodiment via WoW's third-person camera tightly avatar-anchored.
- **R3 (Simultaneity)** — MET when both R1 and R2 are concurrent (i.e., during sustained-attention play after spatial+kinesthetic internalization).
- **R4 (Spatial+kinesthetic cornerstone)** — MET for experienced players (failure mode: new-player navigation+control fumbling). WoW's class-skill-rotation reaches internalization quickly relative to e.g. Counter-Strike's twitch demands.
- **R5 (Blending)** — MET. WoW Rheric/Kumacho-class testimony shows 4–5 dimensions in simultaneous blend during raid-content + exploration; "consciousness and environment were integrated fluidly" register (p.173 lines 6093–6094) recurrent in WoW interview-data.

**Verdict:** WoW is the volume's strongest sustained-incorporation case across all six dimensions over time. This is what justifies its position as Calleja's primary methodological backbone (the only game cited in all 9 chapter-units that admit per-game citation).

- **SPATIAL → SCENE** (~80 words). Place-as-inhabited is the Burkean Scene. Spatial-internalization (C-008 virtual-environment habitation) is the process of the Agent coming-to-dwell-in the Scene. The Scene/Agent ratio in incorporation: the cornerstone-pair (R4) is the dramatistic ground on which the Agent acts; without internalized Scene, no incorporation. Calleja's anti-fidelity-determinism argument (Slater displacement) maps to Burkean *scenic dramatism* refusing setting-fidelity in favor of dramatic-functionality. *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*
- **SHARED → AGENT (with co-agent ratios)** (~80 words). Other agents populate the dramatistic field; SHARED foregrounds the Agent-Agent ratios (cohabitation = static co-presence ratio; cooperation = joint-Purpose ratio; competition = opposed-Purpose ratio). The human-vs-AI distinction (C-028) maps to Burkean *consubstantiation* — only human-controlled co-agents can be fully consubstantial with the player; AI co-agents support cohabitation but not full Agent-Agent consubstantiation. EVE Online Guiding Hand heist is the consubstantiation-rupture paradigm. *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*
- **NARRATIVE → ACT + PURPOSE** (~80 words). Story-content is the sequence of Acts toward Purposes (scripted and emergent). Push narrative = Purpose-saturated + Act-constraining; pull narrative releases Purpose to Player-Act; alterbiography (C-009) is the emergent-Act layer. The Bioshock-Steinman intersection is the paradigm Act/Purpose-ratio: pre-encounter Purpose-priming (audio-diaries) becomes Player-Act content when assimilated to alterbiography. Calleja's dissolution of the narratology-vs-ludology binary (P-014) is structurally the assertion that Act-Purpose ratios cannot be analytically separated. *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*
- **AFFECTIVE → ATTITUDE** (~80 words). Burke's later sixth pentad-term (Attitude) captures the emotional-dispositional coloring of action. AFFECTIVE is precisely the response-state dimension — the player's Attitude toward the Scene/Agency/Act/Purpose/Agent configuration. The atmospherics-vs-rules disjunct (tension T-008) maps to Attitude-via-Scene vs Attitude-via-Agency; Calleja's "rhetorical strategies of affect tightly combine the aesthetic and systemic dimensions" (L4D2 anchor, IG-08 line 5119) directly anticipates Burkean Attitude as cross-ratio modulator. *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*
- **LUDIC → PURPOSE + ACT** (~80 words). Choice-and-repercussion within goal-structures is Purpose realized through Act; opportunity cost is the dramatistic weighting of alternative Acts. The "Monster Kill" rhetorical amplification (CS:S, IG-09) is the chapter's clearest Purpose-Act-ratio anchor — the system rhetorically validates the player's motivation. The three-tier strategy/tactics/microtactics hierarchy (C-033) maps to time-scaled Purpose/Act ratios. Goal-source taxonomy (C-034: system/self/community) maps to alternative dramatistic-frame attributions. *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*

### E.2 Per-dimension Uexküllian-construct mapping (Layer C)

- **KINESTHETIC → *Funktionskreis*** (~80 words). The perception-action loop (key-press → avatar response → perceptual feedback → next key-press) IS Uexküll's functional-circle structure (*Merkmal* → *Merkorgan* → *Innenwelt* → *Wirkorgan* → *Wirkmal* → back to *Merkmal*). Internalization is the closing of the loop into automaticity. The symbolic/mimetic/symbiotic continuum maps to varying tightness of *Merkorgan*-*Wirkorgan* coupling. Calleja's "feels like an extension" Morris quote anticipates Uexküllian biosemiotic integration. (Corpus index anchor: Uexküll *Foray* §§2–5.) *Provenance: anticipatory-projection (singly-anchored).*
- **SPATIAL → *Umwelt* (+ *Merkwelt* / *Wirkwelt*)** (~80 words). The virtual environment as inhabited-place is precisely *Umwelt* — the subjectively-constituted world of meaning-carriers. *Merkwelt* (perceptual world: rendered geometry, lighting, audio) and *Wirkwelt* (effect world: walkable terrain, interactable objects) jointly compose the navigable virtual space. **This is the volume's strongest Layer-C anchor and the dissertation's signature Layer-C payoff (see §E.4).** Calleja's "habitation" trope (C-008) maps directly to Umwelt-habitation. (Corpus index anchor: Uexküll *Foray* §§6–10 on Umwelt construction.) *Provenance: anticipatory-projection (singly-anchored).*
- **SHARED → *Umwelt*-overlap (intersubjective biosemiotic field)** (~80 words). Multiple agents inhabit overlapping but non-identical Umwelten. Cohabitation = static Umwelt-overlap; cooperation = joint-*Wirkmal*-pursuit Umwelt-coordination; competition = opposed-*Wirkmal* Umwelt-conflict. The human-vs-AI distinction (C-028) maps to full-biosemiotic-subjectivity (human) vs designer-instantiated-Umwelt (AI). EVE Online Guiding Hand heist exposes how Umwelt-overlap can be exploited as a betrayal-vector. (Corpus index anchor: Uexküll *Foray* extending to social Umwelten.) *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*
- **NARRATIVE → *Bedeutungsträger* sequence within *Umwelt*** (~80 words). NPCs, quest items, audio diaries, environmental details function as successive meaning-bearers (*Bedeutungsträger*) within the player's *Umwelt*. Pull narrative maps especially well: player-pulled *Bedeutungsträger* are biosemiotically active (the player constitutes them as meaning-bearing); pushed cut scenes function as designer-imposed non-*Bedeutungsträger* interruptions of the Umwelt-flow. Subordinate construct: *Wirkmal*-loop closure when player completes a scripted-narrative checkpoint. (Corpus index anchor: Uexküll *Foray* §§11–14 on meaning-carrier sequences.) *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*
- **AFFECTIVE → *Merktöne* / *Wirktöne*** (~80 words). The qualitative coloring of perceived objects (their *tone*) is Uexküll's nearest construct to affective response. *Merktöne* (perception-tones) cover atmospherics-as-affect (Inauro on Duskwood "Mirkwood" aesthetic); *Wirktöne* (effect-tones) cover mechanic-as-affect (Red Orchestra suppressing-fire). Calleja's multi-valenced affective range (calming / adrenaline / uncanny) maps onto differential tone-coloring. The atmospherics-vs-rules disjunct (T-008) is resolved as *Merktöne*-vs-*Wirktöne* dual-sourcing within a single Funktionskreis. *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*
- **LUDIC → *Wirkmal* + goal-structure** (~80 words). Ludic engagement is the choice-weighting that determines which *Wirkmal* (effect-marker) the player pursues; goals are organized *Wirkmal*-closure conditions. The Call of Duty IV capture-the-flag exhibit (IG-09) is the volume's strongest single *Wirkmal* exemplar — the flag's meaning is rule-system-assigned (locus of competitive struggle) not iconographic (national patriotism), exactly mirroring Uexküll's distinction between biosemiotic-meaning and conventional-meaning. (Corpus index anchor: Uexküll *Foray* §§11–14.) *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*

### E.3 Per-dimension Aristotelian/Heideggerian grounding-target (Layer A)

- **KINESTHETIC → *aisthesis-kinesis* (Bowin AMT) + *dunamis*/*hexis*** (~80 words). Readiness-to-act is *dunamis-as-hexis* (Aristotelian potency as settled disposition); learned-internalized control is the actualization (*energeia*) of *hexis*. The continuity-of-motion analysis (Sunniva's "continuous motion"; Mirror's Edge as "continuity of movement") is the paradigm Bowin AMT bridge target — Bowin's *aisthesis-kinesis* treatment supplies the ontological vocabulary for what Calleja describes phenomenologically without theorizing. Waiting-sniper passage anticipates *kairos* (Heidegger BCAP). (Corpus index anchors: Bowin AMT §§1–4; Heidegger BCAP on *kairos*.) *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*
- **SPATIAL → spatial *phantasia* (Phantasia Cluster — Hawhee / Papachristou)** (~80 words). Internalization of game-space is the maintenance of spatial *phantasmata* in memory between sessions; macro-phase route-planning IS deliberative *phantasia* (Papachristou). Secondary: Heideggerian *Wohnen* / In-der-Welt-sein supplies the dwelling-in-the-world register Calleja invokes via Tuan without phenomenological grounding. (Corpus index anchors: Hawhee on rhetorical vision; Papachristou on deliberative phantasia; Heidegger BCAP §§ on *Wohnen*.) *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*
- **SHARED → *Mitsein* (Heidegger BCAP / SZ §§25–27) + ethical-political *phronesis*** (~80 words). Being-with as ontological mode of co-inhabitation supplies the existential-analytic register Calleja's "social-context awareness" lacks. *Das Man* (SZ §27) for server-consensus self-policing (Sara Andrews case). *Phronesis* in its ethical-political register for guild-political deliberation. EVE Online Guiding Hand heist as Mitsein-rupture paradigm. (Corpus index anchors: Heidegger BCAP / SZ §§25–27.) *Provenance: anticipatory-projection.*
- **NARRATIVE → *muthos* / emplotment (Aristotelian *Poetics*)** (~80 words). Alterbiography is functionally Aristotelian *muthos* in everything but vocabulary — synthesis of incidents (*kineseis*) into a unified action with beginning/middle/end, now distributed across game sessions. The tabletop-RPG worked example (IG-07 pp.129–130) presents muthos in miniature. Secondary: Heideggerian BCAP on kairotic event-structuring parallels macro-phase off-line anticipation + micro-phase moment-by-moment alterbiography generation. **This dimension also carries the mediated-exegetical Sartre/Iser bridge — see §E.5.** (Corpus index anchors: Aristotle *Poetics* ch.6–10.) *Provenance: anticipatory-projection (with mediated-exegetical Sartre/Iser exception at §E.5).*

Red Orchestra suppressing-fire mechanic-as-designed-affect is the paradigm Aristotelian-*pathē*-as-rhetorical-strategy anchor. Choice-and-repercussion under opportunity cost is the *phronetic* register; *prohairesis* (deliberative choice) operates at the strategy/tactics/microtactics hierarchy levels; deliberative *phantasia* supplies the imagistic substrate of choice-rehearsal. ### E.5 The signature Layer-C payoff: *Umwelt* ↔ virtual-environment habitation

**Argument:** Uexküll's *Umwelt* is arguably more apt than Heideggerian *In-der-Welt-sein* for the bounded, designer-constituted nature of virtual environments. The dissertation's signature Layer-C move.

**Independent triangulating findings:**
1. **IG-00 habitation-trope** — Calleja's volume opens (book pp.1–5) with the autobiographical Gzira vignette grounding "habitation" as the master trope; throughout, the trope serves as the metaphorical anchor of the incorporation thesis (C-008 virtual-environment habitation, Tier-1 across IG-00/02/05/10/11).
2. **IG-05 6-Umwelt-edges** — IG-05 Spatial Phase 2 records 6 anticipatory-projection edges from spatial concepts to *Umwelt* constructs (the chapter's densest Layer-C concentration).
3. **IG-10 Manual §E warrant** — IG-10 Phase 2 explicitly maps R4 (spatial+kinesthetic cornerstone) to Uexküll Funktionskreis + Umwelt as the dimension-pair on which incorporation rests.

**Why *Umwelt* fits better than *Welt*:** Heideggerian *Welt* is the holistic-totality of significance in which *Dasein* finds itself thrown — it is *unbounded* (open-ended significance-totality of a culture or historical-epoch) and *un-designed* (constituted through *Dasein*'s thrown engagement, not through prior intentional shaping). Virtual game-environments are bounded (96 regions in Azeroth; finite zone-count), designer-constituted (Blizzard team-of-N designed every region), ruleful (mechanics enforce edges), and finite (no infinite-recession of horizons; running into the invisible-wall is a feature). Uexküll's *Umwelt* — the species-specific subjective world constituted from a closed-set of relevant *Bedeutungsträger* and biologically-bounded perceptual/effectual channels — *is* bounded, *is* objectively-pre-given-to-the-subject (analogous to designer-constitution), and *is* species-specific in its scope (analogous to game-genre-specific affordance-set).

The dissertation's argument: Calleja's habitation-trope is correctly identified by him as the master figure (P-012), but its proper ontological-ground is *Umwelt*, not *Welt*. This argument operates *with* Calleja (the habitation-trope is correctly central) and *beyond* Calleja (the ontological-ground he gestures at via Tuan/Lakoff+Johnson/Damasio is supplied by Uexküll, not by his cited resources).

### E.6 Sartre/Iser → Phantasia Cluster mediated-exegetical bridge (the ONE exception)

**The volume's only mediated-exegetical Layer-A bridge.** Three anchor sites in IG-07 Narrative (book pp.127–130, Synthesis subsection):

1. **p.127** — Calleja quotes Iser 1991 directly on reader-synthesis: text is neither expectation nor memory; the reader must put together what wandering viewpoint has divided up; "syntheses of dual nature, projections from reader plus textual signals."
2. **p.128** — Calleja quotes Sartre 1967 *What Is Literature?* directly on author/reader interdependence: "the combined efforts of author and reader bring into being the concrete and imaginary object which is the work of the mind. Art exists only for and through other people."
3. **p.130** — Calleja invokes Sartre 1995 *The Imaginary* on synthesizing efforts becoming apparent when representational qualities are ambiguous.

**Why this is mediated-exegetical and not anticipatory:** the citations are DIRECT in Calleja's text (unlike Gibson/Uexküll/Aristotle/Burke, which are absent). Sartre's phenomenology of imagination is a recognized upstream resource for the Phantasia Cluster (Hawhee on rhetorical vision; Papachristou on deliberative *phantasia*). The dissertation's IG-07 → Phantasia Cluster bridge therefore has exegetical-anchor in Calleja's own text via Sartre/Iser, then mediated to the Phantasia Cluster via the classical-phenomenology-of-imagination → rhetorical-phantasia lineage. **This is the only non-anticipatory leverage point in the entire Calleja apparatus** and structurally parallels (in legitimacy-form, not content) the deprecated Gibson→Uexküll mediated chain — except this one survives the cite-check.

**Dissertation deployment:** the Sartre/Iser → Phantasia Cluster bridge supplies upstream resources for the NARRATIVE dimension's ontological-grounding target (*muthos*/emplotment); the bridge is *narrative-dimension-specific* and does not generalize to other dimensions. Provenance: `mediated-exegetical (Sartre/Iser → Phantasia Cluster)`.

### E.7 Incorporation ↔ consubstantiation theoretical move: leverage + risk

**Leverage:** Burkean *consubstantiation* (the rhetorical move by which actor and instrument / actor and scene / actor and co-actor become "of one substance") is structurally parallel to incorporation's double-axis. Assimilation-axis = environment-consubstantiated-with-player; embodiment-axis = player-consubstantiated-with-avatar; SHARED-overlap = player-consubstantiated-with-co-agents. The bridge is theoretically rich.

**Risk:** the bridge is exegetically inadmissible (Burke is wholly absent from Calleja). It must be argued *against the grain* of Calleja's metaphor-disclaimer (FLAG-2 / T-005). The dissertation must explicitly mark the argument as additive and creative, not as exegetical recovery.

### E.8 Where each layer ADDS resolution PIM lacks

- **Layer A (Aristotle/Heidegger)** adds: time-phenomenological grain (macro/micro asymmetry, T-004, resolved via kairos + ekstatic-temporality); choice-and-readiness ontology (resolves R1 ambiguity via *dunamis*/*prohairesis* distinction); narrative emplotment (resolves T-011 via *muthos*); affective taxonomy (resolves T-008 + T-009 via *pathē* + Gross).
- **Layer B (Burke pentad)** adds: cross-dimensional ratio analysis (PIM treats dimensions as independent axes; pentad ratios are inherently multi-dimensional); the Attitude sixth-term supplies AFFECTIVE-cross-modulation register; consubstantiation supplies incorporation-as-rhetorical-figure.
- **Layer C (Uexküll)** adds: bounded-environment ontology (Calleja's habitation-trope grounded in Umwelt, see §E.5); biosemiotic meaning-carrier sequence (alterbiography-as-*Bedeutungsträger*-stream); effect-marker semantics (Wirkmal disambiguates ludic-goal-meaning from iconographic-meaning, resolving CoD IV flag-case).

### E.9 Where each layer CONFLICTS with PIM or with other layers

- **Burke ↔ Calleja**: Burke is *dramatist-motivational* (action explained by motives located in pentad-ratios) while Calleja is *phenomenological-experiential* (action explained by player-reported lived states). The augmentation must acknowledge Burke's motive-attribution as a theoretical supplement, not as displacement of Calleja's experiential primacy.
- **Uexküll ↔ Calleja**: Uexküll is *biosemiotic-objectivist* (Umwelten are species-specific structures constituted prior to subjective interpretation) while Calleja is *subjectivist-qualitative* (involvement-dimensions emerge from interview-coded reports). The augmentation must hold Uexküll's pre-given-structure claim in productive tension with Calleja's grounded-emergence methodology.
- **Layer A ↔ Calleja's metaphor-disclaimer (T-005, FLAG-2)**: Calleja explicitly disclaims literal-ontological reading of incorporation ("ultimately a metaphor like presence or immersion"). Layer A grounding (BCAP / Aristotelian *muthos* / *pathē*) asserts ontological-substance for what Calleja treats as metaphorical-figure. The augmentation must be argued *against the grain* of the disclaimer.
- **Layer B ↔ Layer A**: Burke's pentad is rhetorical-instrumental (an analytic device for motive-attribution) while Aristotelian/Heideggerian grounding is fundamental-ontological. The two layers operate at different levels; the dissertation must specify whether Burke is being used as fundamental-ontology-in-disguise or as rhetorical-instrument-only.
- **Layer C ↔ Layer A**: Uexküll Umwelt is bounded-species-environment; Heideggerian Welt is unbounded-significance-totality. §E.5 argues the dissertation should *prefer* Umwelt over Welt for virtual environments — but this means Layer C displaces part of Layer A for the SPATIAL dimension. The dissertation must articulate the precedence rule.

### E.10 The metaphor-disclaimer constraint (FLAG-2 / T-005)

Calleja explicitly disclaims literal-ontological reading of incorporation: "incorporation is ultimately a metaphor like presence or immersion" (book p.169, paraphrased) while simultaneously claiming it provides "a more robust concept" (p.170+ paraphrased). Robustness must therefore live in the metaphor's *tracking of the multi-dimensional phenomenon*, not in literal ontological gain. **This is a constraint on Layer-A grounding, not an obstacle.** The dissertation's Layer-A move must be framed as: "Calleja's metaphor tracks the phenomenon better than competing metaphors (presence, immersion); the metaphor's tracking-better is explained by the underlying phenomenology Calleja gestures at but does not theorize; the dissertation's Layer-A makes that phenomenology explicit." This is augmentation, not exegetical correction.
