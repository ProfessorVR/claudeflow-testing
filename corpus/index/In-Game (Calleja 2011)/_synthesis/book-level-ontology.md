## 1. The 6×2 canonical spine

Calleja's volume is built on a single load-bearing apparatus: a six-dimensional Player Involvement Model (PIM) cross-cut by a two-phase temporal distinction. This 6×2 grid (six dimensions × {macro, micro}) is the volume's organizing geometry — it determines chapter architecture (Ch.3 frames the grid, Chs.4–9 fill in the six dimensions, each opening with a macro section before pivoting to micro), it determines the analytic vocabulary every subsequent chapter and the appendix deploy, and it determines the conditions of possibility for the volume's titular synthesis: incorporation.

The six dimensions (D-001 through D-006: kinesthetic, spatial, shared, narrative, affective, ludic) are not a partition. Calleja's strongest meta-claim about them is that they are **layered and transparent** (P-006): each dimension changes the perception of the others without occluding them, and the inclusion or exclusion of any one alters how the others operate. This means the apparatus is structurally taxonomic but experientially gradient — a tension that runs through the volume and is flagged for Phase 3B as FLAG-1 (anti-essentialism vs. taxonomy) and FLAG-5 (diagrammatic vs. phenomenological registers).

The two temporal phases (T-001 macro / T-002 micro) bear unequal load. Micro is the volume's analytic center of gravity — the in-session, *in ludo res* moment where ergodic-dispositional engagement operates and where the dimensions are immediately readable. Macro is the off-line involvement that contextualizes micro: pre-session and post-session thinking, planning, anticipation, community participation, motivation to return. As FLAG-4 records, macro is consistently under-developed across the dimension chapters relative to micro.

The 6×2 grid is the substrate for everything else in the book.

## 2. Tier-1 concepts — the volume pillars

Twenty-three concept-nodes carry Tier-1 status. They cluster into five functional families:

**(a) The frame apparatus and its prerequisites.** C-001 PIM itself; C-002 involvement (the umbrella analytic / spine term Calleja prefers over "immersion" or "presence" throughout); C-006 the three-tier attentional hierarchy (attention → involvement → presence/immersion) that licenses why involvement must be modeled before presence. C-004 macro phase and C-005 micro phase (in ludo res) are first-class TEMPORAL-PHASE nodes since they pervade Chs.3–9 as first-order analytic categories.

**(b) The synthesis term and its gates.** C-003 incorporation — the titular replacement frame for immersion and presence — sits at the apex of the ontology. It is gated by C-013 the spatial+kinesthetic cornerstone (necessary minimum substrate; kinesthetic→spatial dependency: you must move to inhabit), structured by C-014 the double-axis / bidirectional metaphor (player absorbs environment AND avatar is absorbed into environment — replacing the unidirectional plunge of immersion), and constrained by C-015 the simultaneity condition (dimensions must operate at sustained intensity simultaneously; incorporation is sustainable-but-fleeting). C-008 virtual environment habitation is the master metaphor that survives the demolition of presence + immersion — the operative trope across IG-00, IG-05, IG-10, IG-11.

**(c) The methodological signature.** C-007 family-resemblance methodology (Wittgensteinian) is iteratively applied at three levels: at IG-01 to game-definition (refusing Juul's and Salen+Zimmerman's essentialisms); at IG-03 to PIM dimensions (as overlapping/non-essential axes); at IG-10 to incorporation (as family-resemblance of experiential modes). Family-resemblance is not a local move — it is the volume's methodological signature, a finding propagated cleanly across IG-01/03/10 by the Phase 2 agents. C-038 the grounded-theoretic qualitative methodology (three years' participation in WoW + Planetside, 25 in-game semi-structured interviews, iterative coding generating dimensions as clusters of emphasis) is the empirical engine that produces and constrains the apparatus, and the seed for the dissertation's empirical-adaptation chapter.

**(d) The dispositional turn.** C-010 ergodicity-as-dispositional-readiness is Calleja's reformulation of Aarseth (a strong theoretical move that re-grounds ergodic effort in cognitive/dispositional readiness rather than physical input). C-011 internalization is the universal mechanism by which learned dimensional engagement becomes automatic and frees attentional capacity for other dimensions — applied paradigmatically to kinesthetic control-learning but operative for spatial place-internalization (IG-05) and shared-involvement automaticity (IG-06) too. These two concepts jointly explain how the dimensional architecture works *as a temporal-experiential process*, not just a taxonomy.

**(e) The lit-review diagnosis.** C-018 the four conceptual challenges of presence/immersion (absorption-vs-transportation conflation; ergodic-vs-non-ergodic difference; technological/fidelity determinism; monolithic perspective) is the diagnostic enumeration IG-02 supplies and IG-10 resolves. C-017 subjective distance (player ↔ game environment) is the experiential variable the book theorizes — its shortening yields the sensation of inhabiting on-screen space and proto-formulates what IG-10 articulates as bidirectional incorporation.

**(f) Three excisions.** Tier-1 concept-positions C-035 magic-circle excision, C-029 scripted-narrative vs. alterbiography (which dissolves the narratology-vs-ludology binary), and (Tier-2) C-036 lusory-attitude excision and C-037 fun-essentialism excision constitute the volume's negative-purgative work. The magic-circle excision is Tier-1 because it ramifies across IG-03/06/10; the others are Tier-2 because their force is concentrated in IG-03's negative-definitional section (pp.46–53). C-009 alterbiography is Tier-1 because the concept does load-bearing work in IG-07 and returns substantively in IG-09 (ludic cross-weaving) and IG-10 (incorporation testimony).

## 3. Tier-2 concepts — chapter-central

Thirteen concept-nodes carry Tier-2 status. They are load-bearing within one chapter and ramify into two or three others.

**Chapter-typology innovations.** C-020 symbolic / mimetic / symbiotic control continuum (IG-04, Figure 4.3) is the kinesthetic chapter's primary typology. C-023 the five-structure spatial taxonomy (unicursal corridor, multicursal corridor, rhizomatic zone, open landscape, arena — augmented by miniature + tactical spaces) is the spatial chapter's typology, displacing Nitsche's labyrinth/tracks-and-rails distinction. C-033 the strategy/tactics/microtactics three-tier hierarchy is the ludic chapter's analytic innovation, tracking goal-tier and temporal-distance. These are the three chapter-typology peaks.

**Domain-imports that ramify.** C-021 agency (Murray/Schott vs. Giddens/capability vs. Mateas+Stern local/global) is the book's primary agency theorization, concentrated in IG-04 but recurring in IG-09. C-022 contingency (Malaby — performative + social) operates across IG-04, IG-06, IG-09. C-031 excitatory homeostasis + rhetorical strategies of affect (Bryant+Davies via IG-08) and C-032 escapism vs. escape (Tolkien via IG-08) are the affective chapter's load-bearing imports.

**Cognitive-spatial substrate.** C-024 cognitive mapping / mental cartography (Tolman + Lynch imageability + Golledge+Garling) and C-025 the Tuan space → place transition jointly furnish the cognitive substrate of spatial involvement.

**Social-theoretic frame.** C-026 MMOGs as third places / on-line societies (Oldenburg via Steinkuehler + Williams) and C-027 the cohabitation/cooperation/competition three-not-four shared-subtypes carry the social-theoretic load of IG-06.

**Narrative-theoretic moves.** C-030 push narrative vs. pull narrative is the analytic distinction that operationalizes the scripted/alterbiography co-constitution.

**Synthesis support.** C-012 cybernetic circuit / cybernetic feedback loop (Dovey+Kennedy) returns from IG-03 into IG-08 (where it becomes a cognitive + emotional + kinesthetic loop) and IG-10/11 (where it grounds the player↔machine inhabitation claim). C-016 experiential gestalts (Lakoff+Johnson) is IG-10's specific philosophical anchor for incorporation. C-019 avatar anchoring + system reactivity is the structural differentia between game-environment presence and other-media presence — concentrated in IG-02 but operative in IG-04 and IG-05. C-034 the goal-source taxonomy (system / self / community) is IG-09's classification structure.

C-036 lusory-attitude excision and C-037 fun-essentialism excision close out the Tier-2 set.

## 4. Tier-3 concepts — supporting

Only two concept-nodes carry Tier-3 status after aggressive dedup:

- **C-028 human-vs-AI co-agent distinction** (IG-06) — load-bearing within IG-06's social-theoretic argument (player disposition is fundamentally restructured by perceived human-vs-AI status; even rudimentary AI grounds cohabitation, but only persistent human-controlled agents support the reputation-and-betrayal layer, with the EVE Online Guiding Hand Social Club heist as the betrayal exemplar) but does not ramify materially into other chapters.

(Many candidate Tier-3 concepts from the 13 unit JSONs were either merged upward into Tier-2 nodes — e.g. IG-07's environmental-storytelling, narrative-affordances, push/pull and Ryan's narrative-as-cognitive-construct collapsed into C-029/C-030 — or judged below the volume-relevance threshold and not lifted to canonical status. The book-level ontology should not be a flat reproduction of unit concept-lists; it must compress aggressively to expose the load-bearing structure. Where in doubt, single-chapter concepts were dropped rather than promoted.)

A second Tier-3 node — **the three-tier attentional substrate** (Fan et al. 2002; Baddeley + Hitch 1994; Sturm + Willmes 2001) imported in IG-03 to ground internalization — is absorbed into C-006 the three-tier attentional hierarchy rather than carried as a separate node. The two Tier-3 entries thus stand for the principled minimal supporting-concept stratum.

## 5. The six involvement dimensions as first-class nodes

Each of the six dimensions is encoded as a first-class INVOLVEMENT-DIMENSION node (D-001 through D-006) rather than a generic CONCEPT, because the dimensions function differently from concepts in the volume's analytic apparatus: they are the *axes* along which involvement is decomposed, they carry chapter-level operational definitions, macro and micro signatures, and primary typologies, and they are the units the dissertation's PIM Operationalization Manual treats as load-bearing analytic instruments. Each dimension node carries its operational definition from IG-03 (Calleja's volume-canonical formulations), its macro and micro signatures, and a pointer to its chapter's primary typology (e.g. D-001 → C-020 symbolic/mimetic/symbiotic; D-002 → C-023 five-structure; D-006 → C-033 strategy/tactics/microtactics).

The macro and micro temporal phases are encoded as first-class TEMPORAL-PHASE nodes (T-001 / T-002) referencing the corresponding CONCEPT entries (C-004 / C-005). This dual encoding lets edges land either on the abstract phase-construct or on its conceptual content as needed in Phase 3B's global-edges work.

## 6. Position consolidation

Eighteen canonical POSITION entries were synthesized from ~40 unit-level position claims. The five tier-1 frame-setting positions (P-001 incorporation displaces immersion+presence; P-002 blending culminating in incorporation; P-003 digital-game involvement needs dedicated apparatus; P-004 involvement-prior-to-presence/immersion; P-005 the macro/micro temporal structure) carry the volume's foundational program. P-006 (dimensions layered and transparent) and P-007 (ergodicity as disposition) carry the structural-experiential principles. P-008/P-009 are the magic-circle and fun excisions; P-010/P-011 are the family-resemblance and unit-of-analysis methodological positions; P-012/P-013 articulate incorporation's specific commitments (habitation-not-plunge + spatial+kinesthetic cornerstone + continuity-with-everyday + subjective-disposition requirement); P-014 is the narratology-ludology dissolution; P-017 the disciplinary-political offer of the PIM as common framework.

Three Tier-2 positions carry chapter-internal load: P-015 (escapism legitimate + affective dimension under-developed — Calleja's acknowledged self-limitation); P-016 (rules constrain AND enable meaning; without repercussions, actions lose meaning); P-018 (WoW + Planetside as prototypical case-sites and their binary-comparative scaffold as method). The lusory-attitude excision is encoded as concept C-036 rather than a separate position, since the dedup pass found it functioning analytically more as a methodological-conceptual move than as a thesis-against-an-interlocutor.

## 7. Consolidation moves — what got merged

The most consequential dedup operations:

- **"Involvement" cluster** (C-002): seven synonyms collapsed (involvement / player engagement / engagement / involvement-as-umbrella / IG-02's involvement-prerequisite / IG-03's C-INV / IG-00's involvement-as-umbrella). This is the volume's most-used term and the most-recurrent dedup target.
- **"Habitation" cluster** (C-008): eight synonyms collapsed (habitation / virtual-environment habitation / metaphorically-habitable-environment / inhabited space / place-Tuan / IG-00's virtual-environment-habitation / IG-02's metaphorically habitable environment / IG-05's master metaphor / IG-10's C05 / IG-10's metaphor-of-habitation-grounded-in-everyday-life). Habitation appears under different names across five different chapters; consolidating it surfaces its load-bearing status.
- **"Ergodicity-as-disposition"** (C-010): seven synonyms collapsed across IG-01/02/03/04/09. This is the conceptual reformulation that converts Aarseth's input-centric ergodicity into a dispositional readiness — the volume's single biggest theoretical pickup-and-extend move.
- **"Internalization"** (C-011): six synonyms across IG-03/04/05/06. Calleja applies internalization to every dimension; consolidating it surfaces it as a universal mechanism rather than a kinesthetic peculiarity.
- **"Incorporation"** (C-003): six synonyms across IG-00/10. The volume's titular term carries multiple sub-namings (incorporation-preview, incorporation-as-immersion-as-transportation-expanded, incorporation-vs-merely-involved). All collapsed; the three structural gates (cornerstone C-013, double-axis C-014, simultaneity C-015) carry their own canonical IDs as the rubric components.
- **"Alterbiography"** sub-cluster (C-009): IG-07's alterbiography-of-self / -entity / -miniature variants merged with the umbrella alterbiography concept; the focalization sub-distinction (Genette) is recorded in the JSON synonyms_merged list rather than promoted to its own node.
- **Three excisions** (C-035/036/037): each excision is encoded as a single POSITION-AS-CONCEPT node (a hybrid type) — Calleja treats them as both methodological exclusions and analytic positions; encoding them dually would inflate the count without clarifying structure.
- **Family-resemblance + refusal-of-essentialism** (C-007): IG-01's C03-FAMILY-RESEMBLANCE and C17-REFUSAL-OF-ESSENTIALISM, IG-03's C-WITT, and IG-10's family-resemblance-of-experiential-modes all collapsed. The fragmentation across units was the strongest dedup signal — same concept, three different appearances, one canonical node.

## 8. Tensions surfaced during dedup — flagged for 3B

Eight tensions were flagged in the JSON's `notable_tensions_flagged_for_3B` block (FLAG-1 through FLAG-8). The most consequential for Phase 3B's global-edges + tension-edges work:

- **FLAG-1** anti-essentialist methodology vs. taxonomic apparatus — the deepest internal tension; partly discharged by the layered-transparent principle but never fully resolved.
- **FLAG-2** metaphor-disclaimer vs. analytic-superiority claim about incorporation — bites any project that wants to ground incorporation ontologically (e.g. the dissertation's Layer A): Calleja explicitly disclaims that incorporation gets closer to the thing itself, so any ontological grounding must argue against the grain of Calleja's metalinguistic stance.
- **FLAG-3** bottom-up emergence vs. exhaustive-taxonomy presentation — two MMOG case-sites is a thin empirical base for a claim to exhaust player experience.
- **FLAG-4** macro vs. micro developmental asymmetry — visible across IG-03 through IG-09; macro is the under-elaborated phase.
- **FLAG-5** diagrammatic-structural vs. experiential-phenomenological registers of the PIM — the wagon-wheel diagram and the gradient-continuous phenomenology pull against each other.
- **FLAG-6** generic 'affordances' usage without Gibson attribution — the Phase 0 critical finding that falsifies the Gibson-as-implicit-Uexküll-bridge hypothesis; consequential for the Manual's Layer C framing.
- **FLAG-7** three-not-four shared-modalities slip — internal-text inconsistency in IG-06 worth auditing.
- **FLAG-8** affective chapter's substantive under-development — acknowledged by Calleja himself; relevant to the dissertation's likely Layer A + Gross 2017 bridge work on affective involvement.

## 9. What this ontology is *for*

This ontology will be consumed by:

- **3B (global edges + tensions)**: canonical_ids supply the edge endpoints; tier assignments inform edge-weighting; the eight flagged tensions seed 3B's tension-edges work directly.
- **3C (concept matrix + contested nodes)**: the 38 canonical concepts × 13 units constitute the matrix's row-column structure; tier assignment informs which concepts qualify as "contested" (FLAG-2 around incorporation; the agency cluster in C-021; the scripted/alterbiography co-constitution in C-029).
- **3D (PIM Operationalization Manual + games concordance + immersion debate map)**: the six INVOLVEMENT-DIMENSION nodes directly seed Manual Section A; C-013/C-014/C-015 (cornerstone + double-axis + simultaneity) seed Manual Section B (incorporation rubric); C-018 (the four challenges) seeds the immersion-vs-incorporation debate map.
- **3E (bibliography / interlocutor / terminology)**: synonyms_merged lists feed the terminology appendix; the interlocutor associations embedded in the concept definitions feed the interlocutor map.
- **Wave 2 (Mermaid graphs)**: the Tier-1 concepts populate the global graph (≤30 nodes); the six dimensions populate the involvement-model graph; macro/micro temporal arc visualization uses T-001/T-002 directly.

The book-level ontology is the analytic instrument the rest of the synthesis pipeline operates on.

- **definition**: IG-06's central social-theoretic frame, importing Oldenburg's third-place concept via Steinkuehler + Williams: MMOGs are full-fledged on-line societies better at bridging than bonding social capital; governance, reputation, and persistent social bonds operate at MMOG scale.
- **type**: CONCEPT
- **units**: IG-06
- **centrality**: important

#### 27. Cohabitation / cooperation / competition (three-not-four shared subtypes)
- **canonical_name**: Cohabitation / cooperation / competition (three-not-four shared subtypes)
- **definition**: Calleja's three modalities of shared involvement, including ambient awareness of social context. The 'three-not-four enumeration slip' surfaced in IG-06 as a notable internal-text inconsistency.
- **type**: CONCEPT
- **units**: IG-03, IG-06
- **centrality**: important

#### 28. Human-vs-AI co-agent distinction
- **canonical_name**: Human-vs-AI co-agent distinction
- **definition**: IG-06's position that player disposition is fundamentally restructured by perceived human-vs-AI status of co-agents — even rudimentary AI grounds cohabitation, but only persistent human-controlled agents support the reputation-and-betrayal layer (EVE Online Guiding Hand exemplar).
- **type**: CONCEPT
- **units**: IG-06
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 29. Scripted narrative vs. alterbiography (co-constitutive)
- **canonical_name**: Scripted narrative vs. alterbiography (co-constitutive)
- **definition**: Calleja's narrative-theoretic move that dissolves the narratology-vs-ludology binary: scripted narrative (designer-installed story-elements) and alterbiography (emergent player-narrative) are co-constitutive of narrative involvement, not mutually exclusive.
- **type**: CONCEPT
- **units**: IG-07, IG-09
- **centrality**: core

#### 30. Push narrative vs. pull narrative
- **canonical_name**: Push narrative vs. pull narrative
- **definition**: Calleja's analytic distinction between designer-pushed scripted progression and player-pulled emergent-narrative pursuit — the dispositional reorientation that determines focalization and story-time/discourse-time interplay.
- **type**: CONCEPT
- **units**: IG-07
- **centrality**: important

#### 31. Excitatory homeostasis + rhetorical strategies of affect
- **canonical_name**: Excitatory homeostasis + rhetorical strategies of affect
- **definition**: Bryant+Davies 2006 media-psychology frame, central to IG-08: affect is the emotional-tonal modulation of involvement generated via the cognitive+emotional+kinesthetic feedback loop, ranging across calming/adrenaline/uncanny. Designed and player-precipitated affect both count.
- **type**: CONCEPT
- **units**: IG-08
- **centrality**: important

#### 32. Escapism vs. escape (round-trip vs. one-way)
- **canonical_name**: Escapism vs. escape (round-trip vs. one-way)
- **definition**: Calleja's defense of escapism via Tolkien (Escape of the Prisoner vs. Flight of the Deserter): escapism is a legitimate round-trip imaginative activity that returns to the everyday, distinct from one-way escape. Imagination is the human faculty enabling escapism; secondary worlds (Tolkien 1983) are its habitable form.
- **type**: CONCEPT
- **units**: IG-08
- **centrality**: important

#### 33. Strategy / tactics / microtactics three-tier hierarchy
- **canonical_name**: Strategy / tactics / microtactics three-tier hierarchy
- **definition**: Calleja's IG-09 analytic innovation: strategies = plans in pursuit of primary goals; tactics = plans in pursuit of subgoals; microtactics = plans during moment of action. The three-tier hierarchy tracks goal-tier and temporal-distance and is the ludic dimension's load-bearing typology.
- **type**: CONCEPT
- **units**: IG-09
- **centrality**: important

#### 34. Goal-source taxonomy (system / self / community)
- **canonical_name**: Goal-source taxonomy (system / self / community)
- **definition**: Calleja's three-way classification of goal-origin: game-stipulated, player-stipulated, community-stipulated. Refuses both Suits's lusory-attitude single-source goal-genesis and McIntosh's claim of universal primary-goal concreteness in games.
- **type**: CONCEPT
- **units**: IG-09
- **centrality**: important

#### 35. Magic-circle excision
- **canonical_name**: Magic-circle excision
- **definition**: Calleja's deliberate exclusion of the Huizinga/Salen+Zimmerman/Juul magic-circle concept on two grounds: spatial redundancy (in digital games only navigable virtual space can be acted in) and empirical absence (no game-specific experiential bracketing in his interview data). One of three excisions structuring IG-03.
- **type**: POSITION-AS-CONCEPT
- **units**: IG-03, IG-06, IG-10
- **centrality**: core

#### 36. Lusory-attitude excision
- **canonical_name**: Lusory-attitude excision
- **definition**: Calleja's refusal of Suits's lusory-attitude criterion (voluntary adoption of inefficient rule-restricted means) on two grounds: digital games enforce rules mechanically (no voluntary adoption needed) and Suits's argument is circular.
- **type**: POSITION-AS-CONCEPT
- **units**: IG-03
- **centrality**: important

#### 37. Fun-essentialism excision
- **canonical_name**: Fun-essentialism excision
- **definition**: Calleja's rejection of the fun=games equation on two grounds: fun imposes a normative status that ignores variation across players' experiential states (including MMOG labor/tedium per Steinkuehler, Yee, Dibbell) and is analytically empty as a category.
- **type**: POSITION-AS-CONCEPT
- **units**: IG-03
- **centrality**: important

#### 38. Grounded-theoretic qualitative methodology (WoW + Planetside)
- **canonical_name**: Grounded-theoretic qualitative methodology (WoW + Planetside)
- **definition**: Calleja's three-year qualitative research design: extended personal participation in WoW (Blizzard 2004) and Planetside (SOE 2003), 25 in-game semi-structured interviews (two 2-hour sessions, in-game), focus groups, forum monitoring, blog tracking, machinima review; iterative coding generates the six dimensions as clusters of emphasis. The dissertation's empirical-adaptation seed.
- **type**: METHODOLOGICAL-ANCHOR
- **units**: IG-03, IG-11, IG-APX
- **centrality**: core

#### 39. KINESTHETIC involvement
- **canonical_name**: KINESTHETIC involvement
- **definition**: Engagement with avatar / game-piece control, spanning from conscious learning of the controls to fluent internalized movement; foregrounded when controls 'make themselves present' through unmastery or complex-sequence demand. The ergodic-dispositional substrate of the other dimensions.
- **type**: INVOLVEMENT-DIMENSION
- **units**: IG-00, IG-03, IG-04, IG-10
- **centrality**: core

#### 40. SPATIAL involvement
- **canonical_name**: SPATIAL involvement
- **definition**: Engagement with spatial qualities of a virtual environment via spatial control, navigation, and exploration; the internalizing of game-space such that the player senses inhabiting a place rather than perceiving a representation of space.
- **type**: INVOLVEMENT-DIMENSION
- **units**: IG-00, IG-03, IG-05, IG-10
- **centrality**: core

#### 41. SHARED involvement
- **canonical_name**: SHARED involvement
- **definition**: Engagement with awareness of and interaction with other agents (human- or computer-controlled) under modalities of cohabitation, cooperation, and competition; encompasses guild-political deliberation, ambient social-context awareness, and the human-vs-AI distinction.
- **type**: INVOLVEMENT-DIMENSION
- **units**: IG-00, IG-03, IG-06
- **centrality**: core

#### 42. NARRATIVE involvement
- **canonical_name**: NARRATIVE involvement
- **definition**: Engagement with both story-elements scripted into the game and emergent stories generated by ongoing interaction with the game world, its embedded objects, inhabitants, and events. Dissolves the narratology-vs-ludology binary via the scripted/alterbiography co-constitution.
- **type**: INVOLVEMENT-DIMENSION
- **units**: IG-00, IG-03, IG-07, IG-10
- **centrality**: core

#### 43. AFFECTIVE involvement
- **canonical_name**: AFFECTIVE involvement
- **definition**: Engagement with emotional states elicited by play — from calming aesthetic response through adrenaline-rush arousal to action-horror uncanny — including both designer-installed rhetorical strategies of affect and player-precipitated affective interpretations.
- **type**: INVOLVEMENT-DIMENSION
- **units**: IG-00, IG-03, IG-08
- **centrality**: core

#### 44. LUDIC involvement
- **canonical_name**: LUDIC involvement
- **definition**: Engagement with choices and their repercussions across game-stipulated, player-stipulated, and community-stipulated goals; opportunity cost is constitutive of well-balanced ludic systems — without repercussions, actions lose meaning.
- **type**: INVOLVEMENT-DIMENSION
- **units**: IG-00, IG-03, IG-09
- **centrality**: core
