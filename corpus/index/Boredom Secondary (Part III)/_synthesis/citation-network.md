## 4. The verified companion relations

The strongest links are explicit, self-declared companion relations — not inferences:

- **bor-sec-02 ⇄ bor-sec-03** — *Affectivity in Heidegger* I and II, a declared two-part study. II's abstract names I;
  I's footnote 13 explicitly **defers boredom and BT's temporal structure to II**. The deferral is itself a citable claim.
- **bor-sec-25 → bor-sec-26** — *The Bored Mind* cites *The Good of Boredom* as "forthcoming" (p.351) and defers specific
  technical work to it in six separate in-text citations. They divide labour: **mechanism** (25) vs. **eudaimonic
  value-theory payoff** (26).
- **bor-sec-28 → bor-sec-31** — Seo et al. **reuse Kim et al.'s boredom-inducing video stimulus** (same co-authors) and
  claim methodological improvement over it. *(P/anticipatory-application)* **Part III must treat these as one evolving
  research programme, not two independent convergent findings** (see CR-11).
- **bor-sec-12 → bor-sec-13** — Slaby's footnote 2 cites his own 2010 paper. Separately, **bor-sec-55 reproduces a whole
  section of bor-sec-12 verbatim**, with permission, per its own footnote 5.
- **bor-sec-49 → bor-sec-02/03** — Dahlstrom cites Elpidorou & Freeman (2015a, n.1 p.120; 2015b, n.16 p.123) and
  **converges** with them before turning the shared reconstruction into a critique (CR-05).

## 5. The Hadjioannou volume's internal silence

Strand G is 11 chapters of *one book*. Its internal citation count is **2**.

Chapters 6 (McManus), 7 (Withy), and 8 (Elpidorou & Freeman) are, respectively: the authenticity-and-affect chapter that
never raises boredom; the chapter arguing boredom can occupy the *Grundbefindlichkeit* role; and the chapter arguing
profound boredom is authenticity-disclosing. **None engages the others** (DA-10). The volume contains the thesis, the
counter-default, and the bridge, in adjacent chapters, unconnected.

## 6. Methodological caveat

`cites-cluster-author` edges were recorded when a unit's builder found a real citation, and builders were explicitly
prompted to look for sibling units. Absence is therefore **mostly** verified rather than merely unrecorded — several
builders documented negative checks explicitly (bor-sec-43 checked ~33 first-authors against every other unit and found
zero overlap; bor-sec-39 checked for Charoenpit and Jaques and found neither; bor-sec-32 confirmed Yakobi cites a
*different* Elpidorou paper — "Is Boredom One or Many?" (2020) — not the regulatory papers).

Still: a citation this cluster's builders did not look for would not appear here. The strand-matrix zeros for B and E are
strong but not exhaustive proof of absence. The **positive** findings (Elpidorou's centrality; the 28→31 shared stimulus)
are direct textual evidence and do not depend on this caveat.
