# Terminology Appendix — Boredom Secondary (Part III)

**72 lemmas** spanning the cluster's four vocabularies: German/Greek phenomenological terms, boredom constructs,
measurement instruments, and theory-models. Cross-disciplinary false friends are marked **⚠** — these are terms that
appear in both halves of the cluster meaning different things, and they are where citation errors happen.

Gate: ≥50 lemmas required. Delivered: 72.

## Cross-disciplinary false friends — the citation-error surface

The nine ⚠ terms above are where this cluster's two halves collide. Restated as a checklist for Part III:

| Term | Humanities sense | STEM sense | Consequence of conflating |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stimmung / mood** | Disclosive attunement to beings as a whole | Low-intensity, longer-duration affect | Collapses DA-09; makes the regulatory account look like a translation of Heidegger rather than a rival |
| **Befindlichkeit** | Always-already-finding-oneself-attuned | (rendered "state-of-mind") an inner mental state | Imports the psychologism the term exists to refuse |
| **Angst** | The ground-attunement disclosing thrownness | Clinical/trait anxiety | Turns an ontological structure into a symptom |
| **engagement** | Absorbed being-with (or its *absence*, per fcm-05's dinner party) | A beta/(alpha+theta) ratio, **or** flow, **or** Wang & Degol's tripartite model | Three different objects under one word; bor-sec-05's dinner-party structure shows outward engagement can *mask* boredom |
| **flow** | — | Csikszentmihalyi's channel model | Boredom is a *quadrant*, not an absence |
| **Gebanntheit / entrancement** | Temporal character of being-left-empty | (Quaranta's own cinematic gloss) | Misattributes Heidegger's apparatus to a source that never uses it |
| **Destruktion** | Recovery + clearing-away | "destruction" | Loses the positive moment |
| **Bildung** | Formation, in Heidegger's recovered sense | "education"/"training" | Collapses the very distinction Thomson and Mansikka are drawing |
| **Volk** | The collective addressee of a *Grundstimmung* | "population"/"cohort"/"sample" | ⚠⚠ **The most dangerous one for Part III.** Reaching for Heidegger's collective account of boredom to describe a student *cohort* imports the Rektoratszeit's political freight. Take the collective account from Hughes's structural critique instead (DA-08, DA-11). |
| **pathos** | Facticity / being-affected (Sheehan, via *De Anima*) | — | Do **not** route Sheehan's πάθος into the *Rhetoric* II tradition; the lineages are adjacent, not identical |
| **predictive processing** | — | A modelling formalism (prediction error, precision-weighting) | ⚠ Not "predictive analytics" (ML regression). A first pass of the concordance conflated them on a substring match. |
