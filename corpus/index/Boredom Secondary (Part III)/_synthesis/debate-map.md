# Debate Map — Boredom Secondary (Part III)

Eleven documented disagreements across the 55-unit cluster. Each entry gives the two poles, the units on each pole with
page support, and a resolution note stating where the cluster actually stands — including where it stands *nowhere*,
because several of these are live and unresolved.

**A note on construction.** Three of these axes (DA-05, DA-08, DA-10) are **assembled by this cluster** from units that do
not cite one another on the point. They are tagged **`P/anticipatory-application`** and are the cluster's own analytical
contribution rather than a dispute the sources stage themselves. The remaining eight are staged by the sources.

Provenance: **FE** = the source's own published claim · **P/anticipatory-application** = our interpretive construction.

## DA-01 · Boredom as deficit ↔ boredom as functional/adaptive

**Poles.** Boredom is a failure state — of attention, of meaning, of self-regulation — versus boredom is an adaptive
signal that does useful work.

| Pole | Units |
|---|---|
| Functional / adaptive | bor-sec-25, bor-sec-26 (home), bor-sec-20, bor-sec-05, bor-sec-06, bor-sec-13 |
| Deficit / aversive | the psychological literature reviewed *within* bor-sec-25/26 and bor-sec-21; bor-sec-32 (attentional-control failure) |
| Critical of the functional turn | bor-sec-08, bor-sec-07 |

**The state of the debate.** Elpidorou's regulatory theory concedes essentially every phenomenological feature a deficit
account would cite — aversiveness, disengagement, attentional failure, ambiguous arousal — and then reinterprets their
*functional significance*, landing on the adaptive side via a three-part adaptive-value case (bor-sec-25, pp.474–475, FE).

**Resolution — by partition, not by verdict.** The decisive move is in the companion paper: **state** boredom (occasional,
non-pathological) is functional, argued via a pain-capacity analogy (bor-sec-26, p.327, FE); **trait** boredom / boredom
proneness remains genuinely harmful but is reframed as a **dysfunction of that same mechanism** (bor-sec-26, p.333, n.5,
FE). This *reconciles* the deficit literature rather than refuting it. Anyone citing "Elpidorou says boredom is good"
without the state/trait partition is misciting him.

**Two independent arrivals, neither citing the regulatory literature.** Slaby reconstructs Heidegger's "telling refusal
is a telling" — a message-bearing, proto-functional structure — then judges its activist payoff over-extended
(bor-sec-13, pp.114–118, FE). Darling's predictive processing supplies a candidate *mechanism* (prediction error,
precision-weighting) for what Elpidorou motivates (bor-sec-20, FE). *(P/anticipatory-application)* Part III should cite
Elpidorou and Darling as complementary — *why* boredom regulates versus *how* — not interchangeably.

**The live objection.** Hughes treats Elpidorou's drift toward normative/psychological theorizing as a **departure from**
Heidegger's project rather than a development of it (bor-sec-08, p.6, n.9, FE). Gibbs's "positive attunement" is
existential-disclosive, **not** evolutionary-adaptive, and the cluster records explicitly that it should not be treated as
the same construct as the regulatory account (bor-sec-07, FE). The functional turn is *contested at the level of what
kind of claim it is*, not merely whether it is true.

## DA-02 · Profound boredom *is* boredom ↔ profound boredom is a distinct existential mood

**Poles.** FCM's third form is continuous with ordinary boredom versus it is *sui generis*, sharing only a name.

| Pole | Units |
|---|---|
| Continuous (qualified) | bor-sec-04 (home) |
| Middle position | bor-sec-09 |
| Orthodox-Heideggerian (sits on the distinct pole without arguing for it) | bor-sec-10, bor-sec-11, bor-sec-12, bor-sec-13 |
| Stages without adjudicating | bor-sec-03, bor-sec-05 |

**Resolution — argued, and narrower than usually reported.** Elpidorou & Freeman *construct* the sui-generis reading
themselves, out of Heidegger's own formal-indication remarks (p.16), and then **reject it** with four counter-arguments
(pp.17–20), landing on "profound boredom is boredom" — but only in a **qualified, hybrid** sense: continuous with and
conditioned by ordinary boredom (FCM 156, p.19), while identical to *neither* state nor trait boredom (pp.9–15, FE).

Their argument for non-identity with trait boredom is structurally elegant and worth preserving: profound boredom and
trait boredom share the feature of being **totalizing**, but have **opposite existential valence** — trait boredom
(Bargdill/Maltsberger testimonies) is incapacitating and despair-inducing; profound boredom is motivating and
authenticity-disclosing (pp.14–15, 22, FE).

Mihačević stakes a genuine middle: profound boredom cannot be entirely alien since it shares the structural pair
*Leergelassenheit*/*Hingehaltenheit* with the other two forms, yet it is "boredom that wishes not to be boredom"
(bor-sec-09, p.39, FE).

**Unresolved.** The Heideggerian units mostly do not answer the challenge — Quaranta footnotes E&F's thesis and never
engages it (bor-sec-10, p.35, FE). The debate is one-sided in the literature, not settled.

## DA-03 · Angst ↔ profound boredom as the *Grundstimmung*

**Poles.** Which mood occupies the disclosive ground role in Heidegger's architecture.

| Pole | Units |
|---|---|
| Angst | bor-sec-45, bor-sec-50, bor-sec-48; *Being and Time*'s own near-unique designation (SZ 310) |
| Profound boredom | bor-sec-51; FCM's own post-1929 naming |
| Flags the shift without pursuing it | bor-sec-02 (p.662, n.2) |

**Resolution — unresolved, and the cluster shows why.** Elpidorou & Freeman note that *Grundstimmung* appears only
**once** in *Being and Time*, and becomes central only after 1929 (bor-sec-02, FE) — so the question is periodizing, not
merely doctrinal. Withy supplies the only affirmative case for boredom: profound boredom, like angst, can occupy the
*Grundbefindlichkeit* role that calls Dasein to the vocation of being Dasein, with page-precise support at FCM 143
(*Anrufen*) and FCM 165 (bor-sec-51, FE).

*(P/anticipatory-application)* The sharpest evidence is **negative and intra-volume** — see DA-10.

## DA-04 · Self-report ↔ physiological marker as ground truth

**Poles.** When the subject's report and the instrument disagree, which one is boredom?

| Pole | Units |
|---|---|
| Self-report is the criterion | most Strand C/D/E designs by default: bor-sec-22, bor-sec-30, bor-sec-31, bor-sec-33 |
| Physiology is the criterion | bor-sec-24 (implicit measures at times beat explicit report), bor-sec-28 (in practice) |
| Documents the dissociation | bor-sec-21, bor-sec-28, bor-sec-35 |
| Supplies a principled reason for the wall | bor-sec-04, bor-sec-48, bor-sec-55 |

**The evidence for dissociation is strong and independent.**
- Kim et al.: all 13 analyzed participants showed temporally coincident EEG-and-gaze "Potential Boredom Regions", while
  self-report (mean 6.38/9) correlated **significantly with no physiological feature** (bor-sec-28, FE).
- Raffaelli et al.: field-wide arousal contradictions (London et al. 1972 increased HR; Pattyn et al. 2008 decreased HR)
  are candidates for **directional fractionation** — subjective low arousal coexisting with autonomic high arousal
  (bor-sec-21, FE).
- Charoenpit & Ohkura: the paper's stated rule ("high pupil diameter ratio indicates the learner is bored") is
  **contradicted in sign by its own correlation tables** (positive with interest, negative with boredom) (bor-sec-35, FE).
- Clark & Hassert: implicit gaze/pupil measures at times **outperform** explicit self-report for indexing memory
  confidence — and metacognition is in principle opaque to the reporting subject (bor-sec-24, FE).

**Resolution — the cluster refuses the framing.** *(P/anticipatory-application)* The humanities strand supplies the reason
the question is malformed: a *Grundstimmung* is **awakened, not ascertained** (fcm-03), and Hadjioannou shows the same
argument-form independently for angst — reflective, objectifying access **distorts the affect it targets** (bor-sec-48,
FE). If that is right, self-report is not a noisy channel onto a hidden state; it is a *different kind of act*. The
dissociation is then the predicted result, not an anomaly.

**Consequence for Part III (the cluster's single most actionable finding).** A design that validates EEG/gaze *against*
self-report is assuming the answer to a question this cluster documents as open. Report both channels; do not silently
treat either as criterion.

## DA-05 · Frontal alpha asymmetry as boredom marker ↔ confound with arousal · **`P/anticipatory-application`**

**Constructed by this cluster.** None of the three units cites another on this point; the disagreement is real and
evidenced, but it is assembled here rather than staged by the sources.

| Position | Unit | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| FEA indexes boredom | bor-sec-30 | Uses F4−F3 alpha as the boredom index; leftward FEA shift across a boring task correlates with low trait boredom (r=−.48, −.47, both p<.01), frontally specific, reference-montage robust (N=54, FE). **Never discusses arousal or confound anywhere** — verified by direct search of the source. |
| Alpha is principally a *global arousal* marker | bor-sec-27 | ~40% alpha power reduction eyes-closed→eyes-open, **spatially uniform across all sites**, co-varying with skin-conductance level; decomposes the resting EEG into a global arousal component (alpha) and a focal activation component (delta/theta/beta) (N=28, FE). **Never mentions boredom.** |
| Asymmetry features underperform for boredom | bor-sec-31 | DASM/RASM (and plain differential entropy) were **rarely selected** by the Wrapper Subset Evaluator; gamma/beta features carried the boredom signal instead (N=28, FE). |

**Resolution — a live caution, not a settled verdict.** Barry gives a *mechanism* by which an alpha-derived index could
track arousal rather than boredom; Seo gives *feature-selection evidence* that asymmetry is not what a classifier picks
for boredom; Perone relies on the index without addressing either. Perone's own null result on Hypothesis 1 (no baseline
FEA–trait-boredom correlation) is consistent with the caution.

**Consequence for Part III.** Any FEA-based boredom index needs an arousal control (skin conductance or an equivalent) and
an explicit baseline rationale. Note that bor-sec-33 (Yuvaraj), the cluster's most Part-III-relevant EEG study, uses **no
resting baseline at all** — both conditions are active eyes-open viewing — which sidesteps Barry's caution rather than
answering it.

## DA-06 · Boredom as a unified construct ↔ a heterogeneous family

**Poles.** Does "boredom" name one thing, or several that share a word?

| Pole | Units |
|---|---|
| Diagnostic, leaning heterogeneous | bor-sec-21 (home) |
| Dissolves the dichotomy | bor-sec-20 |
| Treats boredom as plural under Heidegger, unified elsewhere | bor-sec-06 |
| Argues the field's taxonomy conflates distinct states | bor-sec-16 |

**Resolution — deliberately unresolved by its home source.** Raffaelli et al. do **not** resolve to either pole. Their
argued position is diagnostic: field-wide inconsistency (in arousal direction, in fMRI regions recruited) is compatible
*either* with non-standardized operationalization of one construct *or* with genuine subtype heterogeneity. Their forward
recommendation — "arriving at an empirically validated taxonomy of different types of boredom" — commits them to taking
heterogeneity seriously as the more novel hypothesis, while explicit hedging ("preliminary evidence", "if different types
of boredom do indeed exist") keeps them short of asserting it (bor-sec-21, abstract p.2451 and Conclusion p.2460, FE).

Darling **dissolves rather than adjudicates**: emotion and mood are levels of one hierarchy (differing in timescale), not
distinct kinds (bor-sec-20, pp.3, 20, FE). Feldges & Pieczenko attack from the taxonomic side: Götz & Frenzel's four-type
model conflates an antecedent state (indifferent), a genuine core (calibrating), and resultant directed-emotion states
(searching/reactant) (bor-sec-16, pp.9–10, FE).

## DA-07 · Monotony/environment-caused ↔ meaning/agent-caused

**Poles.** Does boredom come from the world (a determinate boring thing, repetition) or from the subject (loss of
significance)?

| Pole | Units |
|---|---|
| Staged unresolved in Heidegger's own architecture | bor-sec-03 |
| Hybrid | bor-sec-08 |
| Environment/institution-focused | bor-sec-16, bor-sec-19, bor-sec-15 |

**Resolution — the asymmetry is internal to the canonical account.** Elpidorou & Freeman's exposition stages the axis
without naming it: the **first** form is caused "from outside" by a determinate boring thing, while the **second and
third** "arise from out of Dasein itself" — an unresolved but load-bearing asymmetry in the increasing-depth story
(bor-sec-03, pp.679–680, FE). The canonical account therefore *contains* both poles and distributes them across the forms
rather than choosing.

Hughes lands on a genuine hybrid: pandemic boredom is primarily **environment/situation-caused** (absence of
world-sustaining intersubjective projects), yet its residual significance is **agent/situation-relative** (heterogeneous
factical circumstances) and not reducible to a uniform environmental mechanism (bor-sec-08, pp.9–12, FE).

## DA-08 · Individually structured ↔ collectively induced boredom · **`P/anticipatory-application`**

**Constructed by this cluster.** bor-sec-08 and bor-sec-55 have no citation relation and reach the same limit from
opposite directions.

| Position | Unit | Claim |
|---|---|---|
| The individualist framework has no place for collective boredom | bor-sec-08 | Heidegger's account presupposes that *being withdrawn* from the world depends on **others remaining absorbed in it**; a society-wide, simultaneous withdrawal (lockdown) therefore has no place in his framework (p.11, n.13, FE). |
| Heidegger himself made it collective | bor-sec-55 | Documents Heidegger reframing profound boredom's *Grundstimmung* as a ***Volk*-level** communal attunement whose demand is collective self-founding rather than individual resoluteness (FE) — with the political entanglement that follows. |
| Boredom is socioeconomically produced | bor-sec-14 | Sociologizes the ostensibly trait-like fundamental attunement as leisure-economy-produced, without abandoning the ontological register (pp.316–324, FE). |

**Resolution — the individualism is a real limit, and Heidegger's own escape route is politically compromised.** Hughes
identifies the structural gap; Slaby & Thonhauser show that the historical Heidegger *did* scale the analysis up — into
a *Volk*-attunement, in the Rektoratszeit texts. That is an answer to the scaling question and a warning about the
answer's provenance simultaneously.

**Consequence for Part III.** The subjects are boredom-in-a-cohort — a class, a VLE session, a shared VR deployment. The
canonical phenomenological account is individualist *by construction*; the cluster documents two independent routes to a
collective revision, only one of which is usable without extensive qualification.

## DA-09 · Boredom as mood (non-intentional) ↔ boredom as emotion (object-directed)

**Poles.** Is boredom a mood — non-intentional, world-disclosing — or an emotion with an object?

| Pole | Units |
|---|---|
| Mood | bor-sec-16 (reporting the phenomenological camp: Heidegger, Schopenhauer, Bollnow, Prinz) |
| Emotion | bor-sec-25, bor-sec-26 (boredom-as-emotion is the regulatory theory's premise) |
| Collapses the distinction | educational psychology as reported in bor-sec-16 (Pekrun, Götz & Hall: moods as "low-intensity emotions") |
| Dissolves it hierarchically | bor-sec-20 |

**Resolution — a genuine cross-disciplinary fault line, and a translation problem underneath it.** Feldges & Pieczenko
report that the philosophical camp treats boredom as a mood on Prinz's intentionality criterion, while educational
psychology collapses moods into low-intensity emotions (bor-sec-16, pp.3–4, 11, FE). This is not a quibble: the entire
regulatory account presupposes boredom is an **emotion** (something with an object and a function), which is exactly what
the Heideggerian account denies.

Beneath it sits an unresolved translation debate: *Befindlichkeit* has at least **eight** competing English renderings —
state-of-mind, disposition/disposedness, ontological disposition, findingness, affectedness/affectivity, situatedness,
attunement, or left untranslated — with Elpidorou & Freeman electing the last (bor-sec-02, p.663, n.4, FE). Whether fear
is a mood or an object-directed emotion is contested even within *Being and Time* scholarship (bor-sec-02, p.668, FE).

## DA-10 · Whether boredom is even a candidate for the authenticity role · **`P/anticipatory-application`**

**Constructed by this cluster** — and the sharpest single piece of evidence in it, because both units are **chapters of the
same book**.

| Position | Unit | Claim |
|---|---|---|
| Angst is the paradigmatic authenticity-relevant *Grundstimmung* still owed a full account | bor-sec-50 (Ch. 6) | McManus's footnote 35 (p.148) treats angst as the mood still owing an account — **without ever raising boredom as a candidate** (FE). |
| Profound boredom merits comparable authenticity-disclosing status | bor-sec-04 (Ch. 8) | Argues profound boredom is motivating and authenticity-disclosing, against trait boredom's incapacitation (pp.14–15, 22, FE). |

**Resolution — an unstated asymmetry, not a disagreement.** These two chapters, twenty pages apart in Hadjioannou's
*Heidegger on Affect*, are talking past each other. McManus is not *arguing against* boredom's candidacy; boredom simply
does not appear in his field of candidates. *(P/anticipatory-application)* That is evidence about the state of the
literature rather than about boredom: even in a volume containing a dedicated chapter arguing profound boredom's
authenticity-disclosing role, the authenticity-and-affect chapter defaults to angst without comment.

Withy (Ch. 7) sits between them, giving the affirmative case for boredom in the *Grundbefindlichkeit* role (bor-sec-51,
FE) — so the volume contains the thesis, the counter-default, and the bridge, without any of the three engaging the
others.

## DA-11 · Heidegger's philosophy ↔ Heidegger's politics

**Poles.** Can the boredom analysis be read on a general existential-phenomenological level, bracketing the political
entanglement?

| Pole | Units |
|---|---|
| Bracket it (explicitly, with the danger noted) | bor-sec-18 |
| Refuse to bracket it | bor-sec-12, bor-sec-55, bor-sec-52 |

**Resolution — contested, and the refusers have the textual evidence.** Mansikka footnotes the "danger" of giving
positive meaning to Heidegger's "strength and power" given his Nazism (via Ott 1993) and elects to read the philosophy on
a general existential-phenomenological level, bracketing the political question (bor-sec-18, pp.264–265, n.6, FE).

Against that: Slaby argues Heidegger's activist construal of profound boredom is **not required by the phenomenon
itself** but reflects — and later feeds — his political-existential temperament (bor-sec-12, pp.242–246, FE). Slaby &
Thonhauser show the *Volk*-attunement reframing operating in the Rektoratszeit texts directly (bor-sec-55, FE).
Vallega-Neu interrogates Heidegger's Nazism against his own claimed capacity to "ward off" distortion through vigilance
(bor-sec-52, FE).

*(P/anticipatory-application)* The cluster's own position, forced by DA-08: the collective scaling of boredom's
*Grundstimmung* — which Part III needs, since its subjects are cohorts — is **exactly** the move that runs through
Heidegger's most politically compromised material. This is not a reason to avoid it; it is a reason to take the
collective account from Hughes's structural critique rather than from Heidegger's own *Volk*-level answer.
