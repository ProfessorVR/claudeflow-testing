# Cluster-Level Ontology — Boredom Secondary (Part III)

The conceptual architecture of a 55-unit secondary-literature cluster that reads boredom simultaneously through
phenomenology (Heidegger's *Langeweile*) and hard measurement (EEG, pupillometry, gaze). Loci are given as the
cited source's own page numbers. Provenance tags: **FE** = the source's own published claim; **FAITHFUL-\<author\>** =
close paraphrase of a specific passage; **P/anticipatory-application** = *our* interpretive link, never the source's claim.

Companion files: `debate-map.md` (the disagreements), `boredom-construct-measure-concordance.md` (the construct×measure
grid), `concept-matrix.csv` (source × construct presence), `boredom-terminology-appendix.md` (lemmas).

---

## 1. The governing problem: two boredoms that barely speak

This cluster exists because "boredom" names two objects that share a word and almost nothing else.

For the humanities strands (A, B, G), boredom is an **attunement** (*Stimmung*) — a way beings as a whole are made
manifest. It is not a state inside a person; it is a disclosure of how one stands amid what is. Its deepest form
(*tiefe Langeweile*) is, on Heidegger's account, the *Grundstimmung* of contemporary Dasein, and it is reached only
when passing-the-time is no longer permitted.

For the STEM strands (D, E, F), boredom is a **state** with signatures: alpha and theta power, frontal asymmetry
slopes, fixation dispersion, pupil diameter, response-time decrements, self-report scale scores. It is something a
classifier can be trained on — Seo et al. reach 86.73% (bor-sec-31, FE).

The cluster's payload is not to dissolve this difference but to make its **seam** legible, because Part III's empirical
analysis of student boredom stands on both feet at once. The concordance (§3 of the plan;
`boredom-construct-measure-concordance.md`) is where that seam is drawn.

## 2. The measurement wall — the cluster's central structural finding

The two boredoms do not merely differ in vocabulary. There is a **principled barrier** between them, and this cluster's
most important structural finding is that *both sides independently arrive at it from opposite directions*.

**From the phenomenological side**, Heidegger's methodological claim is that a fundamental attunement is *awakened*
(*Wecken*), never *ascertained* like something present-at-hand (FCM §16, p.59 / pdf 42; see `fcm-03`). This is not a
stylistic preference — it is an argument that reflective, objectifying access *distorts or destroys* the very phenomenon
it targets. Hadjioannou (bor-sec-48) makes structurally the identical argument for *angst*: reflection is not a neutral
instrument on affect (FAITHFUL-Hadjioannou, pp.69–104). Elpidorou & Freeman note that Heidegger's use of **formal
indication** is precisely a device for resisting having his concepts checked against empirical psychology at all — and
then proceed to check anyway, arguing the check is legitimate (bor-sec-04, p.16, FE).

**From the empirical side**, the same wall shows up as data. Kim et al. instrument boredom with both EEG and gaze and
find that **self-report correlated significantly with no physiological feature** (bor-sec-28, N=13, FE). Charoenpit &
Ohkura state a rule — high pupil-diameter ratio indicates boredom — that their own correlation tables contradict in
sign (bor-sec-35, FE, flagged as an internal inconsistency of the source). Perone et al.'s Hypothesis 1 (baseline
frontal asymmetry differs by trait-boredom subtype) is **not supported** (bor-sec-30, N=54, FE).

*(P/anticipatory-application)* Read together, these are not independent embarrassments. The humanities strand supplies a
**reason** why self-report should not be treated as ground truth for an attunement, and the STEM strand supplies
**evidence** that it does not behave as ground truth. This is the cluster's strongest joint claim, and it is the one
Part III most needs: it converts "which instrument is right?" into a structural question about what kind of object
boredom is.

## 3. The structural-moment grammar, inherited and contested

The cluster inherits FCM's two structural moments — **being left empty** (*Leergelassenheit*) and **being held in limbo**
(*Hingehaltenheit*) — and its counter-move, **passing-the-time** (*Zeitvertreib*). What the cluster adds is contest over
how they distribute across the three forms.

- Mihačević rejects the uniform reading (against Svendsen and against Elpidorou & Freeman): only the *second* form is a
  genuine loss of personal meaning; the first merely suspends it; the third negates the personal but not meaning-as-such
  (bor-sec-09, pp.40–41, FE).
- Hernández Albarracín et al. reject the *staged/escalating* reading outright, insisting the three forms are equally
  available co-present modalities rather than steps toward profundity (bor-sec-11, p.220, FE).
- Slaby reconstructs all three forms at high fidelity and then contests Heidegger's closing **activist** move — the
  demand for resolute self-assertion — offering instead Pessoa's affirmed, undemanding dwelling (bor-sec-13 §4;
  bor-sec-12, FE).

`deepens-into`: first → second → third (FCM's own ordering, contested by bor-sec-11).

## 4. The functional turn and its partition

The cluster's psychological centre of gravity is Elpidorou's **regulatory theory**: boredom is informative (it signals
an unsatisfactory situation) and regulatory (it motivates goal-change) (bor-sec-25, FE). Its companion argues the
normative payoff — boredom's *good* (bor-sec-26, FE).

The decisive move is a **partition rather than a verdict**: state boredom (occasional, non-pathological) is functional
and adaptive; trait boredom / boredom proneness remains genuinely harmful but is reframed as a *dysfunction of that same
mechanism* (bor-sec-26, p.333, n.5, FE). This reconciles rather than refutes the deficit literature, and it is why the
cluster's deficit↔functional axis is not a simple two-pole fight (see `debate-map.md`, DA-01).

Two independent arrivals at a proto-functional position are worth recording, because neither cites the regulatory
literature:
- Slaby reconstructs Heidegger's **"telling refusal is a telling"** faithfully — a message-bearing account — and then
  judges its activist payoff over-extended (bor-sec-13, FE). *(P/anticipatory-application)* This is a philosophically
  independent version of the caution the regulatory account supplies from cognitive science.
- Darling's predictive-processing synthesis supplies a candidate **mechanism** (prediction error, precision-weighting)
  for what the regulatory account motivates (bor-sec-20, FE). *(P/anticipatory-application)* Part III should cite the two
  as complementary, not interchangeable: Elpidorou answers *why* boredom regulates; Darling proposes *how*.

## 5. The individualism problem

Heidegger's boredom analysis is built on an individual Dasein. Two units, from different strands and with no citation
relation, converge on the limit:

- Hughes: Heidegger's account presupposes that *being withdrawn* from the world depends on **others remaining absorbed in
  it**. A society-wide, simultaneous withdrawal — pandemic lockdown — has no place in his individualist framework
  (bor-sec-08, p.11, n.13, FE).
- Slaby & Thonhauser: Heidegger himself reframed profound boredom's *Grundstimmung* as a ***Volk*-level collective
  attunement**, whose demand is collective self-founding (bor-sec-55, FE) — a politicization the same volume's other
  chapters do not surface.

*(P/anticipatory-application)* For Part III, whose subjects are boredom-in-a-cohort (a class, a VLE session, a shared VR
deployment), this is not an academic point: the canonical phenomenological account is individualist by construction, and
the cluster documents two independent routes to its collective revision.

## 6. The neuro-marker problem

Three units converge on a genuine methodological difficulty that **none of them states as such**, and the cluster records
it as a cluster-level finding *(P/anticipatory-application)*:

1. Perone et al. use **frontal alpha asymmetry** (F4−F3, alpha 8–13 Hz) as the boredom index and never discuss arousal
   or confound anywhere in the paper (bor-sec-30, FE — verified by direct search of the source).
2. Barry et al. establish that **alpha is a global arousal marker**: ~40% power reduction from eyes-closed to eyes-open,
   spatially uniform, tracking skin-conductance level (bor-sec-27, N=28, FE). Barry's paper never mentions boredom.
3. Seo et al. find that frontal-asymmetry features (DASM/RASM) were **rarely selected** by feature selection for boredom
   classification, underperforming relative to gamma/beta band features (bor-sec-31, N=28, FE).

Together: the marker Perone relies on is, per Barry, principally an arousal signal, and per Seo, not the feature a
classifier actually picks for boredom. This is documented as `debate-map.md` DA-05, and it is a live caution for any
Part III EEG design.

## 7. The evolutionary arc, in one line

Existential-phenomenological analysis (2001–2015) → functional/regulatory psychology (2018) → neuro/eye operationalization
(2018–2025) → VR/education application (2019–2025), with the phenomenological strand never absorbed and increasingly
contesting the psychological one from outside. See `boredom-scholarly-evolution.md`.

#### 24. Regulatory theory of boredom
- **mr_translation**: regulatory theory of boredom
- **definition**: Elpidorou's account: boredom is an emotion that is informative (signalling an unsatisfactory situation) and regulatory (motivating goal-change), with a three-part adaptive-value case and an explicit limit — boredom signals lack of interest, not lack of importance (bor-sec-25, p.473). The cluster's most-cited in-cluster theory.
- **type**: THEORY-MODEL
- **units**: bor-sec-25, bor-sec-26
- **centrality**: core

#### 25. Boredom Feedback Model
- **mr_translation**: Boredom Feedback Model (BFM)
- **definition**: Tam et al.'s model built on inadequate attentional engagement (IAE) — a discrepancy between actual and desired attentional engagement — as boredom's core, with feedback between attention and affect.
- **type**: THEORY-MODEL
- **units**: bor-sec-22
- **centrality**: important

#### 26. Goldilocks zone
- **mr_translation**: Goldilocks zone
- **definition**: The band of task difficulty that avoids boredom on either side (too easy or too complex), inherited from Danckert & Elpidorou and given a formal reading in Darling's predictive-processing synthesis.
- **type**: THEORY-MODEL
- **units**: bor-sec-20
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 27. Four-type taxonomy of academic boredom
- **mr_translation**: Goetz & Frenzel four-type taxonomy
- **definition**: Indifferent, calibrating, searching, reactant boredom. Feldges & Pieczenko's central critique is that the taxonomy conflates an antecedent state, a genuine core form, and resultant directed-emotion states under one label (bor-sec-16, pp.9-10).
- **type**: THEORY-MODEL
- **units**: bor-sec-16, bor-sec-21
- **centrality**: important

#### 28. Magical transformation theory of emotion
- **mr_translation**: Sartre's magical transformation theory
- **definition**: Emotion as a pre-reflective magical transformation of the world. Elpidorou & Freeman's own extension applies it to boredom — Sartre never discusses boredom himself — producing a non-pathologizing functional reading via machinery incompatible with the Heideggerian one (bor-sec-05).
- **type**: THEORY-MODEL
- **units**: bor-sec-05, bor-sec-06
- **centrality**: important

#### 29. Engagement index
- **mr_translation**: beta/(alpha+theta) engagement index
- **definition**: Pope, Bogart & Bartolome's ratio formula converting band power into a scalar engagement estimate; the historical bridge from EEG spectra to an "engagement" construct, surveyed in Raffaelli et al.
- **type**: THEORY-MODEL
- **units**: bor-sec-21
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 30. Directional fractionation
- **mr_translation**: directional fractionation hypothesis
- **definition**: The hypothesis that subjective low arousal and autonomic high arousal can dissociate in boredom, proposed in Raffaelli et al.'s review to reconcile contradictory HR findings. The mechanism underwriting DA-04's self-report/physiology split.
- **type**: THEORY-MODEL
- **units**: bor-sec-21
- **centrality**: important

#### 31. All-Things-Considered Judgment model
- **mr_translation**: All-Things-Considered Judgment model of owned emotion
- **definition**: McManus's own model of authentic (owned) emotion, offered against decisionist and standpoint rivals. Notable for the cluster: McManus treats *angst*, never boredom, as the paradigmatic authenticity-relevant Grundstimmung still owed an account — while the same volume's chapter 8 argues profound boredom deserves exactly that status (DA-10).
- **type**: THEORY-MODEL
- **units**: bor-sec-50
- **centrality**: important

#### 32. Motivation model (gaze-based)
- **mr_translation**: gaze-based motivation assessment model
- **definition**: Wang, Xu & Chen's inference-rule model mapping gaze features (average pupil diameter, fixation count) plus self-input onto motivational factors, driving personalised pedagogical-agent feedback. Its headline 81.3% accuracy is borrowed from a cited companion study, not validated in the demo paper itself.
- **type**: THEORY-MODEL
- **units**: bor-sec-41
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 33. Langeweile
- **german_lemma**: Langeweile
- **mr_translation**: boredom ("the long while")
- **definition**: The German term whose morphology (lange Weile, "long while") carries the temporal thesis the English "boredom" loses: boredom is the lengthening of the while, not merely an unpleasant feeling.
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-09, bor-sec-12, bor-sec-13, bor-sec-55
- **centrality**: core

#### 34. Grundstimmung
- **german_lemma**: Grundstimmung
- **mr_translation**: fundamental attunement / ground mood
- **definition**: A mood that discloses beings as a whole; awakened, never ascertained. The cluster's pivotal term: candidate occupants are contested (angst vs. profound boredom — DA-03), as is its scale (individual vs. Volk-level — DA-08).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-01, bor-sec-02, bor-sec-04, bor-sec-11, bor-sec-13, bor-sec-49, bor-sec-51, bor-sec-52, bor-sec-54, bor-sec-55
- **centrality**: core

#### 35. Stimmung
- **german_lemma**: Stimmung
- **mr_translation**: mood / attunement
- **definition**: Mood as a disclosive way of being attuned to the world, as against an inner feeling-state. The general category of which boredom is a species, and the site of the mood/emotion distinction contested in DA-09.
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-02, bor-sec-03, bor-sec-16, bor-sec-46, bor-sec-49, bor-sec-50, bor-sec-52, bor-sec-54
- **centrality**: core

#### 36. Befindlichkeit
- **german_lemma**: Befindlichkeit
- **mr_translation**: findingness / disposedness / attunement (contested)
- **definition**: Being and Time's structure of always-already-finding-oneself-attuned. Its English rendering is itself a documented debate with at least eight competing translations (bor-sec-02, p.663 n.4). Withy reconstructs it as a disclosive structure broader than mood (bor-sec-51).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-02, bor-sec-03, bor-sec-49, bor-sec-51, bor-sec-52, bor-sec-55
- **centrality**: core

#### 37. Leergelassenheit
- **german_lemma**: Leergelassenheit
- **mr_translation**: being left empty
- **definition**: The first structural moment: beings' telling refusal (Versagen) of themselves. Shared across all three forms, which is Mihačević's argument that profound boredom cannot be an entirely alien form (bor-sec-09, p.39).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-09, bor-sec-12, bor-sec-13
- **centrality**: important

#### 38. Hingehaltenheit
- **german_lemma**: Hingehaltenheit
- **mr_translation**: being held in limbo
- **definition**: The second structural moment: being impelled toward what makes Dasein possible. Paired with Leergelassenheit as the structural unity that is FCM's analytic payload.
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-09, bor-sec-12, bor-sec-13
- **centrality**: important

#### 39. Zeitvertreib
- **german_lemma**: Zeitvertreib
- **mr_translation**: passing the time
- **definition**: The counter-move that drives boredom off and "drives time on". Mansikka reads the contemporary entertainment industry's continuous driving-away of boredom as foreclosing the very possibility profound boredom might open (bor-sec-18, pp.267-268).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-09, bor-sec-12, bor-sec-13, bor-sec-18
- **centrality**: important

#### 40. Augenblick
- **german_lemma**: Augenblick
- **mr_translation**: moment of vision
- **definition**: The Kierkegaardian-derived instant toward which profound boredom impels Dasein; the temporal character of being-held-in-limbo. Gibbs makes it the title concept of an educational reading (bor-sec-07).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-07, bor-sec-12, bor-sec-13, bor-sec-55
- **centrality**: important

#### 41. Gebanntheit
- **german_lemma**: Gebanntheit / Bann
- **mr_translation**: entrancement
- **definition**: Being held spellbound by time's single threefold horizon; the temporal character of being-left-empty. Quaranta's "Temporal Entrancement" is her own cinematic gloss and not a citation of this term — a distinction the cluster records rather than smooths over (bor-sec-10).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-12, bor-sec-13
- **centrality**: important

#### 42. Versagen
- **german_lemma**: Versagen
- **mr_translation**: telling refusal
- **definition**: Beings' refusal of themselves that is simultaneously a telling — the proto-functional structure in Heidegger's own text. Slaby reconstructs "telling refusal is a telling" faithfully and judges its activist payoff over-extended (bor-sec-13); Slaby & Thonhauser politicize its addressee into a Volk (bor-sec-55).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-13, bor-sec-55
- **centrality**: important

#### 43. Dasein
- **german_lemma**: Dasein
- **mr_translation**: Dasein (being-there)
- **definition**: The entity for whom being is an issue. Its individualist construction is the cluster's live problem: Hughes shows a society-wide simultaneous withdrawal has no place in it (bor-sec-08), Slaby & Thonhauser record Heidegger's own Volk-level reframing (bor-sec-55).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-01, bor-sec-02, bor-sec-08, bor-sec-42, bor-sec-45, bor-sec-51, bor-sec-55
- **centrality**: core

#### 44. das Man
- **german_lemma**: das Man
- **mr_translation**: the "they" / the they-self
- **definition**: The anonymous everyone into whose averageness Dasein falls. Mansikka's pedagogical point: a student's outward engagement and success are fully compatible with governance by das Man rather than an authentic self-relation — engagement and authenticity are independent axes (bor-sec-18, p.264).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-18, bor-sec-55
- **centrality**: important

#### 45. Gestell
- **german_lemma**: Gestell
- **mr_translation**: enframing
- **definition**: The technological mode of revealing that sets everything in order as standing-reserve. The organizing term of the cluster's technology-and-education sub-strand (Aroles & Küpers, Mertel, Thomson), where its homogenizing effect on curricula is the nearest analogue to monotony.
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-15, bor-sec-17, bor-sec-19
- **centrality**: important

#### 46. Bestand
- **german_lemma**: Bestand
- **mr_translation**: standing-reserve
- **definition**: What enframing reduces entities to: optimizable resource. Thomson's account of students and teachers reduced to meaningless optimizable resources produces an "ontological drift toward meaninglessness" (bor-sec-19).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-17, bor-sec-19
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 47. Eigentlichkeit
- **german_lemma**: Eigentlichkeit
- **mr_translation**: authenticity / ownedness
- **definition**: Owning one's existence as one's own. The axis on which the cluster's sharpest intra-volume tension turns: whether angst or profound boredom is the authenticity-disclosing Grundstimmung (DA-10).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-18, bor-sec-50, bor-sec-55
- **centrality**: important

#### 48. Paideia
- **greek_lemma**: παιδεία
- **transliteration**: paideia
- **mr_translation**: education as soul-transformation
- **definition**: Education as the transformation of the soul in its entirety, recovered against the competency-accumulation model. Thomson's and Mansikka's shared pivot for reading Heidegger's ontological education.
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-18, bor-sec-19
- **centrality**: important

#### 49. Bildung
- **german_lemma**: Bildung
- **mr_translation**: formation / cultivation
- **definition**: Formation, set against both the 19th-century "cultivation of subjective qualities" misreading that Heidegger dismisses and the instrumental metrics model. Gibbs opposes quality-metrics education to Rortyan edification and Bildung-as-flourishing (bor-sec-07).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-07, bor-sec-19
- **centrality**: important

#### 50. Pathos
- **greek_lemma**: πάθος
- **transliteration**: pathos
- **mr_translation**: affect / being-affected
- **definition**: Sheehan's argument that πάθος, de-psychologized and read as facticity, is die Sache selbst of Heidegger's entire project. Drawn from the metaphysical/psychological lineage (De Anima, Metaphysics), NOT from Rhetoric II's taxonomy of persuasive emotions — a productive adjacency to the dissertation's rhetorical project, not an overlap (bor-sec-46).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-46, bor-sec-47
- **centrality**: important

#### 51. Hedone
- **greek_lemma**: ἡδονή
- **transliteration**: hedone
- **mr_translation**: pleasure
- **definition**: Read by Keane, via Heidegger's 1924 Aristotle lectures, as a mode of self-having/Befindlichkeit rather than a sensation — the disclosive structure FCM later specializes into boredom's forms (bor-sec-47).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-47
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 52. Kairos
- **greek_lemma**: καιρός
- **transliteration**: kairos
- **mr_translation**: the opportune moment
- **definition**: The right or opportune time as against chronological duration. Tömmel's love-as-passion analysis makes kairotic temporality the structural inverse of boredom's dragging chronos (bor-sec-53).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-53
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 53. Enthymeme
- **greek_lemma**: ἐνθύμημα
- **transliteration**: enthymema
- **mr_translation**: enthymeme
- **definition**: The rhetorical syllogism proceeding from endoxa rather than necessary premises. Part of Keane's reconstruction of Heidegger's rehabilitation of Aristotle's Rhetoric as "the first systematic hermeneutic" of everyday being-with-others (BT SZ 138) — high independent relevance to the dissertation's Part I (bor-sec-47).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-47
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 54. Irre
- **german_lemma**: die Irre
- **mr_translation**: errancy
- **definition**: The turning-away constitutive of truth's double concealment. Vallega-Neu's thesis that bodily disposition is constitutive of attunement collapses any clean separation between truth-revealing and errancy-prone attunements (bor-sec-52).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-52
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 55. Aletheia
- **greek_lemma**: ἀλήθεια
- **transliteration**: aletheia
- **mr_translation**: unconcealment / truth
- **definition**: Truth as unconcealment, constituted by the double concealment of mystery and errancy. The frame within which Vallega-Neu locates bodily disposition (bor-sec-52).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-19, bor-sec-52
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 56. Leib
- **german_lemma**: Leib / Körper
- **mr_translation**: lived body / objective body
- **definition**: The lived-versus-objective body distinction. Vallega-Neu's "bodying forth" makes bodily disposition constitutive of attunement — the cluster's only sustained treatment of embodiment, and a standing gap in the otherwise disembodied Heideggerian boredom literature (bor-sec-52).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-52
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 57. Volk
- **german_lemma**: Volk
- **mr_translation**: a people
- **definition**: The collective addressee onto which Slaby & Thonhauser show Heidegger reframing profound boredom's Grundstimmung, whose demand becomes collective self-founding rather than individual resoluteness — with the political entanglement that follows (bor-sec-55).
- **type**: TERM
- **units**: bor-sec-55
- **centrality**: important

#### 58. Formal indication
- **german_lemma**: formale Anzeige
- **mr_translation**: formal indication
- **definition**: Heidegger's methodological device by which a concept points toward a phenomenon to be enacted rather than defining it for external verification. Elpidorou & Freeman identify it as Heidegger's explicit resistance to having his concepts checked against empirical psychology — and proceed to check anyway, arguing the check is legitimate (bor-sec-04, p.16). The formal statement of the measurement wall.
- **type**: METHOD
- **units**: bor-sec-04, bor-sec-47, bor-sec-48
- **centrality**: core

#### 59. Awakening not ascertaining
- **german_lemma**: Wecken
- **mr_translation**: awakening (a mood), as against ascertaining
- **definition**: The methodological claim that a fundamental attunement must be let-become-wakeful rather than established as present-at-hand. Hadjioannou makes the structurally identical argument for angst: reflective access distorts the affect it targets (bor-sec-48). The cluster's principled reason that self-report is not a neutral instrument. Bridges to fcm-03.
- **type**: METHOD
- **units**: bor-sec-04, bor-sec-48, bor-sec-55
- **centrality**: core

#### 60. Minimal reporting guideline
- **mr_translation**: eye-tracking minimal reporting guideline
- **definition**: Holmqvist et al.'s empirically grounded standard for what an eye-tracking study must report (sampling rate, calibration procedure, apparatus specification, fixation-detection algorithm). The cluster's methodological authority for any Part III eye-tracking work; it also documents that VR-integrated trackers have measurably worse precision and 45-81ms added latency versus glasses-based trackers (bor-sec-36).
- **type**: METHOD
- **units**: bor-sec-36
- **centrality**: important

#### 61. Deficit versus functional boredom
- **mr_translation**: boredom-as-deficit <-> boredom-as-functional/adaptive
- **definition**: DA-01. Whether boredom is a failure state or an adaptive regulatory signal. Resolved by partition rather than verdict: state boredom functional, trait boredom a dysfunction of the same mechanism (bor-sec-26). See debate-map.md.
- **type**: DEBATE-AXIS
- **units**: bor-sec-05, bor-sec-06, bor-sec-07, bor-sec-08, bor-sec-13, bor-sec-16, bor-sec-20, bor-sec-25, bor-sec-26, bor-sec-32
- **centrality**: core

#### 62. Is profound boredom boredom
- **mr_translation**: profound boredom is boredom <-> a distinct existential mood
- **definition**: DA-02. Whether FCM's third form is continuous with ordinary boredom or sui generis. Elpidorou & Freeman construct the sui-generis reading and reject it, landing on a qualified continuity (bor-sec-04); Mihačević stakes a middle position (bor-sec-09). See debate-map.md.
- **type**: DEBATE-AXIS
- **units**: bor-sec-03, bor-sec-04, bor-sec-05, bor-sec-09, bor-sec-10
- **centrality**: core

#### 63. Angst versus boredom as Grundstimmung
- **mr_translation**: angst <-> profound boredom as the ground attunement
- **definition**: DA-03. Which mood occupies the disclosive ground role. Flagged in Elpidorou & Freeman (bor-sec-02, p.662 n.2), unresolved in McManus, who never raises boredom as a candidate (bor-sec-50), and answered for boredom by Withy (bor-sec-51). See debate-map.md.
- **type**: DEBATE-AXIS
- **units**: bor-sec-02, bor-sec-45, bor-sec-50, bor-sec-51
- **centrality**: core

#### 64. Self-report versus physiological ground truth
- **mr_translation**: self-report <-> physiological marker as ground truth
- **definition**: DA-04. Which measure adjudicates when they disagree. Sharpened by Kim et al.'s null correlation (bor-sec-28) and Raffaelli et al.'s directional-fractionation hypothesis (bor-sec-21); given a principled basis by the awakening/ascertaining distinction. The cluster's most consequential axis for Part III. See debate-map.md.
- **type**: DEBATE-AXIS
- **units**: bor-sec-16, bor-sec-21, bor-sec-22, bor-sec-24, bor-sec-28, bor-sec-35
- **centrality**: core

#### 65. Frontal asymmetry marker versus arousal confound
- **mr_translation**: FEA-as-boredom-marker <-> confound-with-arousal
- **definition**: DA-05. Whether frontal alpha asymmetry indexes boredom or general arousal. Constructed by the cluster from three units that never cite one another on the point (bor-sec-30, bor-sec-27, bor-sec-31). See debate-map.md.
- **type**: DEBATE-AXIS
- **units**: bor-sec-27, bor-sec-30, bor-sec-31
- **centrality**: core

#### 66. Unified versus heterogeneous boredom
- **mr_translation**: boredom as unified construct <-> heterogeneous family
- **definition**: DA-06. Whether "boredom" names one thing or many. Raffaelli et al. stay diagnostic, treating field-wide inconsistency as compatible with either non-standardized operationalization or genuine subtype heterogeneity, while committing to taxonomy as the forward path (bor-sec-21); Darling dissolves rather than adjudicates it (bor-sec-20). See debate-map.md.
- **type**: DEBATE-AXIS
- **units**: bor-sec-06, bor-sec-20, bor-sec-21
- **centrality**: important

#### 67. Monotony versus meaning causation
- **mr_translation**: monotony/environment-caused <-> meaning/agent-caused
- **definition**: DA-07. Whether boredom is caused from outside by a determinate boring thing or arises from Dasein itself. Staged unresolved in Heidegger's own first-versus-later-forms asymmetry (bor-sec-03); Hughes lands on a hybrid (bor-sec-08). See debate-map.md.
- **type**: DEBATE-AXIS
- **units**: bor-sec-03, bor-sec-08, bor-sec-16
- **centrality**: important

#### 68. Individual versus collective attunement
- **mr_translation**: individually structured <-> collectively induced boredom
- **definition**: DA-08. Whether boredom's phenomenology presupposes an individual withdrawing against an absorbed background. Hughes shows the individualist framework has no place for simultaneous society-wide withdrawal (bor-sec-08); Slaby & Thonhauser document Heidegger's own Volk-level reframing (bor-sec-55). Directly consequential for cohort-level Part III designs. See debate-map.md.
- **type**: DEBATE-AXIS
- **units**: bor-sec-08, bor-sec-14, bor-sec-55
- **centrality**: core
