# bor-sec-09 — Mihačević, Borislav (2022), "Boredom That Wishes Not To Be"

**Type:** READING/CLAIM · **Discipline:** Continental philosophy / Heidegger studies · **Depth:** deep (Strand A) · **Corpus-role:** explicator-critic of FCM's threefold boredom analysis who stakes an original diagnosis of Heidegger's "sublimation" of the phenomenon in a truth/meaning conflation, rather than (per Svendsen) in an ontic/ontological confusion.

**Citation:** Mihačević, Borislav. 2022. "Boredom That Wishes Not To Be." *Diametros* 19(72): 26–43. doi:10.33392/diam.1744.

---

## 1. Thesis/Aim

Mihačević sets out to defend and refine a charge already current in the secondary literature: that Heidegger's 1929/30 account of boredom involves a "sublimation or transgression" of the phenomenon beyond its own phenomenological limits (FE, p.26). He agrees with the general charge — Heidegger does overreach the ontic description of boredom — but rejects the standard explanation of *why*. Where Svendsen locates the overreach in Heidegger's privileging of the ontological question of the meaning of Being over the ontic commonness of bored life, Mihačević argues the deeper fault lies in Heidegger's failure to separate the *truth* of Being from the *meaning* (or sense) of Being (FE, p.29). Because Heidegger's own framework treats meaning and truth of Being as the same thing (FAITHFUL-Mihacevic, p.29, citing Heidegger 1949), the compelling, binding character that properly belongs only to truth gets illegitimately carried over onto meaning — which, the author argues in his own closing analysis, is something we are *free* to make or withhold (FE, p.41). That conflation, not the ontic/ontological confusion Svendsen diagnoses, is what authorizes boredom's "stretching" beyond its descriptive limits.

## 2. Structure

- **§1 Introduction (pp.26–29):** pathos/paschein and thaumazein as the *arche* of ancient philosophizing versus anxiety (*Angst*) as the *arche* of Heidegger's fundamental ontology; the claim that profound boredom occupies, for Heidegger, the same structural position anxiety occupies in *Being and Time*; a survey of skeptical secondary voices (Ciocan, Elpidorou & Freeman, Svendsen, Gibbs) and the author's own qualified agreement with each.
- **§2 Preliminary considerations of metaphysics (pp.29–31):** the three Novalis-derived considerations — world (homesickness), finitude, individuation (solitude) — that frame the 1929/30 course before boredom itself is analyzed.
- **§3 The awakening of attunement (pp.31–36):** the demand to "let boredom be awake" rather than making it conscious; the *becoming-bored*/*being-bored* distinction; brisk recapitulations of the first form (station/waiting), second form (dinner party/Mitsein), and third form ("it is boring for one"/indifference of beings); the closing turn to Das Man, technological rationality, and the contemporary refusal of boredom.
- **§4 Conclusion (pp.36–41):** the three metaphysical questions (world, finitude, individuation) reprised through profound boredom, then two further "hidden" questions — freedom and truth — derived from the FCM's own excursus on animal poverty-in-world, the *logos*, and *aletheia*; a critique of Elpidorou and Freeman's claim that all three forms share a "lack of meaning" and "altered perception of time"; the author's counter-claim that only the second form is genuine loss of *personal* meaning; the culminating truth/meaning distinction and a closing citation of Jean-Luc Nancy on thinking and sense.

## 3. Constructs

The article is a textual-philosophical argument with no empirical apparatus; every construct below is treated exegetically, not operationalized.

- **Profound/deep boredom (FCM 3rd form)** — the paper's central object. Treated as "it is boring for one," the indifference of beings as a whole, and the site where Heidegger derives *aletheia* and freedom from the boredom analysis (FAITHFUL-Mihacevic, pp.35–39).
- **Being-bored-by (1st form)** — glossed briefly via the classic waiting/passing-the-time structure; used mainly as the baseline against which the second and third forms are shown to be progressively less situation-bound (FAITHFUL-Mihacevic, p.32).
- **Being-bored-with (2nd form)** — given fuller treatment via the party example; read through *Being and Time*'s *Mitsein*, and identified as the form in which personal meaning is genuinely lost (FAITHFUL-Mihacevic, pp.33–34, 40).
- **Situational vs. trait/state boredom** — the author explicitly borrows Elpidorou and Freeman's "state boredom" (1st form) / "trait boredom" (2nd form) labels to mark the same distinction (FAITHFUL-Mihacevic, p.32 n.50, p.34 n.58).
- **Monotony/meaninglessness** — reworked as the paper's central interpretive stake: whether "loss of meaning" is uniform across all three forms (Svendsen, Gibbs, Elpidorou & Freeman) or differentiated (the author's own view) (FE, pp.40–41).
- **Temporality of boredom (time-drag)** — extensively reworked in §4: the author contests a linear, ecstatic-repetition reading of the third form's altered time-sense, proposing instead a *nunc stans* ("standing now") / Aristotelian *noeseos noesis* ("thinking of thinking") model (FE, pp.39–40).
- **Flow/engagement (contrast pole)** — touched only obliquely, via *Gelassenheit* (non-willing, meditative thinking) posed against the "calculative, planned thinking" of everyday busyness (FAITHFUL-Mihacevic, p.39); not developed as an empirical contrast.
- Not treated: boredom proneness, mind-wandering/task-unrelated thought, attentional disengagement/meta-awareness, boredom-as-regulatory-signal.

## 4. Reading of FCM

Mihačević does not re-derive Heidegger's argument; the cluster's FCM units already carry that ground-level exposition (`fcm-04-first-form`, `fcm-05-second-form`, `fcm-06-third-form`, `fcm-07-particular-profound`). His own contribution is threefold.

First, he **faithfully compresses** the three-form architecture — the station (fcm-04), the dinner party (fcm-05), and "it is boring for one" (fcm-06) — retaining Heidegger's two structural primitives, being-held-in-limbo and being-left-empty, across all three (FAITHFUL-Mihacevic, pp.32–35). His closing conclusion on technological rationality and the contemporary refusal of boredom (pp.35–36) tracks fcm-07's diagnosis of profound boredom as the fundamental attunement of *contemporary* Dasein, though he does not name §§37–38 directly.

Second, he **extends** FCM's own three questions (world, finitude, individuation) by tracing, through Part Two's discussion of animal poverty-in-world and the *logos*, two further questions Heidegger's course implies but does not name as such: freedom and truth (FE, pp.37–38). This is presented as drawing out something latent in Heidegger's own text, not as an external addition.

Third, and most sharply, he **contests** a natural reading of fcm-06's temporal analysis. Where the third form's *Augenblick*/entrancement structure could be read (via *Being and Time*'s ecstatic temporality) as a kind of repetition, Mihačević argues profound boredom "knows no future or past since its very nature is recurrence" in a *non*-repetitive sense — closer to medieval *nunc stans* or Aristotelian *noeseos noesis* than to any linear temporal structure (FE, pp.39–40). He explicitly rejects treating this as a hidden ground of the *history* of Dasein, in tension with fcm-06's own framing of the third form as the temporal core of the whole boredom analysis (contests-reading-of, targeting fcm-06-third-form).

## 5. Findings/Claims

1. Profound boredom cannot be an "entirely alien form" of boredom, because it still exhibits the shared structural pair (being-held-in-limbo, being-left-empty) that defines the first and second forms — hence the article's title formula, "boredom that wishes not to be boredom" (FE, p.39).
2. Two further metaphysical questions — "What is freedom?" and "What is truth?" — are implicit in Heidegger's own excursus on animal poverty-in-world and *aletheia*, alongside the course's announced triad of world/finitude/individuation (FE, pp.37–38).
3. Contra a repetition-based reading of the third form's altered temporality, the phenomenon is better modeled as *nunc stans*/*noeseos noesis* — a non-linear, meditative "standing now" rather than an ecstatic return (FE, pp.39–40).
4. The claim that all three forms share a uniform "loss of meaning" (attributed to Svendsen and to Elpidorou & Freeman) is rejected: the first form only *suspends* personal meaning (retrieved once the boring situation ends); the second form is the sole site of genuine loss of personal meaning; the third form negates *personal* meaning but not meaning-as-such (FE, pp.40–41).
5. The paper's central diagnosis: Heidegger's sublimation of boredom stems from identifying the truth of Being with the meaning of Being. Truth is binding (we cannot simply choose not to be addressed by *aletheia*); meaning/sense is not binding (we are "free to make or not sense out of something," p.41). Reading profound boredom's compelling, overpowering character as *meaning* rather than *truth* is, on this account, the specific mechanism of Heidegger's overreach (FE, p.41).

## 7. Analytical role

*(Every claim in this subsection is our own reading, tagged `anticipatory-application`; none is asserted by the source itself.)*

Mihačević's truth/meaning distinction supplies Part III with a conceptual tool for separating two senses in which a participant's disengagement might be "meaningless": a compelled, binding withdrawal (structurally closer to profound boredom, where the world's address is refused but not optional) versus a freely-elected disengagement or lapse of interest (closer to his sense of "meaning" as something one is free to make or withhold) (P/anticipatory-application). This distinction could sharpen how Part III's survey analysis frames the desktop-3D versus video-only strata and the lab-VR versus deployment-2D-PC poles: a participant reporting "boredom" under low-agency deployment conditions may be reporting something closer to a *compelled* (truth-like) withdrawal, whereas a participant in a richer, more optional engagement context may be reporting a *freely-withheld* (meaning-like) one — two phenomenologically distinct reports that a single self-report boredom scale would flatten (P/anticipatory-application).

Separately, the paper's reading of the second form — boredom "arising from Dasein itself" despite full outward social participation at the party — offers phenomenological vocabulary for the VLE correction already logged elsewhere in this project: spatial co-presence and group-assignment can coexist with a "WE unmet want," i.e., surface engagement masking an unmet deeper want, structurally analogous to Mihačević's dinner-party case where "I was bored after all" surfaces only in retrospect despite the evening being enjoyable (P/anticipatory-application). This does not license importing Heidegger's *Mitsein* apparatus wholesale into the survey analysis; it only suggests that "reported enjoyment" and "structural disengagement" are not mutually exclusive findings, and that Part III's instruments should not treat them as such (P/anticipatory-application).
