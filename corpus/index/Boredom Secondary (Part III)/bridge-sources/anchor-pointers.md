### What the table shows

The two right-hand columns are **complementary, not overlapping**:

- 21 units bridge to FCM · 30 ground a dataset channel · **5 do both**
- but of those 5 — bor-sec-03, 04, 06, 11, 16 — **every one grounds only the unspecified whole-dataset.**
- **15 units ground a specific hard channel** (EEG / pupil / gaze / blink / ML). **Not one of them bridges to FCM.**

> **`bridges-to-fcm` ∩ `grounds a hard channel` = ∅.**

Those 15 are exactly `bor-sec-27`–`bor-sec-41`: **the whole of Strand D (7) plus the whole of Strand E (8), and nothing
else in the cluster.** The instrument boundary and the strand boundary are the same line.

**The precise form of the claim — first-party operation, not contact.** Two FCM-bridged units do make contact with a
hard instrument, and the claim must be stated so as not to hide them:

| Unit | Instrument touched | How |
|---|---|---|
| `bor-sec-06` (Elpidorou 2023) | behavioural (RT / errors) | **cites** Danckert & Allman (2005) duration-estimation-error — literature-cited, not first-party data |
| `bor-sec-16` (Feldges 2020) | heart rate / HRV | **cites** London et al. (1972) on autonomic arousal — literature-cited, and Feldges himself flags the evidence as unclear |

Neither administers anything. So the exact claim is: **no FCM-bridged unit *operates* a hard instrument first-party.**
Two *cite* one. These two are not a leak in the finding — they are already in the Phase 3 record as the two
non-self-report edges reaching the FCM-form rows (`_synthesis/graph-construct-measure-density.mmd`, edges labelled
`1 (lit. survey)` → heart rate and `1 (cited)` → behavioural). The FCM forms remain reachable by **self-report and
nothing else** in the only sense a study design cares about: something you could actually run.

This is the cluster's central finding arriving by a fourth route, and the cheapest one to check: it is visible in the
shape of the pointer table itself. The FCM column empties exactly where the hard-channel column fills, at the
Strand C/D boundary — `bor-sec-26` is the last unit with an FCM-adjacent identity, `bor-sec-27` the first with an
instrument, and nothing spans them. (The other three routes: profound-boredom × hard instrument = ∅; the strand
citation matrix, where D and E are citationally isolated; and the concordance's empty FCM-form rows. See
`_synthesis/boredom-construct-measure-concordance.md` §2 and `_synthesis/citation-network.md` §2.)

The consequence for Part III is in the graph as `WALL ==> P3M`: **the design has no precedent in these 55 sources.**
Nothing here instruments profound boredom. That is a contribution to claim, not a gap to conceal.
