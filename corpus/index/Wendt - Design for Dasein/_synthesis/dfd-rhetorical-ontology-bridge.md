# dfd — Rhetorical-Ontology Bridge (keystone)

**What this is.** The load-bearing payload of the Wendt entry: a mapping of Wendt's phenomenological/post-phenomenological **design** concepts onto the dissertation's **actualization-of-desire chain** (A₀→A₄), so that any design decision about a virtual environment can be *located at the chain node it intervenes on* — and so the environment can be analyzed and (re)designed with the rhetorical ontology as the spine and Wendt as the design-side lens.

**Provenance discipline (three voices, kept distinct).**
- **Wendt-says** — reported from the units; carries a unit + locus `(p.N / pdf N)`.
- **RO-mapping** — the dissertation's actualization-of-desire chain; the user's own construction.
- **Environment-application** — applying the mapping to a virtual environment (the BME VLE seed, or game VLEs).
Every RO-mapping and environment-application claim below is **`provenance: anticipatory-application`** — the dissertation's interpretive construction, *not* a claim Wendt makes. Wendt is Heideggerian/phenomenological and design-applied; the chain's Aristotelian spine is the dissertation's. The two genuinely meet at **being-in-the-world** (Wendt designs *for* it; the RO culminates emotion/action *as* its "affective architecture").

---

## 1. The spine (RO recap, in one breath)
The actualization-of-desire chain: **A₀** (sensible object + *aisthētikon* within the motion/time horizon) → **M₀→A₁** (completed perception + its residual hedonic/discriminative tone) → **M₁→A₂** (the *phantasma* — the image *taken-as*) → **M₂→A₃** (completed cognitive actuality: the three orientational modes + *doxa*'s ratification + emotion) → **M₃→A₄** (the *orektikon* motion: species-specific desire mobilizes the body into completed action / *praxis*), which **sediments back** through *hexis* into the dispositional ground of the next pass (the diachronic *pathetic* loop). RODA reads this chain *in motion*; this bridge locates **design levers** at each node.

## 3. Node by node

### A₀/A₁ — the perceptual encounter and its medium  `anticipatory-application`
The chain begins where the object meets the *aisthētikon*. Wendt's post-phenomenology says the **medium is not a neutral conduit but a constitutive mediator**: "mediators help to constitute what is real for us," not intermediaries that merely convey (dfd-04, p.96/pdf 98), and every technology **amplifies while it reduces** — the telescope shows the far and hides the near (dfd-05, Ihde, p.127/pdf 129). On the RO, this is exactly the A₀/A₁ claim that the medium *structures* the perceptual motion and the hedonic/discriminative tone it deposits. Wendt adds the affordance refinement: the object is **perceived according to its possible actions before it is named** (dfd-05, Gibson, p.130/pdf 132) — i.e., the sensible object arrives already action-tinged, which is the RO's claim that A₁ deposits a discriminative-pursuit tone, not a neutral datum.
- **Design lever:** raise A₀/A₁ legibility and presence; decide deliberately what the medium amplifies vs. reduces.
- **Seed (BME VLE):** the environment is *doubly mediated* — the VR room mediates a *screen* that mediates *360° clinical footage*. The seed read shows the frictions land precisely here: **darkness depresses the A₁ tone** (the scene "barely seen"), **occlusion and required proximity gate whether a perception actualizes at all**, and **buffer latency breaks the object's availability** (`bme-vr-env-analysis/bme-vle-read.md` §5).

### M₁→A₂ — the *phantasma*, the taking-as  `anticipatory-application`
A₂ is the image *taken-as* — making-present-as. Wendt's embodiment chapter is the design-native account of this: design should articulate **what it means for an object *to be*** rather than what it *is* — "from a metaphysics of design to a phenomenology of design" (dfd-05, p.133/pdf 135) — which is "designing the *as*." And the tool analysis supplies the condition under which the *phantasma*, not the apparatus, is what shows up: in the **ready-to-hand** mode the equipment **withdraws** (dfd-01, p.28/pdf 30); a *transparent* (not *invisible*) interface lets the medium recede yet return when needed (dfd-05, p.146/pdf 148).
- **Design lever:** design the taking-as directly (framing, point-of-view, annotation, gaze-anchoring); pursue transparency so the medium withdraws into A₂ rather than obtruding at A₀/A₁.
- **Seed:** the **360°+POV "two views"** are an A₂ design — built so the clinical scene is taken *as* something one is *inside of* (the practitioner's standpoint), not merely watched; the "Hide POV" toggle is a withdrawal control.

### M₂→A₃ — *doxa*'s ratification and emotion  `anticipatory-application`
A₃ completes the cognitive actuality: the *phantasma* is engaged, *doxa* ratifies an aspect as true, and emotion is the being-moved keyed to that appearance. Wendt's meaning-formation account maps cleanly: **sense vs meaning** — something makes *sense* in one context; its *meaning* is the sum of imaginable contexts (dfd-05, p.137/pdf 139) — and the **interface is an active mediation** producing an emergent user-system "third entity" (dfd-05, Hookway, p.143/pdf 145). Crucially for a *learning* environment, Wendt insists **meaning, not utility, is the ultimate concern** (dfd-06, p.163/pdf 165). On the RO this is the A₃ moment where the disclosed content is *ratified* and *charged* — and it is the hinge of **engagement vs boredom**.
- **Design lever:** scaffold the ratifying *doxa* and its affective charge; design for meaning-formation, not mere task-completion.
- **Seed:** *needs-finding is an A₃ act* — the student must ratify "this is an unmet clinical need" (a *doxa*) and be *moved* by it. The **Interviews tab** supplies the *doxa*-laden evaluative framing (clinicians naming what is scarce, hard, rural-inaccessible). This is where the **FCM-boredom** signatures live (`king-salvo-digest-pointer.md`): dark, occluded, latent, passive long-form watching is a first-form-boredom risk surface that depresses A₃ charge.

### A₃→A₄ — completed action (the keystone)  `anticipatory-application`
The *orektikon* motion mobilizes desire into completed action. This is where the bridge meets **RODA's keystone** — *rhetorical incorporation*, the A₃→A₄ substantial union (see `../bridge-sources/roda-crosswalk.md`). Wendt names the design stance that lets this complete: design is the **enacting of a plan** — "doing philosophy with the hands" (dfd-00, p.6/pdf 8) — and a **transparent (not invisible) interface keeps the user *active***, where the invisible-interface fantasy and "standing reserve" render the user *passive* (dfd-05, p.146/pdf 148; dfd-01, p.42/pdf 44). On the RO, a chain that completes into committed action is the engaged case; a chain that stalls before A₄ is the bored/disengaged case.
- **Design lever:** design so the chain *completes* into action the participant owns; resist passivity-inducing "frictionless" design that severs the participant from the act.
- **Seed:** completed acts are note-taking, co-present peer discussion, and downstream BME design proposals.

### A₄ + loop — *hexis* and the diachronic register  `anticipatory-application`
Completed action sediments back through *hexis* into the ground of the next pass. Wendt's process concepts live here: **problem-solution co-evolution *in use*** (dfd-03) — the problem space is discovered by acting, not specified in advance; **multistability and creative misuse** (dfd-04, p.111/pdf 113) — the environment will be taken up in unplanned ways; and **"care has to be designed into being"** (dfd-02, Fry, p.61/pdf 63). The RO's *pathetic* loop — each actualization reshaping the dispositional ground — is the diachronic register Wendt gestures at with **design for coping and breakdown** (dfd-01, p.34/pdf 36): breakdown is not failure but the revelatory moment that re-forms the disposition.
- **Design lever:** design the task/learning-loop for repetition that sediments the desired *hexis*; treat breakdown as designable; expect and harvest creative misuse.
- **Seed:** across repeated sessions the environment cultivates a **clinical-observer *hexis*** — learning to *see* clinically — which is the diachronic learning outcome the design is for.

## 4. The keystone thesis
**Designing a virtual environment = designing the conditions under which the participant's actualization-of-desire chain runs (engaged) or stalls (bored).** `provenance: anticipatory-application`

Wendt and the rhetorical ontology converge at **being-in-the-world**: Wendt's whole project is *designing for Dasein* (design as a modern mode of being-in-the-world, dfd-00, p.12/pdf 14), and the RO culminates emotion/action *as* "the affective architecture of being-in-the-world." So the design question is not "is the interface usable?" but "**does the environment's design let the participant's chain actualize all the way to owned action, and sediment the right disposition?**" RODA answers *whether and how the chain ran* (behavior-in-motion; the A₃→A₄ incorporation keystone); this bridge answers *what in the design afforded or foreclosed it at each node* — and hands the manual (`dfd-vle-design-manual.md`) the levers to redesign it.

## 6. Limits / open problems
- The mapping is a **homology, not an identity**: Wendt's concepts are Heideggerian-applied; forcing one-to-one equivalence with Aristotelian *aisthēsis*/*phantasia*/*doxa*/*orexis* would overreach. Each row is a *design-lever location*, not a metaphysical equation.
- Wendt himself **refuses an operational methodology** (dfd-06): the levers here are diagnostic + prescriptive *stances*, not a procedure. Operationalization is the manual's job — and remains partly open (see manual §E).
- **Empirical** confirmation (do these levers actually move engagement/boredom?) awaits the 4 quarters of student data; until then every application is a *hypothesis*.
