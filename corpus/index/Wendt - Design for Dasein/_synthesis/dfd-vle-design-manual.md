# dfd — VLE Design Manual (payload A)

**What this is.** An operational instrument that turns Wendt's *Design for Dasein* into criteria, questions, and moves for **analyzing and (re)designing virtual environments** (learning and game). Use it *with* the keystone `dfd-rhetorical-ontology-bridge.md`: the bridge locates design at chain nodes; this manual supplies the criteria (A), the diagnostic grid (B), the mediation typology (C), the redesign playbook (D), and the limits (E). All application claims are **`provenance: anticipatory-application`** (the dissertation's construction, not Wendt's). Loci are `(p.N / pdf N)`.

---

## A. Wendt's design principles as criteria
Distilled and de-duplicated across the units into ten criteria. Each is a *stance to design by* — not a checklist item to "pass."

1. **Design for the *potential* of experience, not its dictation.** Experience is personal and emergent; you raise the probability of qualitative outcomes, you do not author them (dfd-00, p.13/pdf 15).
2. **Treat the medium as a constitutive mediator.** A medium co-shapes what is real and always *amplifies while it reduces*; choose what it foregrounds and hides (dfd-04, p.96/pdf 98; dfd-05, p.127/pdf 129).
3. **Design the verb — what a thing *does*, via affordances perceived before naming** (dfd-05, p.133/pdf 135; p.130/pdf 132).
4. **Pursue transparency, not invisibility/"intuition."** Let the medium withdraw in use yet return when needed; the invisible-interface fantasy makes users passive "standing reserve" (dfd-05, p.146/pdf 148; dfd-01, p.42/pdf 44).
5. **Design for coping and breakdown.** Breakdown is unavoidable and *revelatory*; support the movement between absorbed use (ready-to-hand) and deliberation (present-at-hand) (dfd-01, p.34/pdf 36).
6. **Fit individual sense to systemic meaning; meaning over mere utility.** Something makes *sense* in one context; its *meaning* is the sum of imaginable contexts — and a product need not be functionally perfect to matter (dfd-05, p.137/pdf 139; dfd-06, p.163/pdf 165).
7. **Co-evolve problem and solution; prototype to think; reject the waterfall.** The brief is a starting point for exploration; you discover the problem by making (dfd-03, p.76–79/pdf 78–81).
8. **Design *with* multistability and creative misuse, holding strong-but-bounded intent.** The environment will be taken up in unplanned ways; design for beneficial outcomes, not a single dictated use (dfd-04, p.111/pdf 113; dfd-06, p.162/pdf 164).
9. **Design *care* into being; cultivate the disposition; preserve specialization.** Keep makers (and learners) bound to outcomes; maintain enough focus that care is not spread thin (dfd-02, Fry, p.61/pdf 63; dfd-06, p.158/pdf 160).
10. **Objects are more than material — advocate for preferable worlds; delegate responsibly; refuse dark patterns** (dfd-06, p.162/pdf 164; dfd-04, p.101/pdf 103).

## B. Virtual-environment reading grid
Diagnostic questions, organized by chain node (so a reading slots straight into the bridge). Score each as *affords / neutral / forecloses* for the participant's chain.

**A0/A1 — perceptual encounter & medium** (criteria 1, 2)
- What does the medium **amplify**, and what does it **reduce**? Is the perceptual object legible (contrast, scale, latency, occlusion)?
- Does the object arrive **action-tinged** (perceived by what it affords) or as inert content?

**M1→A2 — the taking-as** (criteria 3, 4)
- Is the *taking-as* designed (framing, POV, annotation), or left to chance?
- Does the apparatus **withdraw** (transparent) so the object is present — and can it **return** to awareness without breaking the user?

**M2→A3 — ratification & emotion** (criteria 5, 6, 10)
- What ratifying **judgment** (*doxa*) is the design trying to occasion, and what **affective charge** carries it?
- Is the design optimizing **meaning** or only task-completion? Where is the **boredom risk** (monotony, passivity, latency)?

**A3→A4 — completion into action** (criteria 4, 7)
- Does the chain **complete** into action the participant *owns*, or stall before it? Does "frictionless" design sever the participant from the act?

**A4 + loop — disposition** (criteria 8, 9)
- What **hexis** does repeated use sediment? Does the design **support coping/breakdown** and **harvest creative misuse**?

### B.1 Worked reading — the BME clinical needs-finding VLE
(from `../bridge-sources/bme-vr-env-analysis/bme-vle-read.md`)
- **A0/A1 — forecloses (fixable):** double mediation (room→screen→360 footage) plus **darkness, occlusion by other avatars, required proximity, and buffer latency** depress the perceptual tone and gate whether the clinical scene actualizes at all.
- **A2 — affords:** the **360°+POV "two views"** design the taking-as (be *inside* the procedure); "Hide POV" is a withdrawal control. ✓
- **A3 — partially affords:** the **Procedures + Interviews** pairing can scaffold the needs-*doxa*, but passive long-form 360 watching in a dark room is a **first-form-boredom risk**.
- **A3→A4 — under-supported:** little in-world means to convert observation into an owned act (needs-capture, marking).
- **Loop — implicit:** repeated sessions could sediment a clinical-observer *hexis*, but nothing yet designs that repetition.

### B.2 Worked reading — one game element (G&G, illustrative)
The Gnomes & Goblins **companion creature that reacts to the player** is an **alterity** design (a quasi-other, §C) engineered for social/affective presence — a strong A3 charge (being-with a responsive other) that screen media cannot match. It is the contrast case for the **VR-vs-screen differential** (`../bridge-sources/game-vle-pointers.md`): where the BME VLE's avatars currently *occlude* (an alterity relation gone wrong → friction), G&G's creature *solicits* (alterity relation designed → engagement). (Illustrative; see Part II G&G materials for the worked analysis.)

## C. Ihde's mediation typology applied to VR/VLE
The four human–technology relations (dfd-04, p.87–89/pdf 89–91), turned into VR design questions:
- **Embodiment relation** — the rig (headset, controllers, avatar) experienced *through*; the world shows up, the apparatus withdraws (kin to ready-to-hand). *VR Q:* is the rig embodied/withdrawn, or obtruding? *BME VLE:* darkness/occlusion/latency push it toward obtrusion → A0/A1 foreclosure.
- **Hermeneutic relation** — the object is *read/interpreted* (you act *with* it): the media-player UI, the Procedures/Interviews menu, the scrub bar, the `E`/`Q` key model. *VR Q:* is the readable layer legible and quick to interpret?
- **Background relation** — shapes experience without focal awareness: room ambiance, lighting, audio, the persistent space. *VR Q:* does the background support or sabotage? *BME VLE:* the dark room is a background relation actively sabotaging A1.
- **Alterity relation** — technology as **quasi-other**: other users' avatars, companions, agents. *VR Q:* do co-present others register as quasi-others (social presence) or as obstacles? *BME VLE:* "our bodies are in the way" — alterity collapsing into occlusion.
- **Multistability** (dfd-04, p.111/pdf 113): the environment *is what it can do* across contexts; expect creative misuse and design for beneficial outcomes rather than one dictated path.

## D. (Re)design playbook — "what to design next" (by chain node)
Concrete moves, seeded from the BME VLE frictions; generalizable to any VLE.

**A0/A1 — restore the perceptual object**
- Raise legibility: lighting/contrast presets per room; larger or curved screens; higher-fidelity/closer default viewing pose.
- Defeat occlusion: dedicated viewing positions, "ghost"/fade co-located avatars near a screen, or teleport-to-view.
- Cut latency: pre-buffer on room-entry; show a load state so the wait is legible, not a dead object.

**M1→A2 — design the taking-as**
- Gaze- or controller-anchored POV; spotlight/annotation layers on the 360 scene; keep the "Hide POV" transparency control and make withdrawal/return one action.

**M2→A3 — scaffold the needs-*doxa* and its charge**
- Pair each **Procedure** with its **Interview** so the evaluative framing (what is scarce/hard) rides alongside the scene; add a reflective prompt ("what unmet need do you see?").
- Fight first-form boredom: segment long 360 clips, vary stimulus, give the learner something to *do* while watching (mark, compare).

**A3→A4 — let the chain complete**
- In-world **needs-capture** (mark a moment, tag a need, drop a note); make observation convertible into an owned artifact.
- Afford **peer discussion** at the point of observation (co-present annotation), turning being-with into shared ratification.

**A4 + loop — sediment the disposition**
- Spaced repetition across sessions toward a clinical-observer *hexis*; surface a learner's own prior marks; **harvest creative misuse** (unexpected paths) as design data, not error.

## E. Open problems / limits
- **Measuring meaning/engagement** is conceded-delicate (dfd-06); the design hypotheses here are testable only against the forthcoming 4 quarters of student data — until then they are hypotheses, not findings.
- **No turnkey methodology:** Wendt deliberately refuses one (dfd-06); this manual is criteria + moves, not a repeatable procedure.
- **Multistability & emergent goals** resist operationalization (dfd-04/06): the playbook designs *conditions*, not outcomes.
- **Ethics of mediation:** "advocating for preferable worlds" and refusing dark patterns (criterion 10) is asserted without a decision procedure.
- **Homology caveat:** the chain-node placements are *design-lever locations* (see the bridge §6), not metaphysical equations between Wendt's Heideggerian terms and the Aristotelian chain.
