## Why this crosswalk
Wendt's *Design for Dasein* and RODA are **two faces of one chain.** RODA reads the **actualization-of-desire chain in motion** — how a designed world moves a participant's desire through A₀→A₄ into action, culminating in *rhetorical incorporation* (the A₃→A₄ substantial union). Wendt supplies the complementary **design-conditions lens** — what an environment's design *affords or forecloses* at each node. RODA = **behavior-in-motion** (episode diagnosis); Wendt = **design-conditions** (environment critique + prescription). Both ride the dissertation's A₀→A₄ spine and the same Aristotelian–Heideggerian ontology, so they compose without translation.

## Division of labour (how to use them together)
- Use **RODA** to analyze *what a participant did* in a virtual environment — episode-level, behavior-in-motion, the chain as it actually ran.
- Use the **Wendt lens** (`dfd-rhetorical-ontology-bridge.md` + `dfd-vle-design-manual.md`) to analyze/critique *what the environment's design afforded or foreclosed* at each chain node, and to prescribe (re)design.
- **Together:** RODA diagnoses the chain; Wendt's lens diagnoses *and redesigns the conditions*. The BME VLE seed read (`bme-vr-env-analysis/bme-vle-read.md`) already shows both — chain dynamics **and** design frictions (darkness depressing A0/A1, occlusion gating A2, latency breaking availability).

**Preserved lock:** RODA holds **compulsion = *bia* ≠ addiction**; the Wendt lens does not import the looser "compulsion." Keep the three voices distinct: Wendt-says / RO-mapping / environment-application.
