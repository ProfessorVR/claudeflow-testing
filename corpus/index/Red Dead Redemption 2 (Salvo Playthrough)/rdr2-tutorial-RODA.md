## §0 — FRAME

- **Unit of analysis:** the **engineered *hexis*-bundle** — not a discrete act but a *cultivated disposition*, laid down by repetition across the protracted arc. The early game is RDR2's *paideia*: it builds the desiring, dwelling, morally-scored agent that the later capstone (the Sonny / homestead-assault scene, *outside* clips 1–16) will rupture and partially supplant.
- **Two interwoven hexeis:** a **technē-hexis** (skill — horsemanship, gunplay, tracking, heist-craft, minigame rhythm) and an **ethical-hexis** (disposition — gang-loyalty, belonging-through-labor, the honor-scored moral self, the outlaw-economy livelihood). Both are installed *in the same breath* from the very first interactive clip (clip 3b's tutorial threads three honor events through the control schema).
- **The orekton arc (the through-line desire-object, re-pitched three times):**
  1. **Blizzard-survival** (clips 1–4): the orekton is *food, warmth, shelter for the gang* — posited before the player holds the stick (clip 1, 03:49–05:01) and serviced by the hunt (clip 4, 00:58–02:10 scarcity → 07:52 the kill).
  2. **Standing / belonging** (clips 5–7, 6a–d): the orekton becomes *being-of-use within Dutch's gang* — the camp economy's "every day one of you… the camp gets its slice" (clip 5, 65:00–70:06), the hail-by-name "Morning, Mr. Morgan!" (clip 6a, 00:00), donation-as-worth (clip 6d, 05:54–06:07).
  3. **The outlaw economy** (clips 8, 13, 16): the orekton matures into *theft-as-livelihood* — the bounty/wanted economy (clip 8), gambling's variable-reward pull (clip 13), and the fence-unlock that converts a one-off robbery into a standing income node (clip 16, 11:50–12:08).
- **Home-horizon (A₀) shift:** Colter (the blizzard cabin, clip 1, 05:18) → **Horseshoe Overlook** (the protracted home-base, installed clip 5, 65:00 "This place is perfect"). The whole bundle is dwelt *against* this fixed A₀.

## §1 — The TECHNĒ Cultivation (skill built beat-by-beat)

The technē-hexis is installed as a *legible curriculum*, most of it front-loaded into clips 1–9 and demonstrated at ceiling in clip 9.

- **The control schema (clip 3b — the technē genesis).** The Colter on-rails corridor smuggles the entire verb-set in under narrative cover: **mounted movement** (00:00, follow-the-leader through the whiteout), **camera / free-look in cover** (04:17 — "the 2D surrogate for the head"; where a VR player turns to look, here look is a stick-driven symbolic act), **shooting** (07:08, "Go shoot him"), **inventory / looting** (09:00–13:52, "grab as many supplies as you can" — the *acquisitive verb* of the whole playthrough installed as a pure satchel-UI abstraction), **melee grapple** (14:52), **horse-handling** (17:00, "Easy, easy"), **tracking / Eagle Eye** (24:00), and **weapon-selection** (27:00, shotgun-from-saddle). The clip closes the combat curriculum on the **wolf-attack** (33:00–36:00), the first sustained A₂→A₄-on-repeat under time-pressure ("Keep them away from us!" → "Good work, Arthur").
- **Hunting (clip 4 — the clean didactic loop).** Charles voices each chain-stage aloud, making this the corpus's most *legible* RODA material: A₀ scarcity-horizon (00:58–02:10) → A₁ trained perception ("get your eye in," 06:10–06:44) → A₂/A₃/A₄ compressed into the drawn-bow kill ("Good tension on the string before releasing… Now, Arthur," 07:52–08:04) → harvest/provision (19:19–21:17). The hexis is *named* as such: "Takes a lifetime of practice to master" (19:09).
- **The horse-bond + the legendary hunt (clip 9 — the technē APEX).** What clip 4 *seeded* under a tutor, clip 9 *demonstrates* untutored and at ceiling — and its **silence is diagnostic**: a mature hexis is no longer narrated, only enacted (the transcript is pure whisper-padding). Horsemanship (whistle-call co-agency handshake 00:00–02:30; gait/stamina ride 02:30–05:00) and the hunter's eye-and-hand (Eagle-Eye tracking 07:30–09:30; *study* the bear 09:30–11:00; the kill 12:30) converge in the **legendary kill via Dead Eye** (20:30–22:30) — horsemanship + tracking + shot-placement consummated in one A₄.
- **Heist-skills (clip 5 — technē taught directly).** The game *scripts skill-acquisition*: the **detonator-wiring** mini-mechanic ("set up the detonator… unspool the wire and then attach it," 40:01–41:00), **moving-train traversal** under fire (42:42–45:00), **safe-cracking** (45:00–51:00), and **cooperative wagon-repair** (58:00–65:00).
- **Minigames (clips 5b, 13 — pure symbolic-rhythm technē).** Five-finger-fillet (clip 5b, 00:30–01:00 splay + rhythm-prompt) and gambling (clip 13, 07:00–09:30 the wager decision) are skills where *phantasia supplies no body at all* — the hand is iconographic, card/chip tokens are flat symbols. These are the cleanest **contrast cases for the honor machine**: fillet is **wager-scored, not honor-scored**, so the Burke motive resolves to *agency* (the means/instrument) + the *agent's* dexterity, the moral pentad disengaged.
- **Gear-as-technē (clip 15).** The second/off-hand holster (06:00–07:00) is a *technē-hexis gear* acquisition: it durably enables dual-wield marksmanship — an **expansion of agency-as-technē** (more lethal capacity), pointedly paired with a *contraction* of agency-as-*prohairesis* (see §5).

## §2 — The ETHICAL-*HEXIS* Cultivation (the disposition built underneath the skill)

The ethical-hexis is the bundle's deeper objective (see §7, the headline finding). It is laid down in four sedimenting strata.

- **The opening loyalty-seed (clip 1).** Before the player holds the stick, Dutch's **grief-and-rally speech** (04:11–05:01) installs the *ethical hexis of gang-loyalty* as the orienting good — allegiance to Dutch and to gang-survival. It simultaneously runs the **Burkean scene-attribution engine** that the whole game reuses: the gang's losses are attributed to *circumstance/scene*, never to an *agent's* culpability ("There was nothing more you could have done," 03:42; "a storm like this one," 05:00). That exculpatory frame is the moral lens the player is *handed* for reading their own future acts. "Stay with me" (05:18) is the loyalty imperative; Charles's "we need you strong" (06:09) seeds the mutual-obligation disposition.
- **Camp labor / belonging (clips 6a–d — belonging-through-labor).** The orekton here is not loot or honor-points but *being-at-home / being-of-use*. The hail-by-name "Morning, Mr. Morgan!" (6a, 00:00) is the **shared-channel belonging engine**; coffee (6a, ~00:20) and **wood-chopping** (6a, ~01:00) run the full A₂→A₄ arc in seconds on the *ludic* channel at near-zero — and that triviality *is* the point: **low ludic + high shared/spatial/affective = dwelling, not goal-pursuit**. The chore-mechanic ("lift with your knees" → haul → "Good work, Mr. Morgan," 6b, 02:00–02:36) braids a thin technē onto the ethical disposition. The camp even *names its own monotony*: "Laundry and sewing. The exciting life of an outlaw… if they only knew how boring it really is" (6b, 04:53) — the **labor of belonging cost as boredom** (a candidate site for the FCM *second form of boredom*, being-bored-*with* the camp-situation). The verbal signature of incorporation is explicit: the campfire "is in our skin now" (6c, 02:52).
- **The gang-bond (clips 5, 11, 14 — the collective face).** Clip 5 cultivates **in-group fidelity + subordination to Dutch + out-group ruthlessness** — the "Outlaws for life" creed (25:29–27:00) and the camp's contributive "gets its slice" economy (65:00). Clip 11 is the **collective-violence** face (the O'Driscoll raid). Clip 14 is the **redemptive/solidarity** face — *Rescue Sean*, whose collective orekton is **a person, not plunder** (recover Sean), the "leave no man behind" creed made concrete.
- **The honor system (clips 3a, 7, 8 — the moral-scoring self).** RDR2 externalizes Burke's motive-attribution into a **scored meter** — a design-supplied *doxa*-cue a VR world tends to leave implicit. Clip 7 is the **densest single run of honor-as-motive-attribution**: five consecutive honor-positive resolutions (old man's horse 06:18–08:10; defend Tilly 23:00; defend Karen 24:16; spare the witness Jimmy Brooks 28:00–31:00; return the borrowed horse 32:10). Clip 8 cultivates the **lawful-hunter disposition** — violence felt as sanctioned, clean, and *rewarded* because routed through the state's wanted/honor economy. (Clip 3a — the *inert* fork — is the diagnostic exception; see §3.)

## §3 — The HONOR System's SELECTIVE Scoring (the diagnostic)

The corpus-relevant finding is that the honor machine is a **partial, external doxa-cue** — it scores *selectively*, and the pattern of what it scores vs. withholds reveals its character as a motive-attribution machine rather than a moral conscience.

- **INERT on the choke/spare fork (clip 3a).** The playthrough's *first true ethical fork* (11:31–11:45) — Dutch hands Arthur lethal discretion over a pleading, disarmed captive ("Do what you want with him… I don't care") — and the game **scores it as nothing**: the player's own title flags that choke and spare are mechanically identical (no honor delta). The *only* phenomenal residue of a life-or-mercy act is the **tolling of a bell** — an **aesthetic / affective surrogate for the motive-score the machine withholds**. The ethical event is *real* (a genuine A₃→A₄ choice over a fully-realized patient of *bia*) but **machine-inert**: the disposition is cultivated in the player's phantasia and conscience, *not* in the HUD. Crucially the *bia* thread (captive forced agent→patient at 10:18; extracted-from at 10:55) is what gives the fork its weight — the honor system's silence over it is the datum.
- **INERT on ordinary brawls too (clip 10) — author-confirmed.** The synthesis first inferred the saloon over-beating to be honor-*active*; the author confirms it is **not**: the bar-fight produces **no honor change at all**, even as the won fight continues past its win-condition into the over-beating of a floored opponent (the crowd baying "Finish him, Tommy… Kill him, Tommy," 04:23–05:11; the agent→patient inversion). The brawl is *felt* — and read by an intervening townsman (not Trelawney) as excess — yet **machine-mute**. So the honor machine is silent not only over the intimate mercy-fork but over **ordinary interpersonal violence**.
- **ACTIVE on legible pro-social / heroic acts (clips 6, 7) — author-confirmed.** Where the hard intimate choice and the ordinary brawl score nothing, the *easy, witnessed* good deed scores at once: **+honor for camp chores** (belonging-as-honor — the white-icon increment after each chore-set, clip 6) and for **defending the weak** (+10 honor for beating the man who was beating his friend, clip 7 ~24–26 min; the defend-Tilly / defend-Karen resolutions; sparing the witness Jimmy Brooks; returning the borrowed horse). → **The selectivity axis is public-legible-pro-social (scored) vs intimate-ambiguous / ordinary-violence (silent):** the machine rewards the contribution-to-collective and the witnessed heroic deed, yet withholds judgment on the hardest intimate moral choice (kill-or-spare a pleading captive) and on ordinary brawling — leaving the mercy-fork to the bell-toll and the player's conscience. It registers **public virtue** but not **intimate conscience** — a motive-attribution engine, not a moral one.
- **What the contrast reveals.** The machine scores the **legible/excessive** and withholds on the **maximal/intimate**. It is loud where an act is socially witnessed and faction-coded (clip 7's five public resolutions; clip 11's O'Driscoll kills as *near-free* violence against a pre-authorized faction; clip 15's honor **decrease** for killing *lawmen*) and silent where the moral weight is private and unwitnessed (3a's captive). It also **launders motive**: clip 16's "don't kill nobody… robbing your crooked cousin by marriage" (01:30–01:55) pre-softens the target so theft is attributed away from *agent* (Arthur) toward *scene/purpose* (the job, the crooked victim); clip 7's Uncle re-attributes a good deed to *scene* not agent — "if you lot hadn't been here, I probably would have robbed them" (08:40). The honor meter is therefore best read as an **external, partial doxa-cue** the player may consult or overrule — a 2D rhetorical instrument (see §8), not a complete account of the cultivated ethical self.

## §4 — The *BIA* / Agency Moments (forced; agency-stripping; agent→patient inversion)

*Bia* (force contrary to the agent's *orexis*/reason, dragging the agent toward the patient-pole) structures the bundle as a recurring rhythm of inversions — suffered, inflicted, and reversed.

- **The cinematic *bia*-baseline (clip 1).** The opening cutscene withholds agency *entirely*: the player is patient/spectator, agency withdrawn by the cinematic form itself (a *soft bia*). This baseline is what makes the later restoration of agency at the candidate control-handoff (05:47, "We've got some work to do") legible as a transition from patient to agent.
- **The leash (clip 2).** "I think you're going the wrong way" (00:11–00:13) is the world rebuffing an off-trail vector and funnelling the player back — **Rickert recalcitrance** as a **soft *bia***, a micro agent→patient inversion in which the free movement-vector is corralled by scene. The whiteout *masks* the leash: because the screen withholds visible boundaries, the spoken line *stands in* for the invisible wall — high phantasia-load letting a hard corral read as diegetic guidance. The disposition cultivated is *being-led* (obedience-to-path), the privative ground against which the eventual open A₀ registers as freedom.
- **On-rails stretches (clip 3).** Colter is the densest soft-*bia* stretch: scripted positioning ("Arthur, in that cattle shed on the left," 3a 05:09; 3b 04:17) and sustained corridor patter ("stay close to the wall… we have to walk from here," 3b 29:00–31:00) repeatedly suspend navigational agency — the disposition is as much *compliance with the guided corridor* as it is skill.
- **Agent→patient inversions (suffered → inflicted → reversed):**
  - **Suffered (player de-agentialized):** the barn grapple (3b 14:52, Arthur seized, control momentarily inverted, recovered by button-mash); the bear/legendary *charge* and possible *maul* (clip 9, 11:00–12:30 / 18:30–20:30 — a forced-prone takedown, if the frames confirm it, is genuine *bia*: control stripped, the hunter made patient by the hunted); the **drunk-cam** loosened agency (clip 12, 14:00–30:00 — a *comedic, consensual bia*: sluggish stumbling, unreliable prompts, a camera that will not hold, with the orekton being *oblivion/companionship*, so the player **willingly cedes** mastery); and the **narrative *bia*** of clip 15 (Dutch's order to free Micah forecloses deliberation — the agent moved as patient toward an end he need not endorse).
  - **Inflicted (player as agent of *bia*):** the prisoner beating (3b 15:30, 3a 10:55 — *bia* inflicted on the NPC to extract intel); the **Kieran Duffy capture** (clip 5, 27:25–33:08 — sustained agent→patient, the player the inflicting agent, cultivating *dominative cruelty as in-group routine*); the **bounty hogtie** (clip 8, 12:00–13:00 — the lasso/hogtie a literal agent→patient inversion *enacted by the player on the NPC*, the captive reduced to saddle-cargo, the rare case where the player is the *agent* of *bia*); the floored brawler (clip 10, 04:23–05:11); O'Driscoll finishers (clip 11, 14:00–17:00).
  - **Reversed (player un-does another's *bia*):** **freeing Sean** (clip 14, 10:30–11:30) is the **mirror-image of Kieran** — Sean enters the clip as the agency-stripped patient (bound by bounty hunters) and the player's completed A₄ *restores his agency*. The witnessed prison-wagon fugitive (clip 6d, 03:48–04:43) and bound NPC (clip 6c, 06:05) are **mirrored *bia*** at one remove — another's agency-stripping seen, not undergone, the fork (free/rob/ignore) declined.
  - **Social-register *bia*:** Bill's exclusion "What about you?" (clip 10, 07:09) replays the agent→patient inversion at the register of *standing* rather than the body.
- **The anti-*bia* counter-pole.** Camp life (clips 6a–d) is the agency-rich, *voluntary* counter-pole (no hard inversion), and clip 16's "now vs nightfall; house vs coach" plan (04:41–05:16) is an explicitly **agency-preserving choice fork** — the act handed to the player's *prohairesis*, the cleanest anti-*bia* design in the bundle.

## §5 — INCORPORATION across the arc (Calleja channels; the two disclosednesses)

- **World-disclosedness (R1–R7; dwelling-in-the-world).** RDR2 achieves enclosure by **phantasmatic subtraction** — the screen is a window, not an HMD enclosure, so phantasia must withdraw the still-present room/bezel — and it earns that subtraction by **sheer world-density**: weather (the clip-2 whiteout), wildlife and the hunt (clips 4, 9), NPC routines and camp friction (clips 6a–d, "Hope you choke on it!"), the dense Valentine town-vista (clip 7, 09:00). Dwelling is *accreted by traversal* (the long silent rides of clips 8, 9, 14) rather than occupied bodily. The verbal index of incorporation is the camp "in our skin now" (clip 6c, 02:52). All six Calleja channels fire across the bundle, with **kinesthetic** (symbolic-only — see §8), **spatial**, and **ludic** carrying the technē clips and **affective + narrative + shared** carrying the ethical clips.
- **Co-disclosedness (being-with; *Miterschlossenheit*).** This is the bundle's most important contrast with G&G. *Gnomes & Goblins* reaches only the **threshold** of co-disclosedness — the goblins are disclosed and *look* at the player, but interaction is designed out, so the consubstantial "WE" is structurally withheld (gg-RODA §III). **RDR2 reaches the acting-together "WE" that G&G withholds.** The gang is a population of co-agents who act *alongside* the player in **jointly-sourced acts**:
  - the **train heist** (clip 5) — assigned roles, Dutch ceding the lead "Just make the call. You want to take the lead? Go" (09:30–12:41), the slip-and-haul "I'm slipping! Pull me up!" (42:42) — a shared *orekton* consummated by the collective;
  - the **O'Driscoll raid** (clip 11, 08:30–14:00) — the corpus's purest **co-disclosed combat**: the player absorbed into a *collective* act fought with the posse, not a solo duel;
  - the **rescue** (clip 14) — a 3-man squad riding abreast (the **shared channel is the strongest channel of the clip**), the bond re-sealed by recovering one of its own.
  This is genuine consubstantiation in Burke's sense ("a way of life is an acting-together") — the gang's banditry *is* its shared way of life, and the player is admitted to **co-source** it. The diagnostic wrinkle (clip 11): the raid is performed by the whole posse (*scene* = collective), yet the honor machine **individuates** it — honor attaches to **Arthur alone** (Burke's agent-term sheared off the scene). The machine *privatizes a shared killing*.
- **One in substrate, different in being.** As in G&G, world-disclosedness and co-disclosedness are the **two poles of one incorporation** — but RDR2 *consummates* the second pole where G&G held it at the threshold. The data-discrepancy axis (not a ceiling) sets the *route*: RDR2 reaches the "WE" through dialogue-dense, narratively-scaffolded co-presence; G&G reaches only the look.

## §6 — DIACHRONIC / Layer-5 (sedimentation + the headline finding)

- **What sediments (technē).** A self-directed craftsman's competence: the hunter's eye (clip 4 → clip 9, now untutored), horsemanship and the bond (clip 9), gunplay/Dead Eye consolidated under duress (clips 3b, 10, 11, 15), heist-craft (clip 5), and the gear that *expands* the skill-hexis (clip 15's dual-wield). Hexis-by-repetition is explicit: the wolf-waves (3b 33:00), the iterated hunt ("now let's try for another," 4 08:04), the chore-loops (6a–d), the gambling variable-reward schedule (13 12:00–15:00).
- **What sediments (ethical).** Gang-loyalty (clip 1) → belonging-through-labor (6a–d) → the contributive in-group creed (clip 5) → the honor-scored moral self (clips 3a, 7, 8) → out-group ruthlessness (clips 5, 11) braided with redemptive solidarity (clip 14) → and finally **theft-as-livelihood** (clip 16's fence-unlock converts a discrete act into a *standing disposition of the outlaw economy*; A₀ is permanently altered into a recurring income node). A moral *counter*-horizon is also seeded — Charles's Native-dispossession talk (clip 5, 58:00–65:00) — against which the gang's plunder is quietly weighed.
- **THE HEADLINE FINDING.** Across the protracted arc the **ethical-hexis cultivation is a primary design objective, not a by-product of the technē curriculum.** The very first interactive clip (3b) threads *three* honor events through the control tutorial — the bundle is **dual from the outset**; the camp clips (6a–d) spend long *dialogue-empty* minutes building belonging by sheer repetition with the ludic channel near-zero (dwelling, not goal-pursuit); the densest single clip (7) is a *moral*-scoring run, not a skills run.
