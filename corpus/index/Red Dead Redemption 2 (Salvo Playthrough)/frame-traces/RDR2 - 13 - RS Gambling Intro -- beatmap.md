## Salience

This clip's contribution to the protracted hexis-bundle is the seeding of a **gambling/risk disposition** that is doubly valenced: on the surface a **techne** (card- and odds-reading, bet-sizing, bluff-reading — a transferable skill-hexis), and beneath it an **ethical disposition toward risk and gain** that flirts with *pleonexia* (avarice) under a variable-reward schedule. RODA reads the loop as iterated actualization-of-desire: the *orekton* is the pot/money (with mastery as a secondary orekton), and each hand runs M0→A1 (read the table) → A2 (phantasma of the winning hand) → A3 (the committal *doxa* "I hold the better hand," charged with confidence or dread) → A4 (the placed wager). The signature Burkean event is the **attribution tension** the minigame stages by design — outcomes are perpetually disputable between **scene** (luck/fate) and **agency/agent** (skill) — which is precisely the pentadic machinery RDR2's HONOR system exploits when gambling tips into repercussion. The **ethical fork / honor event / bia moment**, if present, will sit in the peak-stakes window (≈15:00–18:00): a cheating mechanic or cheating-accusation would route the act through the honor-scoring machine and can invert the gambler from **agent to patient** in a forced draw (*bia*) — but **this is unconfirmed without frames**, since the transcript supplies no dialogue to anchor an accusation or confrontation. As a 2D/high-phantasia-load specimen, the clip is a clean demonstration of large data-discrepancy: the entire motivational weight of the wager rests on imagination loading flat card-and-chip tokens with hedonic stakes the screen does not itself carry.
