## Salience

This clip is the **redemptive/solidarity beat of the protracted hexis-bundle** — the in-group-fidelity face of the same ethical *hexis* whose ruthless face appears in the Kieran capture (clip 5) and the O'Driscoll raid (clip 11). Where those cultivate *dominative cruelty* toward the out-group, "Rescue Sean" cultivates the **complementary disposition: the gang-as-family obligation to recover one of its own** — and the **collective OREKTON here is a *person*, not plunder** (recover Sean), which is exactly what makes the beat ethically distinct from the heist's money-orekton. The combat **TECHNE** (overwatch ambush, cover-shooting under a *protect-the-captive* no-friendly-fire constraint) is in service of that ethical end, not in tension with it. The single most important RODA structure here is a **bia REVERSAL**: Sean enters the clip as the agency-stripped **patient** (bound by the bounty hunters), and the player's completed act (A4) **un-does that bia, restoring his agency** — the mirror-image of the clip-5 Kieran beat where the player *inflicts* bia. On honor: the kills are scored by the **honor-machine as ACT**, but the SCENE (lawless armed captors) frames the violence as **righteous rescue → low/neutral or positive honor**, so there is **no ethical fork** in the clip (no spare/kill-civilian choice) — its honor signature is *clean*, which is itself the datum: the game lets the player feel virtuous while killing, because the motive-attribution is pre-loaded toward loyalty. **Caveat (provenance):** because the transcript is dialogue-bare (whisper hallucinations only), this entire reading is **role- and mission-structure-inferred and author/frame-confirmable** — the frame layer is the *primary* evidence for this clip, not a supplement.
