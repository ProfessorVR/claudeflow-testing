## Salience

This clip is the **gang-formation keystone** of the protracted hexis-bundle. It cultivates the ETHICAL disposition of in-group fidelity + subordination to Dutch + out-group ruthlessness (the "outlaw for life" creed, 25:29–27:00; the camp's "gets its slice" economy, 65:00–70:06), braided with heist/combat **TECHNE** (cover-shooting, detonator-wiring, moving-train traversal, safe-cracking, cooperative wagon-repair). The **collective OREKTON** — gang survival → the train's money → Horseshoe prosperity — is the through-line, with Dutch's private revenge deliberately sublimated into plunder (06:21–07:34). Two events anchor the clip's honor/agency stakes: (1) **Kieran Duffy's capture** (27:25–33:08) — sustained **BIA**, agent→patient inversion, the player as the inflicting agent, cultivating dominative cruelty as routine; and (2) the **railway-hostage fork** (51:00–52:30) — an explicit **ETHICAL FORK that the honor system SCORES** (kill / spare / take), the one moment where the player's prohairesis is itself the honor-machine's input, immediately doubled by a second bia (forcing the workers onto the train). The Charles wagon-talk (58:00–65:00) supplies the moral counter-horizon — Native dispossession — against which the gang's plunder is quietly measured.
