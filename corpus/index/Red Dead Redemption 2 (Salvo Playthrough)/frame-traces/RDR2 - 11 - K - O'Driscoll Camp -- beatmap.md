## SALIENCE

This clip is the corpus's **collective-violence pole** and its analytic value is doubled by an accident of capture: **it is dialogue-silent**, so the entire 23:31 throws its weight onto the **ludic / kinesthetic / shared** involvement channels with *no narrative-channel scaffolding at all*. The protracted hexis-bundle it cultivates is almost pure **techne** — mounted approach, cover discipline, gunplay and Dead Eye sustained across a long firefight (est. 08:30–14:00) — executed not solo but *with the gang*, so the dominant incorporation signature is **shared/co-disclosed combat** (the player is absorbed into a collective act, not an individual duel). Where Clip 3's O'Driscoll homestead ended on a single pleading **patient** and an honor-machine that scored the mercy/cruelty fork as *nothing*, this clip is the inverse case: mass, sanctioned violence against O'Driscolls, where the **Burke motive-attribution machine (honor) scores killing as near-free** — the O'Driscoll faction is the game's pre-authorized target, so the orekton (*clear the camp*) is pursued with the disposition the design has already absolved. The diagnostic finding is the **individuation of a collective act**: the raid is performed by the whole posse (scene = collective), yet honor attaches to **Arthur alone** (Burke's agent-term sheared off from the scene) — the machine privatizes a shared killing. The bia thread is the camp itself: an asymmetric descent that makes the O'Driscolls **patients en masse**, with any execute/finisher beat (est. 14:00–17:00) a localized **agent→patient inversion** to confirm at the frames. There is **no ethical fork** here in the Clip-3 sense — the violence is morally pre-resolved by faction — which is precisely the point of the pairing: this clip cultivates the *honor-cheap* combat disposition against which the un-scored mercy of Clip 3 reads as anomalous. **Caveat carried forward:** because the transcript grounds nothing, every claim above is a role-and-runtime inference; the visual layer must confirm the phase structure, the collective/ally presence, the honor-HUD behavior on kills, and any bia/finisher beat.
