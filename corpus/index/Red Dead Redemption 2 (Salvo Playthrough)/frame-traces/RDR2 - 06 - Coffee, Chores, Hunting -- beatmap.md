## SALIENCE

This clip is a **low-arousal, provision-loop episode** whose contribution to the protracted hexis-bundle is the habituation of an **ETHICAL disposition of communal care — the gang-provider** — braided with a **hunting *techne***. Where action-heavy clips cultivate combat skill or honor under duress, this one cultivates *dwelling*: the camp described as "in our skin now" is the verbal signature of incorporation, and the repetitive donate→chore→hunt cadence is precisely how a hexis is laid down by repetition rather than by a single dramatic act. The orekton is stable throughout — feed the family — and the clip closes the provisioning loop by moving from **acquiring favor at camp** (donating, "that'll keep Pearson happy") to **acquiring the resource in the wild** (the hunt). The two honor/Burke events are the camp's motive-attribution feedback ("Good work, Mr. Morgan / Good job, Arthur") scoring Arthur's contributions as favorable *acts*, attributed to the *purpose* of provision — the HONOR system functioning here in its quiet, accretive (not punitive) register. The one **bia** moment is the bound NPC at ~06:05 ("Cruel bastards. Tying me up right by the boot."), an agent→patient inversion that opens a latent **ethical fork** (free / ignore / exploit) whose resolution the transcript cannot settle. On the VR-2D axis, this clip is a clean demonstration of the 2D pole's high phantasia-load: with no acute threat-flinch to anchor embodiment, presence is carried almost entirely by phantasia — the felt weight of the hauled load ("lift with your knees") and the felt body of the solitary mounted hunt, both supplied to a symbolic stick/button interface rather than registered through an HMD.
