## SALIENCE

This clip is the corpus's **ontological-change specimen** — the single rupture pass the methodology names as its third scale variant (RODA v9 §9), and the event Part II's "Ontological Violence" section builds on. Three findings are load-bearing. **(1) The bar-crossing is engineered one step past the last revocable point.** The design keeps the full agentive menu (Aim Weapon / Greet / Antagonize) live through nineteen recorded seconds of threshold hesitation — hesitation the NPC script itself taunts ("What you stood there for?") — and seizes control (02:31.67: HUD off, letterbox on) only *after* the player's own walk-input commits the crossing. The *bia* is therefore parasitic on a genuine voluntary act, which is precisely what makes the inversion violating rather than merely cinematic: the player's agency is not absent but *used*. **(2) The event's core is a designed void.** Forty-seven percent of the seized span is black screen; the one interior image permitted is a degraded low-angle POV from the floor — the patient-pole installed as camera grammar — showing only a hand gripping suspenders while the dialogue rewrites the player's struggle in the past tense ("you struggled and you lost… my pet"). This is **phantasia-conscription at its limit**: the assault is authored by the player's own resonant *doxai*, which is why its ratified content varies by dispositional ground (the draft's adult/child differential) and why the wound it leaves is self-inflicted in the strict sense — the world supplied the frame; the player's *phantasia* supplied the scene. **(3) The hand-back is a ledger, and the ledger is the insult.** Agency returns itemized: exactly −$1.00 (on-screen debit ticker), all three cores depleted — health, stamina, *and deadeye*, all red (author-confirmed; stamina + health additionally by system toast), a *resting tutorial* as the system's sole commentary, and a body that testifies (the 3.5 s stooped gait, frame-bounded at 03:31–03:34.6) to what the screen refused to show. The same recording then documents the carry-over the draft's reception evidence predicts: the player returns, forces the barricaded door, and kills Sonny — a thymotic pass *sourced by the rupture* — and the honor machine, which scored a saloon over-beating, renders **no verdict** on either the rape or the revenge — no gain, no loss; effectively neutral (author-confirmed). The clip thus exhibits the full ontological-change mechanism end to end: voluntary approach → *bia*-seizure → destructive *paschein* under sensory withholding → dispositional sediment (fear/disgust/vengeance-orexis) → a next pass motivated by nothing in the game's economy (one dollar!) and everything in the player's transformed being-toward this world.
