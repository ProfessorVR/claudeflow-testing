## Salience

This clip is the corpus's **apex specimen of the *techne*-hexis bundle**: the skill-disposition seeded under Charles's narration in clip 4 ("get your eye in," "good tension on the string") here runs **untutored and at ceiling** across two crafts at once — horsemanship (gait, calm-the-mount, the cultivated bond) and the hunter's eye-and-hand (track → study → shot-placement → harvest). Where clip 4 was the *seeding* and was legible because a tutor voiced each stage, clip 09 is the *demonstration*, and its silence is diagnostic: the absence of any dialogue (the transcript is pure Whisper "you" padding) is exactly what a mature *hexis* looks like — no longer narrated, only enacted. The protracted disposition it advances is therefore the **self-directed craftsman's competence**, and its distinctive contribution beyond clip 4 is the **animal as co-agent**: the mount, the bear, and the legendary quarry each carry their own *orexis*, so the clip is also where RODA's actualization-of-desire meets a *second* desiring agent on screen. The **horse-bond** (05:00–07:30) is the positive pole of RDR2's animal-treatment register — the same axis the honor-system penalizes when a player abuses a horse — so the care-act here, though it posts no honor toast, *models the ethical disposition* the motive-machine tracks elsewhere; the bond sits between technē and ethics (care toward an animal). The **ethical/agency forks** are two, both on the co-agency seam: (1) the **horse-rear** (02:30–05:00), a soft agent/patient inversion where the animal's fear interrupts the player's act and demands re-attunement; and (2) the **bear/legendary charge** (11:00–12:30, 18:30–20:30), where a maul or knock-down — if the frames show the forced-prone animation — is genuine **bia**: control stripped, the hunter momentarily made patient by the hunted. The **honor-motive attribution** stays quiet and legitimate (hunting-for-provision = **scene/purpose**, as in clip 4), with the legendary kill adding a **mastery/agent** overlay (the trophy-pelt, the Trapper economy). For the VR-2D thesis this clip is doubly load-bearing: the **charging bear** is the single cleanest specimen in the playthrough of *embodiment-registered-as-flinch* (R2 via phantasia, no HMD, the felt body supplied entirely behind stick/button), and the clip stacks the game's three **phantasia-prostheses** — Eagle Eye (tracking-vision), the legendary aura-filter (specialness-as-UI), and Dead Eye (composure-as-time-slow) — each a 2D compensation for an eidetic/affective state a body would otherwise hold, precisely where a VR counterpart (Gnomes & Goblins) would begin to deliver it proprioceptively.
