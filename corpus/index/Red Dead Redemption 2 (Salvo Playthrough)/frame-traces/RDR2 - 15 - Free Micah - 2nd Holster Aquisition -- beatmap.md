## SALIENCE

This clip's contribution to the protracted hexis-bundle is **doubly techne**: it consolidates the **gunplay/Dead-Eye skill disposition** under combat pressure and, at its center (~06:00–07:00), grants the **techne-hexis gear** — the second/off-hand holster that durably enables **dual-wield marksmanship**. The disposition cultivated is therefore a *skill* hexis (a trained capacity for two-handed gunplay), **not** an ethical one; if anything the clip's ethical valence runs the other way. The honor system — read here as Burke's **motive-attribution machine**, scoring each *act* — registers a sustained honor **DECREASE** across the Strawberry firefight (lawmen killed), so the clip's moral residue is the cost of acquiring techne. The governing **ethical event** is structural rather than chosen: the player is bound by narrative **BIA** (Dutch's order) to risk Arthur's life and honor to free **Micah Bell, an ethically grey — indeed treacherous — ally**; deliberation is foreclosed, the agent moved as patient toward an end he need not endorse. The clip thus pairs an *expansion of agency-as-techne* (a second gun, more lethal capacity) with a *contraction of agency-as-prohairesis* (no real choice about whom to save), and it is exactly this pairing — more power, less self-determination, honor spent on a poisonous ally — that makes it valuable to the bundle. For the VR-2D lens, the firefight onset (~03:30–04:30) is the cleanest 2D-pole datum in the clip: embodiment surfaces only as the involuntary **flinch** at on-screen threat, **R2 satisfied through phantasia rather than an HMD**, with phantasia carrying the entire felt body across symbolic stick/button controls.
