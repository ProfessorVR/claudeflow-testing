## SALIENCE

This clip's contribution to the protracted hexis-bundle is the **conversion of theft from a discrete act into a standing disposition of livelihood** — the outlaw *economy* as a durable hexis, not just a one-off techne. Mechanically the body of the clip cultivates **techne** (reconnaissance, casing, crouch-stealth, vehicle theft, the protracted escort-drive), but its keystone is **ethical**: the closing fence-unlock (11:50–12:08) installs Seamus as a permanent buyer of stolen coaches, so the seized stagecoach is less an end than the inauguration of a recurring income channel — the orekton is ultimately the *fence relationship*, not the wagon. The **ethical fork** is pre-softened by design: Seamus's "don't kill nobody" constraint sanctions a *non-lethal* robbery and morally discounts the victim (a "crooked cousin by marriage"), so the honor system scores theft while the scripting attributes the act away from agent (Arthur) toward scene/purpose (the job, the crooked target) — a Burkean motive-laundering. Notably, this clip is **agency-rich, low-*bia***: at 04:41–05:16 the act is handed to the player's *prohairesis* (now vs nightfall; house vs coach), an explicitly agency-preserving choice fork rather than an agent→patient inversion. The honor event is the logged dishonor of theft under a non-lethal cap; the agency event is the deliberate nightfall-wait choice.
