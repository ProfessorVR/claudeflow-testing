## SALIENCE

This clip's contribution to the protracted hexis-bundle is the cultivation of the **lawful-hunter disposition** — an *ethical* hexis in which violence is felt as **sanctioned, clean, and rewarded** because it is routed through the state's wanted/honor economy. Where camp clips cultivate belonging and hunting clips cultivate predatory *techne*, the bounty mission braids combat/capture *techne* with a specific moral coloring: the player is positioned as a **deputized instrument of the law**, and the honor system's **motive-attribution machine** retroactively scores the manner of the violence (clean capture vs. wanton kill), teaching that legitimized violence pays. The orekton throughout is the **bounty** — reward braided with sanction — which holds the pursuit taut across two long, silent dwelling-rides (out and back). The clip carries TWO sharp analytic events. (1) An **ethical fork** at ~11:00–12:00: take the target **alive** (honor up, larger reward) or **kill** him — the cleanest honor-event in the bundle, where the scoring machine attributes motive to the **agent** and to **purpose**. Both events are also the clip's sharpest 2D data-discrepancy points: the gravity of sparing-vs-killing, the rope's drag, and the captive's dead weight all exist only as phantasia-supplied felt body over symbolic stick/button input, and combat embodiment surfaces as the **involuntary flinch at return fire (R2 via phantasia)**. **Methodological flag:** because the transcript is wholly hallucinated, this beat-map is a reconstruction from role + canonical arc; the analysis is unusually visual-dependent and every claim is provisional pending frame reads.
