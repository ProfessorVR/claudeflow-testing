## SALIENCE

This clip is the corpus's **first true ethical fork**, and its analytic value is *negative/diagnostic*: it shows the **honor system (the Burke motive-attribution machine) failing to attribute motive** to a morally maximal act. Everything before the fork cultivates a **techne** bundle — mounted traversal, stealth positioning, gunplay/Dead Eye, looting — interleaved with **narrative/affective** disposition-priming (Dutch's deputizing of Arthur as the "reliable" one; the Arthur/Micah character contrast). All of that builds an *orectic* horizon whose orekton is **provision-and-survival for the gang**, and it trains the player to read moral character (03:10–04:00, the corpse-reveal at 05:24–06:34). The fork at **11:31–11:45** then offers the disposition that *should* be the protracted hexis-bundle's ethical spine — mercy-vs-cruelty toward a pleading, disarmed **patient of bia** — and the game scores it as nothing. Choke and spare are mechanically identical (no honor delta), so the only residue of having taken a life-or-mercy act is the **tolling of a bell**: an aesthetic/affective surrogate for the motive-score the honor machine withholds. The ethical event is therefore real (a genuine A3→A4 choice under a pleading patient) but **machine-inert** — the disposition is cultivated in the player's phantasia and conscience, not in the HUD. The bia thread (captive forced agent→patient at 10:18, extracted-from at 10:55, then disposed-of at 11:31) is what gives the fork its weight; the honor system's silence is the finding.
