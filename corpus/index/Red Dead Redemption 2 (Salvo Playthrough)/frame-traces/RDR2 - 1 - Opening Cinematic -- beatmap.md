## SALIENCE

This clip is the **framing A0** for the entire playthrough: it discloses the designed world/horizon *before* any orekton is determinate and *before* the player holds the controls, so its work is dispositional rather than skill-forming. The protracted **hexis-bundle** it seeds is **ethical, not technical** — specifically the disposition of **gang-loyalty / allegiance to Dutch** as the orienting good against which the game's HONOR machine will later score acts. Dutch's grief-and-rally speech (04:11–05:01) is the keystone: it installs the moral economy of survival-and-belonging and, in Burkean terms, runs a persistent **scene–act ratio** that attributes the gang's losses (Davy's death, the missing Sean/Mac/Jenny) to *circumstance/scene* rather than to any *agent's* culpability ("There was nothing more you could have done"; "a storm like this"). That exculpatory frame is the moral lens the player is handed for reading their own future acts. There is no honor-scored ethical FORK here (the cinematic affords no choice), but the clip is the **bia-baseline**: as a non-interactive cutscene it withholds agency entirely (player as patient/spectator), making the later restoration of agency at the control-handoff (05:47) legible as a transition from patient to agent. The off-screen Blackwater catastrophe ("that boat," 06:00) fixes the player's **thrownness** — they begin already inside a crisis they did not cause and cannot inspect, the originating phantasma supplied wholly by phantasia.
