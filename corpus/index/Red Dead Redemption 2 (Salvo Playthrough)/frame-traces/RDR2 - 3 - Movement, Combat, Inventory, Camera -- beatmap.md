## SALIENCE

This clip is the **techne-hexis genesis** of the entire playthrough: it installs, in sequence, the full control schema the corpus will track downstream — mounted movement (00:00), camera/free-look in cover (04:17), shooting (07:08, climaxing in the wolf waves at 33:00), inventory/looting (09:00), melee grapple (14:52), horse-handling (17:00), tracking (24:00), and weapon-selection (27:00). Because Colter is an on-rails corridor, the clip is also the playthrough's densest **soft-BIA** stretch: companion patter ("stay close," "stay close to the wall," "we have to walk from here") and scripted positioning ("Arthur, in that cattle shed") repeatedly suspend navigational agency, training the player to a horizon the world dictates — the disposition cultivated is as much *compliance with the guided corridor* as it is skill.

Crucially, the clip does **not** cultivate pure techne. Threaded through the tutorial are the playthrough's **first three honor-system motive-attributions**, each a determinate ethical fork: (1) **the prisoner release [16:00–17:00]** — Dutch hands Arthur lethal discretion ("Do what you want with him") and the player elects *mercy* ("Get the hell outta here! Go!"), an A4 scored to the AGENT's disposition; (2) **the Sadie Adler rescue [18:00–20:12]** — intervening against Micah's **bia** on a defenseless widow, with the gang's self-disclosure "We're bad men. We ain't them" defining their ethos *against* the O'Driscolls; and (3) **the John Marston rescue [22:00–38:00]** as a sustained loyalty-act. The honor machine here begins doing its characteristic work: reading acts and attributing them to the agent's character, so that the protracted hexis-bundle this corpus traces is **dual from the outset — techne and ethos installed in the same breath.** The standout agency-stripping (bia) inversions are the **barn grapple [14:52]** (the player seized, control momentarily inverted) and the **prisoner beating [15:30]** (bia inflicted on the NPC to extract intel).
