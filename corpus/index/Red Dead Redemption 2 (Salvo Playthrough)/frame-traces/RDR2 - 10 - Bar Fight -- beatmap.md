## SALIENCE

This clip is the **discrete, validated specimen** of the protracted **thymotic hexis-bundle**: the disposition toward standing-asserted-by-force. Its cultivation is unambiguously **technē, not ethical virtue** — the saloon brawl drills a pugilistic skill-set (block/dodge/counter, kinesthetic + ludic incorporation) and rewards mastery with a win-state, while supplying *no* practical wisdom about when force is fitting. The clip's own dialogue stages that gap: the fight is *won* well before it is *stopped* ("You won the fight already. Surely that's enough"), and the continuation past victory is the moment skill visibly outruns *sophrosynē*. The **ethical fork** is therefore internal to the act, not a branch the player selects: the same orexis that completes cleanly as competent fighting (A2 charged image → A3 committal *doxa* "Oh, and we have our answer" → A4 brawl) overshoots its telos into a **bia event** — the floored opponent inverted from agent to pure patient, the crowd baying "Kill him, Tommy." The **honor system** is the corpus-relevant payoff: it is a Burkean **motive-attribution machine** that scores the over-beating, adjudicating whether the act belongs to *scene* (drunken provocation) or *agent* (Arthur's temper), and the honor delta is the structural verdict laid over a felt deed. This is the clip's signature contribution to the VR-2D argument: **felt-vs-structural agency**. The player FEELS the punch as chosen and the dominance as owned, yet the brawl is largely on-rails (you cannot decline it) and the moral consequence is delivered as an external score — felt agency and structural agency pried apart in one beat. As the 2D / high-phantasia pole, embodiment here is entirely *phantasia*-supplied: symbolic button input, no haptics, the only somatic register the involuntary flinch at on-screen contact (R2 via phantasia). A secondary, quieter bia mirror closes the clip — Bill's social erasure ("What about you?") replays the agent→patient inversion at the register of standing rather than the body.
