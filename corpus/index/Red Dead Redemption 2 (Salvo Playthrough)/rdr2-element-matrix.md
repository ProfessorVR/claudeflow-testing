## 2D-SPECIFIC INSTRUMENTS (the rhetorical/cinematic machinery that closes the data-discrepancy)

The 2D pole *compensates* for its large data-discrepancy with design-side cueing a VR world can leave implicit. Four standing instruments:

1. **The honor meter** — an external *doxa*-cue that scores acts and externalizes Burke's motive-attribution into UI; its **selective** firing (inert on choke/spare [3a], active on the bar-fight excess [10]) is itself a finding about which acts the design deems legible.
2. **The cinematic third-person camera** — the disembodied watching eye; the body is *watched, not worn*, the phantasia-load made visible against the HMD's embodied POV.
3. **Symbolic controls** — stick/button convention-mapped (the control tutorial [3]); phantasia supplies the felt body of riding, shooting, skinning, punching, drinking. The minigames [5b, 13] are the limit case — the hand is iconographic, no body supplied at all.
4. **UI / HUD cueing** — context-prompts, waypoints, bonding toasts, "?" markers, and the **perception-prostheses** (Eagle Eye = painted A1 salience; Dead Eye = composure-as-time-slow; the legendary aura-filter = specialness-as-UI; the drunk-cam = intoxication-as-screen-warp [12]) — each a 2D scaffold for an eidetic/affective state a body would otherwise hold.

## What the matrix shows (for the RODA reading)

1. **The bundle is dual from the outset** — clip 3 installs the full *technē* schema (movement, gunplay, melee, loot, horse, tracking, weapon-select) *and* the first three honor forks in the same breath; clip 9 runs the *technē* at untutored ceiling (its **silence** is the signature of a mature hexis). The ethical hexis is cultivated alongside, not after, the skill hexis.
2. **The honor system is the corpus's Burke-machine made visible** — and its *selective scoring* is the diagnostic: it grades a thymotic excess (bar-fight) but is mute on a life-or-mercy choice (choke/spare), where only a **bell-toll** marks the act. The machine privatizes collective acts too (the O'Driscoll raid [11] is performed by the whole posse, yet honor attaches to Arthur alone).
3. ***Bia* runs in both directions** — inflicted (Kieran's capture [5], the bounty hogtie [8], the interrogated prisoner [3]) and suffered (the barn grapple [3], the bear maul [9], the soft leash [2], Micah's freeing as narrative *bia* [15]). The bar-fight's overshoot [10] is the seam where a clean A4 *becomes* an agent→patient inversion mid-act.
4. **Embodiment is phantasia-supplied throughout** — the flinch (the bear [9], the muzzle-flash [3,11], the thrown punch [10]) is the 2D pole's only somatic register; the perception-prostheses (Eagle Eye, Dead Eye, drunk-cam) are the screen doing the eidetic/affective work the body would. This is the differential's load-bearing finding: *both* poles incorporate; RDR2 reaches it by phantasia scaling to the larger gap.
5. **Dwelling, not just spectacle** — the camp/chores cluster [6×4] cultivates belonging through tedium (a candidate site for second-form boredom), where low ludic + high shared/spatial/affective = *dwelling, not goal-pursuit*, and the felt body of labor/domesticity ("lift with your knees," the coffee's warmth, the hail-by-name) is entirely phantasia-supplied with no threat to flinch at.
