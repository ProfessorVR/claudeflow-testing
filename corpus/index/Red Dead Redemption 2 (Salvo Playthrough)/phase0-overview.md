1. **Ethical-*hexis* cultivation is a primary design objective** — not a by-product of the skill curriculum; the first interactive clip threads honor events through the control tutorial, and the camp cluster builds belonging by repetition (dwelling, not goal-pursuit).
2. **RDR2 *reaches* the acting-together "WE" that G&G withheld** (frame-confirmed: the raid, bounty, jailbreak are jointly-sourced acts *with allies*) — consubstantiation / co-disclosedness *consummated*, where the VR case held the second pole at its threshold. The sharpest fruit of the VR↔2D pairing.
3. **Two distinct 2D consequence-overlays:** the **WANTED / witness system** (legal — visibly active/confirmed) vs the **honor meter** (moral — its selective scoring a flagged hypothesis). The bar-fight's trigger is *scripted* (felt-vs-structural: a foreclosed *whether*).
4. **The 2D pole throughout** — phantasia supplies the felt body (symbolic controls); embodiment registers as the involuntary flinch; the cinematic opening loads the phantasmatic reservoir; phantasia-load is sharpest where the data is thinnest (the whiteout).
