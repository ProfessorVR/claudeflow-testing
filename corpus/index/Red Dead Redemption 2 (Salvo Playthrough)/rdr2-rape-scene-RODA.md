## Step 0 — Frame

**Circumference.** One discrete episode with a long diachronic shadow: the recorded pass runs from open-world approach (Bayou Nwa, en route past Lakay) through the encounter, the seizure, the relocation, and — in the same recording — the return and the kill. Entry point is **A₀-sense** (ambient solicitation: Sonny's hail), not A₂ or A₃; nothing about this event is sought.

**Change classified.** The headline change is an *alloiōsis* of the **player-substance** — perceptual → affective → dispositional, sedimenting as *hexis* — engineered through a staged *phthora*-mode *paschein* of the **avatar-substance** (clubbed, violated, dumped, drained, robbed). The two substance-series are deliberately torn apart at the event's center: the avatar undergoes what the player is prevented from even witnessing. The world-series change is trivial (a cabin interior state, a dollar); the disproportion between world-change (≈nothing) and player-change (potentially being-in-the-world-deep, per the draft's Kyle evidence) is the event's design signature.

**Kinds and renderer (§2.0).** Everything engaged is **virtual** (engine-rendered on a 2D screen — the high data-discrepancy pole); the assault itself, however, is delivered as a **phantasmic** object: during the ~33 s of black screen there is *no virtual object of the assault at all* — the game renders audio prompts and the player's soul-through-*phantasia* renders the event. The kind-distinction firewall thus does real work here: what traumatizes (or fails to traumatize) is not a depicted virtual scene but each player's own *phantasma*, grounded in their resonant traces. This is why reception varies so radically (Tate's spectrum from "Gayyy" to re-traumatization): different resonant grounds render different events from identical inputs.

**The two four-cause analyses, hinged.** The designer's making supplies: the lure-architecture (hail → banter → skull invitation), the threshold trigger, the cutscene, the sensory withholding, the ledgered hand-back. The player's playing supplies: the approach (her own exploration-*orexis*), the Greet selection, the threshold-crossing, and afterward the vengeance pass. The efficient and final causes of the *approach* are entirely the player's — which is exactly what the design needs, and exactly what Sonny's script then weaponizes ("You struggled and you lost": *your* act, *my* past tense).

**The *orekton*.** Individual, epithymetic-bouletic: curiosity/discovery (the skull collection; more broadly the open world's standing promise that seeking is rewarded). Not collective; the union in play at entry is world-disclosedness (dwelling in the bayou), not co-disclosedness.

The design bets the cultivated *hexis* against the semantic content of the bait — and wins.

**A₃ (02:00–02:29) — the committal *doxa*, recorded.** The tape shows what analysis usually reconstructs: **nineteen-plus seconds of motionless hesitation** at the porch, interaction prompts live, doorway pure black. The ratification, when it comes, is behavioral: the walk begins 02:15. Three *doxa*-layers separate cleanly here: the **resonant** layer (swamp-hermit + skulls = danger, for any encultured adult), the **occurrent** layer (the weapon-check says *suspicion is live*), and the ratifying act, which overrides both — this is a *doxa* carried by curiosity against its own warnings, the precise inverse of the bar-fight's thymos-driven ratification.

**A₄ → the bar-crossing (02:29–02:31.67).** The threshold-crossing is the pass's last agentive act. Then the seam, frame-exact: **at 02:31.67 the minimap and prompt panel vanish and the letterbox snaps on — one step *inside* the doorway.** Control is seized *after* the voluntary act commits, never before. **The seized span (02:31.67–03:41.2, ≈69.5 s).** See the beat-map's event-anatomy table. The structure, compressed: 4 s of visible menace (Sonny's approach, "Now come here.", an object raised [UNCERTAIN: club vs blade], whip-pan — the blow itself *cut away from*); **9 s black** with taunts ("Don't you hate old Sonny now / Don't hate him"); 11.7 s of **degraded low-angle POV** — vignetted, chromatically shifted, from the floor looking up: a hand gripping tan suspenders, Sonny leaning over the camera, "Oh, you struggled... and you lost... but it was quite a tussle, I tell you. The efficient cause is rewritten in retrospect — a rhetorical operation on the pentad itself (the act's Agent-slot reassigned while its Agency was black-screened).

## Step 2 — Union diagnosis: rupture (agency-mode), staged in three tearings

Before the event the union-state is high: a mid-game player near Saint Denis has R1–R5 comfortably (assimilated world, embodied avatar, held simultaneously, spatial cornerstone deep in the *hexis*), R6 approaching apex (this is late-arc free-roam), R7 porous-normal. Incorporation is the *precondition* of the design working — the event is aimed at a player who *dwells*.

The rupture then proceeds not as one break but as three ordered tearings:

1. **R3 simultaneity collapses at 02:31.67** — "joined-and-separate" becomes "acted-upon." Standard *bia*-rupture; the bar-fight beatmap's cutscene moments are its mild siblings.
2. **R2 embodiment is *inverted*, not merely suspended (02:44.71–02:56.42).** The game does not cut to third-person spectacle; it installs the **patient-pole as camera grammar** — the floor-level, semi-conscious, chromatically damaged POV. The player is still *in* the body; the body is now purely undergone. Where ordinary rupture expels ("watching a movie about my avatar"), this holds the player *inside a de-agentified first person*. That is the ontological violence: not the loss of the union but its **conversion** — world-disclosedness maintained while its agent-pole is amputated.
3. **R1 assimilation is starved (the two black spans, ≈33 s, 47% of the seizure).** The world withdraws itself as perceptual object at the moment of maximal mattering. Existence-fragility is here *weaponized as design*: the virtual scene ceases (renderer withholding) while the phantasmic scene is conscripted into being. The union's world-pole goes dark; what fills the darkness is the player's own soul rendering.

**Verdict: ruptured (agency), by design — and the rupture *sources* the next act** (§4.1's headline instrument, negative case): the vengeance pass (05:05–06:11) is a Type-2 thymotic chain-pass whose *orekton* — standing-restored, world-repaired — exists only because the union was torn. Nothing in the game's economy motivates it: the material loss is one dollar. The recorded return, Break-Lock, and kill are motivated entirely by the dispositional wound, i.e., by the rupture itself. (Co-disclosedness note: there is no "WE" anywhere in this event — Sonny's "friendship ain't so tough" is a *parody* of consubstantiation, naming the social union precisely while destroying its possibility.)

## Step 3 — Motive-rhetoric (Burke, absorbed)

**The dominant ratio is Agent–Act run in reverse by the script.** Sonny's lines perform motive-attribution *on the player*: "you struggled and you lost" (your act, completed, defeated); "don't you hate old Sonny now — don't hate him" (pre-emptive management of the victim's attribution); "friendship ain't so tough, and neither is you" (the relation re-described as a test the player failed). The NPC does the pentadic work the honor system elsewhere does — and the honor system itself is **silent**: no honor toast at the event, and none at the retaliatory kill — no gain, no loss, effectively neutral (**AUTHOR-CONFIRMED 2026-07-01**; extends the corpus's selective-scoring finding). The concealed term is **Scene**: everything in the design (the fog, the hail, the skull-lure, the black doorway, the trigger placement) is scenic engineering, but the script's rhetoric relocates the motive into the player-Agent ("what you stood there for?", "you struggled"). The player is invited to attribute to themselves an act the scene constructed.

**Courtship and hierarchy.** The approach dialogue is Burkean courtship in the predatory key — "don't be shy," the coaxing repetitions ("Now come here," twice) — and "my pet" is the hierarchy-term: the relation's end-state named as ownership. Victimage runs in both directions: Sonny makes the player the scapegoat of his "friendship" test; the player's return makes Sonny the scapegoat that discharges the event — and the game scores neither.

## Step 4 — Causal & type diagnosis

**The approach:** voluntary, agent-caused; predominant type **epithymetic-bouletic** (curiosity/acquisition — Type 1 shading into Type 3's wishing-for-discovery); *doxa*-ratified at the threshold after visible deliberation. Terminus: intended as *poiēsis* (loot/discovery-product), actualized as neither.

**The seizure:** **pure *bia*** — the paradigm case the methodology's instrument #3 was built for. The *archē* passes outside the agent at 02:31.67; everything through 03:41.2 is "contrary to the desire or reason of the agent" (*Rhet.* I.10). Two precisions the frames force:

1. ***Bia* with a voluntary on-ramp.** The compulsion is triggered by, and only by, the player's own act — the design's cruelest exactness. This does **not** dissolve the *bia* (the intemperance ≠ compulsion guard cuts the other way: the player's curiosity was voluntary, but nothing in the seized span is), yet it changes its rhetorical structure: the *bia* borrows the *form* of consequence ("you chose to enter") while being, in causal fact, a scripted seizure whose content no threshold-*doxa* could have anticipated (the doorway was black; the interior data withheld). Felt-vs-structural axis: **mechanically chosen entry; everything-after foreclosed.** The felt-agency reading ("I brought this on myself") is itself a designed effect — the Agent-attribution the script cultivates.
2. **De-actualization as destructive *paschein*.** The event drags the player from A₄ agent-pole to A₁ patient-pole in the *phthora* mode (*DA* II.5 417b2–5): not perception's preservative being-affected but an extinguishing — enacted on-screen as the literal degradation of the perceptual faculty itself (vignette, chromatic damage, blackout). The game renders *paschein-as-phthora* as an image-treatment. The cores then quantize it: **all three depleted, all red — health, stamina, and deadeye (AUTHOR-CONFIRMED 2026-07-01;** stamina + health additionally by system toast, 04:12 and ~04:44). The deadeye drain carries the significance the draft assigns it: deadeye is the *technē*-hexis meter — its draining says the assault reached *dispositional* substrate, not just bodily resource.

**The vengeance pass (05:05–06:11):** voluntary, **thymotic (Type 2)**, sourced by the rupture (Step 2); *prohairetic* completion visible in the recorded means-selection (waypoint set, return ride, weapon drawn *before* the door, Break Lock). Terminus: *praxis* (the end immanent — standing restored; no loot-motive precedes the kill). The pass empirically instantiates the draft's reception claim ("many players will return to kill the swamp man"): this recording is itself one of the datapoints.

## Step 5 — Phantasia and the affective specifics

**Phantasia-conscription at its limit (instrument #2, the event's engine).** The methodology's own formula — "withholding can be *more* forceful than showing" — is here executed as architecture: 47% of the seized span is black; the one interior shot shows a hand and suspenders. The world supplies an *under-determined phantasma* (sounds, taunts, a grip, a "my pet") and conscripts the player's *phantasia* to author the assault. Step 5's tracing question — *which doxa-layer supplies each evaluative aspect?* — gets a clean answer:

- The **resonant** layer supplies the sexual-assault reading: belt/suspender manipulation + "my pet" + body-dump + the gait, taken-as rape by any player whose encultured backlog contains the schema. This is the draft's Kyle case.
- A player *without* that resonant material (the draft's child case) assembles a different *phantasma* from identical data: robbery — and the design **corroborates it**: the −$1.00 debit is real, on-screen, the world's own receipt for the innocent reading. The frames sharpen the draft here: the game does not merely *permit* the child's interpretation, it *funds* it with a UI event. The dollar is dual-purpose — insult-signature under the adult *doxa*, complete explanation under the child's.
- The **occurrent** layer is Arthur's: "Oh my Lord..." (03:27.67) — a ratification that something happened, with the *what* left unarticulated. The script keeps the avatar's own *doxa* as under-determined as the player's evidence.
- The **deliberative** layer is deferred out of the game entirely — to Bill Williamson's later camp dialogue (the draft's evidence; outside this clip), to forums, to Tate's interviews: the event is designed to be *ratified in retrospect and in public*, which is how it becomes a site for further rhetorical activity (Rickert's furtherance, the draft's closing move).

**The pathetic loop.** Inside the seizure the loop is *imposed* rather than emergent: the taunts arrive during blackout, when the corrective faculty has nothing perceptual to check the inflected *phantasma* against — fear/disgust escalates unopposed because the design has removed the very data a *De Insomniis* correction would need. The emotion-loop is run by the world, on schedule.

**Three magnitude axes (never one scale).** Hedonic intensity: high-negative but *brief* in peaks (the taunt-lines). Existential weight: **maximal** — this is the event's true register; it bears on the player's mattering-structure (safety-in-dwelling, ownership of the avatar-body) rather than on any resource. Cognitive articulation: **deliberately low** — the event resists propositional capture (nothing definitive is shown; even Arthur gets no sentence). High-weight/low-articulation is the trauma-signature the reception data then exhibits: players who "can't say what happened but can't put it down."

**The embodiment note (2D pole).** The draft's "saddle soreness" is frame-bounded: stooped shuffle 03:31–03:34.6, straightening over ~0.2 s. On the 2D pole this is where R2-via-phantasia does its sharpest work — the player *reads* a hurt they were never allowed to feel, and the reading recruits the somatic backlog (everyone knows what walking-hurt looks like from inside). The body is the event's only uncensored witness.

## Step 6 — Diachronic: carry-over and the supplanting verdict

**What sediments.** (i) An ethical-hexis deposit typed by the *praxis* route: a standing wariness-toward-solicitation — the world's hails are re-weighted (the next "come here, stranger" meets a changed resonant *doxa*). (ii) A Stimmung deposit: the bayou (and Lakay-adjacent space) is re-toned; the A₁ basic-valence of this region carries the event. (iii) The vengeance discharge *within* the recording partially metabolizes the thymotic residue — but note what the design withholds even here: no honor delta, no journal-closure shown, no economy of repair. The kill un-does nothing on the ledger (the dollar is not recovered in any observed frame); the discharge is purely dispositional.

**The carry-over (the capstone mechanism, §4.3).** The draft's Kyle evidence is the virtual→actual crossing this event was built to demonstrate: a 17-year-old's standing *doxa* about rape victims ("just claw at the dude's eyes") is **supplanted** — not amended but replaced — by one pass of undergone helplessness. In RODA terms: the rupture forced a *phthora*-mode *paschein* on a maximally-incorporated player; the resulting *phantasma*-complex (helplessness-from-inside) entered the resonant ground; and the porosity of the union (R7 — this was never a sealed magic circle) carried it out of the game, where it now conditions non-game judgment (victim-blaming rejected; "a new perspective on rape"). **Supplanting verdict: achieved** — for the dispositional region the event targets (the *doxa*-cluster around sexual violence and agency), and precisely *because* the event's core was phantasmic rather than depicted: what the player carries out is not an image the game showed them but one *their own soul rendered*, which is why it is held with first-person authority. The foreclosed-rupture limit (instrument #9) is the contrast case: a design that showed everything would have produced spectacle and *doxa*-confirmation; this design's dissimilarity — its refusal of data at the crux — is what individualizes the player out of absorbed routine and makes ontological change possible.

**The differential note (VR pole).** On the VR pole this event would be *undesignable* at equal force in the same way: the conscription runs on the data-discrepancy, and VR's narrowed discrepancy would demand either depiction (spectacle; likely mere horror) or an HMD-blackout whose phenomenology (sensory deprivation while embodied) is a different — arguably crueler — instrument. The event as built is **native to the high-phantasia pole**: it is the 2D medium's data-poverty turned into its most powerful rhetorical asset. This belongs in the media-general differential section as the limit-case where *larger* data-discrepancy yields *greater* ontological force — the inverse-phantasia-load axis run to its endpoint, and a standing caution against reading the bandwidth axis as a potency ranking.

## Step 7 — Diagnosis (one paragraph)

A maximally-incorporated player is drawn by her own cultivated exploration-*hexis* through a courtship-structured lure into a threshold-triggered *bia* that seizes control one step past the last revocable point; the union is not broken but **converted** — world-disclosedness held open while the agent-pole is amputated, the patient-pole installed as camera grammar, and the event's core delivered as ~33 seconds of engineered darkness that conscripts the player's own *phantasia* to author the assault her screen refuses to show. The act is non-agent-caused (*bia*) with a voluntary on-ramp whose form the script then weaponizes into false Agent-attribution ("you struggled and you lost"); the hand-back itemizes the harm in the only vocabulary the system speaks (−$1.00, cores emptied, a resting tip); the body testifies in a 3.5-second gait; and the honor machine says nothing — before or after the player's rupture-sourced thymotic return and kill. What sediments is not knowledge of a depicted scene but a first-person-authored *phantasma* of helplessness, and it is exactly this phantasmic (not virtual) delivery that gives the event its carry-over force: the supplanting of the player's standing *doxai* about violation and agency — ontological change achieved by a designed rupture whose medium is the player's own soul.
