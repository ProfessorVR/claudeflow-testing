# RDR2 as the 2D Pole — the VR↔Screen Differential, Demonstrated

**Purpose:** apply the methodology's **VR↔2D differential** (`…/methodology/…/vr-vs-screen-differential.md`) to *Red Dead Redemption 2* as the **2D-screen / large-data-discrepancy / high-phantasia-load** pole, paired against *Gnomes & Goblins* (VR / small-discrepancy / low-load). The pairing is a **controlled comparison that isolates phantasia's gap-filling work** — vary the data discrepancy, watch phantasia's labor vary. *(Footage-independent first draft, 2026-06-27; to be enriched with the per-clip beat-maps + frame-reads.)*

## Thesis (carried from the differential module)
Incorporation — and through it ontological change — is reachable in **both** 2D and VR. They do **not** differ in *whether* they incorporate the player, but in the **route**, set by the **data discrepancy**: the gap between the sensorial data the medium presents and the full sensorial field of the non-virtual referent. **Phantasia fills that gap.** RDR2 (flat display, controller input) has a *large* discrepancy → demands *more* phantasia gap-filling and *more* design-side cueing; G&G (HMD, tracked body) a *small* one → demands less. **Both reach full incorporation because phantasia scales to the gap.** Not a ceiling — a difference of degree along the data axis (technology-relative; the metaphysics' sensorium-injection limit case).

## Where RDR2 demonstrates it (anchors in clips 1–16)
- **Symbolic coupling, established:** the control tutorial (clip 3b — *Movement, Combat, Inventory, Camera*) is the explicit installation of convention-mapped controls; the **five-finger-fillet** (clip 5b) and **gambling** (clip 13) are pure symbolic-rhythm skills where phantasia supplies no body at all — the hand is *iconographic*.
- **R2-via-phantasia (the flinch):** the **bar-fight** (clip 10, the validated discrete beat) — a thymotic act executed through a flat image, where the felt-vs-structural agency gap (freedom in the *how*, not the *whether*) is sharpest; combat and the **legendary/bear hunt** (clip 9) are the flinch-inducing beats.
- **Phantasmatic subtraction by world-density:** camp life (clips 6a–d), the foggy/atmospheric world, NPC routines — the 2D world earns enclosure by *richness of meaning-detail*, not by occupying the eyes.
- **The honor system as a 2D motive-overlay:** clips 3a (choke vs spare), 07, 08, 11 — RDR2 externalizes Burke's motive-attribution into a **scored meter**, a design-supplied *doxa*-cue that a VR world tends to leave implicit. This is a 2D-specific rhetorical instrument worth its own note.
- **The cinematic camera:** the third-person, disembodied eye (vs the embodied HMD POV) is RDR2's standing reminder that the body is *watched*, not *worn* — the phantasia-load made visible.

## The controlled comparison (why the pairing earns its place)
G&G (small discrepancy, low phantasia-load) and RDR2 (large discrepancy, high load) **vary the data axis and hold the phenomenon constant** — both actualize desire toward incorporation along the A₀–A₄ chain. Watching phantasia's labor scale *up* from G&G to RDR2 makes the comparison a **study of phantasia itself** (Part I's title), not a ranking of "VR vs 2D power." The G&G **miniaturization** (a *wondrous* productive rupture) and the RDR2 **capstone** (an *agency-stripping* rupture: *bia* + phantasia-conscription) then show the **two routes to the same ontological end** — the dissimilar-shock route and the force route.

## Caveats (do not overclaim)
- Reachability is **symmetric**; the difference is **data-discrepancy magnitude → phantasia-load**, plus rupture texture. Not a wall.
- Technology-relative (the metaphysics' sensorium-injection limit).
- Carry-over evidence on the VR side is a single-subject report (the G&G AWS self-experiment); the RDR2 side's ontological claim leans on the capstone, analyzed separately.
- **Metaphysics → methodology seam:** the *data discrepancy* is introduced in the metaphysics and *deployed* here — reinforcing metaphysics-first ordering.
