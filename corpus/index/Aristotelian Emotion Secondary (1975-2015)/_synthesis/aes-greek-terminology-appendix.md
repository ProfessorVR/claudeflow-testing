# Greek Terminology Appendix — Aristotelian Emotion Secondary (1975–2015)

Lemmas deployed by ≥2 units, canonical form + gloss + units, with **contested glosses** flagged (where the units use a term to opposite argumentative ends). Units: F, K, MA, MG, D.

## Cognition / acceptance (the disputed core)
| Lemma | Gloss | Units | Contested? |
|---|---|---|---|
| *phantasia* | appearance-capacity; "being-appeared-to" (Moss); a movement from actual perception | MA MG D K | **YES** — MA/MG: the *seat* of the passions' cognition; F: not a category at all; D: the capacity *whose content is affirmed* |
| *phantasma* | the appearance/image produced by *phantasia* | MA MG D | — |
| *phainetai / phainomenon* | appears / what appears (technical: a *phantasia*) | MA MG D F | F reads *phainomenon* ("apparent" slight/evil) simply as *the thought that p* |
| *doxa* | belief, opinion | F K MA MG D | **YES** — F/K: constitutive of emotion; MA/MG: *excluded* from the passionate part; D: distinct from the passions' *affirmation* |
| *doxazein* | to believe/opine | F D | — |
| *hupolēpsis* | supposition, taking-to-be | K MG MA D | K sides with *hupolēpsis* (Andronicus) over bare *phantasia* (Aspasius) for the *Rhetoric* emotions |
| *krisis* | judgment, discrimination, decision | F K MG D | K: the II.1 definition is *by effect on* *krisis* |
| *krinein* | to judge/discriminate | F D MG MA | — |
| *pistis* | conviction; (rhet.) proof | D MA MG | D: arousing passion gives grounds for *pistis*; *pistis* ≠ the passions' affirmation |
| *synkatathesis* | (Stoic) assent | MA MG | both: Aristotle's passions are *not* Stoic assent (but for different reasons) |
| *nous* | intellect | F MA MG D | — |
| *dianoia / logismos / logistikon* | thought / reasoning / the reasoning faculty | F MA MG | MA: *doxastikon = logistikon* (NE VI.5) |
| *dokein* | to seem/think | MA MG | Moss: Aristotle's *dokein*-usage in *Insomn.* is looser than *phainesthai* |
