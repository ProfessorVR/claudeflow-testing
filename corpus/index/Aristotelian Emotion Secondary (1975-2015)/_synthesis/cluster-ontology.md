## Canonical READING nodes (per pole)

**Doxastic pole.** [F-R1/R2] emotion's essential definition names a constitutive belief as efficient cause; *phantasia* not deployed. [K-R1/R2/R6] *pathē* are social construals/suppositions (*hupolēpsis*); the II.1 definition (judgment-change) is the real definition.

**Phantastic pole.** [MG-R1/R4/R5] the passions rest on literal *phantasiai*, seated in a belief-incapable non-rational part; the apparent good is a quasi-perceptual appearance. [MA-R3/R4/R5] *akrasia* = the practical equivalent of perceptual illusion; non-rational desire wins by undermining intellect. [MG-R6/MA-R6] acceptance is default-yielding, non-doxastic.

**Affirmation (intermediate).** [D-R4/R5] in having a passion the subject *herself* affirms *phantasia*'s content as veridical — a gradable, person-level treating-as-true distinct from belief/*pistis*. [D-R6] the sun-illusion is *disanalogous* to recalcitrant passion. [D-R8] evaluative *phantasmata* have necessary motivational consequences.

**Cross-cutting / convergent.** [MA-R2/MG-R3] practical cognition is in the first place cognition-of-the-good (a pleasant *phantasia* *is* an evaluative one) — un-Humean, and matching the dissertation's dual-resonance. [K-R3/F-R9] animals lack genuine *pathē* (rational requirement). [K-R4] grief is not a *pathos*. [MG-R7] *phantasia* normally triggers a corresponding belief (the two-tier seam). [D-R10/MA] animals share the basic processes.

## Debate-axis assignment (canonical)
Axis 1 (doxastic↔phantastic) is the cluster's organizing axis; every reading positions on it. F at the doxastic extreme; MA/MG at the phantastic extreme; K judgmental-but-social; **D the intermediate affirmation term that the dissertation should adopt.**
