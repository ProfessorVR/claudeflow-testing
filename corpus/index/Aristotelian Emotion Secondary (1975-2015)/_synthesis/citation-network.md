# Citation Network — Aristotelian Emotion Secondary (1975–2015)

Who cites/answers whom **within** the five-unit cluster (intra-cluster), and the immediate lineage. (Non-cluster scholars are catalogued in `aes-secondary-literature-network.md`.)

## Intra-cluster edges
- **Konstan → Fortenbaugh**: endorses the cognitivist reading (emotions involve judgment) but **corrects** Fortenbaugh's "local definition" claim; quotes Fortenbaugh (2002 ed., p. 94) approvingly on animal-emotion as *analogical* usage. *Relation: extends + qualifies.*
- **Dow → Fortenbaugh**: inherits the cognitivist/belief lineage and the passions-as-pleasures-and-pains thesis (developed in Dow 2011, reused in the 2015 book). *Relation: extends.*
- **Dow → Moss (2009/2012)**: defines his **affirmation** position explicitly *against* Moss's sub-personal "acceptance," claiming to advance beyond it (Ch. 10 n.48); **inverts** Moss's use of the *DA* III.3 sun passage. *Relation: contests-reading-of + extends.*
- **Dow 2015 → Dow 2011 → Dow 2009 (self-citation arc)**: reuses the 2011 emotion-theory chapter; **self-corrects** the 2009 "mere appearance" reading. *Relation: extends + contests (own prior view).*
- **Moss-AG (2012) ⊇ Moss-Akrasia (2009)**: the 2009 article *is* Ch. 5 of the 2012 book. *Relation: contains.*
- **Moss → Konstan / Fortenbaugh**: not direct citation targets of Moss's analytic argument, but her phantastic account is the standing rival to their judgmental readings. *Relation: contests (implicit).*

## Hubs
- **Fortenbaugh (1975)** = the **origin hub**: cited/inherited by Konstan and (via the pleasures/pains lineage) Dow. The cluster's chronological and doxastic anchor.
- **Moss (2009/2012)** = the **pivot hub**: the position against which Dow defines himself, and the standing challenge to the judgmental camp.
- **Dow (2015)** = the **terminus**: cites/answers both Moss and the Fortenbaugh lineage, producing the intermediate (affirmation) synthesis.

## Directed summary (source → relation → target)
- Konstan → extends/qualifies → Fortenbaugh
- Dow → extends → Fortenbaugh (pleasures/pains)
- Dow → contests-reading-of → Moss (acceptance; sun-illusion)
- Dow(2015) → contests → Dow(2009) [self-correction]
- Moss-AG → contains → Moss-Akrasia
- Moss → contests (implicit) → Fortenbaugh/Konstan (judgmental account)

**Network shape:** a chronological spine (F → K, F → D) with a late cross-link (D ⟂ M) that closes the loop — the cluster is a genuine *debate*, not a citation-tree, with the latest unit (Dow) adjudicating between the earliest (Fortenbaugh) and the middle (Moss).
