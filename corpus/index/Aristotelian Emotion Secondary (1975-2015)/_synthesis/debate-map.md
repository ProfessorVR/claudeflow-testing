# Debate Map — Aristotelian Emotion Secondary Cluster (1975–2015)

Structured catalogue of where the five units take **incompatible positions**, complementary to `contested-readings.md` (which argues them). Units: F (Fortenbaugh), K (Konstan), MA (Moss-Akrasia), MG (Moss-Apparent-Good), D (Dow).

## Positions table (author × debate-axis)

| Debate axis | Fortenbaugh (1975) | Konstan (2006) | Moss (2009/2012) | Dow (2015) |

| **1. constitutive cognition** (doxastic↔phantastic) | **belief / *doxa-krisis*** (efficient cause) | **supposition / *hupolēpsis*** (social) | ***phantasia*** (non-doxastic) | **affirmation** (intermediate; *phantasia* affirmed) |
| **2. evaluative content** (judgment↔quasi-perceptual) | propositional judgment | propositional appraisal | quasi-perceptual appearance | representational pleasure/pain |

## Explicit disagreements (10)

1. **Belief vs. appearance as the constitutive cognition.** F/K (judgmental) ⟂ MA/MG (phantastic); D intermediate. *Locus:* Rhet. II.1–11; DA III.3 428a; DA III.7 431a13–14.
2. **The sun-illusion (DA III.3 428b): proof of phantastic motivation (Moss) vs. disanalogy from recalcitrant emotion (Dow).** MG/MA ⟂ D. *The same passage, opposite verdicts.*
3. **Is the seat of passion capable of *doxa*?** MG: no (belief-incapable non-rational part) ⟂ F: yes (habituated alogical opinions) ⟂ D: it affirms but does not believe. *Locus:* DA III.7; NE I.13.
4. **Mode of acceptance:** Moss's "default yielding" (privative) ⟂ Dow's "person-level affirmation" (positive); both ⟂ Stoic *synkatathesis*. *Locus:* Insomn. 459a6–8, 461b29–462a8; SVF.
5. **Animal *pathē*:** K + F deny (analogical only) ⟂ D ("same processes by degree") + MA/MG (phantastic basis present). *Locus:* HA; Rhet.; DA III.3 429a5–8.
6. **Is grief a *pathos*?** K: no ⟂ (latent) the phantastic account would admit it. *Locus:* Rhet. II catalogue; lupe = "pain."
7. **What defines emotion — the II.1 generic clause or the per-emotion definitions?** K: II.1 (judgment-change) ⟂ F: per-emotion (constitutive thought) ⟂ D: pleasures/pains thesis ⟂ M: II.1 as symptom.
8. **Recalcitrance mechanism:** Moss (undermining/default) ⟂ Dow (necessary phantasmatic motivation + degree-graded covering-over). *Locus:* Insomn. 460b3–16; DMA 8 701b33.
9. **Did Aristotle change his mind / is the account stable?** D self-corrects (Dow 2009 → 2015, from "mere appearance" to "affirmation"); Moss treats the corpus as unified; F treats it as systematically coherent across treatises.
10. **Anti-Humanism — mechanism:** Moss (phantasia-of-the-good *is* desire-inducing) ⟂ F (constitutive belief makes the end right) ⟂ D (evaluative phantasmata necessarily motivate).

## Convergences (worth noting, not contested)
- All reject the non-cognitivist "feeling" view of emotion.
- All reject the Humean means-end division of cognition.
- All anchor on Rhet. II.1 + the per-emotion definitions + DA III.3 / DA III.7.
- MA, MG, D all read the *de Insomniis* "covering-over" mechanism as operating **within a single episode** (synchronic) — convergence with direct bearing on the dissertation's T2-1.
