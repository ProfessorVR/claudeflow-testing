## Cross-pipeline bridges (capped)
- **→ Aristotelian Phantasia Secondary (1985–2017)** (existing cluster): via Nussbaum 1978, Modrak 1987, Burnyeat/Schofield on *phantasia*; **Moss vs. Caston/Frede** on what *phantasia* is and does is the live cross-cluster contest.
- **→ Heidegger BCAP / Uncomfortable Situations (Gross)**: via the anti-cognitivist-reduction line (Gross contra Nussbaum/Sorabji "brain science") and the social-emotion reading (Konstan ↔ Gross).
- **→ Corcilius (2011/2013)** (in corpus): non-rational pleasure/pain/desire; the animal-motion / somatic-substrate questions adjacent to Moss's physiology of *pathē*.

## Field shape
The surrounding literature clusters around **Nussbaum** (the most cross-cited and most contested), with two tributaries: an **akrasia/moral-psychology** stream (Charles, Irwin, Lorenz, Pickavé/Whiting, Gallop — feeding Moss) and a **rhetoric/social-emotion** stream (Striker, Sorabji — feeding Konstan and Dow). The *phantasia* commentators (Burnyeat, Schofield, Modrak) are the hinge to the existing Phantasia Secondary cluster.
