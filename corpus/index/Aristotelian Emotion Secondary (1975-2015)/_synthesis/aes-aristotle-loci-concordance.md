## Keystone convergence loci (the debate's pivots)

1. **★ *DA* III.3 427b21–24** — "when we *think* something fearful, emotion follows; when we merely *imagine*, we are unaffected (as before a painting)." **F, MG, D.** *The* passage the dissertation uses to install the *doxa*-gate (diss. 1120). F reads "think" as belief; D reads it as the *affirmation* that recognizing-image-as-image withholds; MG/MA read it as *phantasia* without uptake. **The cluster's verdict: this passage underdetermines "belief" — it shows affirmation/uptake, not full *doxa*.**
2. **★ *DA* III.3 428a18–b9 (sun a foot wide)** — **MA, MG, D.** The illusion test. Moss: proof *phantasia* ≠ *doxa* and motivation tracks appearance. Dow: *disanalogy* from recalcitrant emotion. (See contested-readings §2.)
3. **★ *DA* III.7 431a8–14 ("one in substrate, different in being"; *aisthētikon/orektikon*)** — **F, MA, MG, D.** The tri-identity the dissertation builds its *resonant epithymia* on (diss. 861–876). **MG deploys it to argue the seat is *belief-incapable*** — an objection the dissertation inherits.
4. **★ *DA* I.1 403a3–b2 (enmattered accounts; anger = boiling blood / desire for retaliation)** — **F, K, MA, MG, D (all five).** The form/matter (dialectician/physicist) definition. Dissertation's enmattered-accounts section (diss. 1114–1116).
5. **★ *DA* III.10 433a9–b12 (desire + cognition move the animal; the apparent good; logos vs appetite re future/now)** — **MA, MG, D.** The action passage; dissertation's three-factor / deliberative-phantasia material (diss. 1004, 1101).
6. **★ *Rhetoric* II.1 1377b31–1378a22 (the generic definition; pathē change judgments; "things appear different to the angry/calm")** — **F, K, MA, MG, D (all five).** The judgment-modification clause (diss. *pathetic* loop, 1138–1140) **and** the appearances-distortion passage (Rh. 1377b31, which the dissertation does *not* currently cite but should — it is Moss's evidence that *pathē* generate appearances).
7. **★ *Rhetoric* II.2 1378a30–32 (anger = desire for revenge for an apparent slight, with pain)** — **F, K, D, MG.** The paradigm per-emotion definition (diss. 1111).
8. **★ *Rhetoric* II.5 1382a21–22 (fear = pain from imagining an impending evil)** — **F, K, D, MG.** Grounds fear on *phantasia* (diss. 1061, 1219) — the tension with the dissertation's *doxa*-gate.
9. **★ *de Insomniis* 460b16–20 (the judging faculty ≠ the phantasma faculty; sun) + 460b3–16 (pathē deceive; coward/lover/feverish)** — **MA, MG, D.** The corrective-faculty mechanism = the dissertation's *pathetic*-loop passage (diss. 1144–1146); the cluster reads it **within a single episode (synchronic)** — bears on T2-1.
10. **★ *de Motu Animalium* 701b33–702a19 (the causal chain: phantasia → desire → affections → organic parts → movement)** — **F, MA, MG, D.** The somatic-preparation passage (diss. 1130–1134). D anchors the *necessary* motivational force of evaluative phantasmata here.
11. ***EE* VII.2 1235b25–29 ("the pleasant appears good even to those who do not believe it so; *phantasia* and belief are not in the same part of the soul")** — **MA, MG.** Moss's keystone for the phantastic account; the cluster's sharpest single anti-doxastic text.
12. ***Metaphysics* XII.7 1072a27 (the apparent good = object of appetite; the real good = object of wish)** — **MA, MG.** (The dissertation cites the surrounding 1072a21–29 at lines 51/107.)
13. ***NE* I.13 1102a26–1103a8 (the alogical/logical bipartition; the obedient non-rational part)** — **F, MA, MG, D.** The seat-of-passion psychology.
14. ***NE* VII.3 1146b8–1147b18 (akrasia: "has and doesn't have" knowledge; the syllogism; beasts not akratic)** — **MA, D.** The recalcitrance locus classicus.
