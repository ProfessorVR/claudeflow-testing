# Social Presence in Virtual Worlds (Part III)

A compact three-source index entry built 2026-07-20 (Phase 2 of the new-media presence ingestion work). Purpose: to
revise the Part III introduction's hedged "social interior" passage (see `drafting-loci.md` for the target text).

## Sources / units
- **svw-01** — Boellstorff, "For Whom the Ontology Turns: Theorizing the Digital Real" (*Current Anthropology* 57.4, 2016) — ontological anchor: the digital is real.
- **svw-02** — Davis & Boellstorff, "Compulsive Creativity: Virtual Worlds, Disability, and Digital Capital" (*International Journal of Communication* 10, 2016) — empirical anchor: an inhabited Second Life community.
- **svw-03** — Champion, "Social Presence and Cultural Presence in Oblivion" (Perth DAC 2007) — conceptual anchor: social vs. cultural presence; social presence without multiplayer.

## What the cluster argues
The three sources triangulate one question — *is a virtual world a real place where people can be together, and what
makes it so?* — from anthropology (svw-01, svw-02) and game studies / VE design (svw-03). Together they **confirm** the
existence and inhabited/documented character of socially-capable virtual worlds and the "general-purpose platform
adapted, not curriculum-authored" characterization, while **sharpening** the passage's equation of social interior with
multiplayer + proximity-voice (Champion: social presence can be authored without multiplayer). See
`cluster-ontology.md` §3.
