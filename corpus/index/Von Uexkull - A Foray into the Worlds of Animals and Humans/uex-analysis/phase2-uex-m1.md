## 2. Hierarchical Outline

### Conclusion of Prior Section (pp. 139–140)
1. Nature as contradictory object across scientific environments (p. 139)
2. Nature as unknowable subject behind all produced worlds — conceals itself forever (p. 139)

### Carriers of Meaning (pp. 143–149)
1. **Against the object-illusion** (pp. 143–144)
   - Animals appear free but are bound to a dwelling-world (Wohnwelt) (p. 143)
   - Each animal cuts its dwelling-world from an enclosing world (p. 143)
   - Critique of American maze experiments: the assumption that animals relate to "objects" is false (pp. 143–144)
2. **The stone example: meaning-conferral** (pp. 144–145)
   - Same stone, unchanged physical properties, but transforms from path-stone (Wegton) to thrown projectile (Wurfton) (p. 144)
   - A relationless object becomes a Bedeutungstraeger (carrier of meaning) when entering relationship with a subject (p. 144)
   - No animal is an observer; no animal relates to objects qua objects (p. 144)
3. **Glass bowl: leading vs. supporting properties** (pp. 144–145)
   - Same bowl becomes window (transparency leads) or vase (curvature leads) (pp. 144–145)
   - Properties acquire rank (essential vs. accidental) only when object becomes carrier of meaning (p. 145)
   - Scholastic essentia/accidentia reinterpreted: applies only to meaning-carriers, not to relationless objects (p. 145)
4. **Ladder/fence example: Leistungston** (p. 145)
   - Same pole-structure becomes ladder (climbing tone) or fence (function tone) (p. 145)
   - Spatial structure plan emerges only in the carrier of meaning (p. 145)
5. **We treat meaning-carriers as if they were mere objects** (pp. 145–146)
   - House and its contents treated as objectively existent; human subject excluded from picture (p. 145)

### Environment and Dwelling-shell (pp. 146–149)
6. **Dog-in-the-house thought experiment** (pp. 146–147)
   - Sarris's experiments: dog's "chair" = any dog-seat; carriers of meaning exchangeable within a Ton-class (p. 146)
   - Dog assigns sitting tone, feeding tone, drinking tone, climbing tone, obstacle tone to household things (pp. 146–147)
   - Most human objects are "junk" (Geruempel) to the dog (p. 147)
7. **The forest: subject-multiplied meaning** (pp. 147–148)
   - Sombart quotation: no objectively given forest, only forester-forest, hunter-forest, botanist-forest, etc. (p. 147)
   - Meaning multiplied thousandfold when animal subjects are included (p. 147)
8. **Flower-stem paradigm: four environments** (pp. 147–149)
   - Girl: stem = ornament; ant: stem = path; spittle-bug larva: stem = spigot; cow: stem = clump of food (pp. 147–148)
   - Same components are ripped from the plant's construction plan and reinserted into different environmental construction plans (p. 148)
   - Each carrier of meaning meets a "complement" (Komplement) in the subject's body, which is the consumer of meaning (Bedeutungsverbraucher) (p. 148)
9. **Planned quality of environment vs. body** (pp. 148–149)
   - Bodily construction plan and environmental construction plan seem opposed but are equally closed (p. 148)
   - Each environment is a self-enclosed unit governed by meaning for the subject (p. 148)
   - Spatial extent of the life stage depends on the subject's sensory capacity (pp. 148–149)
10. **Perception marks and effect marks on carriers of meaning** (pp. 149)
    - Every carrier of meaning has dual structure: perception-mark carrier + effect-mark carrier (p. 149)
    - The effect mark cancels the perception mark in every case, completing the action (p. 149)
    - Every action impresses meaning on a meaningless object, making it a subject-related carrier of meaning (p. 149)
11. **Functional cycles by meaning** (p. 149)
    - Most important cycles: medium, nourishment, enemy, sex (p. 149)

## 6. Cross-Pipeline References

### 6a. Heidegger B&T Connections
- **Bedeutungstraeger / carriers of meaning** --> Heidegger's analysis of Zuhandenheit (readiness-to-hand, B&T Sections 15–18): equipment is what it is through its Bewandtnis (involvement), not through objective properties. Uexkuell's "relationless object" maps to Vorhandenheit (presence-at-hand); the Bedeutungstraeger maps to the ready-to-hand entity within a referential totality. [INTERP-high]
- **Wohnwelt / dwelling-world** --> Heidegger's "Wohnen" (dwelling) in the later work and the existential spatiality of Dasein (B&T Sections 22–24). The dwelling-shell as self-enclosed totality resonates with Heidegger's In-der-Welt-sein as always already dwelling. [INTERP-high]
- **Leading vs. supporting properties** --> Heidegger's critique of substance-ontology (B&T Sections 19–21): the "objective" properties of a thing are derivative from its practical involvement. Uexkuell's reinterpretation of essentia/accidentia as meaning-relative parallels Heidegger's claim that categorical determinations presuppose the existential structure of worldhood. [INTERP-high]
- **"No animal relates to an object"** --> Heidegger's later thesis in Fundamental Concepts of Metaphysics (1929/30) that the animal is "world-poor" (weltarm) and does not encounter entities "as" entities. Uexkuell may be a direct source for this position. [INTERP-high]
- **Sombart's forest** --> Resonates with Heidegger's claim that world is not a container of objects but a meaning-structure relative to Dasein. [INTERP-high]
- **Effect mark cancels perception mark** --> Parallels the disappearance of the tool in smooth use (Zuhandenheit); the action's completion erases the perceptual salience that prompted it. [INTERP-high]

### 6b. BCAP Connections
- Uexkuell's "tone" (Ton) vocabulary (Wegton, Wurfton, Sitzton) echoes Aristotle's concept of dynamis and the capacity of things to be "for the sake of" (hou heneka). In BCAP, Heidegger reads Aristotle's physis and techne as self-disclosing; Uexkuell's Bedeutungstraeger could be read as the biological operationalization of the Aristotelian final cause. [INTERP-high — textual hook only]

### 6c. Rickert Connections
- Uexkuell's attack on "relationless objects" and the claim that properties gain rank only through meaning-relations echoes Rickert's value-reference (Wertbeziehung) thesis: objects become scientifically meaningful only through value-orientation. Both reject naive objectivism but from different directions (biology vs. epistemology). [INTERP-high — textual hook only]
