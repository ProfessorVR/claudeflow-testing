# Von Uexkull German Terminology Appendix

Phase 3 deliverable. Alphabetical listing of all unique German terms extracted from Section 8 of Phase 2 analysis files (U-F0 through U-M6). 100 lemmas, de-duplicated across all 14 units.

---

### angeborener Weg (inborn path)
- **German**: angeborener Weg
- **O'Neil translation**: inborn path
- **Alternative translations**: innate path
- **Forms encountered**: angeborener Weg, Erfahrungsweg (its learned counterpart)
- **Definition**: Innate sequence of perception and effect signs guiding behavior without prior experience; structurally identical to the familiar path but given as an innate melody.
- **Key pages**: pp. 126-128
- **Units**: U-F7
- **Translation contested**: false
- **Canonical node link**: bekannter Weg

### Autodermin (autodermin)
- **German**: Autodermin
- **O'Neil translation**: autodermin
- **Alternative translations**: --
- **Forms encountered**: Autodermin
- **Definition**: Chemical self-recognition substance in sea urchin skin that blocks receptors on self-contact, maintaining civil peace among reflex persons.
- **Key pages**: p. 77
- **Units**: U-F2
- **Translation contested**: false
- **Canonical node link**: Reflexrepublik

### Außenfront / Innenfront (outer front / inner front)
- **German**: Außenfront / Innenfront
- **O'Neil translation**: outer front / inner front
- **Alternative translations**: external front / internal front
- **Forms encountered**: Außenfront, Innenfront
- **Definition**: Double structure of sensory organs. Outer front: sieve for physicochemical effects. Inner front: living carillon (Glockenspiel) of cell-bells with self-tones. Perception signs (Merkzeichen) transposed outward become perception marks (Merkmale).
- **Key pages**: pp. 169-170
- **Units**: U-M3
- **Translation contested**: false
- **Canonical node link**: Eigenton, Merkzeichen

### Bauplan (construction plan / body-plan)
- **German**: Bauplan
- **O'Neil translation**: construction plan / body plan
- **Alternative translations**: structural plan, blueprint
- **Forms encountered**: Bauplan, Bauplanregel, Bauplanmelodie
- **Definition**: Structural form-plan governing the composition of either an organism or an environmental meaning-carrier. Bauplanregel: embryonic rule governing organ formation. Bauplanmelodie: species-specific melody determining how cells build organ form.
- **Key pages**: pp. 145, 148, 156-167, 171-172, 184
- **Units**: U-M1, U-M2, U-M3, U-M4, U-M6
- **Translation contested**: true -- Bauplan in biology usually means body plan; Uexkull extends it to environmental structure. Bauplanmelodie loses the musical metaphor in O'Neil's "form development order."
- **Canonical node link**: Bedeutungsregel, Urpartitur

### Bedeutung (meaning)
- **German**: Bedeutung
- **O'Neil translation**: meaning
- **Alternative translations**: significance
- **Forms encountered**: Bedeutung, Bedeutungsbeziehungen (relations of meaning)
- **Definition**: Functional significance an object holds for an organism within its Umwelt. Relations of meaning are the "only certain guides" in Umwelt research.
- **Key pages**: pp. 84, 139-149
- **Units**: U-F3, U-M1, U-M2, U-M3, U-M4, U-M5, U-M6
- **Translation contested**: true -- "meaning" risks overintellectualizing; "significance" (Gadamer's preference) may be closer.
- **Canonical node link**: Umwelt, Funktionskreis

### Bedeutungsduldung (sufferance of meaning)
- **German**: Bedeutungsduldung
- **O'Neil translation**: sufferance of meaning
- **Alternative translations**: meaning-endurance, passive reception of meaning
- **Forms encountered**: Bedeutungsduldung
- **Definition**: Reception of meaning contrary to the receiving subject's interest. Graded from harmless (ant on flower stalk) through slight (spittle-bug juice extraction) to ruinous (grazing, predation).
- **Key pages**: pp. 186-187
- **Units**: U-M5
- **Translation contested**: false
- **Canonical node link**: Bedeutungstrager, Bedeutungsnutzung

### Bedeutungsregel (meaning rule)

- **O'Neil translation**: meaning rule / meaning order
- **Alternative translations**: rule of meaning, significance rule
- **Forms encountered**: Bedeutungsregel

### Bedeutungston (meaning-tone)
- **German**: Bedeutungston
- **O'Neil translation**: meaning-tone
- **Alternative translations**: tone of meaning
- **Forms encountered**: Bedeutungston

### Bedeutungsverbraucher / Bedeutungsverwerter (consumer / utilizer of meaning)

### Eigenton / Ich-Ton (self-tone)
- **German**: Eigenton / Ich-Ton
- **O'Neil translation**: self-tone
- **Alternative translations**: proper tone, specific energy, characteristic tone, ego-tone
- **Forms encountered**: Eigenton, Ich-Ton

- **German**: Funktionskreis
- **O'Neil translation**: functional cycle
- **Alternative translations**: function circle, functional circuit
- **Forms encountered**: Funktionskreis
- **Definition**: Closed loop linking subject and object: perception organ generates perception marks; effector impresses effect marks that cancel perception marks. The basic unit of organism-environment interaction.

- **Units**: U-F0, U-F2, U-F3, U-M1, U-M2, U-M4
