# Phase 3: Contested / Ambiguous Nodes

**Text**: Jakob von Uexkull, *A Foray into the Worlds of Animals and Humans* + *A Theory of Meaning* (1934/1940)
**Source units**: U-F0 through U-F7, U-M1 through U-M6
**Date**: 2026-03-09

This file provides detailed treatment of every concept where Phase 2 units identified conflicting definitions, where Uexkull himself shifts usage across the book, or where significant translation disputes exist.

---

## 1. Bedeutung (Meaning)

- **German**: *Bedeutung*
- **Units defining differently**: U-F3, U-M1, U-M4, U-M6
- **Nature of ambiguity**: Genuine conceptual shift across both books (progressive inflation)

### The Shift

**U-F3 (Perception Marks + Teleology)**: Bedeutung first appears as a relational property -- the biological significance an object holds for an organism within its Umwelt. Bees prefer open forms (stars, crosses) over closed forms (squares, circles) because open forms mean "bloom" (food source) and closed forms mean "bud" (not yet useful). Meaning here is local, organism-relative, and epistemological: "Relations of meaning are the only certain guides in the investigation of environments" (p. 84). It is a methodological principle for the Umwelt researcher.

**U-M1 (Carriers of Meaning)**: Bedeutung becomes an ontological category. A "relationless object" (beziehungsloses Objekt) has no properties of rank until it enters a meaning-relation with a subject, at which point it becomes a Bedeutungstrager (carrier of meaning). The Scholastic essentia/accidentia distinction is reinterpreted: essential and accidental properties exist only within meaning-relations. Meaning is now constitutive, not merely descriptive.

**U-M4 (Composition Theory)**: Bedeutung expands from an organism-object relation to an inter-organismic relation. The meaning rule (Bedeutungsregel) bridges organisms across Umwelten: the bat's peep is simultaneously a "friend-tone" in the bat's Umwelt and an "enemy-tone" in the moth's. Meaning now operates at the level of Nature's compositional architecture, not just individual functional cycles.

**U-M6 (Summary)**: Bedeutung becomes a cosmic, quasi-theological principle: "Meaning ruled them all" (p. 200). It binds organs to media, food to consumers, predators to prey, males to females. The meaning-germ (Bedeutungskeim) is the originary nucleus from which organisms crystallize. Meaning is no longer one feature of biological analysis; it is the constitutive principle of biological reality as such.

### Translation Dispute

"Meaning" (O'Neil) risks overintellectualizing. Gadamer preferred "significance" (Bedeutsamkeit) for related Heideggerian concepts. In the Theory of Meaning's later chapters, Bedeutung arguably means something closer to "functional purposiveness" or "teleological significance" than semantic content. The German carries connotations of both "importance" (es hat Bedeutung = it matters) and "signification" (die Bedeutung eines Wortes = the meaning of a word), and Uexkull exploits both.

## 2. Gegengefuge / Kontrapunkt / Komplement (Counterstructure / Counterpoint / Complement)

- **German**: *Gegengefuge* (U-F0), *Kontrapunkt* (U-M4-M6), *Komplement* (U-M1)
- **Units defining differently**: U-F0, U-M1, U-M4, U-M5, U-M6
- **Nature of ambiguity**: Scale shift + perspectival inversion

### The Shift

**U-F0 (Foreword + Introduction)**: Gegengefuge is defined as the object's structural link between perception-mark carrier and effect-mark carrier within a single functional cycle (p. 49). It belongs to the object: it is the structural feature of the thing that connects how it is perceived with how it can be acted upon. The concept is part of the functional-cycle machinery, local and mechanical in character.

**U-M1 (Carriers of Meaning)**: The concept is reframed as Komplement (complement), which now belongs to the subject rather than the object: it is the body-side counterpart to the carrier of meaning, the structure in the organism that "consumes" meaning (p. 148). The perspectival inversion is significant -- from what the object contributes (Gegengefuge) to what the subject contributes (Komplement).

**U-M4-M6 (Composition Theory, Technique, Summary)**: Kontrapunkt (counterpoint) describes macro-level structural correspondence between entire organisms across Umwelten. The flower is bee-like, the bee is flower-like; the spider's web is fly-like because the spider is fly-like. Counterpoint operates at the level of primal images (Urbilder) and species-level form, not individual object-features.

### The Unacknowledged Connection

Uexkull never explicitly connects Gegengefuge, Komplement, and Kontrapunkt as related concepts operating at different scales. All three describe "structural fit," but:
- Gegengefuge = intra-object fit (within a single functional cycle)
- Komplement = subject-object fit (body meets meaning-carrier)
- Kontrapunkt = inter-organismic fit (species-to-species across Umwelten)

Whether Kontrapunkt is a generalization of Gegengefuge or a genuinely new concept is undetermined by the text.

### Translation Dispute

O'Neil preserves the distinction: "counterstructure" for Gegengefuge, "counterpoint" for Kontrapunkt. This is helpful. Some secondary literature (especially biosemiotic commentary) collapses all three under "counterpoint," losing the important scale differences.

## 3. Planmassigkeit / Naturplan / Plan (Conformity to Plan / Nature's Plan)

- **German**: *Planmassigkeit, Naturplan, Plan*
- **Units defining differently**: U-F3, U-F4, U-F7, U-M4, U-M6
- **Nature of ambiguity**: Migration from deflationary heuristic to inflationary metaphysical postulate

### The Shift

**U-F3 (Goal and Plan)**: Planmassigkeit is introduced explicitly as a methodological replacement for Ziel (goal). Uexkull's imperative: "extinguish the will-o'-the-wisp of the goal" and view vital expressions "from the point of view of the plan" (p. 86). At this stage, "plan" appears to be a heuristic -- a way of describing structural regularity without attributing conscious purpose. It is deflationary: we say "planned" instead of "goal-directed."

**U-F4 (Images + Navigation)**: Naturplane (Nature's plans) are now described as "supraindividual" and as replacing instinct entirely: instinct is a "stopgap product" for those who deny Nature's plans. Plans are "neither a force nor a material substance" (p. 96), yet without them there would be "no orderly Nature, only chaos." The concept has become inflationary -- it does ontological work, not just methodological work.

**U-F7 (Perspectivism + Synthesis)**: The inborn path is "planned to the highest degree" yet "completely meaningless" from the subject's perspective (p. 128). Plan now operates beyond the subject's own awareness, raising the question of whose plan it is.

**U-M4 (Composition Theory)**: The "composer" (Komponist) of cross-Umwelt compositions possesses "supersensory knowledge not bound to time" (U-M5, p. 185). The pea weevil's behavior exhibits what von Baer called "goal-pursuing quality" (Zielstrebigkeit) in U-M6, despite goal-attribution having been rejected in U-F3. The concept is now explicitly metaphysical: there is a designing intelligence behind biological composition.

**U-M6 (Summary)**: Nature is described as a "masterful hand" producing "composition after composition, never showing any transitions from the imperfect to the perfect." The quasi-theological register is unmistakable.

### The Core Ambiguity

Is Planmassigkeit:
1. A regulative idea (Kantian): we describe organisms *as if* they were planned, without committing to a planner?
2. A constitutive principle (ontotheological): Nature really does compose according to a plan, and the composer is real?
3. A formal pattern (structural): organisms exhibit plan-like regularity, and the word "plan" is simply the best available descriptor for non-mechanical order?

Uexkull never commits. The text oscillates between all three readings, and the oscillation is productive -- it allows Uexkull to use teleological language without the metaphysical baggage of traditional teleology -- but it is also the source of the deepest interpretive disagreements in secondary literature.

### Translation Dispute

"Planlike quality" (O'Neil) implies reading 3 (structural). "Conformity to plan" is neutral between readings 1-3. "Plan of Nature" (Naturplan) leans toward reading 2. The German *-massigkeit* suffix (as in Gesetzmassigkeit = lawfulness/conformity to law) suggests structural regularity, but the compound Naturplan pushes toward agency.

## 4. Ton (Tone) -- Systematic Polysemy

- **German**: *Ton, Wirkton, Suchton, Bedeutungston, Merkton, Eigenton, Ich-Ton, Organton, Lebenton, Tonung*
- **Units with different usage**: U-F4, U-F6, U-F7, U-M1, U-M2, U-M3, U-M5, U-M6
- **Nature of ambiguity**: Deliberate polysemy exploited as a unifying concept

### The Six Registers

| Register | Term | Unit | Definition | Scale |
|----------|------|------|-----------|-------|
| Perceptual affordance | *Wirkton* (effect tone) | U-F4 | Functional coloring an object acquires in a subject's Umwelt: sitting tone, feeding tone, obstacle tone | Organism-object |
| Functional search-orientation | *Suchton* (search tone) | U-F6 | Orientation toward a function (any seat), not a specific object (this chair) | Organism-intention |
| Environmental meaning-valence | *Bedeutungston* (meaning-tone) | U-M5 | The meaning-valence of an object as a compositional element; extends "tone" from auditory to semiotic | Inter-organismic |
| Cellular vital response | *Eigenton / Ich-Ton* (self-tone) | U-M2 | Intrinsic qualitative response of a living cell to any stimulus | Cell |
| Organ-level directive | *Organton* (organ tone) | U-M2-M3 | Meaning tone of an organ as a whole, controlling its constituent cells' self-tones | Organ |
| Organism-level composite | *Lebenton* (life tone) | U-M3 | Composite tone of the entire organism, composed from organ tones; extinction = death | Organism |

### The Productive Ambiguity

Uexkull explicitly acknowledges the stretch in U-M5 (p. 192): expanding "tone" from acoustic phenomenon to meaning-tone of objects as carriers of meaning. He treats this not as a problem but as a discovery -- that the same structural principle (qualitative responsiveness, compositional combination) operates at every level from the cell to the world-score.

The polysemy is productive because it allows Uexkull to build a continuous chain from cellular biology to natural philosophy without changing vocabulary: Eigenton (cell) -> Organton (organ) -> Lebenton (organism) -> Wirkton/Bedeutungston (environment) -> Naturpartitur (world-score). Each level is composed of the tones below it, as a symphony is composed of instrumental voices.

### The Risk

The polysemy also risks conflating analogy with explanation. When Uexkull says an organ has a "tone" that controls its cells' "tones," is this a metaphor, a structural description, or a causal claim? The text moves freely among these registers, and the musical vocabulary does genuine conceptual work -- but it also marks the point where explanation terminates and metaphor takes over.

### Translation Dispute

"Tone" in English is primarily acoustic. Uexkull's usage encompasses quality, valence, character, affordance, and compositional voice. No single English word captures the range. O'Neil uses "tone" throughout, which preserves the deliberate polysemy but also reproduces the ambiguity. Some translators have used "quality" or "coloring" for specific instances (Wirkton = "functional quality"), but this breaks the continuity that is central to Uexkull's argument.

## 5. Bauplan (Construction Plan / Body Plan)

- **German**: *Bauplan, Bauplanregel, Bauplanmelodie*
- **Units defining differently**: U-M1, U-M2, U-M3, U-M4
- **Nature of ambiguity**: Extension from descriptive taxonomy to subordinated dynamic principle

### The Shift

**U-M1**: Bauplan refers to the structural plan of an organism's body or the structural arrangement of a carrier of meaning (e.g., the spatial structure of a ladder as "climbable" or "fenceable"). It is primarily a static structural description.

**U-M2**: Bauplanmelodie (form development order/melody) is the species-specific melody that controls how cells build the form of an organ. The concept has become dynamic -- not a static plan but a temporal process of morphogenesis. The musical metaphor (Melodie) is load-bearing: development proceeds as a melody, not as assembly of parts.

**U-M3-M4**: Bauplanregel (form development rule) is explicitly subordinated to the Bedeutungsregel (meaning rule). The same meaning order ("become a mouth") can be realized by different species-specific form melodies (triton mouth vs. tadpole mouth, as demonstrated by Spemann's grafting experiments). The Bauplan no longer governs; it serves.

### The Core Question

Is the Bauplan:
1. A description of an organism's structural organization (taxonomic)?
2. A dynamic developmental melody (morphogenetic)?
3. A rule subordinate to meaning (compositional)?

The text uses all three senses, and the progression from 1 to 3 is part of the argument. But the term's meaning in any given passage depends on context, and Uexkull does not always signal which sense is active.

### Translation Dispute

O'Neil renders Bauplanmelodie as "form development order," losing the musical metaphor (Melodie) that is essential to Uexkull's anti-mechanistic argument. "Building-plan melody" or "structural-plan melody" would preserve it. Standard biological English uses "body plan" for Bauplan, which is too narrow for Uexkull's extended usage.

## 6. Stimmung (Mood / Attunement / Disposition)

- **German**: *Stimmung*
- **Units defining differently**: U-F4, U-F6
- **Nature of ambiguity**: Biological mechanism vs. phenomenological structure

### The Shift

**U-F4 (Images + Navigation)**: Stimmung is introduced through the hermit crab experiments: the same sea anemone acquires three different meanings depending on the crab's internal state -- protective tone (crab with shell), dwelling tone (crab without shell), feeding tone (starving crab). Stimmung here appears to be a biological state variable -- something like "hunger" or "security-seeking" -- that determines which effect image activates.

**U-F6 (Innate Patterns + Magic)**: Stimmung operates in the context of search tones and magical environments: the starling's environment is "laden with feeding tone," forcing a perception image (the imaginary fly) without any external stimulus. Here Stimmung is no longer a response to a physiological state but a prior condition of perceptual disclosure -- the world shows up a certain way because the organism is attuned a certain way.

### The Philosophical Freight

The term *Stimmung* carries enormous weight in German philosophy:
- Heidegger (B&T Sections 29-30): Befindlichkeit/Stimmung is a fundamental existential structure -- mood discloses how Dasein finds itself in the world before any determinate perception
- Dilthey: *Erlebnis* and *Stimmung* as categories of lived experience
- The etymological connection to *Stimme* (voice) and *stimmen* (to tune) links Stimmung to Uexkull's musical vocabulary

Whether Uexkull intends a biological mechanism (dispositional state), a phenomenological structure (prior disclosure condition), or something specific to his own framework (the subject's "tuning" that determines which tones resonate) is never clarified. The secondary literature on Uexkull-Heidegger connections treats this as a genuine meeting point, but the convergence may be terminological rather than conceptual.

### Translation Dispute

O'Neil uses "mood," which is the standard Macquarrie-Robinson translation for Heidegger's Stimmung. Stambaugh uses "attunement," which better captures the musical connotation. For Uexkull's biological usage, "disposition" might be more accurate, but it loses the philosophical resonance that is arguably part of the concept's significance.

## 7. Merkmal (Perception Mark) vs. Standard German "Feature"

- **German**: *Merkmal*
- **Units with different usage**: throughout all 14 units
- **Nature of ambiguity**: Technical reappropriation of common-language term

### The Issue

In standard German, *Merkmal* means "feature," "characteristic," or "trait" -- an objective property of a thing. It is one of the most common words in German scientific prose. Uexkull redefines it as the quality of an object *as perceived by a subject* -- an external mark that exists only within a functional cycle. The tick's butyric acid perception mark is not a feature of the mammal's sweat glands but a mark that exists only in the tick's Umwelt.

This terminological reappropriation runs throughout both texts without ever being fully resolved. When Uexkull says the oak has certain Merkmale for the bark beetle, does he mean "the bark beetle perceives these features" (standard German reading) or "these features exist only as perception marks within the bark beetle's functional cycle" (Uexkullian reading)? The answer is always the latter, but the standard-German resonance creates a persistent undertow of objectivism that Uexkull's framework is designed to overcome.

### Flagged in Phase 2

This issue is flagged as a translation dispute in U-F0 (p. 119: "standard German 'feature' vs. Uexkull's technical 'perception mark'") and U-F3 (p. 133: "standard German 'feature' vs. Uexkull's technical biosemiotic sense"). The ambiguity is intrinsic to the German text, not an artifact of translation.

### Translation Dispute

O'Neil's "perception mark" resolves the ambiguity in favor of the technical Uexkullian sense, which is correct but eliminates the productive double meaning. A reader of the German text encounters a common word (*Merkmal*) being used in an uncommon way, and the friction between ordinary and technical usage is part of Uexkull's rhetorical strategy -- he wants to show that what we casually call "features" are in fact subject-constituted perception marks.

## 8. Umwelt (Environment) -- The Master Term

- **German**: *Umwelt*
- **Units with different valence**: U-F0 (perceptual-biological), U-F7 (perspectival-epistemological), U-M1 (semiotic), U-M4-M6 (compositional-ontological)
- **Nature of ambiguity**: Progressive deepening across two books

### The Shift

While Umwelt is not "contested" in the sense of conflicting definitions, its valence shifts significantly:

**U-F0**: Umwelt = closed perceptual world composed of Merkwelt + Wirkwelt, constituted by the subject's sensory and motor apparatus. Fundamentally a Kantian epistemological concept.

**U-F7**: Umwelt = one of many perspectival "cuts" into an unknowable Nature-in-itself. The oak paradigm shows ten Umwelten on a single object. The concept is now epistemological-ontological: each Umwelt contains "only subjective realities."

**U-M1-M2**: Umwelt = totality of meaning-relations between carriers and consumers of meaning. The dwelling-world (Wohnwelt) becomes either Umwelt (for animals with functional cycles) or Wohnhulle (for plants without them). The concept is now semiotic.

**U-M4-M6**: Umwelt = a voice in Nature's world-score (Partitur), contrapuntally composed with other Umwelten. The concept is now compositional-ontological: each Umwelt is not just a perspectival cut but a composed totality participating in a cosmic composition.

### Not a Translation Issue

The word *Umwelt* in standard German simply means "environment" (as in *Umweltschutz* = environmental protection). Uexkull's technical usage is universally recognized in secondary literature, and the term is typically left untranslated in English-language scholarship. The ambiguity is entirely internal to Uexkull's own shifting usage across the two books.
