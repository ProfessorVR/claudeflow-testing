## 2. Hierarchical Outline

### The Interpretation of the Spider's Web (pp. 161–165)
1. Organ tones compose the life tone; embryonic development proceeds centrifugally from meaning tones (p. 161)
2. "Thinking tone" of the brain: meaning bridges physical and nonphysical (p. 161)
3. **The spider-as-tailor analogy** (pp. 161–163)
   - Human tailor takes measurements, transfers to cloth, produces a mold of the body
   - Spider produces a "faithful rendering" (treues Abbild) of the fly without ever measuring the fly
   - Web as meaning utilizer (Bedeutungsverwerter) attuned to the carrier of meaning (Bedeutungsträger) "prey"
   - Mesh size calibrated to fly body; thread tension calibrated to fly's flight power; threads invisible to fly's crude visual elements
4. **The primal image (Urbild)** (pp. 163–164)
   - Spider weaves before encountering a physical fly — web represents the Urbild of the fly, not the physical fly
   - Defense against mechanicist objection: modern physics (Eddington's two desks) is more "metaphysical" than biology
   - Biology posits factors in the subject beyond sensory phenomenality
5. **Primal scores (Urpartituren)** (pp. 163–164)
   - Embryonic development as musical score: morula, blastula, gastrula
   - Primal score of fly affects primal score of spider — web is "fly-like" (fliegenhaft)
   - Behind the curtain of appearances, primal images connect according to an inclusive meaning plan (Bedeutungsplan)
   - Meaning as biology's pole star vs. impoverished rules of causality
6. **The pea weevil trio** (pp. 164–165)
   - Larva drills tunnel and exit door in the pea before the pea hardens — no trial-and-error possible
   - Meaning transfer (Bedeutungsübertragung) between primal image of pea and primal image of pea weevil
   - Ichneumon wasp exploits the door — a "trio of meaning connections" across three primal scores

### Form Development Rule and Meaning Rule (pp. 165–170)
7. **Loeb's tropism theory** (pp. 165–167)
   - Jacques Loeb: only an effect world (Wirkwelt), no perception world; all animal actions reducible to tropisms (approach/withdrawal)
   - Loeb transformed animal subjects into dead machines; decisive for an entire generation of biologists
   - The meadow scene: naive view (same meadow for all), Loeb's view (tangle of ether waves and chemical effects), Umwelt-theory view (bee does not see with human eyes, nor is it a feelingless machine)
8. **Colors as self-tones (Eigentöne)** (pp. 167–168)
   - Colors are ether waves become perceptible — self-tones of cerebral cells, not electric excitations
   - Goethe and Hering: colors follow their own laws, distinct from physics
   - Ether-wave spectrum is linear; color ribbon forms a closed circle (violet joins red and blue)
   - Complementary colors produce white — relational property of self-tones, not mechanical effect
9. **Sensory organs as double-fronted structures** (pp. 168–170)
   - Organs built like a "Swedish box of matches" — respond selectively to outer stimuli
   - Outer front: sieve for physicochemical effects; inner front: living carillon (Glockenspiel) of cell bells with self-tones
   - Perception signs (Merkzeichen) transposed outward become perception marks (Merkmale) of things
   - Fish have both taste and smell organs despite only encountering water-soluble substances; birds lack olfactory organ — proves organs are not solely products of the outer front
   - The subject's "palette" of perception signs determines the structure of its environment
   - Mathilde Hertz's experiments: bee color ribbon displaced one step toward violet relative to humans

## 6. Cross-Pipeline References

### 6a. Heidegger B&T Connections
- **Urbild (primal image)** → Resonates with Heidegger's treatment of Vorhandenheit vs. Zuhandenheit: the spider relates to the fly not as a present-at-hand object but through a pre-given meaning structure. Heidegger's "towards-which" (Wozu) of equipment parallels Uexküll's meaning-utilizer/meaning-carrier relation. [INTERP-high]
- **"Meaning as pole star" vs. causality** → Parallels Heidegger's critique of Vorhandenheit-based science in B&T §§69b–c: natural science presupposes a de-worlded objectivity that forecloses the question of meaning. Uexküll's methodological claim mirrors Heidegger's ontological one. [INTERP-high]
- **Organ tone / Life tone** → Structurally comparable to Heidegger's Stimmung (attunement/mood) in B&T §29: the organism is always already "tuned" prior to encountering objects. The musical metaphor is shared. [INTERP-high]
- **Double-fronted sensory organ (inner/outer front)** → Parallels Heidegger's analysis of perception in B&T §13: perceiving is not impact of stimuli on a subject but a dwelling-alongside entities. Uexküll's inner front makes a structurally similar point from the biological side. [INTERP-high]

### 6b. BCAP Connections
- Uexküll's Urbild has deep resonance with Aristotle's eidos as morphogenetic cause (formal cause). The primal image is not efficient causation but formal determination — the "what it is to be" that guides development. This connection is explicit in secondary literature on Uexküll's Aristotelianism.

### 6c. Rickert Connections
- Uexküll's distinction between meaning and causality as competing "master plans" for biology maps onto Rickert's distinction between natural-scientific (nomothetic) and cultural-scientific (idiographic) methods. Uexküll's meaning-biology sits uneasily between the two.
