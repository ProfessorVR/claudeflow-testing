## Section 2: Hierarchical Outline

### 2.1 The question of the primordial totality of Dasein's structural whole (§39)
- The preceding analyses (being-in-the-world, das Man, attunement, understanding, discourse, falling) have elucidated Dasein's constitution item by item; the question now is how this structural whole can be grasped *as a totality* in an existential-ontological manner.
- The totality cannot be built up by assembling constitutive items like an architect's plan; it must be accessed through a *single* primordially unitary phenomenon that is already the ontological foundation for each structural item.
- The existential analytic must seek the *most far-reaching and primordial* possibility of disclosure that lies in Dasein itself: a state-of-mind in which Dasein is brought before itself in a *simplified* manner.
- This state-of-mind is anxiety (*Angst*); Dasein's Being reveals itself in anxiety as *care* (*Sorge*).
- Preview of the remainder of Division One: anxiety (§40), care (§41), pre-ontological confirmation (§42), then worldhood/Reality (§43) and truth (§44).

### 2.2 Anxiety as the basic disclosive state-of-mind (§40)
- Anxiety is distinguished from fear: fear is always fear *of* a definite entity within-the-world that threatens from a definite region; anxiety is anxious in the face of something *completely indefinite* — "nothing and nowhere."
- The turning-away of falling is not grounded in fear of entities but in anxiety: falling is fleeing *in the face of itself*, i.e., in the face of Dasein's authentic potentiality-for-Being-its-Self.
- That in the face of which one has anxiety (*das Wovor der Angst*) is Being-in-the-world as such; the world as world collapses into insignificance — the totality of involvements of the ready-to-hand and present-at-hand "sinks away."
- That *about which* anxiety is anxious (*das Worum*) is Dasein's ownmost potentiality-for-Being-in-the-world: anxiety is anxious *for* the sake of Being-in-the-world itself.
- Anxiety *individualizes* (*vereinzelt*) Dasein: it wrenches Dasein out of its absorption in the "they" and the "world" and discloses it as *Being-possible* — free for the freedom of choosing and taking hold of itself.
- Anxiety reveals Dasein's *uncanniness* (*Unheimlichkeit*): Dasein is "not-at-home" (*Nicht-zuhause-sein*). The everyday familiarity of Being-at-home in the publicness of the "they" collapses; the "not-at-home" must be conceived as the more primordial phenomenon, of which everyday at-homeness is a mode of fleeing.

### 2.3 Dasein's Being as care (§41)
- Anxiety shows Dasein as factically existing Being-in-the-world: the fundamental ontological characteristics disclosed are existentiality, facticity, and Being-fallen.
- **Existentiality**: Dasein is always already *ahead of itself* (*sich vorweg*) — projected toward its ownmost potentiality-for-Being. This structure is designated "Being-ahead-of-itself."
- **Facticity**: Dasein is always already *thrown into a world* (*schon-sein-in-einer-Welt*) — it does not first project itself onto possibilities from nowhere but finds itself always already delivered over to a world.
- **Falling**: Dasein is always already *alongside* (*sein-bei*) entities encountered within-the-world, absorbed in the world of its concern, fleeing from uncanniness.
- The formal existential totality of Dasein's structural whole is: **"ahead-of-itself-Being-already-in-(the-world-)as Being-alongside (entities encountered within-the-world)"** — this is *care* (*Sorge*).
- Care is not to be confused with ontic phenomena such as worry (*Besorgnis*), carefreeness (*Sorglosigkeit*), willing, wishing, addiction (*Hang*), or urge (*Drang*) — these are all grounded in care and are existentiell modifications of it.
- Care is ontologically "earlier" than any particular comportment; willing, wishing, urge, and addiction each presuppose care and are derivative from it as modifications of the care-structure (e.g., wishing = Being-ahead-of-itself forfeited to thrownness; addiction = "just-always-already-alongside").

### 2.4 Pre-ontological confirmation: the *cura* fable (§42)
- Because the existential-ontological Interpretation of Dasein as care may seem strange compared to ontic definitions of "man," Heidegger seeks pre-ontological confirmation: a document in which Dasein expresses itself "primordially," prior to theoretical Interpretation.
- The Hyginus fable (*cura* fable): Care (*Cura*) shapes a creature from clay; Jupiter gives it spirit; Earth provides its body; Saturn adjudicates — since *Cura* first shaped it, *Cura* shall hold it as long as it lives; it is called *homo* because made from *humus*.
- The fable confirms: (a) care has priority over body (Earth) and spirit (Jupiter) — care "holds" the creature for its lifetime; (b) the creature's name (*homo*) derives not from its Being but from its material (*humus*); (c) the primordial Being of this creature is assigned to Time (Saturn).
- Burdach's philological note on *cura*: the Latin term carries a double meaning — not only "anxious exertion" but also "carefulness" and "devotedness" (*Sorgfalt*, *Hingabe*). Seneca's epistle on the four Natures and man's *perfectio* through care is also cited.
- The existential-ontological Interpretation generalizes beyond ontic characterization: care is not a set of properties but the *a priori* state of Being underlying every possible interpretation of Dasein, whether as affliction or as devotedness.

## Section 6: Tensions & Open Questions

1. **Anxiety's rarity vs. its structural primordiality (§40, H.190)**: Heidegger claims anxiety is rare under the ascendancy of falling and publicness, yet simultaneously that it is the *more primordial* phenomenon underlying all of everyday Dasein's fleeing. The tension is whether a phenomenon that is factically rare can legitimately serve as the phenomenal basis for disclosing what is ontologically fundamental. Heidegger's response — that rarity is an index of Dasein's concealment from itself, not an objection to anxiety's methodological fitness — leaves open the question of how the analyst *accesses* anxiety if it is mostly latent. [INTERP-low] — Linked to: BT-D1-U8-P1, BT-D1-U8-P3.

2. **Individuation vs. solicitude (§40, H.187–188)**: Anxiety individualizes Dasein by wrenching it out of its absorption in the "they" and disclosing it as singular Being-possible. Yet Dasein's constitution essentially includes Being-with-Others and solicitude (*Fürsorge*). Whether the individualizing effect of anxiety is a *suspension* of Being-with or merely a *modification* of it is not resolved here. The later analysis of anticipatory resoluteness (Division Two, §60) will address this, but the present section leaves a gap. [INTERP-high] — Linked to: BT-D1-U8-P1, BT-D1-U8-P4.

3. **Care and freedom (§41, H.193)**: The care-structure includes Being-free for one's ownmost potentiality-for-Being, and Heidegger states that Dasein "can comport itself towards its possibilities, even *unwillingly*" — freedom characterizes Being towards potentiality-for-Being. But care also essentially includes facticity (thrownness) and falling. The precise relationship between the freedom disclosed in care and the constraint imposed by thrownness and falling remains underdetermined. Whether "freedom" here is positive (capacity for authentic self-choice) or merely formal-structural is a significant interpretive question. [INTERP-high] — Linked to: BT-D1-U8-P4, BT-D1-U8-P5.

4. **The status of the *cura* fable as "confirmation" (§42, H.196–200)**: Heidegger introduces the Hyginus fable as "pre-ontological confirmation" of the existential Interpretation, but the methodological status of such confirmation is unclear. The fable is a literary text, not a phenomenological description; Heidegger acknowledges its "merely historical" (*bloss historisch*) demonstrative force. Whether a mythological-literary document can genuinely confirm an ontological analysis, or whether Heidegger is merely illustrating what he has already argued, is left open. [INTERP-low] — Linked to: BT-D1-U8-P6.

5. **Equiprimordiality vs. the priority of existentiality (§41, H.191–193)**: The three structural moments of care (existentiality, facticity, falling) are declared equiprimordial, yet the care-formula begins with "ahead-of-itself" and Heidegger repeatedly emphasizes that Being-ahead-of-itself is what makes Dasein an entity "for which its Being is an issue." This creates a tension between the claim of equiprimordiality and the apparent structural priority of the existentiality-moment (projection/understanding). [INTERP-high] — Linked to: BT-D1-U8-P4.
