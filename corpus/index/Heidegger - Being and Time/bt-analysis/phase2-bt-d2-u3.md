# Phase 2 Analysis: BT-D2-U3 (§§61–66)

## Section 1: Unit Metadata

- **unit_id**: BT-D2-U3

- **§-range**: §§61–66
- **H. page range**: H.301–333
- **Eng. page range**: 349–382
- **Unit thesis**: This unit demonstrates that temporality (*Zeitlichkeit*) — the ecstatic unity of future, having-been, and present — is the ontological meaning of care, by first consolidating anticipatory resoluteness as Dasein's authentic potentiality-for-Being-a-whole, clarifying the hermeneutical situation and the circular character of the existential analytic, showing that Selfhood is grounded in care rather than in a persisting "I"-substance, and then exhibiting temporality as the "upon-which" of the projection that makes possible the articulated structural whole of care.

## Section 2: Hierarchical Outline

### 2.1 Methodological transition: from anticipatory resoluteness to temporality (§61)
- Authentic Being-towards-death (anticipation) and resoluteness have been exhibited separately; the question is how they connect without artificial construction.
- The sole methodologically legitimate path: take resoluteness as the point of departure and ask whether it does not, *in its ownmost existentiell tendency of Being*, point forward to anticipatory resoluteness as its authentic possibility.
- Every step must be guided by the idea of *existence*; Dasein cannot be pieced together from present-at-hand components.
- Before laying bare the meaning of the Being of care, a pause for explicit methodological reflection is necessary.

### 2.2 Anticipatory resoluteness as authentic potentiality-for-Being-a-whole (§62)
- Resoluteness, as wanting-to-have-a-conscience, projects Dasein upon its ownmost Being-guilty — a Being that is constant and that Dasein is *as long as it is*.
- Being-guilty implies Being-towards-the-end: Dasein's "Being-at-an-end" implies Being-towards-death; resoluteness becomes authentically what it can be only *as* anticipation of death.
- Anticipation discloses death as one's ownmost, non-relational, not-to-be-outstripped, certain, and indefinite possibility; resoluteness thus *catches up* [*eingeholt*] the possibility of death into its potentiality-for-Being.
- Anticipatory resoluteness is authentic resoluteness that resolves to keep itself free for the *whole* of Dasein's potentiality-for-Being: it holds open the Situation, holds-for-true in Being-certain, and resolves to *repeat itself* (authentic resoluteness which resolves to keep repeating itself).
- The question of Dasein's potentiality-for-Being-a-whole is answered: it is factical and existentiell, answered by Dasein *as resolute*.
- Anticipatory resoluteness is not a fabricated escape from death but rather the understanding that follows the call of conscience and frees Dasein for its existence, bringing one without Illusions into the resoluteness of "taking action."

### 2.3 The hermeneutical situation and the circular character of the existential analytic (§63)
- Anticipatory resoluteness has brought Dasein phenomenally before its authentic totality; the hermeneutical Situation — previously inadequate — now has the required primordiality.
- The fore-having (*Vorhabe*): Dasein in its authentic potentiality-for-Being-a-whole. The fore-sight (*Vorsicht*): the idea of existence. The fore-conception (*Vorgriff*): the appropriate existential concepts.
- Dasein's kind of Being *demands* that ontological Interpretation exhibit the phenomena in their primordiality *in spite of* Dasein's own tendency to cover things up; existential analysis has the character of *doing violence* [*Gewaltsamkeit*].
- The "circle" in understanding is not a logical deficiency but belongs to the structure of care: understanding is projective, and all research into the Being of Dasein is itself a kind of Being which Dasein possesses.
- Common sense misunderstands the circle because it recognizes only "factual" entities and fails to see that Being is already understood pre-ontologically; to deny the circle is to reinforce the failure.
- The existential analytic bases itself on the ontical possibility of anticipatory resoluteness — a definite ideal of existence that is Dasein's own, not imposed from outside.

### 2.4 Care and Selfhood: critique of the "I" as substance (§64)
- The care-structure (existentiality, facticity, fallenness) provides the first ontological definition of Dasein's totality, but the question of the *Self* that belongs to care has not been adequately addressed.
- The everyday "I" is interpreted by the they-self: saying "I" expresses Being-in-the-world, but in a fugitive, self-forgetful way.
- Kant's analysis of the "I think" grasps its phenomenal content (the "I" as *res cogitans*, *subjectum*, the formal "I bind together") but fails to exploit it ontologically: the "I" is still treated as something constantly present-at-hand rather than as care.
- Kant correctly sees the impossibility of reducing the "I" to a substance, but still defines the "I" ontologically as "subject" — always present-at-hand; he did not see the phenomenon of the world and therefore could not interpret saying "I" as saying "I-am-in-a-world."
- Selfhood is to be discerned existentielly only in one's authentic potentiality-for-Being-one's-Self — in the authenticity of Dasein's Being as care.
- The *constancy of the Self* [*Ständigkeit des Selbst*] is clarified as the constancy of care, not as the persistence of a *subjectum*; Self-constancy [*Selbst-ständigkeit*] is the authentic counter-possibility to the non-Self-constancy of irresolute falling.
- Care does not need to be founded in a Self; rather, existentiality, as constitutive for care, provides the ontological constitution of Dasein's Self-constancy — including, in its full structural content, Dasein's Being-fallen factically into non-Self-constancy.

### 2.5 Temporality as the ontological meaning of care (§65)
- "Meaning" (*Sinn*) signifies the "upon-which" [*das Woraufhin*] of a primary projection — that in terms of which something can be conceived in its possibility.
- The meaning of care is what makes possible the totality of the articulated structural whole of care, in the unity of its articulation as we have unfolded it.
- Anticipatory resoluteness, taken formally and existentially, is *Being towards* one's ownmost, distinctive potentiality-for-Being; this letting-itself-come-towards-itself is the primordial phenomenon of the *future* [*Zukunft*] as coming-towards [*Zu-kunft*].
- Taking over thrownness authentically — being Dasein as it already was — is the primordial phenomenon of *having-been* [*Gewesenheit*]; only as futural can Dasein come back to its "been."
- Being-alongside what is ready-to-hand in the Situation — making present [*Gegenwärtigen*] — is possible only as the *Present* [*Gegenwart*]; resoluteness brings itself into the Situation by making present.
- The unity of future, having-been, and Present which makes present in the process of having been is *temporality* [*Zeitlichkeit*]. Temporality reveals itself as the meaning of authentic care.
- The ecstases: future, having-been, and Present are not successive "nows" but the phenomena of the "towards-oneself," the "back-to," and the "letting-oneself-be-encountered-by." Temporality is the primordial "outside-of-itself" in and for itself; hence the ecstases are called *Ekstasen*.
- The primary phenomenon of primordial and authentic temporality is the *future*; authentic temporality temporalizes itself primarily out of the future.
- Temporality is essentially ecstatical; it *temporalizes* itself (*zeitigt sich*). It is not an entity but a process of temporalizing.
- Primordial time is *finite*: the authentic future is Being-towards-death as the possibility of nullity, and this future is closed to one — it closes one's potentiality-for-Being.
- Summary theses (H.331): Time is primordial as the temporalizing of temporality; temporality is essentially ecstatical; temporality temporalizes itself primordially out of the future; primordial time is finite.

### 2.6 The task of repeating the existential analysis temporally (§66)
- The Interpretation of care as temporality cannot remain restricted to authentic Being-a-whole; temporality must be confirmed in the concrete content of Dasein's basic state.
- The next task: temporal analysis of Dasein's *inauthenticity* — disclosedness, everydayness, the temporality of the structures exhibited in Division One.
- Selfhood has been taken back into the structure of care; therefore the temporal Interpretation of Self-constancy and non-Self-constancy acquires its own importance, revealing the *temporalization-structure* of temporality as historicality.
- Dasein "uses itself up," reckons with time; this concernful reckoning with time leads to within-time-ness (*Innerzeitigkeit*), which is the basis for the ordinary conception of "time."
- The temporal analysis will not merely repeat the preparatory analyses in the same order but will follow the motives supplied by temporality itself; it will amount to a *repetition* [*Wiederholung*] of the existential analytic in a more primordial manner.
- The ultimate task — not yet reached — is to repeat the temporal analysis within a framework in which the concept of Being is discussed in principle (i.e., the projected but unwritten Division Three).

## Section 6: Tensions & Open Questions

1. **Finitude vs. infinity of time (§65, H.329–331)**: Heidegger asserts that primordial time is finite because the authentic future, as Being-towards-death, closes one's potentiality-for-Being. He then claims that "infinite" or "endless" time is *derivative* — arising from the levelling-off of authentic temporality. But he explicitly defers the demonstration of how inauthentic temporality temporalizes an in-finite time out of the finite, noting that "the problem is not one of how 'derived' infinite time becomes primordial finite temporality; the problem is rather that of how inauthentic temporality arises out of finite authentic temporality." The relationship between finitude and the ordinary experience of "endless" time remains promissory within this unit. [INTERP-low] — Linked to: BT-D2-U3-P6; forward-reference to §§78–83.

2. **The ontical "ideal" of existence as methodological ground (§63, H.310–313)**: Heidegger concedes that the existential analytic bases itself on an ontical possibility — anticipatory resoluteness as a definite way of existing — and that philosophy "will never seek to deny its 'presuppositions'" but must "unfold them with more and more penetration." The question is whether privileging one existentiell possibility (anticipatory resoluteness) over others introduces an unacknowledged normative bias into what purports to be an ontological-structural analysis. Heidegger's response — that the "violence" of the projection is justified because it captures Dasein's Being "in spite of this entity's own tendency to cover things up" — itself depends on the prior acceptance of the authentic/inauthentic distinction. [INTERP-high] — Linked to: BT-D2-U3-P2.

3. **Selfhood without subjectivity (§64, H.316–323)**: Heidegger argues that Selfhood is grounded in care and that Self-constancy is anticipatory resoluteness, not the persistence of a *subjectum*. Yet the care-structure itself includes an "ahead-of-itself" that formally resembles the self-relating reflexivity of transcendental subjectivity. Whether Heidegger has genuinely overcome the subject-ontology or merely redescribed it in existential terms is a persistent interpretive question. The fact that he still speaks of Dasein as "in each case I myself" (H.322–323) and grounds Selfhood in the "silent" reticence of authentic Being-one's-Self raises the question of how far the "I" has really been displaced. [INTERP-high] — Linked to: BT-D2-U3-P3.

4. **Priority of the future and symmetry of the ecstases (§65, H.327–330)**: Heidegger claims that the future has priority and that temporality temporalizes itself "primordially out of the future." Yet he also insists on the "equiprimordiality" of the three ecstases — each ecstasis temporalizes in its own way. The relationship between the structural priority of the future and the claimed equiprimordiality of the ecstases is in tension. If having-been "arises from the future" and the Present is "released" from the future, what does equiprimordiality amount to? [INTERP-low] — Linked to: BT-D2-U3-P5.

5. **The absent Division Three (§66, H.332–333)**: §66 explicitly states that the temporal analysis of Dasein "will remain incomplete and fraught with obscurities" until it is repeated within "a framework in which the concept of Being is discussed in principle." This amounts to an acknowledgment that the central result of this unit — temporality as the meaning of care — cannot be fully validated without the unpublished Division Three's reversal from Dasein's temporality to the temporality (*Temporalität*) of Being itself. The question of Being in general remains unanswered. [INTERP-low] — Linked to: BT-D2-U3-P4, BT-D2-U3-P7.
