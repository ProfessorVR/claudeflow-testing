# Being and Time — Book-Level Ontology (Phase 3)

**Source**: Heidegger, Martin. *Being and Time*. Trans. John Macquarrie and Edward Robinson. New York: Harper & Row, 1962.
**Pipeline stage**: Phase 3 — Book-level synthesis from 17 Phase 2 unit analyses.
**Date**: 2026-03-09

---

## 3A. Canonical Node List

Consolidated from ~200 unit-level concepts across 17 units (BT-Intro-U1/U2, BT-D1-U1 through BT-D1-U9, BT-D2-U1 through BT-D2-U6). Nodes organized into three centrality tiers: **core** (12), **important** (15), **peripheral** (10). Total: 37 canonical nodes.

### Core Nodes (12)

#### 1. Being (Sein)

- **canonical_name**: Being
- **german_lemma**: Sein
- **mr_translation**: Being
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The fundamental theme of the treatise — not a being (Seiendes) but that which determines beings as beings. The question of the meaning of Being (Sinn von Sein) drives the entire inquiry. Not a genus, not definable through traditional logic, yet pre-ontologically understood by Dasein in every comportment toward beings.
- **units**: Intro-U1, Intro-U2, D1-U1, D1-U2, D1-U3, D1-U9, D2-U3, D2-U4, D2-U6
- **aliases**: Sein, question of Being, Seinsfrage, meaning of Being, Sinn von Sein
- **centrality**: core

#### 2. Dasein

- **canonical_name**: Dasein
- **german_lemma**: Dasein
- **mr_translation**: Dasein (untranslated)
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The entity that each of us is, distinguished by the fact that in its Being this Being is an issue for it. Dasein exists — its essence lies in its existence (to-be). It is characterized by mineness (Jemeinigkeit), being-in-the-world, and the structural totality of care. Dasein is the entity through which the question of Being must be raised.
- **units**: all 17 units
- **aliases**: human Dasein, entity which we ourselves are, being-there
- **centrality**: core

#### 3. Being-in-the-world (In-der-Welt-sein)

- **canonical_name**: Being-in-the-world
- **german_lemma**: In-der-Welt-sein
- **mr_translation**: Being-in-the-world
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The unitary, a priori structural whole that constitutes Dasein's basic state. Not a spatial containment but a mode of existing: Dasein is always already "in" a world, engaged with entities, alongside others. Its three structural moments — Being-in, world, the who — are analyzed in Division One and temporally grounded in Division Two.
- **units**: Intro-U2, D1-U1, D1-U2, D1-U3, D1-U4, D1-U5, D1-U6, D1-U7, D1-U8, D2-U3, D2-U4
- **aliases**: In-der-Welt-sein, basic state of Dasein
- **centrality**: core

#### 4. Care (Sorge)

- **canonical_name**: Care
- **german_lemma**: Sorge
- **mr_translation**: care
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The structural totality of Dasein's Being, formally articulated as Being-ahead-of-itself-already-in-(the-world)-alongside (entities encountered within-the-world). Unifies the existential moments of existence (understanding/projection), facticity (thrownness/state-of-mind), and falling. Demonstrated through the phenomenon of anxiety, confirmed by the cura fable, and temporally grounded as the meaning of temporality.
- **units**: D1-U5, D1-U7, D1-U8, D2-U1, D2-U2, D2-U3, D2-U4, D2-U5
- **aliases**: Sorge, concern (Besorgen, narrow sense), solicitude (Fürsorge, narrow sense), structural totality
- **centrality**: core

#### 5. Temporality (Zeitlichkeit)

- **canonical_name**: Temporality
- **german_lemma**: Zeitlichkeit
- **mr_translation**: temporality
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The ontological meaning of care and the primordial horizon for the understanding of Being. Unfolds in three ecstases — future (Zukunft), having-been (Gewesenheit), Present (Gegenwart) — each with an authentic and inauthentic mode. Temporality is not a sequence of "nows" but the ecstatic-horizonal unity that makes possible Dasein's existence, facticity, and falling. Distinguished from Temporalität (temporality of Being as such), which B&T announces but does not fully develop.
- **units**: Intro-U2, D2-U3, D2-U4, D2-U5, D2-U6
- **aliases**: Zeitlichkeit, ecstatic temporality, primordial time, ecstases
- **centrality**: core

#### 6. Being-towards-death (Sein-zum-Tode)

- **canonical_name**: Being-towards-death
- **german_lemma**: Sein-zum-Tode
- **mr_translation**: Being-towards-death
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: Dasein's ownmost, non-relational, not-to-be-outstripped, certain yet indefinite possibility. Death is not an event at the end of life but the possibility of the impossibility of existence — a constitutive structure that individualizes Dasein and opens authentic self-understanding. Anticipation (Vorlaufen) lets Dasein hold open this possibility without fleeing into the "they's" evasive interpretations.
- **units**: D2-U1, D2-U2, D2-U3, D2-U5
- **aliases**: Sein-zum-Tode, anticipation (Vorlaufen), freedom towards death, dying (Sterben)
- **centrality**: core

#### 7. Disclosedness (Erschlossenheit)

- **canonical_name**: Disclosedness
- **german_lemma**: Erschlossenheit
- **mr_translation**: disclosedness
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The "there" (Da) of Dasein — the clearing in which entities can show themselves and Dasein can encounter itself. Equiprimordially constituted by state-of-mind, understanding, and discourse. Disclosedness is more primordial than any propositional truth; it is the existential-ontological condition for truth as unconcealment. Falling both belongs to and conceals this disclosedness.
- **units**: D1-U5, D1-U6, D1-U7, D1-U8, D1-U9, D2-U2, D2-U3
- **aliases**: Erschlossenheit, the "there", Da, clearing, openedness
- **centrality**: core

#### 8. Understanding (Verstehen)

- **canonical_name**: Understanding
- **german_lemma**: Verstehen
- **mr_translation**: understanding
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: An equiprimordial existentiale of disclosedness (alongside state-of-mind and discourse). Understanding is Dasein's projective grasp of its own potentiality-for-Being and of the Being of entities it encounters. Always has the structure of projection (Entwurf) and discloses possibilities, not merely cognitive contents. Developed in interpretation (Auslegung), which articulates the fore-structure. Temporally grounded in the ecstasis of the future.
- **units**: D1-U5, D1-U6, D1-U8, D2-U3, D2-U4
- **aliases**: Verstehen, existential understanding, projection (Entwurf), potentiality-for-Being
- **centrality**: core

#### 9. State-of-mind (Befindlichkeit)

- **canonical_name**: State-of-mind
- **german_lemma**: Befindlichkeit
- **mr_translation**: state-of-mind
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: An equiprimordial existentiale of disclosedness that discloses Dasein's thrownness and facticity. Ontically manifested as mood (Stimmung). Every state-of-mind discloses Dasein as already delivered over to its "there" — it reveals the "that it is" while the "whence and whither" remain in darkness. Fear is an inauthentic mode; anxiety is the fundamental mode that discloses Being-in-the-world as such. Temporally grounded in having-been.
- **units**: D1-U5, D1-U7, D1-U8, D2-U3, D2-U4
- **aliases**: Befindlichkeit, mood (Stimmung), attunement, thrownness-disclosure
- **centrality**: core

#### 10. Falling (Verfallenheit)

- **canonical_name**: Falling
- **german_lemma**: Verfallenheit
- **mr_translation**: falling
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: Dasein's tendency to lose itself in the "they" and in the entities it encounters within-the-world. Not a moral failing or a Fall from a higher state but an essential existential structure: Dasein is proximally and for the most part absorbed in its world. Manifested concretely as idle talk, curiosity, and ambiguity. Characterized by temptation, tranquillizing, alienation, and entanglement (downward plunge). Temporally grounded in inauthentic making-present.
- **units**: D1-U7, D1-U8, D2-U3, D2-U4
- **aliases**: Verfallenheit, fallenness, absorption, entanglement, downward plunge (Absturz)
- **centrality**: core

#### 11. Das Man (the "they")

- **canonical_name**: Das Man
- **german_lemma**: das Man
- **mr_translation**: the "they"
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The anonymous, everyday "who" of Dasein. Not a particular person or sum of persons but the nobody to whom every Dasein has proximally surrendered itself. The "they" prescribes the kind of Being of everydayness through averageness, levelling down, publicness, and disburdening. Dasein's ownmost self must be wrested from the "they" through individuation (anxiety, conscience, resoluteness).
- **units**: D1-U4, D1-U5, D1-U7, D1-U8, D2-U1, D2-U2, D2-U5, D2-U6
- **aliases**: das Man, the "they", they-self (Man-selbst), publicness, anyone
- **centrality**: core

#### 12. Resoluteness (Entschlossenheit)

- **canonical_name**: Resoluteness
- **german_lemma**: Entschlossenheit
- **mr_translation**: resoluteness
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The authentic mode of disclosedness in which Dasein, called by conscience and accepting its Being-guilty (nullity), projects itself upon its ownmost potentiality-for-Being. Involves reticence, anxiety-readiness, and the letting of the ownmost Self act from itself. In its full existential form as anticipatory resoluteness (vorlaufende Entschlossenheit), it unites Being-towards-death with the call of conscience, disclosing the Situation as the authentic "there." Temporally grounded as the authentic present (moment of vision, Augenblick).
- **units**: D2-U2, D2-U3, D2-U5
- **aliases**: Entschlossenheit, anticipatory resoluteness (vorlaufende Entschlossenheit), Situation, moment of vision (Augenblick)
- **centrality**: core

### Important Nodes (15)

#### 13. Worldhood (Weltlichkeit)

- **canonical_name**: Worldhood
- **german_lemma**: Weltlichkeit
- **mr_translation**: worldhood
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The ontological structure of the world as an existentiale of Dasein, analyzed through the referential totality of significance (Bedeutsamkeit). Not the totality of beings but the relational context (involvement, for-the-sake-of-which) within which entities can be encountered as ready-to-hand. Temporally grounded in the horizonal schema of the ecstases.
- **units**: D1-U2, D1-U3, D1-U4, D2-U4
- **aliases**: Weltlichkeit, significance (Bedeutsamkeit), referential totality, involvement (Bewandtnis)
- **centrality**: important

#### 14. Readiness-to-hand (Zuhandenheit)

- **canonical_name**: Readiness-to-hand
- **german_lemma**: Zuhandenheit
- **mr_translation**: readiness-to-hand
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The mode of Being of equipment (Zeug) as encountered in circumspective concern. Equipment is not first perceived as an object with properties (presence-at-hand) and then given a value-predicate; rather, it is encountered as something in-order-to, within a totality of involvements. Readiness-to-hand is ontologically prior to presence-at-hand.
- **units**: D1-U2, D1-U3, D1-U5, D1-U6
- **aliases**: Zuhandenheit, the ready-to-hand, equipment (Zeug), tool-being
- **centrality**: important

#### 15. Presence-at-hand (Vorhandenheit)

- **canonical_name**: Presence-at-hand
- **german_lemma**: Vorhandenheit
- **mr_translation**: presence-at-hand
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The mode of Being of entities as they show themselves when circumspective concern breaks down or when theoretical attitude takes over. The present-at-hand is an entity stripped of its involvement-character, regarded as a mere "thing" with determinate properties. Traditional ontology (Descartes, the metaphysical tradition) privileges this mode, mistakenly taking it as the primary way of Being.
- **units**: D1-U2, D1-U3, D1-U6, D1-U9
- **aliases**: Vorhandenheit, the present-at-hand, occurrentness, res extensa (Descartes' derivative)
- **centrality**: important

#### 16. Authenticity/Inauthenticity (Eigentlichkeit/Uneigentlichkeit)

- **canonical_name**: Authenticity/Inauthenticity
- **german_lemma**: Eigentlichkeit / Uneigentlichkeit
- **mr_translation**: authenticity / inauthenticity
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The two fundamental modes in which Dasein can be itself. Authenticity: Dasein owns its existence, projecting upon its ownmost possibilities. Inauthenticity: Dasein disperses itself in the "they," evading its ownmost Being. Both are genuine modes of existence — inauthenticity is not lesser but the proximal and average way Dasein exists. The distinction runs through every existential structure.
- **units**: D1-U1, D1-U4, D1-U7, D1-U8, D2-U1, D2-U2, D2-U3, D2-U5
- **aliases**: Eigentlichkeit, Uneigentlichkeit, ownedness, self-ownership, they-self vs. authentic self
- **centrality**: important

#### 17. Anxiety (Angst)

- **canonical_name**: Anxiety
- **german_lemma**: Angst
- **mr_translation**: anxiety
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The fundamental state-of-mind that discloses Dasein's Being-in-the-world as such. Distinguished from fear: fear has a definite within-the-world object, anxiety does not. In anxiety, the world sinks into insignificance, the "they" collapses, and Dasein is individualized, thrown back upon its ownmost potentiality-for-Being. Reveals the uncanniness (Unheimlichkeit) underlying everydayness. Serves as the methodological access point for the phenomenon of care.
- **units**: D1-U5, D1-U8, D2-U1, D2-U2
- **aliases**: Angst, dread, uncanniness (Unheimlichkeit)
- **centrality**: important

#### 18. Conscience (Gewissen)

- **canonical_name**: Conscience
- **german_lemma**: Gewissen
- **mr_translation**: conscience
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The call (Ruf) of care that summons Dasein from its lostness in the "they" to its ownmost Being-guilty. Conscience discloses Dasein's nullity — the constitutive not-Being at the ground of its thrown projection. The caller is Dasein itself in its uncanniness; the called is the they-self. Wanting-to-have-a-conscience is the existentiell choice of resoluteness.
- **units**: D2-U2, D2-U3
- **aliases**: Gewissen, call of conscience, Ruf, Being-guilty (Schuldigsein), nullity
- **centrality**: important

#### 19. Discourse (Rede)

- **canonical_name**: Discourse
- **german_lemma**: Rede
- **mr_translation**: discourse
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The third equiprimordial existentiale of disclosedness (with state-of-mind and understanding). Discourse articulates the intelligibility of Being-in-the-world. It is more primordial than language (Sprache), which is the worldly expression of discourse. Constitutive moments: what discourse is about, what is said, communication, making-known. Includes hearing (Hören) and keeping silent (Schweigen). Falls into idle talk (Gerede) in everydayness.
- **units**: D1-U6, D1-U7, D2-U2
- **aliases**: Rede, talk, articulation, language (Sprache, derivative)
- **centrality**: important

#### 20. Thrownness (Geworfenheit)

- **canonical_name**: Thrownness
- **german_lemma**: Geworfenheit
- **mr_translation**: thrownness
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: Dasein's factical character of being delivered over to its "there." Dasein finds itself always already in a world, with a past and possibilities not of its choosing. Thrownness is disclosed in state-of-mind and constitutes the "already-in" moment of care. In Division Two, thrownness is temporally deepened as having-been (Gewesenheit) and ontologically extended through historicality.
- **units**: D1-U1, D1-U5, D1-U8, D2-U1, D2-U2, D2-U3, D2-U5
- **aliases**: Geworfenheit, facticity (existential sense), the "that-it-is"
- **centrality**: important

#### 21. Truth (Wahrheit / Aletheia)

- **canonical_name**: Truth
- **german_lemma**: Wahrheit / Aletheia
- **mr_translation**: truth / unconcealment
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: Reinterpreted from correspondence (adequatio) to unconcealment (Unverborgenheit). Primordial truth is Dasein's disclosedness — the clearing in which entities can be uncovered. Propositional truth (assertion as "true") is derivative, grounded in the more primordial Being-uncovering (Entdeckend-sein). Truth and untruth belong together: Dasein is equiprimordially in truth and in untruth (falling covers over). Truth must be "wrested" from concealment.
- **units**: Intro-U2, D1-U9, D2-U2
- **aliases**: Wahrheit, aletheia, unconcealment, Unverborgenheit, Being-uncovering (Entdeckend-sein)
- **centrality**: important

#### 22. Spatiality (Räumlichkeit)

- **canonical_name**: Spatiality
- **german_lemma**: Räumlichkeit
- **mr_translation**: spatiality
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: Dasein's existential spatiality, constituted by de-severance (Ent-fernung, bringing-close) and directionality (Ausrichtung). Not Cartesian extensio but a mode of Being-in-the-world: Dasein de-severs entities by bringing them into the nearness of concern. Grounded in temporality (the ecstasis of making-present) but not reducible to temporality. Space is neither "in" the subject nor "in" the world but discovered through Dasein's spatial Being-in-the-world.
- **units**: D1-U4, D2-U4
- **aliases**: Räumlichkeit, de-severance (Ent-fernung), directionality (Ausrichtung), bringing-close
- **centrality**: important

#### 23. Historicality (Geschichtlichkeit)

- **canonical_name**: Historicality
- **german_lemma**: Geschichtlichkeit
- **mr_translation**: historicality
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: Dasein's ontological constitution as historical, grounded in the ecstatic-horizonal temporality of care. Dasein "historizes" (geschieht): it stretches along between birth and death, inheriting a heritage and projecting possibilities that have-been. Authentic historicality is fate (Schicksal) — resolute taking-over of inherited possibilities. Communal historicality is destiny (Geschick). Founds the possibility of historiology as a science.
- **units**: D2-U5, D2-U6
- **aliases**: Geschichtlichkeit, historizing (Geschehen), heritage (Erbe), fate (Schicksal), destiny (Geschick), repetition (Wiederholung)
- **centrality**: important

#### 24. Within-time-ness (Innerzeitigkeit)

- **canonical_name**: Within-time-ness
- **german_lemma**: Innerzeitigkeit
- **mr_translation**: within-time-ness
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The most derivative level of temporality: time as publicly encountered "in which" events and entities occur. Characterized by datability, significance, spannedness, and publicness. Expressed through reckoning with time and clock-use. When its existential origins are levelled off, within-time-ness degenerates into the "ordinary" concept of time as an infinite, homogeneous sequence of nows (now-time, Jetzt-Zeit). Aristotle's definition of time as the number of motion belongs here.
- **units**: D2-U6
- **aliases**: Innerzeitigkeit, world-time (Weltzeit), now-time (Jetzt-Zeit), public time, ordinary time concept, reckoning with time
- **centrality**: important

#### 25. Interpretation (Auslegung)

- **canonical_name**: Interpretation
- **german_lemma**: Auslegung
- **mr_translation**: interpretation
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The working-out of possibilities projected in understanding. Not a bare cognitive act but the articulation of what is understood through the fore-structure: fore-having (Vorhabe), fore-sight (Vorsicht), fore-conception (Vorgriff). The hermeneutical "as" (something as something) is the primordial "as-structure" of interpretation. The apophantical "as" of assertion (Aussage) is a derivative modification.
- **units**: D1-U6, D1-U9
- **aliases**: Auslegung, explication, fore-structure (Vorstruktur), as-structure (Als-Struktur)
- **centrality**: important

#### 26. Being-with (Mitsein)

- **canonical_name**: Being-with
- **german_lemma**: Mitsein
- **mr_translation**: Being-with
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The existential structure through which Dasein is always already with Others. Not an additive relation (subject + subject) but an a priori co-constitution of Being-in-the-world. Others are encountered "from the world" of shared concern, not as present-at-hand objects. Gives rise to solicitude (Fürsorge) in its positive modes (leaping-in/dominating vs. leaping-ahead/liberating).
- **units**: D1-U4, D2-U5
- **aliases**: Mitsein, Being-with-one-another, co-Dasein (Mitdasein), solicitude (Fürsorge)
- **centrality**: important

#### 27. Destruktion

- **canonical_name**: Destruktion
- **german_lemma**: Destruktion
- **mr_translation**: destruction (of the history of ontology)
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The critical dismantling of the traditional concepts of ontology to lay bare the original experiences from which they arose. Not destruction in the negative sense but a positive appropriation that loosens the hardened tradition. Targets Kant, Descartes, Aristotle as stations. Only partially carried out in B&T; announced in the Introduction as the second task of the treatise.
- **units**: Intro-U2, D1-U3, D2-U6
- **aliases**: Destruktion, dismantling, de-structuring, critical history of ontology
- **centrality**: important

### Peripheral Nodes (10)

#### 28. Existence (Existenz)

- **canonical_name**: Existence
- **german_lemma**: Existenz
- **mr_translation**: existence
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The kind of Being toward which Dasein can comport itself and always does comport itself somehow. Dasein's "essence" lies in its existence — it is what it can be. Distinguished from the traditional existentia (actuality, presence-at-hand of an entity). Existentialia are the Being-characters of Dasein; categories belong to non-Dasein entities.
- **units**: Intro-U1, D1-U1
- **aliases**: Existenz, to-be, existentialia (vs. categories)
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 29. Fear (Furcht)

- **canonical_name**: Fear
- **german_lemma**: Furcht
- **mr_translation**: fear
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: An inauthentic mode of state-of-mind with a definite threatening entity (Wovor), a specific threatened entity (Worum), and a particular way of fearing. Distinguished from anxiety, which has no definite within-the-world object. Fear flees from a definite threat; anxiety discloses Being-in-the-world as such.
- **units**: D1-U5
- **aliases**: Furcht, fearing, dread (colloquial, not technical)
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 30. Signs (Zeichen)

- **canonical_name**: Signs
- **german_lemma**: Zeichen
- **mr_translation**: signs
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: A particular kind of equipment whose function is to indicate — to make a referential totality conspicuous. Signs serve circumspection by orienting Dasein within its environment. Analyzed as a case study in the structure of reference (Verweisung) and worldhood.
- **units**: D1-U2
- **aliases**: Zeichen, indication, reference
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 31. Idle talk (Gerede)

- **canonical_name**: Idle talk
- **german_lemma**: Gerede
- **mr_translation**: idle talk
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The everyday mode of discourse in which communication spreads and repeats what is said without genuine appropriation. Language becomes groundless — understanding floats free of the things themselves. One of the three concrete modes of falling (with curiosity and ambiguity).
- **units**: D1-U7
- **aliases**: Gerede, groundless talk, chatter
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 32. Curiosity (Neugier)

- **canonical_name**: Curiosity
- **german_lemma**: Neugier
- **mr_translation**: curiosity
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The everyday mode of seeing that seeks novelty for its own sake, never dwelling but always moving on. A fallen mode of circumspection freed from its practical context. Traced to Aristotle's wonder but shown to be its inauthentic derivative.
- **units**: D1-U7
- **aliases**: Neugier, inauthentic seeing
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 33. Phenomenology

- **canonical_name**: Phenomenology
- **german_lemma**: Phänomenologie
- **mr_translation**: phenomenology
- **type**: method
- **definition**: The method of the treatise: "To let that which shows itself be seen from itself in the very way in which it shows itself from itself." Composed of phenomenon (that which shows itself) and logos (letting something be seen). Phenomenology is ontology — access to the question of Being requires the phenomenological method. Hermeneutical in character: an interpretive uncovering.
- **units**: Intro-U2
- **aliases**: Phänomenologie, hermeneutic phenomenology, phenomenon + logos
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 34. Reality (Realität)

- **canonical_name**: Reality
- **german_lemma**: Realität
- **mr_translation**: Reality
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: Traditionally identified with the Being of entities (res). Heidegger argues Reality is ontologically grounded in care: "Reality" as a mode of Being of within-the-world entities presupposes Being-in-the-world. Neither the independence of the "external world" nor realism vs. idealism can be resolved without first clarifying the Being of the entity that raises such questions. Resistance (Widerständigkeit) reveals Reality in practical coping, not in theoretical perception.
- **units**: D1-U9
- **aliases**: Realität, the "Real", res, external world, resistance
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 35. Circumspection (Umsicht)

- **canonical_name**: Circumspection
- **german_lemma**: Umsicht
- **mr_translation**: circumspection
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The kind of sight (Sicht) belonging to practical concern with ready-to-hand entities. Not theoretical observation but a looking-around that guides Dasein's handling of equipment. When circumspection encounters breakdown (conspicuousness, obtrusiveness, obstinacy), the ready-to-hand entity begins to show itself as present-at-hand.
- **units**: D1-U2, D1-U6, D1-U7
- **aliases**: Umsicht, practical sight, concerned looking-around
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 36. Selfhood (Selbstheit)

- **canonical_name**: Selfhood
- **german_lemma**: Selbstheit
- **mr_translation**: Selfhood
- **type**: concept
- **definition**: The existential-ontological constitution of Dasein's self-constancy (Selbst-ständigkeit). Not a persisting substance (soul, subject, person) but grounded in the ecstatic unity of temporality. Authentic Selfhood: Self-constancy in anticipatory resoluteness. Inauthentic selfhood: the they-self's lack of Self-constancy (un-Selbst-ständigkeit).
- **units**: D2-U3, D2-U5
- **aliases**: Selbstheit, Self-constancy, Selbst-ständigkeit, the Self
- **centrality**: peripheral

#### 37. Temporalität (Temporality of Being)

- **canonical_name**: Temporalität
- **german_lemma**: Temporalität
- **mr_translation**: Temporality (of Being as such)
- **type**: concept

## 3C. Tension Layer

Cross-unit tensions where two nodes exhibit both **dependency** and **conflict** edges. These are productive tensions internal to the treatise's argument, not errors.

### T1. Readiness-to-hand ↔ Presence-at-hand

- **Dependency**: Presence-at-hand is a deficient mode that emerges when readiness-to-hand breaks down (D1-U2). Theoretical science presupposes the practical encounter (D1-U3, D1-U9).
- **Conflict**: Traditional ontology privileges presence-at-hand as primary Being; Heidegger inverts the priority. Yet presence-at-hand cannot be eliminated — it is required for natural science, assertion, and ontological thematization. The two modes of Being remain in irreducible tension: practice requires theory for its own self-understanding.
- **Units**: D1-U2, D1-U3, D1-U6, D1-U9

### T2. Authenticity ↔ Das Man (the "they")

- **Dependency**: Authentic existence presupposes the "they" as the from-which of individuation — Dasein must be in the "they" before it can wrest itself from it (D1-U4, D1-U8, D2-U2). The "they" is not eliminable; authentic Dasein is an "existentiell modification" of das Man.
- **Conflict**: The "they" covers over ownmost possibilities, levels down, tranquillizes. Authenticity requires breaking free from precisely this covering. Yet Heidegger insists authenticity is not withdrawal from the social world but a modified engagement with it.
- **Units**: D1-U1, D1-U4, D1-U7, D1-U8, D2-U1, D2-U2, D2-U3

### T3. Disclosedness ↔ Falling

- **Dependency**: Falling belongs to disclosedness as an essential structural moment (D1-U7, D1-U8). Dasein is equiprimordially in truth and in untruth (D1-U9).
- **Conflict**: Falling covers over what disclosedness opens up. Idle talk, curiosity, and ambiguity close down genuine understanding. The tension is constitutive: Dasein cannot be disclosed without also being in some mode of concealment.
- **Units**: D1-U5, D1-U7, D1-U8, D1-U9

### T4. Care ↔ Temporality

- **Dependency**: Temporality is the meaning (Sinn) of care — care is only intelligible through the ecstatic unity of future, having-been, and Present (D2-U3).
- **Conflict**: Care is articulated as a structural totality in Division One without explicit temporal language; the temporal interpretation in Division Two appears to add a new layer rather than merely unpack what was already there. The transition creates a methodological tension: is temporality discovered or imposed? The three ecstases map onto the three moments of care, but the fit is argued, not self-evident.
- **Units**: D1-U8, D2-U3, D2-U4

### T5. Being-towards-death ↔ Being-with

- **Dependency**: Death individualizes — it is the possibility that is "ownmost, non-relational, not-to-be-outstripped" (D2-U1). Yet authentic Being-towards-death enables authentic Being-with: only a Dasein that owns its finitude can authentically encounter Others (D2-U2, D2-U5 — destiny as communal historicality presupposes individual fate).
- **Conflict**: The existential analysis of death seems to dissolve sociality: in anxiety before death, Dasein is radically alone. How communal destiny (Geschick) is founded on individual fate without reducing the communal remains structurally underdeveloped.
- **Units**: D1-U4, D2-U1, D2-U2, D2-U5

### T6. Temporality (Zeitlichkeit) ↔ Within-time-ness (Innerzeitigkeit)

- **Dependency**: Within-time-ness is derived from primordial temporality through a series of levelling-off modifications (D2-U6). World-time arises from Dasein's temporalizing in concern.
- **Conflict**: The "ordinary concept of time" (infinite now-sequence) dominates philosophy from Aristotle to Hegel. Heidegger claims this is derivative, yet his account of primordial temporality must use temporal language already shaped by the ordinary concept. Circularity: the very terms in which Zeitlichkeit is articulated (future, past, present) carry connotations of the now-time they are supposed to found.
- **Units**: Intro-U2, D2-U3, D2-U6

### T7. Thrownness ↔ Projection

- **Dependency**: Dasein is always already thrown AND projecting — these are equiprimordial moments of disclosedness (D1-U5, D1-U8). Care unifies them: Being-already-in (thrownness) and Being-ahead-of-itself (projection).
- **Conflict**: Thrownness discloses limitation, facticity, the given; projection discloses possibility, freedom, the not-yet. Dasein cannot fully project because it is thrown, and cannot simply accept thrownness because it projects. The "thrown projection" (geworfener Entwurf) names this tension without resolving it. In Division Two, the tension intensifies: Dasein is "thrown into its death" (D2-U1), projecting upon a possibility that annihilates all projection.
- **Units**: D1-U1, D1-U5, D1-U8, D2-U1, D2-U2, D2-U3

### T8. Existence ↔ Facticity

- **Dependency**: Care's formula unites them: Being-ahead-of-itself (existence) already-in-a-world (facticity). Neither can stand without the other. Understanding (existence-moment) and state-of-mind (facticity-moment) are equiprimordial.
- **Conflict**: Existence implies freedom, openness, possibility; facticity implies constraint, givenness, limitation. Dasein is never pure freedom (it is always already thrown) and never mere factum (it always projects). The tension structures the entire problematic of authentic self-choice: how can a thrown entity choose itself?
- **Units**: D1-U1, D1-U5, D1-U8, D2-U2, D2-U3

## 3E. Book Trajectory Narrative

### Introduction (§§1–8)

The treatise opens with the revival of the question of Being — a question that, Heidegger contends, has been forgotten since its Greek inception. The Introduction (Units 1–2) establishes the formal structure of the inquiry: Being is not a genus, not self-evident, and not definable, yet every Dasein operates with a pre-ontological understanding of Being in its everyday comportments. Dasein is singled out as the entity whose Being-constitution grants privileged access to the question. The methodological program is laid out: existential analytic of Dasein as preparatory fundamental ontology, phenomenology as the way of access ("to let that which shows itself be seen from itself"), and Destruktion of the history of ontology to recover buried originary experiences. Two critical distinctions are programmatically announced — Zeitlichkeit (temporality of Dasein) and Temporalität (temporality of Being) — but only the former receives full treatment. The Introduction thus functions as a promissory note: it establishes what is to be demonstrated while deferring the demonstration to the Divisions that follow.

### Division One: The Preparatory Fundamental Analysis of Dasein (§§9–44)

Division One (Units D1-U1 through D1-U9) carries out the analytic of Dasein's everydayness in a layered spiral. It begins with the formal indication of Dasein's existential constitution — existence, facticity, falling — and immediately installs Being-in-the-world as the unitary basic state (Unit 1). The environmental analysis of worldhood (Unit 2) establishes readiness-to-hand as ontologically prior to presence-at-hand, dismantling the Cartesian res extensa ontology (Unit 3). The analysis then turns to the "who" of everyday Dasein: Being-with and das Man (Unit 4), followed by the three equiprimordial existentialia of disclosedness — state-of-mind, understanding, and discourse (Units 5–6). Falling (Unit 7) reveals that Dasein's proximal self-understanding is governed by idle talk, curiosity, and ambiguity. The spiral closes in Units 8–9 with the structural totality of care (Sorge) — wrested from concealment through the phenomenon of anxiety — and the existential reinterpretation of truth as unconcealment (aletheia). By Division One's end, the analytic has yielded a complete but still provisional picture: care is the Being of Dasein, but the question of the meaning of care — and with it, the meaning of Being — remains unanswered. The analytic is explicitly "preparatory"; its results are not yet grounded.

### Division Two: Dasein and Temporality (§§45–83)

Division Two (Units D2-U1 through D2-U6) repeats and deepens Division One's analyses by seeking their ontological ground in temporality. The pivot occurs through the phenomena of wholeness and authenticity. Being-towards-death (Unit 1) reveals Dasein's finitude — its ownmost, non-relational, not-to-be-outstripped possibility — and anticipation (Vorlaufen) becomes the structure through which Dasein can hold open its most extreme possibility. The call of conscience and resoluteness (Unit 2) provide the ontic attestation of authentic existence: Dasein accepts its Being-guilty (nullity) and projects itself resolutely upon its Situation. Unit 3 achieves the treatise's central demonstration: temporality (Zeitlichkeit) is the meaning of care, and the three ecstases — future (coming-towards-itself), having-been (thrown back upon), Present (making-present) — correspond to the three structural moments of care. Units 4–6 then show how temporality underlies everyday Being-in-the-world (including spatiality and transcendence), historicality (Dasein's stretching between birth and death, fate, destiny, repetition), and within-time-ness (the derivation of the ordinary time-concept through progressive levelling-off). The book ends not with closure but with an explicit question: does primordial time reveal itself as the horizon of Being? — pointing toward the unwritten Division Three and the transition from Zeitlichkeit to Temporalität that Heidegger never completed in this form.
