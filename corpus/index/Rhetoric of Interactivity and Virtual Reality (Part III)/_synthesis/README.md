# Rhetoric of Interactivity and Virtual Reality (Part III)

A two-source combined entry built 2026-07-20 (Phase 4 of the new-media presence work).

## Grouping decision
**One combined entry, not two standalone entries.** Both sources share a single thread — the **authored environment as
a rhetorical actor on its inhabitant (design-as-rhetoric)** — and divide its labor: Golliher = the **doing lever**
(interactivity → agency), Ulrich = the **moving lever** (immersion → persuasion). Each is small and they compose one
argument, so a combined entry is more faithful and useful than two one-unit entries. Rationale detailed in
`cluster-ontology.md` §1.
