# Bridge Source Digest — Physiological Assessment (King & Salvo, ASEE 2023)

**Citation:** C. E. King, K. R. Feeney, Q. Tang, M. Das, D. Salvo. "Work in Progress: Physiological Assessment of Learning in a Virtual Reality Clinical Immersion Environment." *2023 ASEE Annual Conference & Exposition*, Baltimore, MD, June 25, 2023. Paper ID #37129.
**Role in bridge:** the **depth-channel** evidence (EEG/DMN) and the **time-drag** self-reports. First in the lineage of three.

## Design
Within-subjects pilot, **N = 3** (all male; S1, S2 novice; S3 experienced with the clinical content). Each watched **three 17-minute videos**: **boring** (1989 Microsoft Word tutorial), **exciting** (alien-reproduction-vehicle conspiracy video), **clinical immersion** (360° spinal-deformation surgery). Boredom operationalized via **Fahlman**: "the aversive experience of having an unfulfilled desire to be engaged in satisfying activity"; default-mode-network activity (alpha 8–12 Hz / theta 4–8 Hz) taken as the boredom marker.

## Measures
- **EEG**: 19-electrode cap but **only 4 channels active (F3, F4, P3, P4)**, 200 Hz, 1–35 Hz bandpass.
- **HR, HRV, eye-gaze, pupil**: HP Reverb G2 Omnicept headset.

## Findings (with reliability flags)
- **[clean] Time perception:** all three felt the clinical video **longer than its actual 17 min** (S1: felt 25–30 min). → *Hingehaltenheit* / the "long while" signature.
- **[clean] Self-reported engagement HIGH:** interest 8/8 for two subjects; gaze reported "engaged throughout."
- **[suggestive] EEG:** the **clinical video's EEG more closely resembled the BORING control** than the exciting one. *But* statistically inconclusive — only S1's EEG was clean; S2 had movement artifacts, S3 impedance problems.
- **[inconclusive] HR/HRV:** no conclusive pattern; HR drifted upward across sessions (~70→100+ BPM, Fig 7) — plausibly arousal/fatigue, not condition-specific.
- **"Learning" was never measured as an outcome** despite the title — the study measures physiological/affective state, not learning gains.

## Bridge hooks (→ `fcm-king-salvo-bridge.md`)
1. **Engaged-yet-boring-like paradox** (high self-reported engagement + boring-like EEG + dragging time) = candidate **second-form** boredom (occupied surface, hollow depth). `provenance: anticipatory-application`.
2. **Time dilation** = direct phenomenal correlate of *Hingehaltenheit* / *Langeweile*.
3. **Reliability caveat:** EEG here is N=1-clean; the bridge must lean on the self-report + the larger 2024 gaze dataset, treating 2023 EEG as corroborating only.
