# Book-Level Ontology — FCM (boredom-focused)

The conceptual architecture of *The Fundamental Concepts of Metaphysics* as it bears on boredom. Loci are triple `(p. / GA / pdf)`; GA read from running heads.

## 1. The governing axis: attunement is disclosive

The whole book turns on one inversion: a *Stimmung* is not a private feeling but a way beings as a whole are *made manifest*. A **fundamental attunement (*Grundstimmung*)** discloses; therefore it cannot be *ascertained* like something present-at-hand, only **awakened** (§16, p.59 / GA ~[89] / pdf 42; §17, p.66 / pdf 45). Everyday busyness keeps it asleep. This is the license for treating boredom not as a state to be measured but as an attunement to be interpreted from within — and the hinge on which the King–Salvo bridge swings (a bipolar "engagement" scale measures a state; Heidegger interprets a disclosure).

## 2. The descent through three forms

Boredom is worked as a controlled deepening, each form re-running the same two structural moments at greater depth:

- **First form — becoming bored *by* something** (*Gelangweiltwerden von etwas*), the train-station: a determinate boring thing (§§19–23, pp.78–105 / pdf 51–64).
- **Second form — being bored *with* something** (*Sichlangweilen bei etwas*), the dinner-party: no determinate culprit; the boredom rises *from Dasein itself* (§§24–28, pp.106–131 / pdf 65–77); declared more profound at §28 (p.128 / pdf 76).
- **Third form — profound boredom** (*es ist einem langweilig*, "it is boring for one"): impersonal, still; passing-the-time no longer permitted (§§29–36, pp.132–159 / pdf 78–91).

`deepens-into`: first → second → third.

## 3. The structural-moment grammar

Two moments recur and transform across all three forms:

- **Being left empty** (*Leergelassenheit*) — beings' *telling refusal* (*Versagen*) of themselves; first localized at §23d (p.101 / pdf 62), radicalized at §31a as Dasein "delivered over" to beings' refusal as a whole (p.137 / pdf 80).
- **Being held in limbo** (*Hingehaltenheit*) — being impelled toward what makes Dasein possible; §23c (p.99 / pdf 61), radicalized §31b (p.140 / pdf 82).

Their **structural unity** is the analytic payload (§26, p.126 / pdf 74; §31, p.136 / pdf 80). Against both stands **passing-the-time** (*Zeitvertreib*), the counter-move that drives boredom off and "drives time on" (§23a, p.93 / pdf 58) — visible at its most concrete in *looking at our watch* (§23b, p.96 / pdf 60).

## 4. The temporal core

Profound boredom is, at bottom, a relation to **time**:

- **Entrancement** (*Gebanntheit* / *Bann*) by the single threefold horizon of time = the temporal character of being-left-empty (§32a, p.145 / pdf 84).
- The **moment of vision** (*Augenblick*) toward which we are impelled = the temporal character of being-held-in-limbo (§32b, p.148 / pdf 86).
- **Langeweile** = the "lengthening of the while," the expansion of the temporal horizon (§33, p.152 / pdf 88).

`temporalizes-as`: being-left-empty → entrancement; being-held-in-limbo → moment-of-vision.

## 5. The boredom → questions → world arc

Profound boredom, pressed to its ground, *gives to be questioned* the three **metaphysical questions** — **world, individuation (*Vereinzelung*), finitude** — with the **essence of time as their common root** (§39, p.169 / GA [252–53] / pdf 96). Part Two is therefore not a new topic but the cashing-out of the boredom analysis; §67 explicitly *returns* the world-question to "the direction disclosed by the interpretation of profound boredom" (p.279 / pdf 152).

`grounds-question`: profound-boredom → {world, individuation, finitude}.

## 6. The world-thesis triad (Part Two)

The world-question is pursued comparatively (§42, p.176 / pdf 100):

- **The stone is worldless** (*weltlos*) — no access to beings.
- **The animal is poor in world** (*weltarm*) — a being-open in **captivation** (*Benommenheit*, §58, p.236 / pdf 130) inside a **disinhibiting ring** (*Enthemmungsring*, §60b, p.253 / pdf 139), withheld from beings *as* beings; poverty = **deprivation**, not lesser degree. Biology interlocutors: Driesch, Uexküll (§61b, p.261 / pdf 143).
- **Man is world-forming** (*weltbildend*) — able to take beings *as* beings (the "as"-structure), read from the apophantic *logos* (§72, p.304 / pdf 164); culminating in **projection** (*Entwurf*) as the primordial structure of world-formation (§76, p.360 / pdf 192).

Definitional keystone: **world = the accessibility / manifestness of beings as beings** (§47, p.196 / pdf 110; §68, p.282 / pdf 153).

## 7. The spine, in one line

awaken attunement → three forms of boredom (deepening) → structural moments (empty / limbo) unified temporally (entrancement / moment-of-vision) → profound boredom releases the three questions (rooted in time) → world as the manifestness of beings as such as a whole.
