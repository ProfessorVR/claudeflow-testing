# FCM — Terminology Appendix (Deduplicated Lemma Register)

**Work:** Martin Heidegger, *The Fundamental Concepts of Metaphysics: World, Finitude, Solitude* (*Die Grundbegriffe der Metaphysik. Welt — Endlichkeit — Einsamkeit*), GA 29/30, Freiburg lecture course WS 1929–30, trans. William McNeill & Nicholas Walker, Indiana University Press, 1995.

**Scope:** every distinct key TERM / GREEK / GERMAN lemma drawn from the 15 unit JSONs (`fcm-00` … `fcm-14`), deduplicated and consolidated. Where a lemma appears in several units, the loci listed are first/representative; the "Units" column lists every unit where the lemma (or its direct cognate) is a registered key term. Loci use the running triple convention `(p.NN / GA pp.NN / pdf NN)`.

**Grouping:** I — German lemmas; II — Greek (incl. transliterated Greek and the scholastic Latin technical terms that the units tag under the Greek/Latin apparatus); III — English-technical / translator's coinages. Alphabetized within each group (German and Greek alphabetized by transliteration; articles/prefixes ignored).

**Count:** 150 distinct lemma rows (German 84 · Greek/Latin 37 · English-technical 29).
