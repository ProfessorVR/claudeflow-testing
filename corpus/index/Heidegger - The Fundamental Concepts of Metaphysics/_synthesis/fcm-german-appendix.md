# FCM — German Appendix (Translator's-Glossary Style)

**Work:** Martin Heidegger, *The Fundamental Concepts of Metaphysics: World, Finitude, Solitude* (*Die Grundbegriffe der Metaphysik. Welt — Endlichkeit — Einsamkeit*), GA 29/30, WS 1929–30, trans. William McNeill & Nicholas Walker, Indiana University Press, 1995.

**Form (per entry):** *German headword* — McNeill/Walker English rendering — one-line gloss — locus `(p.NN / GA pp.NN / pdf NN)` — units. Canonical equivalences are anchored on the published McNeill–Walker Glossary (FCM pp. 375–376; unit `fcm-14`); where the published glossary fixes the rendering, that is the rendering used. Alphabetized by German headword (prefixes *Da-/Weg-/Sich-* sorted under their stem where natural; cross-listed otherwise).

**Count:** 66 German lemmas. The required boredom/attunement/world core is included in full (*Langeweile, tiefe Langeweile, Stimmung, Grundstimmung, Befindlichkeit, Leergelassenheit, Hingehaltenheit, Zeitvertreib, Augenblick, Gebanntheit/Bann, Benommenheit, Enthemmung(sring), Versagen, das stehende Jetzt, Dasein, Da-sein/Weg-sein, Vereinzelung, Entwurf, weltlos, weltarm, weltbildend, Heimweh, Lässigkeit, Sichversetzen*).

---

### A — B

**Andrang / andrangen** — *impressing-upon* — the peculiar pressing of the power of time to which we are bound in becoming bored. — (p.98 / GA pp.147–49 / pdf 61) — fcm-04

**Armut / Ar-mut** — *poverty* — heard through *Mut* (mood): "in a mood of poverty," a manner of comporting and bearing oneself, not a mere property (cf. *Schwermut*, *Demut*). — (p.195 / GA pp.287–88 / pdf 109) — fcm-09, fcm-10

**Augenblick** — *moment of vision* — the look of resolute disclosedness (*Blick der Entschlossenheit*) for action; ruptures the temporal entrancement and is itself a possibility of time; credited to Kierkegaard. — (p.149 / GA p.224 / pdf 87) — fcm-04, fcm-06, fcm-07, fcm-08, fcm-13, fcm-14

**Bann / bannen** — *entrancement / to entrance* — the entrancement of time's threefold horizon that characterizes profound boredom (glossary headword *to entrance: bannen*). — (p.147 / GA p.222 / pdf 84) — fcm-06, fcm-14

**Bedrängnis / bedrängen** — *oppressiveness / to oppress* — to press hard; the keystone of the contemporary diagnosis is that an *essential* oppressiveness is absent (*das Ausbleiben einer wesenhaften Bedrängnis*). — (p.164 / GA pp.244–46 / pdf 94) — fcm-07, fcm-14

**Befindlichkeit** — *disposition / state-of-mind* — the disclosive structure of attunement (how one finds oneself disposed); flagged as *absent* from the OCR'd published glossary entry, but operative across the attunement analysis. — (p.66 / GA pp.99–103 / pdf 45) — fcm-03, fcm-14

**Benehmen / Sichbenehmen** — *behaviour* — the animal's specific manner of being: a driven performing (*Treiben*), set against human comportment (*Verhalten*) as action. — (p.237 / GA pp.345 / pdf 130) — fcm-12

**Benommenheit** — *captivation* (also *benumbment*) — the essential structure of animality: held within itself such that any apprehending of something *as* something is withheld; "the fundamental essence of the organism." — (p.238 / GA pp.346 / pdf 131) — fcm-11, fcm-12, fcm-14

### D

**Da-sein / Dasein** — *being-there* — the being of man (vs. the present-at-hand being of the stone); metaphysics is a fundamental occurrence within human Dasein. (Glossary OCR omits the standalone *Dasein* headword — gap flagged in fcm-14.) — (p.6 / GA pp.8–9 / pdf 15) — fcm-00, fcm-01, fcm-03, fcm-06, fcm-08

**Diensthaftigkeit** — *subservience* — the organ subserves the capacity that develops it; "serviceability and subservience are not the same" (vs. *Dienlichkeit*, serviceability, of equipment). — (p.223 / GA pp.325 / pdf 123) — fcm-11

### E

**Eigentum / Eigentümlichkeit** — *proper being / proper peculiarity* — capacity becomes proper to itself without self-consciousness; "*self*" and selfhood are reserved for the human (cf. the king's *Königtum*). — (p.233 / GA pp.339 / pdf 128) — fcm-11, fcm-12

**Eingenommenheit (in sich)** — *absorption in itself* — the animal's intrinsic self-retention that is the basis of behaviour (no reflection involved). — (p.239 / GA pp.347 / pdf 131) — fcm-12

**Einsamkeit** — *solitude* — the published subtitle term (manuscript reading retained over the noticeboard's "Individuation"). — (p.375 / GA pp.~541 / pdf 199) — fcm-14

**Endlichkeit** — *finitude* — the unrest of the "not" in our being-underway; the most originary of the three questions, placed in the middle because it holds world and individuation together. — (p.6 / GA pp.8–9 / pdf 15) — fcm-00, fcm-08, fcm-09, fcm-14

**Enthemmung / enthemmen** — *disinhibition / to disinhibit* — that which releases the inhibited instinctual drive into driven activity and constantly withdraws, never attended to as such. — (p.255 / GA pp.370 / pdf 139) — fcm-12, fcm-14

**Enthemmungsring** — *disinhibiting ring* — the encircling sphere the animal draws about itself, prescribing what can affect or occasion its behaviour; borne along as long as it lives; "*Umwelt* means nothing other than" this. — (p.255 / GA pp.370 / pdf 139) — fcm-12

**Entwurf** — *projection* — the primordial structure that originarily unifies the three moments of world-formation; "projection is world-projection," in it world prevails. — (p.363 / GA pp.527 / pdf 194) — fcm-13

**Ergriffenheit / ergriffen** — *being gripped* — being seized by what concepts are to comprehend; all being-gripped comes from and remains in an attunement; the possibility of being gripped is what awakening an attunement creates. — (p.7 / GA pp.9–10 / pdf 15) — fcm-00, fcm-09

### F

**Fähigkeit / Fähigsein** — *capacity / capability* — the organism (not the organ) has capacities; "the capacity creates its specific organs for itself." — (p.218 / GA pp.320 / pdf 121) — fcm-11

**Fertigkeit** — *readiness* — equipment is *ready* (completed, serviceable, incapable of getting further), as against the intrinsically self-driving capacity. — (p.221 / GA pp.322 / pdf 122) — fcm-11

**Feststellen** — *ascertaining / to ascertain* — the objectifying mode that, applied to attunement, destroys or weakens rather than awakens it (the wrong path to a *Grundstimmung*). — (p.59 / GA pp.90 / pdf 41) — fcm-03, fcm-04

### G

**Gebanntheit** — *being entranced* — time entrances Dasein by the single threefold horizon; the temporal character of being-left-empty in profound boredom. — (p.147 / GA p.222 / pdf 84) — fcm-06

**Gelangweiltwerden von etwas** — *to become bored by something* — the passive idiom naming the first, situation-bound form of boredom (a determinate boring thing). — (p.78 / GA pp.118–19 / pdf 51) — fcm-04, fcm-14

**Gelassenheit** — *releasement* — the freeing of the everyday perspective from psychological/consciousness-theories, the methodological comportment required to meet boredom undisguised. — (p.91 / GA pp.136–38 / pdf 58) — fcm-04

**Genommenheit** — *withholding* — the withholding/being-taken structure feeding the *Benommenheit* (captivation) and *Hingenommenheit* family. — (p.376 / GA pp.~542 / pdf 200) — fcm-12, fcm-14

**Geschick** — *fate / destiny* — the truth of philosophizing is in part rooted in the fate of Dasein. — (p.19 / GA pp.28–29 / pdf 22) — fcm-01

**Grundgeschehen** — *fundamental occurrence* — philosophizing constitutes the fundamental occurrence of Dasein, prior to every occupation. — (p.24 / GA pp.35–37 / pdf 24) — fcm-01

**Grundstimmung** — *fundamental attunement* — the originary, pervasive way Dasein is attuned in its grounds, giving Dasein subsistence and possibility; profound boredom is FCM's privileged *Grundstimmung*. — (p.7 / GA pp.9–10 / pdf 15) — fcm-00, fcm-02, fcm-03, fcm-04, fcm-09, fcm-14

### H

**Heimweh** — *homesickness* — the fundamental attunement of philosophizing ("an urge to be at home everywhere"), possible only because we who philosophize are not at home everywhere (Novalis). — (p.5 / GA pp.6–8 / pdf 14) — fcm-00

**Hingehaltenheit / hinhalten** — *being held in limbo / to hold in limbo* — the first structural moment: held fast yet given leeway, held up toward nothing in particular (by dragging time in form I; by standing time in forms II–III). — (p.87 / GA pp.130–32 / pdf 56) — fcm-04, fcm-05, fcm-06, fcm-07, fcm-14

**Hingenommenheit / hingenommen** — *being taken* — the animal is "taken" by its food/things; captivated being-taken excludes taking up a position over against what takes it. — (p.243 / GA pp.354 / pdf 133) — fcm-04, fcm-12

### I

**Inbegriff** (pl. *Inbegriffe*) — *comprehensive / inclusive concept* — metaphysical concepts that comprehend the whole *and* the comprehending human being; not universals. — (p.9 / GA pp.12–13 / pdf 16) — fcm-00

**Innenwelt** — *inner world* — the second term of Uexküll's title (*Umwelt und Innenwelt der Tiere*). — (p.263 / GA pp.383 / pdf 144) — fcm-12

### L

**Langeweile** — *boredom* — literally the "long while" (*lange Weile*): the lengthening of the while allotted to Dasein, exposing boredom's structural relation to time. — (p.79 / GA pp.118–19 / pdf 51) — fcm-04, fcm-06, fcm-14

**Lässigkeit** — *casualness* — abandoning oneself (*sich überlassen*) to being-there-alongside things; an obstructive casualness that is a more profound being-left-empty (second form). — (p.116 / GA pp.175–76 / pdf 70) — fcm-05

**Leergelassenheit / leer gelassen** — *being left empty* — the second structural moment: the refusal of things that, though at hand, offer nothing; in forms II–III a *self-forming* emptiness. — (p.87 / GA pp.130–32 / pdf 56) — fcm-04, fcm-05, fcm-06, fcm-07, fcm-14

### M — N

**Mut / Gemüt** — *(mood of) courage / overall mood* — the older sense (cheer/spirits/mood); the root anchoring *Schwermut*, *Armut*, *Demut* (glossary headword *(mood of) courage: Mut*). — (p.183 / GA pp.270–72 / pdf 103) — fcm-07, fcm-09

**Not** — *need / deprivation* — emptiness "in the sense of telling refusal … thus emptiness as lack, deprivation, need"; the deepest need is that an essential oppressiveness refuses itself. — (p.162 / GA pp.242–43 / pdf 93) — fcm-07

### R — S

**Reizbarkeit** — *irritability / ability to be stimulated* — Müller's mark of "living substance"; Heidegger inverts the order — stimulation is possible only where there is prior disinhibition and intrinsic encirclement. — (p.255 / GA pp.372 / pdf 140) — fcm-12

**Schrecken** — *terror* — we must hold out in the terror in which man as a whole is in the grip of an attack (*Angriff*); the affective register Part One installs. — (p.21 / GA pp.31–32 / pdf 21) — fcm-01

**Schwermut** — *(mood of) melancholy* — the threshold toward which obstinate boredom propels us; the attunement in which philosophy as creative activity stands (form, not content). — (p.79 / GA pp.118–19 / pdf 51) — fcm-04, fcm-09, fcm-14

**Sichlangweilen bei etwas** — *to be bored with something* — the second, surface-occupied / depth-hollow form; the boring arises from Dasein itself, not from a determinate thing. — (p.106 / GA pp.161 / pdf 65) — fcm-05, fcm-14

**Sichversetzen** — *transposing oneself* — going-along-with another being while remaining oneself; not factical transference, substitution, or "as-if," and not empathy (*Einfühlung*, which presupposes being "outside"). — (p.201 / GA pp.294 / pdf 112) — fcm-11

**das stehende Jetzt** — *the standing now* — the arrested temporality disclosed in (second-form / profound) boredom: the now cut off on both sides, stuck and stretched, standing into Dasein. — (p.376 / GA pp.~542 / pdf 200) — fcm-05, fcm-14

**die stehende Zeit** — *standing time* — the sealing-off of having-been and unbinding of future dissolve the flowing now-sequence into a single stretched "now." — (p.125 / GA pp.188–89 / pdf 75) — fcm-05, fcm-14

**Stimmung / stimmen** — *attunement / to attune* — not an interior feeling-state but a disclosive way of being-there, the medium of thinking and acting; where there is *Stimmung*, there is the possibility of a change of attunement. — (p.67 / GA pp.100–101 / pdf 45) — fcm-03, fcm-09, fcm-14

### T

**tiefe Langeweile** — *profound (deep) boredom* — the third, properly fundamental form ("it is boring for one"); named first as the concealed *Grundstimmung* of the contemporary situation, "a silent fog in the abysses of Dasein." — (p.74 / GA pp.110–11 / pdf 49) — fcm-03, fcm-06, fcm-07, fcm-08, fcm-09, fcm-14

**Trieb / Antrieb** — *(instinctual) drive* — the capable drives itself toward its wherefore in advance; drive permeates the whole movement, so it is never merely mechanical and is in principle un-mathematizable. — (p.5 / GA pp.6–8 / pdf 14) — fcm-00, fcm-11, fcm-14

### U

**Umwelt** — *environing world / environmental world* — Uexküll's term, which Heidegger says "means nothing other than" the disinhibiting ring (problematic if the *human* world is spoken of the same way). — (p.193 / GA pp.285 / pdf 108) — fcm-10, fcm-12

**Umringen / Umring** — *to encircle / encircling ring* — the animal is encircled by the ring of the reciprocal drivenness of its drives, which it cannot escape and within which something is open for it. — (p.249 / GA pp.363 / pdf 137) — fcm-12, fcm-14

### V

**Verhalten** — *comportment* — the human manner of being as action / self-directed comporting-toward, governed by letting-be (and so the possibility of not-letting-be) — set against the animal's mere *Benehmen*. — (p.237 / GA pp.345 / pdf 130) — fcm-12, fcm-13

**Vereinzelung** — *individuation* — the individuation of Dasein with respect to itself; the noticeboard subtitle Heidegger reportedly preferred ("World, Finitude, Individuation"). — (p.6 / GA pp.8–9 / pdf 15) — fcm-00, fcm-08, fcm-09, fcm-14

**Versagen** — *(telling) refusal / to refuse* — beings as a whole refuse Dasein's possibilities, yet every refusal (*Versagen*) is in itself a telling (*Sagen*) that announces and calls. — (p.137 / GA p.207 / pdf 80) — fcm-06, fcm-07, fcm-08, fcm-14

**verweilen / Weile** — *to tarry / the while* — the "whiling" that boredom protracts; at issue in *Langeweile* is a *Weile*, a tarrying, a peculiar enduring (glossary headword *the while: die Weile*). — (p.96 / GA pp.144–46 / pdf 60) — fcm-04, fcm-06, fcm-14

### W

**Walten** — *prevailing / holding sway* — the verbal sense of *physis* ("the self-forming prevailing of beings as a whole"); also *Walten der Welt*, the prevailing of world, the essence of world named positively. — (p.25 / GA pp.37 / pdf 24) — fcm-02, fcm-13

**Wecken** — *awakening* — letting what is asleep become wakeful, as opposed to ascertaining (*Feststellen*) something present-at-hand. — (p.59 / GA pp.90 / pdf 41) — fcm-03

**Weg-sein** — *being-away / being absent* — an essential way of Dasein's being-there, not non-being; possible only because and so long as man is there (the stone, in its being-away, is precisely *not* there). — (p.63 / GA pp.94–96 / pdf 43) — fcm-03

**Welt** — *world* — not the sum of beings but the manifestness within which beings can be encountered *as* beings; the expanse of the "as a whole" disclosed in profound boredom. — (p.168 / GA pp.249 / pdf 96) — fcm-07, fcm-08, fcm-09, fcm-13, fcm-14

**weltarm / Weltarmut** — *poor in world / poverty in world* — the animal thesis: poverty as *deprivation* (*Entbehrung*), not a lesser quantity of world; grounded in captivation. — (p.177 / GA pp.263 / pdf 100) — fcm-09, fcm-10, fcm-11, fcm-12, fcm-14

**weltbildend / (Welt-)bildung** — *world-forming / (world-)formation* — the man thesis: the *Dasein in man* forms world in a threefold sense (brings forth, sets forth an image, constitutes/embraces). — (p.177 / GA pp.263 / pdf 100) — fcm-09, fcm-10, fcm-12, fcm-13, fcm-14

**weltlos** — *worldless* — the stone thesis: it has no access to beings, and *because* it cannot have access it cannot even be deprived of world. — (p.177 / GA pp.263 / pdf 100) — fcm-09, fcm-10, fcm-14

**Wirbel** — *turbulence* — philosophy as the opposite of all comfort and assurance, into which man is spun. — (p.19 / GA pp.28–29 / pdf 22) — fcm-01

### Z

**Zeitlichkeit / sich zeitigen** — *temporality / to temporalize* — boredom springs from a determinate way our own temporality temporalizes itself; temporality grounds the subjectivity of subjects. — (p.127 / GA pp.191–92 / pdf 76) — fcm-05, fcm-06, fcm-08

**Zeitvertreib** — *passing the time* — literally a "driving-away of time"; our immediate everyday relation to boredom — a driving-away of boredom that drives time on. — (p.94 / GA pp.141–42 / pdf 59) — fcm-04

**Zumutung** — *demand / imposition* — the "almost insane demand" that we let boredom be awake; later, the most extreme demand (*die äußerste Zumutung*) that Dasein be shouldered as one's own. — (p.79 / GA pp.118–19 / pdf 51) — fcm-04, fcm-07, fcm-08

**Zwang** — *compulsion / compelling force* — the overpowering compulsion of profound boredom, related to Dasein's innermost freedom (glossary headword). — (p.376 / GA pp.~542 / pdf 200) — fcm-06, fcm-14

**Zweideutigkeit** — *ambiguity* — a positive characteristic of the essence of metaphysics, not a whim or fault of philosophy. — (p.11 / GA pp.15–17 / pdf 17) — fcm-01
