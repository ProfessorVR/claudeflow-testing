# The King–Salvo Bridge — Reading VR/Physiological Boredom through FCM

*An interpretive instrument that applies Heidegger's structural analysis of boredom (FCM Part One + the Part Two hinge) to the empirical record of the King–Salvo VR-clinical-immersion studies. Written **register-neutral** — substrate for either a dissertation chapter or a follow-up empirical paper.*

> **Provenance discipline (read first).** Four layers are kept strictly separate:
> • **FAITHFUL-HEIDEGGER** — claims of FCM, triple-locus cited.
> • **FAITHFUL-EMPIRICAL (FE)** — findings of the King–Salvo papers, **as published**, with reliability flags. These remain accurate reports of the papers even where a later reanalysis revises them.
> • **FAITHFUL-EMPIRICAL–UNPUBLISHED (FE-U)** — findings of the **O-9 reprocessing** of the same raw dataset at full N=8 (2026-07-16), and of the unpublished per-video self-report record. Not what the papers claim; not a projected mapping; a re-derivation from the same raw streams at larger N. Where FE and FE-U disagree, **both are reported and the disagreement is dated** — see §4.
> • **PROJECTED** — every mapping between Heidegger and the empirical layers. These are *neither* Heidegger's claim *nor* the authors' claim; they are this bridge's hypotheses, tagged `provenance: anticipatory-application`. No projected claim is represented as established.
>
> **KEYSTONE REVISED 2026-07-29.** This bridge made a falsifiable prediction, it was tested at N=8, and it came back partly refuted. The superseded reading is preserved and marked throughout rather than overwritten; §4 carries the revision and its reasoning. The layer assignment ruled by the author on 2026-07-29 — **physiology = occupied surface, retrospective self-report = hollow depth** — is stated at §4 and governs §§1, 3, 7 and 8.

## 1. The problem the bridge solves

Across three studies the team accumulated an anomaly its own instrument cannot represent. The "clinical immersion" stimulus — a 360° spinal-deformation surgery — produces, *simultaneously*, readings that a single boredom scale cannot hold together.

**As the anomaly stood on the published record [FE]:** (i) EEG that **resembles the boring control** (2023); (ii) gaze-variance **statistically distinct from boring and resembling the engaging video**, p = 0.0004 (2024); (iii) **high self-reported engagement** (interest 8/8 for two subjects; "engaged throughout"); and (iv) **dilated felt time** — all three subjects judged the 17-minute clip longer, S1 by 25–30 minutes (2023).

**As it stands after the O-9 reprocessing at N=8 [FE-U, 2026-07-16]:** item (i) does not survive — the EEG joins the *engaged* side, and every processed physiological surface (DMN, parietal alpha, gaze variance, cognitive load, pupil) reads the clinical stimulus as engaged — **while the per-video self-report rates it 6.0/9, between boring's 7.0 and interesting's 4.5**, and felt time dilates in 6/8. The anomaly is therefore not weakened but relocated: it now runs between the body and the subject's own account of the episode, and it is *sharper*, because the two sides no longer share an instrument class.

The argument the anomaly funds is untouched, and strengthened. A bipolar **engaged ↔ bored** scale (the Fahlman MSBS the team uses) forces these into one number and so *must* discard one of them. The unpublished record shows a subject refusing that axis from inside a single instrument: asked boredom and engagement as two separate items, **S07 returned boredom-6 *and* engagement-7 on the clinical stimulus** (and 7/6 on the interesting one). FCM supplies a structure in which all of it is jointly consistent.

## 2. The empirical record (FAITHFUL-EMPIRICAL, published — plus the FE-U reprocessing)

**Physiological pilot** (King, Feeney, Tang, Das, Salvo, ASEE 2023, #37129; N = 3). Three 17-min videos: boring (1989 MS-Word tutorial), exciting (alien-reproduction-vehicle video), clinical (360° surgery). Boredom defined (Fahlman) as "the aversive experience of having an unfulfilled desire to be engaged in satisfying activity"; DMN/alpha-theta as marker. **Findings:** clinical EEG ≈ boring control [*suggestive*: only S1's EEG clean; S2 artifacts, S3 impedance]; engagement high [clean self-report]; time dilation in all three [clean]; HR/HRV inconclusive.

**Eye-tracking study** (King, Lo, Das, Salvo, ASEE 2024, #44685; N = 12 + 4 webcam). Same videos, reordered interesting→clinical→boring because boring-first "increased levels of exhaustion." **Findings:** gaze-variance lower for engaging vs boring in 8/12; pupil dilation greater for engaging in 6/12; **ANOVA gaze-variance clinical vs boring p = 0.0004, clinical resembling engaging** [significant]; clinical "in between," corroborated by self-report. **★ The team's own two-arousal distinction:** *high-arousal boredom* — the bored viewer "look[s] around the screen trying to find something to attend to" → **high gaze-variance**; *low-arousal boredom* — a "**zombie stare**," **low gaze-variance**, in S1 & S2 only.

**The reprocessed record (FE-U; O-9, 2026-07-16; N = 8, S01–S08).** The same raw streams the 2023 pilot drew on, reprocessed for all eight subjects using the two papers' *own* published methods (EEG montage F3/F4/P3/P4, 1–35 Hz, DMN marker = alpha 8–12 + theta 4–8; gaze = variance of the 5-point-median Euclidean deviation from centre). Within-subject z-scores: **Boring** is the outlier on every surface (DMN +0.48, parietal α +0.70, gaze variance +0.21, cognitive load −0.19, pupil −0.69), with **Clinical** (−0.24 / −0.32 / −0.20 / +0.33 / +0.40) and Interesting sitting together on the engaged side. **P1's "clinical EEG ≈ boring" does not replicate**: clinical's DMN is *low*, statistically indistinguishable from interesting, and the boring-like reading holds in 4/8 subjects — chance. **Statistics:** only **pupil dilation** separates the conditions at N=8 (Friedman p=0.030; Boring < Clinical in all 8, Wilcoxon p=0.008 Holm, rank-biserial −1.0). All other surfaces are directional only (DMN p=0.20, parietal α p=0.22, gaze variance p=0.61, cognitive load p=0.61, HR p=0.20, HRV p=0.61). **Cite the convergence plus pupil; never per-channel significance.** *Caveats:* ~60% EEG epoch retention and low SNR; S07 missing the Interesting stimulus; S08 HR dropout. Full note: `tmp/Dissertation/Part_III/reanalysis/boredom-o9-physiological-findings.md`.

**Phenomenological evaluation** (King & Salvo, *Biomedical Engineering Education* 2024, 4:381–397; N ≈ 22/20). Four formats of the same OR footage rated for educational benefit and presence. **Finding of interest:** a **presence-vs-preference split** — ~70% felt greater *presence* in VR, yet ~40% judged plain 2D the *most educationally beneficial* (citing convenience, physical discomfort). *Caveat:* no time-perception probe, no boredom operationalization; its negative-affect channel is frustration/"hassle," so its boredom relevance is **indirect**.

## 3. The structural mapping (PROJECTED)

FCM's three forms are not three intensities of one variable; they are three *structures*, distinguished by (a) whether a determinate culprit is present, (b) whether surface occupation and depth-emptiness diverge, and (c) what happens to passing-the-time. Mapped onto the team's signatures:

| FCM form (faithful) | phenomenal signature (faithful) | empirical correlate (faithful-empirical) | mapping status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First — bored *by*** (§§19–23, pp.78–105 / pdf 51–64) | restless *Zeitvertreib*; we scan, fidget, clock-watch for something to occupy us | **high-arousal boredom**: high gaze-variance; "looking around … to find something to attend to" | PROJECTED |
| **Second — bored *with*** (§§24–28, pp.106–131 / pdf 65–77) | outwardly occupied, even denying boredom; emptiness rises *from us*, recognized only afterward; time drags anyway | **clinical**: every physiological surface reads engaged **yet** the post-video self-report rates it 6.0/9 and felt time dilates in 6/8 [FE-U] — and, within one instrument, S07's boredom-6 ∧ engagement-7 | PROJECTED (keystone; revised 2026-07-29) |
| **Third — profound** (§§29–36, pp.132–159 / pdf 78–91) | *Zeitvertreib* falls away; stillness; beings withdraw | **low-arousal "zombie stare"**: collapsed gaze-variance (S1, S2) | PROJECTED |

Three observations make the mapping more than a loose analogy:

**3a.** The 2024 description of high-arousal boredom — *looking around the screen to find something to attend to* — is **not merely like** *Zeitvertreib*; it is *Zeitvertreib operationalized*. Heidegger's first-form subject paces the platform and checks the watch (§23b, p.96 / pdf 60) for exactly the reason the bored viewer scans the screen: to drive off an emptiness by manufacturing occupation. Gaze-variance is a candidate **physiological signature of passing-the-time**.

**3b.** The "zombie stare" matches the third form's *phenomenology* with precision — the ceasing of search, the stillness, the collapse of the will-to-occupy — while (see §6) stopping short of its *metaphysical* claim.

**3c.** The second form is the only one in which **surface and depth come apart** (§§24–25: we are engaged all evening yet were bored; the emptiness is self-formed, §25b p.116 / pdf 70). That structural fact is the bridge's payload, developed next.

## 4. Keystone — the surface/depth divergence *is* the second form (PROJECTED; REVISED 2026-07-29)

> **Superseded reading, preserved.** Until 2026-07-29 this section read the keystone as a *cross-instrument contradiction*: **2023 EEG says the clinical video is boring-like; 2024 gaze says it is engaging-like**, with gaze mapped to the surface and **DMN/alpha-theta mapped to the depth**, and self-report caught between. The O-9 reprocessing at N=8 withdrew its factual basis — the EEG reading came from P1's single clean subject and does not replicate — and, in doing so, exposed a second and prior error in the mapping itself. Both are corrected below. The superseded text stands here because a bridge that made a testable claim and recorded the result is worth more than one that reads as though it was always right.

**The correction runs deeper than the datum.** The old mapping made **DMN/alpha-theta a proxy for the underlying attunement** — a physiological index standing in for the depth. `fcm-05` warns against exactly this move: depth "can be read off only from the specific constitution of boredom itself," and Heidegger's own inversion at §26 cautions against inferring engagement or its absence from any single arousal index or time-relation. The bridge had assigned a *body* signal to the depth channel, which the primary-text unit had already ruled out. The reprocessing did not break the keystone; it removed an error the source material had flagged in advance.

**The layer assignment (author-ruled 2026-07-29).** FCM's second form is the condition in which **outward comportment stays occupied while an emptiness rises from Dasein itself and is recognized only afterward** — at the dinner party we are "totally involved," chatting, in high spirits; we *deny* being bored, and only retrospectively is it granted (`fcm-05` §§24–28; C12, §27). Heidegger's surface is therefore *outward comportment*, and his depth is *the subject's own later recognition of the emptiness*. Map the channels accordingly:

- **The physiology as a whole** = the *surface*. Gaze on task, pupil dilated, cognitive load up, DMN low, no restless searching — outward comportment, now read across five instruments rather than one. In the second form this is precisely what stays occupied. ✔ matches the N=8 record on the clinical stimulus.
- **The post-video self-report** = the *depth*. Answered *after* the episode, it is structurally the retrospective recognition Heidegger describes, and it rates the clinical stimulus 6.0/9 — the emptiness granted once the occupation ends. ✔ matches the FE-U record.
- **Felt duration** = the temporal correlate of the same structure, not a third party. Dilation in 6/8 on clinical is the felt form of *standing time* under engagement (`fcm-05` C7–C9), which is why it can rise *with* occupation rather than against it.

`fcm-05`'s sharpest sentence licenses the whole reassignment: **behavioural engagement is not evidence against boredom; in this form it is the very medium of it.** An engaged body over a retrospectively empty episode is not a contradiction between instruments — it is the second form's signature, rendered in channels.

Two consequences follow, and both strengthen the claim.

**(a) The divergence is unidirectional.** Scored per subject on the clinical stimulus, 4/8 diverge in the keystone direction (physiology engaged, self-report bored), 2/8 are concordant-engaged, 2/8 concordant-bored, and **0/8 show the reverse** — nobody reads physiologically bored while reporting engagement. That asymmetry is what the structure predicts: if occupied comportment is the medium of this boredom, the mirror case has no mechanism. It is a stronger result than the cohort mean, which by itself would permit either direction.

**(b) It survives the obvious objection.** The `(Med)` stratum — three medical students, the group with prior clinical exposure — rates the clinical stimulus at **exactly the same boredom mean (6.00)** as the non-medical five, with the same felt-duration dilation and the same physiological direction; pupil separation holds 3/3 and 5/5. The divergence is not an artifact of clinical naivety.

So the record does not disagree with itself about *how bored* the clinical stimulus is; the body and the retrospective account **report different structural layers of a single second-form episode.** The method that falls out is unchanged and now better founded: *the second form is detectable only as the **disagreement of a surface channel and a depth channel** — never from either alone.* It was the one claim this bridge most wanted tested. It has now been tested at N=8: the divergence holds, the layer assignment was wrong and is corrected, and the channel that was supposed to carry the depth turned out to belong to the surface.

## 5. The phenomenological analog (PROJECTED, weak)

The presence-vs-preference split — high felt *presence* in VR but low judged *benefit* — is a second-form-shaped pattern in a different register: the medium **occupies** the viewer (immersion, presence) while the **educational depth stays hollow** (judged less beneficial than plain 2D). It is offered only as a *converging analogy*: this paper has no time-perception or boredom measure, so it cannot bear evidential weight. It supplies the missing **first-person leg** (lived report) beside the depth channel (EEG) and surface channel (gaze) — useful for triangulation, not proof.

## 6. A category caution (honesty)

Heidegger's **profound boredom** is a *Grundstimmung* that discloses **beings as a whole** and opens the metaphysical questions (§39, p.169 / pdf 96). A 17-minute Microsoft-Word tutorial does **not** induce that. The bridge therefore borrows FCM's **structural grammar** — the three-form architecture, the surface/depth divergence, *Zeitvertreib*, the temporal stretch — as a *heuristic for classifying experiential episodes*, **not** the claim that subjects underwent epochal metaphysical disclosure. The first/second-form distinction transfers cleanly (it is about *structure*, not cosmic scope); "third form" transfers as *phenomenology* (stillness, ceased search) under an explicit analogy, not as metaphysics. Stated plainly so the dissertation/paper can cite the limit rather than trip over it.

## 7. What to measure next (PROJECTED — experimental redesign)

A redesign that the FCM grammar motivates, expressed in the team's own instrument terms:

1. **Instrument the *divergence*, not the level.** *(Redefined 2026-07-29 with the layers of §4.)* The old formula — (gaze-engagement) − (DMN/alpha depth) — is void: both terms are physiological, so it now differences two measures of the same layer. The index the corrected keystone requires is **(composite physiological engagement) − (retrospective self-reported engagement)**, where the first term pools the surfaces that read comportment (pupil first, since it is the only one significant at N=8, then cognitive load, gaze variance, DMN, parietal alpha, each within-subject z-scored) and the second is the subject's own post-episode rating. The second-form prediction: clinical-immersion content maximizes this index even where each channel alone is ambiguous. Because the divergence is unidirectional (§4a), the index should be read **signed, not absolute** — a large negative value means something other than the second form and should be flagged rather than pooled. *This operationalizes §4.*
2. **Keep the felt-duration probe** (it exists in the unpublished instrument and earned its place: dilation in 16/24 episodes across *all* stimulus types, which is what shows the laboratory situation itself to be a holding apparatus).

2b. **Add an in-episode probe** (periodic "how long has this felt?" or retrospective ratio). Time dilation is the cleanest signal already in the data and the most direct measure of *Hingehaltenheit* / *Langeweile* (§32–33, pp.144–152 / pdf 84–88). It discriminates second/third form from mere disengagement.
3. **Replace the bipolar scale with a three-state classifier.** Targets: *first* = high-arousal search (high gaze-variance + intact time-sense); *second* = divergent-occupied (surface≈engaged, depth≈empty, time dilated); *third* = low-arousal withdrawn ("zombie stare," collapsed variance). The planned **Fisher's LDA** is the natural vehicle — fit it to these *three structured classes*, not to one boredom axis.
4. **Treat gaze-variance as the *Zeitvertreib* channel.** High variance = active passing-the-time (first form); collapse = its cessation (third). Validate against a behavioural search measure.
5. **Expect nothing for the *Augenblick*.** The moment of vision is what the entranced horizon *excludes* (§32b, p.148 / pdf 86); it can register, at most, as the *absence* of a signal. Don't design a detector for it; design for its negative space.
6. **Model the carryover.** The 2024 order-effect (boring-first → exhaustion) is data: boredom depletes. Add a between-condition washout and measure after-effects — an open problem FCM leaves untouched (Manual §E4).

**Lean-on / hold-back (revised 2026-07-29).** Lean on: the **N=8 multi-surface convergence** and **pupil dilation** specifically (the only surface significant at that N — Boring < Clinical in all 8, p=0.008); the full 24-episode self-report record including felt duration; **S07's within-instrument divergence**, which stages the two-channel rule inside one instrument and needs no cross-study seam at all; the unidirectionality result; the P2 gaze data at N=12; the high/low-arousal distinction. Hold at corroborating weight: the EEG — **not** because it is N=1, which is no longer true, but because at N=8 it is directional and not significant (DMN p=0.20) with ~60% epoch retention. Analogy only: the phenomenological presence/preference split; the third-form mapping.

The bridge no longer needs the EEG-discount fallback it used to carry, and the reason is worth stating: the old formulation held the keystone hostage to the noisiest instrument in the study, and its fallback (self-report time-dilation vs gaze-engagement) was a *repair*. The corrected keystone runs between instrument classes that do not share a failure mode — the body on one side, the subject's own retrospective account on the other — and it is corroborated inside a single instrument by S07. Nothing in it now depends on the EEG at all.
