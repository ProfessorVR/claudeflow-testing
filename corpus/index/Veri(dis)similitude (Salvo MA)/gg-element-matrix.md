## What the matrix shows (for the RODA reading)
1. **Spatial+kinesthetic cornerstone (R4) is literal in VR** — room-scale 1:1 (S17/M03) makes the cornerstone *embodied* (A04 = literal R2), which a 2D screen game cannot supply. This is the differential's load-bearing element.
2. **Two recalcitrance registers** — the design is *mostly* low-recalcitrance/inviting (it serves objects on demand → "occupied" more than "dwelt"), with one sharp recalcitrant *thing*: the unserviceable rock (S10/M12). G&G dwells the player less through resistance than through **withholding** (M10) — phantasia-conscription, not recalcitrance, is its main pressure.
3. **The exchange is asynchronous reciprocity** (M05): player *places*, goblin *retrieves on a programmed delay* — which is the cleanest in-artifact evidence for the **superficial-agency / hidden-Agent (programmer)** reading (M11) and disambiguates "compulsion" (the goblin's response is *programmed necessity*, not *bia* and not *technē*-capture).
4. **The keystone (M07/E5) is a *productive* rupture, not *bia*** — curiosity-sourced ringing → miniaturization with **no fore-structure** → Angst (F08) that *amplifies* rather than shatters incorporation, then carries over (D05). It is the **temporal-apex (R6)** of the whole episode.
5. **The shared field is a collective, not a dyad** (A06/S16) — the two-goblin tavern makes Burke **consubstantiation/identification with a group** available, strengthening the Shared-channel reading the thesis under-developed.
6. **The candle opener (E0/M02/F01)** is a self-contained discrete pass — a clean *interface-tone* re-signification — and the ideal **second worked example** alongside the miniaturization capstone.
