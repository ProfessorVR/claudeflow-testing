1. **Axe-chop of the fruit NOT captured.** Only an axe-like object *inside* a window (@05:14) and a fallen sphere on the ground (@06:57). Thesis p.32 narrates the goblin "sharpens his axe … chops it from the branch."

### Resolution (author-confirmed; footage corroborates — no re-extraction needed)
1. **Axe-chop — fruit detaches 06:52.8, lands 06:53.5** (6 fps verification, `chop_001–144`). The goblin is perched on the branch beside the fruit from ~06:50 (chop_061); the fruit comes free at chop_090→091 (06:52.8–06:53.0), falls, and lands on the moss at the tree base by 06:53.5 (chop_094); the player reaches for it at 07:01.8 (chop_144). NB: the axe / sharpening / swing is **not visually resolvable** in the stills (goblin + tool are small/distant) — the chop is confirmed by the fruit's **detachment**, not by a legible swing. ✓
2. **Fruit hand-over — 07:13 (place) → 07:21.7 (goblin retrieves)** (6 fps verification, `hand_001–144`). The player carries the fruit (the "peach/orange sphere," = the fallen key fruit) to the goblin in the clearing (goblin visible from 07:11.2 / hand_046) and **sets it on the ground in front of the goblin at 07:13.3–07:13.8** (hand_055→058); the goblin waits, then **bends and picks it up at 07:21.7** (hand_111), clutches it to its chest, and **carries it off** up the path (07:22–07:24). **Two refinements of thesis p.36:** (a) it is a **place-on-the-ground → goblin-retrieves** exchange, **not** a hand-to-hand pass; (b) the player does **not** visibly kneel. The bell is **not** an immediate exchange-gift — it appears separately on the moss ~07:39 (f460), ~15 s later. ✓ *(Analytic payoff: the goblin's acceptance is a delayed programmed trigger, sharpening the "superficial agency / compulsive motion" reading; the "helping tone" is set by the offering gesture, not bodily diminishment.)*
3. **Multiple goblins (not one).** Confirmed — most evident in the **tavern after transformation, where two goblins appear simultaneously** (a goblin draped over the table @08:51 / f534 + a goblin standing behind the mug-table @08:52 / f537). The thesis's singular "the goblin" should become at least two individuals — this strengthens the Burke/Calleja **shared-field + identification/consubstantiation** reading (a *collective*, not a one-on-one dyad).
4. **Scale non-monotonic (08:08–08:44):** the bell functions as a **scale-toggling teleporter** (ring → shrink and/or relocate), making the thesis's "teleportation tone" (p.38) literal — not a one-way shrink.
