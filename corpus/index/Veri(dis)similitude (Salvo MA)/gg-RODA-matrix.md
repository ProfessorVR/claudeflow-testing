Diagnostic: her *orekton* (curiosity) **coincides** with the designer's anticipated end (wonder) — smooth design — yet the transformation **exceeds** any fore-structure she has (B-P13) — the designed rupture.

### Step 2 — Diagnose the union
- **World-disclosedness (R1–R7):** R1 ✓ · R2 ✓ (**literal** embodiment, room-scale) · R3 ✓ (joined-yet-distinct) · R4 ✓ (spatial+kinesthetic, 1:1) · R5 ✓ (spa+kin+aff+nar co-active at climax) · **R6 ✓ — this beat *is* the temporal-apex** of the E1–E4 arc · **R7 — the rupture itself**: the *dissimilar* shock is a **designed incorporation-rupture that re-incorporates her at a new scale** rather than expelling her (the porosity that becomes the carry-over channel).
- **Co-disclosedness (consubstantiation):** weak at the trigger (individual curiosity). Post-rupture the bell becomes a **teleporter**: ringing it carries her among the locations of the tiny world (the tavern one of several), where goblins are at home in their own lives and, at some locations, **look at her but never interact**. The rupture is thus the **hinge from individual world-disclosedness to the *threshold* of co-disclosedness** — the co-world opened and the mutual look — not to a consummated collective "WE" (see §III).
- **Recalcitrance:** *low* (the world yields the transformation on demand). G&G dwells the player by **withholding**, not resisting — its main pressure is phantasia-conscription, not recalcitrance.
- **Verdict:** **incorporation achieved → ruptured → re-achieved at a new register** (the *productive* rupture, C-P33: it *amplifies* rather than shatters absorption).
- **Does the union source the act? →** at the trigger, **NO** (individual curiosity). But the post-rupture re-grounding (K04) is what **sources** the subsequent collective acts. **The keystone is precisely where a non-union-sourced act *produces the conditions* for union-sourced action.**

### Step 3 — Motive-rhetoric (Burke)

- **Attribution:** she attributes the transformation to *the bell/world* ("the bell did it").
- **Concealed term:** her own sedimented *hexis* (the disposition to ring intriguing objects) **and** the **programmer (hidden Agent, M11)**.
- **Attitude register:** **explorer / ironic-wonder** (childish delight → awe).
- **Social cluster:** **mystery** (the withheld bell-power; the goblins' world as mystery) → **courtship** (being *invited in*).

### Step 4 — Cause & type diagnosis
- **7-causes:** the *ringing* is an **agent/voluntary** act; the *miniaturization* is, from the player's side, **programmed necessity** — **but NOT *bia***. **Critical disambiguation [resolved — Part I §A₄ final / methodology v6]:** unlike a forced cutscene (true *bia*, which drags the agent to the patient-pole), here she is **transformed but not de-actualized** — she remains an agent (can move, look, ring again), and the result is not *contrary to her desire or reason*. The thesis's loose "compulsion" (the goblin's programmed motion, C-P22) is a **different** phenomenon (NPC scripting), not the player's.
- **Type:** the ring = **Type 1 Epithymetic** (curiosity/desire-sourced) with a **prohairetic** tinge (a chosen exploratory means).
- **Voluntariness (bia test):** archē internal; not contrary to desire/reason → **voluntary**. **Felt-vs-structural:** agency over *whether* to ring (free) but **not** over *what ringing does* (foreclosed) — freedom relocated to the act, not its consequence (cf. the bar-fight finding "freedom in the *how*, not the *whether*").
- **Hexis-mode:** **technē-hexis** (fluent ringing) cultivated; and the transformation seeds an **ethical/ontological hexis** (a re-calibrated sense of scale, intimacy, and one's place in a world) — the seed of the carry-over.

### Step 5 — Phantasmic & affective specifics
- **Phantasia-conscription (central here):** the no-cues design (M10) withholds what the bell does, so her *phantasma* of "ringing" is under-determined; she authors the act from her own doxa ("bells make noise"), and the world then **overwrites** her phantasma with the miniaturization — a designed *exceeding* of the conscripted image (B-P13, no fore-structure). *(This is the in-artifact mechanism of* veri(dis)similitude*: verisimilar bell → dissimilar result.)*
- **Pathetic loop:** the childish-delight Stimmung (F06) biases the A1 valence toward "safe/inviting," so the shock lands as **wonder**, not threat.
- **Agent→patient inversion: minimal** — the vertigo/stumble (F08; th p.5) is a momentary *paschein* (being-affected) but she **re-stabilizes as agent**. (Contrast genuine *bia*, where the inversion is sustained.)
- **What does NOT fire:** no *thymos*, no social/collective motive at the trigger — those fire only *post*-rupture (the tavern).

### Step 6 — Diachronic & carry-over
- **Hexis sedimented:** a **technē-hexis** (bell as scale/teleport tool) + an incipient **ethical/ontological hexis** (exterior-curio → invited-dweller, K04).
- **Stimmung/doxai for the next pass:** wonder + the doxa "this world can re-make my very scale/place" → **primes the collective tavern incorporation**.
- **(Ontological scale) virtual→actual carry-over + supplanting verdict:** the beat the thesis targets. The miniaturization **unconceals** (B-P42) the contingency of the player's ordinary scale/orientation; with the **AWS reverse-case** (D05/A-P29: ~15-min recalibration on return after 4 hrs), the evidence is that the sedimented virtual orientation **carries over and can partially supplant** the prior dispositional ground on exit. **Supplanting verdict: partial but real** — a transient ontological recalibration; the strongest available datum for RODA's A4→A0′ at the ontological scale.

### Step 7 — Diagnosis
> Within a forest she has come to *dwell* in, the player rings a verisimilar bell out of individual curiosity (a voluntary, epithymetic-prohairetic **Type 1** act whose freedom lies in the *whether*, not the foreclosed *what-it-does*); because the design **withholds** the bell's consequence, her under-determined *phantasma* is **overwritten** by a miniaturization for which she has no fore-structure — a **designed, productive rupture** (not *bia*: she is transformed, not de-actualized) that registers affectively as **Angst/wonder** (vertigo re-stabilized as agency) and, read through Burke, runs on a **Scene–Act ratio** that conceals both her own ringing-hexis and the programmer-Agent. The rupture is the **temporal-apex (R6)** of her incorporation and its **hinge from individual world-disclosedness to the threshold of co-disclosedness**: it re-grounds her exterior-gaze into a tiny guest-among-the-goblins, sediments a *technē*-hexis (the bell-as-tool) and an incipient **ethical/ontological hexis** (a re-calibrated sense of scale and belonging), and — corroborated by the AWS reverse-case — **partially supplants her prior dispositional ground**, carrying over into objective reality. This is *veri(dis)similitude* enacted: a *verisimilar* world entered through fluent dwelling, ruptured by a *dissimilar* possibility, the rupture itself **unconcealing** the ordinarily-transparent constitution of her Being-in-the-world. **[The keystone of Part II.]**
