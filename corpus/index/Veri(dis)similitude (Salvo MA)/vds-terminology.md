# VDS — Terminology / Coinages Glossary

**Source:** Salvo, *Veri(dis)similitude* (MA Thesis, 67 pp.). **Generated:** 2026-06-11 (Phase 1). **RODA-mapping column updated:** 2026-07-01 → **CANONICAL-METHODOLOGY-v9** (was v5-era).
**Purpose:** Every term the thesis coins, adapts, or leans on — with its nearest RODA/methodology correlate — so the re-grounding can decide what vocabulary survives, gets renamed, or maps onto the A₀→A₄ chain. Definitions paraphrased.
**Types:** coinage (author-original) · adapted (modifies a source term) · borrowed (used as-is).

**v9 alignment note (what changed in the mapping column, 2026-07-01):** the union's two poles are now **world-disclosedness** (*Welterschlossenheit*, BT 145) = *incorporation* / Calleja and **co-disclosedness** (*Miterschlossenheit*, BT 160) = *consubstantiation* / Burke — Heidegger's own terms, replacing the loose "faces"/"co-disclosure" language; **compulsion is reserved for the player-side *bia* (force) and *technē*-capture (hexis-runaway)** and never for an NPC; **prohairesis** is the deliberative completion of the bouletic (Type 3), **not a Type 4**; **reality-ethos folds into *veri(dis)similitude***; the kind-distinction **firewall** (experiential convergence ≠ kind-identity) and **genus = virtual-object / *phantasma* = originary virtual object** now govern; **recalcitrance** (Rickert thing/object) and **dwelling** are first-class; the retired stem "phantasm*atic*" must not appear. **Rhetorical actancy + actant (Gries/Latour) are KEPT as-is** (user, 2026-07-01) — the relational ontology grounding "rhetorical ecologies," **not** folded into Burke.

## Reconciliation status (was "priorities"; updated 2026-07-01 to v9)

4. **VDS-T06 functional tone** — **SPLIT** across A₁ *resonant epithymia* / A₂ *phantasma*-charge / A₃ *doxa*-attunement (not 1:1).
5. **VDS-T05 reality ethos** — **FOLD into *veri(dis)similitude***.
6. **VDS-T17 thrownness → A₀ dwelling-horizon**; the two poles are now **world-/co-disclosedness** (Heidegger's own terms, BT 145/160).
7. **VDS-T16 rhetorical actancy (+ T15 actant) — KEEP AS-IS (crucial; user 2026-07-01).** The Gries/Latour relational ontology is the ground of "rhetorical ecologies," distinct from Burke's identification (the social/co-disclosedness pole); do **not** consolidate it into the Burke module.
