## Reconciliation priorities (most urgent first)
1. **VDS-T27 "compulsive/compulsion"** — loosest usage (an NPC's scripting); must be renamed/disambiguated from *bia* and *technē*-capture. Cleanest fix: Burke pentad (hidden Agent = programmer) + "scripted/programmed responsiveness."
2. **VDS-T19 "fallenness"** — carries Heidegger's inauthenticity valence; if RODA treats incorporation neutrally, the "fallen = inauthentic capture" framing needs explicit reconciliation (is incorporation a *fall* or an *achievement*, or both?).
3. **VDS-T02 / VDS-T12** — two registers (Heideggerian / presence-research) for ONE state; collapse or relate explicitly when mapping to Calleja incorporation / A4.
4. **VDS-T06 functional tone is overloaded** — spans A1-residue, A2-phantasma charge, and A3-doxa/mood valence by context; split on re-ground rather than map 1:1.
