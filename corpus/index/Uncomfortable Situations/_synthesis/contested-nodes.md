# Contested Nodes — Gross, *Uncomfortable Situations* (2017)

**Phase 3C synthesis** — concepts whose surface uniformity across the volume masks a meaningful inflection or internal tension between units. Each entry pairs **surface convergence** (where Gross's deployment looks uniform) with the **underlying tension** that emerges when the deployments are cross-read. Evidence anchors are taken from the unit JSONs at the page numbers indicated.

---

## 1. *Situated conceptualization* (Barsalou-Barrett heritage)

**Surface convergence.** Across all six units Gross treats Wilson-Mendenhall et al. 2011 / Barsalou as the cognitive-science alliance the book's rhetorical-ontology project most needs. The Intro's *first footnote* names it (US-00 PDF 11); the Epilogue's closing methodological wager re-affirms it as the "approach that invites a humanist contribution" (US-05).

**Underlying tension.** What "situated" *means* shifts. In Intro and Austen it functions as a programmatic ally — emotions are concept-instantiations whose situational specification maps cleanly onto rhetorical analysis (US-00 PDF 11, 19; US-04 C-SITUATED-CONCEPTUALIZATION). In the Epilogue, by contrast, Gross **identifies Barsalou's own internal limit** — Barsalou must rely on "elliptical figures ('and so forth', 'and many others')" to articulate a non-conceptual background receding indefinitely into the affordances of the social environment (US-05 "Barsalou's elliptical-figures limit"). Situated conceptualization is *both* the volume's bridgehead and a position Gross diagnoses as insufficient at its own boundary.

**Implication.** The volume's signature alliance with cognitive science is conditional: Gross accepts situated conceptualization where it provides analytic traction, but the Epilogue records its underspecification of the very rhetorical-affordance dimension the body chapters require.

## 2. *Affordance* (Gibsonian vs. rhetorical)

**Surface convergence.** Gibson's *Ecological Approach to Visual Perception* (1979) is invoked as foundational across Intro, Radcliffe, Equiano, and Epilogue. The Intro forecasts Gibson as a "humanistic field-guide to threatening/promising environments" (US-00 PDF 32). Radcliffe makes Gibson the chapter spine (US-03 PDF 87). Equiano operationalizes affordance as "race-based slavery as environment" (US-02). The Epilogue reanchors Phelps's data via Griffiths-Scarantino 'Emotions in the Wild' (US-05).

**Underlying tension.** The Intro promises Gibsonian invariance will be "abandoned" in Ch.3 (US-00 PDF 32). Ch.3 honours this and constructs the **cop-car-roof affordance** (US-03 PDF 104-105) — historically fixed, dated to Mario Savio Oct 1 1964 (US-03 T-USR-01). But this is in tension with Gross's *defense* of "Gibsonian monism" later in the same chapter (US-03 PDF 107). Affordance is simultaneously: (a) invariant ecological offering (orthodox Gibson, retained), (b) historically-fixed rhetorical offering, (c) bifurcated into *cultural* vs. *social* registers (US-03 PDF 105). The Equiano chapter pushes a fourth register — the *negative* affordance of an environment from which the agent is *constitutively excluded*.

**Implication.** "Affordance" does four different jobs across three chapters. The contestedness is internal to Gross's positive product: the "ecological-rhetorical theory" actually requires the orthodox Gibsonian level to do work that Gross's rhetorical extension explicitly undercuts.

## 3. *Rhetorical situation* (Bitzer 1968)

**Surface convergence.** Bitzer is cited in the Intro (note 36, PDF 30) as exigence + audience + constraints, with Gross recasting exigence as "basic discomfort." Radcliffe rehabilitates Bitzer as proto-affordance-theory (US-03 PDF 103).

**Underlying tension.** In the Intro Bitzer is a *Tier-2* citation, weighted as minor relative to Berlant's *Cruel Optimism* situation (Tier-1, US-00 PDF 12). In Radcliffe he is *elevated* and treated as the chapter's classical anchor for rhetorical affordance. In the Intro he is something to be *supplemented*; in Radcliffe he is to be *rescued from neglect*.

**Implication.** Gross's relationship to the canonical American rhetorical-situation tradition is itself situated — instrumentalized differently chapter by chapter, never finally adjudicated.

## 4. *Mixed feelings* (Galenic disequilibrium vs. negotiated circumstance)

**Surface convergence.** "Mixed feelings" is the Ch.4 titular concept and is forecast in the Intro as "socially-negotiated convergence of conflicting alternatives on character" against the Galenic-humoural disequilibrium model (US-00 PDF 35; US-04 C-MIXED-FEELINGS, C-NEGOTIATED-CIRCUMSTANCES, C-GALEN-DISEQUILIBRIUM).

**Underlying tension.** The Austen chapter itself houses an internal split that the Intro forecast does not register. Against the Galen tradition (US-04 T-GALEN-DISEQUILIBRIUM-VS-NEGOTIATED-CIRCUMSTANCES), Gross also stages **a second tension** within his own framing: between Dorothea Frede's reading of Aristotle's *Rhetoric* on mixed feelings as "Phileban remedial / homeostatic" (US-04 n.5) and Gross's discovery that Aristotle in fact uses "mixture" vocabulary nowhere at all (US-04 T-PHILEBAN-FREDE-ARISTOTLE). So "mixed feelings" is contested *twice over*: once against Galen, once against a particular Aristotelian reading that Gross's own Intro (US-00 PDF 35; index 167n16) had cautiously retained. The Austen chapter exposes a quiet revision of the Intro's Aristotelian alliance.

**Implication.** The Intro's "Aristotelian rhetoric of emotion" inheritance is not unbroken background but a site of intra-volume revision once Ch.4 actually does the textual work. This is the clearest case of a body chapter modifying a position the Intro had stated more confidently.

## 5. *Aristotelian rhetoric of emotion* (background vs. qualified retention)

**Surface convergence.** Aristotle is named as continuous background across the Intro (catharsis, p.10), Darwin (hatred passage as Rhetoric II cast-of-characters specification, US-01 PDF 51 / E 234; on hyperbole p.47), Equiano (rhetorical = effective + positing-a-social-relationship), Radcliffe (Cydias tribunal-of-conscience case, US-03 PDF 103), and Austen (Austen-as-Aristotelian via Ryle/MacIntyre, US-04 C-AUSTEN-AS-ARISTOTELIAN).

**Underlying tension.** Across the body chapters Aristotle does *different theoretical work* than the Intro's catalog suggests. In Darwin he supplies a *cast-of-characters analytic* — emotion as constitutively relational, with named injurer / injured / expectations of injury — that Ekman uses to *expel* love/hatred from "emotions" while Gross uses the same criterion to *constitute* them (US-01 T-US01-03). In Austen he is **explicitly qualified**: Gross departs at n.16 from Ryle-MacIntyre's "Austenian happiness is Aristotelian + Christian" reading and argues that Austenian happiness is in fact a critique of utilitarian/calculative systematicity (US-04 T-AUSTEN-ARISTOTLE-VS-UTILITARIAN-CRITIC). In Radcliffe Aristotle's *prelinguistic* perceptual situation (the Cydias tribunal-of-conscience as language-constituted threat) is directly opposed to Gibson's prelinguistic perception (US-03 T-USR-07).

**Implication.** "Aristotle" in this volume is not one inheritance but a family of partial uses; the qualified-retention at Austen n.16 marks the strongest single retraction.

## 6. *Bearing up* (Ch.2 titular — conscious endurance vs. structural posture)

**Surface convergence.** The Intro forecasts "bearing up" as Equiano's disposition under slavery — "how Equiano can write he is 'happily situated' as a slave" (US-00 PDF 28). The Equiano chapter executes this as the chapter's titular concept (US-02 "bearing up").

**Underlying tension.** The Intro implies a quasi-volitional reading — Equiano *as* bearing up under conditions. The Equiano chapter itself, however, runs *bearing up* through the OED morphological cluster (bear / born / bore / 'bear up'; US-02 "bear / born / bore OED cluster") in which the bearer is *excluded from the social-recognitional structure* — Anerkennung foreclosed (US-02 "Anerkennung foreclosed") — and where Christ does the bearing because Equiano "cannot bear himself" (US-02 T-Gross-vs-Wollstonecraft-on-conversion). So "bearing up" shifts between (a) Intro's situated-rhetorical posture and (b) Ch.2's prosthetic / non-volitional / structurally foreclosed posture in which the verb *bear* names what Equiano *cannot* finally do for himself.

**Implication.** The Intro reads "bearing up" as agential rhetorical positioning; Ch.2 reveals the concept's load-bearing irony — it names the place where agency runs out. This is a stronger reading than the Intro's volitional gloss admits.

## 7. *Empathy* (humanities sense vs. Preston's operationalization)

**Surface convergence.** "Empathy" appears as a continuous concern across Intro (Lipps's *Einfühlung* / projective emotion p.20), Equiano (compassion / sympathy through distancing mechanisms), Darwin (Sterne peasants-passage; Adam Smith "feeling what we should feel in a like situation"), and Epilogue (Preston's empathy-apathy-reticence triad).

**Underlying tension.** In the body chapters empathy is a *humanities-side* category — Lipps's feeling-into, Adam Smith's sympathy, Berlant's compassion. In the Epilogue Preston operationalizes empathy via fMRI / hospital-patient video / mirror-neuron debates, and the *humanities sense fractures* into three terms (empathy / apathy / reticence) once the operational object is specified (US-05 "empathy / apathy / reticence triad"). The Dean / Preston disanalogy on reticence (US-05 TENS-reticence-most-vs-least-sympathy) makes the contestedness explicit: in the Preston-lab hospital study reticent patients receive the *least* sympathy; in Dean's Holocaust-survivor archive reticence is the marker of *maximal* credibility. Same word, opposite valences across the two-cultures divide.

**Implication.** "Empathy" is the test case for the Epilogue's qualified-No answer to Snow: the term can be jointly studied across disciplines, but only if both sides accept that the construct is not transparent across cultural-historical situations.

## 8. *Fear* (Phelps amygdala vs. Barsalou situated vs. Radcliffe Gothic)

**Surface convergence.** Fear is invoked in the Intro (Hobbes's political economy of fear-and-vainglory in mode 7), Radcliffe (Gothic terror / "real terror is fictional"), Austen (astonishment-as-reflexive-fear-of-bad-news), and Epilogue (Phelps amygdala study, Wilson-Mendenhall fear-of-runner-lost-on-trail vs. fear-of-presentation).

**Underlying tension.** Three *incompatible* fear-objects circulate. (a) Phelps treats fear as amygdala-correlate-of-startle-response in fMRI (US-05 "Phelps Experiment 1"). (b) Barsalou treats fear as situated-conceptualization-instantiation that differs between trail-runner and presentation contexts (US-05 "situated conceptualization (Barsalou)"). (c) Radcliffe treats *terror* as "the stuff of uncertainties and rhetorical form-of-address" — programmatically *fictional* (US-00 PDF 34; US-03 "supernatural-as-spectral"). The Epilogue argues for a rhetorical-situational reframing that includes all three but does not collapse them; the Intro had already declared (PDF 34) that "real terror is fictional."

**Implication.** The volume's strongest single claim about a "basic emotion" is that *fear* is the contested terrain where rhetorical analysis is most needed and where the inter-disciplinary stakes are highest. The Epilogue's Phelps case is therefore the inverse pole of the Radcliffe chapter's Gothic case — and both must be read together to see Gross's *fear*-concept whole.

## 9. *Comfort / discomfort* (bourgeois keyword vs. eighteenth-century theological)

**Surface convergence.** The Intro establishes *comfort* as Moretti's bourgeois keyword (US-00 PDF 25); the titular *Uncomfortable Situations* depends on the comfort/discomfort dialectic Burke's "literature as equipment for living" enables (US-00 PDF 26).

**Underlying tension.** The Equiano chapter introduces an eighteenth-century *theological* sense (what God can give you; Wallin *Eternal Mansion* 1769; US-02 "comfort (eighteenth-century sense)"), used to *ironize* the Intro's bourgeois reading: when Equiano writes he was "comfortable" the word is in a theological register Moretti's thesis does not capture. The Equiano chapter's amending-Ahmed move (US-02 T-amending-Ahmed — Ahmed presupposes a social contract Equiano is excluded from) pushes the same critique from a queer-phenomenological angle.

**Implication.** "Comfort" is contested across three registers — bourgeois (Moretti), theological (Wallin), queer-phenomenological (Ahmed amended) — and the volume's titular *Uncomfortable* draws unevenly from all three.

## 10. *Cognitive Approaches to Literature* (CAL) / *Theory of Mind* critique

**Surface convergence.** Darwin and Austen both attack CAL (Zunshine, Vermeule, Crane, Thrailkill, Keen) for importing Ekman/Damasio infrastructure into literary criticism (US-01 C-US01-07; US-04 C-THEORY-OF-MIND-CRITIQUE). Radcliffe treats Boyd-Zunshine evocriticism as the chapter's foil (US-03).

**Underlying tension.** The *grounds* of the critique shift. In Darwin the objection is methodological — CAL's foundational model is "impoverished Darwinianism" (US-01 C-US01-17). In Austen the objection is textual — Austen's "calculated withholding of omniscient narration on Edward" *blocks* the mind-reading CAL needs (US-04 T-MIND-READING-VS-WITHHELD-OMNISCIENCE). In Radcliffe the objection is logical — affirming-the-consequent and undistributed-middle fallacies (US-03). Three chapters, three different kinds of argument under one banner.

**Implication.** Gross's anti-CAL position is a triangulated critique whose unity is rhetorical-strategic rather than logically singular. The Austen chapter's textual argument is the strongest because CAL's typical reply (better calibration) cannot answer what Austen *withholds*.
