# Book-Level Ontology: Gross, *Uncomfortable Situations* (2017)

**Phase 3A synthesis** | **2026-05-10** | 36 canonical concepts (11 Tier-1, 17 Tier-2, 8 Tier-3) + 18 canonical positions

This document distills the ~198 concepts surfaced across the six Phase-2 unit JSONs (Introduction + four chapters + co-authored Epilogue) into a deduplicated canonical ontology. The driving question of the volume is what kind of *thing* an emotion is, and the driving answer — operative in every unit — is that emotion is constitutively rhetorical-situated: neither (a) a biologically natural kind invariant across history nor (b) a pure cultural performance decoupled from biology, but a negotiated transaction among organism, environmental affordances, and the discursive frames available to it. The book's title-term names the load-bearing category, an *uncomfortable situation* being precisely the predicament in which one's habituated emotional repertoire fails to fit, exposing the rhetorical-constructive layer to view.

## The eleven Tier-1 (volume-pillar) concepts

The volume's spine consists of eleven concepts that recur across at least three units and carry the methodological weight of Gross's project.

**US-C-001 *uncomfortable situation*** is the titular load-bearing category, present in every unit. Its sharpest crystallization arrives in the Darwin chapter (PDF p.59, book p.49) as the formula "cognition practically situated but uncomfortably so," with "second-order discomfort" recruiting the reader into the higher-order rhetorical phenomenon. The Intro forecasts the term; each chapter instantiates it (Equiano's exclusion-as-environment, Radcliffe's hostile affordances, Austen's reference-value negotiation, Preston-Phelps's neuroscience-meets-history).

**US-C-002 *rhetorical ontology of emotion*** is Gross's methodological signature, presupposed but not re-derived from *The Secret History of Emotion* (2006). Across the book, rhetoric is not "ancillary to knowledge in the disciplines, the mere linguistic form into which content is poured" (Austen chapter) but constitutive of what emotion *is*: simultaneously effective (operating on audience regardless of authorial intention) and relational (positing a social relationship — *pathos* in the classical lexicon).

**US-C-003 *situated conceptualization*** (Barsalou-Barrett, Wilson-Mendenhall et al. 2011 *Neuropsychologia*) is the cognitive-science paradigm Gross treats as the volume's most important scientific ally. It opens the book (dinner-party scenario, first footnote) and closes it (Epilogue's invitational framework). Emotion concepts are instantiated in context-dependent, audience-inflected scenarios rather than retrieved from amodal taxonomies; the framework is methodologically compatible with humanities-side rhetorical analysis, which is the volume's central wager.

**US-C-004 *negative / hostile / rhetorical affordance*** is Gross's signature Gibsonian extension. By abandoning Gibson's invariance criterion, Gross opens affordance theory to historically-conditioned hostile environments and culture-specific rhetorical situations. The Mario Savio cop-car-roof case (PDF 104-105) is the chapter's canonical illustration: an environmental object whose rhetorical-affordance ("harangue on me") is constituted by language and historical situation, not direct perception.

**US-C-005 *hostile environment*** is the related Ch.3 phenomenological-and-legal category — an environment whose affordances are constitutively negative-but-not-merely-dangerous (kidnap risk for the freed Black; the Marquis de Montalt's "honorable asylum"). It overlaps with race-based-slavery-as-environment (Ch.2) and reaches the law via "hostile workplace" doctrine for protected classes.

**US-C-007 *seven-mode rhetoric-of-emotion typology*** (mimetic, cathartic, projective, prosthetic, performative, voluntary, systematic) is established at PDF pp.20-21 / book pp.10-11 and absorbs the four-mode reading of *Imitation of Christ* (mimetic / prosthetic / performative / systematic) at p.14. It replaces the hardwired/constructed binary and supplies the analytic toolkit deployed across chapters (mimetic in Darwin; prosthetic in Equiano; projective and systematic in Radcliffe; voluntary and systematic in Austen). *Prosthetic emotion* (US-C-006) is kept distinct at Tier 2 because it receives chapter-length development in Equiano (bearing-the-cross-for-Jesus completes a rhetorical-emotional disposition the slave could not muster on his own).

**US-C-008 *bearing up*** is Ch.2 titular: Equiano's rhetorical-emotional disposition carrying / sustaining / enduring (OED *bear* I-II + 21) under conditions in which the bearer is excluded from social-recognitional structures. It is neither stoic endurance nor pragmatic adjustment nor Hegelian acquiescence alone, but a structure of "carrying-what-one-was-ready-to-curse" that, at the limit, is borne by Christ ("bears the world and all things up," Toplady).

**US-C-009 *mixed feelings (anti-Galenic)*** is Ch.4 titular: not internal proportional mixtures of stable basic emotions (Galenic humours; Ekman blending algorithms) but socially-negotiated convergences of conflicting reference values converging on character. Austen's *Sense and Sensibility* (drafted 1790s as "Elinor and Marianne" just after Bentham) prefigures Kahneman-Tversky 1979 prospect theory.

**US-C-010 *irreconcilable difference / two cultures*** is the Epilogue's titular framework, after C. P. Snow's 1959 Rede Lecture. The volume answers Snow's question with a qualified *No* — and not as a third response between incommensurability (Scarantino-Griffiths) and consilience (E. O. Wilson, Pinker) but as a DISSOLUTION of the problem when emotion is studied as a rhetorical-situational phenomenon.

**US-C-011 *comfort (as bourgeois keyword)*** anchors Gross's "critical history of well-being" against Chandler's "archaeology of sympathy." Following Moretti's *The Bourgeois*, comfort shifts in the late 17th / early 18th c. from a return-to-normality after adversity to taking normality as starting point and pursuing well-being as an end in itself (Robinson Crusoe 1719 as transitional case). The eighteenth-century OED sense — comfort as what *God* gives you, creature-comfort overrated — gives Equiano its lexical pre-history.

**US-C-012 *negation as rhetorical-emotional engine*** is the cross-chapter operator. Sterne's "fear not, envied not" (*Sentimental Journey* 120) opens the trope at the Darwin chapter's close; Austen's "no unhappy reverie" (*Emma* 321) and "Not no feelings" (*S&S*) carry it forward; Radcliffe's "undone" (via Lewis's Antonia) gives it Gothic form via Bartlett's "affectability of nonreferring terms." Negation flouts Grice's Maxim of Manner to flag stereotype-violating situations.

## The seventeen Tier-2 (chapter-central) concepts

These concepts organize one or two chapters' analytic work without recurring across the whole volume (including prosthetic emotion, US-C-006, discussed above):

**US-C-013 *rhetorical situation (Bitzer, extended)*** — Bitzer 1968 recast with exigence as "basic discomfort"; rehabilitated in Ch.3 as proto-affordance theory; bridges to Burke and Aristotle *Rhetoric* II.

**US-C-014 *basic emotions / Ekman program*** — the principal scientific antagonist; glossed as "impoverished Darwinianism" that strips Darwin's interpretive openness.

**US-C-015 *complex states of mind (Darwin)*** — *Expression* p.260 evidence-base of Ch.1: envy, jealousy, suspicion, devotion, maternal love (p.82) — emotions not revealed by fixed expression, forcing literary-humanistic methodology.

**US-C-016 *Cognitive Approaches to Literature (CAL)*** — Howard, Crane, Thrailkill, Keen, Zunshine, Vermeule; proceeds "unecologically toward theory of mind."

**US-C-017 *Literary Darwinism / evocriticism*** — Boyd, Pinker, Carroll, Dutton; "cheesecake for the mind." Six red flags + Ch.1 logical-fallacy diagnosis.

**US-C-018 *creditable emotion (Williams)*** — Intro lever against Massumi's pre-ideological affect; "feeling of freedom carefully controlled."

**US-C-019 *Hegelian strand in Merleau-Ponty*** — Ch.2's interpretive claim against Dreyfus: *Phenomenology* p.84 and footnote 172n1 retain a Hegelian-Marxist line by which the social body can "go limp" like an ill body.

**US-C-020 *vested interests (Carretta)*** — distinguishes interest from felt attachment; the freed Black finds no one with vested interest in his well-being in a race-based-slavery environment.

**US-C-021 *spiritual autobiography / Mount Calvary unsealing*** — Ch.2 conversion-scene set-piece (Narrative 189-190); four-step allegory executes the point that Equiano cannot bear himself.

**US-C-022 *Lewinian Gestalt apparatus*** — Aufforderungscharakter/valence, Umstrukturierung, Aus-dem-Felde-Gehen, Umweltkrafte, "hopeless situation" (1935).

**US-C-023 *paradiastole (redescription)*** — Renaissance figure (Peacham; Skinner) identified as Radcliffe's signature trope; bridged to Austin's perlocutionary/illocutionary distinction.

**US-C-024 *Gothic sentimental subgenre*** — Walpole-Lewis-Radcliffe complex; Antonia's "undone"; Adeline-as-overlooker; Marquis-as-predator; "asylum" as ironic affordance.

**US-C-025 *Kahneman reference-value framework*** — Norm Theory + counterfactual availability + hedonic treadmill; Austen prefigures, Equiano resists; possibly / practically / predictably irrational triad.

**US-C-026 *interested onlookers / situational ambiguity*** — Ch.4 chapter-crux aligned with Barrett-Barsalou's Jim-Webb-angry-or-elated case; John Dashwood and Mrs. Jennings as "Kahnemanite norms."

## Canonical Positions (18)

Eighteen authorial claims structure the volume's argumentative arc. The first five Tier-1 positions are the methodological superstructure:

- **US-P-001** (Gross) — emotion is neither hardwired nor purely constructed; the rhetorical situation determines what 'emotion' means [units 00-05].
- **US-P-002** (Gross) — cognition is practically situated but uncomfortably so [00-03].
- **US-P-003** (Gross) — Darwin is a rhetorician; Ekman misreads the photographic illustrations [00-01].
- **US-P-004** (Gross) — Gibson's affordance theory must be extended into a rhetorical-historical-cultural theory of negative/hostile affordances [00, 03].
- **US-P-005** (Gross) — Equiano's "happily situated" posits a rhetorical-relational attitude, not a hedonic state [00, 02].

Mid-volume Tier-1 / Tier-2 positions take up specific antagonists:

- **US-P-006** (Gross, Tier-2) — Merleau-Ponty's "Freedom" chapter retains a Hegelian-Marxist strand that Dreyfus loses [02].
- **US-P-007** (Gross, Tier-1) — mixed feelings are negotiated reference values, not internal mixtures; Austen prefigures Kahneman-Tversky [00, 04].
- **US-P-008** (Gross, Tier-2) — negation is the cross-chapter rhetorical-emotional engine [00, 01, 03, 04].
- **US-P-009** (Gross, Tier-2) — CAL and Literary Darwinism share affirming-the-consequent / undistributed-middle fallacies [00, 01, 03, 04].
- **US-P-010** (Gross, Tier-2) — sentimental literature is the pivot for a "critical history of well-being," not an "archaeology of sympathy" [00, 02, 03].
- **US-P-011** (Gross, Tier-2) — dominant situated-cognition metaphors recuperate bourgeois comfort and repress unheimlich alienation [00].

The Epilogue positions (Tier-1) execute the volume's titular two-cultures wager:

- **US-P-012** (Gross, Tier-1) — racism is located primarily in INSTITUTIONS, not in physiology; Phelps's amygdala-correlate makes sense only via rhetorical-situational reframing [05].
- **US-P-013** (Gross, Tier-2) — Phelps Exp.2 "familiarity" equivocates between face-to-face acquaintance and televisual celebrity; markedness of Black vs. White celebrity contaminates the design [05].
- **US-P-014** (Preston, Tier-1) — empathy must be theorized as one term in an empathy / apathy / reticence triad; mirror-neuron account is circular [05].
- **US-P-015** (Preston, Tier-2) — eight interlocking limits (technological, monetary, journal-impact, mass-media, localization-illusion, professional-expertise, reverse-inference, technocratic-hierarchy) generate the brain-abstraction problem [05].
- **US-P-016** (Gross & Preston, Tier-1) — lab topics coincide with socio-historical concerns at experiment-design margins: fear↔post-war anti-racism; empathy↔post-war humanitarianism [05].
- **US-P-017** (Gross & Preston, Tier-1) — methodological wager: situated cognition invites two-cultures collaboration; Snow's irreconcilable differences DISSOLVE under methodological diversity [00, 05].
- **US-P-018** (Gross, Tier-2) — Ryle and MacIntyre are right that Austen speaks of happiness as an Aristotelian, but MacIntyre is wrong that Austenian happiness is Aristotelian + Christian; Austen is a critic of systematic-theological happiness [04].

## Merge logic and deliberate non-merges

Three merge patterns apply. (i) **Typological subdivisions** collapse into their parent: all seven modes fold into US-C-007 (prosthetic alone elevated to US-C-006 for its chapter-length deployment). (ii) **Technical-vocabulary clusters** collapse: Lewinian Gestalt terms into US-C-022; Kahneman-prospect-theory facets into US-C-025; Phelps-study facets into US-C-027; empathy/Preston facets into US-C-028; Afro-Pessimism bridges into US-C-032; negation operators into US-C-012; Ahmed terms into US-C-030. (iii) **Antagonist-program merges** keep CAL (US-C-016), evocriticism (US-C-017), and Ekman (US-C-014) distinct, since Gross's polemic engages them at different doctrinal layers (humanities reception vs. evolutionary-psychology branch vs. scientific source).

Three deliberate non-merges: (1) *hostile environment* (US-C-005) vs. *negative affordance* (US-C-004) — chapter-titular phenomenological-and-legal category vs. theoretical mechanism. (2) *Hegelian strand in Merleau-Ponty* (US-C-019) vs. *vested interests* (US-C-020) — philosophical interpretive claim against Dreyfus vs. biographical-analytic formula after Carretta. (3) *Spiritual autobiography / Mount Calvary unsealing* (US-C-021) vs. *prosthetic emotion* (US-C-006) — the former carries genre-form and textual locus information lost if folded into the abstract mode.

The 36-concept ontology covers the methodological pillars (uncomfortable situation, rhetorical ontology, situated conceptualization, negative affordance, hostile environment, seven-mode typology, bearing up, mixed feelings, irreconcilable difference, comfort, negation), the antagonist programs (Ekman, CAL, Literary Darwinism), the disciplinary bridges (Kahneman, Hegelian Merleau-Ponty, queer phenomenology, Griffiths-Scarantino, Aristotelian heritage), and the Epilogue test-objects and claims (Phelps, empathy/apathy/reticence, reverse inference, racism-in-institutions, lab-topics-coincide-with-history). All eleven Tier-1 concepts appear in three or more units; the three claimant types (Gross / Preston / Gross & Preston) are all represented among the eighteen positions.
