# Terminology Appendix — VR Pedagogy Secondary (Part III)

85 lemmas drawn from the cluster's 34 units, alphabetized. Each entry: **term** — gloss — `ped-sec-NN` source unit(s).
Instrument/scale names are marked (INSTRUMENT); theoretical models (MODEL). Full 270-term raw inventory (including
minor variants) lives in each unit's own `.json` `concepts[]` array.

- **Affective path** — CAMIL's/Makransky & Lilleholt's route from presence through interest/motivation/self-efficacy to learning outcomes. `ped-sec-06, ped-sec-09, ped-sec-11`
- **Automatic-interaction assumption** — Kreijns et al.'s named CSCL design pitfall: assuming social interaction occurs simply because the environment permits it. `ped-sec-30`
- **Avatar** — a user's graphical self-representation within a virtual environment. `ped-sec-04, ped-sec-21`
- **CASA (Computers Are Social Actors)** — the theory that people apply social rules to media/computers themselves. `ped-sec-01`
- **CAVE (Cave Automatic Virtual Environment)** — a room-scale, projection-based immersive VR system, used in ped-sec-33's studies. `ped-sec-33`
- **Coherence principle** — Mayer's multimedia-learning design principle: exclude extraneous material. `ped-sec-08, ped-sec-13, ped-sec-33`
- **Cognitive load theory (CLT)** (MODEL) — Sweller's framework distinguishing intrinsic, extraneous, and germane processing demand. `ped-sec-10, ped-sec-33`
- **Cognitive path** — the route from presence/agency through extraneous cognitive load to (negative) learning outcomes. `ped-sec-06, ped-sec-09, ped-sec-11`
- **Cognitive presence** — the Community of Inquiry's construct for meaning-construction through sustained reflection/discourse. `ped-sec-26`
- **Cognitive theory of multimedia learning (CTML)** (MODEL) — Mayer's dual-channel, limited-capacity model of multimedia learning. `ped-sec-07, ped-sec-10`
- **Cognitive-only restriction** — Kreijns et al.'s named CSCL design pitfall: designing solely for task/cognitive processes, neglecting the social-affective dimension. `ped-sec-30`
- **Common-method variance (CMV)** — inflation risk when subjective mediator and outcome measures share an administration method. `ped-sec-11`
- **Community of Inquiry (CoI)** (MODEL) — Garrison, Anderson & Archer's three-presence (cognitive/social/teaching) framework for online educational transactions. `ped-sec-26, ped-sec-31`
- **Cone of Learning (Dale's Cone)** — a popular (and often-critiqued) hierarchy of learning-retention-by-modality. `ped-sec-22`
- **Co-presence / salience of others** — a sub-factor of social presence: the felt salience of other participants in a mediated space. `ped-sec-04, ped-sec-31`
- **Culturing (of social presence)** — Gunawardena & Zittle's finding that social presence can be actively fostered within a medium, not just fixed by it. `ped-sec-25, ped-sec-28`
- **Cybersickness** — HMD-induced nausea/disorientation/eye strain from sensory-conflict during VR use. `ped-sec-01, ped-sec-23`
- **Deliberate practice** — structured, repeated, feedback-driven skill practice; a Strand-D professional-education design principle. `ped-sec-18, ped-sec-20`
- **Design elements framework** — Radianti et al.'s fourteen-category coding scheme for IVR application design features. `ped-sec-22`
- **Design for learning** — Fowler's pedagogy-first (vs. technology-first) approach to VR-learning design. `ped-sec-05, ped-sec-33`
- **Desirable difficulty** — the idea that added task friction (e.g., VR motor cost) can, within limits, support learning. `ped-sec-14`
- **Distraction hypothesis** — the account that IVR's sensory richness competes for attention with learning content. `ped-sec-13`
- **Equivalence hypothesis** — the claim that a learning intervention's effect is stable across deployment settings when medium is held constant. `ped-sec-12`
- **Essential/extraneous/generative processing** — the three-way partition of cognitive load in generative-learning-adapted CTML. `ped-sec-07, ped-sec-10, ped-sec-13`
- **Expertise-reversal effect** — the finding that instructional supports helpful for novices can hinder more knowledgeable learners. `ped-sec-12`
- **Extraneous processing / seductive details** — task-irrelevant cognitive load induced by high-fidelity or entertaining-but-irrelevant stimuli. `ped-sec-07, ped-sec-08, ped-sec-10, ped-sec-13`
- **Five affordances (VRCL)** — VanderMeer et al.'s named set of VR-collaborative-learning affordances. `ped-sec-34`
- **Flow** (Csikszentmihalyi) — sustained absorptive engagement in an activity matched to skill/challenge level. `ped-sec-04`
- **Generative learning theory** — the select-organize-integrate model of active learning processing. `ped-sec-07, ped-sec-08`
- **GRADE** — a standardized quality-of-evidence rating system used in systematic reviews. `ped-sec-19`
- **Gunawardena Social Presence Scale (SPRES)** (INSTRUMENT) — Gunawardena & Zittle's Likert scale operationalizing social presence for CMC, with intimacy/immediacy/affective-association/co-presence sub-factors. `ped-sec-25`
- **Hedges g** — a standardized-mean-difference effect-size statistic, bias-corrected for small samples. `ped-sec-15`
- **Immediacy** — psychological/communicative closeness conveyed through verbal and nonverbal cues; a SPRES sub-factor. `ped-sec-25, ped-sec-34`
- **Immersive Tendencies Questionnaire (ITQ)** (INSTRUMENT) — Witmer & Singer's companion instrument measuring individual differences in tendency to become immersed. `ped-sec-02`
- **Intimacy** — interpersonal closeness/warmth conveyed in mediated communication; a SPRES sub-factor. `ped-sec-25`
- **Inverted-U / Yerkes-Dodson challenge** — Fowler's caution that increasing technological affordance may help learning only up to a point, then hurt it. `ped-sec-05`
- **JBI critical appraisal checklists** — Joanna Briggs Institute standardized tools for assessing study quality in systematic reviews. `ped-sec-23`
- **Kirkpatrick classification** — a four-level (reaction/learning/behavior/results) framework for evaluating training outcomes. `ped-sec-15`
- **Liking is not learning** — Makransky et al.'s own summary phrase for the presence/enjoyment-vs-outcome dissociation. `ped-sec-10`
- **MERSQI** — the Medical Education Research Study Quality Instrument, used to grade simulation-education study quality. `ped-sec-15`
- **Media richness theory** (MODEL) — the communication-theory account that a medium's bandwidth/cue-richness determines its capacity to convey interpersonal presence; explicitly rejected by Garrison, Anderson & Archer. `ped-sec-01, ped-sec-26, ped-sec-30`
- **Media-vs-method confound (Clark)** — Clark's (1983) argument that instructional method, not delivery medium, drives learning outcomes. `ped-sec-04, ped-sec-06, ped-sec-07, ped-sec-12, ped-sec-24`
- **Mediated vs. non-mediated presence** — the distinction between presence experienced through a communication medium and unmediated (face-to-face) presence. `ped-sec-31`
- **Modality principle** — Mayer's design principle: present words as narration rather than on-screen text when paired with graphics. `ped-sec-07, ped-sec-10`
- **Multi-user virtual environment (MUVE)** — a virtual environment supporting concurrent multiple users. `ped-sec-21`
- **Nature and Determinants of Presence Model (NDPM)** (MODEL) — Chow's SEM-derived model of presence's psychological determinants. `ped-sec-03`
- **Novelty effect** — the risk that early enthusiasm for a new technology inflates short-term outcome measures. `ped-sec-13`
- **Perceptual illusion of nonmediation** — Lombard & Ditton's core definition of presence: a failure to perceive/acknowledge the existence of the medium. `ped-sec-01`
- **Personalization principle** — Mayer's design principle: conversational rather than formal tone improves multimedia learning. `ped-sec-07`
- **Practical inquiry model** — the process-model component underlying the Community of Inquiry's cognitive presence (triggering event → exploration → integration → resolution). `ped-sec-26`
- **Pre-training principle** — Mayer's design principle: teach names/characteristics of key components before the main lesson. `ped-sec-07, ped-sec-33`
- **PRISMA** — the standardized reporting framework for systematic reviews and meta-analyses. `ped-sec-15, ped-sec-23, ped-sec-34`
- **Promise/pitfall framework** — Mayer et al.'s own organizing structure for weighing IVR's benefits against its risks. `ped-sec-07`
- **Real vs. perceived affordance (Norman)** — the distinction between an environment's actual capability and a user's perceived capability. `ped-sec-24`
- **Redundancy principle** — Mayer's design principle: avoid presenting identical information in both narration and on-screen text. `ped-sec-10, ped-sec-33`
- **Reification (Winn & Jackson)** — making an abstract concept perceptible/tangible in a virtual environment. `ped-sec-04`
- **Representational fidelity** — the degree to which a virtual environment perceptually resembles its real referent; one of Dalgarno & Lee's core affordances. `ped-sec-04, ped-sec-09, ped-sec-16`
- **Rich-get-richer effect** — Gunawardena's finding that already socially expressive communicators show compensatory socio-emotional expression online. `ped-sec-25`
- **SEM (structural equation modeling) / mediation** — a statistical technique for testing hypothesized causal path structures among latent constructs. `ped-sec-03, ped-sec-09, ped-sec-11, ped-sec-16`
- **Seductive details** — engaging but task-irrelevant content/stimuli that impose extraneous cognitive load. `ped-sec-10, ped-sec-13`
- **Segmenting principle** — Mayer's design principle: break content into learner-paced segments. `ped-sec-08, ped-sec-33`
- **Simulator Sickness Questionnaire (SSQ)** (INSTRUMENT) — the standard cybersickness self-report instrument, originally designed for flight-simulator contexts; its fitness for HMD-VR is contested by Cossio et al. `ped-sec-02, ped-sec-23`
- **Social information-processing perspective** — Walther's account that CMC users adapt to channel limitations over time rather than being permanently constrained by them. `ped-sec-25`
- **Social loafing** — reduced individual effort in group settings, a named risk for VR-collaborative learning. `ped-sec-33`
- **Social presence** — the sense of others as meaningfully present/salient in a mediated communication; this cluster's Strand-F-grounding construct. `ped-sec-25, ped-sec-26, ped-sec-28, ped-sec-29, ped-sec-31, ped-sec-32`
- **Social presence dial (Bob Lucky)** — a metaphor, cited by Biocca et al., for treating social presence as a continuously adjustable rather than binary property of a medium. `ped-sec-29`
- **Social presence theory (1976)** (MODEL) — Short, Williams & Christie's origin theory; the shared ancestor of every Strand-F unit. `ped-sec-01` (as cited/external — not itself an in-cluster source)
- **Teacher immediacy** — instructor behaviors that reduce psychological distance from students; linked to satisfaction in CMC contexts. `ped-sec-28`
- **Teaching presence** — the Community of Inquiry's construct for instructional design, facilitation, and direct instruction. `ped-sec-26`
- **Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)** (MODEL) — Davis's model of perceived usefulness and ease of use as predictors of technology adoption/use. `ped-sec-03, ped-sec-16`
- **Three-stage skill acquisition** — the cognitive/associative/autonomous stage model of procedural skill learning, relevant to Strand D. `ped-sec-18`
- **Trim-and-fill / Orwin's Failsafe N** — publication-bias diagnostic techniques used in meta-analysis. `ped-sec-15, ped-sec-32`
- **Value-added research design** — a study design manipulating instructional features *within* a medium rather than comparing across media. `ped-sec-06, ped-sec-07, ped-sec-08`
- **Vection** — the illusory sensation of self-motion induced by visual stimuli, relevant to cybersickness. `ped-sec-01`
- **Virtual patient (VP)** — an interactive, screen-mediated simulated-patient scenario used in health-professions education. `ped-sec-19`
- **VR features (fidelity + control)** — the composite latent construct combining representational fidelity and interactivity/immediacy of control in several Strand C SEM models. `ped-sec-09, ped-sec-11, ped-sec-16`
- **Willingness to suspend disbelief** — a media-user variable (not a system property) contributing to presence, per Lombard & Ditton. `ped-sec-01`
- **Yerkes-Dodson** — see *Inverted-U*. `ped-sec-05`
- **Zero-history groups** — newly formed groups with no prior shared interaction history, relevant to CSCL social-interaction pitfalls. `ped-sec-30`
