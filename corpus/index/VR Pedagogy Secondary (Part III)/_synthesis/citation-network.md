# Citation Network — VR Pedagogy Secondary (Part III)

Intra-cluster citations: who cites whom within this cluster's own 34 units. External citation hubs (authors/
theories cited by multiple in-cluster units but not themselves an in-cluster source) live in the companion file
`vr-pedagogy-secondary-literature-network.md`.

## Intra-cluster citation network

65 `cites-cluster-author` edges (well above the `max(10, N/4)=8.5` floor; corrected 2026-07-16 — one prior
edge, ped-sec-09→ped-sec-16, was a mis-linkage: ped-sec-09's "Merchant et al. 2014" is the external 2014
meta-analysis, not ped-sec-16 [Merchant 2012], so it is now an external `cites` edge, per
citation-corrections-2026-07-16.md). Densest hub structure:

**Makransky research program (the cluster's single densest citation cluster).** CAMIL (ped-sec-06) cites eight
other in-cluster units as its own empirical grounding: ped-sec-02, ped-sec-04, ped-sec-08, ped-sec-09, ped-sec-10,
ped-sec-11, ped-sec-13, ped-sec-22. In turn, ped-sec-09 (Makransky & Lilleholt) cites ped-sec-02, ped-sec-04,
ped-sec-06, ped-sec-10 (its "Merchant 2014" cite is the external meta-analysis, not the in-cluster
ped-sec-16); ped-sec-11 (Makransky & Petersen SEM) cites ped-sec-01, ped-sec-02, ped-sec-04,
ped-sec-08, ped-sec-09, ped-sec-10; ped-sec-12 (equivalence study) cites ped-sec-09, ped-sec-10, ped-sec-11,
ped-sec-17. This single research program accounts for roughly a third of all intra-cluster citation traffic.

**Mayer research program.** ped-sec-07 (Promise and Pitfalls) cites ped-sec-02, ped-sec-08, ped-sec-10, ped-sec-12,
ped-sec-13. ped-sec-13 (Parong & Mayer cognitive-affective) cites ped-sec-08 and ped-sec-09.

**Strand F's internal citation network.** ped-sec-32 (Richardson et al. meta-analysis, the strand's capstone) cites
ped-sec-25, ped-sec-26, ped-sec-27, ped-sec-28 — nearly the entire earlier CMC-tradition core. ped-sec-30 (Kreijns
et al.) cites ped-sec-25, ped-sec-26, ped-sec-27. ped-sec-27 (Tu) cites ped-sec-25 and ped-sec-26. ped-sec-31
(Terry & Doolittle) cites ped-sec-01, ped-sec-02, ped-sec-26 — the strand's only unit citing outside Strand F,
into Strand A's presence-foundations literature.

**The Strand-F/VR-bridge citation gap, confirmed at the network level.** ped-sec-33 (De Back et al.) cites
ped-sec-04, ped-sec-05, ped-sec-08, ped-sec-16 — all Strands B/C, none of Strand F. ped-sec-34 (VanderMeer et al.)
cites ped-sec-33, ped-sec-04, ped-sec-09, ped-sec-10, ped-sec-13, ped-sec-22 — again all outside Strand F. This is
the citation-network confirmation of `debate-map.md` DA-10: the two VR-bridge units cite the cluster's VR/immersive
literature richly but never its social-presence literature.

**Presence-foundations hub.** ped-sec-02 (Witmer & Singer) is cited by ped-sec-04, ped-sec-06, ped-sec-07,
ped-sec-09, ped-sec-11, ped-sec-16, ped-sec-31 — the cluster's most externally-cited single in-cluster unit (7
citing units), reflecting its role as the field's origin presence-measurement instrument.
