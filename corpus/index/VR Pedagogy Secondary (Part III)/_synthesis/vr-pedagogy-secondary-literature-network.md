**Reading.** Short, Williams & Christie's 1976 theory is, by a wide margin, this cluster's single most important
external hub — cited by every Strand-F unit and reaching into Strand A via Lombard & Ditton. Clark, Sweller, and
Mayer form a second tier grounding the methods-not-media/cognitive-load side of Strands B/C. **No external hub
bridges both halves of the cluster simultaneously** — a citation-network-level confirmation that the CMC
social-presence program and the VR-pedagogy program, while sharing distant theoretical ancestry (both descend,
loosely, from general communication/media theory), do not share contemporary citation traffic. This corroborates
`debate-map.md` DA-10 and `cluster-ontology.md` §5 from the opposite direction: not only do the cluster's own
VR-bridge units fail to cite the CMC canon, the CMC canon's own citing units never reach back into VR-specific
theory either.
