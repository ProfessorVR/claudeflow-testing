**Insertion site**: §1.5 L5 — MA-8 somatic preparation footnote (the existing footnote citing MA 8 701b33-702a3 + DA I.1 403a30-b1 on the hylomorphic pair)

**Appended text**:

> For the technical reconstruction of *MA* 7--8 as a continuous transformation-motion (the "Cardiocentric Incoming/Outgoing Motion" or CIOM model) in which the somatic preparation---thermic alteration of pneuma, consistency-alteration of flesh around the joints---is causally internal to the single transduction from peripheral sense-alteration to limb-motion (and not a discrete subsequent stage), cf.~Corcilius \& Gregoric, "Aristotle's Model of Animal Motion," *Phronesis* 58:1 (2013), pp.~52--97, esp.~pp.~73--74.

**Why this matters**:

- Provides a third-party scholarly anchor for what was previously argued from primary text alone (MA 8 701b33-702a3 + MA 7 702a17-19)
