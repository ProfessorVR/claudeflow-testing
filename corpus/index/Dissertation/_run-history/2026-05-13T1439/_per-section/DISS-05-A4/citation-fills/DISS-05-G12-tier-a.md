## 1. Claim and anchor

§1.5 claims that unreflective (Type 1 simple-appetitive) action has its structural condition in the pre-deliberative chain — *aisthēsis* → resonant *kinēsis* → *resonant epithymia* → motion-to-action — without intervening *doxa*-articulation. The interpretive support is from the *Heidegger and Rhetoric* volume (Gross & Kemmann editors).

## 3. Verbatim from corpus/index

From `phase2-u-fp3c.md` §17a (hexis as pre-reflective being-composed):

> "Hexis is *the determination of the genuineness of being-there in a moment of being-composed as to something*" (p. 119). The various *hexeis* are the various modes of being able to be composed. Praxis as the how of being-in-the-world appears as the being-context that we can also designate as *existence* (p. 119). Being-composed is not optional or indeterminate; in *hexis* lies the primary orientation toward the *kairos*: "I am there, come what may!" (p. 119).

The "I am there, come what may!" formulation is the canonical Heideggerian statement of the pre-reflective dispositional ground that licenses unreflective action.
