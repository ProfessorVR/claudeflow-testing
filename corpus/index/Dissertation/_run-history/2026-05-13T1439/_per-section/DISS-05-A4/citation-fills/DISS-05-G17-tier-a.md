## 3. Verbatim from corpus/index (Nussbaum 1985)

Nussbaum's central thesis: *phantasia* + *orexis* + *aisthēsis* form the action-explanatory triad even prior to any propositional deliberation. The *phantasia*-presented appearance is what *orexis* engages, and this engagement produces motion without requiring a deliberative-doxastic episode in every case.

Key Nussbaum positions (per `phx-01-nussbaum.md` synthesis):
- *Phantasia* "ties abstract thought to concrete perceptible objects or situations" (Nussbaum 1985, p. 265, as cited by Hawhee 2011 p. 142)
- "Deliberative *phantasia* can also involve picturing, or at least some sort of envisaging" (Nussbaum 1985, p. 264, as cited by Hawhee 2011 p. 148)
- Pre-deliberative emotional mobilization: anger, fear, and similar *pathē* arouse appropriate motion as soon as the *phantasia* presents the relevant evaluative content — no separate deliberation required
