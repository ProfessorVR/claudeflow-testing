## 3. Verbatim from corpus/index

### DA III.7, 431a8–12 (perception's pursue/avoid charge):

> "Whenever the soul as it were affirms or denies something good or bad, it pursues or avoids it. Hence the soul never thinks without an *aisthēma*; what affects feeling does it through a *phantasma*."

This is the canonical site for the *epithymia* / `resonant epithymia` pair: perception is already at-the-level-of-pursue-or-avoid (the basic affective valence layer); the *phantasma* preserves this pursue/avoid charge as residue when the object departs.
