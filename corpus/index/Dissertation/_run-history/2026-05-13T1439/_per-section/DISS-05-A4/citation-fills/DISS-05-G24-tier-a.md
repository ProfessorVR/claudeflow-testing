## 3. Verbatim from corpus/index

### DA III.3, 428a19–24 (phantasia ≠ doxa):

> "Imagination differs from any other form of cognition; it is not perception, science, nor opinion. For everyone who has an opinion believes (*pisteuei*) what he opines; but no one who imagines an object believes anything about that object; for imagination is in our power to construct whenever we wish... but opinion is not in our power."

The key structural distinction: *doxa* entails *pistis* (belief/affirmation); *phantasia* does not. *Doxa*-ratification of the *phantasma* is therefore the propositional uptake of "this is so" that the *phantasma* alone does not perform.

### SZ §32 (per BT-D1-U6 narrative, H.149):

The hermeneutic-as structure (*hermeneutische Als*) underlies the propositional-as (*apophantische Als*). The phantasma's "taking-as" operates at the pre-propositional level of *Verstehen*; *doxa*'s ratification is the apophantic moment in which the as-structure becomes propositionally explicit.
