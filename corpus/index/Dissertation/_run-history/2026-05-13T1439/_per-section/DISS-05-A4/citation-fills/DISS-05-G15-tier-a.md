## 3. Verbatim from corpus/index

### DA I.1, 403a30–b1:

> "The dialectician would define anger as a desire for retaliation, or something of the sort, while the natural scientist would define it as a boiling of the blood and warm stuff around the heart."

### Rhet. II.2, 1378a30–32 (the canonical Aristotelian definition):

> "Let anger be defined as a desire accompanied by pain (*orexis meta lypēs*), for a conspicuous revenge (*timōria phainomenē*), for a conspicuous slight (*oligōria phainomenē*) at the hands of men who have no call to slight oneself or one's own."
