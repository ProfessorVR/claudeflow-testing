## 3. Verbatim from corpus/index

### Rhet. II.12, 1389a3–10 (youth-character):

> "The character of the young: they are passionate, hot-tempered, and apt to be carried away by their impulses, especially the impulse of anger… They love both honor and victory more than money, the latter most of all because they have not yet learned what it means to be without it."

### Rhet. II.13, 1389b13–22 (age-character):

> "The character of the old, men who are past their prime, may be said to be formed for the most part of elements that are the contrary of these. They have lived many years; they have often been taken in, and often made mistakes; and life on the whole is a bad business."

### Rhet. II.14, 1390a30–b2 (prime-character):

> "The men in their prime are obviously between the two in character… they will neither trust everyone nor distrust everyone, but judge people correctly."

The character-stratified modulation is the canonical Aristotelian site for the hexis-as-character-modulating-*pathē* thesis.
