## 3. Verbatim from corpus/index

Key canonical verbatim from DA III.10:

> "So we have come to the conclusion that there are two faculties which originate movement, appetite and intellect — if one may regard *phantasia* as a kind of thinking" (DA III.10, 433a9–10).

> "Locomotion involves three factors: that which originates the movement, that by which it originates it, and that which is moved" (DA III.10, 433b13–14).

> "The originator of the movement is twofold: one is unmovable and the other is itself moved as well as a mover. The unmoved is the *prakton agathon*, the practical good; the mover and moved is the *orektikon* (for that which is moved is moved insofar as it desires)" (DA III.10, 433b15–18).
