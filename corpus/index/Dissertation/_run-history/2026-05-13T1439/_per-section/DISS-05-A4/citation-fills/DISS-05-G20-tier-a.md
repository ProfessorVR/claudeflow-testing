## 1. Claim and anchor

§1.5 claims the hexis modulates the chain at TWO points:
1. **Input-to-doxa modulation**: the hexis shapes how the basic affective valence is propositionally articulated when doxa engages the phantasma
2. **Doxa-gating modulation**: the hexis gates which doxa-contents the agent finds compelling under the conditions of the present *kairos*

This two-modulation thesis is the chapter's signature architectural articulation.

## 3. Verbatim from corpus/index

### From Rhet. II.12 (the canonical character-modulation passage):

> "The characters of men in their youth are such as I have said… they will commit excessive injustices when they injure others, but not those that involve criminality" (1389a3–4). The character-states are the durable hexeis through which the *pathē*-arousal is modulated.

### From BCAP §17b-beta (p. 125):

> "*Hexis* is nothing other than a how of *pathos*, being-out-of-composure, in relation to being-composed-as-to" (p. 125). *Hexis* is itself a basic determination of *aretē*: "according to its being-origin, *aretē* is a *hexis*, a being-composed as to..., in terms of the moment" (NE B 5, 1106a 13).
