**Pipeline**: `corpus/index/Aristotle - Complete Works/`

**Bekker locus**: NE VI.5, 1140a24–1140b30 — phronēsis distinguished from technē; phronēsis as the state "concerned with action about things that are good or bad for man" (1140b5–6); phronēsis preserves doxa orthē about what is good and bad for human beings in the changing particular situations of life.

### 2.2 Heidegger GA 18 §17–§18

**Pipeline**: `corpus/index/Heidegger - Basic Concepts of Aristotelian Philosophy/`
**Unit**: **U-FP3c** (`phase2-u-fp3c.md`, two-pass MANDATORY)

The hexis-as-existential-ontological reading at §17 (pp. 119–127) supplies the architectural ground: hexis is not a property but a *mode of being-there* — "the determination of the genuineness of being-there in a moment of being-composed as to something" (p. 119). The praxis-hexis cultivates *fresh resolution* in the sense that each new *kairos* requires the hexis to be re-actualized: "I am there, come what may!" (p. 119).

## 3. Verbatim from corpus/index

### From BCAP §17b-alpha (p. 122–124), the hexis-as-cultivated-through-undergoing-situations thesis:

> *Aretē* as *hexis* is not a property or possession brought from without; **it is a *mode of being-there itself***. The peculiar category of the "how" — how being-there is *determined by its being*, characterized by *temporality*, by the stretching across time. *Aretē* comes to be *di' ethous*, "through habit" (NE B 1, 1103a 25 sq.). **The possibilities of being-composed are graspable only by way of *undergoing* various situations involving risk.** We appropriate *hexis* not as possession but as *chresiamenoi eschomen* — "using, taking-opportunities" (NE B 1, 1103a 30 sq.). Through being with one another in dealing with human beings, we come to be steady and level-headed; through bringing ourselves into situations involving risks, we learn courage or leave cowardice behind (pp. 122–123).

### From BCAP §17b-beta (p. 125–126), the praxis-hexis doxa-gating:

> *Hexis* is itself a basic determination of *aretē*: "according to its being-origin, *aretē* is a *hexis*, a being-composed as to..., in terms of the moment" (NE B 5, 1106a 13). *Hexis* also has the further meaning of *dynamis* — the definite orientation toward the being of human beings. *Hexis* is related to the *zōē praktikē meta logou*, **the sort of being-in-the-world in which the world is encountered in the character of *sumpheron*, *blaberon*, *hēdy*, and *lyperon*** (p. 125).
