## 3. Verbatim from corpus/index

### DA I.1, 403a30–b1 (the dialectical-physiological pair):

> "The natural scientist would define [anger] as a boiling of the blood and warm stuff around the heart. The latter assigns the material conditions, the former the form or formula; for the form is the principle of the thing, but this can have no being except as it is realized in a matter belonging to it. Thus the affections of soul are at once enmattered formulas (*logoi enuloi*)."

### MA 8, 701b33–702a3 (the pneuma-mediated physiology):

> "The bodily parts are altered by being warmed and cooled. The warming and cooling are produced by the pneuma which is itself a kind of bodily part, and are caused by the *phantasmata* and *aisthēseis*."

### MA 8, 702a15–21 (the locomotion mechanism):

> "When the affection of expansion produces motion in this manner, and the affection of contraction in that manner, the result is that the animal moves and goes through alterations… the *phantasia* makes ready the affection, and the desire the alteration."
