### White on the cognitive-engagement of phantasia (`phx-02-white.md` § 4):

> "*phantasia* is 'a dimension of the life of all animal souls.' Beyond reception, the animal soul is 'a *preserver* and a *storehouse* of the sensible forms of things' (pdf 16). The lingering, resonating, echoing presence of sensible forms freed from their original matter is 'the essence of *phantasia* for Aristotle.'"
