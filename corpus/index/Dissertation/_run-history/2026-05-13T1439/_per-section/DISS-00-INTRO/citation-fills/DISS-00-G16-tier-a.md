## What (compound primary-text footnote)
> The claim that cognition itself specifies under a different *logos* draws together three Aristotelian moves: (i) the method-of-soul-inquiry-as-itself-cognitive (*De Anima* I.1, 402a4–10); (ii) the actualization-coincidence of perceiver and perceptible at *DA* II.5 (425b26–426a26), where Aristotle insists the *energeia* of the sensible is "one and the same in act" with the *energeia* of the perceiver; (iii) the higher-order awareness thesis at *DA* III.2 (425b12–25): "since it is through sense that we are aware that we are seeing or hearing, it must be either by sight that one is aware of seeing, or by some sense other than sight."
