## What (primary-text footnote)
> *Phantasia* is "not found without sensation," but it is also "not the same as sensation"; Aristotle distinguishes it both from sense and from belief on the ground of its kinetic constitution as a residual movement caused by actual perception (*De Anima* III.3, 427b14–16; cf. 428b10–429a9).

## Why
The "not-a-mixture" claim — that phantasia is not constituted by combining sense + thought but is its own kinetic structure — needs the 427b14–26 passage where Aristotle explicitly resists the mixture model.

## Verbatim (≤25 words)
"When we merely imagine something fearful or encouraging we remain unaffected, but when we *think* it fearful, emotion is immediately produced" (*DA* III.3, 427b21–24).
