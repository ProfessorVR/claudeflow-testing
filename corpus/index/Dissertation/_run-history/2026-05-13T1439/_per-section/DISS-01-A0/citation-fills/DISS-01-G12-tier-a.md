## What (framing-flag insertion + dual Heidegger anchor)
> *Framing*: The proposed identification of the dyadic potency-actualization structure of Aristotelian *kinēsis* (mover–moved, capacity–exercise) with the proto-form of Heideggerian *In-der-Welt-sein* (Dasein–world, comportment–involvement-context) is interpretive. Heidegger himself anticipates this reading in BCAP §26 (the 1924 anticipation of *Zuhandenheit*; `phase2-u-sp2a.md` line 20) and develops the Being-in-the-world structure as the worldhood-environment-circumspection complex in *Being and Time* §§14–18 (H.63–88).

## Why
The bridge from Aristotelian *kinēsis* to Heideggerian *In-der-Welt-sein* runs through Heidegger's own 1924 reading; the dissertation's identification is structurally Heideggerian rather than directly Aristotelian. Flag + dual anchor is the honest citation.
