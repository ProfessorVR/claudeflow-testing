## What (framing-flag insertion + Heidegger anchor)
> *Framing*: I follow Heidegger's 1924 reading in *Basic Concepts of Aristotelian Philosophy* (GA 18), §§25–26, which reinterprets Aristotle's κίνησις as ἐντελέχεια τοῦ δυνάμει ὄντος (*Phys.* Γ1, 201a10) and translates this not as "motion" in the Newtonian sense but as the *presence of a being in its ability-to-be precisely insofar as it is an ability-to-be* — what Heidegger throughout calls *Bewegtheit*. The identification *kinēsis* ≅ *Bewegtheit* is Heidegger's terminological choice; I take it over as a framing-level move.

## Why
The Heideggerian translation *kinēsis* → *Bewegtheit* (rather than the typical "movement"/*Bewegung*) is not Aristotelian but Heidegger's; the dissertation needs a framing-level flag at the first use to make the citation chain transparent.
