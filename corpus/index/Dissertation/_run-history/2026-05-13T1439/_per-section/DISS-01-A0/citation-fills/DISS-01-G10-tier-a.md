## What (interpretive-flag insertion + dual primary citations)
> *Interpretive*: The structural parallel between soul-as-the-actuality-of-an-organic-body (*De Anima* II.1, 412a27–b1) and time-as-the-number-of-motion-requiring-a-soul-to-count (*Physics* IV.11, 219b1–2; IV.14, 223a21–26) is drawn by the present analysis; Aristotle himself notes the soul-time connection at *Phys.* IV.14 but does not extend the actuality–motion analogy to it. I take the parallel as supported by the shared *energeia*-structure on both sides.

## Why
The parallel is interpretively load-bearing for §1.0's architectural argument, but Aristotle does not state it in so many words. Honest practice requires the substantive interpretive flag plus the two anchors (DA II.1 + Phys. IV.11+IV.14) that support the inference.

## Verbatim (≤25 words each, primary anchors)
- "The soul is the first actuality (*entelecheia*) of a natural body which has life in potentiality" (*DA* II.1, 412a27–b1).
- "Time is the number of movement in respect of the before and after" (*Phys.* IV.11, 219b1–2).
