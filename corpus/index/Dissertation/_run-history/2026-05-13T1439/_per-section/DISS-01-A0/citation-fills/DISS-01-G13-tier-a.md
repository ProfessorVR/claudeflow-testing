## What (framing-flag insertion + dual locus)
> *Framing*: The identification of Heidegger's hermeneutical circle (*Being and Time* §§32–33, H.150–160) with the iterative completion-structure of Aristotelian *energeia-ateles* / *entelecheia* (the actualization of the potential *qua* potential — incomplete actuality returning to itself; *Phys.* III.1, 201a10–11) is interpretive. Heidegger does not draw the parallel in §§32–33 explicitly; I take it as a structural homology grounded in the shared "fore-having / fore-sight / fore-conception" *projection-toward-completion* schema.

## Why
