## What (primary-text footnote)
> The three-factor schema — (i) the unmoved-mover (desired object); (ii) the moved-mover (*orektikon*); (iii) the moved (the animal) — is Aristotle's own analysis at *De Anima* III.10, 433b13–18: "These three are required: that which moves, the means by which it moves, and that which is moved" (433b13–16). Aristotle expands the schema across 433b18–25 by identifying the unmoved mover with the practical good (*to prakton agathon*) or its apparent counterpart.
