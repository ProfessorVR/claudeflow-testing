## What (compound primary-text footnote)
> Aristotle distinguishes the two higher kinds of *phantasia* at the close of *De Anima* III.10–11: "all *phantasia* is either calculative or sensitive" (*DA* III.10, 433b29); the sensitive grade "exists also in the other animals" while the calculative or deliberative grade "exists in those animals which can calculate" (*DA* III.11, 434a5–10). Aristotle adds the crucial cognitive operation that distinguishes the higher grade: "one has the ability to make ONE *phantasma* out of MANY *phantasmata*" (434a9–10, *ἓν ἐκ πλειόνων φαντασμάτων ποιεῖν*).

## Why
The sensory/deliberative distinction is the structural backbone of the section's tripartite-orientational-modes scheme. *DA* III.10 433b29 + *DA* III.11 434a5–10 are the canonical Aristotelian loci.
