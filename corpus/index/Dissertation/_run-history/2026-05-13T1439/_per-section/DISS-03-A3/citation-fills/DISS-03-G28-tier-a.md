## What (optional primary-text footnote)
> Aristotle defines *pathē* generically at *Rhetoric* II.1, 1378a20–22: "All those feelings that so change men as to affect their judgements, and that are also attended by pain or pleasure." The methodological principle at 1378a25 stipulates that "each emotion must be analysed under three heads: (a) state of mind, (b) persons toward whom felt, (c) grounds."

## Why
The "determinate *pathē*" thesis depends on Aristotle's tripartite analysis-protocol (state / persons / grounds) at *Rhet.* II.1 1378a20–22 + 1378a25. This is the generic-definition locus that grounds the chapter's treatment of articulationally-concrete *pathē*.
