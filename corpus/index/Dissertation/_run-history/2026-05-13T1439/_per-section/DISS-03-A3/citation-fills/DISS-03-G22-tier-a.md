## What (primary-text footnote)
> Aristotle introduces *phantasia bouleutikē* (deliberative imagination) at *De Anima* III.11, 434a5–10 and develops its distinguishing capacity at 434a16–21: the deliberative grade "exists in those animals which can calculate; for whether one will do this or that is already a work of calculation; and there must be a single measure, for one pursues what is greater. Hence one has the ability to make ONE *phantasma* out of MANY *phantasmata*" (434a7–10; *ἓν ἐκ πλειόνων φαντασμάτων ποιεῖν*).
