## What (optional primary-text footnote)
> "Knowledge and sensation are divided to correspond with the realities: potential to potentialities, actual to actualities... [the soul's faculties] must be either the things themselves or their forms; not the things themselves (the stone is not in the soul), but their forms" (*De Anima* III.8, 432a3–9). Because "no one can learn or understand anything in the absence of sense," the mind "when actively aware of anything is necessarily aware of it along with an image (*phantasma*); for images are like sensuous contents except that they contain no matter" (432a3–10).
