## What (parenthetical citation in dissertation house style)
> The three ecstases (*Ekstasen*) of temporality — futural (*Zukunft*), having-been (*Gewesenheit*), Present (*Gegenwart*) — are introduced at *Being and Time* §65 (H.323–331) as the unitary structure of *Zeitlichkeit*; each ecstasis is a "rapture toward" (*Entrückung zu*) a horizonal schema, and the three together constitute the existential meaning of care (*Sorge*).

## Why

## Verbatim (paraphrase from corpus/index, ≤200 words)
"Temporality is ecstatico-horizonal: ecstases are raptures toward a 'whither'; the future's horizonal schema is the 'for-the-sake-of-itself,' having-been's is 'that in the face of which' Dasein is thrown, the Present's is the 'in-order-to'; these schemata in their unity constitute the ontological condition for worldhood" (BT §69c, paraphrased at `phase2-bt-d2-u4.md` line 60).
