## What (compound secondary-source footnote; parenthetical SZ-pagination)
> Heidegger develops ecstatic temporality (*ekstatische Zeitlichkeit*) as the three-fold unity of futural, having-been, and Present at *Being and Time* §65 (H.323–331): "Temporality temporalizes itself ... in the unity of ecstatic future, having-been, and Present." The having-been ecstasis (*Gewesenheit*) is the existential meaning of state-of-mind (*Befindlichkeit*); the futural ecstasis is the meaning of understanding (*Verstehen*); the Present is the meaning of falling (*Verfallen*) — see §68a–c (H.336–349). The full historicality unfolds at §§72–77 (H.372–404), where the unity is reconstructed as Dasein's stretching-along (*Erstreckung*) between thrown beginning and projected end.
