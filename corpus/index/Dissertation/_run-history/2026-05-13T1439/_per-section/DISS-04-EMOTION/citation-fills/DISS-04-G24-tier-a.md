## What (primary-text footnote + soft interpretive flag)
> *Soft flag*: I deploy Aristotle's compressed-syllogism drinking case at *Movement of Animals* 7, 701a32–33 — "I want to drink, says appetite; this is drink, says sense or imagination or thought: straightaway I drink" — as a diagnostic for the **dimensional independence** of basic affective valence from articulationally-concrete *pathē*. Aristotle himself does not draw the dimensional-independence inference; that move is mine, supported by (i) the textual compression that elides the *pathē* layer entirely from the rapid-action case, and (ii) the absence of any *pathos*-naming in the rapid-action syllogism, which contrasts with the *pathē*-laden cases of MA 8 (701b1–702a21).

## Why
The drinking-case as diagnostic for dimensional independence is interpretively load-bearing. Soft flag + MA 7 anchor is the honest citation.

## Verbatim (≤25 words)
"For the actualizing of desire is a substitute for inquiry or thinking. I want to drink, says appetite; this is drink, says sense or imagination or thought: straightaway I drink" (*MA* 7, 701a32–33).
