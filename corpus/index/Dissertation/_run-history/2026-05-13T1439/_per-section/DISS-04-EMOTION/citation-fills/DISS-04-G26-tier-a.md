## What (secondary-source footnote + soft interpretive flag)
> *Soft flag*: I extend Heidegger's *paschein*-with-*soteria*-character (BCAP p. 132) to anger and fear specifically. Heidegger himself develops the structure for *pathos* generally as "the *being-taken* of being-there ... taken with that which is there in the world — *from without*, but from without *in the sense of the world as the wherein of my being*" (BCAP p. 133). The extension to anger/fear in particular is supported by (i) Aristotle's classification of anger and fear among the *pathē* in *Rhet.* II.2 and II.5, and (ii) the *On Memory* 453a14–22 passage on the kinetic persistence of "already-excited anger and terror" — which makes the *soteria*-structure (the *kinēsis* preserves itself rather than dissipating) particularly visible in these cases.

## Why
The extension is dissertation-interpretive but well-grounded; the soft flag plus BCAP p. 132 anchor (plus *Rhet.* II.2/II.5 + *Mem.* 453a14–22 supporting anchors) is the honest citation.
