## What (primary-text footnote)
> Aristotle defines fear at *Rhetoric* II.5, 1382a20–22 as "pain or disturbance due to imagining (*phantasias*) some destructive or painful evil in the future." The *desire* dimension is implicit in the future-oriented avoidance — *phobos* is a *kinēsis* of soul toward fleeing the imagined evil; cf. *DA* I.1, 403a16–b19, where fear is given as a paradigm of how *pathē* are "logoi enmattered" — embodied affective movements.
