## Why
The articulational-resolution restatement requires the *preservation-not-destruction* doctrine at both anchors: Aristotle's at *DA* II.5 (preservative-paschein) and Heidegger's at BCAP p. 132 (*soteria*-character of *paschein*). Together they ground the dissertation's account of how the basic valence is "resolved" (articulated) without being negated.
