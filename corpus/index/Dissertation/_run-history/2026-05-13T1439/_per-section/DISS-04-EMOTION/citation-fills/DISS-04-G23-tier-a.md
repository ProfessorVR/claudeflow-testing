## What (primary-text footnote)
> The variable presence of *phantasia* across action-types is Aristotle's own analysis at *Movement of Animals* 7: rapid action telescopes the practical syllogism so that "I want to drink, says appetite; this is drink, says sense or imagination or thought: straightaway I drink" (701a32–33). For deliberative action, the syllogism's particular-premise step is filled by *phantasia* or *aisthēsis* identifying the present object as an instance of the desired kind (cf. MA-01-P4 at `phase2-ma-01.md` line 132).

## Why
The variable-presence claim depends on the contrast between (i) rapid/unreflective action (telescoped syllogism, *MA* 7, 701a32–33) and (ii) deliberative action (full syllogism with *phantasia*-supplied particular premise). The MA 7 anchor is canonical for this contrast.
