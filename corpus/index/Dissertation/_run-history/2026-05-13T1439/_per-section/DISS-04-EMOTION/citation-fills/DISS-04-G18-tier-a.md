## What (compound primary-text footnote; reflects FINAL terminology)
> *Per FINAL terminology*: `resonant epithymia` is the residual-phase counterpart of *epithymia*. The dual textual base: (i) *On Dreams* 1, 459a17–18 (the second *kinēsis* "echoes" the first, persisting after the live sense has departed; see Caston's "echo" diagram, `phx-04-caston.md` line 37); and (ii) *DA* II.5, 416b32–417a20 on the perceptive faculty's transition from potentiality to first actuality. The pair grounds the affective-kinetic persistence that the dissertation analytically extracts as `resonant epithymia`.

## Why
The new FINAL terminology architecture (Q2 decisive: `resonant epithymia` is basic-only, distinct from `resonant pathē` which is articulationally concrete) requires the dual anchor: *On Dreams* for kinetic persistence + *DA* II.5 for the actuality-of-the-percipient that grounds the persistence.

## Verbatim (≤25 words each)
- *DA* II.5: "The actualization of the percipient is the same as that of the perceptible" (cluster 416b32–417a20).
- *On Dreams* 1, 459a17–18 (paraphrased from Caston): "the second change [phantasia] is necessarily similar to the sensation, and from this it follows that the second change can be false as well as true."
