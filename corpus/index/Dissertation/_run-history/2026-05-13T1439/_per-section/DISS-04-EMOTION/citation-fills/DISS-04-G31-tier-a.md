## What (primary-text footnote)
> Aristotle establishes the *energeia*-coincidence of the perceptible and the perceiver at *De Anima* II.5 (and the parallel locus at III.2, 425b25–426a26): "the actualization of the percipient and that of the perceptible is one and the same actualization." This grounds the dissertation's account of how the *energeia* of the *aisthētikon* is constitutive of (rather than merely accompanying) the percept.

## Why
The *energeia*-of-aisthētikon claim is structurally load-bearing for §1.4's account of how the basic affective valence is co-given with live perception. *DA* II.5 416b32–417a20 plus III.2 425b26 are the canonical loci.
