## What (secondary-source footnote)
> The defense of the *Rhetoric* as ontological — rather than merely psychological or rhetorical-technical — runs through Heidegger's BCAP, pp. 113–115, where *pathē* are identified as "*ginomena en tē psyche*" co-given with *hēdonē* and *lupē*. The decisive ontological move: "the analysis of *pathē* will show 'how what is designated as *pathos* *defines being-in-the-world in a fundamental sense*'" (BCAP p. 114; `phase2-u-fp3b.md` line 192). And the further p. 115 specification: the *pathē* are "a 'being-disposed-as-higher-or-lower' of the being-there in question" — i.e., a structural feature of Dasein's being, not a contingent psychic episode.

## Why
The "Rhetoric-as-ontological" defense is one of Heidegger's most-quoted BCAP claims; pp. 113–115 is the canonical locus. The corpus-index entry localizes the page-precise quotations.
