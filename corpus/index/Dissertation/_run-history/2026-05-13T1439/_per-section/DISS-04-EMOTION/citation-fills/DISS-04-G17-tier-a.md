## What (primary-text footnote)
> The "basic affective valence" definition rests on *De Anima* III.7, 431a8–12, where perception that registers a pleasant or painful object initiates the pursue/avoid response: "To perceive then is like bare asserting or thinking; when the object is pleasant or painful, the soul makes a sort of affirmation or negation and pursues or avoids the object." The hedonic-co-givenness with live *aisthēsis* is precisely what the FINAL terminology calls *epithymia* in its live phase.

## Why
The basic-affective-valence definition is canonically anchored at *DA* III.7 431a8–12, where Aristotle locates the pleasant/painful registration as an aspect of perception itself rather than a downstream cognitive addition. The locus is also DISS-00-G07's referent — the two sections share the textual anchor.
