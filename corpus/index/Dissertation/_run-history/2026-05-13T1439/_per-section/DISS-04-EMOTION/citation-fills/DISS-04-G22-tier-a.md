## What (primary-text footnote)
> Aristotle distinguishes two senses of *paschein* at *De Anima* II.5, 417a14–417b28: one that is a destruction by a contrary (ordinary alteration), and one that is a "preservation or fulfilment of a potentiality by what is actual" — and locates sense-perception in the latter, preservative mode. "Sense-perception is the latter kind"; thus the perceiver is not destroyed by the perceived but actualized by it, in a paschein that *preserves* rather than *destroys* its bearer (cf. concept-matrix entry at `phase2-da-03.md` line 308: "Sense-perception as a kind of being-affected; preservation not destruction").

## Why
The "articulational concretion" thesis depends on Aristotle's preservative-paschein doctrine at *DA* II.5 417a14–417b28, which the corpus index foregrounds as the key alteration-vs-actualization distinction.
