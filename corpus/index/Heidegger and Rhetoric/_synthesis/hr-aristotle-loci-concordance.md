# HR Aristotle Loci Concordance

**Volume**: *Heidegger and Rhetoric*, ed. Gross & Kemmann, SUNY Press 2005.

**Phase**: 3D — Cross-Pipeline Citation Concordance (Aristotle primary loci)

**Methodology**: Aggregated the `aristotle_loci` array from each of the 8 Phase 2 unit JSONs. De-duplicated by canonical (Aristotle work, first Bekker number — or book/chapter if no Bekker) key. Each locus is bridged to the Aristotle pipeline unit whose Bekker range contains the cited passage.

**Bridge resolution**:
- **Rhetoric**, **Nicomachean Ethics**, **Politics**, **Physics**, **Metaphysics**, **De anima** → Aristotle pipeline unit (RHET-01 through MA-01) via Bekker-number lookup
- **De motu animalium** (MA-01 exists) — Struever's reading of MA is a unique contribution
- **De partibus animalium**, **Topics**, **Categories**, **Poetics**, **Rhetorica ad Alexandrum**, **Posterior Analytics** → no existing pipeline unit (Phase 4 candidates for new Aristotle-pipeline units)

**Counts**: 66 distinct Aristotle loci; 15 multi-cited (≥2 distinct contributors); 5 volume-pillars (≥4 distinct contributors).
