## Section A — RHETORIC DEFINED IN HEIDEGGER'S WORK

*Compiler's note*: The term 'rhetoric' can be found in at least eight places. Seven of those come from the Marburg period, when Heidegger makes regular reference to Greek rhetoric from a philosophical perspective. In order to get a full picture of Heidegger's relationship to rhetoric, one must consider rhetoric more broadly as the everydayness of being-with-one-another (Alltäglichkeit des Miteinanderseins). See indexes compiled by Petkovšek and Feick/Ziegler.

**Entry count**: 8 loci, each with explicit rhetoric passages.
**Phase 3 cross-pipeline role**: This is the master cross-pipeline bridge index. Each locus has an explicit `cross_pipeline_link` field pointing to BCAP / BT / Aristotle pipeline units where applicable.

### BIB-A-1 — Lecture manuscript 1923/24: *Wahrsein und Dasein nach Aristoteles*

- **Publication status**: unpublished
- **Rhetoric passages cited by Kemmann**:
  - 'Rhetorik' die Fundamentallehre vom Logos—die ursprüngliche Logik (3), following Aristotle's Rhetoric 1.2-3
- **Cross-pipeline link**: Aristotle pipeline → Rhetoric Bk 1.2-3 (proof and pisteis); BCAP pipeline preludes — 1923/24 manuscript not yet in BCAP corpus but prefigures GA 18's GBA-rhetoric thesis

### BIB-A-2 — Lecture Course SS 1924: *Grundbegriffe der aristotelischen Philosophie*

- **Publication status**: published
- **GA volume**: GA 18
- **Rhetoric passages cited by Kemmann**:
  - Die Auslegung des Daseins des Menschen hinsichtlich der Grundmöglichkeit des Miteinandersprechens am Leitfaden der Rhetorik (103-267)
  - Der konkrete Beleg für die Ursprünglichkeit des Sehens [des Logos] ist die ganze 'Rhetorik' (61)
  - Die Rhetorik ist nichts anderes als die Auslegung des konkreten Daseins, die Hermeneutik des Daseins selbst (110)
  - Besinnung über das Sprechen (113)
  - 'Leitfaden' für die Bestimmung der Stellung der 'wissenschaftliche[n] Begriffsbildung im Dasein des Menschen' (123f)
- **Cross-pipeline link**: BCAP pipeline (entire) — this is the BCAP source text; volume's most-cited single passage 'Die Rhetorik ist nichts anderes...' (110) is the epigraph for HR-01-GROSS-A and HR-05-STRUEVER

- **Aristotle pipeline**: BIB-A-1 cites Aristotle's Rhetoric 1.2-3; BIB-A-7 cites Rhetoric 2.5 (1382a 20–1383b11). All loci reference the Greek text that the Aristotle pipeline catalogs.
- **Section B**: Section B (the 1922-1925 working corpus around GA 18) is mostly outside existing pipelines except BIB-B-4/B-5/B-6/B-8 which prefigure BT's temporality + Dilthey-genealogy.
