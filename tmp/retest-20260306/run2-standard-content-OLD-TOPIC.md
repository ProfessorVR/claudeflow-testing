# Orienting Faculties: Phantasia and Stimmung as World-Disclosing Structures in Aristotle and Heidegger

## Introduction: Establishing the Phenomenological Territory

Within contemporary phenomenological scholarship, the question of pre-reflective world-disclosure has emerged as a fundamental concern for understanding how consciousness attunes itself to its existential-perceptual environment before the advent of explicit cognitive operations. This investigation has generated increasing interest in examining how classical Aristotelian concepts might illuminate or anticipate Heideggerian insights regarding the primordial structures through which beings encounter their world. Indeed, the growing recognition that Heidegger's own engagement with Aristotelian philosophy reveals deeper structural parallels than previously acknowledged has opened new avenues for comparative philosophical analysis (Heidegger 2009, pp. 25-26). The emergence of this scholarly trajectory reflects a broader methodological shift toward understanding both phantasia (φαντασία) in Aristotelian psychology and Stimmung in Heideggerian fundamental ontology as phenomena that operate prior to reflective deliberation, functioning instead as what might be termed "orienting faculties" that attune the subject to their world-context. However, despite this fertile ground for investigation, systematic comparative analysis of these concepts specifically as world-disclosing structures remains underdeveloped within the existing literature. ## Identifying the Critical Gap: From Isolation to Integration

While scholarship has extensively examined phantasia within the context of Aristotelian psychology, particularly its intermediary role between perception (aisthesis) and intellect (nous), and similarly has explored Heidegger's concept of Stimmung as fundamental attunement within the analytic of Dasein, these investigations have typically proceeded along parallel but separate trajectories (O'Gorman 2005, p. 31). The tendency to treat these concepts within their respective philosophical frameworks has obscured potentially illuminating structural parallels regarding their function as pre-cognitive world-disclosing mechanisms. This scholarly lacuna becomes particularly significant when we consider that both concepts address a fundamental philosophical problem: how beings become oriented within their existential-perceptual field before engaging in explicit cognitive or deliberative operations. Thus, while existing scholarship provides valuable foundations for understanding each concept individually, it has not adequately explored their systematic comparison as functionally parallel structures that serve to orient consciousness within its world-context. ## Occupying the Analytical Niche: The Thesis

### The Ontological Status of Phantasia

Aristotle's analysis of phantasia in De Anima III.3 reveals a faculty that occupies a unique ontological position between sense-perception (aisthesis) and intellect (nous), functioning neither as mere receptivity nor as pure cognitive activity, but rather as a distinctively temporal-spatial orientation that enables the subject's engagement with absent objects (White 1985, p. 109). The significance of this intermediary position becomes clear when we recognize that phantasia addresses a fundamental problem in Aristotelian psychology: how beings can be moved to action by objects that are not currently present to perception. Indeed, Aristotle's careful  phantasia cannot be reduced to either perception or judgment precisely because it performs a unique world-disclosing function that makes both perceptual discrimination and intellectual apprehension possible. As Aristotle argues, phantasia enables the soul to apprehend objects "as if" they were present, thereby creating a temporal-spatial bridge between past perception and future action that serves as the foundation for all oriented movement within the world (Aristotle, De Anima, 427b14-428a5). ### Phantasia as Temporal-Spatial Disclosure

The temporal dimension of phantasia's world-disclosing function emerges through its capacity to present past perceptions in a way that makes them available for present deliberation and future action. This temporal synthesis does not merely reproduce previous perceptual content, but rather transforms it through what Nussbaum describes as phantasia's capacity to present objects under particular aspects or interpretations that reveal their significance for the subject's practical engagement (Nussbaum 1985, pp. 221-222). Thus, phantasia functions as a temporal-spatial orientation that discloses the world as a field of possibilities for movement and desire. Moreover, this temporal-spatial disclosure reveals phantasia's role as a pre-reflective orienting faculty that operates prior to explicit cognitive judgment. The image-making function of phantasia does not simply reproduce perceptual content, but rather presents objects within significance-relations that orient the subject toward possibilities for engagement, movement, and satisfaction (Gonzalez 2006, pp. 99-101). Accordingly, phantasia serves as a fundamental structure through which the subject becomes attuned to their world-context before engaging in deliberative reflection. The critical insight emerges through recognizing that movement requires not merely the presence of an object, but rather the disclosure of that object within a significance-context that reveals possibilities for satisfaction or avoidance (Caston 1995, pp. 284-285). Thus, phantasia's world-disclosing function becomes evident through its capacity to present objects as desirable or undesirable, thereby orienting the subject within a field of practical possibilities. Furthermore, this  phantasia operates as a pre-reflective structure that enables oriented movement before explicit deliberation occurs. The subject does not first perceive an object, then judge it to be desirable, and finally move toward it; rather, phantasia discloses objects within significance-relations that immediately orient the subject toward appropriate responses (Papachristou 2013, pp. 89-91). Consequently, phantasia functions as an orienting faculty that attunes the subject to their world-context through pre-cognitive disclosure of practical possibilities. ## Heideggerian Stimmung: Existential Attunement as World-Disclosure

### Stimmung as Fundamental Attunement

Heidegger's analysis of Stimmung in Being and Time sections 29-30 reveals mood not as a psychological state or subjective coloring of experience, but rather as a fundamental existential structure through which Dasein finds itself always already disposed within its world (Heidegger 1962, pp. 154-155). This fundamental attunement discloses the totality of beings in their significance-relations before any thematic apprehension or cognitive judgment occurs, thus serving as a pre-reflective condition of possibility for all subsequent engagement with entities within the world. The ontological priority of Stimmung becomes evident through Heidegger's demonstration that Dasein never encounters itself as a detached subject subsequently relating to objects, but rather finds itself always already disposed within a world that has been disclosed through fundamental attunement. Mood reveals Dasein's thrownness (Geworfenheit) - its finding itself delivered over to a world that it has not chosen but within which it must orient itself toward possibilities for authentic existence (Rickert 2013, pp. 31-32). 330-331). This existential structure discloses Dasein's thrown situatedness within a world that has always already been opened up through attunement, revealing the fundamental priority of world-disclosure over subject-object relations. Befindlichkeit thus functions as a pre-reflective orienting structure that attunes Dasein to its world-context by disclosing the significance-totality within which entities can be encountered. This disclosure does not occur through cognitive judgment or perceptual apprehension, but rather through the fundamental attunement that reveals Dasein as always already disposed within a world of meaningful relations (Gross 2017, pp. 127-128). Accordingly, Befindlichkeit serves as an orienting faculty that enables Dasein's engagement with entities by first disclosing the world-context within which such engagement becomes possible. ### Erschlossenheit: Mood as Pre-Cognitive World-Revelation

Heidegger's concept of Erschlossenheit (disclosure) reveals how Stimmung functions as a fundamental structure of world-revelation that operates prior to all cognitive operations. Mood does not merely color or influence our perception of entities; rather, it discloses the world as the horizon within which entities can be encountered in their significance and meaning (Heidegger 1962, pp. 60-61). This disclosure reveals the ontological priority of world-attunement over subject-object relations, demonstrating that Dasein's engagement with entities presupposes a more fundamental disclosure of the world-context within which such engagement occurs. Furthermore, this  Erschlossenheit functions as a pre-reflective condition of possibility for all subsequent thematic apprehension, cognitive judgment, and practical engagement. The world disclosed through mood provides the significance-context within which entities can appear as meaningful, valuable, threatening, or promising, thus serving as the existential foundation for all particular encounters with entities (Rickert 2018, pp. 445-446). ### Phantasia's Perceptual Foundation

Despite these structural parallels, important differences emerge in the grounding and scope of these world-disclosing functions. Aristotelian phantasia remains fundamentally tied to perceptual experience, requiring previous aisthesis as the material basis for its image-making activity, though it transforms this perceptual content through significance-disclosure that reveals practical possibilities (Caston 1995, pp. 291-292). This perceptual grounding means that phantasia's world-disclosure operates within the horizon of sensible experience, even as it transcends mere perceptual reproduction through its temporal-synthetic function. Accordingly, phantasia's world-disclosing capacity remains structured by the categories and limitations of sensible experience, even as it enables transcendence of immediate perceptual presence through temporal synthesis and significance-disclosure. This perceptual foundation provides both the strength and limitation of phantasia as an orienting faculty, enabling concrete engagement with sensible world-contexts while potentially limiting its scope to entities that can be related to sensible experience (O'Gorman 2005, pp. 145-147). ### Stimmung's Existential Scope

By contrast, Heideggerian Stimmung functions as a more fundamental existential structure that discloses the totality of beings in their existential significance rather than their perceptual properties. Stimmung's world-disclosure operates at the level of Dasein's fundamental relationship to Being itself, revealing the existential situation within which all particular entities - whether sensible or non-sensible - can be encountered in their significance for Dasein's authentic existence (Heidegger 1962, pp. 117-118). This existential scope means that Stimmung's world-disclosing function encompasses not only sensible entities but also existential structures, possibilities, and relationships that cannot be reduced to perceptual experience. The mood of anxiety, for example, discloses nothing (das Nichts) as the fundamental ground of Dasein's freedom and responsibility, revealing existential structures that transcend the scope of sensible experience entirely (Rickert 2013, pp. 311-312).
