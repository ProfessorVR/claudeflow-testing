## Subsection role

Develop the *pathetic* loop in both registers.

### Synchronic register (single chain-pass within a discrete event)

A_0 → A_1 → A_2 → A_3 → A_4, with emotion entering at the A_3 *doxa*-gate (for Type 3 actions) and converting *orexis* into species-specific affective-orectic mobilization at M_3 → A_4. The synchronic register is what the typology (Subsection 4) and the doxa-gate (Subsection 5) articulate: each chain-pass is directional from A_0 to A_4 within a single discrete event.

### Diachronic register (across chain-passes)

Residues of prior actualizations sediment as *hexis* / *Geworfenheit*, modulating subsequent passes through two distinct mechanisms:

(i) ***Stimmung*-saturation** — biases basic valence at A_1. This is Heidegger's *Befindlichkeit* / *Stimmung* operating at the perceptual register. Aristotelian warrant: *De Insomniis* 2, 460b1–10 — the cowardly excited by fear thinks he sees his foes approaching, the amorous person sees the object of desire even with little resemblance to go upon. This is the canonical Aristotelian description of affective priming of recognition: the agent's prior emotional state saturates the perceptual disclosure of present objects.

(ii) **Corrective-faculty impairment** — also from *De Insomniis* 460b3–16, where existing emotional state modulates the *gating* operation at A_2 → A_3. The corrective faculty's normal operation (resisting the *taking-as-true* of evaluatively distorting *phantasmata*) is impaired under emotional excitement; the gate opens more readily to *phantasmata* that ratify the agent's current affective register.

Both are diachronic modulations of existing synchronic transitions — they do not add new transitions, but they bias the existing ones.

### The judgement-modifying claim

The loop's *judgement-modifying* function (*Rhetoric* II.1, 1378a21–22 — *pathē* as "feelings that so change men as to affect their judgements") is the synchronic expression of the diachronic mechanism: emotion modifies the very judgement that would normally check or correct it. The *Rhetoric*'s definition of *pathē* and the *De Insomniis* mechanism are not two different doctrines but two articulations of the same loop-structure operating across two temporal registers.

### Optional Gross deployment

If Source E Gross deployment is natural for the anti-internalist closure of the diachronic mechanism, deploy *Gross, "Introduction" in Heidegger and Rhetoric (2005), p. 4* (anti-internalist anchor — *pathos* as condition of possibility for *logos*) — verbatim from Source E. This is the canonical Gross statement of the anti-internalist thesis that the diachronic register articulates at the bodily-dispositional level.

### Recursive-feedback close

Close with the recursive-feedback claim: A_4 completion → fresh *hexis*-formation → next chain-pass enters at a new A_0 with the sedimented dispositional ground subtly shifted. The chain is therefore not merely iterative but spiraling: the agent who has just acted is not the same agent who would next act, because the act itself has reshaped the dispositional ground.

State that this is what Heidegger names *Geschichtlichkeit* (*Being and Time* §74, H.385–386) — Dasein's historicity is the diachronic structure of the *pathetic* loop's recursive self-modification. The Heideggerian articulation does not add to Aristotle's structure; it names what the Aristotelian text already specifies.
