**Subsection role.** Develop the thesis that *phantasia* is the temporal-kinetic medium that pervades every actualization in the chain. Open with *De Anima* III.3, 429a1–2: *phantasia* is "a movement resulting from actual perception" — a residual motion that persists, recombines, and re-presents, supplying the condition for evaluative response in the absence of immediate stimuli.

Develop *phantasia*'s temporal range using *Rhetoric* I.11, 1370a27–30: "Both the man who remembers and the man who hopes will be attended by an imagination of what he remembers or hopes." Each act of remembering and each act of hoping is attended by a *phantasma*; *phantasia* is therefore the capacity by which the soul holds the present, the remembered past, and the anticipated future together as a single phenomenal manifold.

Develop the temporal-depth claim with *Rhetoric* II.5, 1382a21–22 on fear: "a pain or disturbance due to imagining some destructive or painful evil in the future." The feared evil does not yet exist; the *phantasma* of it does; and the soul moves as though the feared thing were present. *Phantasia*'s temporal range is therefore what makes fear, hope, anger, and pity possible at all — they are responses to the *phantasmatic* presence of absent objects.

Integrate the secondary scholarship from Source D: Nussbaum on *phantasia* as material-supplier for practical reasoning; Hawhee on rhetorical activation of the audience's phantasmatic capacity; O'Gorman's synthesis that *phantasia* "forms the objects of belief and desire"; White on residual motion as "lingering, resonating, echoing presence"; Frede on residual motion having "a life of its own."

State the rhetorical cascade: new *phantasma* → new *doxa* → new emotion → new orientation → new action. *Phantasia* is the leading edge of every such cascade because every other moment of the chain operates on the *phantasma* (or, in the resonant-residue case at A_1, on the *resonant aisthēma* + *resonant epithymia* that *phantasia* will subsequently take up to constitute the *phantasma* at A_2).

Close with the structural claim: every action is mediated by *phantasia* because every action is mediated by an appearance — whether the immediate *phantasma* identifying the present object as drink, the settled *phantasma* informing the universal premise of a practical syllogism, the projected *phantasma* synthesized by deliberative *phantasia bouleutikē* (*De Anima* III.11, 434a5–10), or the vivid *phantasma* induced by rhetorical *enargeia* in the audience. This is the unifying thread that the typology will refract into three distinct routes.
