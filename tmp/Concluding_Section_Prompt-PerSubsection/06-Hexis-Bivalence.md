**Subsection role.** Develop the *hexis* dimension that §1.4 set up at L195–201.

Open with *Categories* 8, 8b25–9a13: *hexis* is a settled disposition, more stable than *diathesis*, encompassing virtues, crafts, knowledge, and bodily states. State that settled *doxai* are ONE SPECIES of *hexis* (the doxastic-cognitive species; warrant: *De Motu Animalium* 7, 701a7–16, where the practical syllogism's universal premise is a settled *doxa*) but that *hexis* is the broader genus. Reference *Metaphysics* Δ.20, 1022b4 (*hexis* as activity of haver and had) and *Nicomachean Ethics* II.1, 1103a14–b25 (moral excellence comes about as a result of habit) for the broader doctrine.

Then deploy Heidegger's bivalent reading from BCAP §17. The two block-quotes are load-bearing.

**Technē-hexis block-quote (BCAP p. 127).** Use `\begin{adjustwidth}{0.5in}{0in}` ... `\end{adjustwidth}` markup. Verbatim text from Source B:

> "Through practice, by frequently-undergoing, it comes about that being-oriented puts the prescription further and further out of play. Training has the precise sense of reducing deliberation insofar as it is through training that the completedness of attaining a result comes about."

Develop the structural reading: training reduces deliberation; the *technē*-grounded *doxa* runs the chain WITHOUT engaging higher-order *pathē*. The expert pianist's hands, the carpenter's chisel-grip, the orator's settled cadence — each is a *technē*-hexis that fires the Type 2 chain through transparent procedural *doxa*. The "completedness of attaining a result" is the *technē*-hexis's defining ergonomic structure: the result is external, the operation is means-to-end, and training is the cultivation of routine.

**Praxis-hexis block-quote (BCAP p. 128).** Use `\begin{adjustwidth}{0.5in}{0in}` ... `\end{adjustwidth}` markup. Verbatim text from Source B:

> "Cultivating *hexis* never depends on an operation, a routine. In an operation, the moment is destroyed. Every completedness, as settled routine, breaks down in the face of the moment. Appropriation and cultivation of *hexis* through habituation means nothing other than correct repetition.... The distinction lies in the fact that *praxis* depends on the *how*. The how is only appropriated in such a way that the human being enables himself *to be composed at each moment*; not routine but holding-oneself-open, *dynamis* in the *mesotēs*."

Develop the structural reading: *praxis*-hexis is holding-oneself-open for fresh *kairos*-resolution; it is the *hexis* through which an agent enters Type 3 evaluatively complex action well — neither bypassing *doxa* (Type 1) nor running it on routine (Type 2). The "moment" (*Augenblick*, in Heidegger's later vocabulary) is precisely what *technē*-hexis destroys and what *praxis*-hexis sustains.

Connect *praxis*-hexis to *phronēsis* (*Nicomachean Ethics* VI.5, 1140a24–b30: "a true and reasoned state of capacity to act with regard to the things that are good or bad for man") — *phronēsis* is the canonical practical-rational *hexis* that exemplifies *praxis*-hexis's holding-oneself-open structure.

Connect *technē*-hexis to BCAP pp. 122–123 ("for there is no *technē* for the *kairos*") — the *kairos* is precisely what *technē*-hexis cannot resolve; it requires *praxis*-hexis.

**Mapping onto the three types.** Map the bivalence onto the three types:

- Type 1 (Simple appetition) bypasses both *technē*-hexis and *praxis*-hexis: it is appetitive-immediate.
- Type 2 (Habitual-rational) routes through *technē*-hexis: settled procedural *doxa* fires without evaluative engagement.
- Type 3 (Evaluatively complex) routes through *praxis*-hexis: the agent enters fresh evaluative resolution under cultivated dispositional readiness.

Note that *praxis*-hexis uniquely exercises a two-level reach: it modulates BOTH the basic-valence input at A_1 (the *Stimmung*-saturation of perceptual disclosure) AND the doxa-gate at A_2 → A_3 (the corrective faculty's preparation for fresh evaluative ratification). *Technē*-hexis, by contrast, runs the chain forward without engaging the evaluative axis at all.
