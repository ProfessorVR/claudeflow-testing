## 1. The tutorial scope — and why it extends beyond Chapter 1

### 1.1 Defining the boundary

In the strict sense, the RDR2 tutorial is **Chapter 1: Colter** — the snowy mountain prologue beginning with the gang's flight from Blackwater and ending with the descent to Horseshoe Overlook. But the user's notes (correctly, on a structural reading) extend the unit of analysis through the early missions of **Chapter 2: Horseshoe Overlook**, on the principle that *the tutorial is whatever introduces the systems*. The Honor system, hunting, the camp economy, the train robbery, donating to the camp pot, crafting, the General Store — all are introduced in early Chapter 2 and complete the systems-introduction arc.

The DIA-method augmentation at Step 0 (`ontological-wiring-A0-A4.md` §6) explicitly directs the analyst to set the circumference deliberately. For the RDR2 tutorial we therefore set:

> **Scope**: Chapter 1 (Colter), in full, plus the opening missions of Chapter 2 (Horseshoe Overlook) through the first Honor-mechanic foregrounding (the user's RDR2-07 beat: "Mission, General Store, Honor"). This is the systems-introduction arc; after this point the game opens into its standard sandbox.

The snowy prologue's spatial-kinesthetic deprivation gives way to the camp's spatial-social abundance; the player, having been *forced* through Chapter 1's linear corridor, enters Chapter 2 as someone whose disposition has been cultivated rather than instructed. The descent is the representative form of the tutorial's overall act-shape.

## 2. Step 0 — Scope, circumference, entry point

Per the augmented Step 0 (`ontological-wiring-A0-A4.md` §6): set the episode boundary, identify the entry point (sense at A₁ / imagination at A₂ / thought at A₃), and decide whether the analysis is synchronic (within a chain-pass) or diachronic (across passes).

**Boundary**: as set in §1.1 — Chapter 1 (Colter) + early Chapter 2 (Horseshoe Overlook) through the first Honor-mechanic foregrounding.

**Entry point**: **A₁ (sense entry)**, with a precise twist that is itself structurally significant.

Most game tutorials presuppose an A₂ entry — the player brings *phantasmata* of "what a third-person open-world shooter is" from prior games, and the tutorial confirms or extends them. RDR2's opening cinematic — the snowy approach, the visual obscurity, the muffled audio, the gang silhouettes blurring into the blizzard — deliberately strips the player of those held *phantasmata*. The chain re-enters at A₁: the player perceives the gameworld as fresh-perception before the *phantasma* of "this-is-a-Rockstar-game" can take hold. (This is the user's RDR2-1 "Opening Cinematic" finding restated in the new vocabulary: "the cinematic draws on visual and auditory cues to immerse players, employing what Calleja terms 'incorporation,' where players feel an embodied connection to the space" — what the user identified as "incorporation" is the A₁ dual-resonance deposit *engineered before any A₂ phantasma can interfere*.)

This is engineering: Rockstar's choice of opening cinematic + blizzard + muffled audio is a structural intervention to force A₁ entry rather than A₂. It is the precondition for everything that follows.

**Register**: both. The Chapter 1 prologue is a single tight *synchronic* chain-pass (one extended episode, the Colter survival arc). Chapter 2's opening missions are a *diachronic* sequence (multiple chain-passes within an emerging hexis-band). The analysis must hold both registers.

The featured terms — **Scene, Agent, Act, Attitude** — together with the deliberate *under-foregrounding* of Purpose are the formal-grammatical signature of an ethical-hexis cultivation rather than a competitive-technē-hexis cultivation. (In the WoW raid: Act, Agent, Purpose are featured; Attitude is foregrounded but Purpose is held as god-term. In RDR2 tutorial: Purpose is *muted* so that Attitude can be *cultivated* without becoming instrumental to Purpose.)

This is the structural opposite of the dark-pattern compulsion design. Where compulsion design *engineers Agency-Purpose collapse* (means become ends), the RDR2 tutorial *engineers Purpose to recede* so that the action's end can be *immanent in the doing*. The technical name for this in the dissertation's §1.5 v3 vocabulary: the RDR2 tutorial is *praxis*-mode (*energeia*) rather than *poiēsis*-mode (*kinēsis*). The ends are not deferred to a product; they are realized in the activity itself.

## 5. Step 3 — Ratio dynamics

The dominant ratio across the tutorial arc:

> **Scene–Agent**: how the gameworld qualifies the inhabiting player. The Colter blizzard does not just contain the act; it *qualifies the agent* — the player is constituted as cold, exhausted, dependent on the gang. The descent to Horseshoe Overlook is not just a movement through Space; it is the Scene-Agent ratio resolving toward warmth, abundance, community.

This is RDR2's signature motive-engine. Other operative ratios:

- **Act–Agent (alterbiographic)**: each completed act remakes who the player is. The first hunt, the first horse-bond, the first Honor decision are not just acts; they sediment as hexis. This is the diachronic dimension that the tutorial sets up for the rest of the game.
- **Scene–Act**: the gameworld constrains and enables what the player can do. RDR2's environmental storytelling is constantly active.
- **Agency–Purpose**: deliberately *muted* — the tutorial does not let means-become-ends. The grind-resistance of the RDR2 economy at this point in the game is a structural choice.
- **Agent–Agent (co/counter)**: the gang as co-agents, the O'Driscolls as counter-agents. Sets up the social-motive apparatus.

**The player attributes their behavior to:** *the situation* (Scene-Act) and *who Arthur is becoming* (Act-Agent). Not to *their own development* (which is Act-Agent in the competitive register), not to *the tools* (Agency-Purpose), not to *other players* (the social-Agent-Agent in the competitive register). This attribution pattern is the diagnostic signature of *praxis*-mode play.

## 6. Step 4 — Crosswalk binding (the fusion)

Reading the featured pentad terms through their bound Calleja dimensions via `validated-crosswalk.json`:

### 6.1 Scene × Spatial — the strongest binding, decisive in RDR2

The user's RDR2-02 analysis ("Opening Restriction of Exploration") already gets the structural mechanism:

> "By limiting movement, the game narrows player agency, channeling attention to predefined elements. This supports Burke's dramatistic framework of scene dominating agency: the setting defines the player's role and potential actions."

In the new vocabulary: the Colter corridor *engineers a tight Scene-Agency ratio with Scene as the constraining term*, so that when the gameworld finally opens at the descent the player's spatial-inhabitation criterion has been met *before* the gameworld becomes spatially navigable in the sandbox sense. This is the **R4 cornerstone (spatial + kinesthetic)** being constructed via deprivation. By the time the player rides into Horseshoe Overlook, the gameworld is *already* inhabited — Calleja's Spatial dimension's micro-phase has been engineered through Chapter 1's macro-phase restriction.

This is a brilliant engineering choice — and the new method names it precisely. The mechanism is: cultivate the Spatial cornerstone under deprivation (Colter), then release the gameworld with the cornerstone *already met*, so that what Calleja calls "place-as-inhabited" is the player's default mode-of-encounter rather than a state that has to be earned by sandbox exploration.

### 6.2 Agent × Shared — deepened by consubstantiation (the keystone)

The user's RDR2-1 ("Opening Cinematic") notes: "By rooting the player's early experience in survival and camaraderie, the game primes the player to adopt the characters' perspectives." This is the **A₂ avatar-identification** + the **A₃ doxastic ratification of the gang-as-collective-agent** beginning simultaneously.

The mechanism is precise:

- **A₂**: the *phantasma* of Arthur-as-me is constructed via the cinematic + the first-person interiority (Arthur's spoken thoughts) + the gang's address ("Arthur, give me a hand"). The Hazlitt-Burke identification quote (RoM 83–84, cited at §1.3 of the dissertation) operates here: *the warmth/liveliness of the present phantasma of "Arthur" identifies the future-self the player will become with their present being*.
- **A₃**: doxa ratifies "I AM Arthur Morgan in this gang in 1899." This is involuntary — the user's earlier framing of "rhetorical attunement" identified this precisely: the player does not *decide* to be Arthur; the player *finds themselves* taking-as-true that they are him.

The Shared-Agent binding is engineered via three reinforcing channels:
1. Linguistic: the gang addresses Arthur by name; the player hears "Arthur" hundreds of times in Chapter 1.
2. Pronominal: NPCs use "we" (Dutch's "we got each other") and "us" (Hosea's "let's get them") — the collective-agent *phantasma* is grammaticalized.
3. Mechanical: cooperative actions in Colter (helping John, gunfights with Bill, splitting the O'Driscoll group) routinely require *acting-together* (Burke's RM-01 ground of consubstantiality).

### 6.3 Agency × Kinesthetic — the most-noted but least decisive binding

The user's notes give substantial coverage of the controls-naturalization process (RDR2-03-02: "Movement, Combat, Inventory, Camera"). The mechanism is Heidegger's *Zuhandenheit*: tools transition from *present-at-hand* (consciously operated) to *ready-to-hand* (transparent).

The new method's contribution: name this as *technē-hexis cultivation at the Agency-Kinesthetic cell*. The control scheme becomes a *hexis* — a settled disposition. **But**: the tutorial is careful not to let *technē-hexis* be the whole story. The Honor system, introduced at RDR2-07, *re-engages the evaluative axis* the technē-hexis was bypassing. This is the **ethical-hexis layering on top of the technē-hexis** that makes RDR2 structurally distinctive.

### 6.4 Attitude × Affective — the transverse modifier

The five attitude registers (Part B.3 of the DIA manual) on Chapter 1 + early Chapter 2:

- **Competitive** (agonistic striving): present in the gunfights, the O'Driscoll camp clear-out, but *not foregrounded* — the gunfights are framed as survival, not competition. 
- **Completionist** (engagement sustained by impossibility of final acquisition): present in the camp chores, the donation-to-pot mechanic, but again *not foregrounded* — the chores are framed as *acting-together with the gang*, not as a checklist.
- **Explorer** (dwelling-toward and ascending-through the scene): the descent from Colter is the structural moment Explorer is cultivated; the journal mechanic (user's RDR2 "Inspect Oil Derrick" + "Camera Acquisition") later operationalizes it.
- **Devotional** (reverent self-subordination to the gameworld's order): **the dominant Attitude the tutorial cultivates**. Arthur's devotion to Dutch, the player's devotion to Arthur, the visual reverence with which the gameworld is rendered — all coordinate to engineer a devotional register.
- **Ironic-meta** (detached, playing with the game's rhetoric): *suppressed* by the tutorial. The opening cinematic is designed to defeat the player's prior ironic-meta dispositions from other games.

The devotional register's cultivation is decisive. **Devotional Attitude is the dispositional ground for ethical-hexis** (per §1.5 v3 + `ontological-wiring-A0-A4.md` §4.5): the *philia*-attentive, *kalon*-oriented disposition in which the action's end is immanent in the doing.

## 7. Step 5 — Identification and consubstantiation: the keystone in RDR2

The user's notes name the keystone explicitly at RDR2-02 ("It forces a reflexive identification with the protagonist, leveraging Burke's principle of consubstantiality") and again, more thoroughly, in the Combined Analysis (Honor System, gang interactions, Mary-Beth conversation).

The new method's contribution: the keystone is **the A₃→A₄ substantial-union event** (`ontological-wiring-A0-A4.md` §3), and the tutorial is the engineering site for this event.

### 7.1 The R1–R5 incorporation rubric, ontologically wired

- **R1 assimilation** (world → consciousness) = the *resonant aisthēma* of the Colter blizzard + the Heartlands' light + the camp's auditory texture deposited at A₁ across many chain-passes. By Horseshoe Overlook arrival, the gameworld is internalized as kinetic content. **Met.**
- **R2 embodiment** (player → world via avatar) = the *phantasma* of Arthur-as-me at A₂, doxastically ratified at A₃. The first-person interiority Rockstar gives Arthur (the journal, the spoken thoughts) is engineered to make this *phantasma* especially determinate. **Met.**
- **R3 simultaneity** (joined-and-separate, held simultaneously) = the player is one with Arthur *while remaining a distinct locus of motives*. The structural feature that makes RDR2 morally interesting: the player's judgments diverge from Arthur's (e.g., the player may judge that Dutch is leading the gang into disaster long before Arthur ratifies that *doxa*). The divergence is preserved by the joined-and-separate structure. **Met, distinctively.**
- **R4 cornerstone** (spatial + kinesthetic) = met by end of Colter. The deprivation-then-release engineering at §6.1 ensures this.
- **R5 blending** (4–5+ dimensions co-active) = met by mid-Chapter 2 (the camp chores + first hunt + train robbery weave 5–6 dimensions per scene).

**Verdict: incorporation MET by Horseshoe Overlook arrival.** The tutorial is, in DIA terms, an *incorporation engineering arc* — every beat is structured to advance one or more R-criteria.

### 7.2 The Burke direction: consubstantiation as the social-substantial event

What the new method adds beyond the user's already-correct identification:

> At A₃, the doxa-ratified *phantasma* of "the gang" — Arthur, Dutch, Hosea, John, Charles, Lenny, Sean, Javier, Bill, Karen, Mary-Beth, Tilly, Reverend Swanson, Sadie, Jenny, Davey, Mac, Susan Grimshaw — is the social substance the player becomes one-with. When the player at A₄ acts in any beat (helping carry supplies, hunting for the camp, defending the camp, sitting at the fire), the action's terminus is *jointly realized*: the meat fed to the camp is meat *we* secured; the train job done is a job *we* pulled. The "one substrate, two in being" structure (DA III.7 431a13–14) operates at the social level.

This is consubstantiation in the technical Burkean sense (RM-01 p.21: "substantially one … yet a distinct locus of motives — both joined and separate"). The user's notes named it; the new method names *where and how* it happens (the A₃→A₄ joint chain-actualization) and *why it has the moral weight it has* (because the Aristotelian structure preserves the joined-and-separate condition at the formal level).

### 7.3 Why this matters: the tutorial pre-empts the Mary-Beth conversation, the Sean rescue, and the train job's moral weight

The user's later scene analyses (Mary-Beth, Sean rescue, train robbery) all depend on the keystone being already-met *before they happen*. The tutorial's structural function is to set up the keystone so that these later scenes can *exploit* it.

The Mary-Beth intimate conversation (user's RDR2 Mary-Beth scene) is a Type 3 bouletic + ethical-hexis scene **only because** the consubstantiation has been engineered in the tutorial. Without the tutorial's keystone-engineering, the conversation would be a competitive-completionist dialogue-tree exercise. With it, the conversation is a moment of *philia*-attentive ethical engagement.

Similarly: the Sean rescue, the train robbery, the camp party celebrating Sean's return — each leverages the keystone. The tutorial is the engineering, and the rest of the game is the exploitation.

## 8. Step 6 — Attitude and hexis-bivalence

The augmented Step 6 (`ontological-wiring-A0-A4.md` §6, Step 6) requires identifying not just the dominant attitude register but also its *hexis-status* — is it cultivated as *technē-hexis* (transparent routine) or as ethical *hexis* (holding-oneself-open for fresh *kairos* resolution)?

The tutorial's hexis-bivalence work is **layered** — and this is the structural reason RDR2 is a critical touchstone for game-as-ethics:

### 8.1 *Technē*-hexis layer

The Kinesthetic-Agency cell is cultivated as *technē-hexis*: the control scheme becomes transparent. Aiming, sprinting, looting, riding, calming the horse, eating, sleeping — all sediment into procedural routines that fire the chain through M₂→A₃ on transparent doxa without engaging the evaluative axis. This is necessary; without it the player cannot operate in the gameworld.

### 8.2 Ethical-hexis layer

Layered *on top of* the technē-hexis, the tutorial cultivates an ethical-hexis at three distinct cells:

| Cell | Ethical-hexis content | Tutorial moment |
|---|---|---|
| **Scene × Spatial** | The *kairos*-attentive dwelling-toward (explorer-devotional register): the gameworld asks to be *attended to*, not just navigated | The descent from Colter; the journal/inspection mechanic |
| **Agent × Shared** | The *philia*-attentive *Mitsein* with the gang: the gang-members ask to be *known*, not just used | The camp interactions (greeting NPCs, conversations, the camp party) |
| **Attitude × Affective** | The standing-toward Arthur's moral situation: the Honor system asks the player to *take a position*, not just to optimize a meter | The Honor-mechanic introduction (RDR2-07); the Mary-Beth intimate conversation; the Wanted system's moral feedback |

The ethical-hexis layer is what the user's notes were tracking under "rhetorical attunement" — the alignment that is "not merely cognitive but deeply affective and embodied." In the new vocabulary, this is the **two-level reach of the ethical *hexis*** (§1.5 v3, summarized at `ontological-wiring-A0-A4.md` §2.8): the ethical *hexis* modulates *both* the basic-valence input at A₁ (Stimmung-saturation: the player enters each new scene already affectively disposed) *and* the doxastic ratification at M₂→A₃ (the player ratifies *phantasmata* with evaluative complexity in mind).

### 8.3 The *kairos*-responsiveness moment

Heidegger's *kairos*-responsiveness (BCAP 128) — the cultivated capacity to meet the singular demand of the situation as it arises — is engineered into the RDR2 tutorial through specific design choices:

1. **Variable timing in scripted scenes**: the Honor decisions in the tutorial are not yes/no buttons; they are unfolding situations in which the player's *kairos*-responsiveness is tested. (User's RDR2-07: "Decisions affecting Honor create a feedback loop where the player's choices shape Arthur's reputation and narrative trajectory.")
2. **Random encounters from early game**: the user's "Helping Random Stranger" notes correctly identify that random events "maintain player interest by introducing unscripted interactions and moral choices." Structurally: they cultivate the holding-oneself-open disposition. **The random encounter at Horseshoe Overlook is the first opportunity for the cultivated ethical-hexis to actualize freshly.**
3. **The journal mechanic** (user's "Inspect Oil Derrick"): the journal's reflection-pause is engineered to cultivate the *attending-to-the-singular* disposition.

### 8.4 The structural diagnostic: RDR2 vs. WoW vs. dark-pattern compulsion design

| Disposition | Cultivated by | Operates at | Action-type |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Technē*-hexis runaway (compulsion) | Daily-login, gacha-pull, FOMO-gated currency | All cells, but especially Agency × Ludic | Type 4 → Type 1 regression |

## 9. Step 7 — Diagnosis and ends

### 9.1 One-paragraph behavioral diagnosis

The RDR2 tutorial (Chapter 1 Colter + early Chapter 2 Horseshoe Overlook) is a sustained **incorporation-engineering arc** that cultivates an **ethical-hexis** at the Scene × Spatial, Agent × Shared, and Attitude × Affective cells, layered atop a *technē-hexis* cultivated at the Agency × Kinesthetic cell. The tutorial's dominant motive-engine is a **Scene–Agent ratio** (the gameworld qualifying the inhabiting player) operating through the deliberate strategy of **Spatial deprivation in Chapter 1 yielding to Spatial-Social abundance at the Horseshoe descent**, which constructs the **R4 cornerstone (spatial + kinesthetic)** of incorporation under conditions where Calleja's R5 blending becomes available almost immediately on entering the open gameworld. The keystone — **consubstantiation↔incorporation as the A₃→A₄ substantial-union event** — is engineered through the social *phantasma* of the gang at A₂, doxastically ratified at A₃ via linguistic + pronominal + mechanical channels, and consummated at A₄ in the jointly-realized actions of camp life. The featured pentad terms (Scene, Agent, Act, Attitude) and the deliberate **muting of Purpose** in the macro-phase are the formal-grammatical signature of *praxis*-mode (ends immanent in doing) rather than *poiēsis*-mode (ends in a product) — the structural reason the RDR2 tutorial generates the *Stimmung* that critics and players have variously called "presence," "immersion," "incorporation," "rhetorical attunement," "ontological change." All are different descriptions of the same underlying engineering: the chain has been wired to run through A₃'s ethical evaluative axis on each pass, doxa engaging evaluatively complex content, the *resonant epithymia* concretizing into Type 2 thymotic (defense of Mitsein), Type 3 bouletic (the wished-end of *acting-well-with-this-gang*), and Type 4 prohairetic (the deliberate choice of means *kairos*-responsively) action — *not* into Type 1 epithymetic compulsion loops.

### 9.2 Ends this analysis serves

#### E.1 Design critique

The RDR2 tutorial is the structural counter-example to dark-pattern design. Where dark-pattern design engineers **Agency–Purpose collapse** (means become ends → Type 4 → Type 1 regression → *technē*-hexis runaway), the RDR2 tutorial engineers **Purpose-recession with ethical-hexis cultivation** (Purpose is muted so that Attitude can be cultivated; the *technē-hexis* is bounded by the ethical-hexis layered on top). This is naming-worthy as a structural strategy because:

- It is *replicable*: a designer can ask, "what is the *kairos*-attentive disposition I am cultivating, and how is the *technē-hexis* it requires bounded by the ethical-hexis I want?"
- It is *measurable*: the diagnostic asks at each scene which Type the action sources, which hexis is operative, and which A-node is the locus of evaluative work.
- It is *distinct from "narrative depth" as the usual praise-term*: the structural feature is not story quality but the engineering of A₃ to *retain* the evaluative axis even under high *technē-hexis* fluency.

The structural diagnostic that game-design ethics has been waiting for is: **does the design cultivate ethical *hexis* alongside *technē-hexis*, or does it engineer technē-hexis runaway?** RDR2 cultivates ethical-hexis from the tutorial forward. Many AAA games engineer technē-hexis runaway. The structural difference is now nameable.

- The **gang-as-collective-agent identification** the tutorial engineers persists in player discourse: players speak of "our camp," "our gang," "what Dutch did to us." The first-person plural is not a figure of speech but the lived state of acting-together that the keystone preserves.

## 14. The DIA-method augmentation for RDR2-style cases

Beyond applying the method, the RDR2 tutorial *augments* the method itself in three specific ways the WoW example did not surface:

### 14.1 The A₁-entry engineering strategy

The RDR2 opening cinematic's strategy of forcing A₁-entry (defeating the player's prior held *phantasmata* of "Rockstar game") is a design move worth naming as a method-augmentation:

> **A₁-entry engineering**: designs can deliberately interfere with the player's habitual A₂-entry by saturating A₁ with fresh-perception content the player has no held *phantasma* for. The blizzard, the muffled audio, the snowy obscurity together force the chain to begin at A₁. This is a precondition for the rest of the engineering to take effect on the player's *current* dispositional ground rather than on the inherited disposition from previous Rockstar games.

This is a generalizable design move. The augmented Step 0 should ask: *what entry-point does the design engineer, and how is it engineered?*

### 14.2 The Purpose-recession move

WoW raids foreground Purpose as god-term; RDR2 mutes Purpose so that Attitude can be cultivated. The Purpose-recession move is a structural strategy worth naming:

> **Purpose-recession**: designs that want to cultivate ethical *hexis* (as opposed to competitive *technē-hexis*) should *deliberately under-foreground* the Purpose pentad-term. The macro-phase Purpose can be present (the gang must survive; the West is dying) but it must not become a competitive god-term that subordinates means. Attitude takes the foregrounding role.

This is the structural counter-move to the "engagement = god-term + ratings/rank/loot" assumption in much AAA design. RDR2 shows that engagement can be engineered without a god-term Purpose.

### 14.3 The deprivation-then-release Spatial strategy

The Colter→Horseshoe descent is a designed deprivation-then-release of the Spatial dimension:

> **Spatial deprivation-then-release**: engineering the R4 cornerstone (spatial + kinesthetic) under conditions of restricted exploration, then releasing the player into the open gameworld with the cornerstone already met, produces incorporation faster than open-sandbox-from-the-start designs achieve. The Colter corridor is the deprivation; the Horseshoe descent is the release; the structural achievement is that the gameworld is inhabited from the first moment of openness, rather than gradually constructed through exploration.

These three augmentations should be added to the DIA method documentation as `method-augmentations-from-rdr2.md` in a future revision pass.
