## IV. Phantasia, Umwelt, and the Soap Bubble of World-Disclosure

### IV.1 Uexküll's Soap Bubble: Species-Specific Bubbles of Meaning

(300–400 words)

Introduce Uexküll's soap bubble metaphor: each organism lives in its own Umwelt, a closed perceptual-active world composed of Merkwelt (perception world) and Wirkwelt (effect world). Different creatures inhabit different worlds even in the same physical space. As Uexküll writes, "everything a subject perceives belongs to its perception world [Merkwelt], and everything it produces, to its effect world [Wirkwelt]."

Briefly recall the Funktionskreis (functional cycle) structure: the closed loop linking subject and object via perception organ and effect organ. The Merkbild (perception image) is the composite sensory representation; mood (Stimmung) determines which Wirkbild (effect image) activates for a given Merkbild.

Note the structural parallel to Aristotle's T-stage chain without forcing identity: both describe closed loops from perception through image to action.
