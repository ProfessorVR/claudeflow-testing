  # Priority Theses (load-bearing — every integration must serve these)

  ## Thesis A — Doxa as Cognitive Gate

  δόξα (doxa "opinion/yes-saying") functions as the cognitive gate through which
  higher-order πάθη (pathē "passions/emotions") are articulated from basic
  affective valence. The gate is *concretional, not generative*: doxa does not
  create πάθος ex nihilo; it articulates the prior dispositional disclosure of
  basic πάθος into determinate πάθη (ὀργή "anger," φόβος "fear," ἔλεος "pity,"
  αἰσχύνη "shame," and their kin) by converting the pre-propositional
  "this-as-pursuable/avoidable" into the propositional "this-IS-fearful," etc.

    Textual anchors:
      • DA III.3, 427b21–24 (the painting passage; ὅταν δοξάσωμεν…εὐθὺς
        συμπάσχομεν vs. ἀπαθῶς ἔχομεν of mere φαντασία)
      • DA III.3, 428a19–24 (doxa requires πίστις, πίστις requires πεπεῖσθαι,
        πεπεῖσθαι requires λόγος; brutes have phantasia but not doxa)
      • Synthesis document Part II (the [T1] synthesis), §§2.1–2.6

    Heideggerian compatibility:
      • Heidegger's "πάθη ground λόγος" thesis (GA 18 p. 176) operates at the
        basic level; the doxa-gate operates at the higher-order level. The
        directions describe different transitions and are not in conflict.

    Common misreading to flag:
      • Treating the doxa-gate as a generative gate (creating πάθος) rather
        than a concretional gate (articulating prior basic πάθος). The
        synthesis is explicit that doxa presupposes the basic πάθος it
        articulates.

  ## Thesis B — Resonant Orexis and Higher-Order Pathē as Two Articulational
     Hows of a Single Ontological Structure

  The basic affective valence (pathos simpliciter / resonant orexis — the bare
  pleasure/pain, pursue/avoid orientation co-given with αἴσθησις) and the
  higher-order πάθη of Rhet. B 2–11 are *not categorically distinct ontological
  strata*; they are *two articulational concretions of a single ontological
  structure*. The structure is σωτηρία-paschein (preservation-being-affected,
  DA II.5, 417b2–16), articulated by the Met. Δ 21 fourfold (1022b15ff) and
  the Rhet. B 1 three constitutive aspects (μεταβάλλοντες "those-undergoing-
  change"; διαφέρουσι πρὸς τὰς κρίσεις "we-differentiate-ourselves with
  respect to position-takings"; οἷς ἕπεται λύπη καὶ ἡδονή "with which pain
  and pleasure are co-given"). Both concretions satisfy all four Δ 21 moments
  and all three Rhet. B 1 aspects; they differ in *articulational
  concretion*, not in ontological kind. Basic valence is the minimal
  articulation (pre-propositional, recognition-grade); higher-order πάθη are
  the maximal articulation (propositional, doxa-grade).

    Textual anchors:
      • DA II.5, 417b2–16 (the σωτηρία/φθορά distinction in πάσχειν)
      • DA II.11, 424a4ff (αἴσθησις as κριτικόν — Heidegger's own concession at
        GA 18 pp. 126–127 that perception is "definite being-positioned")
      • DA III.7, 431a8–12 (the soul makes "a sort of affirmation or negation"
        — pre-propositional φάσις-character at the perceptual level)
      • DA III.10, 433a14–b30 (orexis as moved mover — already a being-toward)
      • MA 701a25–b1 (the drinking case — basic appetite + recognition →
        action without higher-order πάθη; the textual phenomenon that *forces*
        the articulational distinction)
      • Met. Δ 21, 1022b15–22 (the fourfold — applies at both concretion
        levels)
      • Rhet. II.1, 1378a20–22 (three constitutive aspects — applies at both
        levels)
      • GA 18 p. 125 (hexis as "nothing other than a how of πάθος" — the
        hexis-pathos chiasm that opens the structural space for two hows)
      • GA 18 p. 132 (σωτηρία/φθορά distinction in πάσχειν)
      • Synthesis document Part I (the [T2] synthesis), §§1.1–1.8

    Heideggerian compatibility:
      • Heidegger's single-structure thesis is preserved (one ontological
        structure of πάθος); the chapter refines it by introducing the
        articulational distinction GA 18 leaves implicit. The cut is not a
        categorical cut between two strata but an articulational cut within
        a single structure.

    Common misreading to flag:
      • Treating the cut as ontologically categorical (two kinds of πάθος).
        The synthesis is explicit that there is one ontological structure
        realized at two articulational concretions.
      • Treating "magnitude" as merely intensity. The magnitude-axis is
        multi-dimensional (hedonic intensity, existential weight, cognitive
        articulation), and the *articulational* dimension carries most of the
        structural argument.

  ## How the two theses interlock

  Thesis A (doxa-gate) presupposes Thesis B (two hows of one structure): the
  doxa-gate is the articulational gate from minimal-concretion πάθος to
  maximal-concretion πάθος, not a generative gate creating πάθος from nothing.
  Thesis B in turn requires Thesis A to specify *what* mediates the transition
  from the minimal to the maximal articulation: doxa's propositional yes-saying.
  Neither thesis stands alone; integration must reinforce both together.
