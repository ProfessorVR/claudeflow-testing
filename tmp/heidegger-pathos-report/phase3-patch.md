> The unifying ground of this single-structure thesis is the distinction Aristotle draws at *DA* II.5, 417b2-16 between two senses of πάσχειν (*paschein* "being-affected"): φθορά (*phthora* "destruction"), in which one contrary is extinguished by its opposite, and σωτηρία (*sōtēria* "preservation"), in which a potential is *brought into its actuality*—"a development into one's true self or actuality." The synchronic register concerns the structural transitions at any single moment of *pathos*-actualization: basic valence grounds *doxa*, *doxa* concretizes the higher-order *pathē*, the higher-order *pathē* mobilize action. The diachronic register concerns how prior actualizations modulate subsequent ones: a *pathos*-actualization at *t₁* persists as dispositional ground for the *pathos*-actualization at *t₂*. > Two distinct mechanisms operate within this diachronic register.
