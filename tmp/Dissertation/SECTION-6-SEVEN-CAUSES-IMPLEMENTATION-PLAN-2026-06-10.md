## 1. The integrating thesis (the spine of the reframe)

> **The seven causes of *Rhetorica* I.10 are a taxonomy of the *archē kinēseōs* — the moving origin — of specifically human action.** That moving origin is the same thing as (a) the *efficient*-cause node of the four-cause grid (*Physica* II.3: "the primary source of the change or rest") and (b) "that which originates the movement" in the three-factor schema of *De Anima* III.10. The seven causes differentiate this single origin along two questions:
>
> - **(i) Does the *archē* lie within the agent?** → the voluntary / involuntary division. *Chance* (no determinable *archē*), *nature* (internal but non-orectic *archē*), and *compulsion* (external *archē* overriding the agent's *orexis*) are involuntary; *habit*, *reasoning*, *anger*, and *appetite* lodge the *archē* in the agent.
> - **(ii) If it lies within, what species of source is it?** → the four *orexis*-types. *Appetite* moves through *epithymia* (epithymetic), *anger* through *thymos* (thymotic), *reasoning* through *boulēsis* + *prohairesis* (bouletic + prohairetic), and *habit* through the sedimented *technē*-*hexis* whose diachronic structure subsections 06–07 already supply.

This is the single sentence the recast must earn. It does three things at once: it subordinates the seven causes to the four causes (as a refinement of the efficient/final origin), it routes them through the three-factor schema (which locates the *archē*), and it shows the existing four-type analysis to be the interior articulation of the voluntary branch.

**Why it holds together.** *Physica* II.3 makes the efficient cause the answer to "why did it come into being or change?" The Rhetoric's seven causes are seven answers to exactly that question, posed of human action. The three-factor schema then tells us the efficient origin is itself two-layered — an *unmoved* originator (the realizable good, working as final-cause-functioning-as-efficient-trigger) and a *moved* mover (the *orektikon*). The seven causes specify both layers: *what good* moves (the apparent pleasant, the slight-to-be-avenged, the end wished, the familiar) and *what source* it moves (appetite, *thymos*, reason, habit) — or, in the involuntary cases, that the agent's *orektikon* is bypassed (nature), absent-as-determinate (chance), or overridden (compulsion).

## 2. Three explanatory frames, nested (most general → most action-specific)

The section already deploys all three frames but does not yet stack them explicitly. The recast makes the nesting load-bearing.

**FRAME 1 — Four causes + explanatory why's (general; *Physica* II.3, *Metaphysica* I.3, V.2).**
The grid for *any* event/*kinēsis*. Horizontal: material/formal/efficient/final at one time and place. Vertical: each cause embedded in a chain up to first principles (Causes Notes 49, 117–128). The four explanatory why's (*Physica* II.3) are the **diagnostic instrument**: *Why this rather than that?* (formal), *Why made of this?* (material), *Why did it come to be / change?* (efficient), *For the sake of what?* (final). — *Currently:* partly present in 01-A4 (the four-cause convergence at *praxis*). *Add:* the explanatory-why's as diagnostic + the horizontal/vertical structure.

**FRAME 2 — Three-factor schema of motion (mechanics; *De Anima* III.10, 433b).**
The synchronic anatomy of *one* completed action: unmoved originator (*to orekton* / realizable good) → moved mover (*orektikon*) → moved (the animal body at A₄). This is how the four causes are *gathered* into a single *praxis*: the unmoved originator carries final + formal, the moved mover is efficient, the moved is material. — *Currently:* fully developed in 03-ThreeFactor + 01-A4. *Keep;* add one bridge sentence tying it to Frame 1 and forward to Frame 3.

**FRAME 3 — Seven causes (human-action taxonomy; *Rhetorica* I.10, 1369a5–6).**
The taxonomy of the efficient-and-final *origin* specific to human action, sorted by *archē*-location (vol/invol) and, within the voluntary branch, by *orexis*-species. — *Currently:* absent as such; the voluntary-desiderative interior exists as 04-FourTypes. *Build* the hinge + involuntary branch; *re-seat* the four types under it.

## 4. Three orthogonal axes (the clarification that prevents muddle)

The recast introduces a *new* sorting axis (voluntary/involuntary) into a section that already runs two others. The prose must state that these are **three distinct, largely orthogonal axes**, or the typology will read as muddled. This is the single highest-risk conceptual point in the reframe.

- **Axis A — *archē*-location: voluntary ↔ involuntary** (NEW; the seven-cause spine). Is the moving origin in the agent?
- **Axis B — doxastic ratification: pre-doxastic ↔ doxastic** (existing 05). Does *doxa* take-as-true an evaluatively complex content?
- **Axis C — completion type: *kinēsis*/*poiēsis* ↔ *energeia*/*praxis*** (existing 06; *Metaphysica* IX.6). Does the end lie beyond the doing (in a product) or within it?

The existing draft already asserts B and C are orthogonal to the *orexis*-typology (line 245: "division is by source-desire alone; it is orthogonal to a second distinction — whether the action's end lies beyond it... or is immanent"). The recast must **add Axis A and state its interactions**, the most important of which is:

> **Voluntary/involuntary is graded, but addiction does not cross into compulsion.** Aristotelian compulsion (*bia*) requires an *external* mover, the agent contributing nothing (NE III.1). A runaway appetitive *hexis* — addiction — keeps its *archē* **inside** the agent, so however much it overpowers reason it stays on the voluntary side, and the agent remains responsible for the *hexis* he has cultivated and so for how things appear to him (NE III.5, 1114a31–b3, already quoted at 283). The genuine grading the section needs is the NE III.1 "mixed-actions" zone (external pressure met by internal assent), not a collapse of entrenched appetite into compulsion. Axis A interacts with Axis C without merging into it: an addiction is a *praxis*-degrading vicious *hexis* (row 7 + the diachronic loop), not an instance of cause 3.

The payoff is not a dramatic identification but a disciplined refusal: holding the three axes apart is what lets the section keep the addict responsible (Axis A: voluntary, internal *archē*) while still explaining how reason is overpowered (Axis C + the loop) — rather than excusing it as compulsion. That refusal is where the seven-cause frame and the *hexis*/diachronic frame prove to be the same analysis seen from two angles.

## 6. The involuntary branch — design notes (so it earns its length)

The risk with chance/nature/compulsion is padding. The defense: **each marks a distinct necessary condition of voluntary *praxis* by lacking it**, so the involuntary branch is a *via negativa* definition of what the voluntary branch supplies.

- Chance lacks a **determinate** *archē*. → *Praxis* needs the realizable good *as its origin*, not as a coincidence.
- Nature lacks an **orectic** *archē*. → *Praxis* needs the *orektikon* in the chain, not merely an internal principle.
- Compulsion has its *archē* **outside or against** the *orektikon*. → *Praxis* needs the internal *orexis* to be the *un-overridden* mover.

Per the user directive (2026-06-10), keep this whole branch compact; do not dwell. Only one hinge earns space:
- **nature ↔ habit:** "habit is like nature" is Aristotle's own bridge; it licenses treating cultivated *hexis* (cause 4) as quasi-natural while remaining *up to us* in its genesis (NE III.5 1114a31–b3, quoted at 283 — responsibility for one's *hexis* and so for how things appear). This is the transition into the voluntary branch.
- **compulsion stays genuine *bia*.** Addiction/runaway *hexis* is NOT seated here: its *archē* is internal, so it remains voluntary (row 7 + 07), an entrenched intemperance, not the Rhetoric's compulsion. Marking this *non*-identity is the only work compulsion needs to do in this section.

## 7. Diachronic *hexis*/*technē* + "habit-as-cause" integration (the user-named focus)

The user named "the diachronic nature of *hexis* and *technē*" as a focus. The reframe gives it a sharpened structural role:

1. **Habit is the diachronically-defined cause.** Of the seven, only habit is constituted by *repetition over time*. So the seven-cause frame *requires* the diachronic analysis (06–07) to cash out one of its own members. 06–07 are no longer an appendix to the typology; they are the engine of cause #4.
2. **The *technē* / ethical-*hexis* bivalence maps onto Axis C.** *Technē*-*hexis* (cause-4-as-routine) produces *poiēsis*/*kinēsis* completions (end-beyond, deliberation-reducing); ethical-*hexis* produces *praxis*/*energeia* completions (end-internal, openness-sustaining). The *Metaphysica* IX.6 tense-test (already at 321) is the seam. Make the mapping explicit.
3. **The loop is what continuously *re-causes* habit and what entrenches vicious appetite.** Each completed A₄ sediments back (07), reconstituting the *hexis* that row 4 names and, in the pathological limit, hardening appetite into addiction — an intemperate *hexis* that remains *voluntary* (internal *archē*, NE III.5), never the Rhetoric's compulsion. This closes the circuit between the seven-cause frame (synchronic origin) and the diachronic loop (origin-of-the-origin).
