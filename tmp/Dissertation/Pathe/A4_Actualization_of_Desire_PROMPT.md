THESIS THE CONCLUSION MUST EXECUTE
====================================================================

The actualization chain terminates at $A_4$, the completed action ($\pi\rho\tilde{\alpha}\xi\iota\varsigma$), reached through the *orektikon* transition $M_3 \rightarrow M_4$ in which desire ($\textit{orexis}$) becomes overt motion. The *orektikon* transition is one transition with three structurally distinct concretions, corresponding to Aristotle's threefold typology of action: (1) **simple appetition**, in which appetite responds directly to a *phantasma* of the immediately present pleasant or painful object without engaging *doxa* or the higher-order *pathē*; (2) **habitual-rational action**, in which *doxa* fully operates --- typically as a settled *hexis* furnishing the universal premise of a practical syllogism --- but the *doxa*'s content does not engage the evaluative categories of the *Rhetoric*, and so no higher-order *pathē* concretize; (3) **evaluatively complex action**, in which *doxa* engages the *Rhetoric*'s evaluative categories (slight, threat, undeserved suffering, public falling-short), the higher-order *pathē* concretize, and the somatic preparation internal to $M_3 \rightarrow M_4$ takes the specific form the emotion calls for. Across all three types, the kinetic-roles spine of *De Anima* III.10 is preserved: *orexis* is the moved mover, the realizable good is the unmoved originator, the animal via the bodily instrument is that which is moved. What varies is *orexis*'s internal complexity: in (1) it is generic appetite; in (2) it is rationally articulated by settled *doxa* without emotional concretion; in (3) it is the emotion-desire composite. *Hexis* operates differently in (2) and (3): in (2) it enables rapid action through internalized rational structures; in (3) it modulates which *pathē* arise and with what intensity (the diachronic story of §1.7). The chain closes at $A_4$ but does not stop: the completed action transforms the perceptual environment, generating a new $A_0$ for a new cycle. The recursivity is what makes the chain a chain rather than a sequence.
