## 1. BACKGROUND — what this revision fixes

Part II's central concept was being described three incompatible ways: as "one **union** with two **poles**," as "**two unions**" (with one "the **deeper**"), and (in the section title) as "The Two Unions." Through the consult the author settled the metaphysics definitively. The core decisions:

1. It is **not** a "union" (that word implies two priors joined) and **not** "two poles" (that implies parts bolted onto an axis). Both contradict the **equiprimordiality** (Heidegger, *Gleichursprünglichkeit*) of being-in-the-world.
2. It **is** one thing — **rhetorical incorporation = true being-in-a-virtual-world** — that is **one in substance, dual in being**: world-disclosedness and co-disclosedness. This is the exact structural twin of Part I's **resonant *kinēsis*** (one in substrate, two in being: resonant *aisthēma* + resonant *epithymia*).
3. It is an ***energeia*** — **complete, binary, admits no degree**. Anything short of complete is **different in kind** (mere involvement, spectatorship), never "partial incorporation."
4. There is **no "deeper"** disclosedness; the two are co-equal and both required.

The full locked framework is §2 below; the specific edits are §3.

## 2. THE LOCKED FRAMEWORK (conceptual ground truth — do not deviate)

**2.1 Naming / ontology.**
- Rhetorical incorporation = **true being-in-a-virtual-world**: one equiprimordial actuality, **one in substance, dual in being** — world-disclosedness (*Welterschlossenheit*) and co-disclosedness (*Miterschlossenheit*).
- **Retire "union" entirely** (never "union," never "two unions"). **Retire "pole(s)" entirely** — for the disclosednesses AND for the agent/world relation AND for the medium (see 2.7).
- Acceptable formula variants (author's own): "**one in substance, dual in being**," "**one in substance but two in being**," "**one in substance but different in being**," "**one in substrate, different in being**" (this last is Part I's phrase; keep it as the Aristotelian ground).
- **Never "deeper"** (no ranking of the two disclosednesses).
- **Do NOT use "being-at-work"** (a Sachs rendering of *energeia*; the author does not use Sachs). Render *energeia* as "actuality"/"activity" or leave untranslated.

**2.2 Category.** Rhetorical incorporation is an ***energeia*** — **complete, binary, no degree**. The **actualization-of-desire chain is the *kinēsis*** (the process that reaches it); rhetorical incorporation is the actuality that chain achieves and sediments as *hexis*. Short of complete = **different in kind** (mere involvement, spectatorship), not partial incorporation. (This maps onto Part I §§6–7's *praxis*/*energeia* vs *poiēsis*/*kinēsis* distinction — a *praxis*-like actuality whose *telos* is immanent, not a *poiēsis* toward a product.)

**2.3 Co-disclosedness.** = reciprocal being-with the entities of the world — the player **can move and be-moved by them** (animals, humans): Heidegger's social element (*Mitsein*). It comes in **modes** (different in kind), **not degrees**.

**2.4 Fragility & phantasia.** Rhetorical incorporation is **fragile but not brittle.** Phantasia **CAN** (contingently, person-dependent — a barking dog may or may not break it) **sustain** it by filling gaps and integrating intrusions. It **breaks reliably only when the world reveals itself as non-reciprocal** — the **broken-tool structure** (Heidegger, *BT* §16: the hammer shows itself as a thing only when it breaks). This is grounded in the hammer material already in v3 (dwelling as the tool's withdrawal, §245/§333); the new application is the *breaking*: when the world breaks its reciprocity, its entities unconceal as mere things.

**2.5 Phantasia-dependence.** Rhetorical incorporation **depends on** phantasia (the presenting power that discloses the world as dwelt-in and its entities as reciprocal agents; enables the *doxa*/take-as-true; crystallizes the emotion; sustains against disruption). Its **function** is now load-bearing (esp. in the fragility/breaking account); a **full theory of phantasia's role remains "further work"** — keep it a **gesture**, do NOT design out a full account (author standing rule: future-work = gestures only).

**2.6 Terminology lock — "incorporation".** **Bare "incorporation" = Calleja's concept ONLY** (his model, his definition, direct references/quotes). **The author's concept is ALWAYS "rhetorical incorporation."** Every bare "incorporation" referring to the author's concept must become "rhetorical incorporation." This is a dissertation-wide disambiguation sweep (Category E below).

**2.7 The medium.** The **principle/route is medium-invariant** — every case of rhetorical incorporation requires **both** world- and co-disclosedness. The **medium conditions the design STRATEGY/APPLICATION** by which a world reaches them, **not the principle** ("VR's physical locomotion entails different design than 2D — different in application, not in principle"). **VR and 2D are two DIFFERENT MEDIA for interactive media — NOT two ends of one spectrum.** Therefore **retire "poles of the medium," "opposite poles," AND "near edge / far edge."** And: it is **wrong to say "the medium sets the route"** — the medium conditions the *strategy*; the route (world/co-disclosedness) is invariant.

**2.8 G&G reading (the analytic revision).** *Gnomes & Goblins* **DOES achieve** rhetorical incorporation. Evidence: the goblins **are reciprocal** — they scamper in realistic fear from an abrupt approach (moved by the player) — and the miniaturization produces profound phenomenological affect. **Then the design breaks down** post-miniaturization (it is a **demo**): the only remaining agency is **ringing the bell to teleport**, and the player can no longer move or be moved by anything else. The goblins become **non-reciprocal, unconcealing as mere things (like an un-ready-to-hand rock)** — the broken-tool structure — and rhetorical incorporation **breaks**. This is **NOT** a medium-principled withholding of co-disclosedness (the current "co-disclosedness at its threshold / given as a preview" reading is **wrong** and must go).
- **Arc (author's decision C1 = option i):** built → **peaked and disclosed** at the miniaturization (KEEP §395's "the rupture is not the enemy of incorporation but its **completion**" — the disclosure/ownability thesis stands) → **then broken** by the demo's design collapse (ADD this as fragility caught in the act). G&G becomes the one case that shows rhetorical incorporation **achieved *and* broken**.

**2.9 Method verdicts (consequence of 2.2).** The graded language must go binary: *achieved* = the state (on); *merely-involved* = different in kind (Calleja's own involvement-vs-incorporation); *ruptured* = the fragility case (broken); **drop/reread "partial"** (there is no partial rhetorical incorporation). The R1–R7 criteria read as **threshold conditions** (Calleja's own *necessary* conditions — R3 simultaneity, R4 "without which incorporation cannot take place"), a gate, not a sliding scale — which the binary view fits better than "graded."
