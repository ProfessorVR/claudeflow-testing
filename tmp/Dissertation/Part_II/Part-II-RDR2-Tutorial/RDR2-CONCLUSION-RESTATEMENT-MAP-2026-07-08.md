## R-11 — The definition ([D] ¶11)

**[D] ¶11:** "So the definition can now be stated in full. Ontological violence is violence done at the level of the \textit{how} of being: an engineered seizure of the actualization of desire in which a world uses a player's own cultivated disposition as the trigger, inverts the player from agent to patient in the destructive mode of being-affected, withholds the event's content so that the player's \textit{phantasia} must author it, and withholds the verdict so that the player's \textit{doxa} must judge it---returning agency only as a ledger and leaving the transformation to sediment where nothing on the screen can reach.
