## 0b. Wording traps caught by verification (do NOT use the index glosses)

- **Moser:** "a crash of worlds" is her **title only** — body prose says "doubled structure" / "worlds collide." Her geography is **"a shack on the outskirts of the city of Saint Denis"** (not "outside Saint Denis"). Her verb is "load a previous save," never "reload." Her phrase is "safe experimental **environment**," not "space."
- **McEvoy:** her verb is "stifle**s**"; "moral fence" is the index's gloss — her words are "a fence or barrier" (once, pp. 12–13). "Fourth dimension" block is Goldberg qtd. in McEvoy — second-hand.
- **Ruffino:** "a consequence with no actions" is a **section heading** (346); running prose = "the possibility that consequences could manifest with no preceding actions" (351). "White, able-bodied male character**s**" (plural). The Owen line is embedded quotation; the Muriel & Crawford line is Ruffino's own sentence with a parenthetical cite.
- **Tuominen:** "mainstream but **still** radically progressive" (60) — the "still" is required. His freedom-illusory sentences gloss Mitchell (2017) — cite as Tuominen-following-Mitchell. **He never discusses the tutorial/opening hours** — attribute no tutorial claim to him.
- **Bagnoli:** the KKK exception is **stronger** than the index gloss — killing Klansmen *grants* honor. "Feminism, anti-racism, ecology" is a paraphrase; his verbatim triad is "feminism, race, and animal condition" (7). Two embedded-attribution chains: arithmetic-morality = Bogost qtd. in Bagnoli (49); morality-inscribed-in-character = Sicart qtd. in Bogost qtd. in Bagnoli (48).
- **Westerside & Holopainen:** they never name Horseshoe Overlook as "home" (only "the camp"); their Calleja invocation is a single "cf." citation.
