## Thesis-statement
**Incorporation — and through it, ontological change — is reachable in BOTH 2D-screen and VR** (and, more broadly, in any worldly interaction: film, books, images, animals, persons, virtual/phantasmatic artifacts). VR and 2D do **not** differ in *whether* they can incorporate the user, but in the **route**. The **primary phenomenological differentiator is the *data discrepancy*** (introduced in the metaphysics of virtual/phantasmatic things): the gap between the sensorial data a medium presents and the full sensorial field of the non-virtual referent. **Phantasia fills that gap.** A 2D screen has a *larger* data discrepancy → demands *more* phantasia gap-filling (and more design-side cueing); VR a *smaller* one → demands *less*. **Both reach full incorporation because phantasia scales to the gap.** Organized by **veri(dis)similitude**: data + phantasia-fill must make the world verisimilar enough to throw the user in, and dissimilar enough that a rupture lands with ontological force.

> **Technology-relative, not essential** (metaphysics): if the sensorium were fully injected — same data + interactive potentiality as the non-virtual — the VR/non-virtual *experiential* gap would close (though they remain different *kinds*). So the VR↔2D differential is a **current-technology difference of degree along the data axis, not a ceiling.**

## The spine — data discrepancy → phantasia-load → incorporation
1. Every medium presents a bounded **bandwidth of sensorial data** (flat photo < documentary < film < 2D game < VR — the metaphysics' D-Day gradient). More data → "more real"/more viscerally felt.
2. The shortfall vs. the full sensorial field = the **data discrepancy**.
3. **Phantasia fills the discrepancy** — conscripting the perceptual backlog (prior "appleness") to (a) supply missing sensory dimensions and (b) subtract the surrounding real world.
4. **Incorporation** is achieved when phantasia has closed the discrepancy enough to enclose the user. ⇒ **phantasia-load is inversely proportional to the medium's data supply.**
5. ⇒ VR (small discrepancy) needs less phantasmatic + design labor for the same incorporation; 2D (large discrepancy) needs more. Neither is barred from full incorporation or full ontological effect.

## The differential, axis by axis (each a consequence of the data discrepancy)
| axis (RODA locus) | 2D screen (RDR2) | VR (G&G) | the real difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Horizon enclosure (A0)** | *phantasmatic subtraction* — phantasia withdraws the still-present room/bezel from attention | *sensory supplanting* — HMD occupies/replaces the sensory channels (small discrepancy at the horizon) | *means* of enclosure; how forcibly the surround is excluded — not whether enclosure is possible |
| **Perceptual-motor coupling (A1/A4)** | *symbolic only* — convention-mapped button/stick; phantasia supplies the felt body | *mixed* — motion-tracked gesture (more motor data, mimetic→symbiotic for reach/locomotion) **+ retained symbolic** (buttons, triggers) | how much motor data is *supplied* vs. *conscripted*; VR does **not** eliminate symbolic mediation |
| **Embodiment (R2)** | *real via strong incorporation* — the instinctive flinch/duck at an on-screen threat; phantasia supplies the body | *literal/eased* — bodily data supplied directly (tracked body) | **who supplies the body** — phantasia vs. the medium; VR eases/literalizes R2, does not monopolize it |
| **Phantasia load** | **high** — large discrepancy to close | **low** — small discrepancy | the dependent variable (the felt "more work" on a screen) |
| **Ontological reach** | **full** | **full** | **no ceiling on either** — the open question is *how*, not *whether* |
| **Rupture / intrusion** | hardware (dead/broken controller, power-cut) + external intrusion (people, animals, sound) — *easier* (room stays present) | hardware (cable-tether limit, HMD slip, screen-door, power-cut) + external intrusion *harder* (the data-supplanting that shrinks the discrepancy also seals the real world out) | both rupture via hardware & intrusion; VR resists intrusion more but adds its own surfaces (the un-ready-to-hand tether — the hammer noticed only when it breaks) |

## How it sets up the two analyses
- It makes **G&G (small discrepancy, low phantasia-load)** and **RDR2 (large discrepancy, high phantasia-load)** a **controlled comparison that isolates phantasia's gap-filling work**: vary the data discrepancy, watch phantasia's labor vary. *That* — not "VR is more powerful" — is why the pairing earns its place, and it makes the comparison a study *of phantasia* (Part I's title).
- The **Sonny scene** then asks the open question: *how* does a high-discrepancy 2D medium incorporate the player strongly enough that a rupture registers ontologically — via **phantasia-conscription** (withheld assault content authored by settled *doxai*: adult≠child), via **bia** (the agency-stripping cutscene / agent→patient inversion), via the **embodied uptake** prior incorporation installed, or their interaction? **Mechanism is the finding, not the premise.**

## Caveats (don't overclaim)
- Reachability is **symmetric**; the difference is **data-discrepancy magnitude → phantasia-load**, plus rupture texture. Not a wall.
- The data discrepancy is **technology-relative** (the metaphysics' sensorium-injection limit case).
- "Screen-based" = flat-display, controller-input games (the dissertation's RDR2 cases); VR = the G&G counterpoint.
- Carry-over evidence = a **single-subject** phenomenological report (the author's AWS self-experiment) — strong, not controlled.
- **Metaphysics → methodology seam:** the data discrepancy is *introduced* in the metaphysics (virtual vs. non-virtual; the media gradient) and *deployed* here (the VR/2D phantasia-load) — which reinforces **metaphysics-first** ordering (§4a).
