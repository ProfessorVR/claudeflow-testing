## PART B — Current RODA / Part II key terminology (the target vocabulary, v9)

**B1. The spine.** the **actualization of desire**; the **A₀→A₄ chain** — A₀ designed *Umwelt* / dwelling-horizon · A₁ completed perception + dual residue (***resonant aisthēma + resonant epithymia*** = the *resonant kinēsis*) · A₂ the ***phantasma*** proper (pivot; bears identification) · A₃ completed cognition + the committal ***doxa*** · A₄ completed action (*praxis*), recursive → sediments into ***hexis***.

**B2. The central event.** ***rhetorical incorporation*** — the two-poled substantial union ("one in substrate, different in being"):
- **world-disclosedness** = ***incorporation*** (re-grounded from Calleja) = the player one-with the world she acts *in* = **dwelling** in the designed *Umwelt* (assessed by **R1–R7**).
- **co-disclosedness** = ***consubstantiation*** (re-grounded from Burke) = the player one-with the avatar she acts *as* / the others she acts *with* = the *doxa*-ratified **"WE."**

**B3. The desire layer.** ***orexis*** (three species → three types): **epithymia → Type 1 epithymetic · thymos → Type 2 thymotic · boulēsis → Type 3 bouletic**; ***prohairesis*** = the **deliberative completion of the bouletic** (choice of means), **not a Type 4**. ***orekton*** = the object of desire (unmoved originator).

**B4. Disposition & its two modes.** ***hexis*** (dispositional ground) · **habit (*ethos*)** = the repetition-tendency that *cultivates* a *hexis* (**habit ≠ hexis**) · **dual-*hexis*** bivalence: ***technē*-hexis** (production/*poiēsis*, transparent means-end) vs **ethical *hexis*** (*praxis*/*aretē*, holding-oneself-open) · **temporal sedimentation** (the synchronic↔diachronic *hexis* spiral).

**B5. The causal frame.** **four causes** (two analyses — designer's *making* / player's *playing* — hinged at the **producing-art / using-art** relation) · **three-factor schema** (unmoved originator → moved mover → moved) · **seven causes** (*Rhet.* I.10) — three structures of *widening grain*.

**B6. Force, capture, rupture.** ***bia*** = compulsion/force (external *archē*; the forced-cutscene limit; the non-agent branch) — **≠ addiction, ≠ *technē*-capture** · ***technē*-capture** = the hexis-runaway where the wished-end drops out (grind/dark-pattern) · **push-at-the-*bia*-limit** · **rupture** (agency-rupture / social-rupture / the never-stilled chronic slippage) · **foreclosed rupture** (§6 #9 — the *Gestell* limit, the disruption/change axis).

**B7. The ecological ground.** **dwelling** (Rickert, sublated) · **bethinged** (gathered by things) · **recalcitrance** (*thing* vs *object* — the design-diagnostic of whether a world affords dwelling) · the **dwelling-horizon** at A₀.

**B8. The metaphysics firewall.** **three kinds** — non-virtual / virtual / phantasmic (each a dependence-profile) · **genus = virtual object** (the *as-if*, *virtualis* = potency) · the ***phantasma* = the ORIGINARY virtual object**, VR its late technological prosthesis · **THE FIREWALL: experiential convergence ≠ kind-identity** · **existence-fragility** (renderer-dependence; immersion-break = ontological cessation event).

**B9. The retained instrument.** **veri(dis)similitude** (the VR↔screen differential lever) · the **virtual→actual carry-over** (ontological change; the capstone).

**B10. Sublated vocabularies (used only where they illuminate a chain-stage).** Calleja **channels** — Kinesthetic · Spatial · Shared · Narrative (**push/pull**, **alterbiography**) · Affective · Ludic; **R1–R7** incorporation differentiator. Burke **rhetoric of motive** — pentad/**ratios** (motive-attribution) · **identification** (*phantasma*-borne) · **attitude** (read as *hexeis*) · social-motive cluster (hierarchy/mystery/courtship; victimage/scapegoat). **Three orthogonal axes** (TYPE / TERMINUS / *doxa*-RATIFICATION). **Three magnitude axes** (hedonic intensity / existential weight / cognitive articulation).
