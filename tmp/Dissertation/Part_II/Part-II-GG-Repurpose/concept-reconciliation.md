## §1 — "Compulsion" (the highest-priority disambiguation) ⚠
The thesis (p.36) calls the goblin's programmed responses **"compulsive motion."** This collides with **two** technical senses the dissertation must keep distinct (your memory already flags compulsion = *bia* ≠ addiction):
1. ***bia*** = external **forced, involuntary** motion that drags an agent from the agent-pole to the patient-pole (e.g., an unskippable cutscene). **The goblin's behavior is NOT this.**
2. ***technē*-capture** = a habituated runaway where the wished-end drops out (grind/dark-pattern). **The goblin's behavior is NOT this either.**
3. The goblin's "compulsive motion" is, precisely, **scripted efficient causation of an NPC** — a *programmed responsiveness* whose **motive belongs to the hidden Agent (the programmer)**, in Burke's terms. It is *necessity* from the player's point of view, but it is neither *bia* (it forces nothing on the player) nor a hexis-regression (the goblin has no hexis).
- **Fix:** in Part II, **rename** the goblin's "compulsive motion" → **"programmed responsiveness"** (or "scripted efficient causation"), and reserve **"compulsion"** for the player-side *bia*/*technē*-capture senses. This also sharpens the **superficial-agency** reading (the fruit-exchange's *delayed* programmed retrieval, M05, is the cleanest in-artifact evidence).

## §2 — "Fallenness" vs incorporation: a fall or an achievement?
Tension: the thesis (B-P36) says a *successful* true-presence is a **"fallen," inauthentic** mode; RODA tends to treat **incorporation neutrally** (an achieved union).
- **Reconciliation (a synthesis, not a choice):** incorporation **is** an achievement of union **that is inauthentic *by default* (fallen) until ruptured**. The player's absorbed dwelling is genuine union *and* an uncritical, "they"-conditioned absorption — and it is precisely the **rupture** (anxiety/miniaturization) that **individualizes** her (B-C22), pulling her from the "they" and disclosing authenticity as a possibility.
- **Payoff for RODA:** this gives the union pole a **two-valued** character (fallen-union → ruptured → authentic-re-projection) and makes **rupture the hinge between them** — which is exactly the methodological role RODA already assigns rupture. So mark the union/incorporation node as **"inauthentic-until-ruptured,"** and treat the productive rupture as the move to critical/authentic dwelling. (This is a genuine *enrichment* RODA gains from the thesis, not just a reconciliation.)

## §3 — "True presence / Being-in-a-virtual-world-in-the-world" vs incorporation/dwelling
- The coinage is **functionally RODA's incorporation** (Calleja) re-described in Heideggerese, with **"dwelling"** being the very term RODA inherits from **Rickert**. 
- **Action: supersede the Dasein scaffolding** (don't carry the full Being-in-the-world apparatus into Part II as the *frame*), but **keep the insight** — incorporation's experiential criterion = **equipment-transparency + dissolved subject/object** (the craftsman passage, B-P19, kept verbatim). 
- Collapse the **two registers** (Heideggerian "Being-in-a-virtual-world-in-the-world" and presence-research "true presence") into the single RODA term **rhetorical incorporation**, noting the dwelling (Rickert) and involvement (Calleja) lineages where each does work.
