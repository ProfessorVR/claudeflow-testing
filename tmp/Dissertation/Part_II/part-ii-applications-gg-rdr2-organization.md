## The scheme (user, 2026-06-27)
A **set of analysis sections** covering the two artifacts in parallel and then comparing them, preceded by an **Introduction** to the set.

- **Introduction** — introduces the paired set of analyses (both artifacts; the comparative VR↔2D frame).
- **1.1 — G&G: the demo as tutorial.** How the demo *begins incorporating the player's being-in-the-world into a being-in-the-virtual-world*. **Primarily discovery-based**: the player must discover picking up the candle, lighting it to start the game, which objects can be interacted with, etc. — all of which **naturalizes the mechanics** for moving and interacting in that world.
- **1.2 — G&G: the miniaturization / disruption analysis.**
- **2.1 — RDR2: the tutorial analysis.** How the design **naturalizes mechanics** and introduces **setting**, **narrative**, etc. **Must include a clear chunk on the LINEAR start** (the player is not allowed to explore at will): this serves (a) as a crucial **contrast between the linear introduction and the open world afterwards** in terms of player experience, and (b) as the **technical means of forcing the player through the tutorial** to learn the systems.
- **2.2 — RDR2: the rape-scene disruption / ontological change.**
- **Section 3 — VR ↔ 2D differential analysis (deep dive).** A focused, **deliberately not-overly-long** comparison built on Sections 1–2: it notes the **differences in player experience** between the two media types (VR / G&G vs 2D-screen / RDR2) and draws out the **insight generated from that comparison**. The applied payoff of the pairing — the two analyses set against each other to isolate what changes with the medium and what does not.

**Parallel logic + arc:** 1.1/2.1 = tutorial (incorporation + naturalization of mechanics); 1.2/2.2 = disruption → ontological change; **Section 3 = the comparison the parallel was built for.** The arc is: analyze G&G (1) → analyze RDR2 (2) → compare (3). The pairing makes the VR (G&G, small data-discrepancy) vs 2D-screen (RDR2, large discrepancy) differential concrete, and Section 3 reads the difference off the two cases.

## Section 3 — the differential deep-dive (content)
Concise; draws on `tmp/Dissertation/Part_II/Part-II-GG-Repurpose/vr-vs-screen-differential.md` + `corpus/index/Red Dead Redemption 2 (Salvo Playthrough)/rdr2-vs-gg-differential.md`. Reads the **player-experience difference** off the two completed analyses:
- **Horizon enclosure:** VR *sensory-supplants* the surround (the HMD occupies the channels); 2D *phantasmatically subtracts* the still-present room.
- **Body / perceptual-motor:** VR largely *supplies* the body and motion (tracked; mixed motion + symbolic input); 2D *conscripts* the felt body via *phantasia* (symbolic input only).
- **Phantasia load:** **low** in VR (small data-discrepancy), **high** in 2D (large discrepancy) — the dependent variable, the felt "more work" on a screen.
- **Rupture texture:** VR resists external intrusion (the supplanting seals the room out) but adds its own break-surfaces (the tether / HMD, noticed only when it fails); 2D ruptures more easily (the room stays present).
- **The insight (the payoff):** incorporation — and the ontological change it can carry — is reachable from **both**; they differ in **route, not ceiling**. Varying the data-discrepancy and watching *phantasia*'s labor vary makes the pairing a controlled study **of phantasia** (Part I's titular object). *Mechanism is the finding, not "VR is more powerful."*
- **Length:** keep it tight — a differential reading off the two cases, not a third full case study.
