## 0. Genus + unifying principle

- **A₄ genus** = **"the actualization of desire."** NOT "praxis," NOT "action" simpliciter. ("Completed action" allowed only as loose English umbrella, explicitly ≠ *praxis*.)
- **Unifying principle of the typology**: the **mode of givenness of the realizable good** (*to orekton / to prakton agathon*, the unmoved originator of the three-factor schema, *DA* III.10 433b10–12 *(to-confirm)*). All action is driven by the realizable good; the *types* differ by **how the good is given**. The cognitive driver, the *doxa*-role, and the terminus all fall out of this one variable. **Mixed cases = composite givenness** (not exceptions to be footnoted).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Recognition-channel note (per *DMA* 701a32–33):** "sense **or** imagination **or** thought" is the minor-premise "this is X" function — *orthogonal to type*. The type is fixed by the good's-givenness + the orectic species, NOT by which channel recognizes. (Thought recognizing a *present* good stays appetitive; what makes it poiēsis is the good being *absent and projected*.)

## 2. Decisions (final form)

- **D1.** Genus = "the actualization of desire." *[revises GLOSSARY `praxis`]*
- **D2.** Keep ***poiēsis*** as a core term. **No collision** with §1.1 — a *continuity*: *poiēsis* = the activity whose end/actuality lies in the **other** (patient/product), from §1.1's general agency-structure (*Physics* III.3, actuality-in-the-patient) through §1.5's craft-production (*NE* VI.4; *Physics* II.1, artifacts lack an internal *archē*). §1.5's *poiēsis* merely *adds* determinations (deliberate, *technē*-governed, desire-driven, artifact-yielding). One continuity-sentence flags it.
- **D3.** Cultivated dispositions: ***technē*** (productive; Aristotle's own term — a *hexis meta logou poiētikē*, *NE* VI.4) vs **ethical hexis / virtue** (praxis-disposition; the *how-of-standing-in-a-(higher-order)-pathos*; courage, temperance). ***phronēsis*** = the directive intellectual partner of the ethical hexis (*hexis praktikē meta logou*, *NE* VI.5). **RETIRE "*technē*-hexis" / "*praxis*-hexis."** State the *NE* II.1 cultivated-through-doing parallel (builders by building; the brave by brave acts) as what unites them without assimilating production to ethical action.
- **D4.** Homology = **identity-of-structure**: *praxis* : *poiēsis* :: *energeia* : *kinēsis* (*Met.* IX.6, 1048b18–36). The chain's terminal actualization A₄ is itself either an *energeia* (praxis, self-contained) or a *kinēsis* (poiēsis, end-beyond). **Closes the arc with §1.1** (*kinēsis* = *energeia atelēs*). *(Scholarly nuance: nod to the Ackrill puzzles about whether every praxis is an energeia — present as the standard mapping, not a clean identity.)*
- **D5.** ***Doxa* is NOT a hexis; drop "settled *doxa*."** There is no ontological divide between a *doxa* and a "settled *doxa*" — every *doxa* is held-as-true until revised; its truth/falsity is fixed by reality, its persistence/revision by what appears convincing to the holder. So all *doxa* is just *doxa* (a standing/long-held *doxa* is the same kind, merely more entrenched). *Hexeis* (technē, the ethical virtues) are stable cultivated dispositions that **condition which *doxai* an agent holds and how tenaciously** (Nussbaum, note 9: "the way the end appears depends on what sort of man he is"), but a *doxa* is a propositional taking-as-true, not a disposition. **This corrects the diagram's Q4 claim that "settled *doxai* are a doxastic species of hexis"** (rejected) while strengthening its anti-conflation point (*doxa* is absent from the truth-attaining *hexeis* of *NE* VI.3 precisely because it "may be false"; *Cat.* 8b25–9a13). §1.5's pervasive "settled *doxa*"/"settled *doxai*" usages = PHASE 3 correction targets (drop "settled"); §1.4 = logged follow-up.
- **D6.** Typology carved by the **mode of givenness of the realizable good** (§0).
- **D7.** Three types **named by terminus**: appetitive / poiēsis / praxis (§1 table).
- **D8.** **No deliberative/discursive action without *doxa*** (Q3 resolution): the practical-syllogism universal premise *is* a **held/standing *doxa*** — a preformed opinion deliberation draws on, NOT a "species of hexis" (per D5) — and there is no *doxa*-bypass route. (If the universal were *epistēmē* of the necessary, it would not be a *practical* premise — deliberation is about the contingent.) Only appetitive action is pre-doxastic.
- **D9.** **A3 termination behavior**: pure *noēsis* self-encloses (telos = the contemplative activity); **speculative *phantasia*** terminates but is discursive/*phantasma*-dependent (the geometer working a proof on an imagined triangle); **deliberative *phantasia*** alone is intrinsically ordered to A₄. The theoretical mind "moves nothing" (*DA* III.9–10). **Doxastic ratification converts presented content into a *pursued* good.** **Memory is multivalent** — it can (i) terminate in idle recollection, (ii) feed discursive/noetic operations, OR (iii) **feed evaluative/pathetic action by re-presenting content that was doxastically ratified *in the past* (the original slight-as-judged + its anger), carried with its affective charge in the memory.** Such memory-driven emotional action does NOT bypass *doxa* in general (D8 holds): the *doxa* is **inherited** from the past episode, not freshly formed — and on re-presentation it is itself reaffirmable or revisable (D5). Memory is thus a key carrier of the **diachronic loop into synchronic action**.
- **D10.** "doxa-gate" **RETIRED** → **"doxastic ratification"** (the affirmative act) + **"content-conditional"** (the separate fact: higher-order *pathē* arise only when ratified content is evaluatively complex). Located as *doxa*'s **orthogonal ratification of the A3 *phantasma*-content** (the "something to say yes to").
- **D11.** ***Doxa* can be *pathē*-neutral** (= poiēsis / Type 2). "*pathē*-neutral" = free of **higher-order** *pathē* (*Rhetoric* II), NOT of all being-affected: broad receptivity (*paschein*) underlies ALL types incl. *technē*'s craft-responsiveness; basic hedonic tonality persists. *pathē* → *doxa*, but *doxa* ↛ *pathē*.
- **D12.** A₄→hexeis feedback = **GRADED**: praxis → ethical hexis/virtue (+ *phronēsis*); poiēsis → *technē*; appetitive → *empeiría* (*Met.* A.1, 980b28–981a) + temperance/intemperance (*NE* III.10–12). No action disposition-neutral; sedimentation differentiated. The loop = the "frequently of repetition" (BCAP p. 129; §1.3 anchor). The loop also **reaffirms or revises the agent's held *doxai*** — distinct from, and alongside, hexis-sedimentation (per D5).
- **D13.** **Four causes**: light in §1.5 (A₄ as the four-causal convergence; tighten §1's *formal*-cause assignment); reserve the full development + **Burke-pentad bridge** for the next chapter.
- **D14.** **Node notation/labels**: §1.5 chain-recap uses canonical **A₀** (dyad: sensible object + *aisthētikon* within the motion-time horizon) + **M₂→A₃ = "Cognitive Motion"** (Task #20); `$A_n$` math-mode normalization is mechanical (PHASE 5).

## 3. Pathos / hexis structure (grounds D3, D11, D12)

- **Asymmetric co-implication**: the *pathos* (fear) is the *condition of possibility* of the hexis (courage); courage = the cultivated *how-of-standing-in-fear*, not added from outside (BCAP p. 125: "*hexis* is nothing other than a *how* of *pathos*…"; *NE* III.6–7: courage = the mean re fear). Courage presupposes fear, not vice versa.
- **Broad vs. higher-order *pathos***: broad being-affected (*paschein* / *dechesthai*) underlies *all* action + *all* dispositions — incl. *technē*'s responsiveness to the material (a cultivated *poiēsis*/*pathēsis* reciprocity). **Higher-order *pathē*** (*Rhetoric* II: fear, anger; evaluative, agent-implicating) appear only in Type 3 (praxis). Ethical hexeis are **defined *peri pathē***; *technē* is **defined *peri poiēsin*** (presupposes broad receptivity but is not a *how-of-higher-order-pathos* → which is exactly why poiēsis is the *pathē*-neutral type).
- ***Hexis* conditions *doxa* (≠ identical to it)**: the agent's dispositions shape which appearances and *doxai* arise, and how tenaciously they are held (Nussbaum, note 9) — but a *doxa* remains a taking-as-true, not a disposition (D5).
