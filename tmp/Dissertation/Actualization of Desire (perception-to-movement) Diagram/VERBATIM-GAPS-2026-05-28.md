## Workflow note

When extracting any of these, follow the protocol established in verbatim_passages.md:

1. Open the PDF at the offset-corrected page (use the formulas at verbatim_passages.md lines 23–30 for Aristotle; offset +15 for BCAP English-print).
2. Copy verbatim text **exactly**, preserving italicization markers in the source as `_italic_` or `*italic*` notation.
3. Add a new `### [Source] [locus]` section to verbatim_passages.md with the verbatim quote in a blockquote (`> `), followed by `**Source**:`, `**Verified**:`, and `**Used at**:` metadata.
4. Then sync into the diagram: add a canonical entry to `PASSAGES` in the v10 HTML (matching the verbatim text and the key form used elsewhere — `'GA 18, p. X'` / `'BT §N, H.YYY'` / `'Gross 20XX, p. X'`); wire the appropriate popup `cite:` field and parent-node `passages:` array; replace any `[VERBATIM PENDING]` skeleton entries.

Each entry resolved unlocks one more layer of the advisor-rigor citation cover; cumulatively these close out audit findings #7, #10, #11, and #16.
