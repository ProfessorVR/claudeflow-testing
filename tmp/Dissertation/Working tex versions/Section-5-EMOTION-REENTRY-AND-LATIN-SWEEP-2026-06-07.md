## A. OPEN QUESTION — where does the emotion–desire complex re-enter the chain?

The emotion–desire complex — emotion as **"judgment-modifying"** (the *Rhetorica* II.1, 1378a20–22 sense: the emotions modify *kriseis*) — does not merely terminate the synchronic chain at A₄; it **re-enters** the chain and conditions a subsequent pass. The question is *at which node*.

**User's two candidates (2026-06-07):**
1. **M₁ → A₂** (re-entry at the *phantasma*-presentation grade), or
2. **M₀ → A₁** (re-entry one grade earlier, at the *aisthēsis* / hedonic-tonality grade).

**User's argument (favours re-entry no later than the *phantasma*, i.e. A₂):**
> The *phantasma* as presented to the mind must itself already be affectively altered — otherwise the coward, rather than "*seeing*" enemies in the shadows, would see only a shadow. So the re-entry must reach the *phantasma* (A₂) at the latest; the open question is whether it reaches one grade further back, to the perception/hedonic tonality (A₁).
