## Why the Gross injection failed in Version B

The Source E prompt section embedded the four audit-verified Gross quotes (Heidegger and Rhetoric p.4, p.26; Uncomfortable Situations p.3, p.20) with explicit deployment guidance ("MUST be cited verbatim... NO paraphrase"). Despite this, Gross is 0-cited in Version B's body.

Hypothesized causes:
1. **Rolling-context per-section prompts may have summarized away Source E.** Each per-section prompt is ~21KB and the full Concluding_Section_Prompt.md is ~80KB — the LLM may have effectively de-prioritized late-in-prompt content like Source E in favor of the earlier per-section requirements.
2. **The chunks supplied by retrieval did not include Gross.** Even with `--corpus-chunk-count 60` + `--corpus-min-relevance 0.60`, ChromaDB's semantic search produced predominantly Heidegger BCAP chunks. The prompt's verbatim Gross text was therefore the *only* place the LLM saw Gross — but without corpus chunks to anchor against, the LLM may have treated those as descriptive rather than imperative.
3. **The LLM's attention bias toward Heidegger.** When the prompt is heavily Heideggerian, the LLM tends to flatten secondary-author attributions into the dominant author.

To force Gross into the output requires either:
- (a) **Pre-population of section-specific chunks with Gross PDFs forced into the retrieval pool** (would need a `--corpus-author-quotas Gross:3,Hawhee:2,Nussbaum:2` flag — not currently supported).
- (b) **A dedicated Gross-only Subsection prompt** that the rolling-context loop draws from a Gross-locked chunk-pool.
- (c) **Hand-revision of Version A or B** to insert the four Gross quotes manually in Subsections 7 and 8.

Path (c) is the fastest. The Source E section of `Concluding_Section_Prompt.md` now has the verbatim Gross text ready to paste in.
