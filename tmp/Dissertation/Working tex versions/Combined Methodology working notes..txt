Methodology: Diachronic Ontological Incorporation Analysis
The Method and Its Claim
This dissertation advances a method I call Diachronic Ontological Incorporation Analysis (DIA). The method begins from the claim that incorporation is not an episodic feeling of "being there," nor a merely psychological state, but an ontological-experiential achievement that unfolds through the chain of actualization and persists beyond any single encounter through sedimentation into hexis. In this respect, incorporation is preferable to the language of "presence," since it can be structurally differentiated and located across the player's relation to world, avatar, action, and disposition.

Ontological Priority: The A⁰–A⁴ Chain
The ontological priority of the method is Aristotelian-Heideggerian. It treats game experience as a movement from sensible encounter (A⁰), to completed perception (A¹), to phantasmatic stabilization (A²), to cognitive ratification (A³), to enacted comportment (A⁴), such that action does not arise from a neutral subject confronting a neutral object, but from an already disposed being who is open to being-affected by what appears as good, bad, threatening, attractive, shameful, or desirable. Games matter here not because they produce an illusory world detached from reality, but because they furnish designed environments within which such disclosures can be intensified, patterned, repeated, and sedimented.

The chain is read recursively, not linearly: each completed A⁴ action sediments back into the agent's hexis and reshapes the dispositional ground from which the next chain-pass proceeds. Diagram v9 renders this as a back-arrow A⁴ → fresh A⁰ cycle — completed action changes the world. This recursive structure operates in two diachronic registers: Stimmung-saturation biasing basic affective valence at A¹, and corrective-faculty impairment biasing doxastic ratification at A³. Together they constitute the loop that makes ethical transformation difficult and game-design pacing consequential.

The Dispositional Ground: Two Forms of Hexis
Accordingly, the player does not enter a gameworld as a blank slate. Every entry into play is conditioned by a prior dispositional ground: by already sedimented doxai, by basic affective attunements, and especially by two distinct yet interwoven forms of hexis.

Technē-hexis names those cultivated, deliberation-reducing capacities through which play becomes operationally transparent — controller fluency, aiming, parry timing, camera management, menu literacy, rotation memory, build optimization, and other procedural competencies that withdraw into action once mastered. Following Heidegger's reading of Aristotle in the Basic Concepts of Aristotelian Philosophy, technē-hexis "reduces deliberation insofar as it is through training that the completedness of attaining a result comes about" (BCAP 127). Its structural analogue is the ready-to-hand hammer that withdraws into the hammering (Being and Time, §15).

Ethical hexis names a different order of readiness. Whereas technē-hexis concerns efficient means and the practiced transparency of execution, ethical hexis concerns the "how" of one's being in action: the socially and evaluatively inflected openness through which one determines what is fitting, tolerable, shameful, cruel, honorable, playful, or unacceptable in a given world. It is at this level that a player's willingness to deceive, to humiliate, to "play the villain," to protect the weak, to grieve an NPC, or to treat another player as disposable becomes philosophically legible — not merely as choice but as disposition. Ethical hexis exercises a unique two-level reach: it modulates both basic-valence input at A¹ (Stimmung-saturation) and doxastic ratification at A³, where evaluatively complex content crystallizes into determinate civic passion.

The distinction matters structurally:

Dispositional layer	What it includes	What it conditions
Technē-hexis	Controller fluency, aiming, parry timing, menu literacy, camera control, combo timing, build optimization, procedural know-how	What the player can do transparently; which actions require deliberation; how quickly a game moves from friction to incorporation
Ethical hexis	Norms of fair play, tolerance for cruelty, willingness to role-play transgression, sense of responsibility toward NPCs/players, comfort with deceit, honor, shame, pity, restraint	What the player can take as acceptable, compelling, humorous, disturbing, or identity-threatening within a gameworld
A player may be deeply incorporated at the level of technē while remaining ethically resistant — or may ethically normalize behaviors through repeated enactment even when the mechanics are initially unfamiliar. That asymmetry is one of the method's primary diagnostic instruments.

The Rhetorical Analogy
This dispositional ground is what makes game experience structurally analogous to rhetorical persuasion. As in rhetoric, the subject addressed by the gameworld is never neutral; they are already inclined in certain ways, already susceptible to some appeals and resistant to others, and already furnished with patterns of uptake that condition what can count as compelling, moving, or actionable. The gameworld therefore does not simply present content to a player; it addresses and modifies an already formed audience of one, drawing upon existing dispositions while also reworking them through repeated acts of perception, judgment, and embodied performance.

The analogy is not superficial — it is structural:

Prior disposition conditions uptake

Uptake becomes actualization

Actualization sediments into hexis

Hexis conditions future uptake

That loop is the heart of the method's originality, and it runs through both gameworld encounters and ordinary Being-in-the-world. From your MA thesis onward, you have argued that virtual environments can recalibrate phenomenological and ontological orientation and reveal how easily we enter a "fallen" mode of uncritical absorption. The dissertation generalizes and deepens that claim: gameworld actualizations do not remain sealed within the game, but can deposit durable tendencies, sensitivities, skills, tolerances, and evaluative habits that accompany the player back into everyday life.

The Experiential Keystone: Calleja's Incorporation
Calleja's notion of incorporation is retained as the method's principal experiential term. However, incorporation is not treated here as a stand-alone theory of game experience; rather, it names the lived achievement that occurs when the structural dimensions of play — kinesthetic, spatial, shared, narrative, affective, and ludic — converge with the chain of actualization strongly enough that the player is absorbed into, and absorbingly oriented toward, the gameworld. Its fullest articulation lies not at the first moment of sensory contact, but in the substantial union achieved when world, avatar, action, and doxastically ratified orientation come to function together as a coherent mode of being-in-play.

The keystone of the method — consubstantiation/incorporation as Aristotle's "one substrate, two in being" (De Anima III.7, 431a13–14) — is confirmed and given its precise ontological articulation across the chain. The incorporation rubric (R1–R5) maps onto chain nodes as follows:

Rubric criterion	Chain locus	Achievement
R1 Assimilation (world → consciousness)	A¹	Resonant aisthma deposited; gameworld internalized as kinetic content
R2 Embodiment (player → world via avatar)	A² → A³	Phantasma of avatar-as-me, doxastically ratified
R3 Simultaneity (joined and separate)	A³ → A⁴	One substrate, two in being; distinct loci of motive preserved
R4 Spatial-kinesthetic cornerstone	A¹ → A²-A³	Inhabitation criterion met; mimetic-symbiotic continuum cultivated
R5 Blending (4–5 dimensions co-active)	A³	Multiple involvement dimensions woven into single cognitive actuality
Burke's consubstantiation — "substantially one yet a distinct locus of motives, both joined and separate" (Rhetoric of Motives, p. 21) — and Calleja's incorporation name the same structural event from two sides. The method reads them as such.

The Synchronic–Diachronic Distinction
The method is diachronic precisely because incorporation does not end when a session ends. Each completed action sediments into hexis and thus modifies the conditions under which the next gameworld, the next session, or even the next ordinary worldly encounter will be met.

The method therefore distinguishes between two registers of analysis:

Synchronic analysis examines a bounded episode of play as a single passage through the A⁰–A⁴ chain, asking how a given event moves from disclosure to action and what kind of incorporation it achieves. DIA Steps 1–6 constitute the synchronic register.

Diachronic analysis (Step 8) asks how prior actualizations have prepared the player for that event and how the event, once completed, may re-enter the player's dispositional ground as a lasting modification of skill, sensibility, judgment, or comportment — projecting which hexis is being sedimented, which Stimmung will saturate the next A⁰, which doxai are added to the settled-doxa band, and what entry-point the next pass will use.

The RDR2 tutorial provides the method's sharpest empirical case for the diachronic register: a bounded 3–6 hour arc (Chapter 1 Colter through early Chapter 2 Horseshoe Overlook) within which a specific hexis-bundle is engineered, traceable step by step. The headline structural finding — that RDR2's tutorial is the closest case in commercial game design of ethical-hexis cultivation as the primary engineering objective, in structural contrast to WoW's competitive technē-hexis cultivation — illustrates the method's diagnostic power across cases.

The Four Types of Action at A⁴
The chain's terminal node individuates into four structurally distinct types of action, each named by the species of orexis that sources it:

Type 1 — Epithymetic: epithymia; pursuit of the pleasant, avoidance of the painful. The only type that can fire pre-doxastically — the perceptual kritikon alone discriminates pursue/avoid (DA III.7, 431a8–12). The structural target of dark-pattern design.

Type 2 — Thymotic: thymos; answers a slight, defends honor, asserts the self. Always doxastic; irreducibly social — requires a toward-whom, a Mitsein of co-agents and counter-agents.

Type 3 — Bouletic: boulēsis; the rational wish for the end-as-good. Always doxastic; may reach toward impossibles. The dominant type in praxis-mode play (e.g., RDR2's acting-well-with-the-gang as wished end).

Type 4 — Prohairetic: prohairesis; the deliberate choice of means to a wished end. Always doxastic; bounded by what is in our power (NE III.3, 1112a31).

Civic passions (anger, fear, pity, shame, indignation, envy, emulation, kindness, friendship) form no type of their own; they are the species of being-affected through which one of the four desires gets sourced. The compulsion diagnostic — Type 4 → Type 1 regression via technē-hexis runaway — names the structural mechanism of dark-pattern design effectiveness: repeated prohairetic actions sediment into transparent routine without the ethical-hexis correlate that would hold the agent open to fresh evaluation of whether the means still serves a wished end.

Burke's Role: Diagnostic, Not Foundational
Burke's role within the method is diagnostic rather than foundational. The pentad and its ratios do not replace the ontology of action; they provide a rhetoric of motive through which one can analyze how players, designers, and communities articulate the causes of action, distribute responsibility across scene, agent, agency, purpose, and attitude, and rhetorically justify or conceal the source of what has been enacted. Burke is most useful where the method asks not simply what moved the player, but how that movement is afterward rendered intelligible in symbolic terms — and how players retroactively attribute their action to situation, tools, development, or other agents rather than to their own sedimented dispositions.

Calleja's Role: Structural, Not Self-Sufficient
Calleja is used in a subordinate but indispensable way. His model identifies the structural dimensions through which incorporation is engineered and gives the dissertation a precise vocabulary for distinguishing spatial inhabitation, kinesthetic fluency, shared belonging, narrative alterbiography, affective tonality, and ludic involvement across both micro and macro phases. His macro/micro distinction becomes one empirical access point for the diachronic/synchronic recursive loop — the macro phase as the diachronic register across chain-passes; the micro phase as the synchronic register within one. These dimensions are not treated as self-sufficient explanations of action, but as the designed conditions under which ontological uptake and dispositional modification become possible.

The Central Wager
The central wager of Diachronic Ontological Incorporation Analysis, then, is that gameworlds do not merely host action; they participate in the formation of the player's very capacity to act, judge, and be affected. A player brings technē-hexis and ethical hexis into the gameworld; those dispositions are tested and modified in play; and the resulting sediment may be carried back into ordinary Being-in-the-world, where it can inform future perception, judgment, and practice. To study games at the level of incorporation is therefore to study not only what happens in a game, but how game-mediated actualizations become part of the continuing formation of the self.

This is not the claim that every game dramatically changes the player. It is the structurally precise claim that the chain's diachronic register operates, that game designers can engineer it — for better or worse — and that the method is built to ask whether, how, and to what degree a given pattern of incorporation modifies the dispositional grounds of future worldly comportment.
