## 2. The standing rules (inherited verbatim — cite, don't gloss)

1. **Two-channel rule.** The second form is detectable only as the *disagreement of a surface channel and a depth channel* — never from one channel, never from a bipolar engaged↔bored scale. Consequence for each dataset: §4 below.
2. **Ascertaining ≠ awakening.** Instruments read *comportment* (the *Zeitvertreib* layer), never the attunement itself; the second-form subject *sincerely denies* boredom, so self-report is doubly cautioned. Part III must state this as a limit of its own method section (report Tension 1), not bury it.
3. **Never infer depth from a single surface index**; **beware the instrument that levels** (a bipolar scale performs the flattening; aversive-state coding under-detects the zombie stare).
4. **Category caution.** A 17-minute video or a course module does not disclose beings as a whole. What transfers is the **structural grammar** (culprit-structure, emptiness-structure, temporal structure) as a heuristic. Forms 1–2 transfer cleanly; "form 3" only as phenomenology-under-analogy. All such readings are tagged.
5. **Provenance ledger.** Every analytical claim carries FAITHFUL-HEIDEGGER / FAITHFUL-EMPIRICAL / PROJECTED (`anticipatory-application`). Lean-on: P2's N=12 gaze data; clean felt-time reports; the team's own high/low-arousal distinction. Hold-back: P1's EEG (N=1-clean; corroborating only); P3's presence/preference split (analogy only).
6. **Stale-crosswalk note.** The Wendt `roda-crosswalk.md` cites RODA v5/"ADA"; canonical is **v9** (world-/co-disclosedness vocabulary). Nothing structural changes for our use; Part III cites v9's terms and reconciles silently (one footnote at most).

## 4. Protocol by dataset

### 4.1 Survey closed items (Q7/Q8 + demographics; N=241, three terms, 2D-PC era)
- Deployment truth (author-supplied): students almost certainly used the **desktop 3D version or the video fallback**; instrument's "VR platform" label = the 3D app — the terminology gap is stated once in §III.3, then the strata are called **app-users vs video-only**.
- Q8-Yes among app-users = **screen-based presence self-report** = surface-channel evidence of world-disclosedness *candidacy* — never incorporation, never depth (Part II: presence ≠ incorporation).
- Analyses: distributions per term + pooled; Q7×Q8 strata; strata × theme rates; composition-caveated term comparisons; χ²/effect sizes only where they earn keep. **No IA psychometrics** (force-keyed artifacts); **no performance channel** (content items at ceiling).
- **A single self-report instrument cannot certify the second form.** Closed items alone yield: first-form evidence (via culprit reports in open text), surface-engagement rates, and stratum contrasts. Honest ceiling, stated in §III.1.

### 4.2 Survey open text (~870 responses; the exhaustive core)
- Coding per `codebook-v1.md`: FCM signature families + Wendt node codes + involvement-channel vocabulary + emergent. Two full passes, negative-case search, saturation memo, adjudication log, co-occurrence matrices, verbatim-verified exemplar bank (anonymized codes W25-R001…).
- **Admissible divergence pairs (the survey's second-form candidates)** — divergence must be *within-respondent*, one surface-positive signal against one hollowness signal:
  (a) Q8-Yes ∧ open-text emptiness/monotony about the same object;
  (b) praise-of-concept ∧ emptiness-of-execution in one response ("the idea is amazing, the videos not so much");
  (c) Q7-Yes (could use app) ∧ self-reported flight to video-only.
  These are coded **DIV-candidates**, reported as *structural echoes* of the second form [PROJECTED] — never as detections (no depth channel exists here). Everything else codes as first-form culprit evidence, engagement evidence, or *Zeitvertreib*-flight evidence.
- **Friction-prediction test** (the falsifiable move): the Wendt seed read predicted dark rooms / avatar occlusion / must-stand-close / buffer latency / wayfinding as foreclosures. Score each prediction against complaint frequencies: CONFIRMED / DISCONFIRMED / UNATTESTED, with counts. Predictions the students never mention are *findings too* (predicted friction that didn't surface at scale).
- Non-English responses: translate, code on the translation, flag `[translated]`.

### 4.3 Published findings (P1–P3)
- Source order (user-set): **report → FCM digests → PDFs** (pdftotext to .md; never page-images). Reconstruct each paper's constructs/findings with reliability flags already in the digests; verify any quoted number against the PDF text.
- Apply the crosswalk (companion doc) with the ledger's lean-on/hold-back weights. The keystone (EEG↔gaze divergence = second-form signature) is restated, not re-argued — and stated in its EEG-independent fallback form (felt-time vs gaze) so it survives P1's N=1-clean status.
- P3 is re-read at deployment scale: presence≠preference at N=20 → its structural replay at N=241; the institutional 2D-PC move as the same tension resolved at course level [FAITHFUL-EMPIRICAL for the facts; PROJECTED for the reading].

### 4.4 Boredom raw dataset (Phase 2A; O-9/O-10 decisions deferred to its start)
- **Anonymization first** (S01–S08 map kept out of tracked trees). Then: per-subject survey .txt digests (felt-time = the *Hingehaltenheit* thread's primary data); comportment-layer reads of the state-contrast videos against the §D signature table (searching gaze / watch-glance / fidget = form-1 *Zeitvertreib*; stillness/eyes-closed = form-3-analog); the team's own LA/HA/IE/AS labels compared to the three-state signatures — agreement AND divergence both reportable.
- **Bounded RODA episode reads** only where a genuine actualization episode is visible (this is RODA's home turf: recorded episodes).
- Comportment = an additional **surface** channel. Only here (with EEG/HMD depth data) is a true two-channel divergence *measurable* in principle; depth-of-analysis awaits O-9.

### 4.5 Mark (lit-review layer)
- Catalog rules apply (`../sources/mark-attention-span-quotation-catalog.md`): MARK-VERBATIM quotable after book verification; AUTHOR-PARAPHRASE = claims only; AUTHOR-NOTE = the author's voice. Two unmarked pages carry `******` until resolved. Crosswalk rows: states↔forms (levels-vs-structures caveat), residue↔carryover, capture↔conscription, Skinner↔differentiation.
