## 0. The two constraints that govern every module

**0a. Head movement.** Head movement is present in this data. It is visible in the session video, where the head
rolls roughly 90° and pitches far enough down to take the film out of view. It is **not instrumented in this pass**:
both rounds record gaze eye-in-head, not head pose, so a participant who turns away from the film registers no
deviation on any channel here (F-03). The section must **never** say the boring film produced stillness rather than
restlessness. The accurate form, to be used wherever this arises: *the eye channels show withdrawal, and the search
behaviour visible in the session video was not instrumented in this pass.* This binds L3.4, L4.3, L4.4, L5.3 and
L6 in particular, and it binds every figure caption carried into the dissertation.

**0b. Criterion probabilities.** Every criterion-validity probability in this section is a **within-subject
permutation p** — 20,000 shuffles of the three film labels inside each participant, fixed seed (D-09). Three
associations clear alpha analytically and do not clear it under permutation; they are reported as non-significant.
No analytic p is ever quoted as though it were the result. The analytic values exist beside the permutation values
so the size of the correction stays visible, and that is their only use.

### L2. The Apparatus and the Data — method

*Renamed from v1's "The Apparatus and the Record": "record" may not name evidence anywhere in this dissertation.
`scripts/boredom-o9/` may still be called the **methodology of record**, which names a code path and not evidence.*
*This is the module with the most new work in v2. It carries six subsections where v1 carried four.*

- **L2.1 Four cohorts, never conflated.** The published pilot (**N=3**, one clean EEG subject); the published gaze
  study (**N=12** as published); the unpublished per-subject Round 1 dataset (**N=8**, `S01`–`S08`); and Round 2
  (**N=4**, `R2-01`–`R2-04`). This reprocessing's pooled cohort is **N=12 = 8 + 4**, twenty-four Round 1
  subject×stimulus cells and twelve Round 2 cells, thirty-six episodes in all.
  **★ THE TRAP THIS SUBSECTION EXISTS TO CLOSE:** the published gaze study's twelve and this analysis's twelve are
  **two different twelves**. A published-channel derivation is not the raw cohort, and a pooled figure is not a
  replication of a published figure that happens to share an N.
  `VERIFY BEFORE DRAFTING:` whether the published gaze cohort of twelve contains Round 1's eight. Nothing read this
  session establishes it either way. Until it is pinned from the paper, the two twelves are never presented as the
  same twelve, and no sentence implies continuity of cohort.
- **L2.2 The instrument.** Seven items per episode, administered in the break immediately after each film (R-07):
  fatigue-prior, boredom (1–9), engagement (1–9), minutes-until-bored, sleep-fight, felt duration, fatigue-after.
  Boredom and engagement asked as **two separate items, not a bipolar axis** — the design choice that makes
  within-instrument divergence observable at all. Derived: felt-duration ratio (felt ÷ 17); depletion
  (fatigue-after − fatigue-prior). The surveys are free text: 239 values read as bare numbers, 5 converted, 1
  recovered from prose, 5 semantic, 2 missing, every interpretation logged with its raw string.
  **Structural missingness stated here, not buried:** participants who never became bored are absent from
  minutes-until-bored *by design*, so that item's n is reduced and the test runs on those who did.
  `O-15` marks the IRB-facing wording of this description if the module restates it.
- **L2.3 The stimuli, and the equal-focus ruling.** Three 17-minute films: a **1989 Microsoft Word tutorial**
  section as the boring stimulus — task-structured instructional content, not visually empty filler (R-08); a
  clinical procedure film; an interesting film. **All three are treated equally** (R-06). The clinical-centred
  framing of the published work was an IRB and publication necessity and is not the analytic frame here. This
  ruling is what turns v1's clinical-only keystone into L3.6's three-film decomposition.
  `O-10` marks the boring film's exact title and source for the works cited.
- **L2.4 Two engines, two axis conventions, and why nothing rate-dependent is pooled.** Round 1 was recorded in
  Unreal and Round 2 in Unity, and their device axes differ: Round 1 X forward, Y horizontal, Z vertical; Round 2
  Z forward, X horizontal, Y vertical, confirmed from the original MATLAB (F-01). A single hard-coded forward
  direction would give Round 1 a meaningless ~90° offset on every cell. The rounds also differ by roughly a factor
  of forty in effective sampling rate — Round 1 at a median 3.00 Hz, Round 2 at 120.1 Hz. **The poolability rule
  follows and is stated as a rule, with its measurement:** means of per-sample values pool; variances, dispersions
  and any windowed statistic do not, and recomputing the identical gaze feature on Round 2 decimated to Round 1's
  rate moves it by 0.65–0.84×. Every reported statistic carries a poolability flag and a one-line reason. Two
  consequences worth naming in prose: blink *rate* is a Round 2 measure only, because Round 1's 334 ms sampling
  interval equals the mean duration of a blink; and Round 1's openness scale is a percentage against Round 2's
  fraction, so the two are brought onto one scale before any pooled signed-rank test, because the signed-rank test
  ranks the magnitude of paired differences and mixed units silently re-weight participants.
- **L2.5 ★ The openness proxy — promoted from footnote to its own paragraph.** Round 1 has no continuous eye-openness
  channel. Its per-sample validity flag is used as the proxy. That substitution is licensed rather than assumed:
  inside Round 2, where both measures exist, the validity proxy and the vendor's continuous openness correlate at
  **Pearson r = 0.998** across the twelve Round 2 cells. Without that agreement the pooled openness result could not
  be stated at all, which is why the argument is made in the open. Two further facts belong here. Round 2's openness
  is **binary at source** — across 3,416,362 per-eye samples the only values present are {0, 1} — so any threshold
  in the open interval gives identical results and the threshold is not a tuned parameter; the consequence is that
  blink versus extended closure rests **entirely on duration**. The blink ceiling is 500 ms, from VanderWerf et al.
  2003's spontaneous blink of 334 ± 67 ms, rounded to mean + ~2.5 SD, and those blinks were recorded while
  participants watched video, which matches this paradigm. Round 1 closure episodes are read from the per-eye
  sentinel rather than from the parsed pupil series, because the parser masks the sentinel to NaN — correct for
  pupil, fatal for closure.
  `O-02` marks the naming of this surface: one term, one definition, used everywhere.
- **L2.6 Two window conventions.** Round 1's window is the middle ~13.6 minutes of the 17-minute film — roughly two
  minutes were cut inside each 30 s instructed closure as a sync guard, the protocol not yet being settled. Round
  2's crop is essentially the whole film. The film itself began when participants were told to open their eyes and
  stopped when they were told to close them, so the window **is** the stimulus interval and the instructed closures
  are its boundaries (F-08). Because closure accumulates across an episode, that difference is not neutral, so it is
  measured rather than assumed away: Round 2 is analysed at both the full crop (primary) and a Round-1-matched
  window dropping 120 s from each end, and both are carried through the same tests. **The result of that check
  belongs in L3, not here:** eye openness is window-invariant; pupil is cleaner on the matched window.
- **L2.7 The criterion-validity method.** **Already written.** Paste from
  `PROSE-METHODS-CRITERION-VALIDITY-v1-2026-08-04.md` — §A is the six-paragraph full form, §B the two-paragraph
  short form if the module runs tight. Do not rewrite it. Two `******` markers inside it are author decisions:
  `O-14` (multiplicity in the criterion family; the written recommendation is to keep it a family of descriptive
  associations and justify that in a footnote rather than compute a correction and then argue against it) and
  `O-15` (the IRB-facing survey wording). §C of that file holds six anticipated objections with answers already in
  the text; those are defence material, not prose.
  **Placement matters and is a drafting decision, not a formatting one:** the distinction the statement rests on —
  separating the films versus agreeing with the person — must be introduced **before** the results, or the pupil
  finding reads as a failure rather than as the answer to a different question.
- **L2.8 What each channel can and cannot say.** The load-bearing channels are pupil, eye openness and closure, gaze,
  and the seven-item self-report (R-05). **Cognitive load is appendix-only** — vendor-computed from undisclosed
  inputs, and flat against both self-report items (rho −0.09 and +0.11, permutation p 0.65 and 0.60). **Heart rate
  and HRV are removed entirely** — measurement failed for some participants owing to facial and head structure, and
  HR is likewise flat (rho +0.09 and +0.04, permutation p 0.68 and 0.86). Both rulings are **evidenced rather than
  asserted**, which is why the flat numbers are printed. **EEG is reported as a full negative** (L3.8). The standing
  reporting rule closes the module: the evidence is the directional convergence across channels plus the specific
  surfaces that reach significance — never per-channel significance for channels that do not reach it. Anything
  below n = 6 is labelled descriptive, and at n = 4 the signed-rank floor is p = 0.125, so perfect separation is the
  strongest obtainable result and is stated as such wherever it applies.
  `O-08` marks how much of this apparatus the rendered prose carries against what moves to an appendix.

### L3. The Findings — REBUILT

*v1's L3 was organised around the corrected keystone with pupil as the one robust surface. That ordering is wrong
for v4: eye closure is the strongest surface, pupil is second and is honestly weakened by pooling, gaze has its
first correction-surviving result, and the criterion analysis is a new kind of finding that has no place in v1's
scheme at all.*

- **L3.1 What the tests found, in one frame.** Ninety-nine pairwise contrasts were computed across the whole
  analysis. **Nine survive Holm correction, and every one of them is on eye openness, pupil dilation, or excursions
  beyond 10° per minute.** The result is carried by the eye-tracking surfaces; it is not spread across the channel
  set. Nothing is omitted for being non-significant.
- **L3.2 ★ Eye closure — the headline surface.** Pooled N=12: Friedman p = 0.0004; boring below clinical in
  **11 of 12** (Holm p = 0.0020, rank-biserial −0.974) and below interesting in **12 of 12** (Holm p = 0.0015,
  rank-biserial −1.000). Round 1 alone: 7/8 and 8/8. Round 2 alone: 4/4 on both contrasts, descriptive at n = 4.
  Three properties make this the surface the section leans on. It is **window-invariant** — identical under both
  Round 2 conventions. It was **completely unmoved by the re-crop**, which is the strongest available evidence that
  the headline is not an artefact of windowing. And it is the surface that **fills a documented non-coverage** in
  the boredom cluster's construct-measure concordance: blink and eye-closure had no measure in this dataset before.
  Episode counts for scale: 4,365 closure episodes in Round 1 (3,501 blink-length, 864 extended) and 5,131 in Round
  2 (4,895 blink, 236 extended).
- **L3.3 Pupil — second, and honestly weakened by pooling.** Round 1 alone is a perfect separation: boring below
  clinical in **8 of 8**, Friedman p = 0.0302, Wilcoxon p = 0.0078, rank-biserial −1.000. Pooled to N=12 it survives
  but weakens: **10 of 12**, Holm p = 0.0146, rank-biserial −0.872, with the omnibus at p = 0.097 on the full window
  and **p = 0.017 on the Round-1-matched window**. Round 2 alone runs 2 of 4, which is chance. Two honesties are
  owed and both are stated in the prose: **the pooled result is substantially carried by Round 1**, and pupil
  separates boring from clinical but **not** boring from interesting (Holm p = 0.303). The window comparison is
  reported here rather than in the method module, because it is a result: trimming removes the settling-in and the
  wind-down, and pupil is cleaner without them.
- **L3.4 ★ Gaze — amplitude and frequency point opposite ways.** The author's construct has two components and they
  behave differently, which is why a single gaze-variance number was ambiguous in the published work. **Amplitude** —
  how far off centre the eye sits — is higher on the boring film, and it is the component that tracks self-report.
  **Frequency** — departures beyond 10° per minute — is **lower** on the boring film: 7 of 8 against clinical and
  7 of 8 against interesting, both at **Holm p = 0.047**, the first gaze result in this study ever to survive
  correction. Read together: fewer but longer departures when bored, frequent short repositioning when engaged.
  That is the measurable form of the distinction between a zombie stare and engaged focus.
  **Three cautions travel with this subsection and none may be dropped.** Gaze is eye-in-head, not head pose. The
  gaze feature is rate-dependent and is therefore computed per round and never pooled. And the baseline choice is
  made in the open: the same angular deviation is reported against three references — the round's own device
  forward, each cell's own median, and one median per participant — with the finding, reported against prediction,
  that the per-file baseline correlates with self-report *better* than the restoration faithful to the published
  definition (rho +0.52 against +0.36). `O-12` decides which is primary; both are computed and neither is hidden.
- **L3.5 ★ Criterion validity — does the channel agree with the person? (NEW SUBSECTION)** The study's own original
  validation logic, never computed until this pass: the participants' reports were the criterion and the instruments
  were scored against them. Thirty-six paired episodes, within-subject standard scores on both sides, Spearman
  reported with Pearson alongside, **within-subject permutation probabilities throughout**.
  **Four associations survive:** gaze deviation median against boredom (rho +0.524, p = 0.0074) and against
  engagement (rho −0.527, p = 0.0075); eye openness against boredom (rho −0.460, p = 0.0212) and against engagement
  (rho +0.416, p = 0.0370). **Pupil dilation tracks neither report** (rho −0.172, p = 0.404 and +0.036, p = 0.861),
  although it separates the three films more powerfully than anything else.
  **Three associations that clear alpha analytically do not clear it under permutation** and are reported as
  non-significant: gaze degrees from centre against boredom (0.032 → 0.075) and against engagement (0.018 → 0.052),
  and percentage of time beyond 10° against engagement (0.047 → 0.099). One borderline case runs against the
  reported statistic and is stated as such: gaze degrees from centre against engagement sits at permutation
  p = 0.052 under Spearman and 0.048 under Pearson, and is treated as non-significant, which is the conservative
  reading.
  **The finding is the ranking, and the ranking is oppositional.** The channel that best separates the stimuli is
  not the channel that best agrees with the person. Those are two different questions, and `O-13` decides how the
  distinction is presented.
- **L3.6 ★ The three-film decomposition — replaces v1's clinical-only keystone.** Under composite A (pupil + eye
  openness, the two surfaces that survive Holm, both computed from raw per-sample data rather than taken from a
  vendor index), every participant is placed in one of four categories on **each** film:

  | film | divergent | both engaged | both bored | inverse |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Boring | 0 | 0 | **9** | 3 |
  | **Clinical** | **8** | 4 | 0 | **0** |
  | Interesting | 3 | 5 | 2 | 2 |

  **The safe form of the claim, immune to the objection that the composite is partly a boring-detector:** the eyes
  place the clinical film with the interesting one and away from the boring one, while the survey places it nearer
  the boring end. Divergence concentrates on clinical — 8 of 12, against 0 on boring.
  **Composite B, which adds the rebuilt gaze amplitude, gives 7/3/1/1 on clinical, so A is the cleaner instrument**
  and the hypothesis that adding gaze would strengthen the keystone is **not supported**, even though gaze is the
  best single channel against self-report. Composite B is within-round only, every gaze measure being rate-dependent.
  These are category counts at N=12 and no omnibus test is run on them.
  `O-05` decides whether the per-participant matrix across all three films becomes an appendix. Recommendation
  carried forward from v1 and strengthened by the equal-focus ruling: yes — it makes every count auditable.
- **L3.7 The self-report instrument on its own terms.** Boredom separates the films (Friedman p = 0.0139);
  engagement separates them more strongly (p = 0.0052, with boring against interesting at Holm p = 0.0176);

### L4. The Analysis — chain-node walkthrough (THE analytical form)

- **L4.1 The chain in the laboratory.** A₀ = strapped into a headset under mandate, with no leisure motive;
  A₁/A₂ = the charged image; A₃ = committed interest; **A₄ crossed always, because the chain runs always** — the
  only potentiality this situation furnishes is watching, and the diagnosis concerns the worth of what is
  furnished, never a chain that failed to run. The laboratory is a *Hingehaltenheit* apparatus **by design**, and
  the felt-duration result is what shows it: dilation in 25 of 36 episodes with **no separation between the films**,
  so the holding belongs to the situation and not to any stimulus.
- **L4.2 ★ The second form, instrumented — and the reverse case absorbed here rather than deferred.** The layer
  assignment argued, not assumed: Heidegger's surface is outward comportment, his depth an emptiness recognised
  only retrospectively, and the post-film self-report **is** that retrospective recognition. `fcm-05`'s decisive
  sentence: behavioural engagement is not evidence against boredom; in this form it is the very medium of it.
  **The reverse case, stated in full and not held over.** Under the primary composite there are **zero** reverse
  cases at N=12 — nobody reads physiologically bored while reporting engagement on the clinical film. That
  asymmetry is a stronger structural claim than the mean, and it is also **sensitive to how the composite is
  built**: the sweep over all seven non-empty subsets of the three poolable engagement channels produces one or two
  reverse cases under four of the seven definitions, R2-03 being the recurring one. R2-03's own answers explain it
  — boredom 3, 1 and 2 across the three films and engagement 8 on the clinical one. They never became bored by
  anything, so their clinical episode is their own relative physiological minimum rather than an absolute bored
  state, and *reverse* is a within-subject relative statement throughout. The honest form: **the asymmetry holds
  under the primary composite and is reported with the sweep that shows what it depends on.**
  **★ THE QUALIFICATION v2 ADDS, AND IT IS LOAD-BEARING.** L3.5 shows that two of the eye channels **do** track the
  participants' own reports across all thirty-six episodes. That does not undercut the divergence; it is what makes
  the divergence sayable. A channel that tracked nothing could not be said to *diverge* from anything — it would be
  noise. The claim this section can make, and the one it must confine itself to, is that instruments which agree
  with the person's report at rho ≈ 0.5 across their three films nevertheless part company with it on one film in
  particular. The composite that carries the decomposition is itself a mixture on exactly this point: eye openness
  tracks self-report, pupil does not. That mixture is stated rather than smoothed.
- **L4.3 The first form — the *Zeitvertreib* claim moves from the body to the eye.** The boring film behaves as the
  form predicts on the report side: onset within five minutes in 10 of 12, three inside a minute, and sleep-fight
  the sharpest item in the instrument at 12 of 12. **v1's "searching gaze as *Zeitvertreib* operationalized" is
  withdrawn as stated.** The amplitude/frequency split says something more specific and partly the opposite: on the
  boring film the eye departs **further** but **less often**, so what the instrument shows is a small number of
  long excursions rather than continuous searching. Passing the time is also constrained here in a way it is not at
  Heidegger's station — the participant cannot leave, cannot read a timetable, cannot walk; the counter-move has
  almost nowhere to go but the body and the eye. What the counter-move looked like beyond the eye is exactly what
  this pass did not instrument: the search behaviour visible in the session video was not measured here, and the
  section says that rather than converting it into stillness.
- **L4.4 The third form, as analog only — better evidenced, and therefore under harder caution.** The evidence has
  genuinely strengthened since v1. The strongest surface in the whole analysis is now eye closure; S05's 449.2 s
  closure and S08's 124.5 s stand as extended withdrawals of a different order from any blink; felt duration is
  dilated in 25 of 36 episodes; and the frequency result gives the zombie-stare account its first correction-
  surviving measurement. **Precisely because the analog is better evidenced, the category caution binds harder,
  not more loosely.** This is episodic structural grammar only. A seventeen-minute film in a laboratory cannot
  produce the form Heidegger describes, whose scope is beings as a whole; what the instruments show are episodic
  structures that resemble parts of its anatomy, and they are named as resemblances every time. Sleep-exit remains
  its own terminal category and is not the deepest boredom but boredom's escape — the entranced while requires a
  held witness, and S05 stopped being one.
- **L4.5 Against stimulus-essentialism — the anomaly pair becomes an anomaly set.** v1 had two cases. v2 has six
  kinds of case and they make a stronger argument together than separately: **S04's inversion** (the tutorial
  engaging, the clinical film maximally boring, fatigue restored 7 → 1); **S06's boredom on the interesting film**;
  **R2-03, whom nothing bored**; **S03's extremity** (bored at 25 seconds, the episode felt as an hour); **the four
  within-instrument divergent episodes across three participants**; and **the round-level non-replication** — in
  Round 1 the clinical film sits at the engaged extreme, while in Round 2 the engaged extreme is the interesting
  film. The same footage bores, engages, restores and repels. Boredom is a **relation**, not a property of a film
  (FCM §§20–22), and the anomaly set is what demonstrates it.
  `O-07` decides whether prior exposure enters as an analytic variable or a footnote; the strata remain
  near-indistinguishable on the clinical film at N=12, so the exposure objection is still answered, descriptively,
  at 8 against 4.
- **L4.6 The aftermath.** Depletion after the boring film, as the one lane in this corpus running laboratory →
  philosophy: FCM treats boredom as disclosive and never as depleting. `RE-PIN` the depletion counts from
  `out/survey-episodes.csv` and `out/selfreport-surfaces.csv` at drafting — v1's "6/8" is a Round-1-era figure
  computed under a different definition, the pooled item does not separate the films (Friedman p = 0.3965), and the
  claim must be made descriptively at episode level or not at all. S04's −6 belongs here as the counter-instance:
  for one participant the boring film was restorative.

### L5. What the Laboratory Licenses — redeems M7.1, and states one withdrawal

- **L5.1 The divergence index respecified.** Composite physiological engagement minus retrospective self-report,
  read **signed rather than absolute**. The old gaze-minus-EEG formula is void: it differences two measures of the
  same layer. The specification now carries what v1 could not — which channels may enter the composite (pupil and
  eye openness, both Holm-surviving and both computed from raw per-sample data), which may not (anything
  rate-dependent, if the index is to span instrument generations), and what the sensitivity sweep showed about how
  much the category counts depend on that choice.
- **L5.2 The three states made concrete, with the evidence each requires.** Restless search / occupied-but-hollow /
  withdrawn, plus **sleep-exit as a fourth terminal category**. v2 can now say what evidence each state needs and
  which of it this study actually holds: the withdrawn state has eye closure, the strongest surface in the analysis;
  occupied-but-hollow has the two-channel disagreement and the criterion analysis that makes the disagreement
  meaningful; sleep-exit has S05; and **restless search is the state this pass could not instrument** — see L5.3.
- **L5.3 ★ THE WITHDRAWAL.** The Round 2 reconnaissance proposed that the headset's inertial measurement unit
  supplies the visible-restlessness channel and "supersedes the ch-obs rationale entirely." **That claim is
  withdrawn.** Three reasons, all of which belong in the prose. Head motion is **deferred by ruling** and was not
  read in this pass (R-03, R-04). The inertial streams are present in only nine of the twelve Round 2 cells, and a
  within-subject three-way comparison needs all three films from one participant — **only one participant has
  that**, so the channel could not carry a cohort statistic even if it were read. And the channel it would have
  replaced was itself closed on validity rather than on effort: optical flow from the first-person feed tracks the
  stimulus's visual richness rather than the participant's motion, the background being a featureless black void
  for two of the three conditions.
  **What this leaves standing is a limitation, not a capability**, and the section states it as one: the restlessness
  channel remains unsupplied. What it would take is a body or face camera, or a virtual environment with a textured
  surround for every condition — recorded deliberately, not recovered afterward. Future work, gesture scale.
- **L5.4 Back to the deployment.** What the survey could hear only as an echo, the laboratory shows as a measurable
  disagreement between two channels — and what the laboratory cannot show is what a cohort-scale deployment could.

### L6. Limits — GROWN

*v1 listed six limits in a single paragraph. v4 has earned more, and four of them are new.*

- **L6.1 ★ Gaze is eye-in-head, not head pose.** Both rounds record it that way, confirmed three ways including the
  overlay video, where the head rolls roughly 90° and pitches far down while the gaze marker stays near frame
  centre; Round 2's combined gaze never exceeds 38.8°, with zero samples beyond 50°. A participant who turns their
  head away from the film registers no deviation on any gaze channel in this analysis.
- **L6.2 ★ Head movement is deferred, and it is where disengagement is visible.** Stated in the permitted form and
  never in any other: the eye channels show withdrawal, and the search behaviour visible in the session video was
  not instrumented in this pass. Never that the boring film produced stillness.
- **L6.3 ★ The window difference between the rounds.** Round 1's window is the middle ~13.6 minutes, Round 2's
  essentially the whole film. Because closure accumulates, the difference is not neutral; it was measured, and the
  measurement is itself the limit's answer — openness is invariant to it, pupil is not.
- **L6.4 ★ The openness proxy.** Round 1's openness is a validity-flag proxy, not a measurement of the eyelid. It is
  licensed at r = 0.998 inside Round 2, which is strong, and it is still a proxy — and it is the proxy underneath
  the section's headline surface, so the limit is stated where a reader will look for it.
- **L6.5** N=12 across two instrument generations, with no formal power; Round 2 alone is n = 4 and descriptive
  throughout, where the signed-rank floor of p = 0.125 makes perfect separation the strongest obtainable result.
- **L6.6** The criterion comparison is **episode-level throughout** — each physiological figure aggregates a whole
  seventeen-minute film and each rating is given in the break afterward — so nothing here time-locks a moment of
  experience to a moment of signal. The coefficients describe this group and support no population estimate.
- **L6.7** The clinical film's position **does not replicate** across rounds: in Round 2 the engaged extreme is the
  interesting film. The keystone rests on N=12 and this is the honest qualification travelling with it.
- **L6.8** EEG is N=8 permanently, Round 2 having no EEG; HRV is excluded from both rounds and is recoverable;
  cognitive load and heart rate are appendix-only, with their flatness printed as the evidence.
- **L6.9** Category caution — episodic structural grammar, never a *Grundstimmung* claim. No learning-outcome
  claims anywhere.
- **L6.10** DA-08: the canonical account is individualist by construction and the participants here are a cohort.
- **L6.11** Laboratory ↔ deployment transfer limits.

`O-11` — accepting as a stated limit that Round 1 measures the eye only — lands in this module, and its resolution
also governs how L6.1 and L6.2 are worded.
