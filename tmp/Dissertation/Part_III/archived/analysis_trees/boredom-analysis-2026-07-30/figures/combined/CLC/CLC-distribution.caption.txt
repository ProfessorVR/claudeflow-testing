Clinical stimulus, one panel per channel, every one of the 12 subjects plotted as a point (Round 1 circles n=8, Round 2 triangles n=4). Rounds are drawn as SEPARATE boxes throughout and are never merged, because gaze-deviation channels are rate-dependent: recomputing the identical feature on Round 2 decimated to Round 1's effective rate moves it by 0.65-0.84x. Channels marked [not poolable] must not be compared across the round boundary even where the panel places them side by side. This figure is descriptive; the tests across stimuli are in the slope plot and the forest plot.
