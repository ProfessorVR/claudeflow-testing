## cross-cutting/channel-correlation

Spearman correlation across cells, computed WITHIN each round and never pooled, because two of the six channels are rate-dependent and the rounds differ by a factor of ~36 in effective sampling rate. Round 1 n=24 cells, Round 2 n=12 cells - both DESCRIPTIVE at the subject level (8 and 4 subjects). Right panel is the check that licenses Round 1's openness proxy: within Round 2, where both measures exist, the validity-flag proxy and the vendor's continuous openness correlate at Pearson r=0.9979 across the 12 cells, reproducing the r=0.998 the specification cites. Without that agreement the pooled openness result could not be stated.

## cross-cutting/eeg-justification

EEG is N=8 permanently - Round 2 has no EEG. Every cell is reported and NONE is excluded for being noisy, because the retention figures are themselves the evidence for the ruling that EEG was too noisy to carry a finding; removing the worst cells would remove the justification. Retention averages 62.3% (range 36.3-74.4%, 3 of 24 cells below 50%). Test: Friedman across the three stimuli, n=8 - 0 of 5 surfaces reach alpha=0.05, and 0 of 15 pairwise contrasts survive Holm. Keystone concordance is 4/8, which is exactly chance. The channel is reported in full and no finding rests on it.

## cross-cutting/forest-effect-sizes

All 87 pairwise contrasts computed anywhere in the analysis, plotted on one axis; NOTHING is omitted for being non-significant. Test: Wilcoxon signed-rank, Holm-corrected within each surface x scope family; effect size is the matched-pairs rank-biserial correlation. 9 contrasts survive Holm, and every one of them is on eye openness or pupil dilation - which is the finding: the result is carried by the eye-tracking surfaces, not spread across the channel set. Contrasts at n<6 are descriptive; the Round-2-only channels sit at n=4, where the Wilcoxon floor is p=0.125.
