One point per subject-stimulus (n=12 subjects x 3 stimuli = 36); the clinical stimulus is filled and labelled. Boredom >= 5 and composite > 0 define the quadrants. On clinical, N=12: divergent 8, concordant-engaged 4, concordant-bored 0, reverse 0. The composite is the mean within-subject z of pupil dilation, cognitive load and eye openness; gaze-deviation variance is excluded from it because it is rate-dependent and the composite spans both rounds. Both axes are WITHIN-SUBJECT relative: a negative composite means below that subject's own three-video mean, not physiologically bored in absolute terms. No omnibus test is run on the quadrant counts.
