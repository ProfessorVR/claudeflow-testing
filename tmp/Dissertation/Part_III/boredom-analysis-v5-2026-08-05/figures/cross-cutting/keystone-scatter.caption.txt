One point per subject-stimulus (n=12 subjects x 3 stimuli = 36); the clinical stimulus is filled and labeled. Boredom >= 5 and composite > 0 define the quadrants. On clinical, N=12: divergent 5, concordant-engaged 4, concordant-bored 3, reverse 0. The composite is composite A, the mean within-subject z of eye closure and gaze deviation (per-participant baseline); cognitive load is excluded by author ruling R-01 and gaze deviation because it is rate-dependent while the composite spans both rounds. Both axes are WITHIN-SUBJECT relative: a negative composite means below that subject's own three-video mean, not physiologically bored in absolute terms. No omnibus test is run on the quadrant counts.
