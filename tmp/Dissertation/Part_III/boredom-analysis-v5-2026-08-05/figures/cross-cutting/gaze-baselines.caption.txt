The agreement analysis is entirely within-subject, and the per-participant baseline is the only one of the three built the same way: it removes what differs between people and does not matter - how the headset happened to sit on that face - while preserving what differs within a person and does matter, whether they held the eye differently during one film than another. The per-file baseline recenters inside each episode and erases that between-film difference by construction. The device-forward baseline keeps the difference but carries the headset-fit variation with it, which is why it is used for stimulus separation, where between-person differences are not what the test compares. The per-participant baseline was computed for this figure from v3 onward and was scored against self-report for the first time in the v5 pass (X-06), where it is the strongest criterion channel in the study.
