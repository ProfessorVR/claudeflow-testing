## Movement I — ¶1 (~306 w)

**N1.** Two literatures have used one word for twenty-four years without reading each other.
- *evidence* — E01: the only connection is one author citing himself across his own two programs, plus a single critical footnote.

**N2.** The laboratory's own studies stand in a third lineage and drew their operative definition from the second.
- *elaboration* — the third lineage is clinical-immersion engineering education, neither of the two.
- *concession, footnote* — E18: the eleven edited-collection chapters get a footnote at most; roughly thirty-five of the fifty-five surveyed works appear as citations without discussion; the works cited carries all of them.

**C1 vs N1** — *Twenty-four years of mutual silence is a bibliometric artifact. Two fields with different vocabularies and venues have no occasion to cite each other, and non-citation is not incompatibility.*
→ **CONCEDE-AND-LIMIT.** Concede that non-citation alone establishes nothing. The limit: ¶1 states the fact and does not diagnose it; ¶¶9–10 supply a reason that is not bibliometric.

**C2 vs N2** — *Taking a definition from the psychological literature is what a study is supposed to do. This is methodological hygiene, not a defect.*
→ **ANSWER, deferred to ¶8** (same objection at larger scale = C9). ¶1 states the adoption without evaluative loading; ¶8 answers it under 0a's ruling — a consequence of the definition, never a criticism of the studies.

## Movement III — ¶¶5–7 (~748 w)

### ¶5 (~272 w)
**N6.** The EEG tradition nearest this paradigm carries unresolved baseline and correlation problems.
- *evidence* — E05 Kim: thirteen analyzed participants, no significant correlation between questionnaire scores and any physiological feature.
- *evidence* — E06 Barry: alpha indexes global arousal rather than topography; eyes-closed and eyes-open are non-equivalent baselines.
- *evidence* — E07 Yuvaraj: no resting baseline of any kind, both conditions active eyes-open video-viewing; «Q16».
- *elaboration* — Miyauchi, Perone, Yakobi, Seo compactly. E12 governs: no quotation is drawn from Miyauchi.

### ¶6 (~272 w)
**N7.** In the eye-tracking tradition, ocular measures reliably index something other than the reported construct.
- *evidence* — Holmqvist as the reporting standard.
- *evidence* — E08/E09 Scharinger 2015: pupil dilation and EEG alpha both registered the load manipulation and did not correlate with each other (r = −.16, −.14, ns), alpha alone tracking comprehension.
- *evidence* — van den Brink: pupil tracking lapses of attention.
- *evidence, third instance* — E13 VanderWerf: blink duration and amplitude vary systematically with vertical gaze position, so any blink corpus recorded during stimulus presentation is an unknown mixture of elicitation classes; «Q19».
- *restatement* — channels registering one manipulation routinely fail to agree, which makes an oppositional ranking a finding rather than an anomaly.

**C7 vs N7** — *If ocular measures do not index the reported construct, why does this section rest its headline on an ocular measure?*
→ **CONCEDE-AND-LIMIT.** The claim is specific: pupil indexes load and attention rather than boredom-as-reported, and blink corpora are mixtures. It is a distinction among ocular measures, not a verdict on all of them. **The limit is stated without promising a result** — L1 does not pre-empt L3.

### ¶7 (~204 w)
**N8.** A negative through-line runs across this literature, so this section's own EEG negative is characteristic rather than anomalous.
- *evidence* — Kim's null (E05, identical value), Perone's unsupported first hypothesis, Yakobi's uncorrelated markers «Q17», Nacke's non-separating flow subscale «Q21», Miyauchi's alpha indexing appraisal.
- *restatement* — the characterization, stated flatly.

**C6 vs N8** — *Listing other studies' nulls to soften your own is special pleading.*
→ **ANSWER.** Offered as a description of the literature's evidential situation, not as an excuse, and written so — no mitigating verbs, no cushioning.
