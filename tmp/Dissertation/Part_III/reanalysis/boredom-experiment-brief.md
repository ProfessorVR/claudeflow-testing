## 2. The keystone, restated on the wider record

The published contradiction — 2023 EEG reads clinical as boring-like while 2024 gaze-variance reads it as engaging-like (p = 0.0004), with self-report engaged and felt time dilated — is the **second form's signature**: occupied surface, hollow depth, detectable only as the *disagreement of a surface and a depth channel* (FH: being-bored-*with*, §§24–28 — emptiness "arises from out of Dasein itself," p. 128 / GA 192–94 / pdf 76; the *Zeitvertreib* become "our entire comportment," p. 112 / GA 169–70 / pdf 68). [P — the keystone mapping; survives EEG discount via the felt-time-vs-gaze fallback.]

The FE-U record adds a third rendering the published papers could not show: **the divergence appears *inside* the self-report itself.** S07 reports the clinical video at boredom 6 *and* engagement 7; the interesting video at boredom 7 *and* engagement 6. Asked two questions instead of one, the subject refuses the bipolar axis and reports both poles at once — a second-form-shaped answer sheet [P]. This is precisely why a bipolar engaged↔bored scale is disqualified in principle (report IV.4.3): whenever the instrument allows the two layers to speak separately, they do.

## 3. Three-form re-reading

**Form 1 — becoming-bored-by (determinate culprit, restless *Zeitvertreib*).** The boring stimulus behaves exactly as the first form predicts: near-immediate onset (≤5 min in 7/8; two subjects under 40 seconds), high sleep-fight (≥6 in 6/8), felt-time stretched to 20–60 min, and P2's **high gaze-variance searching** as the visible passing-the-time [FE/FE-U; P mapping; FH: "holds us in limbo and yet leaves us empty," p. 87 / GA 130–32 / pdf 56; the watch-glance as "helpless gesture," p. 97].

**Form 2 — being-bored-with (occupied surface, hollow depth).** The clinical stimulus is the second-form site: gaze engaged (P2), EEG hollow (P1, corroborating), self-report split (S07), felt-time dilated in 6/8 despite "interest" [P]. The FE-U boredom mean of 6.0 on clinical *with* engagement answers ≥5 in half the cohort is the in-between profile the bridge predicted for the wider cohort.

**Form-3-analog — the exit, not just the stare.** P2's zombie stare (collapsed variance, 2 subjects) maps to the withdrawn low-arousal pole [P, analogy-discipline]. The FE-U record adds the limit case: **S05 fell asleep during the boring video and felt it as *5 minutes*** — shorter than actual. Where dilation is the signature of being-held (wakeful *Hingehaltenheit*), sleep is not maximal boredom but boredom's *escape*: the entranced while requires a held witness, and when the witness exits, the long while vanishes with it [P; FH: the third form's stillness is "a being compelled to listen," p. 136 — sleep is precisely no longer listening]. Methodological consequence: felt-duration probes saturate weirdly at the sleep boundary; a three-state classifier must treat sleep-exit as its own terminal category, not as extreme boredom.

## 4. Temporality: the felt-duration column as *Hingehaltenheit* data

Felt duration exceeds the 17-min actual in 16 of 24 video-episodes, across ALL stimulus types — dilation is the default under laboratory viewing, sharpest where boredom is high (S03's boring: "1 hour"). Two structural observations [P]:
1. **Dilation tracks the being-held structure, not the stimulus category** — the interesting video dilates too (5/8), consistent with FCM's claim that what bores is "neither beings nor things as such … but temporality as such" (p. 158 / GA 236 / pdf 90): the lab situation itself (strapped into an HMD, held before a mandated video) is a *Hingehaltenheit* apparatus.
2. **The drag begins early**: median boredom onset on boring is ≤5 min of a 17-min stimulus — the remaining 12+ minutes are endured *as* dragging while. This is the datum the deployment survey's C1-DRAG codes should be read against.

## 5. Carryover and depletion

Boring viewing raises fatigue in 6/8 (S03: 3→8; S07: 3→7) [FE-U], corroborating P2's published order-change rationale (boring-first "increased levels of exhaustion") [FE]. Two order artifacts matter: S04 entered clinical at fatigue 7 (post-interesting) and gave it boredom 9 — prior depletion inflates boredom on the next stimulus; and S04's boring video *restored* him (fatigue 7→1, engagement 7). FCM leaves boredom's aftermath untheorized (disclosive, never depleting — report Tension 3); the empirical record insists on it. This is the one bridge lane that runs lab→philosophy [P], and it triangulates with attention residue (Mark p. 97, Leroy).

## 6. Anomaly cases — against stimulus-essentialism

- **S04's inversion** (clinical = 9-bored; Word tutorial = 7-engaged, felt 8 min, fatigue 7→1): after a depleting clinical episode, the low-demand tutorial arrives as *relief* — Mark's rote state as restful occupation (engaged-unchallenged) [P]. Boredom is not a property of the video.
- **S06 bored by the interesting video** (8, onset 1 min) and mild on boring (4, engagement 5) — taste/expertise variance; echoes P3's expert-disenchantment pattern.
- **S07's double-high answers** — see §2.
These cases are the dissertation's evidence that the *cause-effect and subject/object-property schemata fail* for boredom, exactly as FCM's first-form analysis dismantles them (§§19–23) [P]: the same stimulus is boring, engaging, restful, and intolerable depending on the episode's temporal-dispositional situation.
