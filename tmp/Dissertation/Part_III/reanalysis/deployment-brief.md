# Reanalysis Brief — The VLE in Deployment (P3 + the three-term survey corpus)

**Phase 4 deliverable.** Built 2026-07-06. Companions: `boredom-experiment-brief.md` (the lab) · `cross-case-synthesis.md`. Sources: P3 digest (FCM bridge-sources; canonical) → survey-analysis 00–05 → PDFs for quote verification at drafting. Provenance: FE / FE-U / P as standing.

## 1. The pilot record (P3, spring 2022, N=22/20) [FE]
Four formats of the same OR footage (2D YouTube / 2D-PC Unreal / Cardboard / Quest 2). **Presence ≠ preference:** 70% reported greater felt presence in VR, yet 40% judged plain 2D most beneficial (VR combined 40%; worst = Cardboard 40%). Reasons for 2D: convenience/ease 44.5%, discomfort/disorientation 38.9%; the library headset was "a hassle" — "not a single student" checked one out. **Expert disenchantment:** more prior VR/gaming experience → more likely to prefer 2D. The VR-preferring minority cited embodied first-person presence (66.6%) and distraction-removal (26.7%). Limits: no boredom operationalization, no time probe; open-access deployment let students default to the convenient format.

## 2. The institutional resolution [FE facts; P reading]
Between pilot and deployment the course **moved to a 2D PC-based experience as primary** — full-size, fully-online, asynchronous VR did not scale (author, 2026-07-06). **[Scaling rationale, AUTHOR-CONFIRMED 2026-07-15:** the concrete driver was headset logistics — acquiring and distributing 120+ Meta Quest headsets to students every quarter was simply not possible; PC was chosen first as the largest student platform population (vs. Mac).**]** The course thus resolved P3's central tension exactly as its students had: presence lost to feasibility. Read through Wendt: **availability is an A₀-level design condition** — the horizon an institution can actually furnish. This is not a fall from the "real" VR design problem but the discovery of the actual one: the 2D route is the route that must be designed well, and the surveyed deployment is its test.

## 3. The deployment record (W25/S25/W26, N=241, 873 responses) [FE]
One hybrid artifact, three routes: desktop-3D app (~51% every term), YouTube fallback (~44%, term-stable, V=.009), headsets effectively unused. Immersion-Yes among app-users 70.7% pooled (87/123 of app-users answering the immersion item Yes/No; corrected from ≈69%, 2026-07-23) — **P3's 70% presence figure replicated at scale on the screen route.** Full findings in `survey-analysis/05-findings.md` (F1–F8); the brief integrates them as one narrative:

**The geography of foreclosure has two zones.** Outside the world: install/compatibility/hardware (the video-only stratum's story — the Windows-only build, the download labor, the sign-in; 61 students never got in). Inside the world: crashes (29), navigation (21), latency (25) — the app-users' story. *[Counts throughout refreshed 2026-07-23, all-Fable recoding — see `survey-analysis/07-repass-impact-memo.md`.]* The Wendt seed-read predicted the inside zone correctly (lag CONFIRMED, nav CONFIRMED) and could not see the outside zone from a dev machine; avatar occlusion went unattested because solo desktop use removed its conditions. [FE; P: the world's first gate is the installer.]

**The world gives something to see and nothing to do.** Interest/content is the massive afford channel (D2: 166, incl. 16 incorporation-positive co-codes); action is the missing one (D3: 37, one positive co-code corpus-wide [W25-R044/Q10]; 57 interactivity requests; "just being playback of videos"; "make assignments based on the VR"). The chain is solicited at A₂/A₃ and foreclosed at A₃→A₄ — the seed-read's predicted first-form-boredom risk, now student-attested. The passivity surface: "a lot of space of inactivity. Videos were unnecessarily long"; "many hours long, and I'm not sure what exactly to be looking for during that time." [FE; P]

**The flight to video is the deployment's *Zeitvertreib*.** Fallback (48 coded) splits into excluded flight (couldn't run it) and **hassle flight despite access** (n=47): "not worth the effort to even learn how to use it and download it all when I could just pull it up on youtube." The effort-arithmetic students perform is P3's convenience finding operating at scale — boredom's avoidance route institutionalized as a course resource. [FE; P]

**The late question is not friction but justification.** Frictions decline across terms (crash 21→4→4) while value-skepticism rises (1.3 / 0.7 / 4.2 per-100): "the youtube videos were enough"; "less dependency on the VR aspect altogether." ## 4. Boredom-form diagnosis at deployment scale [P throughout]
- **Form 1 dominates and is design-legible:** determinate culprits everywhere (the friction catalogue), plus monotony/length complaints on the passive footage. Every form-1 culprit is a design lever.
- **Form-2 candidacy lives in the justification-demand:** the no-culprit residue + the within-respondent divergence (W26-R037) + the arc from "broken" to "pointless." A single-channel survey cannot certify it; the *shape* is second-form (occupied offering, hollow yield).
- **Form 3 does not appear** in course-survey talk (as expected; withdrawal shows as non-response and non-use, which the instrument records as silence).
- **The temporal column is mute** (C1-DRAG = 1) — retrospective surveys don't elicit felt time; the lab record carries that thread (see boredom brief §4).

## 5. Design notes (feed §III.5; Wendt playbook keyed by node)
1. **A₀/threshold:** browser-deliverable or pre-installed access; Mac support; zero-setup entry. The biggest single lever — it re-admits the excluded half.
2. **A₃→A₄:** give the world a task — in-world needs-capture, checkpoints, "options on which route you want to take," assignments that must be done *in* the environment. Answers both the stall and the justification-demand.
3. **A₂/legibility:** footage quality, captions/transcripts (repeated accessibility requests), guidance on *what to look for* (an *orekton* supplied: "I'm not sure what exactly to be looking for").
4. **Co-disclosedness:** the WE-affordance (proximity-voice co-watching) and group assignment BOTH already exist [corrected 2026-07-13]; the design lever is to **collapse the activation energy of the synchronous meet** — default/scheduled co-watch blocks, graded group session — so the differentiator isn't left to lose, every term, to the convenience gradient. NOT "supply a missing WE" (it isn't missing) — the gap is student-side activation.
5. **The motto:** design against "why not just YouTube?" — every element must do something video cannot (task, place, together), or it will be fled to the cheaper route.
