The corpus's deepest methodological fact: **wherever an instrument allows two layers to speak separately, they diverge** — across instruments (EEG vs gaze), within one instrument (boredom-6 ∧ engagement-7), within one respondent (main-pro ∧ not-more-immersive), within one sentence ("the idea is amazing, the videos not so much"). ## 2. Why elements bore — the account

**(i) Boredom is never a stimulus property.** The same Word tutorial bores seven subjects and restores the eighth (fatigue 7→1, engagement 7); the "interesting" video bores S06 in a minute; the clinical footage is the most presence-praised AND most fled content in the deployment. The cause-effect and object-property schemata fail empirically exactly where FCM dismantles them conceptually (§§20–22). What bores is a *relation*: the episode's temporal-dispositional situation meeting a world's offer. [FE-U/FE; P]

**(ii) The two moments have empirical faces.** *Being-left-empty* (Leergelassenheit): the world's offer withdraws solicitation — grainy dark footage "hard to really understand what was going on," hours of material with "not sure what exactly to be looking for" (an *orekton* withheld: nothing determinate to want), lobby "modelled poorly… I didn't find it to be very academic." *Being-held-in-limbo* (Hingehaltenheit): mandated duration without task — 17 lab minutes felt as 20–60; "space of inactivity" in "unnecessarily long" videos; buffering as micro-limbo (3-sec waits between intention and image). Boredom arrives where the two meet: **held before an offer that gives nothing to complete.** [P; FE anchors throughout]

**(iii) The stall has an address: A₃→A₄.** Across all cases the strongest single pattern is solicited attention with foreclosed completion. The lab's clinical video interests without tasking (engagement claims + boredom onsets at 2–10 min); the deployment affords interest massively (D2) and action not at all (D3, zero positive co-codes; 51 requests). In chain terms: the environment reliably produces A₂/A₃ (charged images, committal interest) and reliably fails to open A₄ (*praxis*). **A world that cannot be acted in can only be watched, and watching has a boredom half-life.** This is the dissertation's design-relevant discovery, predicted by the Wendt seed-read and student-attested at scale. [P; FE]

**(iv) Boredom's avoidance route is design-visible.** *Zeitvertreib* appears at three scales: in-episode (the searching gaze; the watch-glance "helpless gesture"), in-course (the flight to YouTube — effort-arithmetic toward the cheaper route, 47 students despite access), and institutional (the 2D-PC move itself — the course performing the same convenience resolution its students perform). Passing-the-time is not noise around the data; it IS the data's shape. [FE; P]

**(v) The deepest deployment question is second-form-shaped.** When frictions fell, the complaint became "why does this exist?" — occupied offering, hollow yield, no culprit to name. A survey cannot certify the second form (one channel), but the *arc* — from determinate culprits to a justification-demand — is the cohort-level silhouette of being-bored-*with*: everything works, and it is still empty. The design consequence differs in kind from bug-fixing: form-1 responds to repair; the justification-demand responds only to **a for-the-sake-of-which** — a reason this world, rather than a video, is where learning happens. [P]

**(vi) The aftermath is real and untheorized.** Boring episodes deplete (fatigue up 6/8; boring-first exhausted P2's original order; Mark/Leroy's attention residue names the psychology). FCM treats boredom as disclosive, never as depleting — the one lane where this corpus sends traffic back toward the philosophy. Design implication: pacing, washout, and sequence are boredom variables, not logistics. [FE-U/FE; P; open problem]

**(vii) The gradient matters, but only with the action pole.** Phantasia-load orders the routes (HMD → desktop-3D → passive video): presence is attested on desktop and asked-for more intensely (provisioning requests), and the video route is the flattest. But the skepticism arc shows intensity alone does not justify a world — G&G's lesson from Part II recurs empirically: incorporation consummates at A₃→A₄, not at image richness. **Route sets the load; the task sets the dwelling.** [P; FE anchors]

## 3. What the synthesis yields for the three Part III goals
1. **Boredom explained (goal 1):** the empirical results arise as the frame predicts — divergence wherever layers speak separately; dilation under wakeful holding; culprit-boredom where design forecloses; the justification-demand where it merely runs. The frame explains HOW and WHY (the two moments, the stall's address, the avoidance routes) without ever needing the bipolar construct the instruments kept refuting.
2. **Engagement assessed by the author's own methodology (goal 2):** presence ≠ incorporation held up at every scale (P3's split; the deployment's presence-with-skepticism; the lab's occupied-but-hollow clinical episodes). Engagement that lasts is chain-completion, not attention capture.
3. **Design (goal 3, → §III.5):** five levers, ranked — threshold (re-admit the excluded), task (open A₄; answers the justification-demand), *orekton*-guidance (what to look for), the WE (**collapse the activation energy of the already-present co-watching affordance** — proximity-voice + assigned groups both exist; the lever is scheduled/graded synchronous meets, NOT supplying a missing WE; corrected 2026-07-13, see `survey-analysis/05-findings.md` F7 note), pacing/washout (respect the aftermath). Motto: *design against "why not just YouTube?"* — with Mark's capture-economy as the adversarial mirror (the same knowledge that exploits a stalled chain can restart one) and Wendt principle 10 as the norm.
