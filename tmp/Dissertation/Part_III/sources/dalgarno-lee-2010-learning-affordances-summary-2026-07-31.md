## 1. What the paper is

The founding theory-model of desktop-3D VLE pedagogy — a conceptual paper, not an empirical one. It collects no data. Its aim is to isolate what is *specifically* distinctive about 3-D virtual learning environments against both 2-D digital alternatives and learning-management systems, and to derive a defensible list of learning **affordances** — task-possibilities an environment makes available to an agent (after Gibson 1979 and Greeno 1994) — from two decades of published applications. The motivating problem is the field's evidentiary weakness: "very few empirical studies" document knowledge or skill gains for desktop-3D over equivalent 2-D delivery (10), against a literature the authors call "largely 'show-and-tell', presenting only anecdotal evidence or personal impressions that cannot be usefully generalised beyond the local context" (23).

## 2. The model

**Two categories of distinguishing characteristics** (ten items total), which function as the model's independent variables:

- **Representational fidelity** (six): realistic environment display; smooth view/motion changes; consistent object behavior; user representation (avatar); spatial audio; kinaesthetic/tactile feedback (15, Table 1). Brna's "social fidelity" is folded in for multi-user contexts (16).
- **Learner interaction** (four): embodied actions (view control, navigation, manipulation); embodied verbal and non-verbal communication; control of environment attributes; construction/scripting of objects (16–17, Table 1).

**The pivotal theoretical move:** presence, co-presence, and identity construction are **not** treated as properties of the technology. Adopting Slater's immersion/presence distinction (immersion = objective system properties; presence = the subjective sense of being in a place), the model positions them as *consequences*: presence and co-presence arise as "a consequence of the representational fidelity and learner interactions facilitated by the environment" (14). Co-presence — "being there together" — is explicitly traced to Short, Williams & Christie's 1976 social-presence tradition and Biocca et al. (14). This is the earliest appearance of the social-presence register *inside* the VR-pedagogy literature — the same junction M1's seam paragraph documents as otherwise untrafficked.

## 3. The five learning affordances (verbatim)

1. "3-D VLEs can be used to facilitate learning tasks that lead to the development of enhanced spatial knowledge representation of the explored domain" (18).
2. "3-D VLEs can be used to facilitate experiential learning tasks that would be impractical or impossible to undertake in the real world" (18–19).
3. "3-D VLEs can be used to facilitate learning tasks that lead to increased intrinsic motivation and engagement" (19–20) — grounded in Csikszentmihalyi: "the high degree of fidelity and the natural interface of 3-D VEs may increase the likelihood that learners will experience this feeling of flow" (19).
4. "3-D VLEs can be used to facilitate learning tasks that lead to improved transfer of knowledge and skills to real situations through contextualisation of learning" (20–21) — situated-learning grounded (Lave & Wenger; Brown, Collins & Duguid).
5. "3-D VLEs can be used to facilitate tasks that lead to richer and/or more effective collaborative learning than is possible with 2-D alternatives" (23).

Note the grammatical form the authors chose: every affordance is phrased "can be used to **facilitate learning tasks** that lead to…" — the affordance is never the display property itself; it is always a *task-possibility* the environment furnishes. The benefit arrives only if a task actualizes it.

## 4. Results (what the model's own research program returned)

- The paper itself: none — it states plainly that the characteristic→affordance links are *assumptions* requiring validation, and the existing evidence is anecdotal (23).
- **Methodological verdict:** citing Clark's media-vs-method argument, the authors explicitly reject simple 2-D-vs-3-D media comparisons, which would demonstrate only the "trivial fact" that *unharnessed* 3-D affordances yield minimal unique learning benefit (24). (Strand C — Makransky, Parong & Mayer, Barrett — later ran precisely such comparisons; their null-learning results are, on Dalgarno & Lee's own terms, what unharnessed affordances predict.)
- **The 2012 self-test** ("Exploring the Relationship between Afforded Learning Tasks…", ascilite 2012, pp. 236–45): surveying 53 higher-education staff on which afforded tasks yielded which benefits, respondents endorsed every benefit at nearly uniform strength regardless of task; no hypothesized task-to-benefit link reached significance; the authors' own deflating explanation — the uniformity "could be more a reflection of their enthusiasm and positive attitude towards the use of 3D VLEs than an evidence-based assessment of the actual learning benefits" ("Afforded Tasks" 243). The field's central model stands beside its own inconclusive self-test (as M1 ¶1 already states).

## 5. Implications for this section

- **Affordance doctrine convergence.** The paper's Gibsonian grammar — an environment *furnishes task-possibilities*; benefits follow only when a task actualizes them — is the field's own vocabulary for the doctrine ruled 2026-07-31 (`feedback-a3a4-potentiality-always-present`): there is always a potentiality to actualize; the design failure is the worth of what is furnished. Dalgarno & Lee's "unharnessed affordances → minimal unique benefit" (24) is the literature's independent statement of the A₃→A₄ diagnosis: our VLE's video-only content leaves fidelity and interaction characteristics unharnessed by any learning task — exactly the failure mode the model predicts (and exactly what the exemplar bank's monotony/value-skepticism responses voice).
- **Affordance 5 and the WE.** Collaboration is one of the five founding affordances of the desktop-3D medium — richer collaboration *than 2-D alternatives* is part of the medium's original warrant. The unactivated co-watching capability (M3.5/M5.2) is thus not a bonus feature gone unused but one of the medium's five constitutive affordances going unactualized.
- **Presence relocated at the origin.** The founding model already refuses hardware-owned presence (presence/co-presence as consequences of what the environment lets its learner do and see) — M1 ¶1 carries this; it also means our monitor-presence findings confirm the field's own founding theory rather than contradict it.
- **Task-first phrasing worth citing.** When the design analysis (M5) argues the *task* lever, Affordance 2's exact wording ("experiential learning tasks that would be impractical or impossible to undertake in the real world") is the strongest available warrant: the operating room is the paradigm case of a real-world setting students cannot enter — the affordance our VLE was built on — while the *tasks* clause is the part the deployment has not yet furnished.
