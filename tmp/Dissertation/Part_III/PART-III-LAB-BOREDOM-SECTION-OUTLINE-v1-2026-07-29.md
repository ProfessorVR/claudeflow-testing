### L2. The Apparatus and the Record — method
- **L2.1 Three studies, three cohorts, never conflated.** P1 (N=3, one clean EEG), P2 (N=12 gaze), the unpublished
  **N=8** per-subject record. A published-channel derivation is not the raw cohort.
- **L2.2 The instrument.** Seven items per video including **felt duration**; boredom and engagement asked as
  **separate items, not a bipolar axis** — the design choice that makes divergence observable at all.
- **L2.3 The reprocessing (O-9).** Methods taken from the papers themselves, not inferred: F3/F4/P3/P4, 1–35 Hz,
  DMN = alpha+theta; gaze = variance of the 5-point-median deviation from centre. Full N=8.
- **L2.4 What each channel can and cannot say.** EEG SNR and ~60% retention; pupil as the one statistically robust
  surface; **ch-obs probe-closed** (black-void flow confound — a stated methodological limit, not a shortfall);
  and the standing reporting rule: **convergence + pupil, never per-channel significance.**

### L3. The Findings
- **L3.1** Convergence — Boring is the outlier on all five surfaces; Clinical and Interesting cluster engaged.
- **L3.2 ★ The keystone corrected.** P1's headline came from one clean subject; at N=8 clinical DMN is *low* and the
  boring-like reading holds in **4/8 = chance**. Corrected keystone: **all-physiology-engaged vs
  self-report-bored** (clinical self-report 6.0/9, between boring 7.0 and interesting 4.5).
- **L3.3** Pupil — Friedman p=0.030; Boring < Clinical in **all 8**, p=0.008 Holm, rank-biserial −1.0.
- **L3.4** Felt duration — dilation in **16/24 episodes across all three stimulus types**, which is what shows the
  laboratory situation *itself* to be a holding apparatus rather than the stimulus doing the holding.
- **L3.5** The four cases: **S07** (within-instrument divergence), **S05** (sleep-collapse, felt as 5 min),
  **S04** (inversion — clinical 9-bored, the tutorial 7-engaged, fatigue 7→1), **S03** (extremity — "1 hour," onset
  25 s).
- **L3.6** Decomposition and stratum: **4 divergent / 2 concordant-engaged / 2 concordant-bored / 0 reverse** — the
  divergence is unidirectional. **(Med) stratum rates clinical identically (6.00 vs 6.00)**, which answers the
  prior-clinical-exposure objection empirically.

### L4. The Analysis — chain-node walkthrough (THE analytical form)
- **L4.1 The chain in the laboratory.** A₀ = strapped into an HMD under mandate, no leisure motive; A₁/A₂ = the
  charged image; A₃ = committed interest; **A₄ withheld by construction** — the subject may only watch. The
  laboratory is a *Hingehaltenheit* apparatus **by design**, which is why dilation appears on *every* stimulus.
- **L4.2 ★ The second form, instrumented.** The layer assignment argued, not assumed: Heidegger's surface is
  outward comportment ("totally involved," in high spirits), his depth is an emptiness **recognized only
  retrospectively**; the post-video self-report *is* that retrospective recognition. `fcm-05`'s decisive sentence:
  **behavioural engagement is not evidence against boredom; in this form it is the very medium of it.** Note the
  correction this supersedes — the old mapping made a brain measure the depth channel, which `fcm-05` C13 forbids.
- **L4.3 The first form.** The boring stimulus behaves exactly as predicted: onset ≤5 min in 7/8 (two under 40 s),
  high sleep-fight, searching gaze as *Zeitvertreib* operationalized.
- **L4.4 The third form, as analog only.** Zombie stare (2 subjects, P2) + **S05's sleep-exit as its own terminal
  category** — sleep is not maximal boredom but boredom's *escape*; the entranced while requires a held witness.
- **L4.5 Against stimulus-essentialism.** S04's inversion and S06's boredom on the "interesting" film: the same
  footage bores, engages, restores and repels. Boredom is a **relation**, not a property (FCM §§20–22).
- **L4.6 The aftermath.** Depletion in 6/8 after the boring stimulus. FCM treats boredom as disclosive and never as
  depleting — **the one lane in this corpus running laboratory → philosophy.**

### L5. What the Laboratory Licenses — redeems M7.1
- **L5.1** The divergence index **respecified**: composite physiological engagement − retrospective self-report,
  read **signed rather than absolute** (because the divergence is unidirectional, a large negative value means
  something else and should be flagged). The old gaze-minus-DMN formula is void — it differences two measures of the
  same layer.
- **L5.2** The three-state proposal made concrete: restless search / occupied-but-hollow / withdrawn, **plus
  sleep-exit as a fourth terminal category**, with the evidence each state requires.
- **L5.3** Back to the deployment: what the VLE could not measure and now could.
