## Core architectural decision (signed off)
**Strong *doxa*-ratification + tier-split.** *Doxa* = the **involuntary yes-saying / *taking-as-true*** (NOT "acceptance," NOT Dovian "affirmation"). Emotions of the *Rhetoric* = the *doxa*-ratified articulation of the basal hedonic charge; recalcitrance/basal affect = pre-doxastic *phantasia*, conceded to Moss. The disagreement with Moss is surgical: *constitutive* ratification (us) vs. *separable normal accompaniment* (her, AG 99).

## Sign-off ledger
- **Flag 1 — RESOLVED + UPGRADED.** Corrective faculty is **impaired, not down**: *discourse of reason always operates* (so a *doxa* always forms, with conviction), but the *pathos*/drunkenness/fever degrades its **discriminating accuracy** → **conviction ≠ accuracy**. Sorting variable: does the (graded) discriminating *doxa* **contradict** the appearance (sober+rope → basal startle only; conceded) or **ratify** it (sober+bear → fear; impaired → *false doxa* → full mis-grounded emotion)? **Lead example = beer-goggles** (manifestly a judgment); coward/lustful (Moss's passage 11) reclaimed as impaired-faculty *false doxa*; fever = severe-limit only.
- **Flag 2 — RESOLVED + UPGRADED.** *Doxa* IS a mode of being-affected (*paschein*). Payoff: **this is the condition of possibility of rhetoric** — persuasion = alteration of *doxa*; alterability = being-affected (*Metaph.* V.21 1022b15); if *doxa* did not partake of being-affected it would be a necessary truth (triangles/three sides) and rhetoric would be impossible. Keep *doxa* the propositional/truth-committal aspect so it can do constitutive work.
- **Flag 3 — agreed.** *orektikon* = moved-**mover** (acknowledge active outflow at M₃→A₄); *phantastikon* = self-affection (consistent with making-present); reconcile no-seat with the chain via "one substrate, many in being."
- **Flag 4 — agreed.** Akrasia = struggle/competing-desires; distinguish **good-as-judged** (*doxa*) from **good-as-desired/felt** (appetite-pull) to avoid revealed-preference collapse.
