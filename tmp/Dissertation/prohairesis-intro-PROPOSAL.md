## §1.3 — deliberative *phantasia* (RECOMMENDED; clean home)

**Where:** the deliberative-*phantasia* passage (≈ L91), whose **calculative-practical sub-function** "measures the projected possibilities against one another … to determine which course of action shall be enacted … the deliberative process terminates in **a determination of the greater good**." (L93 then calls deliberative *phantasia* "the terminus of practical reasoning" and what "distinguishes rational action from appetitive reaction.")

**Why:** this *terminating determination* simply **is** *prohairesis* — the deliberate choice in which deliberation issues. Deliberative *phantasia* is its cognitive precondition. This is the most natural anchor in the whole chapter.

**Proposed insertion** (append to the L91 sentence ending "…a determination of the greater good."):
> This terminating determination---the resolved settling-upon a realizable course to enact---is what Aristotle calls \textit{prohairesis} (\gk{προαίρεσις}, ``deliberate choice''): we deliberate not about ends but about what contributes to them (\textit{Nicomachean Ethics} III.3, 1112b11--16), and deliberative \textit{phantasia} is the cognitive organ through which the candidate means are projected and weighed until one is chosen. \textit{Prohairesis} accordingly presupposes deliberative \textit{phantasia} as its condition.

## §1.4 — *hexis* bivalence / *praxis* side (CANDIDATE; confirm placement)

**Where:** the *praxis-hexis* paragraph (≈ L199): "The cultivated *praxis-hexis* sustains rather than reduces the capacity for **fresh resolution before the *kairos***; it does not withdraw into transparency but holds the agent open for renewed deliberative engagement on each occasion."

**Why:** the "fresh resolution before the *kairos*" that *praxis-hexis* sustains is precisely *prohairesis* (the deliberate choice answering the situation). Naming it here ties the *praxis* disposition to the prohairetic type.

**Proposed insertion** (append to the L199 sentence):
> The fresh resolution this disposition sustains---the deliberate choice of what to do, here and now, in answer to the \textit{kairos}---is \textit{prohairesis} (\gk{προαίρεσις}, ``deliberate choice''): where the \textit{techn\=e-hexis} contracts deliberation toward routine, the \textit{praxis-hexis} keeps the agent open for renewed prohairetic resolution on each occasion.
