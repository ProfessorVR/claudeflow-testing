# Appendix — The Burke × Calleja Crosswalk (convergence evidence)

**Status:** POPULATED 2026-06-26 from the validated crosswalk artifacts (`validated-crosswalk.json`, `validation-report.md`, 2026-05-27).
**What this appendix is for:** the methodology claims that Burke's pentad and Calleja's PIM, built for wholly different ends, *converge* on a shared core — and that convergence is the evidence licensing their *fusion* (rather than mere juxtaposition) into rhetorical incorporation. This appendix presents that convergence **as evidence, not as method.** The 6×6 pentad×PIM grid (originally the analytic of the "DIA" method) has since been **demoted**: the method is now RODA, which traces the actualization-of-desire chain (A₀–A₄), and the grid no longer functions as the analytic. What survives — and what this appendix preserves — is the grid's *evidentiary* value: two independent inquiries deposited the same structure.
**Provenance (load-bearing):** every pentad↔PIM binding is `anticipatory-projection` — *neither Burke nor Calleja makes the cross-claim.* The bidirectional validation upgrades each binding from "one-directional guess" to "bidirectionally warranted," but they remain the dissertation's projections, not claims by either author.
**Sources:** `corpus/index/Game-Behavior Analysis Method (Burke × Calleja)/validated-crosswalk.json` + `validation-report.md`.

## C.1 The headline — independent convergence on a four-binding core

Two operationalization passes were authored separately and **one-directionally, without coordination**: a Burke→PIM mapping (each pentad term → Calleja dimensions) and a PIM→Burke mapping (each PIM dimension → pentad term). Cross-checking the two directions against each other and against the source texts, they converge on a stable **bijective core of four primary bindings**:

| Burke motive term | Calleja involvement dimension | Both directions | Strength |
|---|---|:--:|---|
| Scene | Spatial | ✅ ✅ | primary — strongest |
| Agent | Shared | ✅ ✅ | primary (deepened by consubstantiation) |
| Agency | Kinesthetic | ✅ ✅ | primary |
| Attitude | Affective | ✅ ✅ | primary — *extended* (Attitude enters from the *Rhetoric*) |

The Attitude↔Affective binding is the sharpest single piece of evidence. Calleja's pipeline had assigned the Affective dimension the disposition-target "Attitude (Burke's later sixth term)" *before* a *Rhetoric* pass existed to supply Attitude as a pentad term; the *Rhetoric* pass then independently derived Attitude as the disposition-term that maps to Affective. The two halves of the bridge were built toward each other blind, and met exactly.

## C.2 The keystone — Consubstantiation ↔ Incorporation

Above the term-by-dimension grid sits the **synthesis-level** binding the whole fusion turns on — and the one DRAFT-v3 foregrounds. It is a *homology, not an equation* (functional, not lexical: Burke never says "embodiment," Calleja never says "substance"):

| Burke — consubstantiation (*Rhetoric*, 21) | Calleja — incorporation (*In-Game*, 169) |
|---|---|
| "substantially one" with the other | R1 — assimilation (environment *into* consciousness) |
| yet a "distinct … locus of motives" | R2 — embodiment (player *into* world, via avatar) |
| "both joined and separate," held at once | R3 — simultaneity (the necessary condition) |
| "a way of life is an acting-together" | R4/R5 — spatial-kinesthetic cornerstone + blending |

Five divergences keep it a homology and not an identity: *medium* of union (symbolic vs. phenomenological); *ontological status* (real-or-persuaded vs. experiential-state); *grounding theory* (substance-metaphysics vs. experientialist cognition); *directionality* (asymmetric-deployed vs. bidirectional); *scope* (society-wide vs. ergodic-bounded). RODA's own grounding — the actualization-of-desire chain, "one in substrate, different in being" — is what reconciles the two without collapsing them.

## C.3 The one productive divergence — the Ludic split

Exactly one binding genuinely disagreed across the two directions: Burke maps **Agency → Ludic** ("rules-as-means"), while Calleja routes **Ludic → Purpose + Act** ("choice-and-repercussion"). Both are textually warranted, so neither was rejected; the fusion **resolves the divergence by splitting the Ludic dimension along Burke's own Agency/Act cut**:

- the rule-*system* (mechanics, cooldowns, economy — the instrument wielded) = **Agency**;
- the act of choosing-under-repercussion (the weighed decision and its consequence) = **Act + Purpose**.

This is not a contradiction patched over but a refinement the fusion produces that neither framework states alone: Calleja's single "Ludic" dimension is dramatistically *two* moments, and Burke's pentad supplies the cut.

## C.5 The methodological reading

Two pipelines that set out from incommensurable grounds — Burke's grammar of motive-attribution, Calleja's phenomenology of involvement — and come to rest on one structure are not thereby *proven* right, but they are strongly **corroborated**: where the same form is deposited by hands that never met, the form most likely lies in the matter and not in the eye. That is the evidentiary work this crosswalk does for the method. It does not supply the analytic — the chain does — but it supplies the warrant for treating world-disclosedness (incorporation) and co-disclosedness (consubstantiation) as the two poles a single convergence brings to light, rather than two lenses laid over a scene.
