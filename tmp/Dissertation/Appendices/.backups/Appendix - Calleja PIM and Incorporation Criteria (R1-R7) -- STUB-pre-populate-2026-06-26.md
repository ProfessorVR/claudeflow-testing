## A.2 The incorporation criteria (R1–R7)

> R3–R7 are Calleja's own five-component test for *incorporation* as against mere *involvement* (fail any one → only involved); R1–R2 are RODA's addition from the double-axis; the numbering and extension are RODA's operationalization. Anchor: *In-Game* pp. 169–173, 178.

- **R1 — Assimilation.** *[TO POPULATE: verbatim + gloss]* (the environment taken into awareness as immediate surroundings).
- **R2 — Embodiment.** *[TO POPULATE]* (the player embodied, via avatar, within that environment — the converse direction).
- **R3 — Simultaneity.** *[TO POPULATE]* (R1 and R2 held at once; the strictest gatekeeper; joined-and-separate).
- **R4 — Cornerstone.** *[TO POPULATE]* (the spatial–kinesthetic base: an oriented body in an inhabited place).
- **R5 — Blending.** *[TO POPULATE]* (several involvement dimensions co-active and fused, not attended in turn).
- **R6 — Temporal apex.** *[TO POPULATE]* (incorporation as the culmination of a sustained arc; deepens with duration).
- **R7 — Intrusion-integration.** *[TO POPULATE]* (real-world stimuli blend in rather than rupturing — the porosity / channel of carry-over).
