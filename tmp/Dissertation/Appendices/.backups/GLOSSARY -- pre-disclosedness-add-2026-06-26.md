## Greek (Aristotelian)

Alphabetical by transliteration. **Chosen rendering** indicates the dissertation's authorial preference; **alternates** indicates standard alternatives that may appear in quoted material (preserved verbatim per V1).

### *aisthēsis* (αἴσθησις)

- **Chosen**: "sense-perception"; in user prose: italicized Greek `*aisthēsis*` (Unicode macron)
- **Alternates**: "perception"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* II.5, 416b32–417a20; III.2, 425b26
- **Definition**: The act/faculty of sense-perception; the joint actualization of the percipient and the perceptible
- **Related**: *aisthēma* (the perceptual content); *aisthētikon* (the sense-faculty); *kritikon* (the discriminative dimension)

### *aisthēma* (αἴσθημα)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "perceptual content"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.2, 425b26ff
- **Definition**: The content of perception; the perceived
- **Used in compounds**: *resonant aisthēma* (dissertation's coined term for the perceptual-content residue at A_1)

### *aisthētikon* (αἰσθητικόν)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "sense-faculty"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* II.5
- **Definition**: The faculty of sense-perception; the sense-power
- **Note**: Part of the *A_0* dyad in the dissertation's restructured chain (per Task #20); replaces "faculty of sense" in user prose per `feedback-greek-faculty-names.md`

### *aisthētērion* (αἰσθητήριον)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "sense-organ"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* II.12, 424a17–25; III.2
- **Definition**: The bodily organ of sense (eye, ear, etc.) that receives the sensible form; the physiological correlate of *aisthētikon*
- **Note**: Replaces "sense-organ" only when emphasizing the embodied/organic dimension; otherwise "sense-organ" stays in prose

### *alēthēs* (ἀληθής)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "true / truth"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.7, 431a–b (with O'Gorman commentary at §1.0 L53)
- **Definition**: The true; truth (especially as the *telos* of contemplative thought)

### *alloiōsis* (ἀλλοίωσις)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "alteration"
- **Locus**: *Physics* VII.3, 245b1–13; *De Anima* II.5
- **Definition**: Qualitative change; specifically the preservative alteration that constitutes perception
- **Used in compounds**: "enmattered preservative *alloiōsis*" (dissertation's framing of perception)

### *anamnēsis* (ἀνάμνησις)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "recollection"
- **Locus**: *On Memory and Recollection* 2, 451b–453a
- **Definition**: Active recollection (distinct from *mnēmē* as memory-state)
- **Related**: *mnēmē*

### *archē* (ἀρχή)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "principle / origin"
- **Locus**: *Metaphysica* V.1 (Δ.1), 1012b34–1013a23 (six senses; chain-relevant = sense 4)
- **Definition**: The productive source from which a thing first arises or motion proceeds; "that from which (not as an immanent part) a thing first arises, and from which the movement or the change naturally first proceeds" (1013a7–10). Aristotle's summary: "it is common, then, to all to be the first point from which a thing either is or comes to be or is known" (1013a17–19)
- **Note**: Convention footnote applied at §1.1 L53 explains the choice of Greek over English "principle"/"origin"

### *boulēsis* (βούλησις)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "rational wish"
- **Alternates**: "wish" (M&R); "deliberative wish"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* II.3, 414b2; *De Anima* III.9–10
- **Definition**: One of the three species of *orexis* — the rational/deliberative wish for the good
- **Related**: *orexis* (genus); *epithymia*, *thymos* (other species)
- **Note**: Triggered by *nous*-mediated deliberation; not couplable to bare *aisthēsis*

### *bouleutikē* (βουλευτική)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "deliberative" (in context: deliberative *phantasia*)
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.11, 434a5–10
- **Definition**: The deliberative grade of *phantasia* that enables composition of multiple *phantasmata* into unified objects of practical reasoning

### *chronos* (χρόνος)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "time"
- **Locus**: *Physics* IV.11, 219b1–2
- **Definition**: Time as "the number of motion in respect of the before and after"
- **Note**: Per Task #20 restructure, *chronos* is part of the encompassing horizon (not a chain-node) in the dissertation's actualization chain

### *diakrisis* (διάκρισις)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "discrimination / distinction"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.7, 431a8–12
- **Definition**: Perceptual discrimination (related to *krisis*); the discriminative-affirming/negating structure of perception

### *doxa* (δόξα)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "opinion / belief"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.3, 428a18–b9
- **Definition**: Opinion; the propositional truth-claim ratified at A_3 in the cognitive actuality
- **Related**: *doxastic* (adjectival form, italicized in dissertation)

### *eidos* (εἶδος)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "form / kind"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* II.1, 412a19–20
- **Definition**: Form (Aristotelian sense); the form of a natural body

### *energeia* (ἐνέργεια)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "actuality / being-at-work"
- **Locus**: *Metaphysica* IX.6; *Physics* III.1
- **Definition**: Actuality; the actively-completed state (contrast: *dynamis* as potentiality)

### *energeia ateles* (ἐνέργεια ἀτελής)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "incomplete actuality"
- **Locus**: *Physics* III.1, 201a10–14
- **Definition**: The actuality of the potential as potential; the in-between status of motion (neither pure potency nor completed actuality)
- **Related**: *entelecheia*, *kinēsis*, Heidegger's *Bewegtheit*

### *entelecheia* (ἐντελέχεια)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "entelechy" / "having-end-within"
- **Locus**: *Metaphysica* IX.8, 1050a21–23
- **Definition**: The actuality-as-completion; etymologically "having-its-end-within" (from *telos* + *echein*)
- **Note**: Burke's interpretive use at *Grammar of Motives* p. 261 (formal priority via entelechy)

### *ἐντελέχεια τοῦ δυνάμει ὄντος*

- **Chosen**: Greek script as Heidegger uses it; English translation in parens
- **English**: "the actuality of what exists potentially, as such"
- **Locus**: *Physics* III.1, 201a10; Heidegger BCAP §26
- **Definition**: Aristotle's definition of motion; Heidegger's BCAP anchor for *Bewegtheit*

### *epithymia* (ἐπιθυμία)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "appetite"
- **Alternates**: "desire" (M&R DA)
- **Locus**: *De Anima* II.3, 414b2; *De Motu Animalium* 6, 700b22
- **Definition**: One of the three species of *orexis* — bodily/appetitive desire; the pursue-pleasant/avoid-painful structure
- **Related**: *orexis* (genus); *thymos*, *boulēsis* (other species)
- **Critical claim**: *Epithymia* is the only orectic species couplable to bare *aisthēsis*; thymos and boulēsis require additional cognitive enrichment (per Task #19)
- **Used in compounds**: *resonant epithymia* (dissertation's coined term for the basic-orectic-charge residue at A_1)

### *ergon* (ἔργον)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "work / function"
- **Locus**: *Nicomachean Ethics* I; *De Anima* II
- **Definition**: The characteristic function or work of a thing

### *hexis* (ἕξις)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "disposition / settled state"
- **Locus**: *Categories* 8, 8b27ff; *Metaphysica* Δ 20; *NE* II.5
- **Definition**: A settled disposition; a stable condition acquired through habituation; the dispositional ground that is shaped by completed actions and shapes future actions
- **Plural**: *hexeis* (ἕξεις) — the diachronic feedback channel (per §1.1 L55)

### *hexeis* (ἕξεις)

- **Plural of *hexis*** — see above

### *hoi kinei* (ᾧ κινεῖ)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "that by means of which (motion is originated)"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.10, 433b13–25
- **Definition**: The middle factor of the three-factor schema — the faculty of appetite/*orektikon* — that "at once moves and is moved"

### *kinēsis* (κίνησις)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "motion / movement"
- **Locus**: *Physics* III.1, 201a10
- **Definition**: Motion; Aristotle: "the fulfilment [*entelecheia*] of what is potentially, as such"; under Heidegger's reading = *Bewegtheit* (being-moved)
- **Note**: Per Task #20, *kinēsis* is part of the encompassing horizon (not a chain-node)

### *kinētikon* (κινητικόν)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "motion-faculty"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.9–10
- **Definition**: The faculty/principle of locomotion; the *orektikon* in its motion-producing aspect; "that by which the soul moves the body" (DA III.10, 433a9–10)
- **Note**: Replaces "faculty of motion" in user prose per `feedback-greek-faculty-names.md`. Closely linked to *orektikon* in the three-factor schema of DA III.10.

### *krisis* (κρίσις)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "discrimination / judgment"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.7, 431a8–12; *Rhetoric* II
- **Definition**: Discrimination; in §1.4 register-context, the propositional-evaluative component of higher-order *pathē*

### *kritikon* (κριτικόν)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "discriminative" (faculty/dimension)
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.7, 431a8–12; Heidegger BCAP p. 126
- **Definition**: The discriminative dimension; the basic hedonic-evaluative structure internal to *aisthēsis* itself (good/bad disclosure co-given with perception)

### *logos* (λόγος)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "account / articulation / structural specification"
- **Locus**: throughout; especially *De Anima* I.1, 403a25–b19 (enmattered formulas)
- **Definition**: Account; articulation; structural-formal specification (NOT generically "reason")
- **Plural**: *logoi*
- **Note**: Used in §1.0 L74 as "different *logos*" — cognition and being-affected as two *logoi* of one actuality

### *mnēmē* (μνήμη)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "memory"
- **Locus**: *On Memory and Recollection* 1, 449b24–451a18
- **Definition**: Memory; the having of a *phantasma* related as *eikōn* to its object
- **Related**: *anamnēsis*

### *noēsis* (νόησις)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "thinking / intellection"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.4–8
- **Definition**: The act of thinking; the noetic capacity in action
- **Related**: *nous* (the noetic faculty); *noetic* (adjectival form)

### *nous* (νοῦς)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "intellect"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.4–8
- **Definition**: Intellect; the noetic faculty
- **Related**: *noēsis*

### *orexis* (ὄρεξις)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "desire" (genus)
- **Alternates**: "appetite" (J.A. Smith DA convention — *opposite* of modern)
- **Locus**: *De Anima* II.3, 414b2; *De Anima* III.9–10
- **Definition**: The GENUS of desire/striving/reaching-out
- **Species**: *epithymia* (appetite); *thymos* (spirit); *boulēsis* (rational wish)
- **Note**: J.A. Smith's DA renders *orexis* as "appetite" and *epithymia* as "desire"; Forster (MA) and Roberts (Rhetorica) use the opposite convention. Dissertation adopts the modern (Forster/Roberts/contemporary scholarship) convention. See §1.0 L78 convention footnote.

### *orektikon* (ὀρεκτικόν)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "the faculty of *orexis*" / "the orectic faculty"
- **Alternates**: "faculty of desire"; "appetitive faculty" (old convention)
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.10, 433b13–25
- **Definition**: The faculty/part of the soul responsible for *orexis*; the moved-mover in the three-factor schema of locomotion
- **Note**: In user prose, no English gloss (per convention footnote at §1.0 L78)

### *orectic* (English adjectival form)

- **Chosen**: italicized in dissertation; "orectic"
- **Definition**: Adjectival form of *orexis*; "*orectic* action" = action driven by *orexis* (any species)
- **Note**: Replaces "appetitive action" in dissertation prose (since "appetitive" specifically tracks *epithymia*)

### *ousia* (οὐσία)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "substance / being"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* II.1, 412a19; *Categories*; *Metaphysica* Z
- **Definition**: Substance; the being-character of beings

### *pathos* (πάθος)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "being-affected" (genus, per Met Δ 21 fourfold)
- **Alternates**: "affection" (translator's English in *De Motu Animalium* per Forster); "passion"
- **Locus**: *Metaphysica* V.21 (Δ.21), 1022b15–21 (the canonical fourfold)
- **Definition**: The general structure of being-affected — the genus of any being's affectability. Admits of articulational levels: less-articulated = basic hedonic tonality co-given with *aisthēsis* (the *kritikon*-internal valence); fully concretized = higher-order *pathē*
- **Cross-cutting axis**: bodily-physiological — every level of *pathos* is realized through enmattered alteration
- **Related**: *pathē* (plural — defaults to higher-order); see §1.0 L101 footnote for full register

### *pathē* (πάθη)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "emotions" (the higher-order, fully concretized form)
- **Alternates**: "affections" (Forster MA at 702a17–19); "passions"
- **Locus**: *Rhetoric* II.1–11; *De Anima* I.1, 403a25–b19 (enmattered formulas)
- **Definition**: The plural form, defaulting to higher-order *pathē* — the determinate emotions of *Rhetoric* II (anger, fear, pity, shame, and their kin). Each comprises a propositional *krisis*, a species-specific orectic stance, and a hedonic tonality
- **Related**: *pathos* (genus)

### *phantasia* (φαντασία)

- **Chosen**: untranslated Greek throughout dissertation; italicized
- **Alternates**: "imagination" (M&R, common); "mental image"; "appearance" (Frede 1992 — gives three functions)
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.3, 427b14–428a18; III.7, 431a14–431b8
- **Definition**: The faculty/capacity of imaging; the temporal medium through which the soul holds together past (memory), present (perception), and future (anticipation)
- **Convention footnote**: §1.0 L51 (Papachristou-anchored)
- **Related**: *phantasma* (the determinate image-product); *phantasmata* (plural)
- **Grades**: sensory *phantasia* (animals) vs. *bouleutikē* deliberative *phantasia* (rational beings)

### *phantasma* (φάντασμα)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "image-product / appearance"
- **Locus**: *On Memory* 1, 451a14–18
- **Definition**: The determinate image-product generated by *phantasia*; the appearance
- **Plural**: *phantasmata*
- **Cross-link**: §1.0 L59 footnote distinguishes *phantasia* (faculty) / *phantasma* (product) / *phantasmata* (manifold)

### *phantasmata* (φαντάσματα)

- **Plural of *phantasma*** — see above

### *phantastic* (English adjectival form)

- **Chosen**: italicized in dissertation
- **Definition**: Adjectival form derived from *phantasia*; e.g., "*phantastic* motion" (M_1→A_2)

### *phantastikon* (φανταστικόν)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "phantastic-faculty / imagination-faculty"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.3, 428a1–4; III.11
- **Definition**: The faculty/capacity that produces *phantasmata*; the imagination as soul-power
- **Note**: Replaces "faculty of phantasia" or "imagination-faculty" in user prose per `feedback-greek-faculty-names.md`

### *praxis* (πρᾶξις)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "action"
- **Locus**: *Nicomachean Ethics*; *De Motu Animalium*
- **Definition**: Completed action; the terminal actuality of the chain at A_4

### *psychē* (ψυχή)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "soul"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* I–III
- **Definition**: Soul; "the principle of animal life" (DA I.1, 402a5–7)

### *sumplokē* (συμπλοκή)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "combination"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III; O'Gorman *Aristotle's Phantasia*, p. 20
- **Definition**: Combination; the mental calculation through which opinion is formed and truth realized

### *telos* (τέλος)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; in user prose used in place of English "end" (per *phantasia*-untranslated convention)
- **Alternates**: "end"; "goal"
- **Locus**: *Metaphysica* IX.8; *Physics* V.5, 229b25 (motion named by terminus)
- **Definition**: End; goal; the terminus toward which motion proceeds and which gives motion its name
- **Convention**: First-use anchor at §1.1 L53

### *thymos* (θυμός)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek; "spirit"
- **Alternates**: "passion"; "spirited desire"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* II.3, 414b2; *De Motu Animalium* 6, 700b22
- **Definition**: One of the three species of *orexis* — spirited desire (honor-defense, ambition, retaliation); the Platonic-inherited *thymoeides*
- **Related**: *orexis* (genus); *epithymia*, *boulēsis* (other species)
- **Note**: Requires social/honor-cognitive content; not couplable to bare *aisthēsis*

### *to einai ou tauton* (τὸ εἶναι οὐ ταὐτόν)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek
- **English**: "their being is not the same"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.2, 425b26
- **Definition**: Aristotle's qualifier in the actualization-coincidence passage: "the actualization of the sensible object and that of the percipient sense is one and the same actualization, though their being is not the same." Names the structural possibility that one actuality admits of two distinct accounts.

### *to kinoun* (τὸ κινοῦν)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek
- **English**: "that which originates movement"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.10, 433b13–25
- **Definition**: The unmoved originator (first factor of the three-factor schema)

### *to kinoumenon* (τὸ κινούμενον)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek
- **English**: "that which is moved"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.10, 433b13–25
- **Definition**: The moved (third factor of the three-factor schema) — the animal, via the bodily instrument

### *to orekton / to prakton agathon* (τὸ ὀρεκτόν / τὸ πρακτὸν ἀγαθόν)

- **Chosen**: italicized Greek
- **English**: "the desirable" / "the realizable good"
- **Locus**: *De Anima* III.10, 433b13–25
- **Definition**: The unmoved originator in the three-factor schema of locomotion; the realizable good that draws *orexis* into actualization

## German (Heideggerian)

Alphabetical by German term. **Chosen rendering** is the dissertation's authorial preference; **alternates** are commonly-used translator-renderings preserved in quoted material.

### *Befindlichkeit*

- **Chosen**: "disposedness" (Kisiel)
- **Alternates**: "state-of-mind" (M&R); "attunement" (Stambaugh)
- **Locus**: *Being and Time* §29, H.134–138
- **Definition**: The ontological existentiale that discloses Dasein's thrownness; the always-already-disposed structure of being-in-the-world (NOT a mental state). Manifests ontically as *Stimmung*
- **Rationale for "disposedness"**: anti-mentalistic; preserves the reflexive-receptive structure of *sich befinden* ("finding-oneself-disposed-somehow") without locating the disposing inside a subject
- **Convention footnote**: §1.2 L55

### *Bewegtheit*

- **Chosen**: "being-moved" (Metcalf-Tanzer BCAP)
- **Alternates**: "movedness"; "being-in-movement"
- **Locus**: *Basic Concepts of Aristotelian Philosophy* (GA 18) §§25–26
- **Definition**: Heidegger's recovery of Aristotelian *kinēsis* at the level of fundamental ontology; the basic character of beings as moved; "the mode of being of worldly beings whose way of being is to be underway-toward what they are not yet"
- **Convention footnote**: §1.1 L57
- **Related**: *kinēsis* (Greek source); *Bewegung* (conventional German, displaced)

### *Bewegung*

- **Chosen**: NOT adopted; preserved as the conventional German rendering Heidegger displaces with *Bewegtheit*
- **English**: "movement"
- **Definition**: Standard German for "movement"; Heidegger contrasts this with his preferred *Bewegtheit* to emphasize the ontological-character reading

### *Dasein*

- **Chosen**: UNTRANSLATED (per M&R/Stambaugh/Kisiel convention)
- **Locus**: *Being and Time* §§1–9 and throughout
- **Definition**: Heidegger's technical term for the entity whose way of being is at issue for itself; the being whose existence is its own possibility
- **Note**: Standard scholarly practice; no inline English gloss

### *Erstreckung*

- **Chosen**: "stretching-along" (M&R)
- **Locus**: *Being and Time* §72, H.373
- **Definition**: The way Dasein stretches itself along between birth and death; the unity of ecstatic temporal horizons that makes the "between" possible

### *Gegenwart*

- **Chosen**: "present" (M&R)
- **Alternates**: "making-present"
- **Locus**: *Being and Time* §65, H.328–329
- **Definition**: The present-ecstasis of originary temporality; the temporalizing-toward of making-present
- **Convention footnote**: §1.1 L129

### *Geschichtlichkeit*

- **Chosen**: "historicity" (M&R; §1.1 prose)
- **Alternates**: "historicality" (M&R alternative)
- **Locus**: *Being and Time* §74, H.385–386
- **Definition**: The historical character of Dasein's being; the stretching-along of Dasein in the unity of ecstatic-temporal raptures
- **Convention footnote**: §1.1 L113, L129

### *Gewesenheit*

- **Chosen**: "having-been" (M&R)
- **Alternates**: "having-been-ness"
- **Locus**: *Being and Time* §65, H.328–329, H.339–340
- **Definition**: The having-been ecstasis of originary temporality; the existential meaning of *Befindlichkeit*; "a `positive' ecstasis" (not mere past-as-no-longer)
- **Convention footnote**: §1.1 L129

### *Geworfenheit*

- **Chosen**: "thrownness" (M&R)
- **Locus**: *Being and Time* §29, H.135
- **Definition**: Dasein's factical "that-it-is-and-has-to-be"; the always-already-delivered-over character disclosed in *Befindlichkeit*

### *Innerzeitigkeit*

- **Chosen**: "within-time-ness" (M&R)
- **Locus**: *Being and Time* §66, H.333; §§78–81, H.404–421
- **Definition**: The mode of Being "in time" characteristic of entities encountered within-the-world; derivative of ecstatic-horizonal temporality

### *Mitsein*

- **Chosen**: "Being-with" (M&R)
- **Locus**: *Being and Time* §§22–27
- **Definition**: An existentiale: Dasein is essentially Being-with Others

### *Rede*

- **Chosen**: "discourse" (M&R)
- **Locus**: *Being and Time* §34, H.160–166
- **Definition**: The existential-ontological foundation of language; the Articulation of intelligibility, equiprimordial with state-of-mind/*Befindlichkeit* and understanding/*Verstehen*

### *Seinscharakter*

- **Chosen**: "character of Being" (M&R-aligned)
- **Locus**: throughout BCAP and BT
- **Definition**: The being-character of a being; how a being IS what it is

### *Sorge*

- **Chosen**: "care" (M&R)
- **Locus**: *Being and Time* §§39, 41, 42, H.191–200
- **Definition**: The being of Dasein: "ahead-of-itself-Being-already-in-(the-world-)as Being-alongside (entities encountered within-the-world)"
- **Note**: M&R "care" is standard but potentially misleading given ontic senses (worry, concern)

### *Stimmung*

- **Chosen**: "attunement" (Rickert, *Ambient Rhetoric* pp. 131, 146)
- **Alternates**: "mood" (M&R); "Being-attuned"
- **Locus**: *Being and Time* §29, H.134–136; *Fundamental Concepts of Metaphysics* (GA 29/30) §16, p. 67
- **Definition**: The ontic-phenomenal mood; the ontological-atmospheric correlate of *Befindlichkeit*. Per Heidegger: "a mood [*Stimmung*] assails us. It comes neither from the 'outside' nor from the 'inside', but arises out of Being-in-the-world" (BT H.136); "like an atmosphere in which we first immerse ourselves in each case and which then attunes us through and through" (FCM p. 67)

## Notation & framework terms

### Actualization chain notation

- **$A_n$**: actualization stages (nodes)
- **$M_n \rightarrow A_{n+1}$**: transitions between nodes (motion-subscript indexes the source actuality; terminus given by actuality at arrow's head — per *Physics* V.1, 224b7–9)
- **$A_0$**: dyadic structure of sensible object and faculty of sense (in their conjoint actuality), within the encompassing horizon of motion and time — *per Task #20 restructure, 2026-05-22*
- **$M_0 \rightarrow A_1$**: actualization of perception
- **$A_1$**: completed perception with dual residual trace (*resonant aisthēma* + *resonant epithymia*)
- **$M_1 \rightarrow A_2$**: *phantastic* motion (actualization of the *phantasma*)
- **$A_2$**: the *phantasma* proper, crystallized by *phantasia* at the central organ
- **$M_2 \rightarrow A_3$**: cognitive motion (per Task #20 relabel; was "cognitive engagement")
- **$A_3$**: completed cognitive actuality (universal grasped, past recalled, possibility evaluated, belief held; articulated by intellection, memory, deliberative/speculative thinking; orthogonal committal dimension of *doxa*)
- **$M_3 \rightarrow A_4$**: *orektikon* motion (actualization of *orectic* action)
- **$A_4$**: completed action / *praxis*

### Pathos-register (4-fold)

- **Ontological/perceptual sense**: any being's affectability — the structure of being-occurrable-to (the genus *pathos*)
- **Basic-affective-valence sense**: the hedonic tonality co-given with every *aisthēsis*; constitutive feature of *aisthēsis*'s *kritikon* (less-articulated pole)
- **Higher-order *pathē* sense**: the determinate emotions of *Rhetoric* II — each comprising propositional *krisis* + species-specific orectic stance + hedonic tonality (fully concretized pole)
- **Bodily-physiological sense**: the enmattered alteration; cross-cutting axis (present at every level)
- **Anchor**: *Metaphysica* Δ.21, 1022b15–21
- **Convention footnote**: §1.0 L101 (Option β restructure applied)

### Articulational-concretion distinction

- The basic affective valence and the higher-order *pathē* are LEVELS of articulational specificity within the genus *pathos*, NOT exclusive states. The cognitive actuality at A_3 *articulationally concretizes* the latent *resonant epithymia* into the determinate higher-order *pathē* (NOT "flips" — the basic valence persists, the higher-order is the more-articulated form).
