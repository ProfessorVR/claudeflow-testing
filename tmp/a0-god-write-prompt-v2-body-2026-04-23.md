**Subsection 1 — A₀ as First Actuality (~400 words).**
Declare the architectural role. Motion and time constitute the first actuality of the chain, prior in account and substance to A₁. Invoke priority-of-actualities (Aristotle, *Metaphysics* IX.8, 1049b4-10, 1049b12-17) as the organizing principle. State that each motion takes its name from its terminus (Aristotle, *Physics* V.5, 229b25) and that the chain is a sequence of completed actualities, each serving as the unmoved originator of the next. Establish that the soul's inherent rhetorical nature and its capacity for being-affected (*paschein*) generate the progressively complex kinetic and affective actualizations the chain will trace. Cross-reference: *see [Chapter X: The Motion-Inducing Soul — Rhetoric, Attunement, and Actualization (Heidegger, Rickert, Burke)] for the full defense of the rhetorical-nature claim.*

**Subsection 2 — Kinesis as Energeia Ateles (~800 words).**
Develop Aristotle's definition of motion as the fulfillment of the potential *qua* potential (*Physics* III.1, 201a10-14). DEPLOY *Metaphysics* IX.6, 1048b18-34 EARLY as the decisive textual warrant for the incompleteness thesis — the contrast between *kinesis* (making-thin, walking, building: "it is a different thing that is being moved and that has been moved") and *energeia* proper (seeing, thinking, living well: "it is the same thing that at the same time has seen and is seeing"). Argue explicitly the reason motion must be intermediate: if motion were the actuality of what is already actual, motion would have already ceased (once the house exists, no more building); if motion were merely potential without actualization, nothing would be happening; therefore motion must occupy an intermediate ontological register. Use the buildable example (*Physics* III.1, 201b7-14) and the bronze-statue example (*Physics* III.1, 201a28-31) as illustrations subordinated to the argument. Develop the *dunamis*/*energeia* coordination explicitly, holding the two Greek terms together as coordinate but distinct.

**Subsection 3 — Priority of Actuality and the Chain Structure (~600 words).**
Develop *Metaphysics* IX.8, 1050a4-16 as architectural anchor. Engage Kenneth Burke's *A Grammar of Motives* p. 261 treatment of entelechy — preserve Burke's decisive distinction between temporal priority (the seed before the plant) and priority in form and substantiality (the man prior to the boy because the man has attained the complete form toward which the boy is moving). Keep Burke compressed to ~150-200 words of integrated discussion; do NOT use a full block quote. Integrate with *Metaphysics* IX.8, 1050a21-23 (actuality's etymological derivation from *ergon* and extension to *entelecheia*) and *Metaphysics* IX.9, 1051a29-33 (the geometer's construction revealing potentiality through actualization). Resolve the apparent tension between priority-of-actuality and the *ateles* character of motion: the *telos* gives *kinesis* its intelligibility, while the incompleteness of motion as such gives it its distinct mode of being.

**Subsection 4 — The Relational Structure of Kinesis (~500 words).**
Develop *Physics* III.2, 202a13-20 on the mover-moved unity ("a single actuality of both alike") and *Physics* III.3, 202a21-24 on *poiesis*/*pathesis* as the two sides of that single actuality (AGENT'S *poiesis* / PATIENT'S *pathesis*). Use the Thebes-Athens road analogy (*Physics* III.3, 202b13-14) — the road from Thebes to Athens and the road from Athens to Thebes are one road under two descriptions. State the payoff for the chain: A₀ supplies the dyadic structure that A₁ will instantiate — the sensible quality's active potency (*poiesis*) meeting the sense-organ's receptive potency (*pathesis*) as the single actualization of both. Include a brief paragraph establishing residual motion as a structural possibility of *kinesis* — motion that can persist even as its originating cause withdraws — without developing the dual-trace framework (which belongs to the subsequent section on A₁→A₂). Cross-reference the subsequent section on aisthesis.

**Subsection 5 — Chronos as the Number of Motion (~700 words).**
Develop *Physics* IV.11, 219b1-5 definition of time as "the number of motion in respect to before and after." Draw the distinction between the number by which we count and the number that is counted (*Physics* IV.11, 219b5-10). Ground the account experientially with "we perceive movement and time together" (*Physics* IV.11, 219a4-8) and the reciprocity claim ("the movement goes with the magnitude," *Physics* IV.11, 219a10-13) — neither motion nor time is epistemically prior. Develop the now as boundary and measure: it determines time (*Physics* IV.11, 219b12-13); time is "made continuous by the now and divided at it" (*Physics* IV.11, 220a5); the now is "the boundary of the past and the future" (*Physics* IV.14, 223a4-6). Use the body-carried-along analogy (*Physics* IV.11, 219b22-25) to explain the now's relation to motion.

**Subsection 6 — The Soul's Constitutive Role and the Motion-Time Parallel (~900 words; this subsection does the decisive architectural work).**

Two movements within this subsection:

(A) Develop *Physics* IV.14, 223a25-27 on the soul's role in time's full actualization: "if nothing but soul, or in soul reason, is qualified to count, it is impossible for there to be time unless there is soul." Mark the "ontologically incomplete" gloss as INTERPRETIVE — Aristotle's own formulation is an epistemically-cautious conditional; the stronger ontological claim (time-without-soul is ontologically incomplete) is the interpretive reading this chapter adopts. Motion without soul exists; time without soul is latent as countability without being fully actualized as numbered.

(B) Develop the motion-time parallel as the full architectural payoff: just as motion occupies an intermediate ontological register between pure potentiality and complete actuality (per Subsection 2's *energeia ateles* argument), time occupies an intermediate register between brute change and fully articulated numerical order. This shared intermediate-register structure is what allows A₀ to be a genuine actuality (not merely a potentiality to be superseded) while remaining structurally open to further actualizations at A₁, A₂, and beyond. A₀'s mode of being is active grounding rather than static completion. This parallel licenses the chain's iterated actuality-structure — every A₍ₙ₎ inherits this intermediate-register character, which is why each completed actuality can become the unmoved originator of the next motion rather than terminating in itself.
