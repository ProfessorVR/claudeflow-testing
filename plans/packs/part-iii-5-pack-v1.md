### P5 EVIDENCE BANK (graded; established in §§III.1–III.4 and the entry record)
- **E1 CONFIRMED (the keystone of the lens)** — The design bridge's thesis: designing a virtual environment is designing the conditions under which the participant's actualization-of-desire chain runs (engaged) or stalls (bored). Wendt's book supplies the stance (experience as design's object; transparency over invisibility; meaning over utility; conditions of possibility, not guarantees; design as care; advocate preferable worlds and refuse dark patterns), not a turnkey method.
- **E2 CONFIRMED (lever 1 — threshold)** — Threshold frictions dominated the deployment record (crashes 24, install 22, hardware 22; one impossible sign-in; Mac incompatibility complaints); 61 of the 241 surveyed students (≈ a quarter of the class) never entered; entry-tax fed the flight of 47 more. *(v1.1: class size added per G-E.)* Carried facts (verified upstream, §§III.2–III.3 banks + entry record): seventeen-minute stimuli; the Week-10 instrument of a ten-week quarter course; the environment is a multi-user virtual medical center ("the VR clinic" in student words); the course runs asynchronously online. Remedy family: zero-setup delivery (browser-grade access, pre-installed lab access), cross-platform support. Warrant: the friction scorecard + the two flight anatomies.
- **E3 CONFIRMED (lever 2 — the task)** — Interest affords (159; all 21 incorporation co-occurrences), action forecloses (36 mentions, zero positive co-codes; 51 interactivity requests; "just playback"). Remedy family: in-world tasks — needs-capture inside the environment, routes to choose, checkpoints, assignments that must be done in the world. This lever also answers the justification-demand: a task is a reason.
- **E4 CONFIRMED (lever 3 — the object of the looking)** — The withheld object ("not sure what exactly to be looking for"; hours of footage; dark/grainy legibility complaints; caption/transcript requests). Remedy family: supply the *orekton* — viewing briefs (what to look for and why), captions/transcripts, segmenting of long procedures.
- **E5 CONFIRMED (lever 4 — the WE)** — Co-disclosedness appears as demand, not experience (isolation complaint; collaboration requests; "watch with others" named the platform's main pro). Remedy family: scheduled shared sessions; watch-together as the default first experience; group tasks in-world.
- **E6 CONFIRMED (lever 5 — the aftermath)** — Boredom depletes (post-boring fatigue up in 6/8; the reordered study; attention residue, Mark 97). Remedy family: pacing, washout, sequence design — no boring-first ordering; recovery intervals; module lengths respecting the drag onset (≤5 minutes on hostile material).
- **E7 CONFIRMED (the mirror)** — The capture economy instruments attention to seize it at its weakest (targeted notifications exploiting bored/rote states); the same knowledge that exploits a stalled chain can restart one; the norm is Wendt's: advocate preferable worlds, refuse dark patterns.
- **E8 AUTHOR-NOTE (gesture only)** — The author's recorded idea: ambient attention-span instrumentation (eye tracking + data logging in the background) that could alert a teacher or clinician when focus behavior degrades — a care-oriented use of the capture economy's own toolkit.
- **E9 CONFIRMED (gesture only — the instrument proposal)** — What the corpus implies for measurement: two channels always (surface + depth); a three-state reading (restless search / occupied-but-hollow / withdrawn) instead of a bipolar axis; a felt-duration probe; sleep-exit as its own terminal category; divergence, not level, as the diagnostic.
- **E10 CONFIRMED (limits)** — Category caution stands at the close; the desktop deployment leaves the body's channel untested (locomotion, room-scale naturalization — the headset's differential); one course, one platform, small lab cohorts; structural claims only.
- **E11 CONFIRMED (feasibility as condition)** — The 2D turn showed availability to be the zeroth design condition; the five levers are ordered so that the cheapest, largest gains come first (threshold before task before instrument).
