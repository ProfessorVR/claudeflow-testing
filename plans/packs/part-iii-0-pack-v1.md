### P6 CONCEPTUAL SEMANTICS (locked formulations)
- **The chain (A₀→A₄):** A₀ the ontological horizon (the designed *Umwelt*: sensible object + sense-faculty within motion and time) → A₁ completed perception with its dual residue (resonant *aisthēma*; resonant *epithymia*) → A₂ the *phantasma* (the determinate charged image; the pivot) → A₃ completed cognition with committal *doxa* → A₄ completed action (*praxis*), sedimenting into *hexis*.
- **Rhetorical incorporation:** the substantial union of participant and world toward which the chain tends, consummated at the A₃→A₄ transition; two poles held as one — world-disclosedness and co-disclosedness — "one in substrate, different in being"; emphatically not a change of kind.
- **Phantasia-load:** the smaller the data a medium delivers, the more the beholder's own image-making must contribute to make a world of it.
- **The three forms of boredom (FCM):** becoming-bored-by (determinate culprit; restless passing-the-time), being-bored-with (occupied surface, hollow depth; no nameable culprit), profound boredom (analog-only at episodic scale). Two structural moments: being-left-empty; being-held-in-limbo. *Zeitvertreib* = passing-the-time, boredom's visible counter-move.
- **The two-channel rule:** the second form is detectable only as the disagreement of a surface channel and a depth channel — never from one channel, never from a bipolar engaged↔bored scale.
- **Category caution:** episodic stimuli do not disclose beings as a whole; what transfers is the structural grammar, as heuristic; instruments read comportment, not the attunement itself.
- **The hinge (for M4):** designer = producing-art, participant = using-art; the designer furnishes the material and formal causes of the participant's act and *occasions* it; efficient and final causes remain the participant's own — occasioning, never causing. This is the exact difference from behaviorist conditioning.
- **The reanalysis stance:** Part III conducts no new experiments; it re-reads published findings and primary data through the frame; the empirical data yield the conclusions, the frame explains how and why they arose.
