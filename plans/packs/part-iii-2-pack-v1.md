### P5 EVIDENCE BANK (graded)
- **E1 CONFIRMED** — P1 design (ASEE 2023): within-subjects pilot, N=3; three 17-minute videos — boring (a 1989 word-processor tutorial), interesting (an alien-conspiracy video), clinical (360° spinal-deformation surgery); EEG cap with four active channels (F3, F4, P3, P4) at 200 Hz targeting default-mode-network alpha (8–12 Hz) and theta (4–8 Hz); heart rate, gaze, and pupil from the headset's sensor suite.
- **E2 CONFIRMED** — P1 findings with reliability flags: felt-time dilation clean across all three participants (the clean subject felt the 17-minute clinical video as 25–30 minutes); self-reported engagement high (interest 8 of 8 for two participants); the EEG finding — the clinical video's profile more closely resembled the boring control than the interesting one — suggestive only (one clean recording; one with movement artifacts; one with impedance problems); heart measures inconclusive; learning itself never measured despite the study's title.
- **E3 CONFIRMED** — P2 design (ASEE 2024): N=12 headset eye-tracking (plus a four-person webcam sub-study, average cross-correlation 0.874); measures: pupil dilation, gaze position, deviation from center, and the variance of gaze deviation (five-point sliding-window median) at 120 Hz; ANOVA at α = 0.05; presentation order changed to interesting→clinical→boring because a boring-first order had increased participants' exhaustion.
- **E4 CONFIRMED** — P2 findings: gaze-variance on the clinical video statistically distinct from the boring video (p = 0.0004) and resembling the interesting one; all other feature-by-video comparisons nonsignificant; pupil dilation greater for interesting than boring in 6 of 12; gaze-variance lower for interesting than boring in 8 of 12; and the study's own distinction between **high-arousal boredom** (a searching gaze, roving for something to attend to) and **low-arousal boredom** (the "zombie stare," collapsed variance, two participants).
- **E5 CONFIRMED (FE-U; unpublished raw record, anonymized S01–S08)** — The per-video instrument: seven items — fatigue before; boredom (1–9); engagement (1–9); minutes until boredom began; sleep-fight (1–9); felt duration; fatigue after. Aggregates, N=8: mean boredom — boring 7.0, clinical 6.0, interesting 4.5; felt duration ≥ 20 minutes against 17 actual — boring 5/8, clinical 6/8, interesting 5/8 (16 of 24 episodes dilated); boredom onset ≤ 5 minutes — boring 7/8 (two under forty seconds), clinical 5/8; post-fatigue ≥ pre-fatigue after the boring video — 6/8.
- **E6 CONFIRMED (FE-U; the cases)** — S07: clinical rated boredom 6 AND engagement 7; interesting rated boredom 7 AND engagement 6; fatigue after boring climbed 3→7. S05: fell asleep during the boring video and felt it as "5 min"; sleep-fight 9. S04: entered the clinical video at fatigue 7 and rated it boredom 9, engagement 1, felt 30; then rated the boring tutorial boredom 1, engagement 7, felt 8 minutes, fatigue falling 7→1. S03: felt the boring video as "1 hour," onset 25 seconds, sleep-fight 8; fatigue after boring climbed 3→8; the clinical video restored fatigue 6→3. S06: bored by the interesting video (rating 8) with onset at 1 minute, while giving the boring tutorial engagement 5.
- **E6a CONFIRMED (FE-U; boring-video felt durations, per participant)** — S01 15 · S02 20 · S03 "1 hour" · S04 8 · S05 "5 min (fell asleep)" · S06 20 · S07 30 · S08 25 (actual length 17 minutes throughout).
- **E7 CONFIRMED facts / PROJECTED reading** — The keystone: the same clinical stimulus read boring-like on the depth proxy (P1 EEG) and engaging-like on the surface channel (P2 gaze, p = 0.0004), with self-report engaged and felt time dilated; the frame reads the disagreement as the second form's signature (occupied surface, hollow depth) [P]; the reading survives discounting the EEG entirely, since felt-time-versus-gaze provides the two channels within P2's own design logic.
- **E8 CONFIRMED (status)** — The raw record is the author's own unpublished study data, reported with that status marked; participant identities anonymized.

### P6 CONCEPTUAL SEMANTICS
As §III.1 pack P6, plus the three forms in full: **becoming-bored-by** (determinate culprit; conspicuous restless *Zeitvertreib*; surface and depth aligned); **being-bored-with** (no nameable culprit; the whole comportment becomes the passing-of-time; surface occupied while depth is left empty — hence two-channel detection only); **profound boredom** (passing-the-time impossible; at episodic scale invoked ONLY as analog). Two moments: *Leergelassenheit* (being-left-empty), *Hingehaltenheit* (being-held-in-limbo). Dilation = wakeful being-held; sleep = exit, not maximum.
