## 1. What this chapter is, and where it sits in Part II
Part II runs: **metaphysics → methodology (incl. the deferred §M.6 VR↔screen differential) → applications (G&G, then RDR2/Sonny)**. This chapter is the **first application** — and the deferred Phase-2 G&G analysis on the books. It reads G&G as a worked demonstration of the method: a designed *Umwelt* that actualizes the player's desire into **rhetorical incorporation** and, at its keystone beat, ruptures and re-grounds her being-in-the-world. It is **~80% re-ground** of the MA's existing prose onto the A₀–A₄ spine, ~2 passages kept verbatim, 2 superseded, with the net-new apparatus (Calleja R1–R7, Burke consubstantiation, Rickert recalcitrance) layered at marked slots.

**The contribution, in one line:** G&G shows the actualization-of-desire chain driving a *productive* rupture — a designed miniaturization that does not expel the player but **re-incorporates her at a new scale**, hinging her from individual **world-disclosedness** to collective **co-disclosedness**, and partially **supplanting** her prior dispositional ground on exit. This is *veri(dis)similitude* enacted.
