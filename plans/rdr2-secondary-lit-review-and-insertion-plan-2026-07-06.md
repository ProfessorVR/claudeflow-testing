## 1. What the analysis of the cluster shows (the findings the plan is built on)

### 1.1 The two scene-coverage facts (the spine of both goals)
- **The Sonny event is treated by exactly one scholar: Moser (rdr-09)** — her "Saint Denis 'pet' shack" passage, used to mark the player/character *lifeworld asymmetry*: the player can reload a save and "undo" the event for Arthur, but the player's own lifeworld still remembers it. This is (a) the single mandatory interlocutor for our analysis, (b) a first-mover claim we can state in the lit review, and (c) the strongest single insertion candidate — the reload-to-undo paradox independently corroborates the draft's central claim that the event happens *in* the player rather than in front of them. (Caution: Moser locates the shack "outside Saint Denis"; our frame evidence places it near Lakay in Bayou Nwa. Same event — verify her wording against the PDF and handle the geographic gloss without embarrassing either text.)
- **The Valentine tutorial bar-fight is treated by exactly one scholar: McEvoy (rdr-08)** — "dual purpose": combat tutorial + establishing Arthur's potential dishonor. One interlocutor for §2.1's closing frame; near-open lane otherwise.

### 1.2 The debates our analysis actually intervenes in (from `debate-map.md` / `contested-readings.md`)
- **D10 — experiential vs structural-ideological reading** (Moser/Westerside vs Tuominen/Wright): the cluster's deepest split, and the lit review's natural thesis — the actualization-of-desire chain gives a *structural account of the experiential*, mediating the two registers. (This framing already exists in `rdr-cross-pipeline-bridge.md` §5 as anticipatory-application; the lit review is where it gets said in the dissertation's own voice.)
- **D3 — is agency real?** (Ruffino + Vanderhoef & Payne: foreclosed · Tuominen: illusory · McEvoy + Moser: genuine-but-fenced): the Sonny analysis cuts through this axis — the event needs agency to be *real* precisely so it can be seized and used ("the compulsion is built on top of consent's form"). Neither foreclosed nor merely fenced: weaponized. Ruffino's TB formula — "a consequence with no actions" — is the exact structural inverse of the Sonny event (an action whose consequence is withheld from view), a made-to-order foil.
- **D5 — what does the honor system do?** (pedagogy: Moser/McEvoy · interpellation: Tuominen · arithmetic-inscribed-in-Arthur: Bagnoli): the draft's honor-silence finding (the honor-neutral vengeance kill) gains rigor against all three, and the cluster supplies a precedent pattern: the **"KKK-killing costs no honor" exception** flagged by Bagnoli and Tuominen (honor-neutral in Moser too). The system's designed silences are documented scholarly territory — our finding extends a known pattern to an undocumented case.
- **D6 — empathy machine?** (Moser affirmative, alone): Moser's transfer claim (in-game empathy → real-world empathy) is *under-evidenced by her own admission*; our Kyle/Tate reception evidence supplies precisely the kind of testimony her account lacks — a two-way trade (her theory frames our evidence; our evidence funds her claim).
- **D1 — complicit or critical?** (Tuominen subversive · Wright surface-critique-undercut · Bagnoli progressive-but-Arthur-bound): the draft's Bogost/"developer intent" paragraph currently cites only Bogost; the debate lets it acknowledge that whether Rockstar *can* mount procedural social critique is itself contested — Wright as the counterweight keeps the paragraph honest.

### 1.3 The field's shape (for the lit review's narrative)
From `rdr-scholarly-evolution-2019-2023.md`: a short window (2019–2023), two poles set at once in 2019 (Bagnoli ideology vs Westerside phenomenology-of-place), one consolidation (Tuominen 2020), then fan-out into ecology, temporality, empathy, paratext, authenticity, UX — **pre-paradigmatic, sparse internal citation, no second consolidation**. Two constants: the honor system and the frontier myth. The opening for us: a fanned-out field whose two registers remain unreconciled, entered near-first on both of our target scenes.

### 1.4 Relevance tiers (drives the harvest effort)
- **Tier 1 — direct scene/system overlap, body insertions likely:** Moser (rdr-09), McEvoy (rdr-08), Ruffino (rdr-10).
- **Tier 2 — system/debate overlap, 1–2 insertions each plausible:** Tuominen (rdr-03), Bagnoli (rdr-01), Vanderhoef & Payne (rdr-06), Westerside & Holopainen (rdr-02).
- **Tier 3 — lit-review presence only (a sentence or clause each):** Wright (rdr-05), Ventomäki (rdr-07), Crowley (rdr-04), Donald & Reid (rdr-11), immersion/UX FDP (rdr-12).
