## 1. What made the RDR2 lit review work (the model, analyzed)

Anatomy of `RDR2-Lit-Review-DRAFT-v1.md` → final form in `Part-II-COMBINED-v4-2026-07-15.tex`
("The Critical Field", ~1,240 words, 5 paragraphs), with its plan
(`plans/rdr2-secondary-lit-review-and-insertion-plan-2026-07-06.md`) and quote bank
(`rdr2-secondary-quote-bank.md`):

**Process features (reproduce all of these):**
1. **Plan → quote bank → draft, with a user gate between each.** The bank was built
   index-entry-first, then every quote verbatim-verified against `pdftotext` extractions;
   pagination offsets established empirically from headers/footers, never trusted from the index.
2. **The quote bank as a first-class deliverable:** citation-key + pagination-offset table (§0);
   a **wording-traps section** (§0b — index glosses that differ from body prose, title-only
   phrases, embedded second-hand quotations, required words like Tuominen's "still"); per-target
   candidate quotes each carrying verbatim text, printed page, function ("warrant/foil/precedent"),
   and a keep/cut recommendation; then a **user annotation round** whose verdicts became final
   dispositions. This round is a large part of why the result was the best so far.
3. **Traceability + anti-collision machinery in the draft itself:** margin comments with bank IDs;
   a coverage-check footer keyed to the plan's movements; a **double-citation ledger** deciding,
   for every quote wanted in two places, which site gets the full quote and which gets a trimmed
   clause or an alternate passage.
4. **Relevance tiers driving harvest effort** (Tier 1 body-insertion sources get the full
   harvest; Tier 3 = one clause each in the review only).

**Craft features (reproduce, adapted to register):**
1. **A narrative arc, not an inventory.** Movement 1 tells the field's history as a story with
   dates and named actors (2019's two poles → fan-out → the 2023 close), every author given one
   precise intellectual verb (read, coined, pressed, answered, distilled, measured) and one
   clause-length verbatim.
2. **A distillation sentence after the survey** ("Beneath this variety run two constants…").
3. **The field's deepest split staged as an unresolved methodological question** — and the field's
   own silence about it ("the two camps rarely cite one another") reported as a *finding*.
4. **Only the debates the analysis will enter** get sub-paragraph treatment (agency; honor) —
   everything else stays at one clause.
5. **The coverage gap stated as near-first-mover positioning** (McEvoy only; Moser only).
6. **The close hands the field's impasse to the dissertation's method** — the mediation claim:
   a structural account *of* the experiential; what neither register can say alone.
7. Short quotes woven into the author's own syntax; no block quotes; no bold run-in heads.

## 2. How the Part III case differs (request analysis)

1. **The skeleton is already locked.** Unlike RDR2 (where the plan invented the five movements),
   M1's structure was approved module-by-module on 2026-07-16 and the LIT-SLOT pass filled every
   slot with unit-verified citations. **The outline governs content and order; the RDR2 model
   contributes process and craft** — the quote-bank pipeline, the narrative arc inside each
   sub-module, the verb-per-author economy, the ledger discipline. No re-litigation of structure.
2. **Register differs.** RDR2 ran the MA plainer voice with MLA-style inline author-page cites.
   M1 runs the **Part III desktop scientific register** (feedback 2026-07-19): direct declarative
   prose, concrete study facts (designs, Ns, effect sizes — numerals fine), minimal figuration;
   Chicago footnote on first mention of a source (per the M0.1 convention set this morning),
   **parenthetical page-cites for quotations**; the dissertation's voice surfaces only at the
   analytic seams (the DA-10 honesty clause, the Part I–II hooks the outline assigns).
3. **Scale differs.** RDR2: 11 studies / ~1,240 w. M1: ~25 named sources across six sub-modules.
   Proposed target: **~3,200–3,900 words** (M1.1 ~450 · M1.2 ~500 · M1.3 ~800 · M1.4 ~450 ·
   M1.5 ~400 · M1.6 ~900), subject to user adjustment at plan review.
4. **The RDR2 craft moves have exact M1 analogues** (use them):
   - "Two camps rarely cite one another" ↔ **DA-10, the seam**: the CMC social-presence program
     and the VR-pedagogy program share a 1976 ancestor (Short, Williams & Christie) and zero
     contemporary citation traffic — the field's non-conversation reported as a finding (M1.6),
     with the honesty clause that bridging them is Part III's own synthesis.
   - "The field's weighted verdict, not a curated selection" ↔ **DA-01** (immersion raises
     presence without raising learning — six units, two review-level) and **DA-03**
     (psychological determination of presence as the cross-tradition majority).
   - The mediation-claim close ↔ M1.2's "Calleja's incorporation as the education literature's
     unmade bridge — **which Part II has already built, and this section cashes empirically**."
   - The chronological arc ↔ the cluster's own history: 1976 ancestor → the 1997/1998 founding
     instruments (Lombard & Ditton; Witmer & Singer) → the 2003 critiques (Biocca; Kreijns) →
     the Makransky/Parong comparison decade → the 2020s reviews (Radianti; Mayer; VanderMeer;
     Cossio 2025).
5. **Reserved-quote discipline is heavier here.** M3/M4/M5 inherit M1's base cite-where-used
   (Kreijns Pitfall 2 → M5.2; Terry & Doolittle's relocation + Biocca's being-with → M4.3;
   Lombard & Ditton's "we are together" → M1.6/M4.3; De Back, Garrison, VanderMeer → M5.2;
   Kononowicz asymmetry → M2.3/M6; Makransky & Lilleholt mechanism → M4.1). The bank's
   double-citation ledger must pre-assign every such quote: full text at its argumentative home,
   clause or paraphrase in M1.
6. **Citation-form traps (Gross rigor; folder names are storage handles, NEVER citation forms):**
   - Folder "Colin et al. - VR Desktop-3D and 2D (2022)" → cite **Barrett et al. 2022**.
   - Folder "Makransky et al. - Equivalence Home and Class (2019)" → cite **Makransky, Mayer,
     Veitch et al. 2019** (three Makransky-led 2019 papers in the entry — disambiguate each).
   - Version-of-record years override folder years: **Dalgarno & Lee 2010** (folder 2009),
     **Fowler 2015** (folder 2014), **Parong & Mayer 2021** cognitive/affective (folder 2020).
   - **Cook et al. 2011** (TES meta) ≠ Cook, Erwin & Triola 2010 (VP meta, NOT in entry) —
     M1.3 scopes each correctly; the VP skills numbers are **Kononowicz et al. 2019**.
   - Scope notes the outline itself imposes: Chow 2016 = self-presence only; Richardson et al.
     2017 = self-report perceived learning, never grades; De Back 2021 = collaborative-learning
     outcomes only, NOT social presence; Makransky/Veitch varies WHERE not WHETHER-3D;
     Richardson & Swan 2003 = same research program as G&Z, not independent convergence.

  `~/projects/archon-cli/target/debug/archon evidence find "<wording>" --mode exact` (hybrid for
  discovery when the digest gives only a gloss). Accept only `exact-1.00 · bbox ✓` spans; quote
  text enters the bank ONLY as the retrieved span. Known ops facts: snapshot current
  (2026-07-17, 21,852 vec); `PageNumber` locators LOW TRUST — trust chunk page + P2 sub-span
  resolution; harmless broken-pipe panic when piping; binary can vanish after `cargo clean`
  (rebuild recipe in the Stage-3 handoff §5a).
- **Layer 3 — PDFs (final confirmation).** `pdftotext` (never image-reads) from
  `corpus/pedagogy/` (+ boredom cluster PDFs for M1.5) for: (a) every pagination offset
  (printed vs PDF page, established from headers/footers); (b) any span archon cannot anchor at

  (d) the wording-traps sweep (index gloss vs body prose). Anything still unverifiable →
