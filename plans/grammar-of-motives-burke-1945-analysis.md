## 1. Why this work, why now

1. **First Burke pipeline in the corpus; the pentad is the dissertation's primary motive-analytical instrument.** Hawhee 2009 *Moving Bodies* is already-cited as a corpus Burke anchor (Calleja plan v3 §3 Phase 3D); but the primary-source pentad statement, ratios, philosophic-school deployments, and applied dramatism walkthroughs all live in *Grammar*. No prior pipeline has captured them. The dissertation's user-motive-analysis layer is unbuildable without this pipeline.
2. **Reciprocal augmentation of the Calleja PIM pipeline.** Calleja (2011) gives the *structural* answer to "what is the player doing?" (six involvement dimensions × macro/micro temporal phase). Burke (1945) gives the *motive* answer to "why is the player doing it?" (five pentad terms × ten dyadic ratios). The dissertation will deploy **Calleja-PIM × Burke-pentad as a dual-axis analytical apparatus** for human user interaction in games. The Calleja plan v3 already specifies Burke as Layer B anticipatory augmentation; this Burke plan flips the relation — Burke becomes the primary pipeline, with Calleja-deployment as the anticipated game-analytic target.
3. **Anticipates three augmentation layers** the dissertation's ontological theory will integrate (parallel to Calleja plan v3 §1.3):
   - Layer A: **Aristotle + Heidegger ontological substrate** (already-indexed Phantasia Cluster + Heidegger BCAP + Bowin AMT). Each pentad term gets a `dissertation_ontological_target` field. Particularly: Burke himself anchors **Act** in Aristotle/Aquinas (Part 2.III) — direct exegetical bridge to the existing Aristotle corpus index; **Scene** invokes a long materialist lineage (Hobbes, Spinoza, Darwin, Lucretius); **Agent** runs through Berkeley → Hume → Leibniz → Kant → Hegel → Marx (a complete idealism lineage with explicit cite chains).
   - Layer B: **Calleja PIM dimensions** as the structural-elements counterpart. Each pentad term gets candidate Calleja-dimension mappings (provisional, to be tested against Calleja's text in the dissertation): *Scene → Spatial primarily; secondarily kinesthetic-as-body-in-space*; *Act → Ludic + Narrative + Kinesthetic*; *Agent → Shared + the avatar/character question*; *Agency → Kinesthetic (controls) + Ludic (rules-as-means)*; *Purpose → Ludic goals + Narrative arc*; *Attitude (Rhetoric forward-ref) → Affective*. The crosscut is non-bijective by design — dissertation will operationalize the many-to-many mapping.
   - Layer C: **Uexküll's biosemiotic framework** — provisional pentad mappings: *Scene → Umwelt / Merkwelt+Wirkwelt*; *Act → Wirkmal-actualization within Funktionskreis*; *Agent → the subject of the functional circle (Innenwelt)*; *Agency → Merk-/Wirk-organ pairing*; *Purpose → the closure of the functional loop on biological need / valenced object*. Uexküll is also Gibson's upstream source — the Gibson-via-Calleja bridge (Calleja plan v3 §1.5) is doubly anchored here.
4. **Concept-bridge density to existing pipelines:**
   - **Calleja *In-Game* (2011)** — primary reciprocal pipeline; bidirectional cross-mapping at the pentad-term × PIM-dimension level (≥30 candidate edges anticipated)
   - **Aristotle Complete Works + Phantasia Cluster** — Burke Part 2.III "Act" exegetes Aristotle/Aquinas; *energeia* / *dynamis* / *poiema-pathema-mathema* mappings expected; *aisthesis-kinesis* + deliberative *phantasia* bridges plausible
   - **Heidegger BCAP** — Burke's "Act" treatment + Aristotelian *praxis/poiesis* distinction has direct phenomenology resonance with Heidegger's *Basic Concepts of Aristotelian Philosophy*; the **scene-act ratio** parallels Heidegger's *In-der-Welt-sein* / situational-disclosure analysis
   - **Heidegger *Being and Time*** — Burke's substance vocabulary (paradox of substance, dialectic substance, actus and status) overlaps with SZ §§9–13 (Dasein's essence-as-existence; the actus/potentia question)
   - **Uncomfortable Situations (Gross 2017)** — Burke's "Attitude" (forward ref to *Rhetoric*) maps onto Gross's situated emotion; *pathē* are Burke's "incipient acts"
   - **Rickert *Ambient Rhetoric*** — Burke's scene-act ratio is a precursor to Rickert's ambient-rhetorical environment thesis; explicit cite chain plausible
   - **Uexküll *Foray*** — Layer C bridges as above; Burke's "scene" reads as proto-*Umwelt* in places (esp. Part 1.I scene-agent ratio + Part 2.I scene)
   - **Hawhee *Moving Bodies* (2009, already-anchored Burke index)** — bridge to the secondary-literature Burke reading; Hawhee's "moving bodies" thesis especially relevant to scene-agent ratio + agency
   - **Dalton dissertation** — every pentad term becomes a candidate operationalizable construct for game user-motive analysis
5. **Pedagogically central architecture.** Burke explicitly designed *Grammar* as the first volume in a projected trilogy (*Grammar* → *Symbolic* → *Rhetoric*) about the resources of dramatistic analysis. The Introduction (book pp. xv–xxiii) is one of the most cited theoretical statements in 20th-century rhetorical/literary theory. The pentad's analytical clarity makes the Operationalization Manual a high-leverage deliverable: every pentad-term entry + every dyadic ratio + every philosophic-school deployment is dissertation-deployable as analytical apparatus.

## 9. Anticipated risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
