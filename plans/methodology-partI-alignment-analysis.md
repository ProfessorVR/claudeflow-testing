## 1. PRIMARY REVISION — *prohairesis* is the bouletic's completion, not a coordinate Type 4
**The divergence.** The methodology repeatedly frames the fourth type as a coordinate peer: "**Type 4, *prohairesis***" (§2.4); "*boulēsis* → Type 3 Bouletic **+ *prohairesis* → Type 4 Prohairetic**" (§3 table); "predominant **type (1–4)**" (Step 4). Part I now explicitly forecloses this: the types are "**not four coordinate kinds but three keyed to the species, with a fourth, the *prohairetic*, that is no further species of desire but the deliberative completion of the rational wish**… the **completion, not the peer**, of the bouletic."

**Why it matters (not merely cosmetic).** The methodology's *content* already agrees ("*boulēsis* wishes the end; *prohairesis* chooses the means") — but the "Type 4 / (1–4)" labeling implies a four-species *orexis* taxonomy, which Part I rejects to keep the taxonomy clean: **three** *orexis*-species, with *prohairesis* as the bouletic's deliberative completion-phase (the *NE* III.2–3 extension). In diagnosis this changes how a deliberated game-action is named — "bouletic, carried through to choice (*prohairesis*)," not "a distinct Type 4 desire."

**Proposed fix (3 spots; "four types" survives as a grouping, its 4th member re-statused):**
- **§2.4:** "Type 4, *prohairesis*…" → "The fourth, the *prohairetic*, is **not a fourth *orexis*-species** but the **deliberative completion of the bouletic** (*NE* III.2–3): *boulēsis* wishes the end, *prohairesis* chooses and enacts the means."
- **§3 chain table / l.135:** "*boulēsis* → Type 3 Bouletic + *prohairesis* → Type 4 Prohairetic" → "*boulēsis* → Type 3 Bouletic, **completed by *prohairesis*** (deliberated choice of means; not a fourth species)."
- **Step 4 / §6:** "predominant type (1–4)" → "predominant type (the three *orexis*-keyed types; *prohairesis* = the bouletic completed by deliberation)."

## 2. REFINEMENTS (high-value, low-risk; mostly let the methodology *cite* the final Part I text)
**(a) Adopt Part I's "three structures of action — ontology and method" framing for §2.** Part I's "three lenses of widening grain, nested" (3-factor = bare anatomy ⊂ 4-causes = general explanation ⊂ 7-causes = individuation) is a cleaner articulation of exactly what §2.2–§2.4 contain, and it names the dual use (ontology *and* diagnostic method) the methodology operationalizes at Step 0/Step 4. Either reorder §2 to 3-factor-first (Part I's order) or keep the current order but adopt the "three structures / widening grain / ontology-and-method" framing and cite Part I.

**(b) Sharpen three-factor ⊂ four-causes with Part I's exact formulation.** §2.3 says the three-factor schema is "how efficient and final operate within a motion"; Part I gives the precise version — "**three of the four causes lie within the three factors; the formal cause alone stands outside, read off their convergence** as the *eidos* of the motion." Importing this sentence tightens §2.3.

**(c) Add the three *termini* + the floor-of-action to the A₄ treatment.** The methodology has *poiēsis*/*praxis* only *via* the *hexis* bivalence (§7). Part I roots it one level up, at the **terminus** (telos-in-product vs telos-immanent), adds **theōria** as the set-aside *nous*-route terminus, and names the **pre-doxastic floor of action** (bare epithymetic startle). Recommend: in §3/Step 0, state A₄'s three termini (note *theōria* is set aside as deed-less) and the floor, and tie *poiēsis*/*praxis* to the *hexis* bivalence explicitly.

**(d) Ground "capture" / the two compulsions in Part I, and clarify its locus.** Part I (l.202 fn + l.225) now supplies the explicit grounding for the methodology's **bia ≠ capture** distinction: "the addict's runaway appetite… is the agent's own through and through: voluntary in its origin… *not* an external constraint." Refinement: cite this; AND clarify a small slippage — Part I's worked runaway is **epithymetic intemperance** (appetite), whereas the methodology's "capture" is a **technē-hexis runaway** ("the wished-end drops out"; the grind). Both are *voluntary runaways that resemble compulsion but are not bia*; the methodology should say so and ground *both* species in Part I l.225 (and distinguish "habit" the repetition-tendency from the "*hexis*" it forms, per Part I l.202 — a second small fix to §2.4's "habit → hexis-ground").

**(e) Confirm + cite the pathetic-loop insertion points.** Part I's final loop matches the methodology's Step-5 schema exactly: **tone @A₁** (*Stimmung*-saturation enters as diffuse hedonic tone), **figure @M₁→A₂** (*resonant epithymia* works the resemblance into a determinate figure — Part I's *De Insomniis* 460b1–18 mechanism), **gate @A₃** (*doxa* ratifies). The methodology can now cite Part I's De Insomniis grounding rather than asserting the three points.
