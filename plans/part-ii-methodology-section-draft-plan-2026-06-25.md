## 5. The Burke justification (§M.4) — the two-gap argument

**Gap it fills (thesis sentence):** *Burke alone furnishes both the social-substance union that lets a collective "WE" **source** an action — which Aristotle's individual-agent praxis and Calleja's player-world incorporation cannot generate — and the post-action grammar of **motive-attribution and concealment** (pentad/ratios + Attitude) that the chain, as an account of what moved the agent, structurally lacks.*

**Stage as a two-gap argument, not a survey.** After the chain (Aristotle/Heidegger) and the world-face (Calleja) are in place, name the residue each leaves: **(a)** no account of the *WE* that sources action; **(b)** no account of how a completed act is *narrated* and its source *concealed*. Burke fills exactly these two — and only these two.

**Gap 1 — the source-of-action union.** Aristotle's *praxis* is owned by a single *psuchē*; *thymos* "needs a toward-whom" but Aristotle never thematizes the *union of agents* from which collective desire issues. Calleja's incorporation is player↔world and non-social at its core. Burke's **consubstantiation** is act-based ("substance was an act; a way of life is an acting-together," RoM 21), **compensatory to a division** (RoM 22) the player literally arrives in (body vs avatar), and therefore can **source** the next act — "back the gang" *because* I am substantially one with them. This makes the keystone **generative, not decorative** (the RDR2 brawl, §4.1) — *the* proof that Burke is load-bearing.

**Gap 2 — motive-rendering & concealment.** The chain explains what moved the agent; it does not explain how the agent (and analyst) **narrate** that motion or how the real dispositional source gets **concealed**. Burke's pentad is a grammar of motive-**attribution** operating *after* the act (Scene–Act "the situation made me"; Act–Agent "who I've become"; Agency–Purpose "the tools"; Agent–Agent "them"), and his **diagnostic-not-classificatory** casuistry (GM-00: one act relocated across loci) shows the same act yielding rival motive-statements — the concealed term typically **the player's own sedimented *hexis***. **Attitude** (Burke's own 1962 sixth term) = the disposition-with-which, read as *hexeis*.

**Sublation in three beats:** **Preserve** the term *consubstantiation*; **Transform** from symbolic persuasion → the desire-sourcing A₃ doxa-ratified "WE"; **Elevate** to the **other-face** of the single event — stating expressly that Burke saw only the social face, never the world-face or the unifying actualization-of-desire (that unification is the contribution).

**Why Burke over rivals:** Aristotle's *Rhetorica* alone has no consubstantial union and no pentad/ratio grammar (using it would force RODA to reinvent Burke); Perelman = adherence-of-minds, propositional/audience-centered, no substance-union; Bitzer = scene-heavy/act-thin (a degenerate special case of Scene–Act, and it pushes toward the scenic-determinism Burke's casuistry exposes); Booth = author–reader communion inside the text, no substance-union or desire-sourcing. **Decisively:** Burke's consubstantiality descends from his *paradox of substance*, so it **docks onto Aristotle's *energeia*/*dynamis* ontology** (Burke himself exegetes Aristotle/Aquinas on *act*, GM-06) and onto *DA* III.7 — no rival offers a substance-ontology Aristotle can absorb without importing an alien epistemology. And Burke is the only motive-theorist already **dramatistic** (language as a mode of action), matching RODA's action-first commitment.

**The convergence clincher:** the blind **Burke↔Calleja crosswalk** (Appendix A) — two independently-authored pipelines converging on a four-binding core (Scene↔Spatial, Agent↔Shared, Agency↔Kinesthetic, Attitude↔Affective) and on consubstantiation↔incorporation — is **independent evidence the two track one structure**, which is what *licenses the §4 fusion rather than mere juxtaposition*. This is the answer to "why these two together."

**Guards:** eclecticism/kitchen-sink (Burke fills two specific gaps, absorbed *under* the chain; cite the blind convergence); over-application (no 5/6-box checklist — bind every pentad use to a chain-stage or the union, the §5 rule of use); persuasion-model drift (the kept sense is consubstantial *union* + motive-*rendering*, not audience-persuasion); determinism trap (Scene–Act ≠ "the design made me" — it is one rival motive-statement, concealed term often the player's *hexis*); provenance firewall (`burke-direct` vs the dissertation's construction); citation hygiene (**"Grammar 261" unverifiable — no PDF in corpus; mark, do not assert**).
