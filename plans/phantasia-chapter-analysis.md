3. **The subsumption thesis (2.5) is the chapter's culminating argument.** Mood doesn't replace phantasia — it provides the "ontological completion" that Aristotle's account lacks. 4. **The 4-step temporal schema (2.4.3) is a key architectonic structure:**
   1. Stimmung (Mood) → Opens temporal horizon of meaningfulness
   2. Phantasia → Supplies internalized appearances (phantasmata) attuned within this mooded horizon
   3. Doxa → Assents to the appearance as bearing determinate evaluative significance
   4. Pathos (Emotion) → Organizes the appearance into a structured emotional response attended by pleasure or pain

   And in temporal terms: Stimmung = pre-temporal resonance; Phantasia = temporal trace; Pathos = structured motion in time.

5. **Section numbering has one error.** Section 1.5.3 is labeled "(T1)" but its content is about temporal synthesis and continuity of consciousness — this is not T1 (aisthesis). Likely a typo or legacy from an earlier numbering scheme. Flag for author revision.
