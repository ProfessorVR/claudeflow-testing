## 2. Hierarchical Outline

- **Thesis statement**: Phantasia is "the temporal and kinetic capacity that generates a continuous, affectively charged narrative of experience"
  - Phantasia synthesizes discrete perceptual instants into continuous experience
  - Phantasia structures emotion and opinion through "taking-as"
  - Phantasia grounds all higher cognitive functions in the kinetic-temporal horizon of motion and time
- **T-stage preview** (compressed): T1 sensation → T2 perceptual actualization → T3 phantasma generation → T4 phantasma activation
  - Note: T0, T5, T6 introduced later in dedicated sections
- **Guiding principle**: Motion in time is the ontological ground of all psychic activity
- **Chapter roadmap**: the outfielder problem → T-stage exposition → emotion/pathos/doxa → temporal synthesis → Heidegger bridge
