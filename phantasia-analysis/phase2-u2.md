## 2. Hierarchical Outline

- **The Outfielder Problem**: Full T1-T6 walkthrough
  - T1: Crack of bat → auditory sensation
  - T2: Visual tracking → perceptual actualization
  - T3: Ball positions A→B→C stored as phantasmata
  - T4: Pattern recognition → "that's a fly ball to center"
  - T5: Calculating angle, speed, trajectory; updating in real time
  - T6: Running to catch position; body in motion
- **Loop structure** (p. 4): T-stages don't proceed linearly
  - T1-T3 continuous: new percepts arriving constantly
  - T4 running in background: phantasmata available for recognition
  - T5 updating: recalculates as new data arrives
  - T6 feeds back to T1: action changes perceptual field
- **Piano example**: Habituated phantasia
  - Skilled pianist doesn't consciously consult phantasmata
  - Years of practice → phantasia operates below conscious awareness
  - *Hexis* (disposition/habit) as internalized phantasmata
  - "Muscle memory" reinterpreted through phantasia framework
- **Habituated phantasia**: The faculty operating at the level of bodily disposition
  - Not merely automatic recall but a mode of perception-action coupling
  - Phantasia present even when not consciously activated
