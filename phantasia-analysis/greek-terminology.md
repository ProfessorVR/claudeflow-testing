| 1 | φαντασία | phantasia | imagination, appearance, seeming, presentation | DA III.3, 428a1-3 | All | Central term; the soul's temporal medium. Both faculty (capacity to produce images) and appearance (the seeming itself). | core |
| 2 | φάντασμα | phantasma | image, appearance, inner likeness | DA III.7, 431a14-17 | T3-T4 | Product of phantasia; crystallized from residual motion (*apoleipomene kinesis*). Functions as *eikon* (likeness) for absent objects. | core |
| 3 | αἴσθησις | aisthesis | sensation, perception | DA II.5, 416b33 | T1 | First motion: form received without matter. Instantaneous contact. Contrasted with Stimmung in Ch 2 Table 2. | core |

| 5 | πάθος | pathos | affection, being-affected, emotion, passion | DA I.1, 408b5-7 | T2, T6 | Broader than modern "emotion." Includes any being-affected. Ch 2: structured emotional response in the tripartite formula. | core |
| 6 | δόξα | doxa | opinion, belief, judgment | DA III.3, 427b15-21 | T5 | Formed through phantasia's "taking-as." Assent to appearance as bearing evaluative significance. | core |
| 7 | κρίσις | krisis | judgment, discrimination, discernment | DA III.3, 427b15 | T5 | The discriminative act. Related to doxa but more active: krisis distinguishes, doxa assents. | core |
| 8 | ὄρεξις | orexis | desire, appetite, striving | DA III.10, 433a9 | T6 | Motive force: moves animal toward (pursuit) or away (avoidance). Requires phantasia to provide its object. | core |
| 9 | χρόνος | chronos | time | Phys IV.11, 219b1-2 | T0 | "Number of motion with respect to before and after." Depends on kinesis (asymmetric dependency). | core |
| 10 | μνήμη | mneme | memory, remembrance | De Mem 449b22-25 | T4 | Distinguished from phantasia: memory is past-directed; phantasia is temporally flexible. "Distinct temporal phases." | important |
| 11 | ἕξις | hexis | disposition, habit, having | NE 1103a32-33 | T4-T6 | Internalized capacity through practice. Grounds "habituated phantasia" (piano example). | important |
| 12 | ἀντίχησις | antichesis | internal resonance (author's coinage) | Author's term, Ch 2 sec. 2.3 | T2-T4 | **Coined by author**. From ἀντί- (anti-, against/back) + ἦχος (ēchos, echo/sound). The reverberating of sensible form within the soul. Hub node bridging Aristotelian kinetics and Heideggerian existential analysis. | core |
| 13 | ἀπολειπομένη κίνησις | apoleipomene kinesis | left-behind motion, residual motion | DA III.2, 425b24-25 | T2-T3 | The kinesis that persists in the sense organ after the sensory object withdraws. Raw material from which phantasmata crystallize. | core |
| 14 | εἰκών | eikon | likeness, image, icon | De Mem 450b11-451a2 | T4 | Phantasma functioning as a likeness — standing in for the absent object. | peripheral |
| 15 | νοῦς | nous | intellect, mind, reason | DA III.4, 429a10 | T5 | "The soul never thinks without a phantasma" — nous depends on phantasia. | peripheral |
| 16 | νῦν | nun | now, the present instant | Phys IV.14, 223a4-6 | T0 | The paradoxical boundary between past and future. Aisthesis operates at the *nun*; phantasia extends beyond it. | important |
| 17 | δύναμις | dynamis | potentiality, capacity, power | DA II.5 | T1 | Perceptual capacity in its unactualized state. Transitions to *energeia* upon sensory encounter. | important |
| 18 | ἐνέργεια | energeia | actuality, activity, being-at-work | DA II.5 | T1-T2 | Actualized perceptual capacity. The moment of seeing/hearing IS the energeia. | important |
| 19 | φρόνησις | phronesis | practical wisdom, prudence | NE VI | T5 | Operates through deliberative phantasia. Enables comparison of alternatives and planning. | peripheral |
| 20 | δίωξις | dioxis | pursuit, chasing | BCAP pp. 205-206 | T6 | One of the two basic orientations of living beings (Heidegger's BCAP reading of Aristotle). | important |
| 21 | φυγή | phyge | avoidance, flight, fleeing | BCAP pp. 205-206 | T6 | The complementary orientation to dioxis. Together they exhaust the action possibilities. | important |
| 22 | ἀλλοίωσις | alloiosis | qualitative change, alteration | DA II.5 | T1 | The kind of kinesis undergone in sensation. A species of change, not locomotion. | peripheral |
| 23 | συμπλοκή | sumploke | combination, weaving, intertwining | DA III | T5 | The combining of multiple phantasmata in deliberation. Phantasia "weaves together" images. | peripheral |
| 24 | πάσχειν | paskhein | to be affected, to undergo, to suffer | DA II | T1-T2 | The passive reception aspect of aisthesis. The perceiver "undergoes" the form of the object. | peripheral |
| 25 | δοξάζειν | doxazein | to opine, to take to be, to form an opinion | DA III.3, 427b21 | T5 | The act of forming doxa. "In forming opinions we are not free" — the phantasma compels. | peripheral |
| 26 | ἐνάργεια | enargeia | vividness, visual clarity | Rhetoric | T4 | Rhetorical technique: creating vivid phantasmata in the audience through description. | peripheral |
